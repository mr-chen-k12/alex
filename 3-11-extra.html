Skill: Order of Operations (Like Question 6)

1. Simplify: $3 \cdot 2^2 - \sqrt{16} + (4 - 2)^3$
Explanation: Apply PEMDAS.
1. Parentheses: $(4 - 2) = 2$. Equation: $3 \cdot 2^2 - \sqrt{16} + 2^3$
2. Exponents/Roots: $2^2 = 4$, $\sqrt{16} = 4$, $2^3 = 8$. Equation: $3 \cdot 4 - 4 + 8$
3. Multiply: $3 \cdot 4 = 12$. Equation: $12 - 4 + 8$
4. Add/Subtract (left to right): $12 - 4 = 8$, then $8 + 8 = 16$.
2. Simplify: $4 \cdot 1^3 + \sqrt{25} - (5 - 3)^2$
Explanation: Apply PEMDAS.
1. Parentheses: $(5 - 3) = 2$. Equation: $4 \cdot 1^3 + \sqrt{25} - 2^2$
2. Exponents/Roots: $1^3 = 1$, $\sqrt{25} = 5$, $2^2 = 4$. Equation: $4 \cdot 1 + 5 - 4$
3. Multiply: $4 \cdot 1 = 4$. Equation: $4 + 5 - 4$
4. Add/Subtract: $4 + 5 = 9$, then $9 - 4 = 5$.
3. Simplify: $5 \cdot 2^2 - \sqrt{36} + (6 - 5)^4$
Explanation: Apply PEMDAS.
1. Parentheses: $(6 - 5) = 1$. Equation: $5 \cdot 2^2 - \sqrt{36} + 1^4$
2. Exponents/Roots: $2^2 = 4$, $\sqrt{36} = 6$, $1^4 = 1$. Equation: $5 \cdot 4 - 6 + 1$
3. Multiply: $5 \cdot 4 = 20$. Equation: $20 - 6 + 1$
4. Add/Subtract: $20 - 6 = 14$, then $14 + 1 = 15$.

Skill: Adding Mixed Numbers (Like Question 12)

4. Simplify: $5 \frac{3}{4} + 2 \frac{1}{2}$
Explanation: First, find a common denominator for the fractions, which is 4.
Convert $\frac{1}{2}$ to $\frac{2}{4}$.
Add the fractions: $\frac{3}{4} + \frac{2}{4} = \frac{5}{4}$.
Add the whole numbers: $5 + 2 = 7$.
Combine: $7 \frac{5}{4}$. Since $\frac{5}{4} = 1 \frac{1}{4}$, add the 1 to 7 to get $8 \frac{1}{4}$.
5. Simplify: $8 \frac{2}{3} + 4 \frac{5}{6}$
Explanation: Find a common denominator, which is 6.
Convert $\frac{2}{3}$ to $\frac{4}{6}$.
Add the fractions: $\frac{4}{6} + \frac{5}{6} = \frac{9}{6}$.
Add the whole numbers: $8 + 4 = 12$.
Combine: $12 \frac{9}{6}$. Simplify the improper fraction: $\frac{9}{6} = 1 \frac{3}{6} = 1 \frac{1}{2}$. Add 1 to 12 to get $13 \frac{1}{2}$.
6. Simplify: $7 \frac{4}{5} + 3 \frac{3}{10}$
Explanation: Find a common denominator, which is 10.
Convert $\frac{4}{5}$ to $\frac{8}{10}$.
Add the fractions: $\frac{8}{10} + \frac{3}{10} = \frac{11}{10}$.
Add the whole numbers: $7 + 3 = 10$.
Combine: $10 \frac{11}{10}$. Since $\frac{11}{10} = 1 \frac{1}{10}$, add 1 to 10 to get $11 \frac{1}{10}$.

Skill: Properties of 3D Figures (Like Question 24)

7. How many vertices (corners) does a rectangular prism have?
Explanation: A rectangular prism (like a standard box) has 4 corners on the top face and 4 corners on the bottom face, giving a total of 8 vertices.
8. How many faces does a cube have?
Explanation: A cube is a special type of rectangular prism where all sides are equal. Like standard dice, it has 6 flat faces.
9. If a 3D shape has 6 rectangular faces, how many edges does it have?
Explanation: A shape with 6 rectangular faces is a rectangular prism. It has 4 edges around the top base, 4 around the bottom base, and 4 vertical edges connecting the bases, totaling 12 edges.

Skill: Area of Rectangles with Fractions (Like Question 27)

10. What is the area of a rectangle with a length of $\frac{2}{3}\text{ in.}$ and a width of $\frac{1}{2}\text{ in.}$?
Explanation: The formula for the area of a rectangle is length $\times$ width.
$A = \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{1}{2}$. Multiply straight across to get $\frac{2}{6}$.
Reduce the fraction by dividing top and bottom by 2: $\frac{1}{3}\text{ sq in.}$
11. What is the area of a rectangle with a length of $1 \frac{1}{2}\text{ in.}$ and a width of $\frac{3}{4}\text{ in.}$?
Explanation: First, convert the mixed number to an improper fraction: $1 \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{2}$.
Multiply length by width: $\frac{3}{2} \times \frac{3}{4} = \frac{9}{8}$.
Convert back to a mixed number: $\frac{9}{8} = 1 \frac{1}{8}\text{ sq in.}$
12. What is the area of a rectangle with a length of $\frac{5}{8}\text{ in.}$ and a width of $2\text{ in.}$?
Explanation: Write the whole number as a fraction: $2 = \frac{2}{1}$.
Multiply length by width: $\frac{5}{8} \times \frac{2}{1} = \frac{10}{8}$.
Reduce the fraction: $\frac{5}{4}$.
Convert to a mixed number: $\frac{5}{4} = 1 \frac{1}{4}\text{ sq in.}$
