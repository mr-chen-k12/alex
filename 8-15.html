f. GENERALIZE
Write the prime factorization of 30, 300, and 3000. What patterns do you see in the prime factorizations of these numbers?

Solution:

The prime factorizations are: \[ 30 = 2^1 \cdot 3^1 \cdot 5^1 \] \[ 300 = 30 \cdot 10 = (2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5) \cdot (2 \cdot 5) = 2^2 \cdot 3^1 \cdot 5^2 \] \[ 3000 = 300 \cdot 10 = (2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5^2) \cdot (2 \cdot 5) = 2^3 \cdot 3^1 \cdot 5^3 \]

Pattern: The factor of \(3\) remains raised to the first power, while the exponents of \(2\) and \(5\) increase by 1 for each added zero.

2.
A wooden board measures \(6\frac{1}{2}\) feet long. How many inches long is the board?

Answer: 78 inches

Convert feet to inches (\(1 \text{ foot} = 12 \text{ inches}\)): \[ 6 \times 12 = 72 \text{ inches} \] \[ \frac{1}{2} \times 12 = 6 \text{ inches} \] Total: \(72 + 6 = 78 \text{ inches}\).
3.
Marcus answered 18 out of 20 questions correctly on a quiz. What fraction of the questions did he get correct in simplest form? What percent did he get correct?

Fraction: \(\frac{9}{10}\)

Percent: \(90\%\)

Fraction: \(\frac{18}{20} = \frac{18 \div 2}{20 \div 2} = \frac{9}{10}\)
Percent: \(\frac{9}{10} = \frac{90}{100} = 90\%\)

Write \(3\frac{1}{3}\) as an improper fraction. Then multiply it by \(\frac{3}{10}\). What is the product?

Improper Fraction: \(\frac{10}{3}\)

Product: \(1\)

Convert to improper fraction: \[ 3\frac{1}{3} = \frac{(3 \times 3) + 1}{3} = \frac{10}{3} \] Multiply: \[ \frac{10}{3} \times \frac{3}{10} = \frac{30}{30} = 1 \]
7.
A bag contains 3 red marbles, 5 blue marbles, and 2 green marbles. If one marble is drawn, what is the probability that it will not be blue?

Answer: \(\frac{1}{2}\)

Total marbles = \(3 + 5 + 2 = 10\).
Marbles that are not blue = \(3 \text{ (red)} + 2 \text{ (green)} = 5\).
Probability = \(\frac{5}{10} = \frac{1}{2}\).
8.
Find the sum: \(5\frac{1}{2} + 2\frac{2}{3} + 1\frac{1}{6}\)

Answer: \(9\frac{1}{3}\)

Find the common denominator (LCD = 6): \[ 5\frac{1}{2} = 5\frac{3}{6} \] \[ 2\frac{2}{3} = 2\frac{4}{6} \] \[ 1\frac{1}{6} = 1\frac{1}{6} \] Add whole numbers and fractions: \[ (5 + 2 + 1) + \left(\frac{3}{6} + \frac{4}{6} + \frac{1}{6}\right) = 8 + \frac{8}{6} = 8 + 1\frac{2}{6} = 9\frac{1}{3} \]
9.

Answer: \(3\frac{5}{9}\)

20.
Two angles of a triangle measure \(45^\circ\) and \(75^\circ\). What is the measure of the third angle?

Answer: \(60^\circ\)

The sum of angles in a triangle is \(180^\circ\): \[ 180^\circ - (45^\circ + 75^\circ) = 180^\circ - 120^\circ = 60^\circ \]
21.
The ratio of boys to girls in a classroom is 4 to 5. If there are 20 girls, how many boys are there?

Answer: 16 boys

Set up a proportion: \[ \frac{\text{boys}}{\text{girls}} = \frac{4}{5} = \frac{x}{20} \] Since \(5 \times 4 = 20\), multiply \(4 \times 4 = 16\) boys.
