1. Complex Probability: A bag contains 4 red marbles, 5 blue marbles, and 6 green marbles. If you pull out a red marble and do NOT replace it, what is the probability that you will pull out another red marble on your second turn?

Step 1: Find the probability of the first event. There are 4 red marbles and 15 total marbles. Probability = \(\frac{4}{15}\).

Step 2: Find the probability of the second event. Since one red marble was removed, there are now 3 red marbles left out of 14 total marbles. Probability = \(\frac{3}{14}\).

Step 3: Multiply the probabilities: \(\frac{4}{15} \times \frac{3}{14} = \frac{12}{210}\).

Step 4: Reduce the fraction: \(\frac{12 \div 6}{210 \div 6} = \frac{2}{35}\).

Answer: \(\frac{2}{35}\)

2. Complex Probability: Your sock drawer has 6 pairs of black socks and 4 pairs of grey socks. You pull out a pair of grey socks and don't replace them. What is the probability of pulling out a pair of black socks next?

Step 1: First pull probability (grey): \(\frac{4}{10}\). (Though this already happened, it sets up the second pull).

Step 2: Because you removed a grey pair, there are now 3 grey pairs and 6 black pairs left, making 9 total pairs.

Step 3: The probability of pulling black next is the number of black pairs left divided by the new total: \(\frac{6}{9}\).

Step 4: Reduce: \(\frac{6}{9} = \frac{2}{3}\).

Answer: \(\frac{2}{3}\)

3. Fraction to Decimal: Change the mixed number \(3 \frac{2}{5}\) to a decimal.

Step 1: Keep the whole number (3) as a whole number.

Step 2: Divide the numerator by the denominator for the fraction part: \(2 \div 5 = 0.4\).

Step 3: Combine them: \(3 + 0.4 = 3.4\).

Answer: 3.4

4. Decimal to Fraction: Convert \(0.65\) to a fraction in simplest form.

Step 1: Write the decimal numerals as the numerator: 65.

Step 2: Write the decimal place value as the denominator. Since 5 is in the hundredths place, use 100: \(\frac{65}{100}\).

Step 3: Reduce by dividing top and bottom by 5: \(\frac{65 \div 5}{100 \div 5} = \frac{13}{20}\).

Answer: \(\frac{13}{20}\)

5. Percent to Fraction: Convert \(45\%\) to a fraction in simplest form.

Step 1: Write the percent as the numerator: 45.

Step 2: Write 100 as the denominator: \(\frac{45}{100}\).

Step 3: Reduce by dividing by 5: \(\frac{45 \div 5}{100 \div 5} = \frac{9}{20}\).

Answer: \(\frac{9}{20}\)

6. Percent to Decimal: Convert \(8\%\) to a decimal.

Result: \(0.08\).

7. Decimal to Percent: Convert \(0.425\) to a percent.

8. Finding a Ratio: What is the dog-to-cat ratio at an animal shelter with 45 animals if there are 20 cats?

Step 1: Find the number of dogs. Total animals (45) - cats (20) = 25 dogs.

Step 2: Make a fraction with the first listed label (dogs) as numerator and second (cats) as denominator: \(\frac{25}{20}\).

Step 3: Reduce to simplest form: \(\frac{25 \div 5}{20 \div 5} = \frac{5}{4}\).

Answer: \(\frac{5}{4}\)

9. Order of Operations: Solve: \(5^2 - 3 \times 4 + 10\)

Step 1: Exponents first: \(5^2 = 25\). Equation becomes \(25 - 3 \times 4 + 10\).

Step 2: Multiplication next: \(3 \times 4 = 12\). Equation becomes \(25 - 12 + 10\).

Step 3: Addition and Subtraction left to right: \(25 - 12 = 13\).

Step 4: \(13 + 10 = 23\).

Answer: 23

10. Order of Operations: Solve: \(12 + (9 - 4)^2 \div 5\)

Step 1: Parentheses first: \(9 - 4 = 5\). Equation becomes \(12 + (5)^2 \div 5\).

Step 2: Exponents next: \(5^2 = 25\). Equation becomes \(12 + 25 \div 5\).

Step 3: Division next: \(25 \div 5 = 5\). Equation becomes \(12 + 5\).

Step 4: Addition: \(12 + 5 = 17\).

Answer: 17

11. Two-Step Equation: Solve for \(x\): \(4x + 9 = 33\)

Step 1: Subtract to get the variable term alone. Subtract 9 from both sides: \(4x = 33 - 9\) → \(4x = 24\).

Step 2: Divide to find the missing factor. Divide by 4: \(x = 24 \div 4\) → \(x = 6\).

Answer: x = 6

12. Two-Step Equation: Solve for \(y\): \(6y - 5 = 43\)

Step 1: Add 5 to both sides: \(6y = 43 + 5\) → \(6y = 48\).

Step 2: Divide by 6: \(y = 48 \div 6\) → \(y = 8\).

Answer: y = 8

13. Volume of a Rectangular Prism: Find the volume of a rectangular prism with a length of 10 inches, a width of 4 inches, and a height of 5 inches.

Step 1: Use the formula \(V = Length \times Width \times Height\).

Step 2: Substitute the values: \(V = 10 \times 4 \times 5\).

Step 3: Multiply: \(40 \times 5 = 200\) cubic inches.

Answer: 200 cubic inches

14. Adding Integers: Solve: \(-8 + 15\)

Rule: Adding integers with different signs: Subtract the numbers and the bigger digit gets the sign.

Step 1: Subtract: \(15 - 8 = 7\).

Step 2: 15 is larger than 8 and is positive, so the answer is positive.

Answer: 7

15. Subtracting Integers: Solve: \(-6 - (-11)\)

Rule: Change the subtraction symbol to addition, and change the second number to its opposite.

Step 1: Rewrite as \(-6 + (+11)\).

Step 2: Use addition rules (different signs, subtract): \(11 - 6 = 5\).

Answer: 5

16. Subtracting Integers: Solve: \(7 - 15\)

Step 1: Change subtraction to addition and flip the second sign: \(7 + (-15)\).

Step 2: Subtract the absolute values: \(15 - 7 = 8\). The larger digit (15) is negative, so the answer is negative.

Answer: -8

17. Adding Integers: Solve: \(-4 + (-9)\)

18. Solving a Proportion: Solve for \(x\): \(\frac{4}{9} = \frac{x}{36}\)

Step 1: Multiply the known cross products: \(4 \times 36 = 144\).

Step 2: Divide by the remaining number: \(144 \div 9 = 16\).

Answer: x = 16

19. Subtracting Mixed Numbers (with Regrouping): Solve: \(7 \frac{1}{5} - 2 \frac{4}{5}\)

Step 1: Regroup because you cannot subtract 4 from 1. Borrow 1 whole from 7 (making it 6). Add the whole (\(\frac{5}{5}\)) to \(\frac{1}{5}\) to get \(\frac{6}{5}\).

Step 2: The problem is now \(6 \frac{6}{5} - 2 \frac{4}{5}\).

Step 3: Subtract fractions: \(\frac{6}{5} - \frac{4}{5} = \frac{2}{5}\).

Step 4: Subtract whole numbers: \(6 - 2 = 4\).

Answer: \(4 \frac{2}{5}\)

20. Subtracting Mixed Numbers (with Regrouping): Solve: \(9 \frac{3}{8} - 5 \frac{7}{8}\)

Step 1: Regroup. Change 9 to 8. Add the denominator to the numerator: \(3 + 8 = 11\). The fraction becomes \(\frac{11}{8}\).

Step 2: The problem is now \(8 \frac{11}{8} - 5 \frac{7}{8}\).

Step 3: Subtract fractions: \(\frac{11}{8} - \frac{7}{8} = \frac{4}{8}\).

Step 4: Subtract whole numbers: \(8 - 5 = 3\).

Step 5: Reduce: \(3 \frac{4}{8} = 3 \frac{1}{2}\).

Answer: \(3 \frac{1}{2}\)

21. Divide Mixed Numbers: Solve: \(4 \frac{1}{2} \div 1 \frac{1}{2}\)

Step 1: Change to improper fractions: \(\frac{9}{2} \div \frac{3}{2}\).

Step 2: "Keep, Change, Flip". Keep \(\frac{9}{2}\), change \(\div\) to \(\times\), flip \(\frac{3}{2}\) to \(\frac{2}{3}\).

Step 3: Multiply: \(\frac{9}{2} \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{18}{6}\).

Step 4: Reduce: \(\frac{18}{6} = 3\).

Answer: 3

22. Divide Mixed Numbers: Solve: \(2 \frac{2}{3} \div 4\)

Step 1: Change to improper fractions: \(\frac{8}{3} \div \frac{4}{1}\).

Step 2: Keep, Change, Flip: \(\frac{8}{3} \times \frac{1}{4}\).

Step 3: Multiply: \(\frac{8}{12}\).

Step 4: Reduce: \(\frac{8 \div 4}{12 \div 4} = \frac{2}{3}\).

Answer: \(\frac{2}{3}\)

25. Area of a Circle: Find the area of a circle with a radius of 5 cm. (Use \(\pi = 3.14\))

Step 1: Formula is \(A = \pi r^2 = 3.14 \times (radius \times radius)\).

Step 2: Sub in the radius: \(A = 3.14 \times 5^2 = 3.14 \times 25\).

Step 3: Multiply: \(3.14 \times 25 = 78.5\).

Answer: 78.5 sq cm

26. Area of a Circle: Find the area of a circle with a diameter of 12 inches. (Use \(\pi = 3.14\))

Step 1: Find the radius. \(Radius = Diameter \div 2 = 12 \div 2 = 6\) inches.

Step 2: Use the formula \(A = 3.14 \times 6^2 = 3.14 \times 36\).

Step 3: Multiply: \(3.14 \times 36 = 113.04\).

Answer: 113.04 sq inches

29. Perimeter of a Complex Shape: Find the perimeter of the L-shaped polygon below. Hint: You must first find the lengths of the missing vertical inner side and the missing bottom side.

Step 1: Find the missing vertical side. The total left height is 11 cm. The right side is 5 cm. So the inner vertical side is \(11 - 5 = 6\) cm.

Step 2: Find the missing horizontal bottom side. The top horizontal side is 6 cm. The inner horizontal side is 8 cm. Total bottom width = \(6 + 8 = 14\) cm.

Step 3: Add the lengths of ALL sides to find the perimeter: \(6 (top) + 6 (inner vert) + 8 (inner horiz) + 5 (right vert) + 14 (bottom) + 11 (left vert) = 50\).
