Saxon Math Course 1 - Lesson 113 Solutions

Lesson Practice

a. Find the sum: $6\text{ ft } 5\text{ in} + 4\text{ ft } 8\text{ in}$
Answer: $11\text{ ft } 1\text{ in}$

Explanation: First, add the feet and inches separately:
$6\text{ ft} + 4\text{ ft} = 10\text{ ft}$
$5\text{ in} + 8\text{ in} = 13\text{ in}$
Since $12\text{ inches} = 1\text{ foot}$, convert $13\text{ inches}$ into $1\text{ foot } 1\text{ inch}$.
Total: $10\text{ ft} + 1\text{ ft } 1\text{ in} = 11\text{ ft } 1\text{ in}$.
b. Find the difference: $3\text{ hr } 15\text{ min} - 1\text{ hr } 40\text{ min}$
Answer: $1\text{ hr } 35\text{ min}$

Explanation: You cannot subtract $40\text{ min}$ from $15\text{ min}$, so borrow $1\text{ hour}$ ($60\text{ minutes}$) from the $3\text{ hours}$.
$3\text{ hr } 15\text{ min}$ becomes $2\text{ hr } 75\text{ min}$.
Now subtract: $(2\text{ hr} - 1\text{ hr}) = 1\text{ hr}$, and $(75\text{ min} - 40\text{ min}) = 35\text{ min}$.
c. Write in standard notation: $1.2 \times 10^4$
Answer: $12,000$

Explanation: Multiplying by $10^4$ shifts the decimal point 4 places to the right. $1.2 \rightarrow 12 \rightarrow 120 \rightarrow 1200 \rightarrow 12000$.
d. Write in standard notation: $1.5\text{ million}$
Answer: $1,500,000$

Explanation: "Million" means multiplying by $1,000,000$.
$1.5 \times 1,000,000 = 1,500,000$.
e. Write in standard notation: $2 \frac{1}{2}\text{ billion}$
Answer: $2,500,000,000$

Explanation: Change the fraction to a decimal: $2.5$. Multiply by $1\text{ billion}$ ($1,000,000,000$).
$2.5 \times 1,000,000,000 = 2,500,000,000$.
f. Write in standard notation: $\frac{1}{4}\text{ million}$
Answer: $250,000$

Explanation: Convert $\frac{1}{4}$ to the decimal $0.25$. Multiply by $1,000,000$.
$0.25 \times 1,000,000 = 250,000$.

Written Practice

1. For cleaning the yard, four teenagers were paid a total of $75.00. If they divide the money equally, how much money will each teenager receive?
Answer: $\$18.75$

Explanation: Divide the total money by the number of teenagers:
$\$75.00 \div 4 = \$18.75$.
2. Which of the following is the best estimate of the length of a bicycle?
A. $0.5\text{ m}$    B. $2\text{ m}$    C. $6\text{ m}$    D. $36\text{ m}$
Answer: B. $2\text{ m}$

Explanation: $0.5\text{ m}$ (approx 1.6 feet) is too short. $6\text{ m}$ (approx 20 feet) is too long. $2\text{ meters}$ (approx 6.5 feet) is the realistic length of an average bicycle.
3. If the chance of rain is 80%, what is the probability that it will not rain? Express the answer as a decimal.
Answer: $0.2$

Explanation: The total probability of all outcomes is 100%.
Chance of no rain = $100\% - 80\% = 20\%$.
To express $20\%$ as a decimal, move the decimal point two places left: $0.20$ or $0.2$.
4. The ratio of students who walk to school to students who ride a bus to school is 5 to 3. If there are 120 students, how many students walk to school?
Answer: $75$

Explanation: The ratio is $5\text{ (walk)} : 3\text{ (bus)}$. Total ratio parts = $5 + 3 = 8$.
Find the value of one part: $120 \div 8 = 15$ students per part.
Students who walk = $5\text{ parts} \times 15 = 75$ students.
5. Write $4.5 \times 10^6$ as a standard numeral.
Answer: $4,500,000$

Explanation: Move the decimal point 6 places to the right: $4.5 \rightarrow 4500000$.
6. Calculate mentally:
a. $(-12)(+3)$    b. $(-12)(-3)$    c. $\frac{-12}{+3}$    d. $\frac{-12}{-3}$
Answer:
a. $-36$
b. $36$
c. $-4$
d. $4$

Explanation:
When multiplying or dividing:
- Different signs yield a negative result.
- Like signs yield a positive result.
7. Calculate mentally:
a. $-12 + -3$    b. $-12 - -3$    c. $+3 + -12$    d. $+3 - -12$
Answer:
a. $-15$
b. $-9$
c. $-9$
d. $15$

Explanation:
a. Adding two negatives makes a larger negative.
b. Subtracting a negative is adding a positive: $-12 + 3 = -9$.
c. Positive plus negative: subtract the smaller from larger, keep sign of larger.
d. Subtracting a negative is adding a positive: $3 + 12 = 15$.
8. Describe a method for arranging these fractions from least to greatest: $\frac{3}{4}, \frac{3}{5}, \frac{4}{5}$. Then arrange them.
Answer: Least to greatest: $\frac{3}{5}, \frac{3}{4}, \frac{4}{5}$

Explanation: One method is to find a common denominator. For $4, 5, \text{and } 5$, the LCM is $20$.
$\frac{3}{4} = \frac{15}{20}$
$\frac{3}{5} = \frac{12}{20}$
$\frac{4}{5} = \frac{16}{20}$
Comparing numerators: $12 < 15 < 16$, thus $\frac{3}{5} < \frac{3}{4} < \frac{4}{5}$.
Alternatively, convert to decimals: $0.75, 0.60, 0.80$.

15. $0.6\text{ m} \times 0.5\text{ m}$

16. $5^2 + 2^5$
Answer: $57$

Explanation: Evaluate the exponents first:
$5^2 = 5 \times 5 = 25$
$2^5 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 2 = 32$
Add them: $25 + 32 = 57$.
17. Find the area of this trapezoid. Show and explain your work to justify your answer.
(Top base = $7\text{ ft}$, Bottom base = $10\text{ ft}$, Height = $4\text{ ft}$)
Answer: $34\text{ sq ft}$

Explanation: The formula for the area of a trapezoid is $A = \frac{1}{2}(b_1 + b_2)h$.
Substitute the given values: $A = \frac{1}{2}(10 + 7) \times 4$.
$A = \frac{1}{2}(17) \times 4 = 17 \times 2 = 34$.
18. $2\text{ feet } 3\text{ inches} - 1\text{ foot } 9\text{ inches}$
Answer: $6\text{ inches}$

Explanation: You cannot subtract $9\text{ in}$ from $3\text{ in}$, so borrow $1\text{ foot}$ ($12\text{ inches}$) from the $2\text{ feet}$.
$2\text{ ft } 3\text{ in}$ becomes $1\text{ ft } 15\text{ in}$.
Subtract: $(1\text{ ft} - 1\text{ ft}) = 0\text{ ft}$. $(15\text{ in} - 9\text{ in}) = 6\text{ inches}$.
19. a. Which line in this figure is not a line of symmetry? (Lines $f$ and $g$ are vertical lines on the left/right, $h$ is the horizontal midline).
b. Does the figure have rotational symmetry? (Disregard lines $f, g, \text{and } h$.) Explain your answer.
Answer:
a. Lines $f$ and $g$ are not lines of symmetry.
b. Yes, it has rotational symmetry.

Explanation:
a. A line of symmetry divides a figure into two identical halves that are mirror images. Folding the figure along lines $f$ or $g$ does not yield perfectly overlapping halves. Only $h$ (and a hypothetical vertical center line not labeled) act as lines of symmetry.
b. If you rotate the capsule shape $180^\circ$ around its center point, it will map exactly onto itself. Thus, it has point/rotational symmetry.
20. How many cubes one centimeter on each edge would be needed to fill this box?
(Dimensions: $5\text{ cm} \times 3\text{ cm} \times 2\text{ cm}$)
Answer: $30\text{ cubes}$

Explanation: Find the volume of the rectangular prism: $V = \text{length} \times \text{width} \times \text{height}$.
$V = 5 \times 3 \times 2 = 30\text{ cm}^3$.
Since each cube is $1\text{ cm}^3$, exactly 30 cubes are needed.
21. Elizabeth worked for three days and earned $\$240$. At that rate, how much would she earn in ten days?
Answer: $\$800$

Explanation: First, find her daily rate by dividing the total earned by the number of days:
$\$240 \div 3\text{ days} = \$80\text{ per day}$.
Then multiply the daily rate by $10\text{ days}$: $\$80 \times 10 = \$800$.
22. Seventy is the product of which three prime numbers?
Answer: $2, 5, \text{and } 7$

Explanation: Find the prime factorization of 70.
$70 = 7 \times 10$.
$10 = 2 \times 5$.
So, $70 = 2 \times 5 \times 7$. All three are prime.
23. Saturn is about $900\text{ million}$ miles from the Sun. Write that distance in standard notation.
Answer: $900,000,000\text{ miles}$

Explanation: "Million" signifies $6\text{ zeros}$ after the number, or multiplying by $1,000,000$.
$900 \times 1,000,000 = 900,000,000$.
24. Use the rhombus at the right for problems a-c. (Side = $8\text{ in}$, Height = $7\text{ in}$)
a. What is the perimeter of this rhombus?
b. What is the area of this rhombus?
c. If an acute angle of this rhombus measures $61^\circ$, then what is the measure of each obtuse angle?
Answer:
a. $32\text{ in.}$
b. $56\text{ sq in.}$
c. $119^\circ$

Explanation:
a. A rhombus has 4 equal sides. $P = 4 \times 8\text{ in} = 32\text{ in.}$
b. Area of a rhombus/parallelogram is base $\times$ height. $A = 8\text{ in} \times 7\text{ in} = 56\text{ sq in.}$
c. Consecutive angles in a rhombus are supplementary (add up to $180^\circ$). Obtuse angle = $180^\circ - 61^\circ = 119^\circ$.
25. The ratio of quarters to dimes in Keiko's savings jar is 5 to 8. If there were 120 quarters, how many dimes were there?
Answer: $192\text{ dimes}$

Explanation: Set up a proportion: $\frac{\text{Quarters}}{\text{Dimes}} = \frac{5}{8} = \frac{120}{x}$.
Since $5 \times 24 = 120$, multiply the denominator by the same factor: $8 \times 24 = 192$.
26. a. The coordinates of the three vertices of a triangle are $(0, 0), (0, 4), \text{and } (4, 4)$. What is the area of the triangle?
b. If the triangle were reflected across the y-axis, what would be the coordinates of the vertices of the reflection?
Answer:
a. $8\text{ square units}$
b. $(0, 0), (0, 4), \text{and } (-4, 4)$

Explanation:
a. Plotting the points reveals a right triangle with a base of 4 units and a height of 4 units. Area = $\frac{1}{2}bh = \frac{1}{2}(4)(4) = 8$.
b. Reflecting across the y-axis negates the x-coordinates: $(x, y) \rightarrow (-x, y)$. The points on the y-axis stay the same.
27. What was the median age of the children attending the luncheon?
(Data: $8, 9, 8, 8, 7, 9, 12, 12, 11, 16$)
Answer: $9$

Explanation: First, list the 10 ages in order from least to greatest:
$7, 8, 8, 8, 9, 9, 11, 12, 12, 16$.
Since there is an even number of values, the median is the average of the 5th and 6th middle values: $\frac{9 + 9}{2} = 9$.
28. What was the mean age of the children at the luncheon?
(Data: $8, 9, 8, 8, 7, 9, 12, 12, 11, 16$)
Answer: $10$

Explanation: The mean is the sum of all values divided by the number of values.
Sum = $8+9+8+8+7+9+12+12+11+16 = 100$.
Mean = $100 \div 10 = 10$.
29. The diameter of a playground ball is $10\text{ inches}$. What is the circumference of the ball? (Use $3.14$ for $\pi$.) How can estimation help you determine if your answer is reasonable?
Answer: $31.4\text{ inches}$

Explanation: Circumference formula: $C = \pi \times d$.
$C = 3.14 \times 10 = 31.4\text{ inches}$.
Estimation: Since $\pi$ is approximately 3, the circumference should be roughly $3 \times 10 = 30$. Since $31.4$ is close to $30$, the answer is reasonable.
30. Find the value of $A$ in $A = s^2$ when $s$ is $10\text{ m}$.
Answer: $100\text{ m}^2$

Explanation: Substitute $10$ for $s$ in the formula:
$A = (10\text{ m})^2$
$A = 10\text{ m} \times 10\text{ m} = 100\text{ m}^2$.
