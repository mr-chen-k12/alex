Change each fraction or mixed number to a percent by multiplying by 100%:

j. $\frac{2}{3}$

Answer: $66\frac{2}{3}\%$

Explanation: Multiply the fraction by $100\%$.
$\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{100}{1}\% = \frac{200}{3}\%$
Divide 200 by 3 to get a mixed number: $200 \div 3 = 66$ with a remainder of $2$. Therefore, $66\frac{2}{3}\%$.

k. $\frac{1}{6}$

Answer: $16\frac{2}{3}\%$

Explanation: $\frac{1}{6} \times 100\% = \frac{100}{6}\%$
Reduce the fraction by dividing numerator and denominator by 2: $\frac{50}{3}\%$
$50 \div 3 = 16$ with a remainder of $2$. Therefore, $16\frac{2}{3}\%$.

l. $\frac{1}{8}$

Answer: $12\frac{1}{2}\%$ (or $12.5\%$)

Explanation: $\frac{1}{8} \times 100\% = \frac{100}{8}\%$
Reduce by dividing by 4: $\frac{25}{2}\%$
$25 \div 2 = 12$ with a remainder of $1$. Therefore, $12\frac{1}{2}\%$.

m. $1\frac{1}{4}$

Answer: $125\%$

Explanation: Convert the mixed number to an improper fraction first: $1\frac{1}{4} = \frac{5}{4}$.
$\frac{5}{4} \times \frac{100}{1}\% = \frac{500}{4}\% = 125\%$.

n. $2\frac{4}{5}$

Answer: $280\%$

Explanation: Convert to an improper fraction: $2\frac{4}{5} = \frac{14}{5}$.
$\frac{14}{5} \times \frac{100}{1}\% = \frac{1400}{5}\% = 280\%$.

o. $1\frac{1}{3}$

Answer: $133\frac{1}{3}\%$

Explanation: Convert to an improper fraction: $1\frac{1}{3} = \frac{4}{3}$.
$\frac{4}{3} \times \frac{100}{1}\% = \frac{400}{3}\%$.
$400 \div 3 = 133$ with a remainder of $1$. Therefore, $133\frac{1}{3}\%$.

p. What percent of this rectangle is shaded?

Answer: $37.5\%$ or $37\frac{1}{2}\%$

Explanation: The rectangle is divided into 8 equal squares. There are 3 squares shaded. The fraction shaded is $\frac{3}{8}$.
$\frac{3}{8} \times 100\% = \frac{300}{8}\% = \frac{75}{2}\% = 37.5\%$.

q. CONNECT What percent of a yard is a foot?

Answer: $33\frac{1}{3}\%$

To find the percent: $\frac{1}{3} \times 100\% = \frac{100}{3}\% = 33\frac{1}{3}\%$.

Written Practice (Lesson 94)

1. ANALYZE Ten of the thirty students on the bus were boys. What percent of the students on the bus were boys?

Answer: $33\frac{1}{3}\%$

Explanation: First, write the ratio of boys to total students as a fraction: $\frac{10}{30}$.
Simplify the fraction: $\frac{1}{3}$.
Multiply by $100\%$ to convert to a percent: $\frac{1}{3} \times 100\% = \frac{100}{3}\% = 33\frac{1}{3}\%$.

2. CONNECT On the Celsius scale water freezes at 0°C and boils at 100°C. What temperature is halfway between the freezing and boiling temperatures of water?

Answer: $50^\circ\text{C}$

Explanation: To find the halfway point (average), add the two temperatures and divide by 2:
$(0 + 100) \div 2 = 100 \div 2 = 50$.

3. CONNECT If the length of segment AB is $\frac{1}{3}$ the length of segment AC, and if segment AC is 12 cm long, then how long is segment BC?

Answer: $8\text{ cm}$

Explanation:
Length of AC = $12\text{ cm}$.
Length of AB = $\frac{1}{3}$ of AC = $\frac{1}{3} \times 12 = 4\text{ cm}$.
Since point B is between A and C, segment AC is made of AB + BC. Therefore, BC = AC - AB.
BC = $12 - 4 = 8\text{ cm}$.

4. What percent of this group is shaded?

Answer: $20\%$

Explanation: There is 1 shaded circle out of 5 total circles. The fraction is $\frac{1}{5}$.
$\frac{1}{5} \times 100\% = \frac{100}{5}\% = 20\%$.

*8. $10^4 - 10^3$

Answer: $9,000$

Explanation: Evaluate the exponents first:
$10^4 = 10 \times 10 \times 10 \times 10 = 10,000$
$10^3 = 10 \times 10 \times 10 = 1,000$
$10,000 - 1,000 = 9,000$.

9. How much is $\frac{3}{4}$ of 360?

Answer: $270$

Explanation: Multiply the fraction by the whole number.
$\frac{3}{4} \times 360 = 3 \times (\frac{360}{4}) = 3 \times 90 = 270$.

10. The label wraps around the circumference of the can. How long does the label need to be?

Answer: $25.12\text{ cm}$

Explanation: The formula for the circumference of a circle is $C = \pi \times d$.
$C \approx 3.14 \times 8\text{ cm} = 25.12\text{ cm}$.

*11. ANALYZE How many square centimeters of countertop does the can occupy?

Answer: $50.24\text{ sq cm}$

Explanation: The area occupied is the area of the circular base. The formula for the area of a circle is $A = \pi \times r^2$.
First, find the radius ($r$): $r = \text{diameter} \div 2 = 8 \div 2 = 4\text{ cm}$.
$A \approx 3.14 \times (4)^2 = 3.14 \times 16 = 50.24\text{ cm}^2$.

12. $3\frac{1}{2} + 1\frac{3}{4} + 4\frac{5}{8}$

Answer: $9\frac{7}{8}$

Explanation: Find a common denominator for the fractions, which is 8.
$3\frac{1}{2} = 3\frac{4}{8}$
$1\frac{3}{4} = 1\frac{6}{8}$
$4\frac{5}{8} = 4\frac{5}{8}$
Add the fractions: $\frac{4}{8} + \frac{6}{8} + \frac{5}{8} = \frac{15}{8} = 1\frac{7}{8}$.
Add the whole numbers: $3 + 1 + 4 = 8$.
Combine them: $8 + 1\frac{7}{8} = 9\frac{7}{8}$.

13. $\frac{9}{10} \cdot \frac{5}{6} \cdot \frac{8}{9}$

Answer: $\frac{2}{3}$

Explanation: Cross-cancel before multiplying to make it easier.
The 9 in the first numerator cancels with the 9 in the third denominator.
The 5 in the second numerator and the 10 in the first denominator divide by 5, leaving 1 and 2.
The 8 in the third numerator and 6 in the second denominator divide by 2, leaving 4 and 3.
So you have: $\frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{4}{1} = \frac{4}{6}$.
Simplify $\frac{4}{6}$ by dividing numerator and denominator by 2 to get $\frac{2}{3}$.

17. $\frac{3}{7} = \frac{21}{x}$

Answer: $x = 49$

Explanation: This is a proportion. Look at the numerators: $3 \times 7 = 21$.
To keep the fractions equivalent, multiply the denominator by the same number: $7 \times 7 = 49$.
Therefore, $x = 49$. (Alternatively, cross-multiply: $3x = 147$, so $x = 49$.)

18. $33\frac{1}{3} \div 100$

Answer: $\frac{1}{3}$

Explanation: Convert the mixed number to an improper fraction: $33\frac{1}{3} = \frac{100}{3}$.
Dividing by 100 is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal, $\frac{1}{100}$.
$\frac{100}{3} \times \frac{1}{100} = \frac{100}{300} = \frac{1}{3}$.

19. If ninety percent of the answers were correct, then what percent were incorrect?

Answer: $10\%$

Explanation: The total possible percent is $100\%$.
$100\% \text{ (Total)} - 90\% \text{ (Correct)} = 10\% \text{ (Incorrect)}$.

20. Write the decimal number one hundred twenty and three hundredths.

Answer: $120.03$

Explanation: "One hundred twenty" is $120$. The word "and" indicates the decimal point. "Three hundredths" means the 3 belongs in the second decimal place (the hundredths place), with a 0 in the tenths place. Therefore, $120.03$.
