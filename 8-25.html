Practice Problems — 8-25

Practice Problems

1. Evaluate: $1.5 \times (3.2 + 4.8) - 2.6$
Inside parentheses: $3.2 + 4.8 = 8.0$
Multiply: $1.5 \times 8.0 = 12.0$
Subtract: $12.0 - 2.6 = 9.4$
Answer: 9.4
2. A submarine is at an elevation of -450 ft. It ascends 180 ft and then descends 120 ft. What is its new elevation?
$-450 + 180 = -270$
$-270 - 120 = -390$
Answer: -390 ft
3. Convert the improper fraction $\frac{58}{7}$ into a mixed number.
$58 \div 7 = 8$ with a remainder of $2$
Answer: $8\frac{2}{7}$
4. Evaluate: $\frac{7}{10} + \frac{4}{15}$
Common denominator is 30:
$\frac{21}{30} + \frac{8}{30} = \frac{29}{30}$
Answer: $\frac{29}{30}$
5. Compare using $<$, $>$, or $=$: $\frac{5}{12} \ \bigcirc \ \frac{3}{8}$
Common denominator is 24:
$\frac{5}{12} = \frac{10}{24}$ and $\frac{3}{8} = \frac{9}{24}$
$\frac{10}{24} > \frac{9}{24}$
Answer: >
6. Solve for $m$: $m + 4\frac{3}{8} = 9\frac{1}{4}$
$m = 9\frac{2}{8} - 4\frac{3}{8}$
$m = 8\frac{10}{8} - 4\frac{3}{8} = 4\frac{7}{8}$
Answer: $4\frac{7}{8}$
7. Evaluate: $6\frac{2}{3} \div 1\frac{1}{9}$
Convert to improper fractions: $\frac{20}{3} \div \frac{10}{9}$
Multiply by reciprocal: $\frac{20}{3} \times \frac{9}{10} = \frac{2 \times 3}{1 \times 1} = 6$
Answer: 6
8. Find the area of a trapezoid with bases $a = 8\text{ cm}$, $b = 14\text{ cm}$, and height $h = 6\text{ cm}$.
$\text{Area} = \frac{a + b}{2} \times h$
$\text{Area} = \frac{8 + 14}{2} \times 6 = 11 \times 6 = 66\text{ cm}^2$
Answer: $66\text{ cm}^2$
9. Solve for $x$: $4x - 17 = 31$
$4x = 31 + 17$
$4x = 48$
$x = 12$
Answer: 12
10. A car travels 210 miles on 7 gallons of gas. How many miles can it travel on 12 gallons at the same rate?
Unit rate: $210 \div 7 = 30\text{ miles/gallon}$
Total distance: $30 \times 12 = 360\text{ miles}$
Answer: 360 miles
11. Convert $0.375$ to a fraction in lowest terms.
$0.375 = \frac{375}{1000} = \frac{3}{8}$
Answer: $\frac{3}{8}$
12. Evaluate: $2^6 - 3^3 + \sqrt{225}$
$2^6 = 64$, $3^3 = 27$, $\sqrt{225} = 15$
$64 - 27 + 15 = 37 + 15 = 52$
Answer: 52
13. What is 42% of 350?
$0.42 \times 350 = 147$
Answer: 147
14. Find the median of the data set: 18, 24, 12, 31, 27, 15, 20.
Order the numbers: 12, 15, 18, 20, 24, 27, 31
Middle number (4th value): 20
Answer: 20
15. Evaluate: $7.2 \div 0.08$
$\frac{7.2}{0.08} = \frac{720}{8} = 90$
Answer: 90
16. An item priced at $180 is on sale for 15% off. Calculate the final price after adding an 8% sales tax on the discounted price.
Discount: $0.15 \times 180 = $27$
Sale price: $180 - 27 = $153$
Tax: $153 \times 0.08 = $12.24$
Total: $153 + 12.24 = $165.24$
Answer: $165.24
17. Find the area of a circle with diameter $10\text{ cm}$ (use $\pi \approx 3.14$).
Radius $r = 10 \div 2 = 5\text{ cm}$
$\text{Area} = \pi r^2 = 3.14 \times 5^2 = 3.14 \times 25 = 78.5\text{ cm}^2$
Answer: $78.5\text{ cm}^2$
18. Solve for $n$: $2(n + 5) = 3n - 4$
$2n + 10 = 3n - 4$
$10 + 4 = 3n - 2n$
$n = 14$
Answer: 14
19. Find the greatest common factor (GCF) of 48 and 72.
Factors of 48: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24, 48
Factors of 72: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 12, 18, 24, 36, 72
Greatest common factor = 24
Answer: 24
20. A cylinder has a radius of $4\text{ cm}$ and a height of $10\text{ cm}$. Find its volume (use $\pi \approx 3.14$).
$\text{Volume} = \pi r^2 h = 3.14 \times 4^2 \times 10$
$\text{Volume} = 3.14 \times 16 \times 10 = 502.4\text{ cm}^3$
Answer: $502.4\text{ cm}^3$
21. Evaluate: $(-8) \times (-5) - (-18) \div 3$
$(-8) \times (-5) = 40$
$(-18) \div 3 = -6$
$40 - (-6) = 40 + 6 = 46$
Answer: 46
22. What is the simple interest earned on $2,000 at a 5% annual interest rate over 3 years?
$I = P \times r \times t$
$I = 2000 \times 0.05 \times 3 = 100 \times 3 = $300$
Answer: $300
23. Solve for $y$: $\frac{3y - 5}{4} = 7$
$3y - 5 = 28$
$3y = 33$
$y = 11$
Answer: 11
24. A recipe calls for $2\frac{1}{4}$ cups of flour to make 12 muffins. How many cups of flour are needed to make 28 muffins?
Ratio: $\frac{2.25}{12} = \frac{x}{28}$
$x = \frac{2.25 \times 28}{12} = \frac{63}{12} = 5.25 = 5\frac{1}{4}\text{ cups}$
Answer: $5\frac{1}{4}$ cups
25. If the length of a rectangle is doubled and its width is tripled, by what factor does its area increase?
Original Area $= l \times w$
New Area $= (2l) \times (3w) = 6(lw)$
Answer: 6 times
26. Evaluate: $|-14| + |-9| - | -5 |$
$14 + 9 - 5 = 23 - 5 = 18$
Answer: 18
27. Simplify the ratio: $45\text{ minutes}$ to $2.5\text{ hours}$.
$2.5\text{ hours} = 2.5 \times 60 = 150\text{ minutes}$
Ratio: $\frac{45}{150} = \frac{3}{10}$ or $3:10$
Answer: 3 : 10
28. Solve the inequality: $-3x + 8 \le 20$
$-3x \le 12$
Divide by -3 and flip the inequality sign:
$x \ge -4$
Answer: $x \ge -4$
29. In a right triangle, the legs measure $9\text{ cm}$ and $12\text{ cm}$. What is the length of the hypotenuse?
$a^2 + b^2 = c^2$
$9^2 + 12^2 = 81 + 144 = 225$
$c = \sqrt{225} = 15\text{ cm}$
Answer: 15 cm
30. Find the sum of the interior angles of a hexagon (6-sided polygon).
$\text{Sum} = (n - 2) \times 180^\circ$
$\text{Sum} = (6 - 2) \times 180^\circ = 4 \times 180^\circ = 720^\circ$
Answer: 720°
