Concept 1: Calculating Averages (Based on Q3)

1. If the sum of four numbers is 32.8, what is the average of the four numbers?
Explanation: To find the average, divide the total sum by the count of numbers.
Average = Sum ÷ Count
32.8 ÷ 4 = 8.2
2. If the sum of six numbers is 45.6, what is the average of the six numbers?
Explanation: Divide the total sum by the count of numbers.
45.6 ÷ 6 = 7.6
3. If the sum of eight numbers is 100.8, what is the average of the eight numbers?
Explanation: Divide the total sum by the count of numbers.
100.8 ÷ 8 = 12.6

Concept 2: Algebraic Addition (Based on Q5)

4. Solve using algebraic addition: $-8 - -3$
Explanation: Change the subtraction to adding the opposite.
$-8 - (-3)$ becomes $-8 + (+3)$.
Since the signs are different, subtract their absolute values (8 - 3 = 5) and keep the sign of the larger absolute value (negative). The answer is -5.
