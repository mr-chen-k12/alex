Lesson Practice

Find each sum mentally:

a. $-3 + +4$

Answer: $1$
Explanation: The signs are different, so subtract their absolute values ($4 - 3 = 1$). The positive number has a larger absolute value, so the answer is positive.

b. $-3 + -4$

Answer: $-7$
Explanation: The signs are the same, so add their absolute values ($3 + 4 = 7$) and keep the negative sign.

c. $-3 + +3$

Answer: $0$
Explanation: These are additive inverses. Adding a number to its opposite always results in zero.

d. $+4 + -3$

Answer: $1$
Explanation: Subtract the absolute values ($4 - 3 = 1$). Keep the sign of the larger absolute value (positive).

e. $(+3) + (-4)$

Answer: $-1$
Explanation: Different signs; subtract absolute values ($4 - 3 = 1$). The negative number has the larger absolute value.

f. $(+10) + (-5)$

Answer: $5$
Explanation: Different signs; subtract absolute values ($10 - 5 = 5$). Keep the positive sign.

g. $(-10) + (-5)$

Answer: $-15$
Explanation: Same signs; add absolute values ($10 + 5 = 15$) and keep the negative sign.

h. $(-10) + (+5)$

Answer: $-5$
Explanation: Different signs; subtract absolute values ($10 - 5 = 5$). The negative number has the larger absolute value.

Written Practice

1. If $0.6$ is the divisor and $1.2$ is the quotient, what is the dividend?

Answer: $0.72$
Explanation: $\text{Dividend} = \text{Divisor} \times \text{Quotient}$. Therefore, $0.6 \times 1.2 = 0.72$.

2. If a number is twelve less than fifty, then it is how much more than twenty?

Answer: $18$
Explanation: First find the number: $50 - 12 = 38$. Then find how much more it is than twenty: $38 - 20 = 18$.

3. If the sum of four numbers is $14.8$, what is the average of the four numbers?

Answer: $3.7$
Explanation: $\text{Average} = \text{Sum} \div \text{Count}$. Therefore, $14.8 \div 4 = 3.7$.

4. Illustrate this problem on a number line: $-3 + +5$

Answer: $2$
Explanation: Start at $0$. Move $3$ units to the left to reach $-3$. From $-3$, move $5$ units to the right. You land on positive $2$.

7. What is the measure of each angle of an equilateral triangle?

Answer: $60^\circ$
Explanation: A triangle's interior angles sum to $180^\circ$. Since all three angles are equal in an equilateral triangle, $180^\circ \div 3 = 60^\circ$.

8. Quadrilateral $ABCD$ is a parallelogram. If angle $A$ measures $70^\circ$, what are the measures of angles $B, C,$ and $D$?

Answer: Angle $B = 110^\circ$, Angle $C = 70^\circ$, Angle $D = 110^\circ$
Explanation: Opposite angles are equal ($A = C$). Consecutive angles are supplementary (add up to $180^\circ$), so $180^\circ - 70^\circ = 110^\circ$ for angles $B$ and $D$.

9. Spinner Probability
a. If the spinner is spun once, what is the probability that it will stop in a sector with a number 2?
b. If the spinner is spun 30 times, about how many times would it be expected to stop in the sector with the number 3?

Answer:
a. $\frac{3}{8}$. Explanation: Based on the spinner's geometry, sector 1 is $\frac{1}{2}$. The top half is divided into a $\frac{1}{4}$ sector containing a '2' and a $\frac{1}{4}$ sector divided in half again (two $\frac{1}{8}$ sectors) containing '3' and '2'. Total probability for 2 is $\frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{8} = \frac{3}{8}$.
b. Expected $4$ times. Explanation: The probability of 3 is $\frac{1}{8}$. Expected frequency $= 30 \times \frac{1}{8} = 3.75$, which rounds to approximately $4$ times.

10. Find the volume of the rectangular prism at right (Dimensions: 7 in by 5 in by 6 in).

Answer: $210 \text{ in}^3$
Explanation: $\text{Volume} = \text{length} \times \text{width} \times \text{height} = 7 \times 5 \times 6 = 210$.

11. Twelve of the 27 students in the class are boys. What is the ratio of girls to boys in the class?

Answer: $\frac{5}{4}$ or $5:4$
Explanation: First find the number of girls: $27 - 12 = 15$. The ratio of girls to boys is $15 \text{ to } 12$, which simplifies to $5:4$ when divided by 3.

$100 + (25 - 11) \div 7 - 27$

13. The fraction $\frac{2}{3}$ is equal to what percent?

Answer: $66 \frac{2}{3}\%$
Explanation: Multiply by $100\%$. $\frac{2}{3} \times 100 = \frac{200}{3} = 66.\overline{6}\%$.

14. If 20% of the students brought their lunch to school, then what fraction of the students did not bring their lunch to school?

Answer: $\frac{4}{5}$
Explanation: If $20\%$ brought lunch, $80\%$ did not ($100\% - 20\%$). Convert $80\%$ to a fraction: $\frac{80}{100}$, which reduces to $\frac{4}{5}$.

15. Simplify: $\frac{4^2}{2^4}$

Answer: $1$
Explanation: Calculate exponents first. $4^2 = 16$ and $2^4 = 16$. $\frac{16}{16} = 1$.

16. Add: $5 \frac{7}{8} + 4 \frac{3}{8}$

Answer: $10 \frac{1}{4}$
Explanation: Add whole numbers and fractions: $9 \frac{10}{8}$. Convert improper fraction: $\frac{10}{8} = 1 \frac{2}{8}$. Total is $10 \frac{2}{8}$, which simplifies to $10 \frac{1}{4}$.

17. Divide: $1 \frac{1}{2} \div 2 \frac{1}{2}$

Answer: $\frac{3}{5}$
Explanation: Convert to improper fractions: $\frac{3}{2} \div \frac{5}{2}$. Multiply by the reciprocal: $\frac{3}{2} \times \frac{2}{5} = \frac{6}{10}$, which reduces to $\frac{3}{5}$.

18. Subtract: $5 - (3.2 + 0.4)$

Answer: $1.4$
Explanation: Do parentheses first: $3.2 + 0.4 = 3.6$. Then subtract: $5.0 - 3.6 = 1.4$.

19. If the diameter of a circular plastic swimming pool is 6 feet, then the area of the bottom of the pool is about how many square feet? Round to the nearest square foot. (Use 3.14 for $\pi$)

Answer: $28 \text{ sq ft}$
Explanation: Radius is half the diameter ($r = 3$). $\text{Area} = \pi r^2 = 3.14 \times 3^2 = 3.14 \times 9 = 28.26$. Rounded to the nearest whole number is $28$.

20. Explain: We use squares to measure the area of a rectangle. Why do we use cubes instead of squares to measure the volume of a rectangular prism?

Answer: Squares are two-dimensional and measure flat surfaces (Area). Prisms are three-dimensional, taking up space, so they must be measured using three-dimensional units like cubes (Volume).

21. Solve this proportion: $\frac{9}{12} = \frac{15}{x}$

Answer: $20$
Explanation: Cross-multiply: $9x = 12 \times 15 \Rightarrow 9x = 180 \Rightarrow x = 20$. Alternatively, simplify $\frac{9}{12}$ to $\frac{3}{4}$, then scale up by $5$ to get $\frac{15}{20}$.

Rectangle ABCD is 8 cm long and 6 cm wide. Segment AC is 10 cm long. Use this information to answer problems 22 and 23.

22. What is the area of triangle ABC?

Answer: $24 \text{ cm}^2$
Explanation: The area of the full rectangle is $8 \times 6 = 48$. The diagonal cuts it in half, so the triangle is $\frac{1}{2}$ the area. Or use triangle area formula: $\frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = \frac{1}{2} \times 8 \times 6 = 24$.

23. What is the perimeter of triangle ABC?

Answer: $24 \text{ cm}$
Explanation: Add the lengths of the three sides of the triangle: $8 \text{ (base)} + 6 \text{ (height)} + 10 \text{ (hypotenuse AC)} = 24$.

24. Measure the diameter of a nickel to the nearest millimeter.

Answer: $\approx 21 \text{ mm}$
Explanation: A standard U.S. nickel has an official diameter of $21.21 \text{ mm}$, which rounds to $21 \text{ mm}$ for a practical physical measurement.

25. Estimate: Calculate the circumference of a nickel. Round to the nearest millimeter. (Use 3.14 for $\pi$)

Answer: $66 \text{ mm}$
Explanation: $\text{Circumference} = \pi \times \text{diameter} = 3.14 \times 21 = 65.94$. Rounded to the nearest whole millimeter is $66 \text{ mm}$.

26. A bag contains 12 marbles. Eight of the marbles are red and 4 are blue. If you draw a marble from the bag without looking, what is the probability that the marble will be blue? Express the probability ratio as a fraction and as a decimal rounded to the nearest hundredth.

Answer: Fraction: $\frac{1}{3}$, Decimal: $0.33$
Explanation: $\frac{4 \text{ blue}}{12 \text{ total}} = \frac{1}{3}$. As a decimal, $1 \div 3 = 0.333...$, rounded to $0.33$.

30. A full one-gallon container of milk was used to fill two one-pint containers. How many quarts of milk were left in the one-gallon container?

Answer: $3 \text{ quarts}$
Explanation: $1 \text{ gallon} = 4 \text{ quarts} = 8 \text{ pints}$. Two $1\text{-pint}$ containers equals $2 \text{ pints}$, which is $1 \text{ quart}$. Therefore, $4 \text{ quarts} - 1 \text{ quart} \text{ used} = 3 \text{ quarts} \text{ remaining}$.
