Lesson Practice

a. ESTIMATE: Mrs. Smith deposits $3000 in an account that grows 5% annually. How much money will be in the account 1. after the fifth year and 2. after the tenth year? Round answers to the nearest cent.
Answers:
1. $3,828.84
2. $4,886.68
Explanation:
Mrs. Smith started with $3000. The formula for compound interest is \( A = P(1 + r)^t \), where \( P = 3000 \), \( r = 0.05 \), and \( t \) is the number of years.
1. After 5 years: \( A = 3000 \times (1.05)^5 \approx 3000 \times 1.27628 = \$3,828.84 \)
2. After 10 years: \( A = 3000 \times (1.05)^{10} \approx 3000 \times 1.62889 = \$4,886.68 \)
b. ESTIMATE: Mr. Johnson deposited $3000 in a different account that pays 8% interest per year. If he does not withdraw any money, how much will be in the account 1. after five years, and 2. after 10 years? (Round answers to the nearest cent.)
Answers:
1. $4,407.98
2. $6,476.77
Explanation:
Use the formula \( A = 3000(1.08)^t \):
1. After 5 years: \( 3000 \times (1.08)^5 \approx 3000 \times 1.46933 = \$4,407.98 \)
2. After 10 years: \( 3000 \times (1.08)^{10} \approx 3000 \times 2.15892 = \$6,476.77 \)
c. How much more money will be in Mr. Johnson's account than in Mrs. Smith's account 1. after five years, and 2. after 10 years?
Answers:
1. $579.14
2. $1,590.09
Explanation:
Subtract Mrs. Smith's balance from Mr. Johnson's balance for each corresponding year:
1. Year 5: \( \$4,407.98 - \$3,828.84 = \$579.14 \)
2. Year 10: \( \$6,476.77 - \$4,886.68 = \$1,590.09 \)

Written Practice

1. Sarah drew a right triangle with sides 5 inches, 12 inches, and 13 inches long. What was the area of the triangle?
Answer: 30 square inches
Explanation:
In a right triangle with sides 5, 12, and 13, the hypotenuse is the longest side (13). The base and height are the two shorter legs (5 and 12).
Area = \( \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} \)
Area = \( \frac{1}{2} \times 5 \times 12 = 30 \)
2. If 4 yards of fabric costs $6.00, then 12 yards of fabric would cost how much?
Answer: $18.00
Explanation:
12 yards is exactly three times the length of 4 yards (12 ÷ 4 = 3). Therefore, the cost will also be tripled.
\( \$6.00 \times 3 = \$18.00 \)
3. a. Eight is what fraction of 40?
b. Eight is what percent of 40?
Answers:
a. \( \frac{1}{5} \)
b. 20%
Explanation:
a. The fraction is \( \frac{8}{40} \). Dividing the numerator and denominator by 8 reduces it to \( \frac{1}{5} \).
b. To convert \( \frac{1}{5} \) to a percent, multiply by 100%: \( \frac{1}{5} \times 100\% = 20\% \).
4. A multiple-choice question has five choices. Maya knows that one of the choices must be correct, but she has no idea which one. If Maya simply guesses, what is the chance that she will guess the correct answer?
Answer: \( \frac{1}{5} \) or 20%
Explanation:
There is 1 correct choice out of 5 total choices. The probability is \( \frac{1}{5} \), which is equivalent to 20%.
5. If 3/8 of the 40 students in the class ride the bus to school, what is the ratio of students who ride the bus to students who use other transportation?
Answer: 3:5
Explanation:
If \( \frac{3}{8} \) ride the bus, then the remaining \( \frac{5}{8} \) use other transportation.
The ratio of bus riders to others is 3 parts to 5 parts, which is written as 3:5 or \( \frac{3}{5} \).

\( d = 65 \times 3 = 195 \).

11. Solve and check: \( 7x + 12 = 54 \)
Answer: \( x = 6 \)
Explanation:
Subtract 12 from both sides:
\( 7x = 54 - 12 \)
\( 7x = 42 \)
Divide by 7:
\( x = 6 \)
Check: \( 7(6) + 12 = 42 + 12 = 54 \). Correct.
12. Solve and check: \( \frac{x}{5} = \frac{2.4}{1.5} \)
Answer: \( x = 8 \)
Explanation:
Simplify the fraction on the right by multiplying top and bottom by 10: \( \frac{24}{15} \).
Reduce \( \frac{24}{15} \) by dividing by 3: \( \frac{8}{5} \).
So, \( \frac{x}{5} = \frac{8}{5} \).
Since the denominators are equal, the numerators must be equal. Therefore, \( x = 8 \).

16. Use division by primes to find the prime factors of 540. Then write the prime factorization of 540 using exponents.
Answer: \( 2^2 \times 3^3 \times 5 \)
Explanation:
Divide repeatedly by prime numbers:
\( 540 \div 2 = 270 \)
\( 270 \div 2 = 135 \)
\( 135 \div 3 = 45 \)
\( 45 \div 3 = 15 \)
\( 15 \div 3 = 5 \)
\( 5 \div 5 = 1 \)
The prime factors are 2, 2, 3, 3, 3, 5. Written with exponents: \( 2^2 \cdot 3^3 \cdot 5 \).
17. If a 24-ounce box of cereal costs $2.88, what is the cost per ounce?

18. Find the area of a trapezoid that can be split into a rectangle and a right triangle. The rectangle is 5m by 8m. The right triangle attached has a height of 3m and shares the 8m base.
Answer: 52 square meters
Explanation:
Area of the rectangle part = \( 5 \times 8 = 40 \) m\(^2\).
Area of the triangle part = \( \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = \frac{1}{2} \times 8 \times 3 = 12 \) m\(^2\).
Total area = \( 40 + 12 = 52 \) m\(^2\).
19. The radius of a circle is 5 cm. Use 3.14 for \( \pi \) to calculate the:
a. circumference of the circle.
b. area of the circle.
Answers:
a. 31.4 cm
b. 78.5 square cm
Explanation:
a. Circumference \( C = 2\pi r = 2 \times 3.14 \times 5 = 31.4 \) cm.
b. Area \( A = \pi r^2 = 3.14 \times (5)^2 = 3.14 \times 25 = 78.5 \) cm\(^2\).
20. The volume of a pyramid is 1/3 the volume of a cube with the same base and height. What is the volume of a pyramid if it fits exactly inside a cube with side lengths of 6 cm?
Answer: 72 cubic centimeters
Explanation:
First, find the volume of the cube: \( V = s^3 = 6 \times 6 \times 6 = 216 \) cm\(^3\).
The pyramid's volume is \( \frac{1}{3} \) of the cube: \( \frac{1}{3} \times 216 = 72 \) cm\(^3\).

23. 255 students were assigned to eight rooms so that there were 31 or 32 students in each room. How many rooms had exactly 31 students?
Answer: 1 room
Explanation:
If all 8 rooms had 31 students, there would be \( 8 \times 31 = 248 \) students.
We have 255 students, which is 7 extra students (\( 255 - 248 = 7 \)).
To place these 7 extra students, we put 1 extra student in 7 of the rooms (making them rooms of 32).
Therefore, 7 rooms have 32 students, and the remaining 1 room has 31 students.

25. ESTIMATE: If Anthony spins a spinner 80 times, and a specific sector makes up exactly 1/4 of the spinner's area, about how many times should he expect the arrow to stop in that sector?

26. An equilateral triangle and a square share a common side. If the area of the square is 64 mm², then what is the perimeter of the equilateral triangle?
Answer: 24 mm
Explanation:
The area of a square is \( s^2 \). If area is 64, then the side length \( s = \sqrt{64} = 8 \) mm.
Since the equilateral triangle shares a side, its sides are also 8 mm each.
The perimeter of an equilateral triangle is \( 3 \times \text{side} = 3 \times 8 = 24 \) mm.
27. Write \( 33 \frac{1}{3}\% \) as a reduced fraction.

Explanation:
First, convert the mixed number to an improper fraction: \( 33 \frac{1}{3} = \frac{100}{3} \).
Because it is a percent, divide by 100 (multiply by \( \frac{1}{100} \)):
\( \frac{100}{3} \times \frac{1}{100} = \frac{1}{3} \).
28. The weights of five dogs at a clinic are listed below. Find the mean, median, and range of these measures: 160 lb, 175 lb, 180 lb, 190 lb, 170 lb
Answers:

Median: 175 lb

Real-World Connection

Marcus plays basketball on his college team. This season he scored 450 of his team's 1500 points.
a. What percent of the points were scored by Marcus's teammates?
b. Marcus's team played 15 games. How many points did Marcus average per game?
Answers:
a. 70%
b. 30 points per game
Explanation:
a. Points scored by teammates = \( 1500 - 450 = 1050 \). To find the percentage, divide by total points and multiply by 100: \( (1050 \div 1500) \times 100 = 0.70 \times 100 = 70\% \).
b. Divide Marcus's total points by the number of games: \( 450 \div 15 = 30 \) points/game.
