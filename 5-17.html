2. If 145 children are separated as equally as possible into four groups, how many will be in each group? (Write four numbers, one for each of the four groups.)
Answer: 37, 36, 36, 36

Explanation: Divide 145 by 4: 145÷4=36 with a remainder of 1.
This means the base group size is 36. Since there is a remainder of 1, one of the groups will have one extra child (36 + 1 = 37). Therefore, the groups will be 37, 36, 36, and 36.
3. If the parking lot charges $1.50 per half hour, what is the cost of parking a car from 10:30 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.?
Answer: $13.50

Explanation: From 10:30 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. is 4.5 hours.
Since there are two half-hours in every hour, 4.5 hours equals 9 half-hours.
9×$1.50=$13.50.
4. If the area of the square is 144 m², then what is the area of the circle? (Use 3.14 for π.)
Answer: 113.04 m²

Explanation: The area of the square is s2=144, so the side length s is 12 m.
The circle fits perfectly inside the square, so its diameter is 12 m, which means its radius r is 6 m.
Area of the circle = πr2=3.14×(6)2=3.14×36=113.04 m2.

6. The coordinates of the vertices of a triangle are (4, 8), (6, 0), and (0, 0). What is the area of the triangle?
Answer: 24 square units

Explanation: The base of the triangle on the x-axis goes from (0,0) to (6,0), so the base b=6.
The height is the y-coordinate of the peak vertex (4,8), so the height h=8.
Area = 12bh=12(6)(8)=24.

19. What is the area of this irregular polygon?
Answer: 122 sq in.

Explanation: Split the composite figure into simpler shapes by dropping a vertical line from the end of the top 8 in. edge.
This forms a rectangle on the left with width 8 in. and height 10 in. Area = 8×10=80 sq in.
The remaining shape on the right is a trapezoid. Its width at the bottom is 14−8=6 in. Its vertical parallel sides are 10 in. (from the rectangle's shared side) and 4 in. (the right edge).
Area of trapezoid = 12(b1+b2)h=12(10+4)×6=12(14)×6=42 sq in.
Total area = 80+42=122 sq in.

Answer:

Explanation:
