Math Practice Solutions - Set 2

Ratio & Proportion Practice

1. Dogs and cats at a shelter are in a ratio of 4 to 3. If there are a total of 56 dogs and cats, how many are cats?

Answer: 24 cats

Explanation: The ratio is \(4:3\), so there are \(4 + 3 = 7\) total parts. Divide the total number by 7 to find the multiplier: \(56 \div 7 = 8\).
Multiply the cat ratio by 8: \(3 \times 8 = 24\).

2. Sand and gravel were mixed by weight in a ratio of 1 to 4. If 75 pounds of mix were prepared, how many pounds of sand were used?

Answer: 15 pounds

Explanation: The ratio is \(1:4\), making a total of 5 parts (\(1 + 4 = 5\)). The multiplier is \(75 \div 5 = 15\) pounds per part.
Since sand makes up 1 part, \(1 \times 15 = 15\) pounds.

3. Using 30 blue and 30 green color tiles, create a ratio of 5 to 4. How many of each color do you have?

Answer: 25 blue and 20 green

Explanation: We need a \(5:4\) ratio using the most tiles without going over 30 of either. Let the multiplier be \(x\). We need \(5x \le 30\) and \(4x \le 30\). The largest integer \(x\) for \(5x \le 30\) is 6.
Using \(x = 6\): Blue = \(5 \times 6 = 25\), Green = \(4 \times 6 = 20\).

4. The ratio of apples to oranges in a basket is 5 to 2. If there are 35 pieces of fruit in total, how many oranges are there?

Answer: 10 oranges

Explanation: Total parts = \(5 + 2 = 7\). Multiplier = \(35 \div 7 = 5\). Number of oranges = \(2 \times 5 = 10\).

Geometry & Measurements

5. A standard soup can represents what geometric solid?

Answer: Cylinder

Explanation: A soup can has two parallel circular bases connected by a curved surface, which defines a cylinder.

6. If the average of five numbers is 20, what is the sum of the five numbers?

Answer: 100

Explanation: \(\text{Sum} = \text{Average} \times \text{Count}\).
\(\text{Sum} = 20 \times 5 = 100\).

7. If the diameter of a circle is \(2\frac{1}{4}\) inches, what is the radius of the circle?

Answer: \(1\frac{1}{8}\) inches

Explanation: Radius is half the diameter. \(2\frac{1}{4} = \frac{9}{4}\).
Half of \(\frac{9}{4}\) is \(\frac{9}{4} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{9}{8} = 1\frac{1}{8}\) inches.

8. What is the cost of 3.4 pounds of apples priced at $1.50 per pound?

Answer: $5.10

Explanation: Multiply the weight by the unit price: \(3.4 \times 1.50 = 5.10\).

9. Suppose \(\overline{PR}\) is 15 cm long. If \(\overline{PQ}\) is \(\frac{1}{3}\) the length of \(\overline{PR}\), then how long is \(\overline{QR}\)?

Answer: 10 cm

Explanation: Length of \(\overline{PQ}\) is \(\frac{1}{3} \times 15 = 5\) cm.
Since \(Q\) is between \(P\) and \(R\), \(\overline{PQ} + \overline{QR} = \overline{PR}\).
\(5 + \overline{QR} = 15 \implies \overline{QR} = 15 - 5 = 10\) cm.

11. Solve each subtraction problem using algebraic addition:
a. \(-5 - -2\)      b. \(+8 - -8\)
c. \(-4 - +7\)      d. \(-9 - -9\)

Answer:
a. \(-3\)
b. \(16\)

Fractions, Decimals, & Percents

12. Three coins are tossed. What is the probability that all three coins will land tails up?

Answer: \(\frac{1}{8}\) (or 12.5%)

Explanation: Each coin has 2 outcomes. For 3 coins, there are \(2 \times 2 \times 2 = 8\) possible outcomes. Only one outcome is TTT. Probability = \(\frac{1}{8}\).

Algebra & Equations

19. \(y + 3\frac{1}{4} = 7\)

Answer: \(y = 3\frac{3}{4}\)

Explanation: Subtract \(3\frac{1}{4}\) from 7.
\(7 - 3\frac{1}{4} = 6\frac{4}{4} - 3\frac{1}{4} = 3\frac{3}{4}\).

20. \(\frac{7}{4} = \frac{35}{x}\)

Answer: \(x = 20\)

Explanation: The numerator is multiplied by 5 (\(7 \times 5 = 35\)). Do the same to the denominator: \(4 \times 5 = 20\).

21. \(0.08m = \$0.24\)

Answer: \(m = 3\)

Explanation: Divide both sides by 0.08: \(m = \frac{0.24}{0.08} = 3\).

22. \(5p = 15 \cdot 3\)

Answer: \(p = 9\)

Explanation: Multiply the right side: \(15 \times 3 = 45\).
Solve: \(5p = 45 \implies p = 45 \div 5 = 9\).

Word Problems & Advanced Applications

23. A storage room is 15 feet long, 12 feet wide, and 10 feet high.
a. How many 1-by-1-by-1-foot boxes can fit on the floor (bottom layer)?
b. Altogether, how many boxes can fit in the room if stacked to the ceiling?

Answer:
a. 180 boxes
b. 1,800 boxes

Explanation:
a. Floor area = \(15 \times 12 = 180\) square feet. So, 180 boxes fit on the floor.
b. Volume = \(15 \times 12 \times 10 = 1,800\) cubic feet, meaning 1,800 boxes total.

24. ESTIMATE. If a steering wheel has a diameter of \(3\frac{1}{8}\) feet, then wrapping tape around the outside edge once requires about how many feet of tape? Choose the closest answer.
A. 6 ft    B. 10 ft    C. 15 ft    D. 20 ft

Answer: B

Explanation: The outside edge is the circumference. \(C = \pi \times d\).
\(\pi \approx 3\), and \(d \approx 3\).
\(3 \times 3 = 9\). The closest estimate is 10 ft.

25. \(8^2 - \sqrt{16} \times 5 - 3^3\)

Answer: 17

Multiplication: \(64 - 20 - 27\).
Subtraction: \(44 - 27 = 17\).

26. A hexagon can be divided into four triangles. What is the sum of the interior angles of a hexagon?

Answer: \(720^\circ\)

Explanation: Each triangle has an angle sum of \(180^\circ\). Since there are 4 triangles: \(4 \times 180^\circ = 720^\circ\).

27. At midnight the temperature was -5°C. By noon the temperature was 12°C. The temperature had risen how many degrees?

Answer: 17 degrees

Explanation: Difference = \(12^\circ - (-5^\circ) = 12 + 5 = 17^\circ\).

28. To what decimal number is the arrow pointing on the number line below?

Answer: 4.6

Explanation: Between 4 and 5 there are 10 spaces. Each tick mark is 0.1. The arrow is at the 6th mark after 4.

29. What is the probability of rolling an even number with one roll of a standard 6-sided die?

Answer: \(\frac{1}{2}\) (or 50%)

Explanation: A 6-sided die has three even numbers (2, 4, 6) out of six possible outcomes. \(\frac{3}{6}\) simplifies to \(\frac{1}{2}\).

30. What is the area of a right triangle with vertices located at (0, 0), (6, 0), and (0, 4) on a coordinate plane?

Answer: 12 square units

Explanation: The base lies on the x-axis from 0 to 6 (length = 6). The height lies on the y-axis from 0 to 4 (length = 4).
Area = \(\frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = \frac{1}{2} \times 6 \times 4 = 12\).
