Lesson Practice

Simplify the following:
a. $-3 + -2 - -6 + -1$
b. $-5 + (+4) - (+7) - (-8)$
c. $+2 + -5 - +3 + +8 - -2$
d. $4 + (-2) - (-6) - (+5) + (+3)$
e. $5 - -3 + -2 - +4 + +6$
f. $(-15) - (+25) - (-35) + (-45)$

Explanations: Convert subtractions to additions of the opposite, then combine like signs from left to right.

  • a. $-3 - 2 + 6 - 1 = -5 + 6 - 1 = 1 - 1 = $ $0$
  • b. $-5 + 4 - 7 + 8 = -1 - 7 + 8 = -8 + 8 = $ $0$
  • c. $2 - 5 - 3 + 8 - 2 = -3 - 3 + 8 - 2 = -6 + 8 - 2 = 2 - 2 = $ $0$
  • d. $4 - 2 + 6 - 5 + 3 = 2 + 6 - 5 + 3 = 8 - 5 + 3 = 3 + 3 = $ $6$
  • e. $5 + 3 - 2 - 4 + 6 = 8 - 2 - 4 + 6 = 6 - 4 + 6 = 2 + 6 = $ $8$
  • f. $-15 - 25 + 35 - 45 = -40 + 35 - 45 = -5 - 45 = $ $-50$

Written Practice

1. A triangular prism has how many more edges than vertices?

Explanation: A triangular prism has 6 vertices (3 on the top base, 3 on the bottom base) and 9 edges (3 on top, 3 on bottom, 3 connecting the bases). The difference is $9 - 6 = 3$.

Answer: 3

2. A baby weighed 6 lb 10 oz at birth. At her two-month checkup, she weighed 10 lb 4 oz. How many pounds and ounces did the baby gain?

Explanation: Subtract the birth weight from the two-month weight.
$10\text{ lb } 4\text{ oz} - 6\text{ lb } 10\text{ oz}$. Since 4 oz is less than 10 oz, borrow 1 lb (16 oz) from the 10 lbs.
$9\text{ lb } (4+16)\text{ oz} = 9\text{ lb } 20\text{ oz}$.
Now subtract: $9\text{ lb } 20\text{ oz} - 6\text{ lb } 10\text{ oz} = 3\text{ lb } 10\text{ oz}$.

Answer: 3 lb 10 oz

3. There are 8 dogs and 12 cats at the animal shelter. What is the ratio of dogs to cats?

Explanation: The ratio is $\frac{\text{Dogs}}{\text{Cats}} = \frac{8}{12}$. Reduce the fraction by dividing the numerator and denominator by 4 to get $\frac{2}{3}$.

Answer: 2/3

4. A softball team's win-loss ratio was 4 to 1. If the team played 25 games total (with no ties), how many games did they win?

Explanation: Total ratio parts = $4 \text{ (wins)} + 1 \text{ (losses)} = 5 \text{ parts}$.
Total games = 25. Divide total games by total parts: $\frac{25}{5} = 5 \text{ games per part}$.
Multiply by the win parts: $4 \times 5 = 20 \text{ wins}$.

Answer: 20 games

5. If Marcus tosses a standard coin and rolls a standard number cube, what is the probability of the coin landing tails up and the number cube stopping on an odd number?

Explanation: These are independent events. Multiply their probabilities.
$P(\text{Tails}) = \frac{1}{2}$. $P(\text{Odd}) = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}$.
$P(\text{Tails and Odd}) = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{4}$.

Answer: 1/4

6. a. What is the perimeter of this parallelogram?
b. What is the area of this parallelogram?

Explanation:
a. Perimeter is the distance around the outside. $P = 2(\text{base}) + 2(\text{side}) = 2(10) + 2(5) = 20 + 10 = 30\text{ cm}$.
b. Area is base $\times$ height. $A = 10\text{ cm} \times 4\text{ cm} = 40\text{ cm}^2$.

Answer: a. 30 cm; b. 40 cm$^2$

7. If an acute angle of a parallelogram measures $65^\circ$, what is the measure of each obtuse angle?

Explanation: Consecutive angles in a parallelogram are supplementary (they add up to $180^\circ$).
$180^\circ - 65^\circ = 115^\circ$.

Answer: $115^\circ$

8. The center of this circle is the origin. The circle passes through (3, 0).
a. Estimate the area of the circle in square units by counting squares.
b. Calculate the area of the circle by using 3.14 for $\pi$.

Explanation:
a. Counting the full and partial grid squares inside the boundary yields approximately 28 to 29 squares.
b. The radius is 3 units. Area $= \pi r^2 \approx 3.14 \times (3)^2 = 3.14 \times 9 = 28.26$.

Answer: a. ~28-29 sq units; b. 28.26

9. Which ratio forms a proportion with $\frac{3}{5}$?
A $\frac{6}{15}$     B $\frac{9}{15}$     C $\frac{5}{3}$     D $\frac{12}{25}$

Explanation: Reduce each fraction to its simplest form to see which equals $\frac{3}{5}$. Option B ($\frac{9}{15}$) reduces to $\frac{3}{5}$ when both numerator and denominator are divided by 3.

Answer: B

10. Complete this proportion: $\frac{4}{7} = \frac{x}{28}$

Explanation: Notice the denominator is multiplied by 4 ($7 \times 4 = 28$). Multiply the numerator by the same factor: $4 \times 4 = 16$. Alternatively, cross-multiply: $7x = 4 \times 28 \rightarrow 7x = 112 \rightarrow x = 16$.

Answer: 16

11. What is the perimeter of the polygon at right? Dimensions are in inches.

Explanation: Determine the lengths of the two missing inner sides.
The total bottom side is 18, and the top-most horizontal side is 10. The inner horizontal side must be $18 - 10 = 8$.
The total left side is 15, and the lower right vertical side is 9. The inner vertical side must be $15 - 9 = 6$.
Perimeter $= 15 \text{ (left)} + 10 \text{ (top)} + 6 \text{ (inner vertical)} + 8 \text{ (inner horizontal)} + 9 \text{ (right)} + 18 \text{ (bottom)} = 66\text{ in}$.

Answer: 66 in

15. A video game is on sale for 30% off the regular price of $15.00. What is the sale price of the game?

Explanation: If it is 30% off, you pay 70% of the price.
Sale Price = $0.70 \times 15.00 = 10.50$.

Answer: $10.50

16. Between which two consecutive whole numbers is $\sqrt{150}$?

Explanation: Find perfect squares near 150.
$12^2 = 144$ and $13^2 = 169$. Since 150 is between 144 and 169, $\sqrt{150}$ is between 12 and 13.

Answer: 12 and 13

17. Compare: $\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^4 \bigcirc$ the probability of flipping 4 consecutive "tails" with a fair coin

Explanation:
Left side: $\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^4 = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{16}$.
Right side: $P(\text{4 tails}) = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{16}$.
They are exactly equal.

Answer: $=$

18. Divide 0.835 by 0.04 and round the quotient to the nearest whole number.

Explanation: $0.835 \div 0.04 = 83.5 \div 4 = 20.875$.
Rounding 20.875 to the nearest whole number gives 21.

Answer: 21

19. The average of four numbers is 15. What is the sum of the four numbers?

Explanation: Average $= \frac{\text{Sum}}{\text{Count}}$.
Therefore, $\text{Sum} = \text{Average} \times \text{Count} = 15 \times 4 = 60$.

Answer: 60

20. Write the prime factorization of 360 using exponents.

Explanation: Create a factor tree for 360.
$360 = 36 \times 10$
$36 = 4 \times 9 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3$
$10 = 2 \times 5$
Combine all prime factors: $2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 \times 5 = 2^3 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5$.

Answer: $2^3 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5$

21. $-4 + -6 - -5 - +3$

Explanation: Change subtractions to adding the opposite.
$-4 - 6 + 5 - 3 = -10 + 5 - 3 = -5 - 3 = -8$.

Answer: $-8$

22. $2^4 + 3^2 \times 5 - \sqrt{144} \times 2^2$

Explanation: Follow the order of operations (PEMDAS/GEMDAS).
Exponents and roots first: $16 + 9 \times 5 - 12 \times 4$.
Multiplication: $16 + 45 - 48$.
Addition and Subtraction: $61 - 48 = 13$.

Answer: 13

23. How many blocks 1 inch on each edge would it take to fill a container that is 10 inches long, 5 inches wide, and 4 inches tall?

Explanation: Find the volume of the container in cubic inches.
$V = \text{length} \times \text{width} \times \text{height} = 10 \times 5 \times 4 = 200$. Since each block is 1 cubic inch, it takes 200 blocks.

Answer: 200 blocks

24. Two thirds of the 24 students in a class play a musical instrument. How many students do NOT play a musical instrument?

Explanation: If $\frac{2}{3}$ play an instrument, then $\frac{1}{3}$ do not play an instrument.
$\frac{1}{3} \text{ of } 24 = 24 \div 3 = 8$.

Answer: 8 students

25. Use a unit multiplier to find how far a train would travel in 3 hours at a steady speed of 60 miles per hour.
$\frac{60 \text{ miles}}{1 \text{ hr}} \times \frac{3 \text{ hr}}{1}$

Explanation: Multiply the given fractions, canceling the "hr" units in the numerator and denominator.
$60 \times 3 = 180\text{ miles}$.

Answer: 180 miles

26. Use the figure on the right to answer a - c.
a. What is the area of the shaded rectangle?
b. What is the area of the unshaded rectangle?
c. What is the combined area of the two rectangles?

Explanation:

27. Jenny measured the length of four small objects in centimeters. Here are her answers:
3.11, 3.16, 3.18, 3.15
Find the average of Jenny's measurements.

Explanation: Add the decimal numbers together and divide by 4.
$3.11 + 3.16 + 3.18 + 3.15 = 12.60$.
$12.60 \div 4 = 3.15$.

Answer: 3.15

28. Mr. Reynolds is thinking of a whole number from 1 to 50 inclusive, and he asked a student to guess the number. Describe how you can find the probability that the student will guess correctly on the first try.

Explanation: The numbers go from 1 to 50, which means there are exactly 50 possible outcomes. Since the student gets exactly 1 guess, there is 1 favorable outcome out of 50 total possibilities.

Answer: The probability is 1/50. (Identify the total number of options, which is 50, and set the single guess as 1 favorable outcome over 50).

29. The coordinates of three vertices of a triangle are (2, 4), (-2, 4), and (-2, -1). What is the area of the triangle?

Explanation: Plotting these points reveals a right triangle.
The base connects (-2, 4) and (2, 4). Length $= 2 - (-2) = 4$ units.
The height connects (-2, 4) and (-2, -1). Length $= 4 - (-1) = 5$ units.
$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = \frac{1}{2} \times 4 \times 5 = 10\text{ sq units}$.

Answer: 10 square units

30. $\frac{3 \text{ gal}}{1} \times \frac{4 \text{ qt}}{1 \text{ gal}} \times \frac{2 \text{ pt}}{1 \text{ qt}}$

Explanation: Cancel out the units that appear in both the numerator and the denominator (gallons and quarts).
You are left with $3 \times 4 \times 2 \text{ pt} = 24 \text{ pints}$.

Answer: 24 pt
