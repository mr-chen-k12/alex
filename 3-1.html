Math Practice - Set 2

Geometry & Area

1. Calculate the area of a circle with a radius of 5 cm. (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\))
Answer: \(78.5 \text{ cm}^2\)

Explanation: Use the formula \(A = \pi r^2\).
\(A \approx 3.14 \times 5^2 = 3.14 \times 25 = 78.5\) square centimeters.
2. Calculate the area of a circle with a diameter of 12 inches. (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\))
Answer: \(113.04 \text{ in}^2\)

Explanation: First, find the radius. The radius is half the diameter, so \(r = 6\) inches.
\(A = \pi r^2 \approx 3.14 \times 6^2 = 3.14 \times 36 = 113.04\) square inches.
3. What is the circumference of a circle with a radius of 7 cm? (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\))
Answer: \(43.96 \text{ cm}\)

Explanation: Use the formula \(C = 2\pi r\).
\(C \approx 2 \times 3.14 \times 7 = 6.28 \times 7 = 43.96\) centimeters.

Decimals & Real-World Operations

4. What is the quotient when fourteen and four tenths is divided by twelve hundredths?
Answer: \(120\)

Explanation: Set up the division: \(14.4 \div 0.12\).
To clear the decimal in the divisor, multiply both numbers by 100: \(1440 \div 12 = 120\).
5. The time in Chicago is 1 hour earlier than the time in New York. If it is 2:30 p.m. in New York, what time is it in Chicago?
Answer: \(1:30 \text{ p.m.}\)

Explanation: Subtract 1 hour from 2:30 p.m., which gives 1:30 p.m.
6. Marcus paid with a $20 bill for 3 notebooks that cost $4.50 each. How much should he get back in change?
Answer: \(\$6.50\)

Explanation: Total cost = \(3 \times \$4.50 = \$13.50\).
Change = \(\$20.00 - \$13.50 = \$6.50\).
7. Evaluate: \(15 + 2.5(40)\)
Answer: \(115\)

Explanation: Use order of operations (PEMDAS), multiply first:
\(2.5 \times 40 = 100\).
Then add: \(15 + 100 = 115\).
8. If a rectangular tower is built of identical blocks, measuring 4 blocks long, 3 blocks wide, and 5 blocks high, what is the total number of blocks?
Answer: \(60 \text{ blocks}\)

Explanation: Multiply the dimensions: Length \(\times\) Width \(\times\) Height.
\(4 \times 3 \times 5 = 12 \times 5 = 60\).

Ratios, Proportions & Percentages

9. The ratio of cats to dogs at the shelter is 3 to 4. If there are 120 dogs, how many cats are there?
Answer: \(90 \text{ cats}\)

Explanation: Set up a proportion: \(\frac{\text{Cats}}{\text{Dogs}} = \frac{3}{4} = \frac{x}{120}\).
Cross-multiply: \(4x = 360\), so \(x = 90\).
10. Sarah answered 21 out of 25 questions correctly on her test. What percent of the questions did she get correct?
Answer: \(84\%\)

Explanation: Fraction correct = \(\frac{21}{25}\).
Convert to percent by multiplying by 100: \(\frac{21}{25} \times 100 = 21 \times 4 = 84\%\).
11. A savings account earns 2% (0.02) simple interest per year. If Mrs. Smith has $1500 in her account, how much interest will she earn in one year?
Answer: \(\$30\)

Explanation: Multiply the principal balance by the interest rate decimal.
\(1500 \times 0.02 = 30\).
12. Write \(\frac{3}{8}\) as a decimal number.
Answer: \(0.375\)

Explanation: Divide 3 by 8. Add a decimal point and zeroes to the 3.
\(3.000 \div 8 = 0.375\).
13. If you roll a standard six-sided die, what is the probability of rolling a prime number (2, 3, or 5)?
Answer: \(\frac{1}{2}\) or \(50\%\)

Explanation: There are 3 prime numbers (2, 3, 5) out of 6 total possibilities.
\(\frac{3}{6}\) simplifies to \(\frac{1}{2}\), which is 50%.

Fraction Operations

14. \(2 \frac{1}{3} + 4 \frac{1}{2}\)
Answer: \(6 \frac{5}{6}\)

Explanation: Find a common denominator (6).
\(2 \frac{2}{6} + 4 \frac{3}{6} = 6 \frac{5}{6}\).
15. \(5 \frac{1}{4} - 1 \frac{3}{4}\)
Answer: \(3 \frac{1}{2}\)

Explanation: You cannot subtract 3 from 1, so borrow from the 5.
\(5 \frac{1}{4}\) becomes \(4 \frac{5}{4}\).
\(4 \frac{5}{4} - 1 \frac{3}{4} = 3 \frac{2}{4}\), which simplifies to \(3 \frac{1}{2}\).
16. \((3 \frac{1}{3})(\frac{3}{5})\)
Answer: \(2\)

Explanation: Convert the mixed number to an improper fraction: \(3 \frac{1}{3} = \frac{10}{3}\).
Multiply: \(\frac{10}{3} \times \frac{3}{5} = \frac{30}{15} = 2\).
17. \(10 \div 2 \frac{1}{2}\)
Answer: \(4\)

Explanation: Convert the mixed number to an improper fraction: \(\frac{5}{2}\).
Change division to multiplication by the reciprocal: \(10 \times \frac{2}{5} = \frac{20}{5} = 4\).
18. \(\frac{3}{4} \text{ of } \$48\)
Answer: \(\$36\)

Explanation: "Of" means multiply. Divide 48 by 4 to get 12, then multiply by 3 to get 36.
\(\frac{3}{4} \times 48 = 36\).

Algebra & Equations

19. \(5.5 + x = 12.2\)
Answer: \(x = 6.7\)

Explanation: Subtract 5.5 from both sides:
\(x = 12.2 - 5.5 = 6.7\).
20. \(4y = 1.24\)
Answer: \(y = 0.31\)

Explanation: Divide both sides by 4:
\(y = \frac{1.24}{4} = 0.31\).
21. \(\frac{x}{15} = \frac{8}{12}\)
Answer: \(x = 10\)

Explanation: Cross multiply: \(12x = 15 \times 8 = 120\).
Divide by 12: \(x = 10\).
(Alternatively, simplify \(\frac{8}{12}\) to \(\frac{2}{3}\), then multiply by 15: \(x = 10\)).
22. What are the next three terms in this sequence?
\(2, 4, 8, 16, 32, \_\_\_, \_\_\_, \_\_\_\)
Answer: \(64, 128, 256\)

Explanation: Each term is multiplied by 2 to get the next term.
\(32 \times 2 = 64\), \(64 \times 2 = 128\), \(128 \times 2 = 256\).

More Geometry & Word Problems

23. A triangle has a base of 20 cm and a height of 8 cm. What is the area of the triangle?
Answer: \(80 \text{ cm}^2\)

Explanation: Formula: \(A = \frac{1}{2}bh\).
\(A = \frac{1}{2}(20)(8) = 10 \times 8 = 80\).
24. Choose the appropriate unit for the volume of a large swimming pool.
A. cubic inches    B. cubic feet    C. square miles
Answer: B. cubic feet

Explanation: Volume requires "cubic" units, eliminating C. Cubic inches are far too small, making cubic feet the most reasonable measurement.
25. Do the ratios \(\frac{5}{8}\) and \(\frac{15}{24}\) form a proportion? How do you know?
Answer: Yes.

Explanation: Simplify \(\frac{15}{24}\) by dividing the top and bottom by 3, which equals \(\frac{5}{8}\). Since they are equivalent, they form a proportion.
26. A train traveled 240 miles in 4 hours. Divide the distance by the time to find the average speed of the train.
Answer: \(60 \text{ miles per hour}\)

Explanation: Speed = Distance / Time.
\(240 \div 4 = 60\).
27. The sum of the interior angles of a triangle is always \(180^\circ\). If two angles measure \(50^\circ\) and \(60^\circ\), what is the measure of the third angle?
Answer: \(70^\circ\)

Explanation: Add the known angles: \(50^\circ + 60^\circ = 110^\circ\).
Subtract from the total: \(180^\circ - 110^\circ = 70^\circ\).
28. A wheel has a circumference of 2 feet. If it is rolled forward a distance of 50 feet, how many full turns will it make?
Answer: \(25 \text{ turns}\)

Explanation: Divide the total distance by the distance of one turn (the circumference).
\(50 \div 2 = 25\).
29. Three vertices of a rectangle are plotted on a coordinate plane at \((0, 0)\), \((0, 4)\), and \((5, 0)\). What are the coordinates of the fourth vertex?
Answer: \((5, 4)\)

Explanation: The points form a rectangle 5 units wide and 4 units high starting from the origin. The fourth corner must be horizontally aligned with \((5, 0)\) and vertically aligned with \((0, 4)\), placing it at \((5, 4)\).
30. Real-World Connection: A recipe calls for 2 cups of flour to make a batch of 12 cookies. If you want to make 30 cookies, how many cups of flour will you need? Assume the ratio of flour to cookies stays constant.
Answer: \(5 \text{ cups}\)

Explanation: Set up a proportion: \(\frac{2 \text{ cups}}{12 \text{ cookies}} = \frac{x \text{ cups}}{30 \text{ cookies}}\).
Cross multiply: \(12x = 60\).
Divide by 12: \(x = 5\).
