Lesson Practice

a. One hundred students were assigned to three buses as equally as possible. How many students were on each of the three buses?

Answer: Two buses had 33 students, and one bus had 34 students.

Explanation: Divide the total number of students by the number of buses: $100 \div 3 = 33$ with a remainder of $1$. To distribute them evenly, you place 33 students on all three buses, and the 1 leftover student goes onto one of the buses, making its total 34.

b. Paperback books cost $8.25. Sarah has $40.00. How many books can she buy?

Answer: 4 books.

Explanation: Divide Sarah's total money by the cost per book: $40.00 \div 8.25 \approx 4.84$. Since she cannot buy a fraction of a book, she can only afford 4 whole books.

c. Forty-five players need a ride to the tournament. Each car can carry four players. How many cars are needed?

Answer: 12 cars.

Explanation: Divide the players by the car capacity: $45 \div 4 = 11$ with a remainder of $1$. Eleven cars will hold 44 players. Because 1 player still needs a ride, you need a 12th car.

d. Marcus folded a 9 in. by 12 in. piece of construction paper in half. Then he folded the paper in half again (folding the longer side each time). After the two folds, what are the dimensions of the rectangle that is formed?

Answer: $4\frac{1}{2}$ in. by 6 in.

Explanation: The first fold halves the 12 in. side, making the paper 9 in. by 6 in. The second fold halves the 9 in. side ($9 \div 2 = 4.5$). The final dimensions are $4\frac{1}{2}$ in. by 6 in.

e. Maya bought four toys for her puppy. The toys cost $5.50, $6.25, $4.75, and $7.50. Find the average (mean) price of the toys.

Answer: $6.00

Explanation: First, sum the prices: $\$5.50 + \$6.25 + \$4.75 + \$7.50 = \$24.00$. Then, divide by the number of toys (4) to find the mean: $\$24.00 \div 4 = \$6.00$.

Written Practice

1. Sixty-five students will be assigned to three classrooms. How many students should be in each class so that the numbers are as balanced as possible? (Write the numbers.)

Answer: 22, 22, 21

Explanation: Divide 65 by 3: $65 \div 3 = 21$ with a remainder of $2$. Base number is 21. The two remaining students go into two of the classes. Therefore, the classes will have 22, 22, and 21 students.

2. Five friends went out to dinner. Their bill was $62.50. If the friends divide the bill equally, how much will each friend pay?

Answer: $12.50

Explanation: Divide the total bill by 5: $\$62.50 \div 5 = \$12.50$.

3. Julian bought a roll of 45¢ stamps at the post office for $18.00. How many stamps were in the roll?

Answer: 40 stamps

Explanation: Divide the total cost by the cost per stamp. Align the units (18.00 dollars $\div$ 0.45 dollars): $18.00 \div 0.45 = 40$.

4. Twenty-seven smaller blocks were used to build a solid 3-by-3-by-3 block. How many smaller blocks are needed to build a block that is 4-by-4-by-4?

Answer: 64 blocks

Explanation: Find the volume by multiplying the lengths of the edges: $4 \times 4 \times 4 = 64$.

5. Write the standard notation for the following: $(6 \times 10^3) + (2 \times 10^2) + (8 \times 10^0)$

Answer: 6,208

Explanation: Expand each term: $6 \times 1000 = 6000$. $2 \times 100 = 200$. $8 \times 1 = 8$. Add them together: $6000 + 200 + 8 = 6208$. (Notice there is no $10^1$ term, so there is a 0 in the tens place).

6. Ten inches is closest to how many centimeters? Round the answer to the nearest centimeter.

Answer: 25 cm

Explanation: One inch is 2.54 centimeters. $10 \text{ inches} \times 2.54 \text{ cm/in} = 25.4 \text{ cm}$. Rounded to the nearest whole centimeter, this is 25 cm.

7. A rectangular room measures 12 feet by 15 feet. If an architect creates a blueprint using the scale $1 \text{ in.} = 3 \text{ ft.}$, what will be the dimensions of the room on the blueprint?

Answer: 4 in. by 5 in.

Explanation: Divide both actual dimensions by the scale factor of 3. Length: $15 \div 3 = 5 \text{ inches}$. Width: $12 \div 3 = 4 \text{ inches}$.

8. If two angles of a triangle measure $65^\circ$ and $75^\circ$, then what is the measure of the third angle?

Answer: $40^\circ$

Explanation: The sum of the interior angles of any triangle is $180^\circ$. Add the known angles: $65^\circ + 75^\circ = 140^\circ$. Subtract from the total: $180^\circ - 140^\circ = 40^\circ$.

9. Complete the table: Fraction $13/20$ -> a. Decimal? b. Percent?

Answer: a. $0.65$ b. $65\%$

Explanation: Multiply numerator and denominator by 5 to create a base of 100: $\frac{13 \times 5}{20 \times 5} = \frac{65}{100}$. As a decimal, this is 0.65. As a percent, it's 65%.

10. Complete the table: Decimal $2.5$ -> a. Fraction? b. Percent?

Answer: a. $2 \frac{1}{2}$ (or $\frac{5}{2}$) b. $250\%$

Explanation: $2.5$ is "two and five tenths", which is $2 \frac{5}{10}$, simplified to $2 \frac{1}{2}$. Shift the decimal two places to the right for the percent: $2.5 \rightarrow 250\%$.

11. Complete the table: Percent $5\%$ -> a. Fraction? b. Decimal?

Answer: a. $\frac{1}{20}$ b. $0.05$

Explanation: $5\%$ means 5 out of 100, or $\frac{5}{100}$. Simplified by dividing by 5, you get $\frac{1}{20}$. As a decimal, five hundredths is written as $0.05$.

12. Calculate mentally: a. $-8 + -15$ b. $-8 - -15$ c. $-15 + +8$ d. $-15 - +8$

Answer: a. $-23$ b. $7$ c. $-7$ d. $-23$

Explanation:
a. Same signs, add and keep the sign: $-8 + (-15) = -23$.
b. Subtracting a negative is adding a positive: $-8 + 15 = 7$.
c. Different signs, subtract absolute values and keep the sign of the larger absolute value: $-15 + 8 = -7$.
d. Subtracting a positive is adding a negative: $-15 + (-8) = -23$.

13. $8\frac{1}{2} \div 100$

Answer: $\frac{17}{200}$ or $0.085$

Explanation: Convert $8\frac{1}{2}$ to an improper fraction: $\frac{17}{2}$. Dividing by 100 is multiplying by $\frac{1}{100}$. So, $\frac{17}{2} \times \frac{1}{100} = \frac{17}{200}$. As a decimal, $8.5 \div 100 = 0.085$.

14. $0.4n = \$5.32$

Answer: $n = \$13.30$

Explanation: Divide both sides by 0.4: $n = 5.32 \div 0.4$. Shift the decimal one place to the right: $53.2 \div 4 = 13.3$. Formatted as money, it's $\$13.30$.

15. The team captain scored 20 points, which accounted for $40\%$ of the team's total. How many points did the team score in all?

Answer: 50 points

Explanation: Set up the equation: $0.40 \times \text{Total} = 20$. Divide both sides by 0.40: $\text{Total} = 20 \div 0.40 = 200 \div 4 = 50$.

16. A diver received the following scores: 8.2, 9.1, 8.5, 8.2, 8.8, 8.5, 9.0. The highest score and the lowest score are discarded. What is the average of the remaining scores?

Answer: 8.6

Explanation: Highest score is 9.1, lowest is 8.2. Discard them. Remaining scores are 8.5, 8.2, 8.8, 8.5, 9.0. Sum them up: $8.5 + 8.2 + 8.8 + 8.5 + 9.0 = 43.0$. Divide by 5: $43.0 \div 5 = 8.6$.

17. Refer to problem 16. What is the difference between the diver's absolute highest score and lowest score before any were discarded?

Answer: 0.9

Explanation: Highest score (9.1) minus lowest score (8.2) equals $0.9$.

18. What is the area of a trapezoid with a height of 6m, a top base of 4m, and a bottom base of 12m?

Answer: $48 \text{ m}^2$

Explanation: Area formula: $A = \frac{1}{2}(b_1 + b_2)h$. Substitute: $A = \frac{1}{2}(4 + 12) \times 6 = \frac{1}{2}(16) \times 6 = 8 \times 6 = 48$.

19. What is the ratio of vertices to edges on a triangular prism?

Answer: $6 : 9$ (which simplifies to $2 : 3$)

Explanation: A triangular prism has 6 vertices (3 on each triangular face) and 9 edges (3 on each triangle + 3 connecting them). The ratio is 6 to 9, simplifying to 2:3.

20. A square has a horizontal line of symmetry and a vertical line of symmetry. How many total lines of symmetry does a square have?

Answer: 4 lines

Explanation: A square can be folded perfectly in half vertically, horizontally, and diagonally twice. This gives 4 total lines of symmetry.

21. Solve and check: $4k + 2 = 122$

Answer: $k = 30$

Explanation: Subtract 2 from both sides: $4k = 120$. Divide by 4: $k = 30$. Check: $4(30) + 2 = 120 + 2 = 122$.

22. Write the prime factorization of 900 using exponents.

Answer: $2^2 \times 3^2 \times 5^2$

Explanation: Factor 900: $9 \times 100$. $9 = 3 \times 3$. $100 = 10 \times 10 = (2 \times 5) \times (2 \times 5)$. Combining gives two 2s, two 3s, and two 5s. Standard order gives $2^2 \times 3^2 \times 5^2$.

23. You need to make a three-dimensional model of a cereal box out of cardboard. What shapes do you need to cut out?

Answer: 6 rectangles (3 pairs of congruent rectangles).

Explanation: A cereal box is a rectangular prism, which is formed by six rectangular faces.

24. The price of an apple is $1.20. The sales-tax rate is 5%. What is the total cost of the apple, including tax?

Answer: $1.26

Explanation: Calculate the tax: $1.20 \times 0.05 = 0.06$. Add the tax to the original price: $1.20 + 0.06 = 1.26$.

25. The probability of winning the local lottery is one in 500,000. What is the probability of NOT winning?

Answer: $\frac{499,999}{500,000}$

Explanation: The probability of an event plus the probability of its complement is 1. If $P(\text{Win}) = \frac{1}{500,000}$, then $P(\text{Lose}) = 1 - \frac{1}{500,000} = \frac{499,999}{500,000}$.

26. Triangles $DEF$ and $PQR$ are congruent ($\Delta DEF \cong \Delta PQR$). Which angle corresponds to angle $E$?

Answer: Angle $Q$

Explanation: The order of vertices in a congruence statement shows corresponding parts. $E$ is the middle letter of $DEF$, so it corresponds to the middle letter of $PQR$, which is $Q$.

27. What single geometric transformation turns a shape into its mirror image?

Answer: Reflection

Explanation: A reflection "flips" a figure over a line, creating a mirror image.

28. A circular pond has a radius of 6 meters. What is the circumference of the pond? (Use 3.14 for $\pi$)

Answer: 37.68 m

Explanation: Use $C = 2\pi r$. Substitute: $C = 2 \times 3.14 \times 6 = 12 \times 3.14 = 37.68$.

29. Solve this proportion: $\frac{12}{15} = \frac{20}{x}$

Answer: $x = 25$

Explanation: Cross-multiply: $12 \times x = 15 \times 20$. This gives $12x = 300$. Divide by 12: $x = 25$. Alternatively, simplify $\frac{12}{15}$ to $\frac{4}{5}$. Then $\frac{4}{5} = \frac{20}{x} \rightarrow 4 \times 5 = 20$, so $5 \times 5 = 25$.

30. Find the distance ($d$) a train travels if $d = rt$, given the rate ($r$) is $60 \text{ mph}$ and the time ($t$) is $4 \text{ hours}$.

Answer: $240 \text{ miles}$

Explanation: Substitute the given values into the formula: $d = 60 \times 4$. Therefore, $d = 240$.
