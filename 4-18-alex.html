Problem a
Draw two rectangles that are not squares. Draw all the lines of symmetry for each rectangle.
Answer & Visual:
Explanation:

A non-square rectangle has exactly 2 lines of symmetry: one vertical line down the middle and one horizontal line across the middle. Diagonal lines do not work because folding along them does not result in the corners perfectly overlapping.

Problem b
All but one of these letters can be drawn to have a line of symmetry. Which of these letters does not have a line of symmetry?

E   H   I   M   S   X
Answer:

S

Explanation:

E has a horizontal line of symmetry. H, I, and X have both horizontal and vertical lines of symmetry. M has a vertical line of symmetry. The letter S cannot be folded over any line to match perfectly.

Problem c
Which two of these letters have rotational symmetry? (Hint: Rotating your book might help you find the answer.)

C   D   H   K   V   Z
Answer:

H and Z

Explanation:

If you rotate the letters H and Z by 180° (upside down), they look exactly the same as they do right-side up. The others (C, D, K, V) change their appearance or orientation when rotated.

Written Practice

Problem 1
When the greatest three-digit number is divided by the greatest one-digit number, what is the quotient?
Answer:

111

Explanation:

The greatest three-digit number is 999. The greatest one-digit number is 9.
$$999 \div 9 = 111$$

Problem 2
The ratio of the length to the width of a community garden is 4 to 3. If the width of the garden is 15 meters, what is the length?
Answer:

20 meters

Explanation:

Set up a proportion: $$\frac{Length}{Width} = \frac{4}{3} = \frac{L}{15}$$ Cross-multiply or find the scale factor: \(3 \times 5 = 15\), so \(4 \times 5 = 20\).

Problem 3
A shipping box is shaped like a perfect cube with side lengths of 6 centimeters. How many cubic centimeters is the volume of the box?
Answer:

216 cubic centimeters

Explanation:

Volume of a cube is calculated by cubing the side length (\(s^3\)).
$$V = 6 \times 6 \times 6 = 36 \times 6 = 216 \text{ cm}^3$$

Problem 4
A full turn is 360°. How many degrees is \(\frac{1}{8}\) of a turn?
Answer:

45°

Explanation:

Multiply the full turn by the fraction: $$360^\circ \times \frac{1}{8} = 45^\circ$$

Problem 5
Does a regular hexagon have rotational and line symmetry? Use words to explain how you know.
Answer:

Yes, it has both.

Explanation:

Line Symmetry: A regular hexagon has 6 lines of symmetry (three connecting opposite vertices and three connecting the midpoints of opposite sides).
Rotational Symmetry: Because all 6 sides and angles are equal, rotating the hexagon by 60° (or multiples of 60°) around its center perfectly maps it onto its original position.

Problem 6
Sketch a rectangle similar to a 2 cm by 4 cm rectangle. Make the scale factor from the original rectangle to your sketch 3. What is the perimeter of the rectangle you sketched?
Answer:

36 cm

Explanation:

The original rectangle has dimensions 2 cm and 4 cm. With a scale factor of 3, the new side lengths are \(2 \text{ cm} \times 3 = 6 \text{ cm}\) and \(4 \text{ cm} \times 3 = 12 \text{ cm}\).
The perimeter of the new rectangle is \(6 + 12 + 6 + 12 = 36 \text{ cm}\).

Problem 7
What is the area of a right triangle with legs of 6 cm and 8 cm, and a hypotenuse of 10 cm?
Answer:

24 cm²

Explanation:

The area of a right triangle is calculated using the two legs as the base and height (ignore the hypotenuse for area calculations).
$$Area = \frac{1}{2} \times 6 \times 8 = \frac{1}{2} \times 48 = 24 \text{ cm}^2$$

Problem 8
Draw a ratio box for this problem. Then solve the problem using a proportion.

Mr. Davis is organizing his tool bench. He finds that 15 of his wrenches are metric, and that 25% of his wrenches are standard (imperial). How many wrenches does he have in total?
Answer:

20 wrenches

Explanation:

If 25% are standard, then 75% are metric (100% - 25%). We set up a proportion comparing the metric percentage to the count: $$\frac{\text{Percent}}{\text{Count}} \Rightarrow \frac{75}{15} = \frac{100}{x}$$ Cross-multiplying gives: \(75x = 1500\), so \(x = 20\).
He has a total of 20 wrenches.

Problem 9
Convert the fraction \(3 \frac{1}{2}\) to a decimal (a) and a percent (b).
Answer:

a. 3.5    b. 350%

Explanation:

\(\frac{1}{2}\) is equivalent to 0.5, so \(3 \frac{1}{2}\) = 3.5. To find the percent, multiply the decimal by 100 to get 350%.

Problem 10
Convert the decimal 2.4 to a mixed fraction (a) and a percent (b).
Answer:

a. \(2 \frac{2}{5}\)    b. 240%

Explanation:

2.4 is "two and four-tenths", which simplifies to \(2 \frac{2}{5}\). Multiply 2.4 by 100 to find the percent: 240%.

Problem 11
Convert 45% to a fraction (a) and a decimal (b).
Answer:

a. \(\frac{9}{20}\)    b. 0.45

Explanation:

45% as a decimal is 0.45. As a fraction, it is \(\frac{45}{100}\), which reduces by dividing numerator and denominator by 5 to get \(\frac{9}{20}\).

Problem 12
$$15 \frac{1}{4} + 10 \frac{1}{2} + 5 \frac{3}{8}$$
Answer:

\(31 \frac{1}{8}\)

Explanation:

Find a common denominator for the fractions, which is 8: $$15 \frac{2}{8} + 10 \frac{4}{8} + 5 \frac{3}{8}$$ Add the whole numbers: \(15 + 10 + 5 = 30\).
Add the fractions: \(\frac{2}{8} + \frac{4}{8} + \frac{3}{8} = \frac{9}{8} = 1 \frac{1}{8}\).
Total: \(30 + 1 \frac{1}{8} = 31 \frac{1}{8}\).

Problem 13
$$(2 \frac{1}{4} \div 3) \div 1 \frac{1}{2}$$
Answer:

\(\frac{1}{2}\)

Explanation:

Convert mixed numbers to improper fractions: $$(\frac{9}{4} \div \frac{3}{1}) \div \frac{3}{2}$$ Follow PEMDAS and evaluate the parentheses first (multiply by reciprocal): $$(\frac{9}{4} \times \frac{1}{3}) \div \frac{3}{2} = \frac{3}{4} \div \frac{3}{2}$$ Now, multiply by the reciprocal of the second fraction: $$\frac{3}{4} \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{6}{12} = \frac{1}{2}$$

Problem 14
$$12 - (4.3 + 1.85)$$
Answer:

5.85

Explanation:

First, add the numbers in parentheses (aligning the decimals): \(4.30 + 1.85 = 6.15\).
Subtract from 12: \(12.00 - 6.15 = 5.85\).

Problem 15

Divide both sides by 0.45: $$y = \frac{81.00}{0.45} = \frac{8100}{45} = 180$$

Problem 16
Find 7.5% of $42.50 by multiplying 0.075 by $42.50. Round the product to the nearest cent.
Answer:

$3.19

Explanation:

$$0.075 \times 42.50 = 3.1875$$
To round to the nearest cent (hundredths place), look at the third decimal digit (7). Since it is 5 or greater, round up the 8 to a 9. Thus, $3.19.

Problem 17
Round the quotient to the nearest hundredth: $$0.087 \div 5$$
Answer:

0.02

Explanation:

Divide: $$0.087 \div 5 = 0.0174$$ The hundredths place is 1. The next digit is 7, which is 5 or greater, so round the 1 up to 2. The rounded answer is 0.02.

Problem 18
Write the prime factorization of 800 using exponents.
Answer:

\(2^5 \times 5^2\)

Explanation:

Break down 800: \(800 = 8 \times 100\).
\(8 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 = 2^3\).
\(100 = 10 \times 10 = (2 \times 5) \times (2 \times 5) = 2^2 \times 5^2\).
Combine them: \(2^3 \times 2^2 \times 5^2 = 2^5 \times 5^2\).

Problem 19
"All rectangles are similar." True or false?
Answer:

False

Explanation:

While all rectangles have corresponding equal angles (all 90°), their corresponding side lengths do not always share the same ratio. For example, a 1x4 rectangle is not similar to a 2x3 rectangle because \(\frac{1}{2} \neq \frac{4}{3}\).

Problem 20
$$4^3 - 4^2 \div 4 - 4 \times 2$$
Answer:

52

Explanation:

Follow the order of operations (PEMDAS/BODMAS):
Exponents first: \(64 - 16 \div 4 - 4 \times 2\)
Multiplication and Division (left to right): \(64 - 4 - 8\)
Addition and Subtraction (left to right): \(60 - 8 = 52\).

Problem 21
What is the perimeter of this polygon? (An L-shaped polygon with top edge = 15 m, left edge = 12 m, right edge = 7 m, and bottom-inner horizontal edge = 6 m).
Answer:

54 m

Explanation:

To find the perimeter, add all the outer boundaries.
Top edge = 15 m
Right edge = 7 m
Left edge = 12 m
Bottom-inner horizontal edge = 6 m
The missing bottom horizontal segment is \(15 \text{ m} - 6 \text{ m} = 9 \text{ m}\).
The missing inner vertical "step-down" segment is \(12 \text{ m} - 7 \text{ m} = 5 \text{ m}\).
$$Perimeter = 15 + 7 + 6 + 5 + 9 + 12 = 54 \text{ m}$$

Problem 22
What is the area of the polygon from problem 21?
Answer:

150 m²

Explanation:

Split the polygon into two rectangles.
Rectangle 1 (left side column): Width = 9 m (from \(15 - 6\)), Height = 12 m. Area = \(9 \times 12 = 108 \text{ m}^2\).
Rectangle 2 (right side step): Width = 6 m, Height = 7 m. Area = \(6 \times 7 = 42 \text{ m}^2\).
Total Area = \(108 + 42 = 150 \text{ m}^2\).

Problem 23
Figures I and II are congruent parallelograms. Figure I is facing right, while Figure II is flipped horizontally and shifted down. Name the transformations that would position Figure I on Figure II.
Answer:

Reflection and Translation

Explanation:

Because the figure has been flipped horizontally to face the opposite direction, a reflection is required. To then align it with the exact position of Figure II (shifted down and across), a translation (slide) is needed.

Problem 24
The perimeter of a rectangle is 30 cm. If the length of the rectangle is 9 cm, what is the width?
Answer:

6 cm

Explanation:

The perimeter formula for a rectangle is \(P = 2l + 2w\).
$$30 = 2(9) + 2w$$ $$30 = 18 + 2w$$ $$12 = 2w$$ $$w = 6 \text{ cm}$$

Problem 25
A modern observation wheel has a diameter of 150 ft. Find the circumference of the wheel to the nearest ten feet.
Answer:

470 ft

Explanation:

Circumference formula is \(C = \pi \times d\). Using 3.14 for \(\pi\):
$$C \approx 3.14 \times 150 = 471 \text{ ft}$$
The question asks to round to the nearest ten feet. 471 rounded to the nearest ten is 470 ft.

Problem 26
Use a ruler to draw \(\overline{XY}\) \(2 \frac{1}{2}\) inches long. Then draw a dot at the midpoint of \(XY\), and label the point \(M\). How long is \(\overline{XM}\)?
Answer:

\(1 \frac{1}{4}\) inches

Explanation:

The midpoint divides the segment exactly in half. Divide \(2 \frac{1}{2}\) by 2: $$2 \frac{1}{2} = \frac{5}{2}$$ $$\frac{5}{2} \div 2 = \frac{5}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{5}{4} = 1 \frac{1}{4}$$

Problem 27
Use a compass to draw a circle on a coordinate plane. Make the center of the circle the origin, and make the radius 7 units. At which two points does the circle cross the y-axis?
Answer:

(0, 7) and (0, -7)

Explanation:

Since the circle is centered at the origin (0,0) and has a radius of 7, it will extend 7 units straight up and 7 units straight down along the y-axis.

Problem 28
What is the area of the circle in problem 27? (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\).)

153.86 square units

Explanation:

The area formula for a circle is \(A = \pi \times r^2\).
Radius \(r = 7\), so \(r^2 = 49\).
$$A = 3.14 \times 49 = 153.86$$

Problem 30
If Sarah rolls a standard six-sided die twice, what is the probability that she rolls a 1 on both turns?
Answer:

\(\frac{1}{36}\)

Explanation:

The probability of rolling a 1 on a six-sided die is \(\frac{1}{6}\). Since the two rolls are independent events, multiply their probabilities together:
$$P(\text{1, 1}) = \frac{1}{6} \times \frac{1}{6} = \frac{1}{36}$$
