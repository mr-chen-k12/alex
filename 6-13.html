Problem 1
When the product of 6 and 6 is divided by the sum of 6 and 6, what is the quotient?
Answer: 3
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Find the product of 6 and 6:
    $$6 \times 6 = 36$$
  • Find the sum of 6 and 6:
    $$6 + 6 = 12$$
  • Divide the product by the sum to determine the quotient:
    $$36 \div 12 = 3$$

Real-World Connection
Ryan and his brother went to the community swimming pool. The price of admission was \$5.50 each. They each bought a snack bar combo for \$2.25 and a souvenir keychain. Together they spent a total of \$18.50.

a. Find the cost of each keychain.
b. Ryan brought a \$15 gift card to use at the pool entrance. How much should he have left after paying for his own individual costs (admission, snack bar combo, and keychain)?

Answer: a. \$1.50 per keychain  |  b. \$5.75 remaining
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Part a: Sum the entry fees and snack costs for both individuals:
    $$\text{Admission} = 2 \times \$5.50 = \$11.00$$
    $$\text{Snacks} = 2 \times \$2.25 = \$4.50$$
    $$\text{Combined Subtotal} = \$11.00 + \$4.50 = \$15.50$$
  • Deduct this subtotal from the grand total to find the cost of both keychains:
    $$\$18.50 - \$15.50 = \$3.00 \text{ (for 2 keychains)} \implies \$3.00 \div 2 = \$1.50 \text{ each}$$
  • Part b: Sum Ryan's individual expenditures:
    $$\text{Individual Spending} = \$5.50 + \$2.25 + \$1.50 = \$9.25$$
  • Subtract his personal spending total from his \$15 gift card balance to find the remaining funds:
    $$\$15.00 - \$9.25 = \$5.75$$
