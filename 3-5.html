Saxon Math Course 1 - Lesson 90

Lesson Practice

a. Jose was heading south on his bike. When he reached Sycamore, he turned $90^\circ$ to the right. Then at Highland he turned $90^\circ$ to the right again. Assuming each street was straight, in which direction was Jose heading on Elkins?
Answer: North

Explanation: Jose was initially heading South. A $90^\circ$ turn to the right means he faces West. Another $90^\circ$ turn to the right means he faces North.
b. Kiara made one full turn counterclockwise. Mary made two full turns clockwise. How many degrees did Mary turn?
Answer: $720^\circ$

Explanation: One full turn is $360^\circ$. Because Mary made two full turns, you multiply $360^\circ$ by 2. $360 \times 2 = 720^\circ$.
c. David three laps around the park. On each lap he made five turns to the left and no turns to the right. What was the average number of degrees in each of David's turns? Draw a picture of the problem.
Answer: $72^\circ$

Explanation: To complete one full lap (a closed loop returning to the same direction), David must have turned a total of $360^\circ$. If he made 5 turns per lap, the average degree per turn is $360^\circ \div 5 = 72^\circ$.

Written Practice

1. What is the mean of 4.2, 4.8, and 5.1?
Answer: 4.7

Explanation: Add the numbers together: $4.2 + 4.8 + 5.1 = 14.1$. Then divide by the count of numbers (3): $14.1 \div 3 = 4.7$.
2. The movie is 120 minutes long. If it begins at 7:15 p.m., when will it be over?
Answer: 9:15 p.m.

Explanation: 120 minutes is exactly 2 hours. Adding 2 hours to 7:15 p.m. results in 9:15 p.m.
3. Fifteen of the 25 students in Room 20 are boys. What percent of the students in Room 20 are boys?
Answer: $60\%$

Explanation: The fraction of boys is $\frac{15}{25}$. To convert to a percent, multiply by $100\%$. $\frac{15}{25} = \frac{3}{5}$. $\frac{3}{5} \times 100\% = 60\%$.
4. This triangular prism has how many more edges than vertices?
Answer: 3

Explanation: A triangular prism has 9 edges and 6 vertices. $9 - 6 = 3$.
5. The teacher cut a 12-inch diameter circle from a sheet of construction paper.
a. What was the radius of the circle?
b. What was the area of the circle? (Use $3.14$ for $\pi$.)
Answer: a. 6 inches, b. 113.04 sq inches

Explanation:
a. The radius is half the diameter. $12 \div 2 = 6$ inches.
b. Area $= \pi r^2$. $A = 3.14 \times 6^2 = 3.14 \times 36 = 113.04$ square inches.
6. Write a description of a trapezoid.
Answer & Explanation: A trapezoid is a quadrilateral (a four-sided polygon) that has exactly one pair of parallel sides.
7. Arrange these numbers in order from least to greatest:
$$1, -2, 0, -4, \frac{1}{2}$$
Answer: $-4, -2, 0, \frac{1}{2}, 1$

Explanation: Negative numbers are smaller than zero and positive numbers. The further from zero a negative number is, the smaller it is. Thus, $-4$ is the smallest, followed by $-2$, then $0$. Finally, the positive fractions and integers order normally: $\frac{1}{2}$, then $1$.
8. Express the unknown factor as a mixed number: $$25n = 70$$
Answer: $2\frac{4}{5}$

Explanation: Divide both sides by 25: $n = \frac{70}{25}$. Simplify the fraction by dividing the numerator and denominator by 5 to get $\frac{14}{5}$. As a mixed number, 5 goes into 14 two times with a remainder of 4, so $2\frac{4}{5}$.
Refer to the triangle to answer questions 9-11. 9. What is the area of this triangle?
Answer: $150 \text{ mm}^2$

Explanation: Area of a triangle $= \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}$. The base is $20\text{ mm}$ and the height is $15\text{ mm}$. $A = \frac{1}{2} \times 20 \times 15 = 10 \times 15 = 150\text{ mm}^2$.
10. What is the perimeter of this triangle?
Answer: $60\text{ mm}$

Explanation: The perimeter is the sum of all the sides. $15 + 20 + 25 = 60\text{ mm}$.
11. What is the ratio of the length of the shortest side to the length of the longest side? Express the ratio as a fraction and as a decimal.
Answer: Fraction: $\frac{3}{5}$, Decimal: $0.6$

Explanation: The shortest side is 15 and the longest side is 25. The ratio is $\frac{15}{25}$. Dividing numerator and denominator by 5 simplifies it to $\frac{3}{5}$. As a decimal, $3 \div 5 = 0.6$.
12. Write $6.25$ as a mixed number. Then subtract $\frac{5}{8}$ from the mixed number. What is the difference?
Answer: $5\frac{5}{8}$

Explanation: $6.25$ is equal to $6\frac{1}{4}$. To subtract $\frac{5}{8}$, find a common denominator: $6\frac{1}{4} = 6\frac{2}{8}$. You cannot subtract 5 from 2, so borrow 1 from the 6: $6\frac{2}{8} = 5\frac{10}{8}$. Now subtract: $5\frac{10}{8} - \frac{5}{8} = 5\frac{5}{8}$.
13. Ali was facing north. Then he turned to his left $180^\circ$. What direction was he facing after he turned?
Answer: South

Explanation: A $180^\circ$ turn means turning to face the exact opposite direction. The opposite of North is South.
14. Write $28\%$ as a reduced fraction.
Answer: $\frac{7}{25}$

Explanation: $28\% = \frac{28}{100}$. Divide the numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor, 4: $\frac{28 \div 4}{100 \div 4} = \frac{7}{25}$.
15. Analyze: $$\frac{n}{12} = \frac{20}{30}$$
Answer: $n = 8$

Explanation: Simplify $\frac{20}{30}$ to $\frac{2}{3}$. So, $\frac{n}{12} = \frac{2}{3}$. Cross-multiply: $3n = 24$. Divide by 3: $n = 8$.
16. $0.625 \div 10$
Answer: $0.0625$

Explanation: Dividing a decimal by 10 moves the decimal point one place to the left.
17. $\frac{25}{0.8}$
Answer: $31.25$

Explanation: Multiply the numerator and denominator by 10 to remove the decimal: $\frac{250}{8}$. Divide 250 by 8: $250 \div 8 = 31.25$.
18. $3\frac{3}{8} + 3\frac{3}{4}$
Answer: $7\frac{1}{8}$

Explanation: Find a common denominator: $3\frac{3}{4} = 3\frac{6}{8}$. Add the fractions: $\frac{3}{8} + \frac{6}{8} = \frac{9}{8}$. Convert to mixed number: $\frac{9}{8} = 1\frac{1}{8}$. Add the whole numbers: $3 + 3 + 1\frac{1}{8} = 7\frac{1}{8}$.
19. $5\frac{1}{8} - 1\frac{7}{8}$
Answer: $3\frac{1}{4}$

Explanation: You cannot subtract 7 from 1, so borrow from the whole number 5: $5\frac{1}{8} = 4\frac{9}{8}$. Now subtract: $4\frac{9}{8} - 1\frac{7}{8} = 3\frac{2}{8}$. Simplify the fraction: $3\frac{1}{4}$.
20. $6\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{3}{10} \times 4$
Answer: $8$

Explanation: Convert the mixed number to an improper fraction: $\frac{20}{3}$. The expression is $\frac{20}{3} \times \frac{3}{10} \times \frac{4}{1}$. Notice that the 3s cancel out, and $20 \div 10 = 2$. So the problem simplifies to $2 \times 4 = 8$.
21. One third of the two dozen knights were on horseback. How many knights were not on horseback?
Answer: 16 knights

Explanation: Two dozen equals 24. One third of 24 is $24 \div 3 = 8$. If 8 knights were on horseback, then $24 - 8 = 16$ knights were not on horseback.
22. Weights totaling 38 ounces were placed on the left side of this scale, while weights totaling 26 ounces were placed on the right side of the scale. How many ounces of weights should be moved from the left side to the right side to balance the scale? (Hint: Find the average of the weights on the two sides of the scale.)
Answer: $6$ ounces

Explanation: The total weight is $38 + 26 = 64$ ounces. To balance the scale, each side must have the average weight: $64 \div 2 = 32$ ounces. Currently, the left side has 38 ounces. To get down to 32 ounces, $38 - 32 = 6$ ounces must be moved.
23. The cube at right is made up of smaller cubes that each have a volume of 1 cubic centimeter. What is the volume of the larger cube?
Answer: $27\text{ cm}^3$

Explanation: The larger cube is made of a $3 \times 3 \times 3$ grid of smaller cubes. Since each small cube is $1\text{ cm}^3$, the total volume is $3 \times 3 \times 3 = 27$ cubic centimeters.
24. Round forty-eight hundredths to the nearest tenth.
Answer: $0.5$

Explanation: Forty-eight hundredths is written as $0.48$. Look at the hundredths digit (8), which is 5 or greater, so round up the tenths digit from 4 to 5. The answer is $0.5$.
25. $\sqrt{144} - \sqrt{121}$
Answer: $1$

Explanation: The square root of 144 is 12, and the square root of 121 is 11. Substitute the values: $12 - 11 = 1$.
26. The ratio of dogs to cats in the neighborhood is 6 to 5. What is the ratio of cats to dogs?
Answer: 5 to 6

Explanation: Simply reverse the terms of the ratio. If dogs to cats is 6 to 5, cats to dogs is 5 to 6.
27. $10 + 10 \times 10 - 10 \div 10$
Answer: $109$

Explanation: Following the order of operations (PEMDAS/BODMAS), perform multiplication and division first.
$10 \times 10 = 100$
$10 \div 10 = 1$
The equation becomes: $10 + 100 - 1 = 110 - 1 = 109$.
28. The Thompsons drink a gallon of milk every two days. There are four people in the Thompson family. Each person drinks an average of how many pints of milk per day? Explain your thinking.
Answer: 1 pint per day per person

Explanation:
1. Convert gallons to pints: 1 gallon = 4 quarts = 8 pints.
2. Calculate the family's daily consumption: 8 pints every 2 days means $8 \div 2 = 4$ pints per day for the whole family.
3. Calculate individual consumption: 4 pints divided by 4 people equals 1 pint per person per day.
29. Simplify:
a. $10\text{ cm} + 100\text{ mm}$ (Write the answer in millimeters.)
b. $300\text{ books} \div 30\text{ students}$
Answer: a. $200\text{ mm}$, b. $10\text{ books per student}$

Explanation:
a. Convert cm to mm: $10\text{ cm} \times 10 = 100\text{ mm}$. Add the values: $100\text{ mm} + 100\text{ mm} = 200\text{ mm}$.
b. Divide the numbers and keep the units: $300 \div 30 = 10$. The unit becomes books/student.
30. On a coordinate plane draw a segment from point $A(-3, -1)$ to point $B(5, -1)$. What are the coordinates of the point that is halfway between points $A$ and $B$?
Answer: $(1, -1)$

Explanation: Both points share the same y-coordinate ($-1$), meaning the segment is perfectly horizontal. To find the midpoint's x-coordinate, find the average of the x-coordinates: $\frac{-3 + 5}{2} = \frac{2}{2} = 1$. So the halfway point is $(1, -1)$.

Real-World Connection

The students in Mrs. Fitzgerald's cooking class will make buttermilk biscuits using the recipe below. If 56 students each work with a partner to make one batch of biscuits, how much of each ingredient will Mrs. Fitzgerald need to buy? Hint: Keep each item in the unit measure given.

  • $1\frac{3}{4}$ cups of all-purpose flour
  • $1$ teaspoon of baking soda
  • $1$ stick of butter
  • $1\frac{1}{4}$ cups of milk
Answer:
  • Flour: $49$ cups
  • Baking soda: $28$ teaspoons
  • Butter: $28$ sticks
  • Milk: $35$ cups

Explanation: If 56 students work in pairs (with a partner), there are $56 \div 2 = 28$ pairs. This means 28 batches of biscuits are being made. Multiply each recipe ingredient by 28:

- Flour: $1\frac{3}{4} \times 28 = \frac{7}{4} \times 28 = 7 \times 7 = 49$ cups.
- Baking soda: $1 \times 28 = 28$ teaspoons.
- Butter: $1 \times 28 = 28$ sticks.
- Milk: $1\frac{1}{4} \times 28 = \frac{5}{4} \times 28 = 5 \times 7 = 35$ cups.
