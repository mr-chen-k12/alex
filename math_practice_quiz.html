Math Practice - Multiple Choice

Based on your selected problem types, here are 3 practice questions for each concept.

Set 1: Volume of a Rectangular Prism (Based on Problem 3)

1. A rectangular prism has a length of 4 inches, a width of 5 inches, and a height of 10 inches. What is the volume of the prism?
The volume of a rectangular prism is length $\times$ width $\times$ height.
$$V = 4 \times 5 \times 10 = 20 \times 10 = 200 \text{ in}^3$$
2. A rectangular prism has a length of 2 cm, a width of 3 cm, and a height of 7 cm. What is the volume of the prism?
Multiply all three dimensions to find the volume:
$$V = 2 \times 3 \times 7 = 6 \times 7 = 42 \text{ cm}^3$$
3. What is the volume of a rectangular prism with a length of 5 m, width of 5 m, and height of 4 m?
Use the volume formula $V = l \times w \times h$.
$$V = 5 \times 5 \times 4 = 25 \times 4 = 100 \text{ m}^3$$

Set 2: Similar Shapes & Perimeter (Based on Problem 6)

4. A rectangle is 3 cm by 4 cm. If you draw a similar rectangle with a scale factor of 3, what is the perimeter of the new rectangle?
First find the new sides by multiplying by the scale factor of 3: $3 \times 3 = 9 \text{ cm}$ and $4 \times 3 = 12 \text{ cm}$.
Then find the perimeter: $9 + 12 + 9 + 12 = 42 \text{ cm}$.
5. The dimensions of a rectangle are 6 in by 2 in. A similar rectangle is drawn with a scale factor of 4. What is the new perimeter?
The new dimensions are $6 \times 4 = 24 \text{ in}$ and $2 \times 4 = 8 \text{ in}$.
Perimeter $= 24 + 8 + 24 + 8 = 64 \text{ in}$.
6. An original rectangle is 10 ft by 4 ft. If the scale factor for a new similar rectangle is 0.5, what is the perimeter of the new rectangle?
Multiply by 0.5 (which is half): new sides are $5 \text{ ft}$ and $2 \text{ ft}$.
Perimeter $= 5 + 2 + 5 + 2 = 14 \text{ ft}$.

Set 3: Proportions & Percentages (Based on Problem 8)

7. A store has 150 shirts in stock. If 40% of the shirts are red and the rest are blue, how many blue shirts are there?
If 40% are red, then 60% are blue ($100\% - 40\%$).
Set up a proportion or multiply: $0.60 \times 150 = 90$ blue shirts.
8. In a group of 200 students, 25% take an art class and the rest take music. How many students take music?
If 25% take art, 75% take music.
Find 75% of 200: $\frac{75}{100} = \frac{x}{200} \Rightarrow x = 150$.
9. An animal shelter has 50 pets. If 10% are cats and the rest are dogs, how many dogs are there?
If 10% are cats, then 90% are dogs.
$90\%$ of $50 = 0.90 \times 50 = 45$ dogs.

Set 4: Conversions (Based on Problem 11)

10. Convert 45% to a fraction in simplest form and a decimal.
As a decimal, $45\% = 0.45$. As a fraction, it is $\frac{45}{100}$, which reduces to $\frac{9}{20}$ when divided by 5.
11. Convert 80% to a fraction in simplest form and a decimal.
Decimal: $80\% = 0.80$. Fraction: $\frac{80}{100}$, divide top and bottom by 20 to get $\frac{4}{5}$.
12. Convert 5% to a fraction in simplest form and a decimal.
Decimal: $5\% = 0.05$. Fraction: $\frac{5}{100}$, divide top and bottom by 5 to get $\frac{1}{20}$.

Set 5: Order of Operations with Decimals (Based on Problem 14)

13. Evaluate: $$50 - (3.2 + 6.8)$$
Perform addition inside parentheses first: $3.2 + 6.8 = 10.0$.
Then subtract from 50: $50 - 10 = 40$.
14. Evaluate: $$25 - (1.5 + 2.5)$$
Parentheses first: $1.5 + 2.5 = 4.0$.
Subtract: $25 - 4 = 21$.
15. Evaluate: $$100 - (12.4 + 7.6)$$
Add the decimals first: $12.4 + 7.6 = 20.0$.
Subtract from 100: $100 - 20 = 80$.

Set 6: Order of Operations (Based on Problem 20)

16. Evaluate: $$4^2 - 2^2 \times 3 + 8 \div 4$$
Exponents: $16 - 4 \times 3 + 8 \div 4$
Multiplication/Division: $16 - 12 + 2$
Addition/Subtraction (left to right): $4 + 2 = 6$.
17. Evaluate: $$6^2 - 3^2 \times 2 + 15 \div 3$$
Exponents: $36 - 9 \times 2 + 15 \div 3$
Multiplication/Division: $36 - 18 + 5$

18. Evaluate: $$3^2 - 1^2 \times 5 + 12 \div 6$$
Exponents: $9 - 1 \times 5 + 12 \div 6$
Multiplication/Division: $9 - 5 + 2$
Addition/Subtraction: $4 + 2 = 6$.

Set 7: Perimeter of Complex Polygons (Based on Problem 21)

19. What is the perimeter of an L-shaped polygon where the top edge is 12 cm, left vertical edge is 10 cm, right vertical edge is 4 cm, and the bottom-inner horizontal edge is 7 cm?
Find the missing sides:
Bottom edge = Top (12) - Inner horizontal (7) = 5 cm.
Inner vertical = Left (10) - Right (4) = 6 cm.
Perimeter = $12 + 10 + 4 + 7 + 5 + 6 = 44 \text{ cm}$.
20. An L-shaped room has these outer walls: Top = 15 m, Left = 12 m. The rightmost vertical wall is 5 m, and the bottom inner horizontal wall is 8 m. What is the perimeter of the room?
Missing bottom wall = $15 - 8 = 7 \text{ m}$.
Missing inner vertical wall = $12 - 5 = 7 \text{ m}$.
Perimeter = $15 + 12 + 5 + 8 + 7 + 7 = 54 \text{ m}$.
21. Find the perimeter of an L-shaped polygon with Top = 8 ft, Left = 8 ft, Right vertical = 2 ft, and bottom inner horizontal = 5 ft.
Missing bottom = $8 - 5 = 3 \text{ ft}$.
Missing inner vertical = $8 - 2 = 6 \text{ ft}$.
Perimeter = $8 + 8 + 2 + 5 + 3 + 6 = 32 \text{ ft}$.

Set 8: Circumference (Based on Problem 25)

22. A circular track has a diameter of 100 m. Find the circumference of the track to the nearest ten meters. (Use 3.14 for $\pi$)
$C = \pi \times d \approx 3.14 \times 100 = 314 \text{ m}$.
Rounded to the nearest ten, 314 becomes 310 m.
23. A circular plaza has a diameter of 300 feet. What is the circumference of the plaza to the nearest ten feet? (Use 3.14 for $\pi$)
$C = 3.14 \times 300 = 942 \text{ ft}$.
Rounded to the nearest ten feet, it is 940 ft.
24. A large circular garden has a diameter of 50 m. Find the circumference to the nearest ten meters. (Use 3.14 for $\pi$)
$C = 3.14 \times 50 = 157 \text{ m}$.
Rounded to the nearest ten, the 7 rounds the 5 up to a 6, making it 160 m.

Set 9: Operations with Positive and Negative Integers (Based on Problem 29)

25. Evaluate: $$-5 + (-3) - (-8) - (+2)$$
Simplify the signs first: $-5 - 3 + 8 - 2$
Combine left to right: $-8 + 8 - 2 = 0 - 2 = -2$.
26. Evaluate: $$-10 + (-4) - (-5) - (+1)$$
Simplify the signs: $-10 - 4 + 5 - 1$
Combine left to right: $-14 + 5 - 1 = -9 - 1 = -10$.
27. Evaluate: $$-2 + (-6) - (-10) - (+4)$$
Simplify the signs: $-2 - 6 + 10 - 4$
Combine left to right: $-8 + 10 - 4 = 2 - 4 = -2$.
