Question 10

Conclude: Which street is parallel to Elm?

Answer: Oak Street

Parallel lines run in the same direction and never intersect. Elm Street and Oak Street both run horizontally from East to West and never cross each other.

Question 11

a. How many dimes equal one dollar?

b. One dime is what fraction of a dollar?

c. Nine dimes are what fraction of a dollar?

Answers:
  • a. 10 dimes equal one dollar ($10 \times \$0.10 = \$1.00$).
  • b. One dime is $\frac{1}{10}$ of a dollar.
  • c. Nine dimes are $\frac{9}{10}$ of a dollar.
Question 12

Represent: Draw a rectangle that is $5\text{ cm}$ long and $2\text{ cm}$ wide. What is the perimeter?

Answer: $14\text{ cm}$

Perimeter is the total distance around the outside edge:

$$\text{Perimeter} = 5\text{ cm} + 2\text{ cm} + 5\text{ cm} + 2\text{ cm} = 14\text{ cm}$$
Question 13

Describe each type of angle shown below:

Answers:

  • b. Obtuse angle: Open wider than a right angle (greater than $90^\circ$ and less than $180^\circ$).

Question 26

Multiple Choice: Which point on this number line could represent $-3$?

A. point $w$     B. point $x$     C. point $y$     D. point $z$

Answer: B (point x)

$-3$ is a negative number, so it lies to the left of $0$. Since $-3$ is between $0$ and $-10$, point $x$ is the correct point.

Question 27

Explain: How is a segment different from a line?

Answer:

A line segment has two distinct endpoints and a measurable, fixed length. A line extends infinitely in opposite directions without endpoints.

Question 28

Estimate: Concert tickets cost $\$18$ each, not including a $\$4.25$ transaction fee for each ticket. What is a reasonable estimate of the cost to purchase two concert tickets? Explain why your estimate is reasonable.

Answer: About $\$44$ (or around $\$40 - \$45$)

Reasonable Explanation:

  • Round $\$18$ to $\$20$, and round $\$4.25$ to $\$4$.
  • Estimated cost for 1 ticket + fee = $\$20 + \$4 = \$24$.
  • For 2 tickets: $2 \times \$24 = \$48$ (or rounding total per ticket $\$22.25 \approx \$22$, $2 \times \$22 = \$44$).
Question 29

The thermometer shows the high temperature on an April day in Nashville, Tennessee. What was the high temperature that day?

Answer: $60^\circ\text{F}$

The dark column of liquid rises directly up to the line marked $60$ on the Fahrenheit scale.

Question 30

Formulate: Write and solve an addition word problem that has a sum of $43$.

Sample Answer:

Problem: Maya collected $25$ seashells on Saturday and $18$ seashells on Sunday. How many total seashells did Maya collect?

Solution:

$$25 + 18 = 43\text{ seashells}$$
