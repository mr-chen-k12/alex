1.(28)
EXPLAIN During the first six months of the year, the Henderson family’s monthly water bills were $68.50, $72.10, $65.40, $78.20, $69.80, and $72.00. How can the Hendersons find their average monthly water bill for the first six months of the year?
Answer
\(\text{Add the six bills and divide by }6\text{ — the average is }\$71.00\)
Solution
To find the average (mean), add all the monthly bills and divide the total by 6: \(68.50 + 72.10 + 65.40 + 78.20 + 69.80 + 72.00 = \$426.00\). Then divide by 6: \(426.00 \div 6 = \$71.00\).
2.(23, 30)
There were \(3\frac{1}{4}\) gallons of juice in the refrigerator before a party. Afterward, there were \(1\frac{5}{6}\) gallons remaining. How many gallons of juice were consumed during the party?
Answer
\(1\frac{5}{12}\text{ gallons}\)
Solution
Subtract the remaining amount from the starting amount: \(3\frac{1}{4} - 1\frac{5}{6}\). Use the common denominator 12: \(3\frac{3}{12} - 1\frac{10}{12}\). Rename \(3\frac{3}{12}\) as \(2\frac{15}{12}\). Then \(2\frac{15}{12} - 1\frac{10}{12} = 1\frac{5}{12}\) gallons.
3.(28)
A one-year subscription to a monthly magazine costs $21.60. The regular newsstand price is $2.25 per issue. How much is saved per issue by paying the subscription price?
Answer
\(\$0.45\text{ per issue}\)
Solution
A monthly subscription includes 12 issues per year. Cost per subscription issue: \(21.60 \div 12 = \$1.80\). Savings per issue: \(2.25 - 1.80 = \$0.45\).

5.(19)
The perimeter of a square equals the perimeter of a regular hexagon. Each side of the hexagon is 10 cm long. How long is each side of the square?
Answer
\(15\text{ cm}\)
Solution
The regular hexagon has 6 equal sides, so its perimeter is \(6 \times 10 = 60\) cm. Since the square has 4 equal sides and the same perimeter: \(60 \div 4 = 15\) cm per side.
6.(22)
JUSTIFY Only \(\frac{2}{15}\) of the 120 students in a middle school grade participate in the chess club.
a. How many students participate in the chess club?
b. How many students do not participate in the chess club?
Answer
\(\text{a. }16 \qquad \text{b. }104\)
Solution
a. \(\frac{1}{15}\) of 120 is \(120 \div 15 = 8\). So \(\frac{2}{15}\) is \(2 \times 8 = 16\) students. b. The number of non-participants is \(120 - 16 = 104\) students.

30.(17)
Use a protractor to draw a triangle that has two 60° angles. What type of triangle is formed?
Answer
\(\text{An equilateral (or equiangular) triangle}\)
Solution
The angles of any triangle sum to 180°. If two angles are 60°, the third angle must be \(180^\circ - 60^\circ - 60^\circ = 60^\circ\). Since all three interior angles are equal, it forms an equilateral triangle.

Marcus buys school supplies at a store. He estimates the cost of each item by rounding to the nearest dollar. The items are a notebook ($3.89), pens ($2.15), a backpack ($14.95), a calculator ($8.20), and a folder ($0.75).
a. How much does he estimate he will spend on supplies?
b. How does his estimate compare to the actual cost at checkout?
c. How can Marcus change his estimation strategy to ensure he never exceeds his budget?
Answer
\(\text{a. }\$30 \qquad \text{b. Actual is }\$29.94\text{ (estimate is }\$0.06\text{ higher)} \qquad \text{c. Always round up}\)
Solution
a. Rounding each item to the nearest whole dollar: \(4 + 2 + 15 + 8 + 1 = \$30\). b. Actual total: \(3.89 + 2.15 + 14.95 + 8.20 + 0.75 = \$29.94\). His estimate is $0.06 higher than the exact total. c. To ensure he never overspends, Marcus can always round every price UP to the next whole dollar.
32.(Extension)
TERMINATING DECIMAL Divide the decimal numbers: \(18.9 \div 0.28\)
Answer
\(67.5\)
Solution
Shift the decimal point two places to the right for both divisor and dividend to divide by a whole number: \(18.9 \div 0.28 = 1890 \div 28\). Performing the long division: \(1890 \div 28 = 67.5\). The division terminates at the tenths place with zero remainder.
33.(Extension)
REPEATING DECIMAL Divide the decimal numbers and write the quotient with a bar over the repeating digit: \(8.5 \div 0.3\)
Answer
\(28.\overline{3}\)
Solution
Shift decimal points one place to the right: \(8.5 \div 0.3 = 85 \div 3\). Long division gives \(85 \div 3 = 28.333...\). The digit 3 repeats infinitely, so write it with a repeating bar: \(28.\overline{3}\).

35.(Extension)
ORDER OF OPERATIONS Simplify using order of operations (PEMDAS):
$$\left( -\frac{3}{4} + 1.25 \right)^2 \cdot (-2)^3 - 3.6 \div (-0.9)$$
Answer
\(2\)
Solution
Step-by-step simplification:
1. Parentheses: \(-\frac{3}{4} + 1.25 = -0.75 + 1.25 = 0.5\).
2. Exponents: \((0.5)^2 = 0.25\) and \((-2)^3 = -8\).
3. Multiplication: \(0.25 \cdot (-8) = -2\).
4. Division: \(3.6 \div (-0.9) = -4\).
5. Subtraction: \(-2 - (-4) = -2 + 4 = 2\).
