Lesson Practice

a. Change the fractions to decimals to compare: \(\frac{3}{20} \bigcirc \frac{1}{8}\)

Step 1: Convert \(\frac{3}{20}\) to a decimal.
Multiply numerator and denominator by 5: \(\frac{3 \times 5}{20 \times 5} = \frac{15}{100} = 0.15\)

Step 2: Convert \(\frac{1}{8}\) to a decimal.
Divide 1 by 8: \(1 \div 8 = 0.125\)

Step 3: Compare.
\(0.150 > 0.125\)

Answer: \(\frac{3}{20} > \frac{1}{8}\)

b. Change the fractions to decimals to compare: \(\frac{3}{8} \bigcirc \frac{2}{5}\)

Step 1: Convert \(\frac{3}{8}\) to a decimal.
\(3 \div 8 = 0.375\)

Step 2: Convert \(\frac{2}{5}\) to a decimal.
\(\frac{2 \times 2}{5 \times 2} = \frac{4}{10} = 0.4\)

Step 3: Compare.
\(0.375 < 0.400\)

Answer: \(\frac{3}{8} < \frac{2}{5}\)

c. Change the fractions to decimals to compare: \(\frac{15}{25} \bigcirc \frac{3}{5}\)

Step 1: Simplify \(\frac{15}{25}\).
Divide top and bottom by 5: \(\frac{3}{5}\).

Step 2: Compare.
Since \(\frac{3}{5}\) is equal to \(\frac{3}{5}\), the decimals will also be equal (\(0.6 = 0.6\)).

Answer: \(\frac{15}{25} = \frac{3}{5}\)

d. Compare: \(0.7 \bigcirc \frac{4}{5}\)

Step 1: Convert \(\frac{4}{5}\) to a decimal.
\(\frac{4 \times 2}{5 \times 2} = \frac{8}{10} = 0.8\)

Step 2: Compare.
\(0.7 < 0.8\)

Answer: \(0.7 < \frac{4}{5}\)

e. Compare: \(\frac{2}{5} \bigcirc 0.5\)

Step 1: Convert \(\frac{2}{5}\) to a decimal.
\(\frac{2 \times 2}{5 \times 2} = \frac{4}{10} = 0.4\)

Step 2: Compare.
\(0.4 < 0.5\)

Answer: \(\frac{2}{5} < 0.5\)

f. Compare: \(\frac{3}{8} \bigcirc 0.325\)

Step 1: Convert \(\frac{3}{8}\) to a decimal.
\(3 \div 8 = 0.375\)

Step 2: Compare.
\(0.375 > 0.325\)

Answer: \(\frac{3}{8} > 0.325\)

Written Practice

1. (Connect) What is the product of ten squared and two cubed?

Step 1: Calculate ten squared.
\(10^2 = 10 \times 10 = 100\)

Step 2: Calculate two cubed.
\(2^3 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 = 8\)

Step 3: Find the product.
Product means multiply: \(100 \times 8 = 800\)

Answer: 800

2. (Connect) What number is halfway between 4.5 and 6.7?

Step 1: Find the average (mean).
Add the two numbers and divide by 2.

\(4.5 + 6.7 = 11.2\)

\(11.2 \div 2 = 5.6\)

Answer: 5.6

3. (Formulate) It is said that one year of a dog's life is the same as 7 years of a human's life. Using that thinking, a dog that is 13 years old is how many "human" years old? Write an equation and solve.

Step 1: Write the equation.
Let \(d\) be dog years and \(h\) be human years. Since 1 dog year = 7 human years:

\(h = 7 \times d\)

Step 2: Solve for 13 years.
\(h = 7 \times 13\)
\(h = 91\)

Answer: 91 human years old

4. Compare. First convert each fraction to decimal form: \(\frac{2}{5} \bigcirc \frac{1}{4}\)

Step 2: Convert \(\frac{1}{4}\).
\(\frac{1}{4} = 0.25\)

Step 3: Compare.

Answer: \(\frac{2}{5} > \frac{1}{4}\)

9. (Verify) Instead of dividing 200 by 18, Sam found half of each number and then divided. Show Sam's division problem and write the quotient as a mixed number.

Step 1: Find half of each number.
Half of 200 is 100.
Half of 18 is 9.

Step 2: Set up the division.
\(100 \div 9\)

Step 3: Solve.
9 goes into 100 eleven times (99), with a remainder of 1.

Answer: \(11 \frac{1}{9}\)

10. Calculate: \(6 \frac{1}{3} + 3 \frac{1}{4} + 2 \frac{1}{2}\)

Step 1: Find Common Denominator.
The denominators are 3, 4, and 2. The Least Common Multiple is 12.

\(6 \frac{4}{12} + 3 \frac{3}{12} + 2 \frac{6}{12}\)

Step 2: Add whole numbers and fractions separately.
Whole: \(6 + 3 + 2 = 11\)
Fractions: \(\frac{4+3+6}{12} = \frac{13}{12}\)

Step 3: Convert improper fraction.
\(\frac{13}{12} = 1 \frac{1}{12}\)

Step 4: Combine.
\(11 + 1 \frac{1}{12} = 12 \frac{1}{12}\)

Answer: \(12 \frac{1}{12}\)

11. Solve for the missing number: \(\frac{4}{5} = \frac{?}{100}\)

Step 1: Determine the multiplier.
How many times does 5 go into 100? \(100 \div 5 = 20\).

Step 2: Multiply the numerator by the same amount.
\(4 \times 20 = 80\).

Answer: 80

12. (Analyze) \((2\frac{1}{2})(3\frac{1}{3})(1\frac{1}{5})\)

Step 1: Convert to improper fractions.
\(2\frac{1}{2} = \frac{5}{2}\)
\(3\frac{1}{3} = \frac{10}{3}\)
\(1\frac{1}{5} = \frac{6}{5}\)

Step 2: Multiply.
\(\frac{5}{2} \times \frac{10}{3} \times \frac{6}{5}\)

Step 3: Simplify/Cancel.
The 5 on top and 5 on bottom cancel.
\(\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{10}{3} \times \frac{6}{1}\)
\(10 \times 6 = 60\) on top.
\(2 \times 3 = 6\) on bottom.
\(\frac{60}{6} = 10\)

Answer: 10

\(\frac{10}{5} = 2\)

Answer: 2

23. (Analyze) Angles T and R are complementary. If the measure of \(\angle R\) is \(53^{\circ}\), then what is the measure of \(\angle T\)?

Step 1: Understand Complementary.
Complementary angles sum to \(90^{\circ}\).

Step 2: Solve.
\(\angle T + 53 = 90\)
\(\angle T = 90 - 53\)

Answer: \(37^{\circ}\)

25. This parallelogram is divided into two congruent triangles.
(Base = 20 cm, Height = 10 cm, Slant height of triangle side = 12 cm)
a. What is the area of the parallelogram?
b. What is the area of one of the triangles?

a. Area of Parallelogram:
Area = base \(\times\) height
\(A = 20 \times 10 = 200\)

Answer (a): 200 sq cm

b. Area of Triangle:
The parallelogram is two congruent triangles, so divide the total area by 2.

\(200 \div 2 = 100\)

27. (Represent) Sketch a coordinate plane. Graph point A (1, 2), point B (-3, -2), and point C (1, -2). Draw segments to connect them. What type of polygon is figure ABC?

Step 1: Plotting.
Point C (1, -2) and Point A (1, 2) form a vertical line.
Point C (1, -2) and Point B (-3, -2) form a horizontal line.

Step 2: Identify Polygon.
Connecting 3 points forms a triangle. Because one line is vertical and one is horizontal, they meet at a \(90^{\circ}\) angle.

Answer: Right Triangle

30. (Model) Draw a pair of parallel lines. Draw a third line perpendicular to the parallel lines. Complete a quadrilateral by drawing a fourth line that intersects but is not perpendicular. Trace the quadrilateral formed. Is the quadrilateral a rectangle?

Step 1: Visualize.
Two parallel sides. One side perpendicular (90 degrees). The fourth side is slanted (not perpendicular).

Step 2: Identify Shape.
A quadrilateral with exactly one pair of parallel sides is a trapezoid. Because one leg is perpendicular to the bases, it is a "right trapezoid".

Step 3: Answer Question.
A rectangle requires 4 right angles. The fourth line is not perpendicular, so it does not create right angles.

Answer: No, it is a trapezoid.
