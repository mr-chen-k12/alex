Math Practice Problems (1 - 30)

1. Mavis scored 12 of the team's 20 points. What percent of the team's points did Mavis score?
Answer: 60%
Explanation: To find the percent, write the ratio of Mavis's points to the total points as a fraction: \( \frac{12}{20} \). Convert this fraction to have a denominator of 100 by multiplying the numerator and denominator by 5: \( \frac{12 \times 5}{20 \times 5} = \frac{60}{100} \). Therefore, she scored 60%.
2. One fourth of an inch of snow fell every hour during the storm. How many hours did the storm last if the total accumulation of snow was 4 inches?
Answer: 16 hours
Explanation: Divide the total accumulation by the rate of snowfall per hour: \( 4 \div \frac{1}{4} \). To divide by a fraction, multiply by its reciprocal: \( 4 \times \frac{4}{1} = 16 \).
3. Eamon wants to buy a new baseball glove that costs $50. He has $14 and he earns $6 per hour cleaning yards. How many hours must he work to have enough money to buy the glove?
Answer: 6 hours
Explanation: First, find out how much more money Eamon needs: \( $50 - $14 = $36 \). Then, divide the remaining amount by his hourly wage: \( $36 \div $6/\text{hour} = 6 \text{ hours} \).
4. Find the area of this triangle.
Answer: 64 mm²
Explanation: The formula for the area of a triangle is \( A = \frac{1}{2}bh \). The base (\(b\)) is 16 mm and the perpendicular height (\(h\)) is 8 mm. (The 10 mm side is extraneous information for finding the area).
\( A = \frac{1}{2} \times 16 \times 8 = 8 \times 8 = 64 \text{ mm}^2 \).

6. What is the volume of this rectangular prism?
Answer: 480 in.³
Explanation: The formula for the volume of a rectangular prism is \( V = l \times w \times h \).
\( V = 10 \text{ in.} \times 6 \text{ in.} \times 8 \text{ in.} = 480 \text{ in.}^3 \).
7. Find the unknown factor: \( 10w = 25 \)
Answer: \( w = 2.5 \) (or \( 2\frac{1}{2} \))
Explanation: Divide both sides of the equation by 10 to isolate \(w\).
\( w = \frac{25}{10} \)
\( w = 2.5 \)
8. Find the unknown factor: \( 20 = 9m \)
Answer: \( m = \frac{20}{9} \) (or \( 2\frac{2}{9} \))
Explanation: Divide both sides of the equation by 9 to isolate \(m\).
\( m = \frac{20}{9} \)
9. a. What is the perimeter of this triangle?
b. What is the area of this triangle?
Answer: a. 24 in.  |  b. 24 in.²
Explanation:
a. Perimeter: Add all the side lengths together. \( P = 6 + 8 + 10 = 24 \text{ in.} \)
b. Area: Use the formula \( A = \frac{1}{2}bh \). For a right triangle, the legs are the base and height. \( A = \frac{1}{2} \times 8 \times 6 = 24 \text{ in.}^2 \).
10. Write 5% as a:
A decimal number.
B fraction.
Answer: A. 0.05  |  B. \(\frac{1}{20}\)
Explanation:
A. To convert a percentage to a decimal, divide by 100 (move the decimal point two places to the left): \( 5\% = 0.05 \).
B. To convert a percentage to a fraction, put it over 100 and simplify: \( \frac{5}{100} = \frac{1}{20} \).
11. Write \( \frac{2}{5} \) as a decimal, and multiply it by 2.5. What is the product?
Answer: 1.0
Explanation: First, convert \( \frac{2}{5} \) to a decimal by dividing: \( 2 \div 5 = 0.4 \).
Next, multiply by 2.5: \( 0.4 \times 2.5 = 1.00 \). The product is 1.
12. Compare: \( \frac{2}{3} + \frac{3}{2} \bigcirc \frac{2}{3} \cdot \frac{3}{2} \)
Answer: >
Explanation: Evaluate both sides.
Left side (addition): Find a common denominator (6). \( \frac{4}{6} + \frac{9}{6} = \frac{13}{6} \), which is \( 2\frac{1}{6} \).
Right side (multiplication): Multiply across. \( \frac{2 \times 3}{3 \times 2} = \frac{6}{6} = 1 \).
Since \( 2\frac{1}{6} > 1 \), the correct symbol is \( > \).
13. \( \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{100}{1} \)
Answer: \( \frac{100}{3} \) or \( 33\frac{1}{3} \)
Explanation: Multiply the numerators and denominators straight across: \( \frac{1 \times 100}{3 \times 1} = \frac{100}{3} \). As a mixed number, this is \( 33\frac{1}{3} \).
14. \( 6 \div 1\frac{1}{2} \)
Answer: 4
Explanation: Convert the mixed number to an improper fraction: \( 1\frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{2} \).
Rewrite the division as multiplying by the reciprocal: \( 6 \times \frac{2}{3} \).
\( \frac{12}{3} = 4 \).
15. \( 12 \div 0.25 \)
Answer: 48
Explanation: You can divide with decimals by shifting the point: \( 1200 \div 25 = 48 \).
Alternatively, recognize that 0.25 is \( \frac{1}{4} \). How many quarters are in 12? \( 12 \times 4 = 48 \).
16. \( 0.025 \times 100 \)
Answer: 2.5
Explanation: To multiply a decimal by 100, move the decimal point two places to the right. \( 0.025 \rightarrow 2.5 \).
17. If the tax rate is 7%, what is the tax on a $24.90 purchase?
Answer: $1.74
Explanation: Convert 7% to a decimal (0.07). Multiply the purchase amount by the tax rate: \( $24.90 \times 0.07 = 1.743 \). Since this is money, round to the nearest cent, resulting in $1.74.
18. The prime factorization of what number is \( 2^2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5^2 \)?
Answer: 900
Explanation: Calculate the value of each exponent and multiply them together.
\( 2^2 = 4 \)
\( 3^2 = 9 \)
\( 5^2 = 25 \)
\( 4 \times 9 \times 25 = 36 \times 25 = 900 \). (Easier way: \( 4 \times 25 = 100 \), \( 100 \times 9 = 900 \)).
19. Which of these is a composite number?
A 61    B 71    C 81    D 101
Answer: C 81
Explanation: A composite number has factors other than 1 and itself. 81 is divisible by 9 (\( 9 \times 9 = 81 \)), so it is composite. 61, 71, and 101 are prime numbers.
20. Round the decimal number one and twenty-three hundredths to the nearest tenth.
Answer: 1.2
Explanation: One and twenty-three hundredths is written as 1.23. The digit in the tenths place is 2. The digit to its right (hundredths place) is 3. Since 3 is less than 5, we keep the 2 and drop the following digits. The result is 1.2.
21. Albert baked 5 dozen muffins and gave away \( \frac{7}{12} \) of them. How many muffins were left?
Answer: 25 muffins
Explanation: First, find the total number of muffins. 1 dozen = 12, so 5 dozen = \( 5 \times 12 = 60 \) muffins.
If he gave away \( \frac{7}{12} \), he kept \( 1 - \frac{7}{12} = \frac{5}{12} \).
Multiply the fraction kept by the total: \( \frac{5}{12} \times 60 = 5 \times 5 = 25 \).
22. \( 6 \times 3 - 6 \div 3 \)
Answer: 16
Explanation: According to the order of operations (PEMDAS/BODMAS), perform multiplication and division before subtraction.
\( (6 \times 3) - (6 \div 3) \)
\( 18 - 2 = 16 \).
23. How many milliliters is 4 liters?
Answer: 4000 milliliters
Explanation: There are 1,000 milliliters in 1 liter. Therefore, \( 4 \text{ liters} \times 1000 \text{ mL/L} = 4000 \text{ mL} \).
24. Draw a line segment \( 2\frac{1}{4} \) inches long. Label the endpoints \(A\) and \(C\). Then make a dot at the midpoint of \( \overline{AC} \) (the point halfway between points \(A\) and \(C\)), and label the dot \(B\). What are the lengths of \( \overline{AB} \) and \( \overline{BC} \)?
Answer: \( 1\frac{1}{8} \) inches each
Explanation: To find the midpoint, divide the total length by 2.
\( 2\frac{1}{4} \div 2 = \frac{9}{4} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{9}{8} \).
Convert the improper fraction back to a mixed number: \( \frac{9}{8} = 1\frac{1}{8} \). Therefore, both \( \overline{AB} \) and \( \overline{BC} \) are \( 1\frac{1}{8} \) inches long.
25. On a coordinate plane draw a rectangle with vertices at (-2, -2), (4, -2), (4, 2), and (-2, 2). What is the area of the rectangle?
Answer: 24 square units
Explanation: Find the length and width of the rectangle by calculating the distance between the coordinates.
Length (x-axis distance): From -2 to 4 is \( |4 - (-2)| = 6 \) units.
Width (y-axis distance): From -2 to 2 is \( |2 - (-2)| = 4 \) units.
Area = length \( \times \) width = \( 6 \times 4 = 24 \).
26. What is the ratio of the length to the width of the rectangle in problem 25?
Answer: 3:2
Explanation: The length is 6 units and the width is 4 units. The ratio of length to width is 6:4. Dividing both sides by 2 to simplify gives 3:2.
27. How do you calculate the area of a triangle?
Answer: Multiply the base by the height, then divide by 2.
Explanation: The standard formula is \( A = \frac{1}{2}bh \), where \(b\) is the length of the base and \(h\) is the perpendicular height.
28. In the figure at right, angles \(ADB\) and \(BDC\) are supplementary.
a. What is \(m\angle ADB\)?
b. What is the ratio of \(m\angle BDC\) to \(m\angle ADB\)? Write the answer as a reduced fraction.
Answer: a. 135°  |  b. \( \frac{1}{3} \)
Explanation:
a. Supplementary angles add up to 180°. Since \(m\angle BDC\) is 45°, \(m\angle ADB = 180° - 45° = 135°\).
b. The ratio is \( \frac{45}{135} \). Both numbers are divisible by 45. \( \frac{45 \div 45}{135 \div 45} = \frac{1}{3} \).
29. Nathan drew a circle with a radius of 10 cm. Then he drew a square around the circle.
a. What was the area of the square?
b. What was the area of the circle? (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\).)
Answer: a. 400 cm²  |  b. 314 cm²
Explanation:
a. Area of Square: The diameter of the circle spans the entire width/height of the square. Diameter = \( 2 \times 10 = 20 \text{ cm} \). So the square has a side length of 20 cm. Area = \( 20 \times 20 = 400 \text{ cm}^2 \).
b. Area of Circle: The formula is \( A = \pi r^2 \). Using 3.14 for \(\pi\): \( A = 3.14 \times 10^2 = 3.14 \times 100 = 314 \text{ cm}^2 \).
30. Write a word problem that can be solved using the proportion \( \frac{6}{8} = \frac{w}{100} \). Solve the problem.
Answer: w = 75
Explanation:
Example Word Problem: "If 6 out of 8 students prefer pizza over hamburgers, how many students out of a group of 100 would you expect to prefer pizza?"

Solve:
\( \frac{6}{8} = \frac{w}{100} \)
Cross-multiply: \( 8w = 600 \)
Divide by 8: \( w = \frac{600}{8} = 75 \).
