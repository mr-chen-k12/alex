1.(28)
Five volunteers collected cans to be recycled. The number they collected were: 236, 244, 252, 218, and 260. What was the average number of cans collected by the volunteers?
Answer
\(242\text{ cans}\)
Solution
Add: \(236+244+252+218+260=1210\). Divide by 5: \(\dfrac{1210}{5}=242\).

5.(22)
Tomas ran at his fastest pace for 60% of the 3000-meter race.
a. Tomas ran at his fastest pace for how many meters?
b. Tomas did not run at his fastest pace for how many meters?
Answer
\(\text{a. }1800\text{ m}\quad\text{b. }1200\text{ m}\)
Solution
\(60\%=\dfrac{3}{5}\).
a. \(\dfrac{3}{5}\times 3000=1800\) meters.
b. \(3000-1800=1200\) meters.
6.(19, 20)
This rectangle is three times as long as it is wide.
a. What is the perimeter of the rectangle?
b. What is the area of the rectangle?
Answer
\(\text{a. }32\text{ in.}\quad\text{b. }48\text{ in.}^2\)
Solution
The 12 in. side is the length. Width \(=12\div 3=4\) in.
a. Perimeter \(=2(12+4)=32\) in.
b. Area \(=12\times 4=48\) in.\(^2\).

For a and b, find an equivalent fraction that has a denominator of 36.

Solution

34.(new)
Evaluate using the order of operations: \((-2)^2-3\times 0.4+\dfrac15-(-1)\)
Answer
\(4\)
Solution
Exponent first: \((-2)^2=4\). Then multiplication: \(3\times 0.4=1.2\). Now left to right: \(4-1.2=2.8\); \(2.8+\dfrac15=2.8+0.2=3.0\); \(3.0-(-1)=3.0+1=4\).
