Comprehensive Math Practice

Word Problems & Proportions

1. A train travels 450 miles in 6 hours. What is its average speed in miles per hour?

Speed = Distance ÷ Time.

\( \frac{450 \text{ miles}}{6 \text{ hours}} = 75 \text{ mph} \)

Answer: 75 mph

2. The ratio of cats to dogs at the shelter is 5 to 3. If there are 24 dogs, how many cats are there?

Set up a proportion: \(\frac{5 \text{ cats}}{3 \text{ dogs}} = \frac{x \text{ cats}}{24 \text{ dogs}}\)

Cross multiply: \(3x = 120\), so \(x = 40\).

Answer: 40 cats

3. A jacket originally priced at $45.00 is on sale for 20% off. What is the sale price?

Calculate the discount: 20% of $45 = \(0.20 \times 45 = \$9.00\).

Subtract from original: \(\$45.00 - \$9.00 = \$36.00\).

Answer: $36.00

4. If the sales-tax rate is 7%, what is the total cost of a $32.00 purchase?

Calculate tax: \(0.07 \times 32 = \$2.24\).

Add to original: \(\$32.00 + \$2.24 = \$34.24\).

Answer: $34.24

5. John cut \(3\frac{1}{4}\) inches off a piece of wood that was \(15\frac{1}{2}\) inches long. How long is the piece of wood now?

Find a common denominator: \(15\frac{2}{4} - 3\frac{1}{4}\).

Subtract: \(15 - 3 = 12\) and \(\frac{2}{4} - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{4}\).

Answer: \(12\frac{1}{4}\) inches

Fractions, Decimals, & Percents

6. Simplify: \(\frac{2}{3} \cdot \frac{9}{10} \cdot \frac{5}{6}\)

Multiply straight across or cross-cancel first:

\( \frac{2 \cdot 9 \cdot 5}{3 \cdot 10 \cdot 6} = \frac{90}{180} = \frac{1}{2} \)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{2}\)

7. Evaluate: \(4\frac{1}{5} \div 1\frac{2}{5}\)

Convert to improper fractions: \(\frac{21}{5} \div \frac{7}{5}\).

Multiply by the reciprocal: \(\frac{21}{5} \cdot \frac{5}{7} = \frac{21}{7} = 3\).

Answer: 3

8. Add: \(2.45 + 13.2 + 0.08\)

Line up the decimals: \(2.45 + 13.20 + 0.08 = 15.73\).

Answer: 15.73

9. Multiply: \(0.15 \times 0.4\)

Multiply like whole numbers (\(15 \times 4 = 60\)), then count 3 total decimal places.

Answer: 0.06

10. What decimal number is equivalent to \(\frac{7}{20}\)?

Multiply numerator and denominator by 5 to get a base of 100: \(\frac{35}{100} = 0.35\).

Answer: 0.35

11. What percent is equivalent to \(\frac{3}{8}\)?

Divide 3 by 8 to get 0.375. Move the decimal two places to the right.

Answer: 37.5%

12. Write 55% as a reduced fraction.

Write as a fraction over 100: \(\frac{55}{100}\).

Divide both by 5: \(\frac{11}{20}\).

Answer: \(\frac{11}{20}\)

Geometry

13. What is the area of a triangle with a base of 12 cm and a height of 8 cm?

Area = \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot \text{base} \cdot \text{height}\)

\(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 12 \cdot 8 = 6 \cdot 8 = 48\)

Answer: 48 cm²

14. The radius of a circle is 7 inches. What is the circumference? (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\))

Circumference = \(2 \cdot \pi \cdot r\)

\(C = 2 \cdot 3.14 \cdot 7 = 14 \cdot 3.14 = 43.96\)

Answer: 43.96 inches

15. What is the area of that same circle (radius = 7 inches)?

Area = \(\pi \cdot r^2\)

\(A = 3.14 \cdot (7^2) = 3.14 \cdot 49 = 153.86\)

Answer: 153.86 square inches

16. A rectangular prism has a length of 5 m, a width of 4 m, and a height of 3 m. What is its volume?

Volume = \(\text{length} \cdot \text{width} \cdot \text{height}\)

\(V = 5 \cdot 4 \cdot 3 = 60\)

Answer: 60 m³

17. If the perimeter of a regular hexagon is 42 cm, what is the length of each side?

A regular hexagon has 6 equal sides.

\(42 \div 6 = 7\)

Answer: 7 cm

18. What is the area of a parallelogram with a base of 15 feet and a height of 10 feet?

Area = \(\text{base} \cdot \text{height}\)

\(15 \cdot 10 = 150\)

Answer: 150 square feet

19. What is the area of a quadrilateral with the vertices (0, 0), (5, 0), (5, 4), and (0, 4)?

These coordinates form a rectangle with a length of 5 and a height of 4.

\(5 \cdot 4 = 20\)

Answer: 20 square units

20. VERIFY: "All squares are rectangles." True or false? Why?

A rectangle is defined as a quadrilateral with four right angles. Because a square also has four right angles, it fits the definition of a rectangle.

Answer: True

Unit Conversions & Time

21. Simplify: 3 yd + 2 ft (Write the sum in feet)

Convert yards to feet: \(3 \text{ yd} = 9 \text{ ft}\).

\(9 \text{ ft} + 2 \text{ ft} = 11 \text{ ft}\)

Answer: 11 ft

22. Convert 4.5 kilometers to meters.

There are 1000 meters in 1 kilometer.

\(4.5 \cdot 1000 = 4500\)

Answer: 4500 m

23. How many pints are in 3 gallons?

1 gallon = 4 quarts = 8 pints.

\(3 \cdot 8 = 24\)

Answer: 24 pints

24. What time is three-and-a-half hours after 11:15 a.m.?

Add 3 hours: 11:15 am → 2:15 pm.

Add 30 minutes: 2:15 pm → 2:45 pm.

Answer: 2:45 p.m.

25. Simplify the rate: \(\frac{500 \text{ miles}}{10 \text{ gallons}}\)

Divide numerator and denominator by 10.

Answer: 50 miles per gallon (mpg)

Number Theory & Pre-Algebra

26. Write the prime factorization of 72.

Divide by primes: \(72 = 8 \cdot 9 = (2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2) \cdot (3 \cdot 3)\).

Answer: \(2^3 \cdot 3^2\)

27. Arrange these numbers in order from least to greatest: \(\frac{1}{3}\), \(0.3\), \(0.33\), \(\frac{3}{10}\)

Convert to decimals: \(\frac{1}{3} \approx 0.333...\), \(0.3\), \(0.33\), \(\frac{3}{10} = 0.3\).

Note that \(0.3\) and \(\frac{3}{10}\) are equal.

Answer: \(0.3 = \frac{3}{10} < 0.33 < \frac{1}{3}\)

28. Simplify: \(10 - (2 \times 3) + 4\)

Order of operations (PEMDAS): multiply first.

\(10 - 6 + 4 \rightarrow 4 + 4 = 8\).

Answer: 8

29. Evaluate \(x^2 - 2x\) when \(x = 5\).

Substitute 5 for x: \((5)^2 - 2(5)\).

\(25 - 10 = 15\).

Answer: 15

30. Find the lowest common multiple (LCM) of 12 and 15.

Multiples of 12: 12, 24, 36, 48, 60...

Multiples of 15: 15, 30, 45, 60...

Answer: 60
