6.Find the least common multiple (LCM) of 12, 15, and 20.

Prime factors: 12 = 2²×3, 15 = 3×5, 20 = 2²×5.

7.Convert 1940 to a percentage.

0.475 × 100 = 47.5%

8.A triangle has a base of 16 cm and an area of 104 cm². What is its height?

Use A = 12bh.

104 = 12(16)h = 8h

Answer: 13 cm

9.Evaluate: (−3)3 + 42 − 6

(−3)3 = −27 and 42 = 16.

−27 + 16 − 6 = −17

10.Solve for y: 5(y + 3) = 3(y + 11)

Distribute: 5y + 15 = 3y + 33

Subtract 3y and 15: 2y = 18

Answer: y = 9

11.A jacket originally costs $320. It is marked down by 25%, and then an additional 10% coupon is applied to the sale price. What is the final price?

After 25% off: 320 × 0.75 = $240

After another 10% off: 240 × 0.90 = $216.00

12.Find the volume of a rectangular prism with length 6.5 m, width 5.4 m, and height 4 m.

V = lwh = 6.5 × 5.4 × 4 = 140.4 m³

18.Find the perimeter of a semicircle with radius 7 cm. Use π ≈ 3.14 and include the straight diameter edge.

31.Use PEMDAS to evaluate: 7.5 − [25 × (3.6 + 1.4)] + 14

Parentheses first: 3.6 + 1.4 = 5.0

Multiply: 25 × 5 = 2

Convert the remaining fraction: 14 = 0.25

Finish left to right: 7.5 − 2 + 0.25 = 5.75
