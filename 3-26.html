Signed Numbers & Integers

1. Find the sum mentally: $-5 + +7$

Answer: $2$
Explanation: The signs are different. Subtract the absolute values ($7 - 5 = 2$) and keep the sign of the larger absolute value (positive).

2. Find the sum mentally: $-6 + -2$

Answer: $-8$
Explanation: The signs are the same, so add their absolute values ($6 + 2 = 8$) and keep the negative sign.

3. Find the sum mentally: $(+8) + (-4)$

Answer: $4$
Explanation: Different signs; subtract absolute values ($8 - 4 = 4$). Keep the positive sign.

4. Find the opposite of the number: $-15$

Answer: $15$
Explanation: The opposite of a negative number is its positive counterpart.

5. Solve using algebraic addition: $-4 - -7$

Answer: $3$
Explanation: Change subtraction to adding the opposite: $-4 + (+7) = 3$.

6. Solve using algebraic addition: $(+5) - (-8)$

Answer: $13$
Explanation: Change subtraction to adding the opposite: $+5 + (+8) = 13$.

Word Problems & Number Sense

7. If $0.8$ is the divisor and $2.5$ is the quotient, what is the dividend?

Answer: $2.0$
Explanation: $\text{Dividend} = \text{Divisor} \times \text{Quotient}$. Therefore, $0.8 \times 2.5 = 2.0$.

8. If a number is fifteen less than sixty, then it is how much more than thirty?

Answer: $15$
Explanation: Find the number first: $60 - 15 = 45$. Then find how much more it is than thirty: $45 - 30 = 15$.

9. If the sum of five numbers is $20.5$, what is the average of the five numbers?

Answer: $4.1$
Explanation: $\text{Average} = \text{Sum} \div \text{Count}$. Therefore, $20.5 \div 5 = 4.1$.

10. Illustrate this problem on a number line: $+4 + -6$

Answer: $-2$
Explanation: Start at $0$. Move $4$ units to the right to reach $+4$. From $+4$, move $6$ units to the left. You land on negative $2$.

11. Fourteen of the 35 students in the class are girls. What is the ratio of boys to girls in the class?

Answer: $3:2$ or $\frac{3}{2}$
Explanation: Find the number of boys: $35 - 14 = 21$. The ratio of boys to girls is $21 \text{ to } 14$, which simplifies to $3:2$ when divided by 7.

12. A bag contains 15 marbles. Six are green and 9 are yellow. If you draw a marble without looking, what is the probability it will be green? Express as a fraction and a decimal.

Answer: Fraction: $\frac{2}{5}$, Decimal: $0.40$
Explanation: Probability = $\frac{6 \text{ green}}{15 \text{ total}}$. Simplify by dividing by 3 to get $\frac{2}{5}$. As a decimal, $2 \div 5 = 0.40$.

Geometry & Measurements

13. What is the measure of the two acute angles in an isosceles right triangle?

Answer: $45^\circ$ each
Explanation: A triangle's angles sum to $180^\circ$. A right angle is $90^\circ$, leaving $90^\circ$ for the other two. Since it is isosceles, the two remaining angles are equal. $90^\circ \div 2 = 45^\circ$.

14. Quadrilateral $PQRS$ is a parallelogram. If angle $P$ measures $80^\circ$, what are the measures of angles $Q, R,$ and $S$?

Answer: Angle $Q = 100^\circ$, Angle $R = 80^\circ$, Angle $S = 100^\circ$
Explanation: Opposite angles are equal ($P = R$). Consecutive angles are supplementary (add up to $180^\circ$), so $180^\circ - 80^\circ = 100^\circ$ for angles $Q$ and $S$.

15. Find the volume of a rectangular prism with a length of 8 in, width of 4 in, and height of 5 in.

Answer: $160 \text{ in}^3$
Explanation: $\text{Volume} = l \times w \times h = 8 \times 4 \times 5 = 160$.

16. If the diameter of a circular rug is 8 feet, then the area of the rug is about how many square feet? Round to the nearest square foot. (Use 3.14 for $\pi$)

Answer: $50 \text{ sq ft}$
Explanation: Radius is half the diameter ($r = 4$). $\text{Area} = \pi r^2 = 3.14 \times 4^2 = 3.14 \times 16 = 50.24$. Rounded to the nearest whole number is $50$.

Rectangle WXYZ is 12 cm long and 5 cm wide. Segment WY (the diagonal) is 13 cm long. Use this information to answer problems 17 and 18.

17. What is the area of triangle WXY?

Answer: $30 \text{ cm}^2$
Explanation: The area of the full rectangle is $12 \times 5 = 60$. The diagonal cuts it in half, so the triangle is $\frac{1}{2}$ the area. Or use formula: $\frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = \frac{1}{2} \times 12 \times 5 = 30$.

18. What is the perimeter of triangle WXY?

Answer: $30 \text{ cm}$
Explanation: Add the lengths of the three sides of the triangle: $12 \text{ (base)} + 5 \text{ (height)} + 13 \text{ (hypotenuse WY)} = 30$.

19. A full two-gallon container of juice was used to fill four one-quart pitchers. How many quarts of juice were left in the two-gallon container?

Answer: $4 \text{ quarts}$
Explanation: $1 \text{ gallon} = 4 \text{ quarts}$, so $2 \text{ gallons} = 8 \text{ quarts}$. If $4 \text{ quarts}$ were used, $8 - 4 = 4 \text{ quarts}$ remaining.

Fractions, Decimals & Percents

20. The fraction $\frac{3}{8}$ is equal to what percent?

Answer: $37.5\%$
Explanation: Convert the fraction to a decimal: $3 \div 8 = 0.375$. Multiply by $100\%$ to get $37.5\%$.

21. If 30% of the students ride the bus, what fraction of the students do NOT ride the bus?

Answer: $\frac{7}{10}$
Explanation: If $30\%$ ride the bus, $70\%$ do not ($100\% - 30\%$). Convert $70\%$ to a fraction: $\frac{70}{100}$, which reduces to $\frac{7}{10}$.

22. Simplify: $\frac{3^3}{3^2}$

Answer: $3$
Explanation: Calculate exponents first: $3^3 = 27$ and $3^2 = 9$. $\frac{27}{9} = 3$. Alternatively, subtract the exponents: $3^{(3-2)} = 3^1 = 3$.

23. Add: $4 \frac{5}{6} + 2 \frac{1}{6}$

Answer: $7$
Explanation: Add whole numbers ($4+2=6$) and fractions ($\frac{5}{6} + \frac{1}{6} = \frac{6}{6} = 1$). Combine them: $6 + 1 = 7$.

24. Divide: $2 \frac{1}{4} \div 1 \frac{1}{2}$

Answer: $1 \frac{1}{2}$ or $\frac{3}{2}$
Explanation: Convert to improper fractions: $\frac{9}{4} \div \frac{3}{2}$. Multiply by the reciprocal: $\frac{9}{4} \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{18}{12}$. Reduce the fraction: $\frac{3}{2} = 1 \frac{1}{2}$.

25. Subtract: $8 - (4.5 + 1.2)$

Answer: $2.3$
Explanation: Do parentheses first: $4.5 + 1.2 = 5.7$. Then subtract from 8: $8.0 - 5.7 = 2.3$.

Algebra & Proportions

26. Analyze and solve: $8^2 + (6^2 - 16) \div \sqrt{25} - 2^3$

Answer: $60$
Explanation: Order of Operations (PEMDAS).
$64 + (36 - 16) \div 5 - 8$
$64 + (20) \div 5 - 8$
$64 + 4 - 8$
$68 - 8 = 60$

27. Solve this proportion: $\frac{8}{14} = \frac{12}{x}$

Answer: $21$
Explanation: Cross-multiply: $8x = 14 \times 12 \Rightarrow 8x = 168 \Rightarrow x = 21$. Alternatively, simplify $\frac{8}{14}$ to $\frac{4}{7}$, then scale up by $3$ to get $\frac{12}{21}$.

28. A spinner is divided into 4 equal sectors labeled 1 through 4. If spun 40 times, about how many times would it be expected to land on a 4?

Answer: $10$ times
Explanation: The probability of landing on a 4 is $\frac{1}{4}$. Multiply this probability by the total number of spins: $40 \times \frac{1}{4} = 10$.
