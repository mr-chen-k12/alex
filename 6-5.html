Problem 1
Gabriel gave the clerk a \$5 bill to pay for a half gallon of milk that cost \$1.06 and a box of cereal that cost \$2.39. How much money should he get back?
Answer: \$1.55
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Find the total combined cost of the milk and cereal:
    $$\$1.06 + \$2.39 = \$3.45$$
  • Subtract the total cost from the \$5 bill to find the remaining change:
    $$\$5.00 - \$3.45 = \$1.55$$

Step-by-Step Explanation:

Problem 11
For each statement, write either "true" or "false."
(a) Every square is also a rectangle.
(b) Every rectangle is also a square.
Answer: (a) true  |  (b) false
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • (a) True: Rectangles simply require four right angles. Squares possess four right angles, meaning they fulfill all geometric requirements of a rectangle.
  • (b) False: Squares strictly require all four side boundaries to be of perfectly identical length, a requirement standard rectangles do not fulfill.
