Lesson 2 Complete Solutions

Written Practice (Problems 1–30)

Problem 1
When the product of 2 and 3 is subtracted from the sum of 4 and 5, what is the difference?
Answer: 3
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Find the sum of 4 and 5:
    $$4 + 5 = 9$$
  • Find the product of 2 and 3:
    $$2 \times 3 = 6$$
  • Subtract the product from the sum:
    $$9 - 6 = 3$$
Problem 2
Write 4 cents twice, once with a dollar sign and once with a cent sign.
Answer: $0.04 and 4¢
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • With a dollar sign, four cents is four hundredths of a dollar: \$0.04
  • With a cent sign, it is simply 4¢.
Problem 3
The sign shown is incorrect. Show two ways to correct the sign.
(Sign: Fruit 0.75¢ per apple)
Answer: $0.75 per apple OR 75¢ per apple
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • "0.75¢" literally means less than one penny (three-quarters of a single cent).
  • To correct it, express it as 75 whole cents (75¢) or as a decimal fraction of a dollar (\$0.75).
Problem 4
Which operation of arithmetic is the inverse of addition?
Answer: Subtraction
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Inverse operations "undo" each other. Subtraction reverses the process of addition.
Problem 5
If the dividend is 60 and the divisor is 4, what is the quotient?
Answer: 15
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Set up the division problem:
    $$60 \div 4 = 15$$
Problem 6
For the fact family 3, 5, and 15, write two multiplication facts and two division facts.
Answer: 3 × 5 = 15, 5 × 3 = 15, 15 ÷ 3 = 5, 15 ÷ 5 = 3
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Multiplication is commutative:
    $$3 \times 5 = 15 \quad \text{and} \quad 5 \times 3 = 15$$
  • Division facts use the product as the dividend:
    $$15 \div 3 = 5 \quad \text{and} \quad 15 \div 5 = 3$$
Problem 7
List the properties used in the first and second steps to simplify the expression \(5 + (27 + 35)\).
Step 1: \((27 + 35) + 5\)
Step 2: \(27 + (35 + 5)\)
Answer: a. Commutative property of addition, b. Associative property of addition
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Step 1: The order of the addends changed from \(5 + (Group)\) to \((Group) + 5\). This is the Commutative Property.
  • Step 2: The grouping parentheses shifted from \((27 + 35)\) to \((35 + 5)\). This is the Associative Property.
Problem 8
Simplify: $20.00 - $14.79
Answer: $5.21
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Align the decimal points and subtract, borrowing where necessary.
    $$20.00 - 14.79 = 5.21$$
Problem 9
Simplify: $1.54 × 7
Answer: $10.78
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Multiply 154 by 7: \(154 \times 7 = 1078\).
  • Since there are two decimal places in \$1.54, put two decimal places in the answer: \$10.78.
Problem 10
Simplify: \(\frac{\$30.00}{8}\)
Answer: $3.75
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Divide 30.00 by 8:
    $$30.00 \div 8 = 3.75$$
Problem 11
Simplify: \(\$4.36 + 75\text{¢} + \$12 + 6\text{¢}\)
Answer: $17.17
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Convert all amounts to dollars: \$0.75 and \$0.06.
  • Align the decimals and add:
    $$4.36 + 0.75 + 12.00 + 0.06 = 17.17$$
Problem 12
Simplify: \(\$10.00 - (\$4.89 + 74\text{¢})\)
Answer: $4.37
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • First add the amounts in parentheses:
    $$\$4.89 + \$0.74 = \$5.63$$
  • Subtract the sum from \$10.00:
    $$\$10.00 - \$5.63 = \$4.37$$
Problem 13
Add the column of numbers: 8, 5, 4, 6, 5, 4, 3, 7, 2, 4, 1, 8
Answer: 57
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • A great strategy is to group numbers that add up to 10 (e.g., 6+4, 5+5, 7+3, 8+2).
  • Summing straight down: \(8+5=13\), \(13+4=17\), \(17+6=23\), \(23+5=28\), \(28+4=32\), \(32+3=35\), \(35+7=42\), \(42+2=44\), \(44+4=48\), \(48+1=49\), \(49+8=57\).
Problem 14
Simplify: \(3105 \div 15\)
Answer: 207
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Divide left to right: 15 goes into 31 two times (\(2 \times 15 = 30\)).
  • Bring down 0 to make 10. 15 goes into 10 zero times.
  • Bring down 5 to make 105. 15 goes into 105 seven times (\(7 \times 15 = 105\)).
  • The quotient is 207.
Problem 15
Simplify: \(40 \overline{) 1630}\)
Answer: 40 R 30 (or 40.75)
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • 40 goes into 163 four times (\(4 \times 40 = 160\)) with a remainder of 3.
  • Bring down the 0 to make 30. 40 goes into 30 zero times.
  • The quotient is 40 with a remainder of 30. (Can also be written as \(40 \frac{30}{40}\) or \(40.75\)).
Problem 16
Simplify: \(81 \div (9 \div 3)\)
Answer: 27
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Calculate the expression inside the parentheses first:
    $$9 \div 3 = 3$$
  • Divide 81 by the result:
    $$81 \div 3 = 27$$
Problem 17
Simplify: \((81 \div 9) \div 3\)
Answer: 3
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Calculate the expression inside the parentheses first:
    $$81 \div 9 = 9$$
  • Divide the result by 3:
    $$9 \div 3 = 3$$
Problem 18
Simplify: \((10)(\$3.75)\)
Answer: $37.50
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Multiplying by 10 moves the decimal point one place to the right:
    $$10 \times \$3.75 = \$37.50$$
Problem 19
Simplify: \(3167 - (450 - 78)\)
Answer: 2,795
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Subtract the numbers inside the parentheses first:
    $$450 - 78 = 372$$
  • Subtract that result from the starting number:
    $$3167 - 372 = 2795$$
Problem 20
Simplify: \((3167 - 450) - 78\)
Answer: 2,639
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Evaluate the parentheses first:
    $$3167 - 450 = 2717$$
  • Subtract 78 from the result:
    $$2717 - 78 = 2639$$
Problem 21
Simplify: \(\$20.00 \div 16\)
Answer: $1.25
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Divide 20.00 by 16:
    $$20.00 \div 16 = 1.25$$
Problem 22
Simplify: \(70 \cdot 800\)
Answer: 56,000
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Multiply the non-zero digits: \(7 \times 8 = 56\).
  • Count the total zeros in both factors (one in 70, two in 800 = three zeros) and append them:
    $$56,000$$
Problem 23
Simplify: \(3714 + 268 + 47 + 9\)
Answer: 4,038
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Align numbers vertically by place value and add:
    $$3714 + 268 = 3982$$
    $$3982 + 47 = 4029$$
    $$4029 + 9 = 4038$$
Problem 24
Simplify: \(5 \cdot 4 \cdot 3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1\)
Answer: 120
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Multiply sequentially:
    $$5 \times 4 = 20$$
  • $$20 \times 3 = 60$$
  • $$60 \times 2 = 120$$
  • $$120 \times 1 = 120$$
Problem 25
Simplify: \(\$20 - (\$1.47 + \$8)\)
Answer: $10.53
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • First, add the amounts inside the parentheses:
    $$\$1.47 + \$8.00 = \$9.47$$
  • Subtract the total from \$20.00:
    $$\$20.00 - \$9.47 = \$10.53$$
Problem 26
Simplify: \(30 \times 45\text{¢}\)
Answer: $13.50 (or 1350¢)
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Multiply to find the total cents:
    $$30 \times 45 = 1350\text{¢}$$
  • Convert cents to dollars by dividing by 100:
    $$1350 \div 100 = \$13.50$$
Problem 27
Which property can we use to find each missing number?
a. \(10x = 0\)    b. \(10y = 10\)
Answer: a. Property of Zero for Multiplication, b. Identity Property of Multiplication
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • a. Any number multiplied by 0 equals 0. Therefore, \(x = 0\). This is the Property of Zero for Multiplication.
  • b. Any number multiplied by 1 equals itself. Therefore, \(y = 1\). This is the Identity Property of Multiplication.
Problem 28
Evaluate each expression for \(x = 18\) and \(y = 3\):
a. \(x - y\)    b. \(xy\)    c. \(\frac{x}{y}\)    d. \(x + y\)
Answer: a. 15   b. 54   c. 6   d. 21
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Substitute 18 for \(x\) and 3 for \(y\) into each expression.
  • a. \(18 - 3 = 15\)
  • b. \(18 \times 3 = 54\)
  • c. \(18 \div 3 = 6\)
  • d. \(18 + 3 = 21\)
Problem 29
Why is zero called the additive identity?
Answer: Adding zero to any number does not change its value.
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • The word "identity" refers to keeping a number's value exactly the same.
  • Because \(N + 0 = N\) for any number \(N\), zero perfectly preserves the original identity of the number during addition.
Problem 30
The equation below shows the relationship of factors and their product:
\(\text{factor} \times \text{factor} = \text{product}\)
Using the vocabulary we have learned, write a similar equation to show the relationships in division.
Answer: dividend ÷ divisor = quotient
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • In a division equation, the number being divided is the dividend.
  • The number you are dividing by is the divisor.
  • The answer to the division problem is the quotient.
  • Therefore, \(\text{dividend} \div \text{divisor} = \text{quotient}\).
