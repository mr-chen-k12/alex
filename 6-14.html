30. JUSTIFY: List the properties used in the first three steps to simplify the expression \((6 \times 9) \times 4\).
\((6 \times 9) \times 4\)      Given expression
\(6 \times (9 \times 4)\)      a. ___________________
\(6 \times (4 \times 9)\)      b. ___________________
\((6 \times 4) \times 9\)      c. ___________________
a. Associative Property of Multiplication (Parentheses changed grouping structures)
b. Commutative Property of Multiplication (Internal positions swapped from 9 × 4 to 4 × 9)
c. Associative Property of Multiplication (Re-grouped brackets)
Answer: a. Associative | b. Commutative | c. Associative
