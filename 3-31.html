Lesson Practice

a. Find the perimeter of the complex shape:

Answer: 40 cm

Explanation: To find the perimeter, we sum all the outer edges. We are given the left edge (12 cm), top edge (8 cm), right-upper edge (5 cm), and bottom edge (3 cm). We must determine the two missing inner edges.

  • Inner horizontal edge = Top edge - Bottom edge = 8 cm - 3 cm = 5 cm.
  • Inner vertical edge = Left edge - Right-upper edge = 12 cm - 5 cm = 7 cm.

Perimeter = 12 + 8 + 5 + 5 + 7 + 3 = 40 cm.

b. Find the perimeter of the complex shape:

Answer: 70 mm

Explanation: Sum the outer edges. We need to find the missing horizontal and vertical segments.

  • Inner horizontal edge = Top edge - Bottom edge = 20 mm - 16 mm = 4 mm.
  • Inner vertical edge = Left edge - Right-upper edge = 15 mm - 7 mm = 8 mm.

Perimeter = 20 + 7 + 4 + 8 + 16 + 15 = 70 mm.

Written Practice

1. When the sum of \(\frac{1}{2}\) and \(\frac{1}{3}\) is divided by the product of \(\frac{1}{2}\) and \(\frac{1}{3}\), what is the quotient?

Answer: 5

Explanation:

  • First, find the sum: \(\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3} = \frac{3}{6} + \frac{2}{6} = \frac{5}{6}\)
  • Next, find the product: \(\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{6}\)
  • Divide the sum by the product: \(\frac{5}{6} \div \frac{1}{6} = \frac{5}{6} \times \frac{6}{1} = 5\)

2. The average age of three men is 24 years.

a. What is the sum of their ages?

b. If two of the men are 22 years old, how old is the third?

Answer: a. 72 years  |  b. 28 years old

Explanation:

  • a. The sum is the average multiplied by the number of people: 24 × 3 = 72.
  • b. The sum of the two 22-year-olds is 44. The third man's age is the total sum minus their sum: 72 - 44 = 28.

3. A string one yard long is formed into the shape of a square.

a. How many inches long is each side of the square?

b. How many square inches is the area of the square?

Answer: a. 9 inches  |  b. 81 square inches

Explanation:

  • a. 1 yard = 36 inches. Since a square has 4 equal sides, each side is 36 ÷ 4 = 9 inches.
  • b. The area of a square is side × side: 9 × 9 = 81 square inches.

4. Complete this proportion: Five is to three as thirty is to what number?

Answer: 18

Explanation: Set up the proportion: \(\frac{5}{3} = \frac{30}{x}\). Cross multiply to solve for x: 5x = 90, so x = 18.

5. Mr. Cho has 30 books. Fourteen of the books are mysteries. What is the ratio of mysteries to non-mysteries?

Answer: \(\frac{7}{8}\) or 7:8

Explanation: There are 14 mystery books. The number of non-mystery books is 30 - 14 = 16. The ratio of mysteries to non-mysteries is 14 to 16, which reduces to 7 to 8.

6. In another class of 33 students, the ratio of boys to girls is 4 to 7. How many girls are in that class?

Answer: 21 girls

Explanation: The ratio parts add up to 4 + 7 = 11 parts total. The total number of students is 33, so each "part" represents 33 ÷ 11 = 3 students. Since girls represent 7 parts, there are 7 × 3 = 21 girls.

7. Evaluate: \(100 \div 10^2 + 3 \times (2^3 - \sqrt{16})\)

Answer: 13

Explanation: Follow order of operations (PEMDAS):

\(100 \div 100 + 3 \times (8 - 4)\)

\(= 1 + 3 \times 4\)

\(= 1 + 12 = 13\)

8. Robert complained that he had a "ton" of homework.

a. How many pounds is a ton?

b. What is the probability that Robert would literally have a ton of homework?

Answer: a. 2,000 pounds  |  b. 0

Explanation: A standard US ton is exactly 2,000 pounds. It is physically impossible for a student to literally be assigned 2,000 pounds of homework, making the probability 0.

Answer: 210

Explanation: First add the parentheses: \(8.4 \div 0.04\). Multiply both numbers by 100 to remove decimals: \(840 \div 4 = 210\).

14. Find the unknown number: \(7 \frac{1}{2} + 6 \frac{3}{4} + n = 15 \frac{3}{8}\)

Answer: \(1 \frac{1}{8}\)

Explanation: Convert fractions to a common denominator of 8.

\(\frac{60}{8} + \frac{54}{8} + n = \frac{123}{8}\)

\(\frac{114}{8} + n = \frac{123}{8}\)

\(n = \frac{123}{8} - \frac{114}{8} = \frac{9}{8} = 1 \frac{1}{8}\)

15. Find the unknown number: \(x - 1 \frac{3}{4} = 7 \frac{1}{2}\)

Answer: \(9 \frac{1}{4}\)

Explanation: Add \(1 \frac{3}{4}\) to both sides.

\(x = 7 \frac{1}{2} + 1 \frac{3}{4} = \frac{15}{2} + \frac{7}{4}\)

\(x = \frac{30}{4} + \frac{7}{4} = \frac{37}{4} = 9 \frac{1}{4}\)

16. Instead of dividing \(10 \frac{1}{2}\) by \(3 \frac{1}{2}\), Guadalupe doubled both numbers before dividing. What was Guadalupe's division problem and its quotient?

Answer: Problem: \(21 \div 7\). Quotient: 3.

Explanation: Double \(10 \frac{1}{2}\) to get 21. Double \(3 \frac{1}{2}\) to get 7. The division problem is \(21 \div 7\), which equals 3. (This verifies the answer for Problem 12).

17. Mariabella used a tape measure to find the circumference and the diameter of a plate. The circumference was about 35 inches, and the diameter was about 11 inches. Find the approximate number of diameters in the circumference. Round to the nearest tenth.

Answer: 3.2

Explanation: Divide the circumference by the diameter: 35 ÷ 11 ≈ 3.1818... Rounding to the nearest tenth gives 3.2 (which is an approximation of pi).

18. Write twenty million, five hundred thousand in expanded notation using exponents.

Answer: \((2 \times 10^7) + (5 \times 10^5)\)

Explanation: 20,500,000 has a 2 in the ten-millions place (\(10^7\)) and a 5 in the hundred-thousands place (\(10^5\)).

19. Name the prime numbers between 40 and 50.

Answer: 41, 43, 47

Explanation: The numbers between 40 and 50 are 41, 42, 43, 44, 45, 46, 47, 48, 49. Removing the ones divisible by 2, 3, 5, or 7 leaves the primes 41, 43, and 47.

20. Calculate mentally:

a. -3 + -8      b. -3 - -8

c. -8 + +3      d. -8 - +3

Answer: a. -11  |  b. 5  |  c. -5  |  d. -11

Explanation:

  • a: adding two negative numbers results in a larger negative (-11).
  • b: subtracting a negative is adding a positive: -3 + 8 = 5.
  • c: -8 + 3 = -5.
  • d: subtracting a positive from a negative goes further negative: -8 - 3 = -11.

21. In \(\triangle ABC\) the measure of \(\angle A\) is \(40^\circ\). Angles B and C are congruent. What is the measure of \(\angle C\)?

Answer: \(70^\circ\)

Explanation: The sum of interior angles in a triangle is \(180^\circ\). Since \(\angle A = 40^\circ\), the remaining \(140^\circ\) must be split equally between congruent angles B and C. \(140^\circ \div 2 = 70^\circ\).

22. Given the right triangle:

a. What is the perimeter of this triangle?

b. What is the area of this triangle?

c. What is the ratio of the length of the 20 mm side to the length of the longest side? Express the ratio as a fraction and as a decimal.

Answer: a. 60 mm  |  b. 150 sq mm  |  c. \(\frac{4}{5}\) and 0.8

Explanation:

  • a. Perimeter: Sum of the sides = 15 + 20 + 25 = 60 mm.
  • b. Area: \(\frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = \frac{1}{2} \times 20 \times 15 = 150\) sq mm.
  • c. Ratio: The longest side is the hypotenuse (25 mm). Ratio is 20/25, which simplifies to \(\frac{4}{5}\). As a decimal: 0.8.

23. The Simpsons rented a trailer that was 8 feet long and 5 feet wide. If they load the trailer with 1-by-1-by-1-foot boxes to a height of 3 feet, how many boxes can be loaded onto the trailer?

Answer: 120 boxes

Explanation: This is a volume problem. Volume = length × width × height = 8 × 5 × 3 = 120 cubic feet. Since each box is 1 cubic foot, 120 boxes will fit.

24. What is the probability of drawing the queen of spades from a normal deck of 52 cards?

Answer: \(\frac{1}{52}\)

Explanation: There is exactly 1 queen of spades in a standard deck of 52 cards.

25. Thermometer reading:

a. What temperature is shown on this thermometer?

b. If the temperature rises \(12^\circ\text{F}\), what will the temperature be?

Answer: a. \(-4^\circ\text{F}\)  |  b. \(8^\circ\text{F}\)

Explanation:

  • a. The scale is marked in intervals of 2 degrees (5 intervals between 0 and -10). The mercury is at the second tick mark below 0, which corresponds to \(-4^\circ\text{F}\).
  • b. \(-4^\circ\text{F} + 12^\circ\text{F} = 8^\circ\text{F}\).

Answer: 140 mm

Explanation: To find the perimeter, sum all the outer sides. We must identify the missing inner vertical and horizontal segments.

  • Inner horizontal segment = Bottom edge - Top edge = 50 mm - 30 mm = 20 mm.

27. Given the figure:

a. What is the area of the shaded rectangle?

b. What is the area of the unshaded rectangle?

c. What is the combined area of the two rectangles?

Answer: a. 80 sq mm  |  b. 140 sq mm  |  c. 220 sq mm

Explanation:

  • a. The shaded (top) rectangle has width 10 mm and height 8 mm. Area = 10 × 8 = 80 sq mm.
  • b. The unshaded (bottom) rectangle has width 20 mm and height 7 mm. Area = 20 × 7 = 140 sq mm.

28. What are the coordinates of the point halfway between (-3, -2) and (5, -2)?

Answer: (1, -2)

Explanation: Use the midpoint formula: \((\frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2})\). The y-coordinate is the same (-2). The halfway point on the x-axis is \(\frac{-3 + 5}{2} = \frac{2}{2} = 1\).

29. A pint of milk weighs about 16 ounces. About how many pounds does a half gallon of milk weigh?

Answer: 4 pounds

Explanation:

  • 1 half gallon = 2 quarts = 4 pints.
  • 4 pints × 16 ounces/pint = 64 ounces total.
  • There are 16 ounces in a pound, so 64 ÷ 16 = 4 pounds.

30. Ruben walked around a building whose perimeter was shaped like a regular pentagon.

a. At each corner of the building, Ruben turned about how many degrees?

b. What is the measure of each interior angle of the regular pentagon?

Answer: a. \(72^\circ\)  |  b. \(108^\circ\)

Explanation:

  • a. When walking around a complete polygon, you make one full rotation of \(360^\circ\). A pentagon has 5 corners, so the exterior angle (turn angle) at each corner is \(360^\circ \div 5 = 72^\circ\).
  • b. The interior angle and exterior angle lie on a straight line and thus add up to \(180^\circ\). The interior angle = \(180^\circ - 72^\circ = 108^\circ\).
