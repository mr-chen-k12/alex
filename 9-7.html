Practice i
Point \(A\) is located at \(8\frac{1}{4}\text{ inches}\) and Point \(B\) is located at \(11\frac{3}{4}\text{ inches}\) on a ruler. Find the distance \(AB\).
Final Answer
\(AB = 3\frac{1}{2}\text{ inches}\)

Subtract the smaller coordinate from the larger coordinate:

$$11\frac{3}{4} - 8\frac{1}{4} = (11 - 8) + \left(\frac{3}{4} - \frac{1}{4}\right) = 3\frac{2}{4} = 3\frac{1}{2}\text{ inches}$$

Practice j
Maria's ruler is divided into sixteenths of an inch. Assuming the ruler is used correctly, what is the greatest possible measurement error that can occur? Express your answer as a fraction of an inch.
Final Answer
\(\frac{1}{32}\text{ inch}\)

The maximum possible measurement error is equal to half of the smallest measuring unit on the scale:

$$\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{16} = \frac{1}{32}\text{ inch}$$

Problem 2
Point \(P\) is at \(2\frac{1}{2}\text{ in.}\) and point \(Q\) is at \(5\frac{1}{4}\text{ in.}\) on a ruler. Find length \(PQ\).
Final Answer
\(2\frac{3}{4}\text{ inches}\)

Subtract the position of \(P\) from \(Q\):

$$5\frac{1}{4} - 2\frac{1}{2} = 5\frac{1}{4} - 2\frac{2}{4} = 4\frac{5}{4} - 2\frac{2}{4} = 2\frac{3}{4}\text{ in.}$$

Problem 3
What is the quotient when the product of 30 and 30 is divided by the sum of 15 and 15?
Final Answer
\(30\)

Step 1 (Product): \(30 \times 30 = 900\)

Step 2 (Sum): \(15 + 15 = 30\)

Step 3 (Quotient): \(\frac{900}{30} = 30\)

Problem 4 ANALYZE
List all single-digit divisors of 2520.
Final Answer
\(1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9\)

Check digits 1 through 9:

• All numbers are divisible by 1.

• 2520 is even \(\rightarrow\) Divisible by 2, 4, 6, 8.

• Sum of digits \(2+5+2+0 = 9\) \(\rightarrow\) Divisible by 3 and 9.

• Ends in 0 \(\rightarrow\) Divisible by 5.

• \(2520 \div 7 = 360\) \(\rightarrow\) Divisible by 7.

Thus, every single-digit number (1–9) divides 2520 evenly.

Problem 17 JUSTIFY
Provide reasons for steps (a) and (b) in solving \(7 + (x + 5) = 25\): $$\begin{align*} 7 + (x + 5) &= 25 && \text{Given equation} \\ 7 + (5 + x) &= 25 && \text{a. }\underline{\quad\quad\quad\quad} \\ (7 + 5) + x &= 25 && \text{b. }\underline{\quad\quad\quad\quad} \end{align*}$$
Final Answer

a. Commutative property of addition

b. Associative property of addition

• Step a reorders \(x + 5\) to \(5 + x\) (Commutative Property).

• Step b regrouped the terms from \(7 + (5 + x)\) to \((7 + 5) + x\) (Associative Property).

Problem 26a
The sequence \(1, 5, 9, 13, \dots\) follows the rule \(k = 4n - 3\). Find the tenth term of this sequence.
Final Answer
\(37\)

Substitute \(n = 10\) into the formula \(k = 4n - 3\):

$$k = 4(10) - 3 = 40 - 3 = 37$$

Problem 26b ANALYZE
What pattern do you recognize in this sequence?
Final Answer
Each term is 4 greater than the preceding term (adds 4 each step).

Difference between terms: \(5 - 1 = 4\), \(9 - 5 = 4\), \(13 - 9 = 4\).

Problem 27
Compare: \(650 - (120 + 80) \bigcirc (650 - 120) + 80\)
Final Answer
\(<\)

Left side: \(650 - (120 + 80) = 650 - 200 = 450\)

Right side: \((650 - 120) + 80 = 530 + 80 = 610\)

Since \(450 < 610\), the comparison symbol is \(<\).

Problem 28
Quadrilateral \(EFGH\) is a right-trapezoid with right angles at vertices \(E\) and \(H\).
• Angle \(G\) is less than \(90^\circ\).
• Angle \(F\) is greater than \(90^\circ\).
Name the acute, obtuse, and right angles in \(EFGH\).
Final Answer

Acute angle: \(\angle G\)

Obtuse angle: \(\angle F\)

Right angles: \(\angle E\) and \(\angle H\)

• An angle less than \(90^\circ\) is acute (\(\angle G\)).

• An angle between \(90^\circ\) and \(180^\circ\) is obtuse (\(\angle F\)).

• Angles measuring exactly \(90^\circ\) are right angles (\(\angle E\) and \(\angle H\)).

Problem 29

Final Answer

a. \(\overline{GF}\) (or \(\overline{FG}\))

b. \(\overline{EF}\) (or \(\overline{GH}\))

a. The parallel bases of trapezoid \(EFGH\) are \(\overline{HE}\) and \(\overline{GF}\).

b. \(\overline{EF}\) forms a \(90^\circ\) angle with \(\overline{HE}\), making it perpendicular.

Problem 30 EXPLAIN
Explain the difference in mathematical meaning between the notations \(\overline{AB}\) and \(AB\).
Final Answer
\(\overline{AB}\) denotes the geometric line segment, whereas \(AB\) represents the numerical length or distance of that segment.

• \(\overline{AB}\) is a geometric figure (the set of points between \(A\) and \(B\)).

• \(AB\) is a real number quantity (e.g., \(AB = 5\text{ cm}\)).
