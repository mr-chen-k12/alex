f. These two pentagons are similar. The scale factor for corresponding sides is 3. How long is segment AE? How long is segment IJ?
Answer: AE = $3$ in., IJ = $3$ in.

Explanation: Since the pentagons are similar with a scale factor of $3$ from the smaller to the larger, we multiply the side lengths of the smaller pentagon by $3$ to get the corresponding side lengths of the larger pentagon.
1. The segment FJ on the large pentagon corresponds to AE on the small pentagon. If $FJ = 9$, then $AE \times 3 = 9$, so $AE = 3$ in.
2. The segment IJ corresponds to segment DE. We know $DE = 1$ in. So, $IJ = 1 \times 3 = 3$ in.
1. The first three prime numbers are 2, 3, and 5. Their product is 30. What is the product of the next three prime numbers?
Answer: $1001$

Explanation: A prime number is only divisible by 1 and itself. The next three prime numbers after 5 are 7, 11, and 13.
Their product is: $7 \times 11 \times 13 = 77 \times 13 = 1001$.
2. On the map 2 cm equals 1 km. What is the actual length of a street that is 10 cm long on the map?
Answer: $5$ km

Explanation: Set up a proportion: $\frac{2 \text{ cm}}{1 \text{ km}} = \frac{10 \text{ cm}}{x \text{ km}}$.
Divide the map length by the scale ratio: $10 \div 2 = 5$. Thus, the street is 5 km long.
3. Between 8 p.m. and 9 p.m. the station broadcasts 8 minutes of commercials. What was the ratio of commercial time to noncommercial time during that hour?
Answer: $\frac{2}{13}$ or $2:13$

Explanation: Total time in an hour = 60 minutes.
Commercial time = 8 minutes.
Noncommercial time = $60 - 8 = 52$ minutes.
The ratio of commercial to noncommercial time is $\frac{8}{52}$. Dividing the numerator and denominator by 4 gives $\frac{2}{13}$.

5. Conclude If the two acute angles of a right triangle are congruent, then what is the measure of each acute angle?
Answer: $45^{\circ}$

Explanation: The sum of angles in any triangle is $180^{\circ}$. A right triangle has one $90^{\circ}$ angle. The remaining two acute angles must sum to $180^{\circ} - 90^{\circ} = 90^{\circ}$. Since they are congruent (equal), we divide by 2: $90^{\circ} \div 2 = 45^{\circ}$.

Answer: Fraction: $\frac{1}{10}$, Decimal: $0.1$

Explanation: There are 5 favorable outcomes (Pacific states) out of 50 total possible outcomes (all US states).
Probability $= \frac{5}{50}$. Simplifying by dividing top and bottom by 5 gives $\frac{1}{10}$.
As a decimal, $\frac{1}{10}$ is written as $0.1$.

15. $10^3 \div 10^2 - 10^1$
Answer: $0$

Explanation: Following order of operations (PEMDAS), resolve exponents first, then division, then subtraction. $$10^3 = 1000, \quad 10^2 = 100, \quad 10^1 = 10$$ Now substitute them back in: $$1000 \div 100 - 10$$ Divide: $$10 - 10 = 0$$ (Alternatively using exponent rules: $10^3 \div 10^2 = 10^{3-2} = 10^1$. Then $10^1 - 10^1 = 0$.)

17. Write the fraction $\frac{2}{3}$ as a decimal number rounded to the hundredths place.
Answer: $0.67$

Explanation: Divide $2$ by $3$. $2 \div 3 = 0.6666...$ To round to the hundredths place (two decimal places), look at the digit in the thousandths place (which is 6). Because 6 is 5 or greater, we round the hundredths digit up by 1. $$0.666... \approx 0.67$$
18. The Zamoras rent a storage room that is 10 feet wide, 12 feet long, and 8 feet high. How many cube-shaped boxes 1 foot on each edge can the Zamoras store in the room?
Answer: $960$ boxes

Explanation: To find the number of 1-foot cube boxes that can fit, we calculate the total volume of the rectangular storage room in cubic feet. $$\text{Volume} = \text{length} \times \text{width} \times \text{height}$$ $$\text{Volume} = 12 \text{ ft} \times 10 \text{ ft} \times 8 \text{ ft} = 960 \text{ cubic feet}$$ Since each box is 1 cubic foot, they can store $960$ boxes.
19. These two rectangles are similar.
a. What is the scale factor from the smaller rectangle to the larger rectangle?
b. What is the scale factor from the larger rectangle to the smaller rectangle?
Answer: a. $3$, b. $\frac{1}{3}$

Explanation:
a. To go from the smaller rectangle (length 3, width 1) to the larger (length 9, width 3), multiply by the scale factor. $\frac{9}{3} = 3$ or $\frac{3}{1} = 3$. The scale factor is $3$.
b. To go from the larger rectangle to the smaller one, we reverse the ratio: $\frac{3}{9} = \frac{1}{3}$.

24. This trapezoid has been divided into a rectangle and a triangle. What is the area of the trapezoid?
Answer: $51 \text{ cm}^2$

Explanation: We can find the area of the two parts and add them together.
1. Rectangle Area: $\text{length} \times \text{width} = 7 \text{ cm} \times 6 \text{ cm} = 42 \text{ cm}^2$.
2. Triangle Area: The base of the triangle is the total bottom base minus the rectangle's base: $10 - 7 = 3 \text{ cm}$. The height is the same as the rectangle: $6 \text{ cm}$.
$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times 3 \times 6 = 9 \text{ cm}^2$$ Total Area: $42 + 9 = 51 \text{ cm}^2$.
(Alternatively, use the trapezoid area formula: $A = \frac{1}{2}(b_1 + b_2)h = \frac{1}{2}(10 + 7) \times 6 = \frac{1}{2}(17) \times 6 = 17 \times 3 = 51 \text{ cm}^2$)
25. Estimate A soup label must be long enough to wrap around a can. If the diameter of the can is 7 cm, then the label must be at least how long? Round up to the nearest whole number.
Answer: $22$ cm

Explanation: The length required to wrap around the can is equal to its circumference. $$C = \pi \times d$$ $$C \approx 3.14 \times 7 = 21.98 \text{ cm}$$ Rounding $21.98$ up to the nearest whole number gives $22$ cm. (Using the fraction $\frac{22}{7}$ for $\pi$ gives exactly $22$: $\frac{22}{7} \times 7 = 22$).

29. $6 \frac{2}{3} \div 100$
Answer: $\frac{1}{15}$

Explanation: First, convert the mixed number to an improper fraction: $$6 \frac{2}{3} = \frac{6 \times 3 + 2}{3} = \frac{20}{3}$$ To divide by $100$, multiply by its reciprocal ($\frac{1}{100}$): $$\frac{20}{3} \times \frac{1}{100} = \frac{20}{300}$$ Simplify the fraction by dividing top and bottom by 20: $$ \frac{20 \div 20}{300 \div 20} = \frac{1}{15} $$

Explanation: Evaluate the left side: $$\left(\frac{1}{10}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{10} \times \frac{1}{10} = \frac{1}{100}$$ Convert to a decimal: $\frac{1}{100} = 0.01$.
Since $0.01 = 0.01$, the correct symbol to place in the circle is $=$.

Real-World Connection Issam wants to make a rock garden in his backyard. He bought $\frac{1}{5}$ ton of gravel and $\frac{1}{2}$ ton of rocks. Issam is not sure if the gravel and the rocks will fit on the trailer he rented. If the trailer can carry a maximum load of 1250 pounds, can Issam take all the gravel and rocks in one trip? Note: 1 ton = 2000 pounds. Support your answer.
Answer: No, he cannot take it all in one trip.

Explanation: First, find the total weight in tons by finding a common denominator (10): $$ \frac{1}{5} + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{2}{10} + \frac{5}{10} = \frac{7}{10} \text{ tons} $$ Now convert tons to pounds (since $1$ ton $= 2000$ lbs): $$ \frac{7}{10} \times 2000 = \frac{14000}{10} = 1400 \text{ pounds} $$ The total weight is $1400$ lbs. The trailer can only carry $1250$ lbs. Since $1400 > 1250$, the load is too heavy.
