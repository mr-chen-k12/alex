Written Practice Lessons

1. On his first six tests, Chris had scores of 90%, 92%, 96%, 92%, 84%, and 92%. Use this information to answer problems 1 and 2.

a. Which score occurred most frequently? That is, what is the mode of the scores?
b. The difference between Chris's highest score and his lowest score is how many percentage points? That is, what is the range of the scores?
Answer:
a. The mode is $92\%$.
b. The range is $12\%$.

Explanation:
a. The score $92\%$ appears three times, which is more frequent than any other score.
b. The highest score is $96\%$ and the lowest is $84\%$. The range is $96 - 84 = 12$.
2. What was Chris's average score for the six tests? That is, what is the mean of the scores?
Answer: The mean is $91\%$.

Explanation:
To find the mean, sum all the scores and divide by the number of tests (6).
Sum: $90 + 92 + 96 + 92 + 84 + 92 = 546$
Mean: $546 \div 6 = 91$
3. In basketball there are one-point baskets, two-point baskets, and three-point baskets. If a team scored 96 points and made 18 one-point baskets and 6 three-point baskets, how many two-point baskets did the team make? Explain how you found your answer.
Answer: The team made $30$ two-point baskets.

Explanation:
Calculate the points from the known baskets:
One-point baskets: $18 \times 1 = 18$ points.
Three-point baskets: $6 \times 3 = 18$ points.
Total known points: $18 + 18 = 36$ points.
Points needed from two-point baskets: $96 - 36 = 60$ points.
Number of two-point baskets: $60 \div 2 = 30$ baskets.
4. Which ratio forms a proportion with $\frac{4}{7}$?
A $\frac{7}{4}$     B $\frac{14}{17}$     C $\frac{12}{21}$     D $\frac{2}{3}$
Answer: C $\frac{12}{21}$

Explanation:
A proportion exists if the two fractions are equivalent. Multiply the numerator and denominator of $\frac{4}{7}$ by 3:
$\frac{4 \times 3}{7 \times 3} = \frac{12}{21}$.
5. Complete this proportion: Four is to five as what number is to twenty?
Answer: $16$

Explanation:
Set up the proportion: $\frac{4}{5} = \frac{x}{20}$
Since $5 \times 4 = 20$, multiply the numerator by 4 as well: $4 \times 4 = 16$. So, $x = 16$.
6. Arrange these numbers in order from least to greatest:
$-1, 1, 0.1, -0.1, 0$
Answer: $-1, -0.1, 0, 0.1, 1$

Explanation:
Negative numbers are smaller than zero and positive numbers. The larger the absolute value of a negative number, the smaller it is. Thus, $-1 < -0.1$. Then zero comes before the positive numbers $0.1$ and $1$.
7. The product of $10^3 \cdot 10^2$ equals which of the following?
A $10^9$     B $10^6$     C $10^5$     D $10$
Answer: C $10^5$

Explanation:
When multiplying exponential expressions with the same base, add the exponents: $3 + 2 = 5$. Therefore, $10^3 \cdot 10^2 = 10^5$.
8. The area of the square in this figure is $100\text{ mm}^2$.

a. What is the radius of the circle?
b. What is the diameter of the circle?
c. What is the area of the circle? (Use $3.14$ for $\pi$.)
Answer:
a. $5\text{ mm}$
b. $10\text{ mm}$
c. $78.5\text{ mm}^2$

Explanation:
a. If the area of the square is $100\text{ mm}^2$, its side length is $\sqrt{100} = 10\text{ mm}$. The side of the square equals the diameter of the circle, so the diameter is $10\text{ mm}$. The radius is half the diameter, so $10 \div 2 = 5\text{ mm}$.
b. As established above, the diameter is equal to the side of the square, which is $10\text{ mm}$.
c. Area $= \pi r^2 \approx 3.14 \times (5)^2 = 3.14 \times 25 = 78.5\text{ mm}^2$.

17. An octagon has how many more sides than a pentagon?
Answer: $3$

Explanation:
An octagon has 8 sides. A pentagon has 5 sides.

18. $4 \times 5^2 - 50 \div \sqrt{4} + (3^2 - 2^3)$
Answer: $76$

Explanation:
Use Order of Operations (PEMDAS/BODMAS):
Parentheses and exponents first: $4 \times 25 - 50 \div 2 + (9 - 8)$
Simplify parentheses: $4 \times 25 - 50 \div 2 + 1$
Multiply and Divide left to right: $100 - 25 + 1$
Add and Subtract left to right: $75 + 1 = 76$.
19. Sector 2 on this spinner is a $90^\circ$ sector. If the spinner is spun twice, what is the probability that it will stop in sector 2 both times?
Answer: $\frac{1}{16}$

Explanation:
A full circle is $360^\circ$. A $90^\circ$ sector is $\frac{90}{360} = \frac{1}{4}$ of the circle. The probability of landing on sector 2 in one spin is $\frac{1}{4}$.
For two independent spins, multiply the probabilities: $\frac{1}{4} \times \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{16}$.
20. If the spinner is spun 100 times, about how many times would it be expected to stop in sector 1?
Answer: $75$ times

Explanation:
Since sector 2 is $\frac{1}{4}$ of the circle, sector 1 is the remaining $\frac{3}{4}$ of the circle.
Expected stops in sector 1 = $100 \times \frac{3}{4} = 75$.
21. How many 1 inch cubes would be needed to build this larger cube (side length 4 in)?
Answer: $64$

Explanation:
The volume of the larger cube determines how many 1-inch cubes fit inside.
Volume $= \text{length} \times \text{width} \times \text{height} = 4 \times 4 \times 4 = 64$ cubic inches.
22. The average of four numbers is 5. What is their sum?
Answer: $20$

Explanation:
Average = Sum $\div$ Number of items.
$5 = \text{Sum} \div 4$
Sum = $5 \times 4 = 20$.
23. When Andy was born, he weighed 8 pounds 4 ounces. Three weeks later he weighed 10 pounds 1 ounce. How many pounds and ounces had he gained in three weeks?
Answer: $1$ pound $13$ ounces

Explanation:
Subtract the birth weight from the later weight: $10\text{ lbs } 1\text{ oz} - 8\text{ lbs } 4\text{ oz}$.
Borrow $1\text{ lb}$ (which is $16\text{ oz}$) from the $10\text{ lbs}$: $10\text{ lbs } 1\text{ oz} = 9\text{ lbs } 17\text{ oz}$.
$9\text{ lbs } 17\text{ oz} - 8\text{ lbs } 4\text{ oz} = 1\text{ lb } 13\text{ oz}$.
24. Lines s and t are parallel.

a. Which angle is an alternate interior angle to $\angle 5$?
b. If the measure of $\angle 5$ is $76^\circ$, what are the measures of $\angle 1$ and $\angle 2$?
Answer:
a. $\angle 3$
b. $m\angle 1 = 76^\circ$, $m\angle 2 = 104^\circ$

Explanation:
a. Alternate interior angles are on opposite sides of the transversal and between the parallel lines. The alternate interior angle to $\angle 5$ is $\angle 3$.
b. $\angle 1$ and $\angle 5$ are corresponding angles, so they are equal: $m\angle 1 = 76^\circ$. $\angle 1$ and $\angle 2$ are supplementary angles (they form a straight line), so $m\angle 2 = 180^\circ - 76^\circ = 104^\circ$.

29. The coordinates of the endpoints of a line segment are $(3, -1)$ and $(3, 5)$. The midpoint of the segment is the point halfway between the endpoints. What are the coordinates of the midpoint?
Answer: $(3, 2)$

Explanation:
Use the midpoint formula: $(\frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2})$.
$x$-coordinate: $\frac{3 + 3}{2} = \frac{6}{2} = 3$
$y$-coordinate: $\frac{-1 + 5}{2} = \frac{4}{2} = 2$
The midpoint is $(3, 2)$.
30. Tania took 10 steps to walk across the tetherball circle and 31 steps to walk around the tetherball circle. Use this information to find the approximate number of diameters in the circumference of the tetherball circle.
Answer: $3.1$

Explanation:
The distance across is the diameter ($10$ steps). The distance around is the circumference ($31$ steps).
To find the number of diameters in the circumference, divide the circumference by the diameter:
$31 \div 10 = 3.1$. (This is an approximation of $\pi$).
