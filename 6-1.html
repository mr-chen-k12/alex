Problem 1
In a bag are 3 red marbles and 3 blue marbles. If two marbles are drawn from the bag at the same time, what is the probability that both marbles are red?
Answer: \(\frac{1}{5}\) (or 20%)
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Method 1: Sequential Selection without Replacement
    Drawing two marbles at the same time is mathematically equivalent to drawing one marble and then drawing a second marble without putting the first one back.
  • Find the probability that the first marble drawn is red. There are 3 red marbles out of 6 total marbles:
    $$P(\text{First is Red}) = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}$$
  • Find the probability that the second marble drawn is red, given that the first was red. Now, 2 red marbles remain out of 5 total remaining marbles:
    $$P(\text{Second is Red} \mid \text{First is Red}) = \frac{2}{5}$$
  • Multiply the two probabilities together to get the compound probability:
    $$P(\text{Both Red}) = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{2}{5} = \frac{2}{10} = \frac{1}{5}$$
  • Method 2: Combinations (Alternative)
    Find the number of ways to choose 2 red marbles out of 3: \(\binom{3}{2} = 3\).
    Find the total number of ways to choose any 2 marbles out of 6: \(\binom{6}{2} = \frac{6 \times 5}{2} = 15\).
    Probability = \(\frac{3}{15} = \frac{1}{5}\).
Problem 2
Which letter has rotational symmetry?
    E      F      G      H
Answer: H
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • An object has rotational symmetry if it looks exactly the same after a rotation of less than \(360^\circ\) around its center.
  • Let's analyze each letter by rotating it by \(180^\circ\) (upside down):
    • E turned upside down becomes \(\exists\) (does not look the same).
    • F turned upside down becomes \(\mathcal{\in}\) (does not look the same).
    • G turned upside down becomes a completely inverted shape.
    • H turned upside down looks exactly like H.
  • Therefore, the letter H possesses order-2 rotational symmetry (at \(180^\circ\)).
Problem 3
Simplify each expression:
(a) \((-3)(-7)\)
(b) \(\frac{-8}{+2}\)
Answer: (a) 21  |  (b) -4
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Part (a): Multiply \((-3)\) by \((-7)\).
    The product of two negative numbers is always positive:
    $$(-3) \times (-7) = +21 = 21$$
  • Part (b): Divide \((-8)\) by \((+2)\).
    The quotient of a negative number and a positive number is always negative:
    $$\frac{-8}{+2} = -4$$
Problem 4
Emily and Stacey bought a used bike. Emily paid \$5 and Stacey paid the remaining 80%. What was the total price of the bike?
Answer: \$25.00
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Determine the percentage of the price that Emily paid. Since the total percentage must be 100%, and Stacey paid 80%:
    $$\text{Emily's Percentage} = 100\% - 80\% = 20\%$$
  • This means Emily's share of \$5 represents exactly 20% of the total cost (\(T\)). We can write this as an equation:
    $$20\% \text{ of } T = \$5 \rightarrow 0.20 \times T = 5$$
  • Solve for \(T\) by dividing both sides by 0.20 (which is the same as multiplying by 5, since \(20\% = \frac{1}{5}\)):
    $$T = \frac{5}{0.20} = 5 \times 5 = 25$$
  • Therefore, the total price of the bike was \$25.00 (Stacey paid \(80\% \times \$25 = \$20\)).
Problem 5
Consider the given hexagon with side measurements as marked:
(a) What is the perimeter of the hexagon?
(b) What is the area of the hexagon?

Answer: (a) 42 cm  |  (b) 80 cm²
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Step 1: Find the missing side lengths.
    Let's look at the horizontal components: The total bottom width is \(12\text{ cm}\). The top-right horizontal edge is \(5\text{ cm}\). Thus, the top-left horizontal edge must be:
    $$12\text{ cm} - 5\text{ cm} = 7\text{ cm}$$
    Let's look at the vertical components: The left side height is \(5\text{ cm}\). The inner vertical step drops down by \(4\text{ cm}\) (indicated by the label). Therefore, the rightmost vertical side height must be:
    $$5\text{ cm} + 4\text{ cm} = 9\text{ cm}$$
  • Part (a): Calculate Perimeter
    Sum all 6 side lengths around the perimeter:
    $$\text{Perimeter} = 12 + 9 + 5 + 4 + 7 + 5 = 42\text{ cm}$$
  • Part (b): Calculate Area
    Split the L-shaped hexagon vertically or horizontally into two separate rectangles.
    Let's split it vertically into a Left Rectangle and a Right Rectangle:
    • Left Rectangle dimensions: Width = \(7\text{ cm}\), Height = \(5\text{ cm}\).
      \(\text{Area}_{\text{left}} = 7 \times 5 = 35\text{ cm}^2\)
    • Right Rectangle dimensions: Width = \(5\text{ cm}\), Height = \(9\text{ cm}\).
      \(\text{Area}_{\text{right}} = 5 \times 9 = 45\text{ cm}^2\)
    Sum the two parts together to get the total combined area:
    $$\text{Total Area} = 35\text{ cm}^2 + 45\text{ cm}^2 = 80\text{ cm}^2$$
Problem 6
Simplify: \(0.6 \div (0.4 \times 0.5)\)
Answer: 3
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Following the Order of Operations (PEMDAS), evaluate the expression enclosed inside parentheses first:
    $$0.4 \times 0.5 = 0.20 = 0.2$$
  • Now substitute this value back into the division problem:
    $$0.6 \div 0.2$$
  • To divide decimals easily, move the decimal point one place to the right for both values (or multiply both numerator and denominator by 10):
    $$\frac{0.6}{0.2} = \frac{6}{2} = 3$$
Problem 7
110 students are in a school. They need to be assigned to four classrooms as equally as possible. How many students are in each classroom? (Write 4 numbers to show this)
Answer: 28, 28, 27, 27 (in any order)
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Divide the total number of students (110) by the number of classrooms (4):
    $$110 \div 4 = 27 \text{ with a remainder of } 2$$
  • A baseline of 27 students is placed into each of the 4 classrooms:
    $$4 \times 27 = 108 \text{ students}$$
  • We have \(110 - 108 = 2\) leftover students who must be distributed as evenly as possible among the classrooms.
  • Add 1 extra student to 2 of the classrooms, making them have 28 students each. The other 2 classrooms remain at 27 students.
  • Thus, the size configurations of the four classrooms are: 28, 28, 27, 27.
Problem 8
Write \(1\frac{1}{2}\) million as a number (#).
Answer: 1,500,000
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • One million written in standard numerical value is:
    $$1,000,000$$
  • Half (\(\frac{1}{2}\)) of a million is calculated by dividing 1,000,000 by 2:
    $$\frac{1,000,000}{2} = 500,000$$
  • Combine the one whole million and the half million together:
    $$1,000,000 + 500,000 = 1,500,000$$
Problem 9
Write the prime factorization of 81 using exponents.
Answer: \(3^4\)
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Break down 81 into its prime factors using a factor tree approach:
    $$81 = 9 \times 9$$
  • Break down each 9 into its constituent prime numbers:
    $$9 = 3 \times 3 \rightarrow 81 = (3 \times 3) \times (3 \times 3)$$
  • Collect the factors together as a product of prime numbers:
    $$81 = 3 \times 3 \times 3 \times 3$$
  • Express this repeated multiplication in shorthand exponential form:
    $$81 = 3^4$$
Problem 10
What is the area of the shaded triangle inside the rectangle?

Answer: 40 cm²
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Identify the core structural dimensions of the shaded triangle from the bounding rectangle:
    • The base (\(b\)) of the triangle spans the entire bottom side = \(10\text{ cm}\).
    • The height (\(h\)) of the triangle matches the vertical height of the rectangle = \(8\text{ cm}\).
  • Apply the standard geometric formula for the area of a triangle:
    $$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}$$
  • Substitute the dimension values into our equation:
    $$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times 10\text{ cm} \times 8\text{ cm} = 5 \times 8 = 40\text{ cm}^2$$
  • Note: The area of a triangle that shares the exact same base and height as a bounding rectangle is always exactly half of that rectangle's total area (\(10 \times 8 = 80 \rightarrow 80 \div 2 = 40\text{ cm}^2\)).
