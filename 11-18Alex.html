f.Explain

Why are these division problems equivalent?

\[\frac{2.25}{0.5} = \frac{2.5}{5}\]

Solution

Heads up — the problem as printed in the book is not true. Check both sides:

\[2.25 \div 0.5 = 4.5 \qquad \text{but} \qquad 2.5 \div 5 = 0.5\]

Since \(4.5 \neq 0.5\), the printed equation \(\dfrac{2.25}{0.5} = \dfrac{2.5}{5}\) is a misprint. The intended equivalent division is \(\dfrac{22.5}{5}\), as explained below.

Dividing by a decimal works because we may multiply the numerator and the denominator of a division fraction by the same number without changing its value. Multiplying both parts of \(\dfrac{2.25}{0.5}\) by \(10\) — that is, shifting both decimal points one place to the right — gives:

\[\frac{2.25}{0.5} = \frac{2.25 \times 10}{0.5 \times 10} = \frac{22.5}{5}\]

And indeed \(22.5 \div 5 = 4.5\), the same as \(2.25 \div 0.5\).

\[\boxed{\frac{2.25}{0.5} = \frac{22.5}{5} = 4.5 \text{, because both parts were multiplied by } 10}\]

\[\boxed{x = 15}\]

Written Practice

1.

Raisins and nuts were mixed in a bowl. If nuts made up five eighths of the mixture, what was the ratio of raisins to nuts?

Solution

The whole mixture is \(1\). If nuts are \(\dfrac{5}{8}\) of it, then raisins are the rest:

\[1 - \frac{5}{8} = \frac{3}{8}\]

So the ratio of raisins to nuts is:

\[\frac{3}{8} : \frac{5}{8} = 3 : 5\]
\[\boxed{3 : 5}\]
2.

The taxi ride cost \(\$1\) plus \(80\text{¢}\) more for each quarter mile traveled. What was the total cost for a 2-mile trip?

Solution

A 2-mile trip contains \(2 \div \tfrac{1}{4} = 8\) quarter miles. The distance charge is:

\[8 \times \$0.80 = \$6.40\]

Add the starting charge of \(\$1\):

\[\$1 + \$6.40 = \$7.40\]
\[\boxed{\$7.40}\]

6.Analyze

Name the properties used to simplify the expression \(4\left(\dfrac{1}{4} + 0.3\right)\).

\[4\left(\frac{1}{4} + 0.3\right) \qquad \text{Given}\] \[4\left(\frac{1}{4}\right) + 4(0.3) \qquad \textbf{a. } \rule{4.5cm}{0.4pt}\] \[1 + 4(0.3) \qquad \textbf{b. } \rule{4.5cm}{0.4pt}\] \[1 + 1.2 \qquad 4(0.3) = 1.2\] \[2.2 \qquad 1 + 1.2 = 2.2\]

Solution

a. The step \(4\left(\tfrac{1}{4} + 0.3\right) = 4\left(\tfrac{1}{4}\right) + 4(0.3)\) multiplies the \(4\) by each term inside the parentheses. That is the Distributive Property (of multiplication over addition).

b. The step \(4\left(\tfrac{1}{4}\right) + 4(0.3) = 1 + 4(0.3)\) uses \(4 \times \tfrac{1}{4} = 1\): a number times its reciprocal equals \(1\). That is the Inverse Property of Multiplication.

Finishing the arithmetic, \(1 + 1.2 = 2.2\).

\[\boxed{\text{a. Distributive Property} \qquad \text{b. Inverse Property of Multiplication}}\]

The perimeter of this rectangle is \(56\) cm:

a. What is the length of the rectangle?

b. What is the area of the rectangle?

Solution

a. The perimeter is twice the length plus twice the width (\(10\) cm):

\[2(\text{length} + 10) = 56\] \[\text{length} + 10 = 28 \quad \Longrightarrow \quad \text{length} = 18 \text{ cm}\]

b. Area is length times width:

\[A = 18 \text{ cm} \times 10 \text{ cm} = 180 \text{ cm}^2\]
\[\boxed{\text{a. } 18 \text{ cm} \qquad \text{b. } 180 \text{ cm}^2}\]

b. Here only the \(6\) repeats: \(0.91\overline{6} = 0.916666\ldots\) The first five decimal places are \(91666\), and the next digit is \(6\), so we round the last place up:

\[\boxed{\text{a. } 23.54545 \qquad \text{b. } 0.91667}\]

14.Analyze

Letter cards \(A\) through \(Z\) are placed in a bag. If a card is chosen at random, what is the probability that it will be the card with the letter \(Z\)? Write the answer as a reduced fraction and as a decimal number rounded to the nearest hundredth.

Solution

There are \(26\) letter cards, and only one of them is \(Z\). So the probability is \(1\) out of \(26\):

\[P(Z) = \frac{1}{26}\]

As a decimal, \(1 \div 26 \approx 0.03846\ldots\), which rounds to \(0.04\).

\[\boxed{\frac{1}{26} \approx 0.04}\]
15.

Draw \(\overline{XY}\) \(2\) cm long. Then draw \(\overline{YZ}\) \(1.5\) cm long perpendicular to \(\overline{XY}\). Complete \(\triangle XYZ\) by drawing \(\overline{XZ}\). How long is \(\overline{XZ}\)?

Solution

Because \(\overline{YZ}\) is perpendicular to \(\overline{XY}\), the triangle is a right triangle with the right angle at \(Y\). So \(\overline{XZ}\) is the hypotenuse. By the Pythagorean theorem:

\[XZ^2 = XY^2 + YZ^2 = 2^2 + 1.5^2 = 4 + 2.25 = 6.25\] \[XZ = \sqrt{6.25} = 2.5 \text{ cm}\]
\[\boxed{XZ = 2.5 \text{ cm}}\]

29.Connect

How many \(\$0.21\) pencils can be purchased with \(\$7.00\)?

Solution

Divide the money by the price of one pencil:

\[7.00 \div 0.21 = 33.33\ldots\]

The quotient is not a whole number, and we can only buy whole pencils. Check: \(33\) pencils cost \(33 \times \$0.21 = \$6.93\), which is within the \(\$7.00\). A \(34\)th pencil would bring the total to \(\$7.14\), which is more than \(\$7.00\). So we round down to \(33\).

\[\boxed{33 \text{ pencils}}\]
30.Conclude

Amanda cut out a triangle and labeled the corners \(a\), \(b\), and \(c\) as shown. Then she tore off the three corners and fit the pieces together to form the semicircular shape shown at right.

a. Try the activity described in this problem, and tape or glue the fitted corners to your paper.

b. Infer Describe the characteristic of triangles demonstrated by this activity.

Solution

a. Do the activity like this:

1. Cut out a paper triangle and label its three corners \(a\), \(b\), and \(c\).
2. Tear (or cut) off the three corners.
3. Fit the three corner pieces together, vertex to vertex, along a straight edge so their angles meet at one point with no gaps or overlaps.
4. Tape or glue the fitted corners to your paper. They form a semicircular shape:

b. The three fitted corners exactly fill a semicircle. A semicircle is half of a circle — it is a straight angle, which measures \(180^{\circ}\). So the three interior angles of the triangle, placed side by side, add up to \(180^{\circ}\):

\[a + b + c = 180^{\circ}\]
\[\boxed{\text{The three interior angles of a triangle add up to } 180^{\circ}}\]
