Part 1: Circumference & Diameter

1. A circle has a radius of 5 cm. What is the circumference? (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\))
Explanation: First, find the diameter. Diameter is twice the radius: \(d = 2 \times 5 = 10\text{ cm}\).
Then, multiply the diameter by \(\pi\): \(C = 10 \times 3.14 = 31.4\text{ cm}\).
2. A circle has a radius of 10 meters. What is the circumference? (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\))
Explanation: The diameter is twice the radius: \(d = 2 \times 10 = 20\text{ m}\).
The circumference is: \(C = 20 \times 3.14 = 62.8\text{ m}\).
3. If a circle has a radius of 3 inches, what is its diameter?
Explanation: The diameter is simply twice the length of the radius. \(d = 2 \times 3\text{ in} = 6\text{ in}\).
4. A circle has a radius of 6 feet. What is the circumference? (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\))
Explanation: The diameter is \(2 \times 6 = 12\text{ ft}\).
The circumference is: \(C = 12 \times 3.14 = 37.68\text{ ft}\).
5. A coin has a radius of 2 mm. What is the circumference? (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\))
Explanation: The diameter is \(2 \times 2 = 4\text{ mm}\).
The circumference is: \(C = 4 \times 3.14 = 12.56\text{ mm}\).

Part 2: Area of a Circle

6. A circle has a radius of 5 cm. What is the area? (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\))
Explanation: The formula for area is \(A = \pi \times r^2\).
Square the radius first: \(5^2 = 25\).
Then multiply by \(\pi\): \(25 \times 3.14 = 78.5\text{ sq cm}\).
7. A circle has a radius of 10 meters. What is the area? (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\))
Explanation: \(A = \pi \times r^2\).
Square the radius: \(10 \times 10 = 100\).
Multiply by \(\pi\): \(100 \times 3.14 = 314\text{ sq m}\).
8. A plate has a radius of 3 inches. What is the area? (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\))
Explanation: \(A = \pi \times r^2\).
Square the radius: \(3^2 = 9\).
Multiply by \(\pi\): \(9 \times 3.14 = 28.26\text{ sq in}\).
9. A button has a radius of 2 mm. What is the area? (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\))
Explanation: \(A = \pi \times r^2\).
Square the radius: \(2^2 = 4\).
Multiply by \(\pi\): \(4 \times 3.14 = 12.56\text{ sq mm}\).
10. A rug has a radius of 6 feet. What is the area? (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\))
Explanation: \(A = \pi \times r^2\).
Square the radius: \(6^2 = 36\).
Multiply by \(\pi\): \(36 \times 3.14 = 113.04\text{ sq ft}\).

Part 3: Converting & Multiplying

11. Write \(\frac{1}{4}\) as a decimal, and multiply it by 0.8. What is the product?
Explanation: First, convert the fraction: \(\frac{1}{4} = 0.25\).
Next, multiply: \(0.25 \times 0.8 = 0.200\), which simplifies to \(0.2\).
12. Write \(\frac{1}{2}\) as a decimal, and multiply it by 1.4. What is the product?
Explanation: Convert the fraction: \(\frac{1}{2} = 0.5\).
Multiply: \(0.5 \times 1.4 = 0.70\), which is \(0.7\).
13. Write \(\frac{4}{5}\) as a decimal, and multiply it by 0.5. What is the product?
Explanation: Convert the fraction: \(\frac{4}{5} = \frac{8}{10} = 0.8\).
Multiply: \(0.8 \times 0.5 = 0.40\), which is \(0.4\).
14. Write \(\frac{3}{4}\) as a decimal, and multiply it by 1.2. What is the product?
Explanation: Convert the fraction: \(\frac{3}{4} = 0.75\).
Multiply: \(0.75 \times 1.2 = 0.900\), which is \(0.9\).
15. Write \(\frac{1}{5}\) as a decimal, and multiply it by 0.3. What is the product?
Explanation: Convert the fraction: \(\frac{1}{5} = 0.2\).
Multiply: \(0.2 \times 0.3 = 0.06\). (Remember to count two decimal places total).

Part 4: Dividing by Mixed Numbers

16. Divide: \(4 \div 1\frac{1}{3}\)
Explanation: First, convert the mixed number to an improper fraction: \(1\frac{1}{3} = \frac{4}{3}\).
Next, multiply by the reciprocal: \(4 \times \frac{3}{4}\).
\(\frac{4}{1} \times \frac{3}{4} = \frac{12}{4} = 3\).
17. Divide: \(5 \div 2\frac{1}{2}\)
Explanation: Convert to an improper fraction: \(2\frac{1}{2} = \frac{5}{2}\).
Multiply by the reciprocal: \(5 \times \frac{2}{5}\).
\(\frac{5}{1} \times \frac{2}{5} = \frac{10}{5} = 2\).
18. Divide: \(2 \div 3\frac{1}{3}\)
Explanation: Convert to an improper fraction: \(3\frac{1}{3} = \frac{10}{3}\).
Multiply by the reciprocal: \(2 \times \frac{3}{10}\).
\(\frac{2}{1} \times \frac{3}{10} = \frac{6}{10}\), which simplifies to \(\frac{3}{5}\).
19. Divide: \(6 \div 1\frac{1}{2}\)
Explanation: Convert to an improper fraction: \(1\frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{2}\).
Multiply by the reciprocal: \(6 \times \frac{2}{3}\).
\(\frac{6}{1} \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{12}{3} = 4\).
20. Divide: \(3 \div 4\frac{1}{2}\)
Explanation: Convert to an improper fraction: \(4\frac{1}{2} = \frac{9}{2}\).
Multiply by the reciprocal: \(3 \times \frac{2}{9}\).
\(\frac{3}{1} \times \frac{2}{9} = \frac{6}{9}\), which simplifies to \(\frac{2}{3}\).
