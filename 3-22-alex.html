Saxon Math Course 1 - Alternative Practice Questions

Lesson Practice

Note: Problems a-c refer to Polygon EFGH divided into two triangles by segment EG.

a. What is the sum of the interior angles of the first triangle, \(\Delta EFG\)?
Answer: 180°

Explanation: Regardless of the size or shape of the triangle, the sum of the interior angles of any triangle is always \(180^\circ\).

b. What is the sum of the interior angles of the second triangle, \(\Delta EHG\)?
Answer: 180°

Explanation: Just like the first triangle, the interior angles of \(\Delta EHG\) sum to \(180^\circ\).

c. GENERALIZE: What is the sum of the measures of the four interior angles of the polygon EFGH?
Answer: 360°

Explanation: The quadrilateral is composed of the two triangles. Adding their angle sums together gives \(180^\circ + 180^\circ = 360^\circ\).

d. What is \(m\angle X\) in \(\Delta XYZ\)? (Given \(m\angle Y = 65^\circ\) and \(m\angle Z = 45^\circ\))
Answer: 70°

Explanation: The sum of angles in a triangle is \(180^\circ\).
\(m\angle X = 180^\circ - (m\angle Y + m\angle Z)\)
\(m\angle X = 180^\circ - (65^\circ + 45^\circ) = 180^\circ - 110^\circ = 70^\circ\).

e. What is the measure of each interior angle of a regular pentagon?
Answer: 108°

Explanation: A pentagon can be split into 3 triangles, so its total interior angle sum is \(3 \times 180^\circ = 540^\circ\). Since it is regular, all 5 angles are equal.
\(540^\circ \div 5 = 108^\circ\).

f. MODEL: Marcus made six left turns as he ran around the perimeter of a large building. Find the average number of degrees in each turn.
Answer: 60°

Explanation: Running completely around a structure means making one full rotation, or \(360^\circ\).
Average degrees per turn = \(360^\circ \div 6 = 60^\circ\).

Written Practice

1. When the sum of \(\frac{1}{3}\) and \(\frac{1}{4}\) is divided by the product of \(\frac{1}{3}\) and \(\frac{1}{4}\), what is the quotient?
Answer: 7

Explanation:
Sum: \(\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{4}{12} + \frac{3}{12} = \frac{7}{12}\)
Product: \(\frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{12}\)
Quotient: \(\frac{7}{12} \div \frac{1}{12} = \frac{7}{12} \cdot \frac{12}{1} = 7\).

2. ANALYZE: David is \(4 \frac{3}{4}\) feet tall. He is how many inches tall?
Answer: 57 inches

Explanation: There are 12 inches in a foot.
\(4 \frac{3}{4} \text{ feet} = 4.75 \times 12 \text{ inches} = 57 \text{ inches}\).

3. If \(\frac{3}{4}\) of the 160 students passed the exam, how many students did not pass?
Answer: 40 students

Explanation: If \(\frac{3}{4}\) passed, then \(\frac{1}{4}\) did not pass.
\(\frac{1}{4} \text{ of } 160 = 160 \div 4 = 40\).

4. Lines \(m\) and \(n\) are parallel, cut by a transversal.
If alternate interior angle \(x\) measures \(105^\circ\), what is the measure of its corresponding angle \(y\)?
Answer: 105°

Explanation:
If lines are parallel, both alternate interior angles and corresponding angles mapped to the same intersection relative position are congruent. Thus, \(y = 105^\circ\).

5. ANALYZE: The population of a small town is about 38,000. Write that number in expanded notation using exponents.
Answer: (3 × 10⁴) + (8 × 10³)

Explanation: The digit 3 is in the ten-thousands place (\(10^4\)) and the digit 8 is in the thousands place (\(10^3\)). Expanded: \( (3 \times 10^4) + (8 \times 10^3) \).

6. CONNECT: Which of these clock parts is the best model of the radius of the clock face?
A. The outer rim    B. The minute hand    C. The central gear
Answer: B. The minute hand

Explanation: The radius is the distance from the center of a circle to its edge. The minute hand extends from the center pivot to the outer edge of the numbers.

7. PREDICT: As this sequence continues, each term equals the sum of the two previous terms. What is the next term in this sequence?
2, 2, 4, 6, 10, 16, ...
Answer: 26

Explanation: This follows a Fibonacci-style pattern. Add the two previous numbers to get the next: \(10 + 16 = 26\).

8. If there is a 35% chance of snow, what is the probability that it will not snow?
Answer: 65%

Explanation: The sum of all probabilities must equal 100%.
\(100\% - 35\% \text{ (chance of snow)} = 65\% \text{ (chance of no snow)}\).

9. Write \(1 \frac{1}{4}\) as a percent.
Answer: 125%

Explanation: To convert a fraction to a percent, multiply by 100%.
\(1 \frac{1}{4} = \frac{5}{4}\).
\(\frac{5}{4} \times 100\% = \frac{500}{4}\% = 125\%\).

10. ANALYZE: \(0.05y = \$0.45\)
Answer: y = 9

Explanation: Divide both sides by 0.05 to isolate \(y\).
\(y = \frac{0.45}{0.05} = \frac{45}{5} = 9\).

11. \(\frac{1 - 0.005}{0.05}\)
Answer: 19.9

Explanation: First, subtract the numerator: \(1 - 0.005 = 0.995\).
Then, divide by the denominator: \(\frac{0.995}{0.05} = \frac{99.5}{5} = 19.9\).

12. \(2 \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{4}{100}\)
Answer: 1/10

Explanation: Convert the mixed number to an improper fraction:
\(2 \frac{1}{2} = \frac{5}{2}\).
Multiply the fractions: \(\frac{5}{2} \cdot \frac{4}{100} = \frac{20}{200} = \frac{1}{10}\).

13. If the volume of each small block is one cubic centimeter, what is the volume of a rectangular prism that is 5 blocks wide, 3 blocks deep, and 2 blocks high?
Answer: 30 cubic centimeters

Explanation: Volume = \(length \times width \times height = 5 \times 3 \times 2 = 30\) blocks.
Since each block is 1 cubic centimeter, the total volume is 30 cubic centimeters.

14. \(5 \frac{1}{4} + 3.85\) (decimal)
Answer: 9.10

Explanation: Convert \(5 \frac{1}{4}\) to a decimal: \(5.25\).
Add the decimals: \(5.25 + 3.85 = 9.10\).

15. \(3 \frac{1}{3} - 1.5\) (fraction)
Answer: 1 5/6 (or 11/6)

Explanation: Convert \(1.5\) to a fraction: \(1 \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{2}\). Convert \(3 \frac{1}{3}\) to an improper fraction: \(\frac{10}{3}\).
Find a common denominator (6): \(\frac{3}{2} = \frac{9}{6}\) and \(\frac{10}{3} = \frac{20}{6}\).
Subtract: \(\frac{20}{6} - \frac{9}{6} = \frac{11}{6}\).
Simplify: \(\frac{11}{6} = 1 \frac{5}{6}\).

16. If a jacket costs $24.89 and the sales-tax rate is 5%, what is the total price including tax? Explain how you can check your answer using estimation.
Answer: $26.13

Explanation:
Tax = \( \$24.89 \times 0.05 = 1.2445 \approx \$1.24 \).
Total Price = \( \$24.89 + \$1.24 = \$26.13 \).
Estimation Check: Round $24.89 to $25.00. 5% of $25 is $1.25. $25.00 + $1.25 = $26.25. Since the actual price is slightly less than $25, our answer of $26.13 makes sense.

17. What fraction of a foot is 8 inches?
Answer: 2/3

Explanation: There are 12 inches in a foot. The fraction is \(\frac{8}{12}\), which simplifies by dividing the top and bottom by 4 to get \(\frac{2}{3}\).

18. What percent of a meter is 15 centimeters?
Answer: 15%

Explanation: There are 100 centimeters in a meter. 15 out of 100 is represented as \(\frac{15}{100}\), which is exactly \(15\%\).

19. The ratio of cats to dogs at the shelter was 7 to 4. If there were 35 cats, how many dogs were there?
Answer: 20 dogs

Explanation: Set up a proportion: \(\frac{\text{Cats}}{\text{Dogs}} = \frac{7}{4} = \frac{35}{x}\).
Multiply by a scale factor: \(7 \times 5 = 35\), so multiply the bottom by 5 as well.
\(4 \times 5 = 20\).

20. Arrange these numbers in order from least to greatest:
\(2, -2, 0, \frac{3}{4}, -\frac{3}{4}\)
Answer: -2, -3/4, 0, 3/4, 2

Explanation: Negative numbers are smaller than positive numbers. Larger negative magnitudes represent smaller values. Order on a number line: \(-2, -\frac{3}{4}, 0, \frac{3}{4}, 2\).

21. CLASSIFY: A quadrilateral is formed with exactly two pairs of parallel sides. What type of quadrilateral is formed?
Answer: Parallelogram

Explanation:
By definition, a quadrilateral with two pairs of parallel opposite sides is called a parallelogram.

22. a. ANALYZE: What is the measure of \(\angle P\) in \(\Delta PQR\) if \(\angle Q = 100^\circ\) and \(\angle R = 50^\circ\)?
b. ANALYZE: What is the measure of the exterior angle adjacent to \(\angle Q\)?
Answer: a. m∠P = 30°, b. 80°

Explanation:
a. The sum of angles in a triangle is \(180^\circ\).
\(m\angle P = 180^\circ - (100^\circ + 50^\circ) = 180^\circ - 150^\circ = 30^\circ\).
b. The exterior angle and its adjacent interior angle are supplementary (add to \(180^\circ\)).
Exterior angle = \(180^\circ - 100^\circ = 80^\circ\).

23. Write 60% as a:
a. simplified fraction.
b. decimal number.
Answer: a. 3/5, b. 0.6

Explanation:
a. \(60\% = \frac{60}{100}\). Divide top and bottom by 20 to simplify: \(\frac{3}{5}\).
b. \(60\% = \frac{60}{100} = 0.60 = 0.6\).

24. The diameter of a circle is 10 mm. What is the area of the circle? (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\).)
Answer: 78.5 mm²

Explanation:
First, find the radius: \(r = \frac{diameter}{2} = \frac{10}{2} = 5\text{ mm}\).
Area formula: \(A = \pi r^2\).
\(A \approx 3.14 \times (5)^2 = 3.14 \times 25 = 78.5\text{ mm}^2\).

25. \(3^2 + \sqrt{64} \div 2^3 + \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2\)
Answer: 10.25 (or 10 1/4)

Explanation: Follow the order of operations (PEMDAS/GEMDAS).
Exponents and roots: \(3^2 = 9\), \(\sqrt{64} = 8\), \(2^3 = 8\), \(\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{4}\) (or \(0.25\)).
Substitute back into equation: \(9 + 8 \div 8 + 0.25\).
Division: \(8 \div 8 = 1\).
Addition: \(9 + 1 + 0.25 = 10.25\).

26. Multiply 180 inches by 1 foot per 12 inches.
\(\frac{180\text{ in.}}{1} \times \frac{1\text{ ft}}{12\text{ in.}}\)
Answer: 15 ft

Explanation: Multiply the fractions and cancel the units of inches.
\(\frac{180}{12}\text{ ft} = 15\text{ ft}\).

27. A bag contains 12 green blocks and 18 yellow blocks.
a. What is the ratio of green blocks to yellow blocks?
b. If one block is drawn from the bag, what is the probability that the block will be yellow?
Answer: a. 2:3 (or 2/3), b. 3/5

Explanation:
a. Ratio = Green / Yellow = \( \frac{12}{18} \). Divide top and bottom by 6 to get \(\frac{2}{3}\).
b. Total blocks = \(12 + 18 = 30\). Probability of yellow = \( \frac{\text{Yellow}}{\text{Total}} = \frac{18}{30} \). Simplify by dividing by 6 to get \(\frac{3}{5}\).

28. CONCLUDE: A garden bed is framed by a triangular wooden border. The three pieces of wood are exactly 15 feet long each. Classified by side length, what type of triangle is formed?
Answer: Equilateral triangle

Explanation:
A triangle with all three sides of equal length is classified as an equilateral triangle. (Note: Since all equilateral triangles are also isosceles, it can technically be called isosceles too, but equilateral is the most precise classification).
