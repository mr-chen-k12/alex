Topic: Prime Numbers & Multiplication (Review of #1)

1. What is the product of the next two prime numbers after 7?
Explanation: A prime number is only divisible by 1 and itself. The prime numbers following 7 are 11 and 13. Their product is $11 \times 13 = 143$.
2. What is the sum of the first four prime numbers?
Explanation: The first four prime numbers are 2, 3, 5, and 7. Their sum is $2 + 3 + 5 + 7 = 17$. (Note: 1 is not a prime number).
3. What is the product of the first three odd prime numbers?
Explanation: The first prime number is 2, but it is even. The first three odd prime numbers are 3, 5, and 7. Their product is $3 \times 5 \times 7 = 105$.

Topic: Ratios (Review of #3)

4. A 60-minute class consists of 15 minutes of lecture, and the rest of the time is spent on group work. What is the ratio of lecture time to group work time?
Explanation: Total time = 60 mins. Lecture time = 15 mins. Group work time = $60 - 15 = 45$ mins. The ratio of lecture to group work is $15:45$. Dividing both sides by 15 simplifies this to $1:3$.
5. There are 24 hours in a day. If you sleep for 8 hours, what is the ratio of hours spent sleeping to hours spent awake?
Explanation: Total time = 24 hours. Sleep time = 8 hours. Awake time = $24 - 8 = 16$ hours. The ratio of sleep to awake time is $8:16$. Dividing by 8 simplifies this to $1:2$.
6. A bag contains 20 apples. If 4 of the apples are bruised, what is the ratio of bruised apples to unbruised apples?
Explanation: Total apples = 20. Bruised = 4. Unbruised = $20 - 4 = 16$. The ratio of bruised to unbruised is $4:16$. Dividing by 4 gives $1:4$.

Topic: Fractions to Decimals (Review of #9a)

7. Convert the fraction $\frac{3}{8}$ to a decimal.
Explanation: To convert a fraction to a decimal, divide the numerator by the denominator. $3 \div 8 = 0.375$.
8. Convert the fraction $\frac{9}{20}$ to a decimal.
Explanation: Divide $9$ by $20$. An easy mental math trick is to multiply the top and bottom by 5 to get a denominator of 100: $\frac{9 \times 5}{20 \times 5} = \frac{45}{100} = 0.45$.
9. Convert the fraction $\frac{5}{16}$ to a decimal.
Explanation: Divide $5$ by $16$ using long division. $5.0000 \div 16 = 0.3125$.

Topic: Basic Probability (Review of #12a)

10. A standard 6-sided die is rolled once. What is the probability of rolling a number less than 3?
Explanation: The numbers less than 3 on a die are 1 and 2. That is 2 favorable outcomes out of 6 possible outcomes. $\frac{2}{6}$ simplifies to $\frac{1}{3}$.
11. A spinner is divided into 5 equal sections labeled 1 through 5. What is the probability of the spinner landing on an even number?
Explanation: The even numbers on the spinner are 2 and 4. That is 2 favorable outcomes out of 5 total sections. The probability is $\frac{2}{5}$.
12. All the letters of the word "MATHEMATICS" are placed into a bag. If you draw one letter at random, what is the probability of drawing a vowel?
Explanation: "MATHEMATICS" has 11 letters total. The vowels are A, E, A, and I (4 vowels). The probability is $\frac{4}{11}$.

Topic: Converting & Rounding Decimals (Review of #17)

13. Write the fraction $\frac{5}{6}$ as a decimal number rounded to the hundredths place.
Explanation: $5 \div 6 = 0.8333...$ To round to the hundredths place, look at the thousandths digit (3). Since 3 is less than 5, keep the hundredths digit the same. Answer: $0.83$.
14. Write the fraction $\frac{2}{7}$ as a decimal number rounded to the hundredths place.
Explanation: $2 \div 7 \approx 0.2857...$ Looking at the thousandths digit (5), we round the hundredths digit (8) up by 1. Answer: $0.29$.
15. Write the fraction $\frac{8}{9}$ as a decimal number rounded to the hundredths place.
Explanation: $8 \div 9 = 0.8888...$ The digit in the thousandths place is 8, which is 5 or greater, so we round the hundredths digit up. Answer: $0.89$.

Topic: Scale Factor (Review of #19)

16. Rectangle A is 4 feet wide and 5 feet long. Rectangle B is 12 feet wide and 15 feet long. What is the scale factor from Rectangle A to Rectangle B?
Explanation: Divide a side length of Rectangle B by the corresponding side length of Rectangle A. $\frac{12}{4} = 3$ or $\frac{15}{5} = 3$. The scale factor is $3$.
17. Triangle X has a base of 20 cm. A similar Triangle Y has a corresponding base of 5 cm. What is the scale factor from Triangle X to Triangle Y?
Explanation: We are going from a larger shape to a smaller shape, so the scale factor is a fraction. Divide the side of Y by the corresponding side of X: $\frac{5}{20} = \frac{1}{4}$.
18. A square has a perimeter of 12 inches. A similar, larger square has a perimeter of 48 inches. What is the scale factor from the smaller square to the larger square?
Explanation: The scale factor for perimeters is the exact same as the scale factor for the side lengths. $\frac{48}{12} = 4$. (To verify: side lengths are 3 and 12, respectively. $\frac{12}{3} = 4$).

Topic: Integer Arithmetic (Review of #21c)

19. Evaluate mentally: $-5 - (+9)$
Explanation: Subtracting a positive 9 is the same as adding a negative 9. $-5 + (-9) = -14$. (If you owe $\$5$ and then lose another $\$9$, you are down $\$14$).
20. Evaluate mentally: $-12 - (+4)$
Explanation: $-12 - 4$ means starting at $-12$ on the number line and moving 4 units further left. This results in $-16$.
21. Evaluate mentally: $3 - (+10)$
Explanation: You have 3 but you are subtracting 10. Since you are subtracting a larger number from a smaller number, the result drops below zero. $3 - 10 = -7$.

Topic: Area of Triangles (Review of #22)

22. A right triangle has legs measuring 6 inches and 8 inches. What is the area of the triangle?
Explanation: The legs of a right triangle serve as its base and height. Area $= \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = \frac{1}{2} \times 6 \times 8 = \frac{1}{2} \times 48 = 24$.
23. A triangle has a base of 10 meters and a height of 7 meters. What is the area of the triangle?
Explanation: Area $= \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = \frac{1}{2} \times 10 \times 7 = \frac{1}{2} \times 70 = 35 \text{ sq m}$.
24. What is the area of a right triangle that has legs measuring 12 cm and 5 cm?
Explanation: Area $= \frac{1}{2} \times 12 \times 5 = \frac{1}{2} \times 60 = 30 \text{ sq cm}$.

Topic: Dependent Probability (Drawing without Replacement)

25. A bag contains 6 red marbles and 4 blue marbles. If you draw one marble, keep it out of the bag (do not replace it), and then draw a second marble, what is the probability that both marbles are red?
Explanation: First draw (red): There are 6 red marbles out of 10 total. Probability = $\frac{6}{10}$.
Second draw (red): Because you did not replace the first marble, there are now 5 red marbles left out of 9 total. Probability = $\frac{5}{9}$.
Multiply the probabilities: $\frac{6}{10} \times \frac{5}{9} = \frac{30}{90} = \frac{1}{3}$.
26. A bag contains 5 green marbles and 3 yellow marbles. What is the probability of drawing two green marbles in a row without replacing the first marble?
Explanation: First draw (green): 5 green out of 8 total ($\frac{5}{8}$).
Second draw (green): 4 green left out of 7 total ($\frac{4}{7}$).
Multiply: $\frac{5}{8} \times \frac{4}{7} = \frac{20}{56}$. Dividing numerator and denominator by 4 simplifies this to $\frac{5}{14}$.
27. A bag contains 4 black marbles and 6 white marbles. You draw a marble and do not replace it, then you draw a second marble. What is the probability that you draw a black marble first, followed by a white marble second?
Explanation: First draw (black): 4 black out of 10 total ($\frac{4}{10}$).
Second draw (white): 6 white out of 9 total marbles remaining ($\frac{6}{9}$).
Multiply: $\frac{4}{10} \times \frac{6}{9} = \frac{24}{90}$. Dividing numerator and denominator by 6 simplifies this to $\frac{4}{15}$.
