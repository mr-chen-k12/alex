Practice A
Jenny had \( 34 \) marbles. Her brother gave her some more marbles. Then she had \( 61 \) marbles. How many marbles did her brother give her? Write an equation and solve.
Final Answer
\( 27 \text{ marbles} \)
Equation: \( 34 + m = 61 \). Subtract: \( 61 - 34 = 27 \).
Practice B
There were \( 250 \) students in the auditorium. After some students left, there were \( 184 \) students remaining. How many students left?
Final Answer
\( 66 \text{ students} \)
Equation: \( 250 - s = 184 \). Subtract: \( 250 - 184 = 66 \).
Problem 1WORD PROBLEM
The basketball team scored \( 42 \) points in the first half and \( 39 \) points in the second half. How many total points did they score?
Final Answer
\( 81 \text{ points} \)
\( 42 + 39 = 81 \).
Problem 2
A length of ribbon was \( 84 \text{ cm} \) long. After cutting off a piece, \( 29 \text{ cm} \) remained. How long was the piece cut off?
Final Answer
\( 55 \text{ cm} \)
\( 84 - p = 29 \implies p = 84 - 29 = 55 \text{ cm} \).
Problem 3
Write the prime factorization of \( 48 \) using exponents.
Final Answer
\( 2^4 \times 3 \)
\( 48 = 16 \times 3 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3 = 2^4 \times 3 \).
Problem 4
Find the perimeter of a rectangle with a length of \( 12 \text{ in.} \) and a width of \( 7 \text{ in.} \)
Final Answer
\( 38 \text{ inches} \)
\( P = 2(12) + 2(7) = 24 + 14 = 38 \text{ in.} \)
Problem 5
Simplify: \( \frac{3}{5} + \frac{1}{5} + \frac{4}{5} \)
Final Answer
\( 1\frac{3}{5} \)
\( \frac{3+1+4}{5} = \frac{8}{5} = 1\frac{3}{5} \).
Problem 6
Solve for $n$: \( 150 - n = 67 \)
Final Answer
\( n = 83 \)
\( n = 150 - 67 = 83 \).
Problem 7
Find the area of a square with sides measuring \( 9 \text{ cm} \).
Final Answer
\( 81 \text{ cm}^2 \)
\( A = \text{side}^2 = 9 \times 9 = 81 \text{ cm}^2 \).
Problem 8
Which digit in \( 4,582.91 \) is in the tenths place?
Final Answer
\( 9 \)
The digit directly to the right of the decimal point is the tenths place.
Problem 9
Round \( 7,384 \) to the nearest hundred.
Final Answer
\( 7,400 \)
The tens digit is \( 8 \) (\(\ge 5\)), so round up to \( 7,400 \).
Problem 10
Evaluate: \( (12 - 4) \times (3 + 5) \)
Final Answer
\( 64 \)
\( 8 \times 8 = 64 \).
Problem 11
Find the average (mean) of \( 14 \), \( 22 \), \( 18 \), and \( 26 \).
Final Answer
\( 20 \)
\( (14 + 22 + 18 + 26) \div 4 = 80 \div 4 = 20 \).
Problem 12
Convert the improper fraction \( \frac{23}{5} \) to a mixed number.
Final Answer
\( 4\frac{3}{5} \)
\( 23 \div 5 = 4 \) with a remainder of \( 3 \).
Problem 13
Solve for $x$: \( 8x = 96 \)
Final Answer
\( x = 12 \)
\( x = 96 \div 8 = 12 \).
Problem 14
Find the Greatest Common Factor (GCF) of \( 24 \) and \( 36 \).
Final Answer
\( 12 \)
Factors of \( 24 \): 1,2,3,4,6,8,12,24. Factors of \( 36 \): 1,2,3,4,6,9,12,18,36. GCF is \( 12 \).
Problem 15
Find the Least Common Multiple (LCM) of \( 6 \) and \( 8 \).
Final Answer
\( 24 \)
Multiples of \( 6 \): 6, 12, 18, 24. Multiples of \( 8 \): 8, 16, 24. LCM is \( 24 \).
Problem 16
Simplify: \( 4.25 + 1.802 \)
Final Answer
\( 6.052 \)
Line up decimal points: \( 4.250 + 1.802 = 6.052 \).
Problem 17
What is the reciprocal of \( \frac{7}{3} \)?
Final Answer
\( \frac{3}{7} \)
Invert numerator and denominator: \( \frac{3}{7} \).
Problem 18
A circle has a radius of \( 5 \text{ cm} \). What is its diameter?
Final Answer
\( 10 \text{ cm} \)
\( \text{Diameter} = 2 \times \text{radius} = 2 \times 5 = 10 \text{ cm} \).
Problem 19
Reduce the fraction \( \frac{18}{24} \) to lowest terms.
Final Answer
\( \frac{3}{4} \)
Divide numerator and denominator by 6: \( \frac{18 \div 6}{24 \div 6} = \frac{3}{4} \).
Problem 20
Compare using \( < \), \( > \), or \( = \): \( 0.4 \quad \bigcirc \quad 0.38 \)
Final Answer
\( 0.4 > 0.38 \)
\( 0.40 \) is greater than \( 0.38 \).
Problem 21
Simplify: \( 10^3 - 10^2 \)
Final Answer
\( 900 \)
\( 1000 - 100 = 900 \).
Problem 22
A triathlete swam \( 1.5 \text{ km} \), biked \( 40 \text{ km} \), and ran \( 10 \text{ km} \). What was the total distance?
Final Answer
\( 51.5 \text{ km} \)
\( 1.5 + 40 + 10 = 51.5 \text{ km} \).
Problem 23
How many inches are in \( 4\frac{1}{2} \text{ feet} \)?
Final Answer
\( 54 \text{ inches} \)
\( 4.5 \times 12 = 54 \text{ inches} \).
Problem 24
Identify the acute angle measure among the following: \( 45^\circ, 90^\circ, 120^\circ, 180^\circ \).
Final Answer
\( 45^\circ \)
An acute angle measures less than \( 90^\circ \).
Problem 25
Solve: \( \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{3}{4} \)
Final Answer
\( \frac{1}{2} \)
\( \frac{2 \times 3}{3 \times 4} = \frac{6}{12} = \frac{1}{2} \).
Problem 26
Find the missing number: \( 3\frac{1}{8} + m = 7\frac{5}{8} \)
Final Answer
\( 4\frac{1}{2} \)
\( m = 7\frac{5}{8} - 3\frac{1}{8} = 4\frac{4}{8} = 4\frac{1}{2} \).
Problem 27
Write \( \frac{3}{100} \) as a decimal and as a percent.
Final Answer
\( 0.03 \text{ and } 3\% \)
\( 3 \div 100 = 0.03 = 3\% \).
Problem 28
Calculate the perimeter of an equilateral triangle with sides of \( 8.5 \text{ cm} \).
Final Answer
\( 25.5 \text{ cm} \)
\( P = 3 \times 8.5 = 25.5 \text{ cm} \).
Problem 29
Simplify: \( 50 - [3 \times (4 + 2)] \)
Final Answer
\( 32 \)
\( 4 + 2 = 6 \implies 3 \times 6 = 18 \implies 50 - 18 = 32 \).
Problem 30
Alex had \( \$50.00 \). He bought three books for \( \$12.50 \) each. How much money did he have left?
Final Answer
\( \$12.50 \)
Total spent = \( 3 \times 12.50 = \$37.50 \). Remaining = \( 50.00 - 37.50 = \$12.50 \).
