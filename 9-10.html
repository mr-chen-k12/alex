Written Practice 1
Jack had $420$ stamps in his collection. He gave $145$ stamps to his brother. How many stamps does Jack have left?
Show your work / notes:
Solution: $420 - 145 = 275$ stamps.

Solution: $8 \times 24 = 192$ pencils.

Written Practice 5
A rectangle has a length of $12\text{ cm}$ and a width of $5\text{ cm}$. Find its perimeter and area.
Show your work / notes:
Solution:

Area $= 12 \times 5 = 60\text{ cm}^2$.
Written Practice 6

Show your work / notes:
Solution: Divide numerator and denominator by $\text{GCF}(16,24) = 8$: $\frac{16 \div 8}{24 \div 8} = \frac{2}{3}$.

Written Practice 26
Find the greatest common factor (GCF) of $24$ and $36$.
Show your work / notes:
Solution: Factors of 24: $1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24$. Factors of 36: $1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 36$. $\text{GCF} = 12$.

Written Practice 29

Written Practice 30
A standard 6-sided die is rolled. What is the probability of rolling an even number? Express as a simplified fraction.
Show your work / notes:
Solution: Even numbers are $2, 4, 6$ ($3$ out of $6$). Probability $= \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}$.
