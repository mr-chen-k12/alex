Problem 1
Solve the following quadratic equation for \( x \): $$ 2x^2 - 4x - 6 = 0 $$
Answer: \( x = 3 \) or \( x = -1 \)
Explanation:

First, we can simplify the equation by dividing the entire equation by 2:

$$ x^2 - 2x - 3 = 0 $$

Next, we factor the quadratic equation. We need two numbers that multiply to \(-3\) and add up to \(-2\). Those numbers are \(-3\) and \(1\).

$$ (x - 3)(x + 1) = 0 $$

Setting each factor to zero gives us our solutions:

$$ x - 3 = 0 \implies x = 3 $$ $$ x + 1 = 0 \implies x = -1 $$
Problem 2
Evaluate the following indefinite integral: $$ \int x \cos(x) \, dx $$
Answer: \( x \sin(x) + \cos(x) + C \)
Explanation:

We use integration by parts, where the formula is \( \int u \, dv = uv - \int v \, du \).

Let:

  • \( u = x \implies du = dx \)
  • \( dv = \cos(x) \, dx \implies v = \sin(x) \)

Substitute these into the formula:

$$ \int x \cos(x) \, dx = x \sin(x) - \int \sin(x) \, dx $$

Now, integrate \( \sin(x) \):

$$ \int \sin(x) \, dx = -\cos(x) $$

Putting it all together and adding the constant of integration \( C \):

$$ x \sin(x) - (-\cos(x)) + C = x \sin(x) + \cos(x) + C $$
