d. Sixty is \( \frac{3}{8} \) of what number?
Answer: 160

Explanation: Set up the equation: \( 60 = \frac{3}{8}x \). To solve for \( x \), multiply both sides by the reciprocal \( \frac{8}{3} \).

Answer: 30 students

Explanation: Let \( x \) be the total number of students. We know \( \frac{3}{5}x = 18 \).
Multiply by the reciprocal: \( x = 18 \times \frac{5}{3} = \frac{90}{3} = 30 \).
1. Three fifths of the townspeople voted. If 120 of the townspeople voted, how many people live in the town?
Answer: 200 people

Explanation: Let \( T \) be the total townspeople. \( \frac{3}{5}T = 120 \).
\( T = 120 \times \frac{5}{3} = 40 \times 5 = 200 \).
2. If 130 children are separated as equally as possible into four groups, how many will be in each group? (Write four numbers, one for each of the four groups.)
Answer: 33, 33, 32, 32

Explanation: Divide 130 by 4: \( 130 \div 4 = 32 \) with a remainder of 2.
This means the base group size is 32. Since there is a remainder of 2, two of the groups will have one extra child (32 + 1 = 33). Therefore, the groups will be 33, 33, 32, and 32.
3. If the parking lot charges $1.25 per half hour, what is the cost of parking a car from 11:15 a.m. to 2:45 p.m.?
Answer: $8.75

Explanation: From 11:15 a.m. to 2:45 p.m. is 3.5 hours.
Since there are two half-hours in every hour, 3.5 hours equals 7 half-hours.
\( 7 \times \$1.25 = \$8.75 \).
4. If the area of the square is 400 m², then what is the area of the circle? (Use 3.14 for \( \pi \).)
Answer: 314 m²

Explanation: The area of the square is \( s^2 = 400 \), so the side length \( s \) is 20 m.
The circle fits perfectly inside the square, so its diameter is 20 m, which means its radius \( r \) is 10 m.
Area of the circle = \( \pi r^2 = 3.14 \times (10)^2 = 3.14 \times 100 = 314 \text{ m}^2 \).
5. Only 4 of the 50 states have names that begin with the letter A. What percent of the states have names that begin with letters other than A?
Answer: 92%

Explanation: The number of states starting with other letters is \( 50 - 4 = 46 \).
The percentage is \( \frac{46}{50} = \frac{92}{100} = 92\% \).
6. The coordinates of the vertices of a triangle are (3, 6), (5, 0), and (0, 0). What is the area of the triangle?
Answer: 15 square units

Explanation: The base of the triangle on the x-axis goes from (0,0) to (5,0), so the base \( b = 5 \).
The height is the y-coordinate of the peak vertex (3,6), so the height \( h = 6 \).
Area = \( \frac{1}{2}bh = \frac{1}{2}(5)(6) = 15 \).

19. What is the area of this pentagon?
Answer: 100 sq in.

Explanation: Split the composite figure into simpler shapes by dropping a vertical line from the end of the top 8 in. edge.
This forms a rectangle on the left with width 8 in. and height 9 in. Area = \( 8 \times 9 = 72 \) sq in.
The remaining shape on the right is a trapezoid. Its width is \( 12 - 8 = 4 \) in. Its vertical parallel sides are 9 in. and 5 in.
Area of trapezoid = \( \frac{1}{2}(b_1 + b_2)h = \frac{1}{2}(9 + 5) \times 4 = \frac{1}{2}(14) \times 4 = 28 \) sq in.
Total area = \( 72 + 28 = 100 \) sq in.

26. If the spinner is spun twice, what is the probability that the arrow will stop on a number greater than 1 on both spins? (The spinner has 8 equal sections: 1, 2, 3, 4, 1, 2, 3, 4)
Answer: \( \frac{9}{16} \)

Explanation:
Numbers greater than 1 on the spinner are 2, 3, and 4. There are two of each, making 6 favorable sections out of 8 total.
Probability on one spin = \( \frac{6}{8} = \frac{3}{4} \).
Probability for two consecutive spins = \( \frac{3}{4} \times \frac{3}{4} = \frac{9}{16} \).

Real-World Connection: You and your friends want to rent go-karts this Saturday. Go-Kart Track A rents go-karts for a flat fee of $10 per driver plus $7 per hour. Go-Kart Track B rents go-karts for a flat fee of $7 per driver plus $8 per hour.
a. If you and your friends plan to stay for 2 hours, which track has the better deal? Show all your work.
b. Which track is the better value if you stay for four hours?
Answer:
a. Track B
b. Track A

Explanation:
a. For 2 hours:
Track A cost: \( \$10 + (\$7 \times 2) = \$10 + \$14 = \$24 \)
Track B cost: \( \$7 + (\$8 \times 2) = \$7 + \$16 = \$23 \)
Track B is cheaper for 2 hours.

b. For 4 hours:
Track A cost: \( \$10 + (\$7 \times 4) = \$10 + \$28 = \$38 \)
Track B cost: \( \$7 + (\$8 \times 4) = \$7 + \$32 = \$39 \)
Track A is cheaper for 4 hours.
