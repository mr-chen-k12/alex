Module 1: The Bait & The Trap (Speech Hook)

Capturing class attention with real gamer scam stories

30-40 Secs
Pro-Gamer Speaker Strategy:

• Start with high energy and a question everyone in 6th grade relates to.

• Expose the shiny "bait" scammers use to trick kids into clicking.

"Raise your hand if you've ever seen a video or pop-up promising 'FREE UNLIMITED ROBUX!' or a free Dominus Empyreus or Kitsune Fruit. When you're playing games like Roblox & Blox Fruits, those deals look super tempting! But today, I want to reveal the truth: there is no secret glitch or free code generator. It’s a trap, and scammers are targeting 6th graders every single day."

Module 2: Spotting the Red Flags (Informative Speech)

Identifying fake links, countdown timers, and password traps

45 Secs
Pro-Gamer Speaker Strategy:

• Count off the 3 major Red Flags using your fingers for visual impact.

• Pause 2 seconds after naming each Red Flag.

"So how do you spot a scam before stepping into it? Look for three big Red Flags.
Red Flag #1: Fake Sense of Urgency. Scammers use flashing timers saying 'CLAIM IN 2 MINUTES OR LOSE IT!' to panic you.
Red Flag #2: Weird Web Links. Instead of `roblox.com`, the URL says something sketchy like `robloxx-free-itemz.net`.
Red Flag #3: Asking for Passwords or Codes. These traps pop up in sketchy Discord DMs & YouTube comments asking for your login info. Real game admins will NEVER ask for your password!"

Module 3: The Hacker's Playbook: Phishing & Theft

Explaining how hackers steal accounts, cookies, and personal data

45 Secs
Pro-Gamer Speaker Strategy:

• Use a serious, authoritative tone to show the consequences of clicking scam links.

• Explain technical terms like Phishing in simple 6th-grade language.

"What actually happens if someone falls for Free Robux generators & fake glitch sites? Scammers use a trick called Phishing. They design a website that looks 100% identical to the real login page. But the second you type your username, password, or session cookies, your key is instantly sent to a hacker's computer. In less than 60 seconds, they trade away your rare pets, change your password, and lock you out of your account forever!"

Module 4: My 4 Rules of Cyber Defense (Action Plan)

Empowering classmates with 4 ironclad security rules

45 Secs
Pro-Gamer Speaker Strategy:

• Deliver these rules like an invincible shield defense strategy.

• Stand tall with strong posture and confident voice tone.

"To keep your accounts completely unhackable, follow my 4 Ironclad Rules of Cyber Defense:
Rule 1: Enable 2FA (Two-Factor Authentication). This requires a 6-digit code sent to a parent's email whenever someone logs in.
Rule 2: Inspect the Domain URL. Make sure it says official sites like `roblox.com` or `minecraft.net`.
Rule 3: Keep Passwords Secret. Never share logins with friends, even on Discord.
Rule 4: When in Doubt, Ask an Adult. If a link looks weird, immediately show Mom, Dad, or our Tech Teacher Mr. Davis before clicking!"
