Written Practice - Set 3

1. Divide 810 by 20 and write the quotient
a. with a remainder.
b. as a mixed number.

Explanation:
Perform long division: $810 \div 20$.
$20 \times 40 = 800$
$810 - 800 = 10$ (This is the remainder).
So, the quotient is 40 with a remainder of 10.

For the mixed number, write the remainder over the divisor: $40 \frac{10}{20}$, which simplifies to $40 \frac{1}{2}$.

Answer:
a. 40 R 10
b. $40 \frac{1}{2}$

2. Six divers received scores of 9.5, 9.6, 9.65, 9.1, 9.8, and 9.75. The lowest score was not counted. What was the sum of the five highest scores?

Explanation:
Identify the lowest score: 9.1. Exclude it from the list.
The five highest scores are: 9.5, 9.6, 9.65, 9.75, 9.8.
Add them together: $9.50 + 9.60 + 9.65 + 9.75 + 9.80 = 48.30$.

Answer: 48.3

3. If 12 students play the flute, and they make up 15% of the school band, how many members does the band have in total?

Explanation:
Let $x$ be the total number of band members.
$15\%$ of $x = 12$
$0.15 \times x = 12$
$x = 12 \div 0.15$
$x = 80$

Answer: 80 members

4. Twenty is $\frac{2}{5}$ of what number?

Explanation:
Set up an equation where $x$ is the unknown number:
$20 = \frac{2}{5}x$
To solve for $x$, multiply both sides by the reciprocal ($\frac{5}{2}$):
$x = 20 \times \frac{5}{2} = \frac{100}{2} = 50$

Answer: 50

5. Write the standard number for the following: $(3 \times 10^5) + (5 \times 10^3) + (2 \times 10^1)$

Explanation:
Expand the scientific notation:
$3 \times 10^5 = 300,000$
$5 \times 10^3 = 5,000$
$2 \times 10^1 = 20$
Notice there are no $10^4$ or $10^2$ or $10^0$ terms, so their place values are zero.
Add them together: $300,000 + 5,000 + 20 = 305,020$.

Answer: 305,020

6. On an architect's blueprint, each inch represents 12 feet. A room is drawn $2 \frac{1}{2}$ inches long. How long is the actual room?

Explanation:
Multiply the drawing length by the scale factor:
$2 \frac{1}{2} \text{ inches} \times 12 \text{ feet/inch}$
Convert $2 \frac{1}{2}$ to an improper fraction: $\frac{5}{2}$.
$\frac{5}{2} \times 12 = \frac{60}{2} = 30$.

Answer: 30 feet

7. Two angles of a triangle measure 50° and 60°.
a. What is the measure of the third angle?
b. Make a rough sketch of the triangle.

Explanation:
a. The sum of angles in a triangle is always 180°. Subtract the two known angles:
$180^\circ - 50^\circ - 60^\circ = 70^\circ$.
b. A triangle with angles 70°, 60°, and 50° is an acute scalene triangle.

Answer:
a. 70°
b. (See sketch above: an acute scalene triangle)

8. Convert $16 \frac{2}{3}\%$ to a fraction.

Explanation:
First, convert the mixed number to an improper fraction: $16 \frac{2}{3} = \frac{50}{3}$.
Since it is a percent, multiply by $\frac{1}{100}$:
$\frac{50}{3} \times \frac{1}{100} = \frac{50}{300}$.
Simplify by dividing numerator and denominator by 50:
$\frac{50 \div 50}{300 \div 50} = \frac{1}{6}$.

Answer: $\frac{1}{6}$

9. Eighteen dollars is what percent of $24?

Explanation:
Create a fraction showing the part over the whole: $\frac{18}{24}$.
Simplify the fraction by dividing by 6: $\frac{18}{24} = \frac{3}{4}$.
Convert $\frac{3}{4}$ to a percentage by multiplying by 100: $\frac{3}{4} \times 100 = 75\%$.

Answer: 75%

10. Thirty percent of what number is 27?

Explanation:
Let $x$ be the unknown number. Convert 30% to a decimal (0.30) or fraction ($\frac{3}{10}$).
Using fraction: $\frac{3}{10}x = 27$
Multiply both sides by $\frac{10}{3}$:
$x = 27 \times \frac{10}{3} = \frac{270}{3} = 90$.

Answer: 90

11. Four fifths of what number is 32?

Set up the equation: $\frac{4}{5}x = 32$.
Multiply both sides by the reciprocal ($\frac{5}{4}$):

Answer: 40

15. $4 \frac{1}{3} - 2 \frac{3}{4}$

Explanation:
Convert to improper fractions:
$\frac{13}{3} - \frac{11}{4}$
Find a common denominator (12):
$\frac{13 \times 4}{3 \times 4} - \frac{11 \times 3}{4 \times 3} = \frac{52}{12} - \frac{33}{12}$
Subtract the numerators:
$\frac{52 - 33}{12} = \frac{19}{12}$.
Convert back to mixed number: $1 \frac{7}{12}$.

16. John is practicing for a triathlon segment. The ratio of the distance he runs to the distance he bikes is 1 to 4. If the distance run was 8 kilometers, what was the total length of his practice session?

Explanation:
Ratio of Run to Bike = $1:4$.
Let the run distance be $1x$ and bike distance be $4x$.
We are given the run distance is 8 km, so: $x = 8$.
The bike distance is $4x = 4(8) = 32$ km.
Total length = Run + Bike = $8 \text{ km} + 32 \text{ km} = 40$ km.

Answer: 40 kilometers

17. The area of the shaded triangle is 6.2 cm². What is the area of the parallelogram?

Explanation:
A diagonal divides a parallelogram into two congruent triangles. Therefore, the total area of the parallelogram is exactly twice the area of the shaded triangle.
Area = $6.2 \text{ cm}^2 \times 2 = 12.4 \text{ cm}^2$.

Answer: 12.4 cm²

18. A lunch box is 8 in. long, 5 in. wide, and 6 in. deep. What is the volume of the box?

Explanation:
The volume of a rectangular prism is calculated as $\text{Length} \times \text{Width} \times \text{Height (Depth)}$.
$V = 8 \text{ in.} \times 5 \text{ in.} \times 6 \text{ in.} = 240 \text{ cubic inches}$.

Answer: 240 in.³

19. A regular hexagon has a total of how many lines of symmetry?

Explanation:
A regular polygon with $n$ sides has $n$ lines of symmetry. A regular hexagon has 6 sides of equal length and 6 equal interior angles, so it has exactly 6 lines of symmetry.

Answer: 6

24. Which of these polygons is NOT a quadrilateral?
A. trapezoid     B. square     C. octagon

Explanation:
A quadrilateral is a polygon with exactly 4 sides. A trapezoid and a square both have 4 sides. An octagon has 8 sides, so it is not a quadrilateral.

Answer: C. octagon

26. The coordinates of three points that are on the same line are (-4, -2), (0, 0), and ($x$, 5). What number should replace $x$ in the third set of coordinates?

Explanation:
Find the slope ($m$) of the line using the first two points: (-4, -2) and (0, 0).
$m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} = \frac{0 - (-2)}{0 - (-4)} = \frac{2}{4} = \frac{1}{2}$.
The equation of the line passing through origin is $y = \frac{1}{2}x$.
Plug in the third point ($x$, 5) where $y = 5$:
$5 = \frac{1}{2}x \rightarrow x = 5 \times 2 = 10$.

Answer: 10

27. Sam flipped a coin three times, and it landed heads up every time. If he flips the coin a fourth time, what is the probability that it will land heads up?

Explanation:
Coin flips are independent events. The outcomes of previous flips do not influence the next flip. For a fair coin, the probability of getting heads on any single toss is always 1 out of 2.

Answer: $\frac{1}{2}$

28. Emily is going to flip a coin five times in a row. What is the probability that the coin will land heads up all five times?

Explanation:
Since each flip is an independent event with a probability of $\frac{1}{2}$ for getting heads, multiply the probabilities together for the five consecutive events:
$P(5 \text{ Heads}) = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{32}$.

Answer: $\frac{1}{32}$

29. The diameter of the circle is 14 cm.
a. What is the area of the square?
b. What is the area of the circle?

Explanation:
The diameter is 14 cm, so the radius ($r$) is half of that, which is 7 cm. The side length of the shaded square is exactly equal to the radius of the circle (7 cm).
a. Area of square = $\text{side} \times \text{side} = 7 \times 7 = 49 \text{ cm}^2$.
b. Area of circle = $\pi r^2 = \pi \times 7^2 = 49\pi \text{ cm}^2$.

Answer:
a. 49 cm²
b. $49\pi \text{ cm}^2$

30. What is the mode and the range of this set of numbers?
12, 15, 12, 18, 20, 14, 15, 12, 19

Explanation:
First, put the numbers in order: 12, 12, 12, 14, 15, 15, 18, 19, 20.
Mode: The number that appears most frequently. 12 appears three times, which is more than any other number.
Range: The difference between the highest and lowest numbers. Maximum is 20, Minimum is 12. $20 - 12 = 8$.

Answer:
Mode = 12
Range = 8
