Math Problem Solutions

Lesson Practice

a. Convert \(66 \frac{2}{3}\%\) to a fraction.
Answer: \(\frac{2}{3}\)
Explanation: To convert a percentage to a fraction, divide by 100. First, convert the mixed number to an improper fraction: \(66 \frac{2}{3} = \frac{200}{3}\). Then, \(\frac{200}{3} \div 100 = \frac{200}{3} \times \frac{1}{100} = \frac{200}{300} = \frac{2}{3}\).
b. Convert \(6 \frac{2}{3}\%\) to a fraction.
Answer: \(\frac{1}{15}\)
Explanation: Convert the mixed number to an improper fraction: \(6 \frac{2}{3} = \frac{20}{3}\). Divide by 100: \(\frac{20}{3} \div 100 = \frac{20}{3} \times \frac{1}{100} = \frac{20}{300} = \frac{1}{15}\).
c. Convert \(12 \frac{1}{2}\%\) to a fraction.
Answer: \(\frac{1}{8}\)
Explanation: Convert to an improper fraction: \(12 \frac{1}{2} = \frac{25}{2}\). Divide by 100: \(\frac{25}{2} \div 100 = \frac{25}{2} \times \frac{1}{100} = \frac{25}{200} = \frac{1}{8}\).
d. Write \(14 \frac{2}{7}\%\) as a fraction.
Answer: \(\frac{1}{7}\)
Explanation: Convert to an improper fraction: \(14 \frac{2}{7} = \frac{100}{7}\). Divide by 100: \(\frac{100}{7} \div 100 = \frac{100}{7} \times \frac{1}{100} = \frac{100}{700} = \frac{1}{7}\).
e. Write \(83 \frac{1}{3}\%\) as a fraction.
Answer: \(\frac{5}{6}\)
Explanation: Convert to an improper fraction: \(83 \frac{1}{3} = \frac{250}{3}\). Divide by 100: \(\frac{250}{3} \div 100 = \frac{250}{3} \times \frac{1}{100} = \frac{250}{300} = \frac{5}{6}\).

Written Practice

1. What is the total cost of a $12.60 item plus 7% sales tax?
Answer: $13.48
Explanation: First, calculate the sales tax by multiplying the cost by 7% (0.07): \( 12.60 \times 0.07 = 0.882 \). Round the tax to the nearest cent ($0.88). Add the tax to the original cost: \( 12.60 + 0.88 = 13.48 \).
2. Convert \(16 \frac{2}{3}\%\) to a fraction.
Answer: \(\frac{1}{6}\)
Explanation: Convert to an improper fraction: \(16 \frac{2}{3} = \frac{50}{3}\). Divide by 100: \(\frac{50}{3} \div 100 = \frac{50}{300} = \frac{1}{6}\).
3. MODEL: Draw a ratio box for this problem. Then solve the problem using a proportion.
Ines missed three questions on the test but answered 90% of the questions correctly. How many questions were on the test?
Answer: 30 questions
Explanation: If she answered 90% correctly, she missed 10% (100% - 90%). Let \(x\) be the total number of questions. Set up the proportion: \(\frac{10}{100} = \frac{3}{x}\). Cross-multiply to get \(10x = 300\), so \(x = 30\).
4. Sound travels about 331 meters per second in air. How far will it travel in 60 seconds?
Answer: 19,860 meters
Explanation: Multiply the speed by the time: \(331 \text{ m/s} \times 60 \text{ s} = 19,860 \text{ meters}\).
5. Write the standard number for \((5 \times 10^4) + (6 \times 10^2)\).
Answer: 50,600
Explanation: Expand each term: \(5 \times 10^4 = 50,000\) and \(6 \times 10^2 = 600\). Add them together: \(50,000 + 600 = 50,600\).
6. If the radius of a circle is seventy-five hundredths of a meter, what is the diameter?
Answer: 1.5 meters
Explanation: The diameter is twice the radius. Radius = 0.75 m. Diameter = \(0.75 \times 2 = 1.50\) m.
7. ESTIMATE Round the product of \(3 \frac{2}{3}\) and \(2 \frac{2}{3}\) to the nearest whole number.
Answer: 10
Explanation: First find the exact product: \(\frac{11}{3} \times \frac{8}{3} = \frac{88}{9}\). Converting back to a mixed number: \(9 \frac{7}{9}\). Since \(\frac{7}{9}\) is greater than \(\frac{1}{2}\), it rounds up to 10. (Alternatively, estimating the factors first: \(4 \times 3 = 12\), but calculating the product exactly then rounding yields a more accurate 10).
8. In a bag are three red marbles and three white marbles. If two marbles are taken from the bag at the same time, what is the probability that both marbles will be red?
Answer: \(\frac{1}{5}\) or 20%
Explanation: The probability of picking the first red marble is \(\frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}\). Since one red marble is removed, there are now 2 red marbles out of 5 total remaining. The probability of picking a second red marble is \(\frac{2}{5}\). Multiply the probabilities: \(\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{2}{5} = \frac{2}{10} = \frac{1}{5}\).
9. Complete the table. Fraction: \(2 \frac{2}{5}\). Find a. (Decimal) and b. (Percent).
Answer: a. 2.4, b. 240%
Explanation: To find the decimal, convert \(\frac{2}{5}\) to 0.4. Thus, \(2 \frac{2}{5} = 2.4\). To find the percent, multiply the decimal by 100: \(2.4 \times 100 = 240\%\).
10. Complete the table. Decimal: 0.85. Find a. (Fraction) and b. (Percent).
Answer: a. \(\frac{17}{20}\), b. 85%
Explanation: To find the fraction, write the decimal as \(\frac{85}{100}\) and simplify by dividing the numerator and denominator by 5, which gives \(\frac{17}{20}\). To find the percent, multiply by 100: \(0.85 \times 100 = 85\%\).
11. Solve: \(7x - 3 = 39\)
Answer: \(x = 6\)
Explanation: Add 3 to both sides to isolate the \(x\) term: \(7x = 42\). Divide both sides by 7: \(x = \frac{42}{7} = 6\).
12. Solve: \(\frac{x}{7} = \frac{35}{5}\)
Answer: \(x = 49\)
Explanation: First, simplify the right side: \(\frac{35}{5} = 7\). So, \(\frac{x}{7} = 7\). Multiply both sides by 7 to solve for x: \(x = 7 \times 7 = 49\).
13. Calculate mentally:
a. \((-3)(-15)\)   b. \(\frac{-15}{+3}\)   c. \(\frac{-15}{-3}\)   d. \((+3)(-15)\)
Answer: a. 45   b. -5   c. 5   d. -45
Explanation:
a. Negative × Negative = Positive. \(3 \times 15 = 45\).
b. Negative ÷ Positive = Negative. \(15 \div 3 = 5\), so \(-5\).
c. Negative ÷ Negative = Positive. \(15 \div 3 = 5\).
d. Positive × Negative = Negative. \(3 \times 15 = 45\), so \(-45\).
14. Solve: \(-6 + -7 + +5 - -8\)
Answer: 0
Explanation: Simplify the signs first: \(-6 - 7 + 5 + 8\). Combine negatives and positives: \(-13 + 13 = 0\).
15. Solve: \(0.12 \div (12 \div 0.4)\)
Answer: 0.004
Explanation: Follow order of operations (parentheses first). \(12 \div 0.4 = 120 \div 4 = 30\). Then, \(0.12 \div 30 = 0.004\).
16. Write \(\frac{22}{7}\) as a decimal rounded to the hundredths place.
Answer: 3.14
Explanation: Perform long division: \(22 \div 7 \approx 3.142857...\) Rounding to the nearest hundredth (two decimal places) gives 3.14.
17. What whole number multiplied by itself equals 10,000?
Answer: 100
Explanation: Find the square root of 10,000. \(\sqrt{10000} = 100\) because \(100 \times 100 = 10,000\).
18. What is the area of this hexagon?
Answer: 68 cm²
Explanation: Split the L-shaped hexagon into two rectangles. Top rectangle has height 5 cm and width 10 cm, Area = \(10 \times 5 = 50\) cm². Bottom rectangle has width 6 cm. Its height is the total height (8 cm) minus the top portion (5 cm) = 3 cm. Area = \(6 \times 3 = 18\) cm². Total Area = \(50 + 18 = 68\) cm².
19. What is the perimeter of this hexagon?
Answer: 36 cm
Explanation: Determine the lengths of all 6 sides. Top = 10. Right = 8. Bottom = 6. The inner vertical segment is the right height minus the left vertical height: \(8 - 5 = 3\). The inner horizontal segment is the top width minus the bottom width: \(10 - 6 = 4\). Left vertical = 5. Perimeter = \(10 + 8 + 6 + 3 + 4 + 5 = 36\) cm.
20. What is the volume of this cube? (Edge = 3 cm)
Answer: 27 cm³
Explanation: The volume of a cube is the edge length cubed (\(V = s^3\)). Volume = \(3 \times 3 \times 3 = 27\) cubic centimeters.
21. What is the mode of the number of days in the twelve months of the year?
Answer: 31
Explanation: The months have the following number of days: 31, 28 (or 29), 31, 30, 31, 30, 31, 31, 30, 31, 30, 31. The number 31 appears 7 times, making it the most frequent value (the mode).
22. ANALYZE If seven of the containers can hold a total of 84 ounces, then how many ounces can 10 containers hold?
Answer: 120 ounces
Explanation: First, find out how much one container holds by dividing the total ounces by the number of containers: \(84 \div 7 = 12\) ounces per container. Then, multiply by 10 to find the total for 10 containers: \(12 \times 10 = 120\) ounces.
23. Write the standard number for \(4 \frac{1}{2}\) million.
Answer: 4,500,000
Explanation: One million is 1,000,000. Half of a million is 500,000. So \(4 \frac{1}{2}\) million is \(4,000,000 + 500,000 = 4,500,000\).
24. Round 58,697,284 to the nearest million.
Answer: 59,000,000
Explanation: Look at the hundred thousands digit (6). Since it is 5 or greater, round the millions digit up by 1. 58 million becomes 59 million.
25. Which arrow is pointing to 0.4?
Answer: Arrow C
Explanation: Between 0 and 1, there are 5 subdivisions. Each tick mark represents \(1 \div 5 = 0.2\). Arrow C points to the second tick mark past zero, which is \(0.2 \times 2 = 0.4\).
26. When Rosita was born, she weighed 7 pounds 9 ounces. Two months later she weighed 9 pounds 7 ounces. How much weight did she gain in two months?
Answer: 1 pound 14 ounces
Explanation: Subtract the birth weight from the later weight. Set it up: 9 lbs 7 oz - 7 lbs 9 oz. Since you cannot subtract 9 oz from 7 oz, borrow 1 pound (16 ounces) from the 9 lbs. 9 lbs 7 oz becomes 8 lbs 23 oz.
Now subtract: (8 lbs - 7 lbs) = 1 lb, and (23 oz - 9 oz) = 14 oz.
27. CONNECT The coordinates of the vertices of a parallelogram are (0, 0), (5, 0), (6, 3), and (1, 3). What is the area of the parallelogram?
Answer: 15 square units
Explanation: The formula for the area of a parallelogram is Base \(\times\) Height. The base is the distance between (0, 0) and (5, 0), which is 5 units. The height is the vertical distance between the \(y=0\) baseline and the top vertices at \(y=3\), which is 3 units. Area = \(5 \times 3 = 15\).
28. Which is the greatest weight?
A. 6.24 lb   B. 6.4 lb   C. 6.345 lb
Answer: B. 6.4 lb
Explanation: To compare the decimals easily, align them by adding zeros:
A. 6.240
B. 6.400
C. 6.345
6.400 is the largest number.
29. Add:
  2 gal 2 qt 1 pt
+ 2 gal 2 qt 1 pt
Answer: 5 gal 1 qt
Explanation: Add columns: 4 gallons, 4 quarts, 2 pints. Convert the smaller units. 2 pints = 1 quart, so you now have 4 gallons and 5 quarts. 4 quarts = 1 gallon, leaving 1 quart left over. Adding that gallon to the 4 gallons gives 5 gallons 1 quart.
30. ANALYZE Gilbert started the trip with a full tank of gas. He drove 323.4 miles and then refilled the tank with 14.2 gallons of gas. How can Gilbert calculate the average number of miles he traveled on each gallon of gas?
Answer: Divide the total miles driven by the number of gallons used (323.4 ÷ 14.2)
Explanation: To find miles per gallon (mpg), the formula is Distance \(\div\) Volume of Gas. He should calculate \(323.4 \div 14.2 \approx 22.8\) miles per gallon.

Real-World Connection

A few students from the local high school decide to survey the types of vehicles in the parking lot. Their results are as follows: 210 cars, 125 trucks, and 14 motorcycles.

a. Find the simplified ratios for cars to trucks, cars to motorcycles, and trucks to motorcycles.
Answer:
  • Cars to trucks: 42 to 25 (or \(\frac{42}{25}\))
  • Cars to motorcycles: 15 to 1 (or \(\frac{15}{1}\))
  • Trucks to motorcycles: 125 to 14 (or \(\frac{125}{14}\))
Explanation:
Cars to Trucks: \(\frac{210}{125}\). Divide numerator and denominator by 5 to get \(\frac{42}{25}\).
Cars to Motorcycles: \(\frac{210}{14}\). Divide both by 14 to get \(\frac{15}{1}\).
Trucks to Motorcycles: \(\frac{125}{14}\). This fraction cannot be simplified further.
b. Find the fraction, decimal, and percent for the ratio of cars to the total number of vehicles. Round to nearest thousandth and tenth of a percent.
Answer:
  • Fraction: \(\frac{210}{349}\)
  • Decimal: 0.602
  • Percent: 60.2%
Explanation: First, find the total number of vehicles: \(210 + 125 + 14 = 349\). The fraction of cars to total is \(\frac{210}{349}\). To find the decimal, perform the division: \(210 \div 349 \approx 0.6017...\) Rounded to the nearest thousandth, it is 0.602. To find the percentage, multiply the exact decimal by 100: \(60.17...\%\) Rounding to the nearest tenth of a percent gives 60.2%.
