1 (11)

After the first hour of the monsoon, 34 millimeters of precipitation had fallen. After the second hour a total of 87 millimeters of precipitation had fallen. How many millimeters of precipitation fell during the second hour?

Answer: 53 millimeters

Solution: \(87 - 34 = 53\) millimeters fell during the second hour.

2 (13)

Each photograph enlargement cost 75¢ and Willie needed 32 enlargements. What was the total cost of the enlargements Willie needed?

Answer: $24.00

Solution: \(32 \times 75¢ = 2400¢ = \$24.00\).

Connect The Ottoman Empire can be said to have begun in 1299 and came to an end in 1922. About how many years did the Ottoman Empire last?

Answer: About 623 years

Solution: \(1922 - 1299 = 623\) years.

At the movie theater, Dolores gave $50 to the ticket seller and got $32.47 back in change. How much did her movie ticket cost?

5 (13)

A great gross is a dozen grosses. A great gross of pencils is how many pencils?

Answer: 1728 pencils

Solution: One gross is \(12 \times 12 = 144\), so a dozen grosses is \(12 \times 144 = 1728\).

6 (22)

Model Diagram this statement and answer the questions that follow. Begin by changing the percent to a reduced fraction.

Twenty-five percent of the 72 marbles in the bag were blue.

a. How many of the marbles in the bag were blue?

b. How many of the marbles in the bag were not blue?

Answer: a. 18 blue marbles   b. 54 marbles not blue

Solution: \(25\% = \frac{25}{100} = \frac{1}{4}\). a. \(\frac{1}{4}\) of 72 \(= 18\) blue marbles. b. \(72 - 18 = 54\) marbles were not blue.

7 (16)

a. Roan estimated that the weight of the water in a full bathtub is a half ton. How many pounds is half of a ton?

b. Explain Describe how you got your answer.

Answer: a. 1000 pounds

Solution: a. One ton equals 2000 pounds, so half a ton is \(2000 \div 2 = 1000\) pounds. b. I changed tons to pounds using \(1\text{ ton} = 2000\text{ lb}\), then divided by 2 because a half means one of two equal parts.

8 (8, 9)

The figure shows a one-inch square. A smaller square that is \(\frac{3}{10}\) of an inch on each side is shaded.

a. What fraction of the square inch is shaded?

b. What percent of the square is not shaded?

Answer: a. \(\frac{9}{100}\)   b. 91%

Solution: a. \(\frac{3}{10} \times \frac{3}{10} = \frac{9}{100}\) of the square inch (9 of the 100 small squares). b. \(1 - \frac{9}{100} = \frac{91}{100} = 91\%\) is not shaded.

9 (24)

a. Write 180 and 240 as products of prime numbers. Then reduce \(\frac{180}{240}\).

b. Find the GCF of 180 and 240.

Answer: a. \(180 = 2^2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5\); \(240 = 2^4 \cdot 3 \cdot 5\); \(\frac{180}{240} = \frac{3}{4}\)   b. 60

Solution: a. \(180 = 2 \cdot 90 = 2^2 \cdot 45 = 2^2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5\). \(240 = 2 \cdot 120 = 2^4 \cdot 15 = 2^4 \cdot 3 \cdot 5\). Cancel common factors: \(\frac{180}{240} = \frac{3}{4}\) (divided top and bottom by 60). b. GCF \(= 2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 = 60\).

Find the number that makes the two fractions equivalent.

12 (18)

Represent Draw a 3-by-3 grid. On the grid sketch a \(2\frac{1}{2}\) by \(2\frac{1}{2}\) square. Assume that the sketch illustrates a square with sides \(2\frac{1}{2}\) inches long. What is the area of the square? Explain how the sketch illustrates the area of the square.

Answer: \(6\frac{1}{4}\) square inches

Solution: Area \(= 2\frac{1}{2} \times 2\frac{1}{2} = \frac{5}{2} \times \frac{5}{2} = \frac{25}{4} = 6\frac{1}{4}\) square inches. The sketch shows why: the shaded square covers 4 whole unit squares, plus four half-unit strips (\(4 \times \frac{1}{2} = 2\)), plus one quarter-unit corner square: \(4 + 2 + \frac{1}{4} = 6\frac{1}{4}\).

13 (8)

Draw \(\overline{AB}\) 3 in. long. Then draw \(\overline{BC}\) 4 in. long perpendicular to \(\overline{AB}\). Complete \(\triangle ABC\) by drawing \(\overline{AC}\). How long is \(\overline{AC}\)?

Answer: 5 inches

Solution: The triangle is a right triangle with legs 3 in. and 4 in. Measuring \(\overline{AC}\) with a ruler gives 5 inches. Check with the Pythagorean theorem: \(AC = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{9 + 16} = \sqrt{25} = 5\) inches.

26 (2, 9)

Justify Name each property used to simplify this equation.

\(\frac{4}{3}\left(\frac{3}{4}x\right) = \frac{7}{8}\)   Given
\(\left(\frac{4}{3} \cdot \frac{3}{4}\right)x = \frac{7}{8}\)   a. ___
\(1x = \frac{7}{8}\)   b. ___
\(x = \frac{7}{8}\)   c. ___

Answer: a. Associative Property of Multiplication   b. Inverse Property of Multiplication   c. Identity Property of Multiplication

Solution: a. The factors were regrouped — Associative Property of Multiplication. b. \(\frac{4}{3} \cdot \frac{3}{4} = 1\) — a number times its reciprocal is 1: Inverse Property of Multiplication. c. \(1x = x\) — Identity Property of Multiplication.

27 (14, 15)

Max is thinking of a counting number from 1 to 20. Deb guesses 13. What is the probability Deb's guess is correct?

Answer: \(\frac{1}{20}\)

Solution: There are 20 equally likely counting numbers and only 1 matches Deb's guess, so \(P = \frac{1}{20}\).

28 (1, 9)

Analyze Evaluate the following expressions for \(x = 4\) and \(y = 8\):

a. \(x - \frac{y}{x}\)    b. \(\frac{xy}{y}\)    c. \(\frac{x}{y} \cdot \frac{y}{x}\)

d. Which property is illustrated by c?

Answer: a. 2   b. 4   c. 1   d. Inverse Property of Multiplication

Solution: a. \(4 - \frac{8}{4} = 4 - 2 = 2\). b. \(\frac{4 \cdot 8}{8} = \frac{32}{8} = 4\). c. \(\frac{4}{8} \cdot \frac{8}{4} = \frac{32}{32} = 1\). d. Reciprocals multiply to 1 — the Inverse Property of Multiplication.

30 (Inv. 2)

Conclude The central angle of a half circle is \(180^\circ\). The central angle of a third of a circle is \(120^\circ\). How many degrees is the central angle of a sixth of a circle?

Answer: \(60^\circ\)

Solution: A full circle is \(360^\circ\). One sixth of a circle is \(360^\circ \div 6 = 60^\circ\). (Also: half of the \(120^\circ\) third-circle angle.)

Real-World Connection You and some friends volunteered to paint the concession stand for your local ball park. The back and side walls (both inside and outside) need painting. The two side walls measure \(9\frac{3}{4}\) by 8 feet each, while the back wall measures 40 feet by 8 feet. Find the total area that must be painted (in square feet). Show each step of your work.

Answer: 952 square feet

Solution: Step 1 — one side wall: \(9\frac{3}{4} \times 8 = \frac{39}{4} \times 8 = 78\) sq ft. Step 2 — two side walls: \(2 \times 78 = 156\) sq ft. Step 3 — back wall: \(40 \times 8 = 320\) sq ft. Step 4 — one side of the stand: \(156 + 320 = 476\) sq ft. Step 5 — paint inside and outside: \(2 \times 476 = 952\) square feet.
