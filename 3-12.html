Math Course Practice Problems

Lesson Practice

a. One side of an equilateral triangle measures 15 cm. What is the perimeter of the triangle?
Answer: 45 cm
Explanation: An equilateral triangle has three sides of equal length. To find the perimeter, multiply the side length by 3. \( 15 \text{ cm} \times 3 = 45 \text{ cm} \).
b. Verify: "An equilateral triangle is also an acute triangle." True or false?
Answer: True
Explanation: All three angles in an equilateral triangle are exactly 60°. Since 60° is less than 90°, all the angles are acute, making it an acute triangle.
c. Verify: "All acute triangles are equilateral triangles." True or false?
Answer: False
Explanation: An acute triangle just needs all its angles to be less than 90°. For example, a triangle with angles 50°, 60°, and 70° is acute, but its sides are not equal, so it is not equilateral.
d. Two sides of a triangle measure 3 inches and 4 inches. If the perimeter is 10 inches, what type of triangle is it?
Answer: Isosceles triangle
Explanation: The sum of the two known sides is \(3 + 4 = 7\) inches. Since the perimeter is 10 inches, the third side must be \(10 - 7 = 3\) inches. The triangle has sides of 3, 3, and 4 inches. Because two sides are equal, it is an isosceles triangle.
e. Verify: "Every right triangle is a scalene triangle." True or false?
Answer: False
Explanation: A right triangle can also be an isosceles triangle. For example, a right triangle with angles 45°, 45°, and 90° has two equal sides. Therefore, not every right triangle is scalene (all sides different).

Written Practice

1. The ratio of the length to the width of the rectangular lot was 5 to 2. If the lot was 60 ft wide, how long was the lot?
Answer: 150 ft
Explanation: Set up a proportion: \(\frac{\text{Length}}{\text{Width}} = \frac{5}{2}\). Since Width = 60, we have \(\frac{L}{60} = \frac{5}{2}\). Cross multiply: \(2L = 300\). Divide by 2: \(L = 150\).
2. Mitch does not know the correct answer to two multiple-choice questions. The choices are A, B, C, and D. If Mitch just guesses, what is the probability that Mitch will guess both answers correctly?
Answer: \(\frac{1}{16}\)
Explanation: The probability of guessing one question correctly is \(\frac{1}{4}\). The events are independent, so multiply the probabilities: \(\frac{1}{4} \times \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{16}\).
3. If the sum of four numbers is 144, what is the average of the four numbers?
Answer: 36
Explanation: Average is calculated by dividing the sum of the numbers by the quantity of numbers. \(144 \div 4 = 36\).
4. The rectangular prism shown below is constructed of 1-cubic-centimeter blocks. What is the volume of the prism?
Answer: 36 cm³
Explanation: By counting the blocks in the image, the prism is 4 blocks wide, 3 blocks deep, and 3 blocks high. Volume = length × width × height = \(4 \times 3 \times 3 = 36\) blocks. Since each block is 1 cm³, the volume is 36 cm³.
5. Write \(\frac{9}{25}\) as a decimal number and as a percent.
Answer: 0.36 and 36%
Explanation: Multiply the numerator and denominator by 4 to get a denominator of 100. \(\frac{9 \times 4}{25 \times 4} = \frac{36}{100}\). As a decimal, this is \(0.36\). As a percent, it is \(36\%\).
6. Write \(3 \frac{1}{5}\) as a decimal number and add it to 3.5. What is the sum?
Answer: 6.7
Explanation: First, convert \(3 \frac{1}{5}\) to a decimal. \(\frac{1}{5} = 0.2\), so \(3 \frac{1}{5} = 3.2\). Then add: \(3.2 + 3.5 = 6.7\).
7. What number is 45% of 80?
Answer: 36
Explanation: Convert 45% to a decimal (0.45) and multiply by 80. \(0.45 \times 80 = 36\).
8. \((0.3)^3\)
Answer: 0.027
Explanation: \((0.3) \times (0.3) \times (0.3) = 0.09 \times 0.3 = 0.027\).
9. \((2 \frac{1}{2})^2\)
Answer: \(6 \frac{1}{4}\) or 6.25
Explanation: Convert to an improper fraction: \(\frac{5}{2}\). Square it: \((\frac{5}{2})^2 = \frac{25}{4}\). Convert back to a mixed number: \(6 \frac{1}{4}\).
10. \(\sqrt{9} \cdot \sqrt{100}\)
Answer: 30
Explanation: Evaluate the square roots first: \(\sqrt{9} = 3\) and \(\sqrt{100} = 10\). Then multiply: \(3 \times 10 = 30\).
11. Twenty of the two dozen members voted yes. What fraction of the members voted yes?
Answer: \(\frac{5}{6}\)
Explanation: Two dozen equals \(2 \times 12 = 24\) total members. 20 voted yes. The fraction is \(\frac{20}{24}\). Divide top and bottom by 4 to simplify: \(\frac{5}{6}\).
12. If the rest of the members in problem 11 voted no, then what was the ratio of "no" votes to "yes" votes?
Answer: 1 to 5 (or 1:5)
Explanation: Total members = 24. Yes votes = 20. No votes = \(24 - 20 = 4\). The ratio of no to yes is \(4:20\). Simplify by dividing by 4 to get \(1:5\).
13. Find the unknown number: \(w + 4 \frac{3}{4} = 9 \frac{1}{3}\)
Answer: \(4 \frac{7}{12}\)
Explanation: Subtract \(4 \frac{3}{4}\) from both sides: \(w = 9 \frac{1}{3} - 4 \frac{3}{4}\). Convert to improper fractions: \(\frac{28}{3} - \frac{19}{4}\). Find a common denominator (12): \(\frac{112}{12} - \frac{57}{12} = \frac{55}{12}\). Convert back to a mixed number: \(4 \frac{7}{12}\).
14. Find the unknown number: \(\frac{6}{5} = \frac{m}{30}\)
Answer: 36
Explanation: Cross multiply: \(5m = 6 \times 30 \rightarrow 5m = 180\). Divide by 5: \(m = 36\). (Alternatively, note that the denominator is multiplied by 6 (\(5 \times 6 = 30\)), so multiply the numerator by 6: \(6 \times 6 = 36\)).
15. In what type of triangle are all three sides the same length?
Answer: Equilateral triangle
Explanation: By definition, a triangle with all three sides of equal length is an equilateral triangle.
16. What mixed number is \(\frac{3}{8}\) of 100?
Answer: \(37 \frac{1}{2}\)
Explanation: Multiply \(\frac{3}{8}\) by 100: \(\frac{3}{8} \times \frac{100}{1} = \frac{300}{8}\). Simplify the fraction: \(\frac{75}{2}\). Convert to a mixed number: \(37 \frac{1}{2}\).
17. Analyze: \(10 + 6^2 \div 3 - \sqrt{9} \times 3\)
Answer: 13
Explanation: Use order of operations (PEMDAS).
Exponents and Roots: \(6^2 = 36\), \(\sqrt{9} = 3\). The expression is now \(10 + 36 \div 3 - 3 \times 3\).
Multiplication and Division (left to right): \(36 \div 3 = 12\), and \(3 \times 3 = 9\). Expression is now \(10 + 12 - 9\).
Addition and Subtraction (left to right): \(10 + 12 = 22\), and \(22 - 9 = 13\).
18. A triangular prism has how many faces?
Answer: 5 faces
Explanation: A triangular prism has 2 triangular bases and 3 rectangular sides connecting the corresponding sides of the bases. \(2 + 3 = 5\).
19. How many quarts of milk is \(2 \frac{1}{2}\) gallons of milk?
Answer: 10 quarts
Explanation: There are 4 quarts in 1 gallon. Multiply \(2.5\) gallons by 4. \(2.5 \times 4 = 10\).
20. Use a factor tree to find the prime factors of 800. Then write the prime factorization of 800 using exponents.
Answer: \(2^5 \times 5^2\)
Explanation: Break down 800:
800 = 8 × 100
8 = 2 × 2 × 2 (\(2^3\))
100 = 10 × 10 = (2 × 5) × (2 × 5) = \(2^2 \times 5^2\)
Combine them: \(2^3 \times 2^2 \times 5^2 = 2^5 \times 5^2\).
21. Round the decimal number one hundred twenty-five thousandths to the nearest tenth.
Answer: 0.1
Explanation: "One hundred twenty-five thousandths" is written as 0.125. The digit in the tenths place is 1. The digit to its right (hundredths) is 2, which is less than 5, so we round down (keep the 1 the same). The answer is 0.1.
22. \(0.08n = \$1.20\)
Answer: \(n = 15\)
Explanation: Divide both sides by 0.08: \(n = \frac{1.20}{0.08}\). To simplify, multiply top and bottom by 100: \(n = \frac{120}{8} = 15\).
23. The diagonal segment through this rectangle divides the rectangle into two congruent right triangles. What is the area of one of the triangles? (Rectangle dimensions: 26 mm by 18 mm)
Answer: 234 mm²
Explanation: The area of the entire rectangle is \(26 \times 18 = 468 \text{ mm}^2\). Since the diagonal divides it into two congruent triangles, the area of one triangle is half the rectangle's area: \(468 \div 2 = 234 \text{ mm}^2\). Alternatively, use the triangle area formula: \(A = \frac{1}{2}bh = \frac{1}{2}(26)(18) = 13 \times 18 = 234 \text{ mm}^2\).
24. Write \(\frac{17}{20}\) as a percent.
Answer: 85%
Explanation: Multiply the numerator and denominator by 5 to get a denominator of 100. \(\frac{17 \times 5}{20 \times 5} = \frac{85}{100}\), which is 85%.
25. On this number line the arrow could be pointing to which of the following? (Arrow is located slightly before 1.5, between 1 and 2)
A) \(\sqrt{1}\)
B) \(\sqrt{2}\)
C) \(\sqrt{3}\)
D) \(\sqrt{4}\)
Answer: B) \(\sqrt{2}\)
Explanation: \(\sqrt{1} = 1\) and \(\sqrt{4} = 2\). The arrow is between 1 and 2, but closer to 1 (approximately 1.4). \(\sqrt{2}\) is roughly 1.414, and \(\sqrt{3}\) is roughly 1.732. The visual position best matches \(\sqrt{2}\).
26. Write this number in standard notation: \((7 \times 10^9) + (2 \times 10^8) + (5 \times 10^7)\)
Answer: 7,250,000,000
Explanation: Expand each term: \(7,000,000,000 + 200,000,000 + 50,000,000\). Adding them together places the 7 in the billions place, 2 in the hundred-millions place, and 5 in the ten-millions place.
27.
a. What is the probability of rolling a 6 with a single roll of a number cube?
b. What is the probability of rolling a number less than 6 with a single roll of a number cube?
c. Name the event and its complement. Then describe the relationship between the two probabilities.
Answer & Explanation:
a. \(\frac{1}{6}\). There is only one '6' on a standard 6-sided die.
b. \(\frac{5}{6}\). The numbers less than 6 are 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 (five possible outcomes).
c. The event is "rolling a 6". Its complement is "not rolling a 6" (or rolling a number less than 6). The relationship is that the sum of the probabilities of an event and its complement is always 1 (\(\frac{1}{6} + \frac{5}{6} = 1\)).
28. The coordinates of the four vertices of a quadrilateral are \((-3, -2), (0, 2), (3, 2),\) and \((5, -2)\). What is the name for this type of quadrilateral?
Answer: Trapezoid
Explanation: The points \((0, 2)\) and \((3, 2)\) form a horizontal line segment (y=2). The points \((-3, -2)\) and \((5, -2)\) form another horizontal line segment (y=-2). Because these two sides are parallel, and the other two sides are not parallel, the shape is a trapezoid.
29. The formula for the area of a triangle is \(A = \frac{bh}{2}\). If the base measures 20 cm and the height measures 15 cm, then what is the area of the triangle? Explain your thinking.
Answer: 150 cm²
Explanation: Substitute the given values into the formula. \(A = \frac{20 \times 15}{2}\). First, multiply the base and height: \(20 \times 15 = 300\). Then, divide by 2: \(300 \div 2 = 150\). The area is 150 square centimeters.
30. Write the rule for this sequence. Then write the next four numbers.
\(\frac{1}{16}, \frac{1}{8}, \frac{3}{16}, \frac{1}{4}, \frac{5}{16}, \frac{3}{8}, \ldots\)
Answer: Rule: Add \(\frac{1}{16}\). Next four numbers: \(\frac{7}{16}, \frac{1}{2}, \frac{9}{16}, \frac{5}{8}\)
Explanation: To see the pattern, convert all fractions to have a common denominator of 16. The sequence becomes: \(\frac{1}{16}, \frac{2}{16}, \frac{3}{16}, \frac{4}{16}, \frac{5}{16}, \frac{6}{16}\). The rule is clearly adding \(\frac{1}{16}\) each time. The next four unsimplified fractions are \(\frac{7}{16}, \frac{8}{16}, \frac{9}{16}, \frac{10}{16}\). Simplifying these gives \(\frac{7}{16}, \frac{1}{2}, \frac{9}{16}, \frac{5}{8}\).

Real-World Connection

A teacher emptied a 1.5 oz snack-sized box of raisins into a dish. The teacher then asked for volunteers to estimate the number of raisins in the dish. Twelve volunteers gave the following estimates:
84, 100, 50, 75, 66, 75, 70, 90, 85, 77, 91, 80.

a. Which type of display—a circle graph or a stem-and-leaf plot—is the most appropriate way to display this data? Draw your display and justify your choice.
b. The dish contained exactly 85 raisins. How many volunteers made a reasonable estimate? Give a reason to support your answer. (Hint: You might think of how far off an estimate is in terms of a percent of 85.)
Answer a: Stem-and-leaf plot.
Justification: A stem-and-leaf plot is appropriate for organizing numerical data ranges and showing the distribution/frequency of values, preserving the exact numbers. A circle graph (pie chart) is used to show parts of a whole (percentages/proportions), which doesn't fit this list of raw estimates.
Display (Stem-and-Leaf Plot):
Stem | Leaf 5 | 0 6 | 6 7 | 0 5 5 7 8 | 0 4 5 9 | 0 1 10 | 0 Key: 8|4 = 84


Answer b: (Answers may vary based on defined "reasonable"). If we define "reasonable" as within 10% of the actual amount (85), then 6 volunteers made reasonable estimates.
Explanation: 10% of 85 is 8.5. An estimate within 10% would be between \(85 - 8.5\) and \(85 + 8.5\), which is a range of 76.5 to 93.5. Looking at the data, the estimates falling into this range are: 84, 77, 90, 85, 91, and 80. That is a total of 6 estimates.
