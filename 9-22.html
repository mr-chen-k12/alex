Problem 1 *1. (12) CONNECT
Mr. Chong celebrated his seventy-fifth birthday in 1998. In what year was he born?
Answer: 1923
Explanation:
To find the year Mr. Chong was born, subtract his age ($75$) from the year he celebrated that birthday ($1998$): $$1998 - 75 = 1923$$ Mr. Chong was born in 1923.
Problem 2 *2. (14) EXPLAIN

a. What is the probability of not spinning a $4$ with one spin?

b. EXPLAIN Describe how you found the answer to part a.

a. $\dfrac{7}{8}$
b. There are 8 equal sectors on the spinner. Only 1 sector has the number 4, which leaves 7 sectors that are not 4.
Explanation:

Part a: The spinner is divided into $8$ equal sectors. Counting the sectors containing the number $4$, there is only $1$ such sector. The number of sectors that do not contain a $4$ is $8 - 1 = 7$.

$$\text{Probability}(\text{not } 4) = \frac{\text{Number of sectors without } 4}{\text{Total number of sectors}} = \frac{7}{8}$$

Part b: To explain: "I counted the total number of equal outcomes ($8$) and the number of favorable outcomes ($7$ sectors that are not labeled $4$). Then I wrote the ratio of favorable outcomes to total outcomes as the fraction $\frac{7}{8}$."

Problem 3 *3. (15)
If $40\%$ of all the citizens voted "No" on the ballot, what fraction of all the citizens voted "No"?
Answer: $\dfrac{2}{5}$
Explanation:
Convert the percentage to a fraction by putting it over $100$ and reducing to simplest form: $$40\% = \frac{40}{100}$$ Divide numerator and denominator by their greatest common factor ($20$): $$\frac{40 \div 20}{100 \div 20} = \frac{2}{5}$$
Problem 4 *4. (13)
The farmer harvested $9000$ bushels of grain from $60$ acres. The crop produced an average of how many bushels of grain for each acre?
Answer: $150$ bushels per acre
Explanation:
To find the average yield per acre, divide the total number of bushels harvested by the total number of acres: $$\text{Average per acre} = \frac{9000 \text{ bushels}}{60 \text{ acres}} = 150 \text{ bushels/acre}$$
Problem 5 5. (8) EVALUATE
With a ruler, draw a segment $2\frac{1}{2}$ inches long. Draw a second segment $1\frac{7}{8}$ inches long. The first segment is how much longer than the second segment?
Answer: $\dfrac{5}{8}$ inch
Explanation:
Subtract the length of the second segment from the first segment: $$2\frac{1}{2} - 1\frac{7}{8}$$ Find a common denominator ($8$) for the fractional parts: $$2\frac{4}{8} - 1\frac{7}{8}$$ Rename $2\frac{4}{8}$ as $1\frac{12}{8}$: $$1\frac{12}{8} - 1\frac{7}{8} = \frac{5}{8} \text{ inch}$$

Problem 7 7. (6)
List the single-digit divisors of $2100$.
Answer: $1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7$
Explanation:
The single-digit positive integers are $1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9$. Test each to see if it divides $2100$ evenly:
  • 1: $2100 \div 1 = 2100$ (Yes)
  • 2: $2100$ ends in $0$, so it is even (Yes)
  • 3: Sum of digits $2+1+0+0=3$, which is divisible by $3$ (Yes)
  • 4: Last two digits are $00$, divisible by $4$ (Yes)
  • 5: Ends in $0$ (Yes)
  • 6: Divisible by both $2$ and $3$ (Yes)
  • 7: $2100 \div 7 = 300$ (Yes)
  • 8: $2100 \div 8 = 262.5$ (No)
  • 9: Sum of digits $3$ is not divisible by $9$ (No)
The single-digit divisors are: $1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7$.

Problem 9 *9. (15) ANALYZE
Find three equivalent fractions for $\dfrac{2}{3}$ by multiplying by $\dfrac{3}{3}, \dfrac{5}{5},$ and $\dfrac{6}{6}$. What property of multiplication do we use to find equivalent fractions?
Equivalent fractions: $\dfrac{6}{9}, \dfrac{10}{15}, \dfrac{12}{18}$
Property: Identity Property of Multiplication
Explanation:
Perform the multiplications: $$\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{3}{3} = \frac{6}{9}$$ $$\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{5}{5} = \frac{10}{15}$$ $$\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{6}{6} = \frac{12}{18}$$ Since $\frac{3}{3} = 1$, $\frac{5}{5} = 1$, and $\frac{6}{6} = 1$, multiplying a fraction by $1$ does not change its value. This principle is called the Identity Property of Multiplication (or Multiplicative Identity Property).

Problem 13 13. (4)
Compare: $(11)(6 + 7) \bigcirc 66 + 77$
Answer: $=$
Explanation:
Evaluate both sides of the expression:
  • Left side: $(11)(6 + 7) = 11 \times 13 = 143$
  • Right side: $66 + 77 = 143$
Alternatively, using the Distributive Property: $(11)(6 + 7) = 11 \cdot 6 + 11 \cdot 7 = 66 + 77$.
Since $143 = 143$, the correct symbol is $=$.

Problem 20 *20. (2, 9) JUSTIFY
Name the four properties used to simplify the expression.

Given: $\dfrac{3}{4} \cdot \dfrac{5}{6} \cdot \dfrac{4}{3}$

a. $\dfrac{3}{4} \cdot \dfrac{4}{3} \cdot \dfrac{5}{6}$

b. $\left(\dfrac{3}{4} \cdot \dfrac{4}{3}\right) \cdot \dfrac{5}{6}$

c. $1 \cdot \dfrac{5}{6}$

d. $\dfrac{5}{6}$

a. Commutative Property of Multiplication
b. Associative Property of Multiplication
c. Inverse Property of Multiplication (or Reciprocal Property)
d. Identity Property of Multiplication
Explanation:
  • Step a: $\frac{5}{6}$ and $\frac{4}{3}$ are reordered. Changing the order of factors uses the Commutative Property of Multiplication.
  • Step b: The factors $\frac{3}{4}$ and $\frac{4}{3}$ are grouped together with parentheses. Changing the grouping uses the Associative Property of Multiplication.
  • Step c: The product of the reciprocals $\left(\frac{3}{4} \cdot \frac{4}{3}\right)$ is replaced by $1$. This uses the Inverse Property of Multiplication (or Reciprocal Property).
  • Step d: Multiplying $1 \cdot \frac{5}{6}$ yields $\frac{5}{6}$. Multiplying any number by $1$ keeps its identity, which uses the Identity Property of Multiplication.

Problem 29 29. (15)
Find a fraction equal to $\dfrac{1}{3}$ that has a denominator of $6$. Add the fraction to $\dfrac{1}{6}$ and reduce the answer.
Equivalent fraction: $\dfrac{2}{6}$
Final Reduced Answer: $\dfrac{1}{2}$
Explanation:
  1. Find equivalent fraction for $\frac{1}{3}$ with denominator $6$: $$\frac{1 \times 2}{3 \times 2} = \frac{2}{6}$$
  2. Add this fraction to $\frac{1}{6}$: $$\frac{2}{6} + \frac{1}{6} = \frac{3}{6}$$
  3. Reduce the result by dividing by $3$: $$\frac{3 \div 3}{6 \div 3} = \frac{1}{2}$$
Problem 30 *30. (15) EVALUATE
Write $2\dfrac{2}{3}$ as an improper fraction. Then multiply the improper fraction by $\dfrac{1}{4}$ and reduce the product.
Improper fraction: $\dfrac{8}{3}$
Final Reduced Product: $\dfrac{2}{3}$
Explanation:
  1. Convert $2\frac{2}{3}$ to an improper fraction: $$2\frac{2}{3} = \frac{(2 \times 3) + 2}{3} = \frac{8}{3}$$
  2. Multiply $\frac{8}{3}$ by $\frac{1}{4}$: $$\frac{8}{3} \times \frac{1}{4} = \frac{8 \times 1}{3 \times 4} = \frac{8}{12}$$
  3. Reduce $\frac{8}{12}$ by dividing numerator and denominator by $4$: $$\frac{8 \div 4}{12 \div 4} = \frac{2}{3}$$
