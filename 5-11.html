1. After 10 years: \( A = 2000 imes (1.1)^{10} pprox 2000 imes 2.59374 = \$5,187.48 \)
2. After 20 years: \( A = 2000 imes (1.1)^{20} pprox 2000 imes 6.72750 = \$13,455.00 \)

Written Practice

1. John drew a right triangle with sides 6 inches, 8 inches, and 10 inches long. What was the area of the triangle?
Answer: 24 square inches
Explanation:
In a right triangle with sides 6, 8, and 10, the hypotenuse is the longest side (10). The base and height are the two shorter sides (6 and 8).
Area = \( rac{1}{2} imes ext{base} imes ext{height} \)
Area = \( rac{1}{2} imes 6 imes 8 = 24 \)
2. If 5 feet of ribbon costs $1.20, then 10 feet of ribbon would cost how much?
Answer: $2.40
Explanation:
10 feet is exactly double the length of 5 feet. Therefore, the cost will also be doubled.
\( \$1.20 imes 2 = \$2.40 \)
3. a. Six is what fraction of 15?
b. Six is what percent of 15?
Answers:
a. \( rac{2}{5} \)
b. 40%
Explanation:
a. The fraction is \( rac{6}{15} \). Dividing the numerator and denominator by 3 reduces it to \( rac{2}{5} \).
b. To convert \( rac{2}{5} \) to a percent, multiply by 100%: \( rac{2}{5} imes 100\% = 40\% \).

Explanation:

16. Use division by primes to find the prime factors of 648. Then write the prime factorization of 648 using exponents.

Explanation:
Divide repeatedly by the smallest prime numbers:

\( 324 \div 2 = 162 \)
\( 162 \div 2 = 81 \)
\( 81 \div 3 = 27 \)
\( 27 \div 3 = 9 \)
\( 9 \div 3 = 3 \)

18. Find the area of this trapezoid by combining the area of the rectangle and the area of the triangle. (Rectangle 6m by 4m, Triangle height 2m, base 4m)
Answer: 28 square meters
Explanation:
Area of the rectangle part = \( 6 imes 4 = 24 \) m\(^2\).
The triangle's height is the total height minus the rectangle's height: \( 8 - 6 = 2 \) m. Its base is 4 m.
Area of the triangle part = \( rac{1}{2} imes 4 imes 2 = 4 \) m\(^2\).
Total area = \( 24 + 4 = 28 \) m\(^2\).

23. The 306 students were assigned to ten rooms so that there were 30 or 31 students in each room. How many rooms had exactly 30 students?
Answer: 4 rooms
Explanation:
If all 10 rooms had 30 students, there would be \( 10 imes 30 = 300 \) students.
We have 306 students, which is 6 extra students.
To place these 6 extra students, we put 1 extra student in 6 of the rooms (making them rooms of 31).
Therefore, 6 rooms have 31 students, and the remaining 4 rooms have 30 students.

26. An equilateral triangle and a square share a common side. If the area of the square is 100 mm², then what is the perimeter of the equilateral triangle?
Answer: 30 mm
Explanation:
The area of a square is \( s^2 \). If area is 100, then the side length \( s = \sqrt{100} = 10 \) mm.
Since the equilateral triangle shares a side, its sides are also 10 mm each.
The perimeter of an equilateral triangle is \( 3 imes ext{side} = 3 imes 10 = 30 \) mm.

28. The heights of the five starters on the basketball team are listed below. Find the mean, median, and range of these measures: 181 cm, 177 cm, 189 cm, 158 cm, 195 cm
Answers:
Mean: 180 cm
Median: 181 cm
Range: 37 cm
Explanation:
First, order the numbers from least to greatest: 158, 177, 181, 189, 195.
Mean: Add them up and divide by 5. \( 158 + 177 + 181 + 189 + 195 = 900 \). \( 900 \div 5 = 180 \).
Median: The middle value in the sorted list is 181.
Range: Subtract the smallest from the largest. \( 195 - 158 = 37 \).

Explanation:

Real-World Connection

Gerard plays basketball on his high school team. This season he scored 372 of his team's 1488 points.
a. What percent of the points were scored by Gerard's teammates?
b. Gerard's team played 12 games. How many points did Gerard average per game?
Answers:
a. 75%
b. 31 points per game
Explanation:
a. Points scored by teammates = \( 1488 - 372 = 1116 \). To find the percentage, divide by total points and multiply by 100: \( (1116 \div 1488) imes 100 = 0.75 imes 100 = 75\% \).
b. Divide Gerard's total points by the number of games: \( 372 \div 12 = 31 \) points/game.
