Lesson 17: Protractor Practice

g. REPRESENT: Use your protractor to draw an angle of $55^\circ$.
Explanation: Align the center of the protractor baseline with the vertex, align one ray with $0^\circ$, and mark a point at $55^\circ$. Draw a ray from the vertex through the mark to create an acute angle measuring $55^\circ$.
Answer: An acute angle measuring $55^\circ$.
h. REPRESENT: Use your protractor to draw an angle of $135^\circ$.
Explanation: Align the center of the protractor baseline with the vertex, set one side along $0^\circ$, and mark $135^\circ$ on the scale. Draw a ray from the vertex through the mark to form an obtuse angle.
Answer: An obtuse angle measuring $135^\circ$.
i. REPRESENT: Use your protractor to draw an angle of $115^\circ$.
Explanation: Align the baseline of the protractor, locate $115^\circ$ starting from $0^\circ$, mark the point, and draw the second ray from the vertex.
Answer: An obtuse angle measuring $115^\circ$.
j. REPRESENT: Use your protractor to draw an angle of $70^\circ$.
Explanation: Place the protractor center on the vertex, measure $70^\circ$ from the $0^\circ$ baseline, mark the point, and connect it to the vertex.
Answer: An acute angle measuring $70^\circ$.
k. ANALYZE: Samantha's protractor is marked at each degree. Assuming the protractor is used correctly, what is the greatest possible measurement error that can be made with Samantha's protractor? Express your answer as a fraction of a degree.
Explanation: When a measuring instrument is calibrated in increments of $1^\circ$, any measurement rounds to the nearest marked unit. The maximum measurement error is half of the smallest calibration unit: $\frac{1}{2} \cdot 1^\circ = \frac{1}{2}^\circ$.
Answer: $\frac{1}{2}^\circ$

Written Practice

1. One thousand, eight hundred fifty sports fans arrived in the morning for a tournament. An additional four thousand, three hundred seventy fans arrived in the afternoon. How many fans in total attended the tournament?
Explanation: Add the morning and afternoon attendance:
$$1,850 + 4,370 = 6,220$$
Answer: 6,220 fans
2. A standard six-sided number cube is rolled once.
a. What is the probability of rolling a 4?
b. What is the probability of not rolling a 4?
a. Explanation: A number cube has 6 equally likely outcomes $\{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}$. There is 1 outcome that is 4.
$$P(\text{rolling a } 4) = \frac{1}{6}$$
b. Explanation: The outcomes that are not 4 are $\{1, 2, 3, 5, 6\}$, which are 5 outcomes out of 6.
$$P(\text{not rolling a } 4) = 1 - \frac{1}{6} = \frac{5}{6}$$
Answer: a. $\frac{1}{6}$  |  b. $\frac{5}{6}$
3. There are 288 students participating in a field day. If they are equally divided into 18 sports teams, how many students will be on each team?
Explanation: Divide the total number of students by the number of teams:
$$288 \div 18 = 16$$
Answer: 16 students
4. Jamestown was established in 1607. The United States Declaration of Independence was signed in 1776. How many years passed between these two historical events?
Explanation: Subtract the earlier year from the later year:
$$1776 - 1607 = 169$$
Answer: 169 years
5. Which of the following does not equal $1\frac{2}{5}$?
A. $\frac{7}{5}$    B. $1\frac{4}{10}$    C. $\frac{8}{5}$    D. $1\frac{6}{15}$
Explanation: Check each option against $1\frac{2}{5}$:
• $\frac{7}{5} = 1\frac{2}{5}$ (Equal)
• $1\frac{4}{10} = 1\frac{2}{5}$ (Equal)
• $\frac{8}{5} = 1\frac{3}{5} \neq 1\frac{2}{5}$ (NOT Equal)
• $1\frac{6}{15} = 1\frac{2}{5}$ (Equal)
Answer: C. $\frac{8}{5}$
6. ANALYZE: Given line $\overleftrightarrow{AB}$ parallel to line $\overleftrightarrow{CD}$, and line $\overleftrightarrow{BC}$ perpendicular to $\overleftrightarrow{CD}$:
a. Which line is parallel to $\overleftrightarrow{CD}$?
b. Which line is perpendicular to $\overleftrightarrow{CD}$?
c. Angle $ABC$ measures how many degrees?
a. Explanation: Line $\overleftrightarrow{AB}$ is given as parallel to $\overleftrightarrow{CD}$.
b. Explanation: Line $\overleftrightarrow{BC}$ intersects $\overleftrightarrow{CD}$ at a right angle, so $\overleftrightarrow{BC} \perp \overleftrightarrow{CD}$.
c. Explanation: Because parallel lines form congruent interior angles with a perpendicular transversal, $\angle ABC = 90^\circ$.
Answer: a. Line $AB$ ($\overleftrightarrow{AB}$)  |  b. Line $BC$ ($\overleftrightarrow{BC}$)  |  c. $90^\circ$
7. Write each number as a reduced fraction or mixed number:
a. $\frac{15}{20}$    b. $4\frac{10}{15}$    c. $40\%$
a. Divide numerator and denominator by $\text{GCF}(15, 20) = 5$: $\frac{15 \div 5}{20 \div 5} = \frac{3}{4}$
b. Simplify fractional part by dividing by $\text{GCF}(10, 15) = 5$: $4\frac{10 \div 5}{15 \div 5} = 4\frac{2}{3}$
c. Convert percentage to fraction and simplify: $40\% = \frac{40}{100} = \frac{2}{5}$
Answer: a. $\frac{3}{4}$  |  b. $4\frac{2}{3}$  |  c. $\frac{2}{5}$
8. GENERALIZE: A bag of apples weighs $3\text{ lb } 5\text{ oz}$. What is the total weight of the bag in ounces?
Explanation: Since $1\text{ pound} = 16\text{ ounces}$:
$$3\text{ lb} = 3 \times 16\text{ oz} = 48\text{ oz}$$
$$48\text{ oz} + 5\text{ oz} = 53\text{ oz}$$
Answer: $53\text{ oz}$
9. Complete each equivalent fraction:
a. $\frac{3}{8} = \frac{?}{24}$    b. $\frac{1}{4} = \frac{?}{24}$    c. $\frac{5}{6} = \frac{?}{24}$

c. Multiply top and bottom by $4$: $\frac{5 \times 4}{6 \times 4} = \frac{20}{24}$
Answer: a. 9  |  b. 6  |  c. 20

27.
a. Compare: $\left(\frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{2}{5}\right) \cdot \frac{3}{2} \bigcirc \frac{1}{3}\left(\frac{2}{5} \cdot \frac{3}{2}\right)$
b. What property of multiplication is illustrated by this comparison?
a. Calculation:
$$\text{Left side: } \left(\frac{2}{15}\right) \cdot \frac{3}{2} = \frac{6}{30} = \frac{1}{5}$$
$$\text{Right side: } \frac{1}{3} \cdot \left(\frac{6}{10}\right) = \frac{6}{30} = \frac{1}{5}$$

b. Property: Regrouping factors does not change product.

29. Write $2\frac{4}{5}$ as an improper fraction. Then write its reciprocal.

$$2\frac{4}{5} = \frac{(2 \times 5) + 4}{5} = \frac{14}{5}$$
Reciprocal: Invert numerator and denominator: $\frac{14}{5} \rightarrow \frac{5}{14}$

30. Find a fraction equal to $\frac{2}{3}$ with a denominator of 12. Add this fraction to $\frac{7}{12}$. Write the sum as a simplified mixed number.
Step 1: Convert $\frac{2}{3}$ to equivalent fraction with denominator 12:
$$\frac{2 \times 4}{3 \times 4} = \frac{8}{12}$$
Step 2: Add $\frac{8}{12}$ to $\frac{7}{12}$:
$$\frac{8}{12} + \frac{7}{12} = \frac{15}{12}$$
Step 3: Convert to simplified mixed number:
$$\frac{15}{12} = 1\frac{3}{12} = 1\frac{1}{4}$$
Answer: Equivalent fraction: $\frac{8}{12}$  |  Sum: $1\frac{1}{4}$

Additional Skills Practice

31. DIVIDING DECIMALS: Divide $13.95 \div 0.45$.
Step 1: Move decimal point two places to the right in both divisor and dividend to divide by a whole number:
$$0.45 \rightarrow 45$$
$$13.95 \rightarrow 1395$$
Step 2: Perform long division:
$$1395 \div 45 = 31$$
Answer: $31$
32. REPEATING DECIMALS: Divide $5.2 \div 0.3$. Express your answer using overbar notation for the infinite repeating quotient.
Step 1: Shift decimal point one place right in divisor and dividend:
$$5.2 \div 0.3 = 52 \div 3$$
Step 2: Divide $52$ by $3$:
$$52 \div 3 = 17 \text{ R } 1 = 17.3333...$$
Step 3: Write using overbar notation over repeating digit:
$$17.\bar{3}$$
Answer: $17.\bar{3}$
33. PERCENTAGE PROBLEM: A winter coat originally costs $\$120.00$. During an end-of-season sale, it is discounted by $35\%$. What is the dollar amount of the discount, and what is the final sale price of the coat?
Step 1 (Find Discount): Convert $35\%$ to decimal and multiply by original price:
$$\text{Discount} = 0.35 \times \$120.00 = \$42.00$$
Step 2 (Find Sale Price): Subtract discount from original price:
$$\text{Sale Price} = \$120.00 - \$42.00 = \$78.00$$
Answer: Discount: $\$42.00$  |  Final Price: $\$78.00$
34. PEMDAS / ORDER OF OPERATIONS: Simplify the following expression containing fractions, decimals, and signed numbers: $$-3.5 + \frac{3}{4} \cdot \left[8 - (-2.4) \div 0.6\right]$$
Step 1 (Parentheses - Division): Divide inside brackets first:
$$(-2.4) \div 0.6 = -4$$
Step 2 (Parentheses - Subtraction): Simplify inside brackets:
$$8 - (-4) = 8 + 4 = 12$$
Step 3 (Multiplication): Multiply fraction by bracket result:
$$\frac{3}{4} \cdot 12 = 9$$
Step 4 (Addition with Negative Decimal): Combine terms:
$$-3.5 + 9 = 5.5 \quad \left(\text{or } 5\frac{1}{2}\right)$$
Answer: $5.5$ (or $5\frac{1}{2}$)
