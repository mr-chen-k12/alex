Review for Test 12

1. A number cube is rolled once.
a) What is the Sample Space of the experiment? Hint: What could be rolled?
b) What is the probability that the # rolled will be greater than 4?

a) Sample Space:

A standard number cube (die) has 6 faces numbered 1 through 6.

$$S = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}$$

b) Probability > 4:

The numbers greater than 4 in the sample space are 5 and 6. There are 2 such numbers.

$$P(\text{greater than } 4) = \frac{\text{favorable outcomes}}{\text{total outcomes}} = \frac{2}{6}$$

Reduced Fraction: $$\frac{1}{3}$$

2. What is the perimeter of a regular hexagon if each side is 10 inches?

A regular hexagon has 6 equal sides.

$$\text{Perimeter} = \text{number of sides} \times \text{length of one side}$$

$$P = 6 \times 10 \text{ inches}$$

Answer: 60 inches

3. The team won 10 of its 30 games. What is the win-loss ratio?

To find the win-loss ratio, we need the number of Wins and the number of Losses.

  • Wins: 10
  • Total Games: 30
  • Losses: \(30 - 10 = 20\)

The ratio of Wins to Losses is:

$$10 : 20$$

Divide both numbers by 10 to simplify:

Answer: \(1 : 2\) (or 1 to 2)

4. \( 0.45 \div 9 \)

Ignore the decimal for a moment: \(45 \div 9 = 5\).

Since the original number (0.45) has two decimal places, the answer must also have two decimal places.

$$0.45 \div 9 = 0.05$$

5. \( 1.2 \div 0.04 \)

To divide by a decimal, move the decimal point to the right in both numbers until the divisor (0.04) is a whole number.

Move 2 places to the right:

  • \(0.04 \rightarrow 4\)
  • \(1.2 \rightarrow 1.20 \rightarrow 120\)

Now divide:

$$120 \div 4 = 30$$

6. \( \frac{1}{2} + \frac{3}{5} \)

Find a common denominator for 2 and 5, which is 10.

$$\frac{1}{2} = \frac{5}{10}$$

$$\frac{3}{5} = \frac{6}{10}$$

Add the numerators:

$$\frac{5}{10} + \frac{6}{10} = \frac{11}{10}$$

Answer: \(1 \frac{1}{10}\)

7. \( 7\frac{1}{4} - 1\frac{3}{4} \)

You cannot subtract \(\frac{3}{4}\) from \(\frac{1}{4}\), so you must borrow from the whole number 7.

Change \(7\frac{1}{4}\) to \(6\frac{5}{4}\) (since we borrowed 1 whole, or \(\frac{4}{4}\)).

$$6\frac{5}{4} - 1\frac{3}{4}$$

  • Whole numbers: \(6 - 1 = 5\)
  • Fractions: \(\frac{5}{4} - \frac{3}{4} = \frac{2}{4} = \frac{1}{2}\)

Answer: \(5\frac{1}{2}\)

8. \( 5 - 2\frac{1}{3} \) (hint: convert 5 so it has a fraction)

Borrow 1 from the 5 to make a fraction.

\(5\) becomes \(4\frac{3}{3}\).

$$4\frac{3}{3} - 2\frac{1}{3}$$

  • Whole numbers: \(4 - 2 = 2\)
  • Fractions: \(\frac{3}{3} - \frac{1}{3} = \frac{2}{3}\)

Answer: \(2\frac{2}{3}\)

9. If the sales tax is 8%, what is the tax on an $8.29 item?

Convert 8% to a decimal: \(0.08\).

Multiply the price by the tax rate:

$$8.29 \times 0.08 = 0.6632$$

Money is always rounded to two decimal places (cents).

Answer: $0.66

10. Which digit in 24.63 is the same as the 8 in 517.8?

First, identify the place value of the 8 in 517.8.

The 8 is to the right of the decimal, so it is in the tenths place.

Now look at 24.63. Which digit is in the tenths place?

The 6 is in the tenths place.

Answer: 6

11. \( 1 - 0.39 \)

Line up the decimal points. Write 1 as 1.00.

  1.00
- 0.39
------

Borrowing is required.

Answer: 0.61

Review for Test #13

1. How many vertices does a cube have?

A vertex is a corner where edges meet.

A cube has 4 corners on the top and 4 corners on the bottom.

Answer: 8 vertices

2. 40 is a composite number. Use division of primes to find the prime factorization of 40.

Divide 40 by the smallest prime numbers:

  • \(40 \div 2 = 20\)
  • \(20 \div 2 = 10\)
  • \(10 \div 2 = 5\)
  • 5 is prime.

Combining these:

Answer: \(2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 5\) or \(2^3 \times 5\)

3. Spinner Problem:
a) What is the probability of the spinner stopping on an even #?
b) What is the probability of the spinner not stopping on an even #?

Looking at the spinner diagram, there are 6 sections with values: 1, 4, 5, 4, 5, 1.

Total Outcomes: 6

Even numbers (4, 4): 2 outcomes

Odd numbers (1, 1, 5, 5): 4 outcomes

a) Probability (Even): \(\frac{2}{6} = \frac{1}{3}\)

b) Probability (Not Even): This is the same as the probability of getting an odd number.

\(\frac{4}{6} = \frac{2}{3}\)

4. Compare \( \frac{1}{5} \bigcirc \frac{1}{3} \)

When the numerators are the same (both are 1), the fraction with the smaller denominator is the larger number (because the whole is split into fewer pieces).

\(\frac{1}{5} = 0.20\)

\(\frac{1}{3} = 0.33...\)

Answer: \( \frac{1}{5} < \frac{1}{3} \)

5. An item costs $13.00 and the sales tax is 7%. What is the total price of the item including sales tax?

Step 1: Calculate Tax

$$13.00 \times 0.07 = 0.91$$

Tax is $0.91.

Step 2: Add to Original Price

$$13.00 + 0.91 = 13.91$$

Answer: $13.91

6. By what fraction equal to 1 should \( \frac{3}{4} \) be multiplied by to form \( \frac{9}{12} \)?

We are looking for \(\frac{x}{x}\) such that:

$$\frac{3}{4} \times \frac{x}{x} = \frac{9}{12}$$

Looking at the numerators: \(3 \times ? = 9\). The answer is 3.

Looking at the denominators: \(4 \times ? = 12\). The answer is 3.

Answer: \(\frac{3}{3}\)

7. \( 3\frac{5}{6} - 1\frac{1}{2} \)

Find a common denominator. The LCD for 6 and 2 is 6.

\(1\frac{1}{2} = 1\frac{3}{6}\)

$$3\frac{5}{6} - 1\frac{3}{6}$$

  • Whole numbers: \(3 - 1 = 2\)
  • Fractions: \(\frac{5}{6} - \frac{3}{6} = \frac{2}{6}\)

Reduce \(\frac{2}{6}\) to \(\frac{1}{3}\).

Answer: \(2\frac{1}{3}\)

8. \( 1\frac{2}{3} + 1\frac{5}{6} \)

Find a common denominator (6).

\(1\frac{2}{3} = 1\frac{4}{6}\)

$$1\frac{4}{6} + 1\frac{5}{6} = 2\frac{9}{6}$$

The fraction \(\frac{9}{6}\) is improper. \(\frac{9}{6} = 1\frac{3}{6} = 1\frac{1}{2}\).

Add this to the whole number 2:

\(2 + 1\frac{1}{2} = 3\frac{1}{2}\)

Answer: \(3\frac{1}{2}\)

9. \( \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{4} + \frac{5}{6} \)

Find the LCD for 3, 4, and 6. The LCD is 12.

  • \(\frac{1}{3} = \frac{4}{12}\)
  • \(\frac{1}{4} = \frac{3}{12}\)
  • \(\frac{5}{6} = \frac{10}{12}\)

Add numerators: \(4 + 3 + 10 = 17\)

Total: \(\frac{17}{12}\)

Convert to mixed number: \(12\) goes into \(17\) once with \(5\) left over.

Answer: \(1\frac{5}{12}\)

10. Write \( 2\frac{3}{4} \) as an improper fraction. Then multiply it by \( \frac{1}{6} \). Write the product as a reduced fraction.

Part 1: Improper Fraction

\(2 \times 4 + 3 = 11\)

Improper: \(\frac{11}{4}\)

Part 2: Multiply

$$\frac{11}{4} \times \frac{1}{6} = \frac{11 \times 1}{4 \times 6} = \frac{11}{24}$$

11 is prime and does not go into 24, so it is already reduced.

Answer: \(\frac{11}{24}\)
