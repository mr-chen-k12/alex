*1.

If the product of two one-digit whole numbers is 35, what is the sum of the same two numbers?

Solution:

The only two one-digit whole numbers that multiply to give 35 are $5$ and $7$ ($5 \times 7 = 35$).

Their sum is: $5 + 7 = \mathbf{12}$

6.

List the single-digit factors of 924.

Solution:

Test digits from 1 to 9:

  • 1: Always a factor $\rightarrow$ Yes
  • 2: Ends in an even digit (4) $\rightarrow$ Yes
  • 3: Sum of digits ($9+2+4=15$) is divisible by 3 $\rightarrow$ Yes
  • 4: Last two digits (24) are divisible by 4 $\rightarrow$ Yes
  • 5: Does not end in 0 or 5 $\rightarrow$ No
  • 6: Divisible by both 2 and 3 $\rightarrow$ Yes
  • 7: $924 \div 7 = 132$ $\rightarrow$ Yes
  • 8: $924 \div 8 = 115.5$ $\rightarrow$ No
  • 9: Sum of digits (15) is not divisible by 9 $\rightarrow$ No

The single-digit factors are: $\mathbf{1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7}$

11. [CONNECT]

The temperature at noon was $3^\circ\text{C}$. The temperature at 5:00 p.m. was $-4^\circ\text{C}$. Did the temperature rise or fall between noon and 5:00 p.m.? By how many degrees?

Solution:

The temperature changed from a positive value to a negative value, so it fell.

$$3^\circ\text{C} - (-4^\circ\text{C}) = 7^\circ\text{C}$$

The temperature fell by $7^\circ\text{C}$.

18. [EXPLAIN]

Does the quotient of $4554 \div 9$ have a remainder? How can you tell without dividing?

Answer:

No, it does not have a remainder.

Explanation: According to the divisibility rule for 9, if the sum of the digits of a number is divisible by 9, the number itself is divisible by 9. Here, $4 + 5 + 5 + 4 = 18$. Since 18 is perfectly divisible by 9, $4554$ can be divided by 9 with no remainder.

*23. [JUSTIFY]

Show the steps and the properties that make this multiplication easier to perform mentally: $8 \cdot 7 \cdot 5$

Solution:

$$8 \cdot 7 \cdot 5 = 8 \cdot 5 \cdot 7 \quad \text{(Commutative Property of Multiplication)}$$

$$= (8 \cdot 5) \cdot 7 \quad \text{(Associative Property of Multiplication)}$$

$$= 40 \cdot 7 = \mathbf{280}$$

24.

Compare: $4000 \div (200 \div 10) \bigcirc (4000 \div 200) \div 10$

Solution:

Left Side: $4000 \div 20 = 200$

Right Side: $20 \div 10 = 2$

Since $200 > 2$:

$$4000 \div (200 \div 10) \mathbf{\Huge >} (4000 \div 200) \div 10$$

Real-World Connection

Lindy and seven friends played miniature golf at a new course. Par for the course is 63. The players' scores were recorded as numbers above or below par. The recorded scores were:

$1, -1, 3, -3, 5, 0, -2, 3$

a. What were the eight scores?
b. Arrange the scores in order from least to greatest.

  • $63 + 1 = \mathbf{64}$
  • $63 - 1 = \mathbf{62}$
  • $63 + 3 = \mathbf{66}$
  • $63 - 3 = \mathbf{60}$
  • $63 + 5 = \mathbf{68}$
  • $63 + 0 = \mathbf{63}$
  • $63 - 2 = \mathbf{61}$
  • $63 + 3 = \mathbf{66}$

The scores are: 64, 62, 66, 60, 68, 63, 61, 66

b. Least to Greatest:

$$\mathbf{60, 61, 62, 63, 64, 66, 66, 68}$$

c. Par or Under Par (Relative score $\le 0$):

Scores that are 63 or lower: 60, 61, 62, 63.

Total: 4 scores ($\mathbf{60, 61, 62, 63}$)
