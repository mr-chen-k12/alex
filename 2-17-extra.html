Word Problems: Finding the Difference
1. The Great Pyramid of Giza was originally 481 feet tall. The Statue of Liberty is 305 feet tall. How many feet taller was the Great Pyramid? Explain how you found your answer.
176 feet.
Explanation: Subtract the height of the Statue of Liberty from the Great Pyramid (\( 481 - 305 = 176 \)).
2. The Amazon River is about 4,000 miles long. The Mississippi River is about 2,340 miles long. How many miles longer is the Amazon River? Explain your thinking.
1,660 miles.
Explanation: Subtract the length of the Mississippi River from the Amazon River (\( 4,000 - 2,340 = 1,660 \)).
3. A commercial airplane flies at an altitude of 35,000 feet. A small private plane flies at 12,500 feet. What is the difference in their altitudes? Explain how you found your answer.
22,500 feet.
Explanation: Subtract the smaller altitude from the larger altitude (\( 35,000 - 12,500 = 22,500 \)).
