1.(28)
EXPLAIN The young tree was 5 feet 8 inches high. How can we find the number of inches in 5 feet 8 inches?
Answer
\(68\text{ inches}\)
Solution
Multiply the feet by 12 (inches per foot) and add the remaining inches: \(5 \times 12 + 8 = 60 + 8 = 68\) inches.
2.(28)
During the first week of November the daily high temperatures in degrees Fahrenheit were 42°F, 43°F, 38°F, 47°F, 51°F, 52°F, and 49°F. What was the average daily high temperature during the first week of November?
Answer
\(46^\circ\text{F}\)
Solution
Add the seven highs: \(42 + 43 + 38 + 47 + 51 + 52 + 49 = 322\). Divide by 7 days: \(322 \div 7 = 46\), so the average was 46°F.

The population of Chandler, AZ increased from 89,862 in 1990 to 176,581 in 2000. How many people did the population increase by over this 10-year time span?

5.(19)
a. A regular hexagon and a regular octagon share a common side. If the perimeter of the hexagon is 24 cm, what is the perimeter of the octagon?
b. JUSTIFY How did you get your answer?
Answer
\(\text{a. }32\text{ cm}\)
Solution
a. A regular hexagon has 6 equal sides, so the shared side is \(24 \div 6 = 4\) cm. A regular octagon has 8 equal sides, so its perimeter is \(8 \times 4 = 32\) cm.
b. Because the figures share a side, the octagon's side must equal the hexagon's side (4 cm). Multiplying by the octagon's 8 sides gives 32 cm.
6.(22)
Diagram this statement. Then answer the questions that follow.
One-third of the 60 fish were goldfish.
a. How many of the fish were goldfish?
b. How many of the fish were not goldfish?
c. What percent of the fish were goldfish?
Answer
\(\text{a. }20 \qquad \text{b. }40 \qquad \text{c. }33\frac{1}{3}\%\)
Solution
a. \(\frac{1}{3}\) of 60 is \(60 \div 3 = 20\) goldfish. b. \(60 - 20 = 40\) fish were not goldfish. c. One-third as a percent is \(33\frac{1}{3}\%\).
7.(Inv. 3)
GENERALIZE Find the area of a square whose vertices have the coordinates (3, 6), (3, 1), (−2, 1), and (−2, 6).
Answer
\(25\text{ square units}\)
Solution
The side length is the distance from x = 3 to x = −2 (5 units), and also from y = 1 to y = 6 (5 units) — confirming a square. Area: \(5 \times 5 = 25\) square units.

a. Round 15.73591 to the nearest hundredth.
b. Estimate the product of 15.73591 and 3.14 by rounding each decimal number to the nearest whole number before multiplying.
Answer

13.(17)
Draw the straight angle AOC. Then use a protractor to draw ray OD so that angle COD measures 60°.
Answer
\(\angle COD = 60^\circ, \quad \angle AOD = 120^\circ\)
Solution
A straight angle measures 180°, so ray OD is drawn 60° above the OC side of the straight line. The remaining part is \(\angle AOD = 180^\circ - 60^\circ = 120^\circ\).
14.(2)
GENERALIZE If we multiply one integer by another integer that is a whole number but not a counting number, what is the product?
Answer
\(0\)
Solution
The whole numbers are {0, 1, 2, 3, …} and the counting numbers are {1, 2, 3, …}, so the only whole number that is not a counting number is 0. Any integer times 0 is 0.
15.(27)
GENERALIZE Use prime factorization to find the least common denominator for the fractions \(\frac{5}{27}\) and \(\frac{5}{36}\).
Answer
\(108\)
Solution
Prime factorizations: \(27 = 3^3\) and \(36 = 2^2 \cdot 3^2\). The least common multiple takes the highest power of each prime: \(2^2 \cdot 3^3 = 4 \cdot 27 = 108\).

26.(14)
ANALYZE If one card is drawn from a regular deck of cards, what is the probability the card will not be an ace?
Answer
\(\frac{12}{13}\)
Solution
A regular deck has 52 cards and 4 aces, so 52 − 4 = 48 cards are not aces. The probability is \(\frac{48}{52} = \frac{12}{13}\).

28.(29)
JUSTIFY Consider the following division problem. Without dividing, decide whether the quotient will be greater than 1 or less than 1. How did you decide?
\(5 \div 4\frac{1}{6}\)
Answer
\(\text{Greater than }1\)
Solution
Compare the divisor to the dividend: the divisor \(4\frac{1}{6}\) is less than the dividend 5. Dividing 5 by a number smaller than 5 gives a quotient greater than 1 (and indeed \(5 \div 4\frac{1}{6} = 1\frac{1}{5}\)).
29.(29)
When Paulo saw the following addition problem, he knew that the sum would be greater than 13 and less than 15. How did he know?
\(8\frac{7}{8} + 5\frac{2}{3}\)
Answer
\(\text{By bounding each addend between whole numbers}\)
Solution
Each addend sits between two whole numbers: \(8 < 8\frac{7}{8} < 9\) and \(5 < 5\frac{2}{3} < 6\). Adding the lower bounds gives more than 13, and adding the upper bounds gives less than 15, so \(13 < \text{sum} < 15\). (The exact sum is \(14\frac{13}{24}\), which checks out.)
30.(17)
Use a protractor to draw a triangle that has a 30° angle and a 60° angle.
Answer
\(\text{Angles: }30^\circ, 60^\circ, 90^\circ\)
Solution
The third angle is \(180^\circ - 30^\circ - 60^\circ = 90^\circ\), so the triangle is a right triangle — a 30°-60°-90° triangle, as drawn.
