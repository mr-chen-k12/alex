Lesson Practice

Solve:

a. \( 6w = 21 \)

Answer: \( w = 3.5 \) (or \( 3\frac{1}{2} \))
Explanation: Divide both sides by 6 to isolate \(w\). \( w = \frac{21}{6} \). Both numbers are divisible by 3, which simplifies to \( \frac{7}{2} \), or 3.5.

b. \( 50 = 3f \)

Answer: \( f = 16\frac{2}{3} \)
Explanation: Divide both sides by 3. \( f = \frac{50}{3} \). 50 divided by 3 is 16 with a remainder of 2, so \( 16\frac{2}{3} \).

c. \( 5n = 36 \)

Answer: \( n = 7.2 \) (or \( 7\frac{1}{5} \))
Explanation: Divide both sides by 5. \( n = \frac{36}{5} \). 36 divided by 5 is 7 with a remainder of 1, so \( 7\frac{1}{5} \) or 7.2.

d. \( 0.3t = 0.24 \)

Answer: \( t = 0.8 \)
Explanation: Divide both sides by 0.3. \( t = \frac{0.24}{0.3} \). Multiply top and bottom by 100 to get \( \frac{24}{30} \), which simplifies to \( \frac{8}{10} \) or 0.8.

e. \( 8m = 3.2 \)

Answer: \( m = 0.4 \)
Explanation: Divide both sides by 8. \( m = \frac{3.2}{8} \). Since 32 divided by 8 is 4, 3.2 divided by 8 is 0.4.

f. \( 0.8 = 0.5x \)

Answer: \( x = 1.6 \)
Explanation: Divide both sides by 0.5. \( x = \frac{0.8}{0.5} \). Multiply top and bottom by 10 to get \( \frac{8}{5} \). 8 divided by 5 is 1.6.

Written Practice

1. If the divisor is 12 and the quotient is 24, what is the dividend?
Answer: 288
Explanation: The relationship in division is: Dividend ÷ Divisor = Quotient. Therefore, Dividend = Divisor × Quotient. \( 12 \times 24 = 288 \).
2. The brachiosaurus, one of the largest dinosaurs, weighed only \( \frac{1}{4} \) as much as a blue whale. A blue whale can weigh 140 tons. How much could a brachiosaurus have weighed?
Answer: 35 tons
Explanation: Multiply the weight of the blue whale by \( \frac{1}{4} \). \( 140 \times \frac{1}{4} = \frac{140}{4} = 35 \).
3. Fourteen of the 32 students in the class are boys. What is the ratio of boys to girls in the class?
Answer: \( \frac{7}{9} \)
Explanation: First, find the number of girls: \( 32 - 14 = 18 \) girls. The ratio of boys to girls is 14 to 18. Written as a fraction, \( \frac{14}{18} \), which simplifies by dividing by 2 to get \( \frac{7}{9} \).
Find each unknown number:

4. \( 0.3m = 0.27 \)

Answer: \( m = 0.9 \)
Explanation: Divide both sides by 0.3. \( m = \frac{0.27}{0.3} = \frac{2.7}{3} = 0.9 \).

5. \( 31 = 5n \)

Answer: \( n = 6.2 \) (or \( 6\frac{1}{5} \))
Explanation: Divide both sides by 5. \( n = \frac{31}{5} \). 31 divided by 5 is 6 with a remainder of 1, so \( 6\frac{1}{5} \) or 6.2.

6. \( 3n = 6^2 \)

Answer: \( n = 12 \)
Explanation: First calculate \( 6^2 = 36 \). The equation becomes \( 3n = 36 \). Divide by 3 to get \( n = 12 \).

7. \( 4n = 0.35 \)

Answer: \( n = 0.0875 \)
Explanation: Divide both sides by 4. \( n = \frac{0.35}{4} = 0.0875 \).
8. Write 0.25 as a fraction and add it to \( 3\frac{1}{4} \). What is the sum?
Answer: \( 3\frac{1}{2} \) (or 3.5)
Explanation: 0.25 as a fraction is \( \frac{1}{4} \). Add \( \frac{1}{4} + 3\frac{1}{4} = 3\frac{2}{4} \). Simplify the fraction to get \( 3\frac{1}{2} \).
9. Write \( \frac{3}{5} \) as a decimal and add it to 6.5. What is the sum?
Answer: 7.1
Explanation: \( \frac{3}{5} \) as a decimal is 0.6. Add 0.6 + 6.5 = 7.1.
10. Write \( \frac{1}{50} \) as a decimal number and as a percent.
Answer: 0.02 and 2%
Explanation: Multiply the numerator and denominator by 2 to get a denominator of 100: \( \frac{2}{100} \). As a decimal, this is 0.02. As a percent (per hundred), it is 2%.
11. \( 12\frac{1}{5} - 3\frac{4}{5} \)
Answer: \( 8\frac{2}{5} \)
Explanation: You cannot subtract \( \frac{4}{5} \) from \( \frac{1}{5} \) directly. Borrow 1 whole from 12 (making it 11) and add it as \( \frac{5}{5} \) to \( \frac{1}{5} \), making the first fraction \( 11\frac{6}{5} \). Now subtract: \( 11\frac{6}{5} - 3\frac{4}{5} = 8\frac{2}{5} \).
12. \( 6\frac{2}{3} \times 1\frac{1}{5} \)
Answer: 8
Explanation: Convert to improper fractions: \( \frac{20}{3} \times \frac{6}{5} \). Cross-cancel: 5 goes into 20 four times, and 3 goes into 6 two times. So, \( 4 \times 2 = 8 \). Alternatively, \( \frac{120}{15} = 8 \).
13. \( 11\frac{1}{9} \div 100 \)
Answer: \( \frac{1}{9} \)
Explanation: Convert to an improper fraction: \( \frac{100}{9} \). To divide by 100, multiply by its reciprocal \( \frac{1}{100} \). \( \frac{100}{9} \times \frac{1}{100} = \frac{1}{9} \).

15. \( 35 - (0.35 \times 100) \)
Answer: 0
Explanation: Follow order of operations (PEMDAS). First multiply inside the parentheses: \( 0.35 \times 100 = 35 \). Then subtract: \( 35 - 35 = 0 \).
16. \( 4 + 4 \times 4 - 4 \div 4 \)
Answer: 19
Explanation: Follow order of operations (PEMDAS). Multiply and divide first: \( 4 \times 4 = 16 \) and \( 4 \div 4 = 1 \). The equation becomes \( 4 + 16 - 1 \). Then add and subtract from left to right: \( 20 - 1 = 19 \).
17. Write the decimal numeral twelve and five hundredths.
Answer: 12.05
Explanation: "Twelve" is the whole number. "And" represents the decimal point. "Five hundredths" means the digit 5 is in the hundredths place (the second decimal place).
18. Find the volume of this rectangular prism. (Dimensions: 10 in., 5 in., 5 in.)
Answer: 250 cubic inches (in³)
Explanation: Volume = length × width × height. \( 10 \times 5 \times 5 = 50 \times 5 = 250 \).
19. If \( a \) equals 15, then what number does \( 2a - 5 \) equal?
Answer: 25
Explanation: Substitute 15 for \( a \). The expression becomes \( 2(15) - 5 \). Multiply first: \( 30 - 5 = 25 \).
20. What is the area of this parallelogram? (Base: 25 mm, Height: 18 mm)
Answer: 450 mm²
Explanation: The area of a parallelogram is Base × Height. \( 25 \text{ mm} \times 18 \text{ mm} = 450 \text{ mm}^2 \). (Note: The 20 mm slanted side is used for perimeter, not area).
21. What is the perimeter of this parallelogram? (Sides: 25 mm and 20 mm)
Answer: 90 mm
Explanation: Perimeter is the distance around the outside. Add the lengths of all four sides: \( 25 + 20 + 25 + 20 = 90 \text{ mm} \).
22. "All rectangles are parallelograms." True or false?
Answer: True
Explanation: A parallelogram is a quadrilateral with two pairs of parallel opposite sides. A rectangle fits this definition completely, with the added property that all its interior angles are right angles.
23. Charles spent \( \frac{1}{10} \) of his 100 shillings. How many shillings does he still have?
Answer: 90 shillings
Explanation: First, find out how much he spent: \( \frac{1}{10} \text{ of } 100 = 10 \text{ shillings} \). Subtract the amount spent from the original amount: \( 100 - 10 = 90 \text{ shillings} \).
24. The temperature rose from –18°F to 19°F. How many degrees did the temperature increase?
Answer: 37°F
Explanation: Find the difference between the final and initial temperatures: \( 19 - (-18) = 19 + 18 = 37 \).
25. How many centimeters long is the line below? (Ruler reads to 48 mm)
Answer: 4.8 centimeters
Explanation: The line ends at the 48 millimeter mark on the ruler. Since there are 10 millimeters in a centimeter, divide by 10 to convert: \( 48 \text{ mm} \div 10 = 4.8 \text{ cm} \).
26. Johann poured 500 mL of water from a full 2-liter container. How many milliliters of water were left in the container?
Answer: 1500 mL
Explanation: First convert liters to milliliters. 2 liters = 2000 milliliters. Then subtract the amount poured out: \( 2000 \text{ mL} - 500 \text{ mL} = 1500 \text{ mL} \).
27. Name this geometric solid.
Answer: Triangular prism
Explanation: The shape has two identical triangular bases connected by three rectangular sides, which is the definition of a triangular prism.
28. Simplify:

a. 2 meters + 100 centimeters (Write the answer in meters.)

Answer: 3 meters
Explanation: First convert centimeters to meters: 100 centimeters = 1 meter. Then add: \( 2 \text{ m} + 1 \text{ m} = 3 \text{ m} \).

b. \( 2\text{ m} \cdot 4\text{ m} \)

Answer: 8 m² (or 8 square meters)
Explanation: Multiply the numbers and the units: \( 2 \times 4 = 8 \), and \( \text{meters} \times \text{meters} = \text{meters squared} \).
29. Solve this proportion: \( \frac{12}{m} = \frac{18}{9} \)
Answer: \( m = 6 \)
Explanation: Simplify the right side first: \( \frac{18}{9} = 2 \). Now you have \( \frac{12}{m} = 2 \). Multiply both sides by \( m \) to get \( 12 = 2m \). Divide by 2 to get \( m = 6 \). Alternatively, cross-multiply: \( 18m = 108 \rightarrow m = 6 \).
30. What is the perimeter of a rectangle with vertices at (-4, -4), (-4, 4), (4, 4), and (4, -4)?
Answer: 32 units
Explanation: The width is the distance between x-coordinates 4 and -4: \( |4 - (-4)| = 8 \). The height is the distance between y-coordinates 4 and -4: \( |4 - (-4)| = 8 \). Since all sides are 8 units, it's a square. Perimeter = \( 8 + 8 + 8 + 8 = 32 \) units.

Real-World Connection

Frida and her family went on a summer vacation to Chicago, Illinois from Boston, Massachusetts. Her family drove 986 miles in 17 hours.

a. How many miles per hour did Frida's family average on their drive to Chicago?

Answer: 58 miles per hour
Explanation: Divide the total miles by the total hours to find the average speed: \( 986 \text{ miles} \div 17 \text{ hours} = 58 \text{ mph} \).

b. If the family's car averages 29 miles per gallon, how many gallons of gas did they use on their trip?

Answer: 34 gallons
Explanation: Divide the total miles by the miles per gallon to find total gallons used: \( 986 \text{ miles} \div 29 \text{ miles/gallon} = 34 \text{ gallons} \).

Motivating Mindset

Coach will give an award to any student who can do ten pull-ups by the end of the year. Kelly did four pull-ups and said, "I'll never be able to do ten pull-ups." What would you say to encourage Kelly?
Answer (Example): "You've already done four, which is a great start! With consistent practice and a growth mindset, you can build your strength over the year to reach ten."
Explanation: This question tests encouraging a growth mindset. An ideal response acknowledges current progress (doing 4 pull-ups is an achievement!), normalizes that learning takes time, and encourages setting smaller goals (e.g., trying for 5 next week) rather than focusing only on the final goal of 10.
