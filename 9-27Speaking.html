1 Warm-Up: What is Screen Time?
30 Seconds
Key Points to Remember: • Screen time includes TV, gaming, tablets, and phones.
• Balance means enjoying screens without missing out on exercise, reading, and sleep.
Quick Pick:
"Hello everyone! My name is Alex. Today I want to talk about screen time. I really enjoy playing Minecraft, but screens are like sweet treats—too much isn't healthy! Balancing screen time means enjoying our games while still making time for school, outdoor fun, and rest."
2 Informative Speech: Brain & Body Health
45 Seconds
Key Points to Remember: • Screen blue light tricks our brains into staying awake at night.
• Staring at displays too long causes dry eyes and neck stiffness.
Quick Pick:
"Did you know our eyes and brains need regular screen breaks? Staring at glowing displays for hours can make our eyes tired and dry. Also, blue light from tablets makes it hard to sleep! By swapping my tablet for reading a storybook an hour before bed, my mind can recharge properly so I feel energized for school!"
3 Problem-Solving: The 50/50 Screen Rule
45 Seconds
Key Points to Remember: • For every 30 minutes on screens, match it with 30 minutes of physical movement.
• Setting timers shows self-control and responsibility.
Quick Pick:
"Here is a great rule I follow called The 50/50 Rule! When I play on a screen for 30 minutes, I match it with equal time playing active games. I use a loud kitchen timer, and when it rings, I turn off the screen right away without complaining. This shows self-control!"
4 Persuasive Speech: Favorite Offline Activity
45 Seconds
Key Points to Remember: • Offline activities spark real-world creativity, friendships, and physical strength.
• Inspire your classmates by sharing what you love to do most outdoors or at home.
Quick Pick:
"What happens when we unplug our screens? We discover awesome real-world activities! When I turn off my tablet, my absolute favorite offline activity is riding my bike in the park. Doing this makes me feel happy, active, and creative. I challenge all of you to try one new unplugged activity this afternoon!"
