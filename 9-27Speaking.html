Module 2: Health Bars & Digital Fatigue

The science of eye strain, posture, and brain stamina

45 Secs
Pro-Gamer Key Points:

• Staring at blue light screens for hours causes real-world "lag" (headaches, dry eyes, slump posture).

• Real-life stamina doesn't recharge if you play until 11 PM on a school night.

"Think about your body and mind like a character stat bar in an RPG. When you game for three or four hours straight without taking a breather, your real-life Stamina Bar drains to zero. You get blurry eyes, slouched posture, and foggy focus. Your brain literally starts lagging during math class! Sitting in a chair all night doesn't level up your physical health."

Module 3: The AFK Cool-Down Strategy

Setting daily targets and taking structured breaks

45 Secs
Pro-Gamer Key Points:

• Self-control rule: Set an alarm timer before starting a gaming session.

• AFK (Away From Keyboard) breaks keep energy high and prevent gamer burn-out.

"That's why I created my own rule called the AFK Cool-Down Strategy. My target is 1.5 hours on weekdays. Every 45 minutes, I go AFK for 10 minutes by shooting 20 free throws & drinking ice water. Setting a loud alarm timer means I decide when to quit—not my parents yelling at me. That's real self-control!"
