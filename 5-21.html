1. Divide 555 by 12 and write the quotient
a. with a remainder.
b. as a mixed number.

Explanation:
First, perform long division: $555 \div 12$.
$12 \times 40 = 480$
$555 - 480 = 75$
$12 \times 6 = 72$
$75 - 72 = 3$ (This is the remainder).
So, the quotient is 46 with a remainder of 3.

For the mixed number, write the remainder over the divisor: $46 \frac{3}{12}$, which simplifies to $46 \frac{1}{4}$.

Answer:
a. 46 R 3
b. $46 \frac{1}{4}$

2. The six gymnasts scored 9.75, 9.8, 9.9, 9.4, 9.9, and 9.95. The lowest score was not counted. What was the sum of the five highest scores?

Explanation:
Identify the lowest score: 9.4. Exclude it from the list.
The five highest scores are: 9.75, 9.8, 9.9, 9.9, 9.95.
Add them together: $9.75 + 9.80 + 9.90 + 9.90 + 9.95 = 49.30$.

Answer: 49.3

3. Cantara said that the six trumpet players made up 10% of the band. The band had how many members?

Explanation:
Let $x$ be the total number of band members.
$10\%$ of $x = 6$
$0.10 \times x = 6$
$x = 6 \div 0.10$
$x = 60$

Answer: 60 members

4. Eight is $\frac{2}{3}$ of what number?

Explanation:
Set up an equation where $x$ is the unknown number:
$8 = \frac{2}{3}x$
To solve for $x$, multiply both sides by the reciprocal ($\frac{3}{2}$):
$x = 8 \times \frac{3}{2} = \frac{24}{2} = 12$

Answer: 12

5. Write the standard number for the following: $(1 \times 10^5) + (8 \times 10^4) + (6 \times 10^3)$

Explanation:
Expand the scientific notation:
$1 \times 10^5 = 100,000$
$8 \times 10^4 = 80,000$
$6 \times 10^3 = 6,000$
Add them together: $100,000 + 80,000 + 6,000 = 186,000$.

Answer: 186,000

6. On Rob's scale drawing, each inch represents 8 feet. One of the rooms in his drawing is $2 \frac{1}{2}$ inches long. How long is the actual room?

Explanation:
Multiply the drawing length by the scale factor:
$2 \frac{1}{2} \text{ inches} \times 8 \text{ feet/inch}$
Convert $2 \frac{1}{2}$ to an improper fraction: $\frac{5}{2}$.
$\frac{5}{2} \times 8 = \frac{40}{2} = 20$.

Answer: 20 feet

7. Two angles of a triangle each measure 45°.
a. What is the measure of the third angle?
b. Make a rough sketch of the triangle.

Explanation:
a. The sum of angles in a triangle is always 180°. Subtract the two known angles:
$180^\circ - 45^\circ - 45^\circ = 90^\circ$.
b. A triangle with angles 90°, 45°, and 45° is a right isosceles triangle.

Answer:
a. 90°
b. (See sketch above: a right isosceles triangle)

8. Convert $8 \frac{1}{3}\%$ to a fraction.

Explanation:
First, convert the mixed number to an improper fraction: $8 \frac{1}{3} = \frac{25}{3}$.
Since it is a percent, divide by 100 (which is the same as multiplying by $\frac{1}{100}$):
$\frac{25}{3} \times \frac{1}{100} = \frac{25}{300}$.
Simplify by dividing numerator and denominator by 25:
$\frac{25 \div 25}{300 \div 25} = \frac{1}{12}$.

Answer: $\frac{1}{12}$

9. Nine dollars is what percent of $12?

Explanation:
Create a fraction showing the part over the whole: $\frac{9}{12}$.
Simplify the fraction: $\frac{9}{12} = \frac{3}{4}$.
Convert $\frac{3}{4}$ to a percentage by multiplying by 100: $\frac{3}{4} \times 100 = 75\%$.

Answer: 75%

10. Twenty percent of what number is 12?

Multiply both sides by 5:

15. $2 \frac{1}{2} - 1 \frac{2}{3}$

Explanation:
Convert to improper fractions:
$\frac{5}{2} - \frac{5}{3}$
Find a common denominator (6):
$\frac{5 \times 3}{2 \times 3} - \frac{5 \times 2}{3 \times 2} = \frac{15}{6} - \frac{10}{6}$
Subtract the numerators:
$\frac{15 - 10}{6} = \frac{5}{6}$.

Answer: $\frac{5}{6}$

16. Stephen competes in a two-event race made up of biking and running. The ratio of the length of the distance run to the length of the bike ride is 2 to 5. If the distance run was 10 kilometers, then what was the total length of the two-event race?

Explanation:
Ratio of Run to Bike = $2:5$.
Let the run distance be $2x$ and bike distance be $5x$.
We are given the run distance is 10 km, so: $2x = 10 \rightarrow x = 5$.
The bike distance is $5x = 5(5) = 25$ km.
Total length = Run + Bike = $10 \text{ km} + 25 \text{ km} = 35$ km.

Answer: 35 kilometers

17. The area of the shaded triangle is 2.8 cm². What is the area of the parallelogram?

Explanation:
A diagonal divides a parallelogram into two congruent (identical) triangles. Therefore, the total area of the parallelogram is exactly twice the area of the shaded triangle.
Area = $2.8 \text{ cm}^2 \times 2 = 5.6 \text{ cm}^2$.

Answer: 5.6 cm²

18. The figurine was packed in a box that was 10 in. long, 3 in. wide, and 4 in. deep. What was the volume of the box?

Explanation:
The volume of a rectangular box is calculated as $\text{Length} \times \text{Width} \times \text{Height (Depth)}$.
$V = 10 \text{ in.} \times 3 \text{ in.} \times 4 \text{ in.} = 120 \text{ cubic inches}$.

Answer: 120 in.³

19. A rectangle that is not a square has a total of how many lines of symmetry?

Explanation:
A standard rectangle has two lines of symmetry: one vertical line through the center and one horizontal line through the center. (Diagonals are not lines of symmetry unless it is a square).

Answer: 2

20. If this shape were cut out and folded on the dotted lines, would it form a cube, a pyramid, or a cone?

The net consists of four triangles. When the outer triangles are folded up along the dotted lines, they meet at a single point (apex), forming a 3D shape with a triangular base and triangular faces. This is a triangular pyramid (specifically, a tetrahedron).

26. The coordinates of three points that are on the same line are (-3, -2), (0, 0), and ($x$, 4). What number should replace $x$ in the third set of coordinates?

Explanation:
Find the slope ($m$) of the line using the first two points: (-3, -2) and (0, 0).
$m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} = \frac{0 - (-2)}{0 - (-3)} = \frac{2}{3}$.
The equation of the line passing through origin is $y = \frac{2}{3}x$.
Plug in the third point ($x$, 4) where $y = 4$:
$4 = \frac{2}{3}x \rightarrow x = 4 \times \frac{3}{2} = 6$.

Answer: 6

27. Robert flipped a coin. It landed heads up. He flipped the coin a second time. It landed heads up. If he flips the coin a third time, what is the probability that it will land heads up?

Explanation:
Coin flips are independent events. The outcome of previous flips does not affect the outcome of the next flip. A standard coin always has 1 Heads and 1 Tails.

Answer: $\frac{1}{2}$

28. James is going to flip a coin three times. What is the probability that the coin will land heads up all three times?

Explanation:
Since each flip is an independent event with a probability of $\frac{1}{2}$ for getting heads, multiply the probabilities together for the sequence:
$P(\text{H, H, H}) = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{8}$.

Answer: $\frac{1}{8}$

29. The diameter of the circle is 10 cm.
a. What is the area of the square?
b. What is the area of the circle?

Explanation:
The diameter is 10 cm, so the radius ($r$) is 5 cm. Based on the image, the side length of the shaded square is exactly equal to the radius of the circle (5 cm).
a. Area of square = $\text{side} \times \text{side} = 5 \times 5 = 25 \text{ cm}^2$.
b. Area of circle = $\pi r^2 = \pi \times 5^2 = 25\pi \text{ cm}^2$ (or approximately $78.5 \text{ cm}^2$).

Answer:
a. 25 cm²
b. $25\pi \text{ cm}^2$ (approx 78.5 cm²)

30. What is the mode and the range of this set of numbers?
4, 7, 6, 4, 5, 3, 2, 6, 7, 9, 7, 4, 10, 7, 9

Explanation:
First, put the numbers in order: 2, 3, 4, 4, 4, 5, 6, 6, 7, 7, 7, 7, 9, 9, 10.
Mode: The number that appears most frequently. 7 appears four times, which is more than any other number.
Range: The difference between the highest and lowest numbers. Maximum is 10, Minimum is 2. $10 - 2 = 8$.

Answer:
Mode = 7
Range = 8
