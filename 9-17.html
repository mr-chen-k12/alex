Lesson Practice
Lesson Practice a (CLASSIFY)
The number $1,000,000,000$ is how much greater than $25,000,000$? What type of problem is this? Explain your reasoning.

Equation: $1,000,000,000 - 25,000,000 = d$

Type of Problem: Comparison Problem

Explanation: We are comparing two numbers to find the difference ($d$) between them using the formula $\text{Greater} - \text{Lesser} = \text{Difference}$ ($g - l = d$).

Answer: $975,000,000$

Lesson Practice b
How many years were there from 1215 to 1791?

Equation: $1791 - 1215 = d$

Explanation: This is an elapsed-time problem ($\text{Later time} - \text{Earlier time} = \text{Difference}$). Subtracting $1215$ from $1791$ yields $576$.

Answer: $576 \text{ years}$

Lesson Practice c
John F. Kennedy was elected president in 1960 at the age of 43. In what year was he born?

Equation: $1960 - e = 43$ (or $1960 - 43 = b$)

Explanation: To find the birth year, subtract his age ($43$) from the year he was elected ($1960$): $1960 - 43 = 1917$.

Answer: $1917$

Lesson Practice d (FORMULATE)
Write a word problem about comparing that fits this equation: $$58\text{ in.} - 55\text{ in.} = d$$

Sample Word Problem: Sarah is $58\text{ inches}$ tall and her younger brother is $55\text{ inches}$ tall. How many inches taller is Sarah than her brother?

Answer: $d = 3\text{ inches}$

Lesson Practice e (FORMULATE)
Write a word problem about elapsed time that fits this equation: $$2003 - b = 14$$

Sample Word Problem: An athlete won a championship in the year $2003$ at $14$ years of age. In what year was the athlete born?

Answer: $b = 1989$

Written Practice
1.
In 2003, the U.S. imported one million, eight hundred seventy thousand barrels of crude oil per day. In 1988, the U.S. imported only nine hundred eleven thousand barrels per day. How many fewer barrels per day did the U.S. import in 1988?

Equation: $1,870,000 - 911,000 = d$

Explanation: Subtract $911,000$ from $1,870,000$: $1,870,000 - 911,000 = 959,000$.

Answer: $959,000\text{ barrels per day}$

2.
West Street Middle School received 18 new computers for the media center. Now there are 31 computers in the media center. How many computers were there before they received the new computers?

Equation: $p + 18 = 31$ (or $31 - 18 = p$)

Explanation: Subtract the newly received computers ($18$) from the current total ($31$): $31 - 18 = 13$.

Answer: $13\text{ computers}$

3.
William and the Normans conquered England in 1066. The Magna Carta was signed by King John in 1215. How many years were there from 1066 to 1215?

Equation: $1215 - 1066 = d$

Explanation: Subtract the earlier year ($1066$) from the later year ($1215$): $1215 - 1066 = 149$.

Answer: $149\text{ years}$

4.
The Coliseum, built by the Romans in the first century A.D., could seat about 50,000 spectators. The Los Angeles Coliseum, built for the 1932 Olympics, could seat about 105,000 spectators at the time. How many fewer spectators could the Roman Coliseum seat?

Equation: $105,000 - 50,000 = d$

Explanation: Subtract $50,000$ from $105,000$: $105,000 - 50,000 = 55,000$.

Answer: $55,000\text{ spectators}$

5. (FORMULATE)
Write a word problem about separating that fits this equation: $$\$20.00 - c = \$7.13$$

Sample Word Problem: Maria went to the store with a $\$20.00$ bill. After buying a book, she had $\$7.13$ left. How much did the book cost?

Answer: $c = \$12.87$

6.
Which properties are illustrated by these equations?
  • a. $\frac{1}{2} \times 1 = \frac{1}{2}$
  • b. $\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{2}{1} = 1$

Explanation:

  • a: Multiplying any number by $1$ keeps its identity. This is the Identity Property of Multiplication.
  • b: Multiplying a number by its reciprocal yields $1$. This is the Inverse Property of Multiplication (or Multiplicative Inverse Property).

Answer: a. Identity Property of Multiplication | b. Inverse Property of Multiplication

7.
Twenty-three thousand is how much less than one million? Use words to write the answer.

Calculation: $1,000,000 - 23,000 = 977,000$.

Explanation: Subtract $23,000$ from $1,000,000$ to get $977,000$, then express the result in word form.

Answer: Nine hundred seventy-seven thousand

8.
Replace each circle with the proper comparison symbol:
  • a. $2 - 3 \bigcirc -1$
  • b. $\frac{1}{2} \bigcirc \frac{1}{3}$

Explanation:

  • a: $2 - 3 = -1$. Since $-1 = -1$, the symbol is $=$.
  • b: Converting to decimals or common denominators: $\frac{1}{2} = 0.5$ and $\frac{1}{3} \approx 0.333$. Since $0.5 > 0.333$, the symbol is $>$.

Answer: a. $=$ | b. $>$

9.
Name three segments in the figure below in order of length from shortest to longest (Points on line: $P, Q, R$ from left to right).

Explanation: Segment $PQ$ is the shortest section, segment $QR$ is the next longer section, and segment $PR$ spans the entire length.

Answer: $\overline{PQ}, \overline{QR}, \overline{PR}$ (or $PQ, QR, PR$)

10. (MODEL)
Draw and shade circles to show that $2\frac{3}{4}$ equals $\frac{11}{4}$.

Explanation: Draw 3 circles and divide each into 4 equal quarters (total of 12 quarters). Shade 2 full circles ($4 + 4 = 8$ quarters) and $3$ quarters of the 3rd circle. The total number of shaded quarters is $8 + 3 = 11$ quarters, or $\frac{11}{4}$.

Answer: 2 fully shaded circles + 1 circle with $\frac{3}{4}$ shaded $= \frac{11}{4}$

11.
  • a. What fraction of the triangle is shaded?
  • b. What percent of the triangle is not shaded?

Explanation: The large triangle is divided into $4$ equal smaller triangles.

  • a: $1$ out of $4$ triangles is shaded, so the fraction is $\frac{1}{4}$.
  • b: $3$ out of $4$ triangles are unshaded. Expressed as a percentage: $\frac{3}{4} = 75\%$.

Answer: a. $\frac{1}{4}$ | b. $75\%$

12.
The number 100 is divisible by which whole numbers?

Explanation: List all factors of $100$ ($1 \times 100$, $2 \times 50$, $4 \times 25$, $5 \times 20$, $10 \times 10$).

Answer: $1, 2, 4, 5, 10, 20, 25, 50, 100$

13.
Solve: $15x = 630$

Explanation: Divide both sides by $15$: $x = \frac{630}{15} = 42$.

Answer: $x = 42$

14.
Solve: $y - 2714 = 3601$

Explanation: Add $2714$ to both sides: $y = 3601 + 2714 = 6315$.

Answer: $y = 6315$

15.
Solve: $2900 - p = 64$

Explanation: Subtract $64$ from $2900$: $p = 2900 - 64 = 2836$.

Answer: $p = 2836$

16.
Solve: $\$1.53 + q = \$5.00$

Explanation: Subtract $\$1.53$ from $\$5.00$: $q = 5.00 - 1.53 = 3.47$.

Answer: $q = \$3.47$

17.
Solve: $20r = 1200$

Explanation: Divide both sides by $20$: $r = \frac{1200}{20} = 60$.

Answer: $r = 60$

18.
Solve: $\frac{m}{14} = 16$

Explanation: Multiply both sides by $14$: $m = 16 \times 14 = 224$.

Answer: $m = 224$

19.
Simplify: $72,112 - 64,309$

Explanation: Subtraction:

$$\begin{array}{r@{\quad}l} 72,112 \\ - 64,309 \\ \hline 7,803 \end{array}$$

Answer: $7,803$

20.
Simplify: $453,978 - 386,864$

Explanation: Subtraction:

$$\begin{array}{r@{\quad}l} 453,978 \\ - 386,864 \\ \hline 67,114 \end{array}$$

Answer: $67,114$

21.
Simplify: $\frac{8}{9} - \left(\frac{3}{9} + \frac{5}{9}\right)$

Explanation: Evaluate inside parentheses first: $\frac{3}{9} + \frac{5}{9} = \frac{8}{9}$. Then perform subtraction: $\frac{8}{9} - \frac{8}{9} = 0$.

Answer: $0$

22.
Simplify: $\left(\frac{8}{9} - \frac{3}{9}\right) + \frac{5}{9}$

Explanation: Evaluate inside parentheses first: $\frac{8}{9} - \frac{3}{9} = \frac{5}{9}$. Then perform addition: $\frac{5}{9} + \frac{5}{9} = \frac{10}{9} = 1\frac{1}{9}$.

Answer: $1\frac{1}{9}$ (or $\frac{10}{9}$)

23.
Simplify: $\frac{9}{2} \times \frac{3}{5}$

Explanation: Multiply numerators together and denominators together: $\frac{9 \times 3}{2 \times 5} = \frac{27}{10} = 2\frac{7}{10}$.

Answer: $2\frac{7}{10}$ (or $2.7$)

24.
Simplify: $\$37.20 \div 15$

Explanation: Divide $37.20$ by $15$: $37.20 \div 15 = 2.48$.

Answer: $\$2.48$

25.
Divide 42,847 by 9 and express the quotient as a mixed number.

Explanation: $42,847 \div 9 = 4,760$ with a remainder of $7$. Written as a mixed number, the remainder becomes the numerator over divisor $9$.

Answer: $4,760\frac{7}{9}$

26.
Justify the steps taken to simplify $25 \cdot 36 \cdot 4$:
  • Given: $25 \cdot 36 \cdot 4$
  • a. $25 \cdot 4 \cdot 36$
  • b. $(25 \cdot 4) \cdot 36$
  • c. $100 \cdot 36$
  • d. $3600$

Explanation:

  • a: Changing the order of factors uses the Commutative Property of Multiplication.
  • b: Grouping factors uses the Associative Property of Multiplication.
  • c: Multiplying inside parentheses ($25 \cdot 4 = 100$) simplifies the expression.
  • d: Multiplying $100 \cdot 36 = 3600$ completes the simplification.

Answer: a. Commutative Property of Multiplication | b. Associative Property of Multiplication | c. Simplify ($25 \cdot 4$) | d. Simplify ($100 \cdot 36$)

27. (GENERALIZE)
Find the next three numbers in this sequence: $$\frac{1}{4}, \frac{1}{2}, \frac{3}{4}, \dots$$

Explanation: The sequence increases by $\frac{1}{4}$ each step ($\frac{1}{4}, \frac{2}{4}, \frac{3}{4}, \dots$). The next three terms are $\frac{4}{4} = 1$, $\frac{5}{4} = 1\frac{1}{4}$, and $\frac{6}{4} = 1\frac{1}{2}$.

Answer: $1, 1\frac{1}{4}, 1\frac{1}{2}$

28.
How many $\frac{2}{3}$s are in 1?

Explanation: Divide $1$ by $\frac{2}{3}$: $1 \div \frac{2}{3} = 1 \times \frac{3}{2} = \frac{3}{2} = 1\frac{1}{2}$.

Answer: $1\frac{1}{2}$ (or $\frac{3}{2}$)

29.
Write $1\frac{2}{3}$ as an improper fraction, and multiply the improper fraction by $\frac{1}{2}$. What is the product?

Explanation:

  1. Convert to improper fraction: $1\frac{2}{3} = \frac{3 \times 1 + 2}{3} = \frac{5}{3}$.
  2. Multiply by $\frac{1}{2}$: $\frac{5}{3} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{5 \times 1}{3 \times 2} = \frac{5}{6}$.

Answer: $\frac{5}{6}$

30.
Using a ruler, draw a triangle that has two perpendicular sides, one that is $\frac{3}{4}\text{ in.}$ long and one that is $1\text{ in.}$ long. What is the measure of the third side?

Explanation: Perpendicular sides form a right triangle. Using the Pythagorean theorem $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$:

$$c = \sqrt{\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{\frac{9}{16} + 1} = \sqrt{\frac{25}{16}} = \frac{5}{4} = 1\frac{1}{4}\text{ in.}$$

Answer: $1\frac{1}{4}\text{ in.}$ (or $1.25\text{ in.}$)

Real-World Connection
Real-World Connection
Patricia's grandmother was born in 1948. In 2004, her grandmother's age in years equaled her cat's age in months. What was the cat's age in years and months? Explain your thinking.

Explanation:

  1. Find grandmother's age in 2004: $2004 - 1948 = 56\text{ years old}$.
  2. Since the cat's age in months equals grandmother's age in years, the cat is $56\text{ months}$ old.
  3. Convert $56\text{ months}$ to years and months by dividing by $12$: $56 \div 12 = 4$ with a remainder of $8$.

Answer: $4\text{ years and } 8\text{ months}$
