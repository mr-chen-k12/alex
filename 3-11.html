Lesson Practice (Page 481)

a. Write $2,500,000$ in expanded notation using exponents.
Answer: $(2 \times 10^6) + (5 \times 10^5)$

Explanation: In $2,500,000$, the $2$ is in the millions place ($10^6$) and the $5$ is in the hundred-thousands place ($10^5$). We multiply each digit by its corresponding power of 10.
b. Write this number in standard notation: $(5 \times 10^9) + (2 \times 10^8)$
Answer: $5,200,000,000$

Explanation: $5 \times 10^9$ is 5 billion ($5,000,000,000$). $2 \times 10^8$ is 200 million ($200,000,000$). Adding them together gives $5,200,000,000$.
c. Simplify: $10 + 2^3 \times 3 - (7 + 2) \div \sqrt{9}$
Answer: $31$

Explanation: Use the order of operations (PEMDAS):
1. Parentheses: $(7 + 2) = 9$
2. Exponents/Roots: $2^3 = 8$ and $\sqrt{9} = 3$
Equation is now: $10 + 8 \times 3 - 9 \div 3$
3. Multiply/Divide: $8 \times 3 = 24$ and $9 \div 3 = 3$
Equation is now: $10 + 24 - 3$
4. Add/Subtract: $34 - 3 = 31$
d. Simplify: $(\frac{1}{2})^3$
Answer: $\frac{1}{8}$

Explanation: $(\frac{1}{2}) \times (\frac{1}{2}) \times (\frac{1}{2}) = \frac{1 \times 1 \times 1}{2 \times 2 \times 2} = \frac{1}{8}$
e. Simplify: $(0.1)^2$
Answer: $0.01$

Explanation: $0.1 \times 0.1$. $1 \times 1 = 1$, and there are two decimal places in total, so $0.01$.
f. Simplify: $(1 \frac{1}{2})^2$
Answer: $2 \frac{1}{4}$ (or $\frac{9}{4}$)

Explanation: First, convert the mixed number to an improper fraction: $1 \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{2}$. Then square it: $(\frac{3}{2})^2 = \frac{3^2}{2^2} = \frac{9}{4}$. Convert back to a mixed number: $2 \frac{1}{4}$.
g. Simplify: $(2 + 3)^2 - (2^2 + 3^2)$
Answer: $12$

Explanation: Evaluate parentheses first.
First term: $(2 + 3)^2 = 5^2 = 25$
Second term: $(2^2 + 3^2) = (4 + 9) = 13$
Subtract: $25 - 13 = 12$

Written Practice

1. The weather forecast stated that the chance of rain for Wednesday is 40%. Does this forecast mean that it is more likely to rain or not to rain? Why?
Answer: It is more likely not to rain.

Explanation: The total probability is 100%. If there is a 40% chance of rain, there is a $100\% - 40\% = 60\%$ chance of no rain. Since 60% > 40%, no rain is more likely.
2. A set of 36 shape cards contains an equal number of hexagons, squares, circles, and triangles. What is the probability of drawing a square from this set of cards? Express the probability ratio as a fraction and as a decimal.
Answer: Fraction: $\frac{1}{4}$, Decimal: $0.25$

Explanation: There are 4 types of shapes, equally distributed among 36 cards. $36 \div 4 = 9$ squares. Probability is $\frac{\text{target outcomes}}{\text{total outcomes}} = \frac{9}{36}$. Reducing the fraction gives $\frac{1}{4}$. As a decimal, $1 \div 4 = 0.25$.
3. If the sum of three numbers is 144, what is the average of the three numbers?
Answer: $48$

Explanation: Average is calculated by dividing the sum of the numbers by the quantity of numbers. Average $= 144 \div 3 = 48$.
4. "All quadrilaterals are polygons." True or false?
Answer: True.

Explanation: A polygon is a closed, two-dimensional shape formed by straight line segments. A quadrilateral is specifically a polygon with four sides. Therefore, every quadrilateral fits the definition of a polygon.
5. Simplify: $\sqrt{441}$
Answer: $21$

Explanation: We are looking for a number that, when multiplied by itself, equals 441. We know $20 \times 20 = 400$, so it must be slightly larger. The last digit is 1, which means the root must end in 1 or 9. $21 \times 21 = 441$.
6. Simplify: $2 \cdot 3^2 - \sqrt{9} + (3 - 1)^3$
Answer: $23$

Explanation: Apply PEMDAS:
Parentheses: $(3 - 1) = 2$. Equation becomes: $2 \cdot 3^2 - \sqrt{9} + 2^3$
Exponents/Roots: $3^2 = 9$, $\sqrt{9} = 3$, $2^3 = 8$. Equation becomes: $2 \cdot 9 - 3 + 8$
Multiplication: $2 \cdot 9 = 18$. Equation becomes: $18 - 3 + 8$
Add/Subtract (left to right): $18 - 3 = 15$, then $15 + 8 = 23$.
7. Write the formula for the perimeter of a rectangle. Then substitute 12 in. for the length and 6 in. for the width. Solve the equation to find the perimeter of the rectangle.
Answer: $36\text{ in.}$

Explanation:
Formula: $P = 2l + 2w$
Substitute: $P = 2(12) + 2(6)$
Solve: $P = 24 + 12 = 36\text{ in.}$
8. Arrange these numbers in order from least to greatest: $1, 0, 0.1, -1$
Answer: $-1, 0, 0.1, 1$

Explanation: Negative numbers are the smallest, so -1 is first. Next is 0. Between 0.1 and 1, 0.1 is a fraction ($\frac{1}{10}$), making it smaller than the whole number 1.
9. If $\frac{5}{6}$ of the 30 members were present, how many members were absent?
Answer: $5$ members were absent.

Explanation: If $\frac{5}{6}$ were present, then $\frac{1}{6}$ were absent ($1 - \frac{5}{6} = \frac{1}{6}$). To find the number of absent members, find $\frac{1}{6}$ of 30: $30 \div 6 = 5$.
10. Reduce before multiplying or dividing: $\frac{(24)(36)}{48}$
Answer: $18$

Explanation: Notice that 24 and 48 share a common factor. 24 goes into 48 exactly twice. Cross out 24 (becomes 1) and 48 (becomes 2). The equation is now $\frac{(1)(36)}{2}$, which simplifies to $36 \div 2 = 18$.
11. Simplify: $\frac{\sqrt{100}}{\sqrt{25}}$
Answer: $2$

Explanation: Evaluate the square roots first. $\sqrt{100} = 10$ and $\sqrt{25} = 5$. The fraction becomes $\frac{10}{5}$, which equals $2$.
12. Simplify: $12 \frac{5}{6} + 15 \frac{1}{3}$
Answer: $28 \frac{1}{6}$

Explanation: Find a common denominator for the fractions, which is 6. Convert $\frac{1}{3}$ to $\frac{2}{6}$.
Add the fractions: $\frac{5}{6} + \frac{2}{6} = \frac{7}{6}$.
Add the whole numbers: $12 + 15 = 27$.
Combine: $27 \frac{7}{6}$. Since $\frac{7}{6} = 1 \frac{1}{6}$, add the 1 to 27 to get $28 \frac{1}{6}$.
13. Simplify: $100 - 9.9$
Answer: $90.1$

Explanation: Align the decimal points: $100.0 - 9.9$. Borrowing carefully gives $90.1$.
14. Simplify: $\frac{4}{7} \times 100$
Answer: $57 \frac{1}{7}$ (or $\frac{400}{7}$)

Explanation: Multiply the numerator by 100: $\frac{4 \times 100}{7} = \frac{400}{7}$. To convert to a mixed number, perform division: $400 \div 7 = 57$ with a remainder of 1.
15. Solve for $w$: $\frac{5}{8} = \frac{w}{48}$
Answer: $w = 30$

Explanation: Use equivalent fractions. How do you get from 8 to 48? Multiply by 6. Do the same to the numerator: $5 \times 6 = 30$. So, $w = 30$.
16. Simplify: $0.25 \times \$4.60$
Answer: $\$1.15$

Explanation: $0.25$ is the same as $\frac{1}{4}$. Divide $\$4.60$ by 4. $4.60 \div 4 = 1.15$.
17. ESTIMATE: The diameter of a circular saucepan is 6 inches. What is the area of the circular base of the pan? Round the answer to the nearest square inch. (Use 3.14 for $\pi$.)
Answer: $28\text{ sq in.}$

Explanation: Area of a circle is $A = \pi r^2$. The radius $r$ is half the diameter, so $r = 3$.
$A = 3.14 \times 3^2 = 3.14 \times 9 = 28.26$. Rounding to the nearest square inch gives $28\text{ sq in.}$
18. Write $3 \frac{3}{4}$ as a decimal number and subtract that number from 7.4.
Answer: $3.65$

Explanation: $\frac{3}{4}$ is $0.75$, so the decimal is $3.75$. Subtract from 7.4: $7.40 - 3.75 = 3.65$.
19. What percent of the first ten letters of the alphabet are vowels?
Answer: $30\%$

Explanation: The first ten letters are a, b, c, d, e, f, g, h, i, j. The vowels in this group are a, e, i. That is 3 out of 10 letters. As a percent, $\frac{3}{10} = 30\%$.
20. Bobby rode his bike north. At Grand Avenue he turned left 90°. When he reached Arden Road, he turned left 90°. In what direction was Bobby riding on Arden Road?
Answer: South

Explanation: Facing North, a 90° left turn puts you facing West. Facing West, another 90° left turn puts you facing South.
21. ESTIMATE: Find the product of 6.95 and 12.1 to the nearest whole number.
Answer: $84$

Explanation: Round the numbers to the nearest whole number before multiplying. 6.95 rounds to 7. 12.1 rounds to 12. $7 \times 12 = 84$.
22. Write and solve a proportion for this statement: 16 is to 10 as what number is to 25?
Answer: $x = 40$

Explanation: Write the proportion: $\frac{16}{10} = \frac{x}{25}$. Multiply both sides by 25 to isolate $x$: $x = 25 \times (\frac{16}{10})$. Simplify: $x = 25 \times 1.6 = 40$.
23. What is the area of the triangle below? (Base = 8 cm, Height = 6 cm, hypotenuse side = 11 cm)
Answer: $24\text{ cm}^2$

Explanation: The formula for the area of a triangle is $A = \frac{1}{2}bh$. The base ($b$) is $8\text{ cm}$ and the height ($h$, indicated by the dashed line meeting the base at a right angle) is $6\text{ cm}$. The $11\text{ cm}$ side is extra information not needed for area. $A = \frac{1}{2}(8)(6) = \frac{1}{2}(48) = 24\text{ cm}^2$.
24. This figure is a rectangular prism. a. How many faces does it have? b. How many edges does it have?
Answer: a. 6 faces, b. 12 edges

Explanation: A standard rectangular prism (like a standard box) always has 6 flat faces (top, bottom, front, back, left, right) and 12 straight edges where those faces meet.
25. Each term in this sequence is $\frac{1}{16}$ more than the previous term. What are the next four terms in the sequence?
$\frac{1}{16}, \frac{1}{8}, \frac{3}{16}, \frac{1}{4}, \dots$
Answer: $\frac{5}{16}, \frac{3}{8}, \frac{7}{16}, \frac{1}{2}$

Explanation: Write all terms with a common denominator of 16: $\frac{1}{16}, \frac{2}{16}, \frac{3}{16}, \frac{4}{16}$. The next four terms are $\frac{5}{16}, \frac{6}{16}, \frac{7}{16}, \frac{8}{16}$. Simplify the fractions: $\frac{6}{16} = \frac{3}{8}$ and $\frac{8}{16} = \frac{1}{2}$.
26. What is the perimeter of the rectangle? (Note: The textbook instructs to use a ruler to measure the rectangle to the nearest quarter of an inch. Based on the handwritten notes, we assume dimensions of $\frac{3}{4}\text{ in.}$ by $1\text{ in.}$ for this solution.)
Answer: $3 \frac{1}{2}\text{ in.}$

Explanation: Assuming length $L = 1\text{ in.}$ and width $W = \frac{3}{4}\text{ in.}$
Formula: $P = 2L + 2W$
$P = 2(1) + 2(\frac{3}{4}) = 2 + \frac{6}{4} = 2 + 1 \frac{1}{2} = 3 \frac{1}{2}\text{ in.}$
27. What is the area of the rectangle? (Using the assumed dimensions from problem 26)
Answer: $\frac{3}{4}\text{ sq in.}$

Explanation: Assuming length $L = 1\text{ in.}$ and width $W = \frac{3}{4}\text{ in.}$
Formula: $A = L \times W$
$A = 1 \times \frac{3}{4} = \frac{3}{4}\text{ sq in.}$
28. The coordinates of the vertices of a parallelogram are $(4, 3)$, $(-2, 3)$, $(0, -2)$, and $(-6, -2)$. What is the area of the parallelogram?
Answer: $30\text{ square units}$

Explanation: Area of a parallelogram is base $\times$ height.
The top base is the horizontal line between $(4, 3)$ and $(-2, 3)$. The distance is $4 - (-2) = 6$ units.
The height is the vertical distance between the y-coordinates of the top and bottom bases. Top is at $y=3$, bottom is at $y=-2$. Distance is $3 - (-2) = 5$ units.
Area $= 6 \times 5 = 30\text{ square units}$.
29. Simplify:
a. $(12\text{ cm})(8\text{ cm})$
b. $\frac{36\text{ ft}^2}{4\text{ ft}}$
Answer: a. $96\text{ cm}^2$, b. $9\text{ ft}$

Explanation:
a. Multiply the numbers and the units: $12 \times 8 = 96$, and $\text{cm} \times \text{cm} = \text{cm}^2$.
b. Divide the numbers and the units: $36 \div 4 = 9$, and $\text{ft}^2 \div \text{ft} = \text{ft}$.
30. Fernando poured water from one-pint bottles into a three-gallon bucket. How many pints of water could the bucket hold?
Answer: $24\text{ pints}$

Explanation: First, recall the conversion: $1\text{ gallon} = 8\text{ pints}$. If the bucket holds 3 gallons, multiply by 8 to find the total pints: $3 \times 8 = 24\text{ pints}$.

Real-World Connection

There are close to 4 million people living on the island of Puerto Rico... If the population density is approximately 1,000 people per square mile, how many people live in a 3.5 square mile area? Write and solve a proportion to answer this question.
Answer: $3,500\text{ people}$

Explanation: Set up a proportion comparing people to square miles:
$\frac{1000\text{ people}}{1\text{ sq mile}} = \frac{x\text{ people}}{3.5\text{ sq miles}}$
Cross-multiply to solve for x: $x = 1000 \times 3.5 = 3,500$.
