Written Practice

1

Willie shot eighteen rolls of film for the school annual. If there were thirty-six exposures in each roll, how many exposures were there in all?

Answer: 648 exposures

Solution: \(18 \times 36 = 648\). (Check: \(18 \times 36 = 18(30 + 6) = 540 + 108 = 648\).)

2

Carpeting is usually sold by the square yard. The number of square feet of area a carpet will cover is a function of the number of square yards installed. Create a function table that shows the number of square feet in 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 square yards.

Answer:

Square yards12345
Square feet918273645

Solution: Since \(1\text{ yd}^2 = 9\text{ ft}^2\), multiply the number of square yards by 9: \(1 \times 9 = 9\), \(2 \times 9 = 18\), \(3 \times 9 = 27\), \(4 \times 9 = 36\), \(5 \times 9 = 45\).

3

a. Write the formula for the area of a rectangle.

b. A professional basketball court is a rectangle that is 50 feet wide and 94 feet long. What is the area of the basketball court?

Answer: a. \(A = l \times w\)   b. 4700 ft²

Solution: a. Area of a rectangle = length × width. b. \(A = 94 \times 50 = 4700\) square feet.

4

The 16-pound turkey cost $14.24. What was the price per pound?

Answer: $0.89 per pound

Solution: Divide the total cost by the number of pounds: \(\$14.24 \div 16 = \$0.89\). (Check: \(16 \times 0.89 = 14.24\) ✓)

5 Model

Draw a diagram of the statement. Then answer the questions that follow.

Three eighths of the 56 restaurants in town were closed on Monday.

a. How many of the restaurants in town were closed on Monday?

b. How many of the restaurants in town were open on Monday?

Answer: a. 21 closed   b. 35 open

Solution: a. \(\frac{3}{8} \times 56 = \frac{168}{8} = 21\). b. \(56 - 21 = 35\).

6 Evaluate

In the following statement, write the percent as a reduced fraction. Then draw a diagram of the statement and answer the questions.

Forty percent of the 30 students in the class were boys.

a. How many boys were in the class?

b. How many girls were in the class?

Answer: 40% = \(\frac{2}{5}\);   a. 12 boys   b. 18 girls

Solution: \(40\% = \frac{40}{100} = \frac{2}{5}\). a. \(\frac{2}{5} \times 30 = 12\) boys. b. \(30 - 12 = 18\) girls.

7

After contact was made, the spheroid sailed four thousand, one hundred forty inches. How many yards did the spheroid sail after contact was made?

Answer: 115 yards

Solution: There are 36 inches in a yard, so divide: \(4140 \div 36 = 115\). (A spheroid is a ball-shaped object.)

8

a. How many \(\frac{1}{5}\)s are in 1?

b. How many \(\frac{1}{5}\)s are in 3?

c. (Explain) How can you use the answer to a to solve b?

Answer: a. 5   b. 15   c. Multiply the answer to a by 3.

Solution: a. \(1 \div \frac{1}{5} = 5\). b. \(3 \div \frac{1}{5} = 15\). c. Three wholes contain three times as many fifths as one whole, so multiply 5 by 3 to get 15.

9 Explain

Describe how to find the reciprocal of a mixed number.

Answer: First write the mixed number as an improper fraction, then invert the fraction (swap the numerator and denominator).

Solution: Example: the reciprocal of \(2\frac{1}{3}\) is found by writing \(2\frac{1}{3} = \frac{7}{3}\), then flipping it to get \(\frac{3}{7}\).

10

Replace each circle with the proper comparison symbol:

a. \(\frac{2}{3} \cdot \frac{3}{2} \bigcirc \frac{5}{5}\)      b. \(\frac{12}{36} \bigcirc \frac{12}{24}\)

Answer: a. \(=\)   b. \(<\)

Solution: a. \(\frac{2}{3} \cdot \frac{3}{2} = \frac{6}{6} = 1\) and \(\frac{5}{5} = 1\), so \(=\). b. \(\frac{12}{36} = \frac{1}{3}\) and \(\frac{12}{24} = \frac{1}{2}\); since \(\frac{1}{3} < \frac{1}{2}\), the symbol is \(<\).

11

Write \(2\frac{1}{4}\) and \(3\frac{1}{3}\) as improper fractions. Then multiply the improper fractions, and write the product as a reduced mixed number.

Answer: \(7\frac{1}{2}\)

Solution: \(2\frac{1}{4} = \frac{9}{4}\) and \(3\frac{1}{3} = \frac{10}{3}\). Then \(\frac{9}{4} \cdot \frac{10}{3} = \frac{90}{12} = \frac{15}{2} = 7\frac{1}{2}\).

12

Complete each equivalent fraction:

a. \(\frac{3}{4} = \frac{?}{40}\)    b. \(\frac{2}{5} = \frac{?}{40}\)    c. \(\frac{?}{8} = \frac{15}{40}\)

Answer: a. 30   b. 16   c. 3

Solution: a. \(40 \div 4 = 10\), so \(3 \times 10 = 30\). b. \(40 \div 5 = 8\), so \(2 \times 8 = 16\). c. \(40 \div 8 = 5\), so \(15 \div 5 = 3\).

13 Generalize

The prime factorization of 100 is \(2 \cdot 2 \cdot 5 \cdot 5\). We can write the prime factorization of 100 using exponents this way: \(2^2 \cdot 5^2\).

a. Write the prime factorization of 400 using exponents.

b. Write the prime factorization of \(\sqrt{400}\) using exponents.

Answer: a. \(2^4 \cdot 5^2\)   b. \(2^2 \cdot 5\)

Solution: a. \(400 = 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 5 \cdot 5 = 2^4 \cdot 5^2\). b. \(\sqrt{400} = 20 = 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 5 = 2^2 \cdot 5\).

14

Refer to this figure to answer a–d:

a. What type of angle is \(\angle ADB\)?

b. What type of angle is \(\angle BDC\)?

c. What type of angle is \(\angle ADC\)?

d. Which ray is perpendicular to \(\overrightarrow{DB}\)?

Answer: a. acute   b. right   c. obtuse   d. \(\overrightarrow{DC}\)

Solution: The square mark shows \(\angle BDC = 90°\), so it is a right angle, and \(\overrightarrow{DC} \perp \overrightarrow{DB}\). \(\angle ADB\) is less than 90° (acute). \(\angle ADC = \angle ADB + \angle BDC\), which is between 90° and 180° (obtuse).

15

Find fractions equivalent to \(\frac{3}{4}\) and \(\frac{2}{3}\) with denominators of 12. Then subtract the smaller fraction from the larger fraction.

Answer: \(\frac{1}{12}\)

Solution: \(\frac{3}{4} = \frac{9}{12}\) and \(\frac{2}{3} = \frac{8}{12}\). Then \(\frac{9}{12} - \frac{8}{12} = \frac{1}{12}\).

18

Answer: \(x = 5\frac{1}{2}\)

Solution: \(x = 6\frac{3}{4} - 1\frac{1}{4} = 5\frac{2}{4} = 5\frac{1}{2}\).

20 Analyze

There were five yards of fabric on the bolt of cloth. Fairchild bought \(3\frac{1}{3}\) yards of the fabric. Then how many yards of fabric remained on the bolt?

Answer: \(1\frac{2}{3}\) yards

Solution: \(5 - 3\frac{1}{3} = 4\frac{3}{3} - 3\frac{1}{3} = 1\frac{2}{3}\).

21

Simplify: \(83\frac{1}{3}\% - 66\frac{2}{3}\%\)

Answer: \(16\frac{2}{3}\%\)

Solution: Subtract the whole numbers and the fractions separately: \(83 - 66 = 17\) and \(\frac{1}{3} - \frac{2}{3} = -\frac{1}{3}\), so \(17 - \frac{1}{3} = 16\frac{2}{3}\). Answer: \(16\frac{2}{3}\%\).

22

Simplify: \(\frac{7}{12} + \left(\frac{1}{4} \cdot \frac{1}{3}\right)\)

Answer: \(\frac{2}{3}\)

Solution: First multiply inside the parentheses: \(\frac{1}{4} \cdot \frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{12}\). Then \(\frac{7}{12} + \frac{1}{12} = \frac{8}{12} = \frac{2}{3}\).

23

Simplify: \(\frac{7}{8} - \left(\frac{3}{4} \cdot \frac{1}{2}\right)\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{2}\)

Solution: First multiply inside the parentheses: \(\frac{3}{4} \cdot \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{8}\). Then \(\frac{7}{8} - \frac{3}{8} = \frac{4}{8} = \frac{1}{2}\).

24

Draw \(\overline{AB}\) \(1\frac{3}{4}\) inches long. Then draw \(\overline{BC}\) 1 inch long perpendicular to \(\overline{AB}\). Complete the triangle by drawing \(\overline{AC}\). Use a ruler to find the approximate length of \(\overline{AC}\). Use that length to find the perimeter of \(\triangle ABC\).

Answer: \(AC \approx 2\) in.; perimeter \(\approx 4\frac{3}{4}\) in.

Solution: Measuring the hypotenuse gives about 2 inches (Pythagorean check: \(\sqrt{1.75^2 + 1^2} \approx 2.02\)). Perimeter \(= 1\frac{3}{4} + 1 + 2 = 4\frac{3}{4}\) inches. (Measurements may vary slightly with your drawing.)

26 Evaluate

Mary wants to apply a strip of wallpaper along the walls of the dining room just below the ceiling. If the room is a 14-by-12-ft rectangle, then the strip of wallpaper needs to be at least how long?

Answer: At least 52 feet

Solution: The strip runs around all four walls, so find the perimeter: \(2(14 + 12) = 2(26) = 52\) feet.

27

Multiply \(\frac{3}{4}\) by the reciprocal of 3 and reduce the product.

Answer: \(\frac{1}{4}\)

Solution: The reciprocal of 3 is \(\frac{1}{3}\). Then \(\frac{3}{4} \cdot \frac{1}{3} = \frac{3}{12} = \frac{1}{4}\).

A sequence of perfect cubes \((k = n^3)\) may be written as in a or as in b. Find the next two terms of both sequences.

Real-World Connection

Real-World Connection

Zachary surveyed the 30 students in his class to find out how they get home. He found that 60% of the students ride the bus.

a. How many students ride the bus?

b. Half the students who do not ride the bus walk home. How many students walk home?

c. Based on his survey, what fraction of the students in the school might Zachary conclude walk to school?

Answer: a. 18 students   b. 6 students   c. \(\frac{1}{5}\)

Solution: a. \(60\% \times 30 = 0.6 \times 30 = 18\). b. \(30 - 18 = 12\) do not ride the bus; half of 12 is 6. c. \(\frac{6}{30} = \frac{1}{5}\) of the class walks, so Zachary might conclude about \(\frac{1}{5}\) of the school walks.
