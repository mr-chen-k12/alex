1.(28)
GENERALIZE In 3 boxes of cereal, Jeff counted 188 raisins, 212 raisins, and 203 raisins. What was the average number of raisins in each box of cereal?
Answer
\(201\text{ raisins}\)
Solution
Add the three counts: \(188 + 212 + 203 = 603\). Divide by 3 boxes: \(603 \div 3 = 201\) raisins per box on average.
2.(11)
On April 29, 2005, the tree pollen count in Waterbury, CT was 1024 parts per cubic meter. On May 2, the count was 1698 parts per cubic meter. By how much did the pollen count increase?
Answer
\(674\text{ parts per cubic meter}\)
Solution
\(1698 - 1024 = 674\) parts per cubic meter.
3.(11)
Gina spent $3.95 for lunch. She had $12.55 left. How much money did she have before she bought lunch?
Answer
\(\$16.50\)
Solution
Add what she spent to what she has left: \(\$3.95 + \$12.55 = \$16.50\).
4.(12)
CONNECT In 1903 the Wright brothers made the first powered airplane flight. Just 66 years later astronauts first landed on the Moon. In what year did astronauts first land on the Moon?
Answer
\(1969\)
Solution
\(1903 + 66 = 1969\).
5.(19)
The perimeter of the square equals the perimeter of the regular hexagon. If each side of the hexagon is 6 inches long, how long is each side of the square?
Answer
\(9\text{ inches}\)
Solution
The regular hexagon has 6 equal sides, so its perimeter is \(6 \times 6 = 36\) inches. The square has the same perimeter, 36 inches, shared by 4 equal sides: \(36 \div 4 = 9\) inches per side.
6.(22)
In the following statement, write the percent as a reduced fraction. Then diagram the statement and answer the questions.
Each week Jessica earns $12 dollars doing yard work. She saves 40% of the money she makes.
a. How much money does she save each week?
b. How much money does she spend each week?
Answer
\(40\% = \frac{2}{5} \qquad \text{a. }\$4.80 \qquad \text{b. }\$7.20\)
Solution
\(40\% = \frac{40}{100} = \frac{2}{5}\). a. She saves \(\frac{2}{5}\) of $12: \(\frac{2}{5} \times 12 = \frac{24}{5} = \$4.80\). b. She spends the rest: \(\$12.00 - \$4.80 = \$7.20\).
7.(29)
EXPLAIN Tom's 27-inch television screen has the dimensions shown. Describe how to estimate its area.
Answer
\(\text{About }352\text{ square inches}\)
Solution
Round each side to the nearest whole inch: \(21\frac{5}{8}\) in. rounds to 22 in. and \(16\frac{1}{8}\) in. rounds to 16 in. Then multiply: \(22 \times 16 = 352\) square inches. (The 27 in. is the diagonal — it is not needed for the area. The exact area is about 349 square inches, so 352 is a good estimate.)

13.(34)
What decimal number names the point marked \(X\) on this number line?
Answer
\(8.25\)
Solution
The labeled ticks are 8.2 and 8.3, with 10 equal spaces between them, so each small space is 0.01. The point X sits on the longer middle tick, 5 spaces past 8.2: \(8.2 + 5 \times 0.01 = 8.25\). (Verified by pixel measurement: the blue dot lines up exactly with that middle tick.)
14.(Inv. 3)
The coordinates of three vertices of a square are (0, 0), (0, 3), and (3, 3).
a. REPRESENT Draw a coordinate plane and plot the points for the coordinates given. Then draw the square.
b. What are the coordinates of the fourth vertex?
c. What is the area of the square?
Answer
\(\text{b. }(3, 0) \qquad \text{c. }9\text{ square units}\)
Solution
a. Plotting (0, 0), (0, 3), and (3, 3) gives three corners of a square; joining them and closing the figure completes it (see the figure). b. The missing corner is directly below (3, 3) and directly right of (0, 0): (3, 0). c. Each side is 3 units, so the area is \(3 \times 3 = 9\) square units.
15.(34)
a. What decimal number is halfway between 7 and 8?
b. What number is halfway between 0.7 and 0.8?
Answer
\(\text{a. }7.5 \qquad \text{b. }0.75\)
Solution
a. Halfway between 7 and 8 is \((7 + 8) \div 2 = 7.5\). b. Halfway between 0.7 and 0.8 is \((0.7 + 0.8) \div 2 = 0.75\).

26.(26)
GENERALIZE Express the following difference two ways: \(3.6\text{ cm} - 24\text{ mm}\)
a. in centimeters
b. in millimeters
Answer
\(\text{a. }1.2\text{ cm} \qquad \text{b. }12\text{ mm}\)

27.(20)
Which is equivalent to \(2^2 \cdot 2^3\)?
A \(2^5\)   B \(2^6\)   C 12   D 24
Answer
\(\text{A}\)
Solution
When multiplying powers with the same base, add the exponents: \(2^2 \cdot 2^3 = 2^{2+3} = 2^5\). (Check: \(4 \times 8 = 32 = 2^5\), while \(2^6 = 64\).)

30.(14)
A bag contains 3 red marbles, 4 white marbles, and 5 blue marbles. If one marble is drawn from the bag, what is the probability that the marble will be
a. red?   b. white?
c. blue?   d. green?
Answer
\(\text{a. }\frac{1}{4} \qquad \text{b. }\frac{1}{3} \qquad \text{c. }\frac{5}{12} \qquad \text{d. }0\)
Solution
There are \(3 + 4 + 5 = 12\) marbles in all. a. \(\frac{3}{12} = \frac{1}{4}\). b. \(\frac{4}{12} = \frac{1}{3}\). c. \(\frac{5}{12}\). d. There are no green marbles, so \(\frac{0}{12} = 0\) — it cannot happen.

REAL-WORLD CONNECTION James's coach asked him to measure ten practice long jumps and to plot the measurements on a number line. The measurements were: 5.15 m, 5.13 m, 5.17 m, 4.99 m, 5.02 m, 5.05 m, 5.22 m, 5.09 m, 5.2 m, and 5.11 m.
a. Plot James's measurements on a number line.
b. What are some observations James can make regarding his data now that he has plotted them on a number line?
Answer
\(\text{See the dot plot and observations below}\)
Solution
a. The dot plot shows all ten jumps (each dot is one jump) between 4.99 m and 5.22 m.
b. Sample observations: almost all of his jumps cluster between 5.02 m and 5.17 m — only one jump (4.99 m) fell below 5 meters, and only one (5.22 m) went past 5.2 m. His best jump is 5.22 m and his shortest is 4.99 m, so the range is \(5.22 - 4.99 = 0.23\) m. The middle of the data is about 5.12 m (median), and the average is about 5.11 m — so James is a very consistent jumper, usually landing just past 5.1 m.
