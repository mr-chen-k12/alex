Part I: Formulate & Generalize Problems

Formulate Problem

Multi-Step Evaluation

Evaluate the following multi-step numerical expression containing fractions, decimals, exponents, and division:

\[ \frac{3}{4} \times \left( 5.2 - 1.8 \right) + 2.5^2 \div 0.5 \]

Step-by-Step Explanation:

  1. Parentheses: \( 5.2 - 1.8 = 3.4 \).
  2. Exponents: \( 2.5^2 = 6.25 \).
  3. Multiply & Divide: \( \frac{3}{4} \times 3.4 = 2.55 \), and \( 6.25 \div 0.5 = 12.5 \).
  4. Addition: \( 2.55 + 12.5 = 15.05 \).
Final Answer: \( 15.05 \) or \( \frac{301}{20} \)
Generalize Problems

Generalization Statements (a–d)

(a) State the general expression for scaling linear function \( f(x) = ax + b \) by constant \( k \).

(b) Express repeating decimal \( 0.a\bar{b} \) in algebraic fraction form.

(c) Write formula for price \( P \) with discount \( d\% \) followed by tax \( t\% \).

(d) Differentiate rules for \( (-a)^n \) versus \( -a^n \) based on parity of \( n \).

(a) \( k \cdot f(x) = k(ax + b) = kax + kb \)

(b) \( 0.a\bar{b} = \frac{10a + b - a}{90} \)

(c) \( \text{Final Price} = P(1 - d)(1 + t) \)

(d) \( (-a)^n = a^n \) if \( n \) is even; \( (-a)^n = -a^n \) if \( n \) is odd. \( -a^n \) is always negative for positive \( a \).

Part IV: Key Advanced Problems 31 through 34

Problem 31

Decimal Division (Terminal Quotient)

Perform long division with decimals to evaluate the quotient:

\[ 18.45 \div 4.5 \]

Step-by-Step Explanation:

  1. Shift decimal points 1 spot to the right: \( \frac{18.45 \times 10}{4.5 \times 10} = \frac{184.5}{45} \).
  2. Divide whole number: \( 184 \div 45 = 4 \) with remainder \( 4 \).
  3. Bring down decimal 5: \( 4.5 \div 45 = 0.1 \).
Final Quotient: \( 4.1 \) (Terminal Decimal)
Problem 32

Decimal Division (Repeating Quotient)

Evaluate division and express the answer as both an exact fraction and a repeating decimal:

\[ 4.2 \div 0.9 \]

Step-by-Step Explanation:

  1. Multiply numerator & denominator by 10: \( \frac{4.2 \times 10}{0.9 \times 10} = \frac{42}{9} \).
  2. Reduce fraction by GCD of 3: \( \frac{42 \div 3}{9 \div 3} = \frac{14}{3} \).
  3. Convert to repeating decimal: \( \frac{14}{3} = 4\frac{2}{3} = 4.6666... = 4.\bar{6} \).
Final Answer: \( \frac{14}{3} \) or \( 4.\bar{6} \)
Problem 33

Percentage Word Problem (Discount & Sales Tax)

A high-end electronics item originally priced at $850.00 is marked down by 20% during a seasonal store promotion. If a local sales tax rate of 6% is applied to the discounted sale price, calculate the final total cost of the item.

Step-by-Step Explanation:

  1. Calculate Sale Price (20% Discount): \[ \text{Sale Price} = \$850 \times (1 - 0.20) = \$850 \times 0.80 = \$680.00 \]
  2. Calculate Sales Tax (6% on $680.00): \[ \text{Tax} = \$680 \times 0.06 = \$40.80 \]
  3. Calculate Final Total: \[ \text{Total} = \$680.00 + \$40.80 = \$720.80 \]
Final Total Price: $720.80
Problem 34

Complex PEMDAS Evaluation

Evaluate the expression step-by-step following PEMDAS order of operations:

\[ (-0.5)^2 + \frac{3}{4} \times \frac{-8 + \frac{1.2}{0.3}}{\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)^3} \]

Step-by-Step Explanation:

  1. Simplify Inner Parentheses Division: \( 1.2 \div 0.3 = 4 \).
  2. Simplify Numerator Addition: \( -8 + 4 = -4 \).
  3. Evaluate Exponents:
    • \( (-0.5)^2 = 0.25 = \frac{1}{4} \)
    • \( \left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)^3 = -\frac{1}{8} = -0.125 \)
  4. Multiply Numerator: \( \frac{3}{4} \times (-4) = -3 \).
  5. Divide Fraction Part: \( \frac{-3}{-\frac{1}{8}} = -3 \times (-8) = 24 \).
  6. Final Addition: \( 0.25 + 24 = 24.25 \) or \( \frac{97}{4} \).
Final Answer: \( 24.25 \) or \( \frac{97}{4} \)
