14.(19)
Infer The relationships between the lengths of the sides of the rectangles are as follows: \[PQ + ST = QR \qquad PS + RU = PR \qquad PQ = 4\text{ in.}\] a. Find the perimeter of rectangle \(PQTS\).
b. Find the area of rectangle \(QRUT\).
Answer
\(\text{a. }20\text{ in.}\qquad\text{b. }48\text{ in}^2\)
Solution
In rectangle \(PQTS\), \(PQ = ST = 4\) in., so \(QR = 4 + 4 = 8\) in. The vertical sides are all equal (\(PS = QT = RU\)), and \(PR = PQ + QR = 12\) in., so \(2 \cdot PS = 12 \Rightarrow PS = 6\) in. a. \(P = 2(4 + 6) = 20\) in. b. \(A = QR \cdot RU = 8 \cdot 6 = 48\) in\(^2\).
15.(20)
Infer Triangle \(PQS\) is congruent to \(\triangle TSQ\).
a. Which angle in \(\triangle PQS\) corresponds to \(\angle STQ\) in \(\triangle TSQ\)?
b. What is the measure of \(\angle P\)?
Answer
\(\text{a. }\angle QPS\qquad\text{b. }90^\circ\)
Solution
a. The congruence \(\triangle PQS \cong \triangle TSQ\) matches vertices \(P \leftrightarrow T\), \(Q \leftrightarrow S\), \(S \leftrightarrow Q\). \(\angle STQ\) is at vertex \(T\) of \(\triangle TSQ\), which corresponds to vertex \(P\) of \(\triangle PQS\) → \(\angle QPS\). b. \(\angle P\) is a corner of rectangle \(PQTS\), so \(m\angle P = 90^\circ\).

32.(Challenge)
New Use long division to divide 7 by 4. Does the decimal terminate or repeat?
Answer
\(1.75\text{ — it terminates}\)
Solution
Work the long division algorithm: 4 goes into 7 once, leaving 3; bring down a 0 (30 tenths) → 7, leaving 2; bring down another 0 (20 hundredths) → 5, leaving 0. The remainder reaches 0, so the decimal terminates.
1.75 ----- 4 ) 7.00 4 -- 3 0 2 8 --- 20 20 -- 0
33.(Challenge)
New Use long division to divide 5 by 6. Does the decimal terminate or repeat?
Answer
\(0.8\overline{3}\text{ — it repeats}\)
Solution
Work the long division algorithm: 6 goes into 5.0 zero times past the decimal — 6 into 50 goes 8, leaving 2; bring down a 0 → 20, 6 goes 3, leaving 2 again. The remainder 2 keeps coming back, so the digit 3 repeats forever: \(0.8\overline{3}\). The decimal repeats.
0.833... -------- 6 ) 5.000 4 8 ---- 20 18 -- 20 18 -- 2 ← remainder 2 repeats!

35.(Challenge)
New Simplify using the order of operations (PEMDAS): \[3^2 + (-2) \times 0.5 - \dfrac{1}{2} + 1.25\]
Answer
\(8.75\)
Solution
Parentheses/exponents first: \(3^2 = 9\). Multiply next: \((-2) \times 0.5 = -1\). Then add and subtract left to right: \(9 + (-1) = 8\); \(8 - \frac{1}{2} = 7\frac{1}{2} = 7.5\); \(7.5 + 1.25 = 8.75\).
