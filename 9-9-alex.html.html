Practice a COMBINING
In the first half of the basketball game, Frank scored $14\text{ points}$. In the second half, he scored $18\text{ points}$. How many points did Frank score in total? Write a combining equation and solve.
Final Answer
$32\text{ points}$

Equation Pattern (Some + More = Total):

$$14 + 18 = P$$

$$P = 32\text{ points}$$

Practice b SEPARATING
Marcie had $\$20.00$. After purchasing a book, she had $\$8.75$ remaining. How much did the book cost? Write a separating equation and solve.
Final Answer
$\$11.25$

Equation Pattern (Starting Amount - Action/Away = Amount Left):

$$\$20.00 - C = \$8.75$$

To find the subtrahend $C$, subtract the difference from the minuend:

$$C = \$20.00 - \$8.75 = \$11.25$$

Practice c COMPARISON
On Monday, $420\text{ students}$ attended the assembly. On Tuesday, $385\text{ students}$ attended. How many more students attended on Monday than on Tuesday? Write an equation and solve.
Final Answer
$35\text{ more students}$

Equation Pattern (Greater - Lesser = Difference):

$$420 - 385 = D$$

$$D = 35\text{ students}$$

1. Jenny saved $\$35$ in June and $\$45$ in July.
2. Tim had $24\text{ pencils}$ and gave away $9\text{ pencils}$ to classmates.
Final Answer

1. Combining plot

Problem 1 COMBINING
In the morning, $142\text{ visitors}$ entered the museum. In the afternoon, $198\text{ visitors}$ entered. How many total visitors entered the museum throughout the day? Write an equation and solve.
Final Answer
$340\text{ visitors}$

$$\text{Some} + \text{More} = \text{Total}$$

$$142 + 198 = V$$

$$V = 340\text{ visitors}$$

Problem 2 SEPARATING
A bakery prepared $120\text{ muffins}$ in the morning. By noon, $84\text{ muffins}$ had been sold. How many muffins remained unsold? Write an equation and solve.
Final Answer
$36\text{ muffins}$

$$\text{Starting} - \text{Went Away} = \text{Remaining}$$

$$120 - 84 = M$$

$$M = 36\text{ muffins}$$

Problem 4 DIVISIBILITY
Which of the following numbers is divisible by both $3$ and $5$? $$\{125, \quad 210, \quad 305, \quad 412\}$$
Final Answer
$210$

• Divisibility by $5$: Last digit must be $0$ or $5$ ($125, 210, 305$).

• Divisibility by $3$: Sum of digits must be divisible by $3$.

For $210$: $2 + 1 + 0 = 3$ (divisible by $3$). Therefore, $210$ is divisible by both $3$ and $5$.

Problem 5 NUMBER LINE
Point $P$ is located three-fifths of the distance from $7$ to $8$ on a number line. What mixed number does point $P$ represent?
Final Answer
$7\frac{3}{5}$

Starting at $7$ and moving $\frac{3}{5}$ unit toward $8$ yields $7 + \frac{3}{5} = 7\frac{3}{5}$.

Problem 6a
Replace the comparison circle with the correct symbol ($<$, $>$, or $=$): $$12 - 0 \bigcirc 12 \div 1$$
Final Answer
$=$

$$12 - 0 = 12$$

$$12 \div 1 = 12$$

Since both sides evaluate to $12$, the comparison symbol is $=$.

Problem 6b PROPERTIES
Name the arithmetic properties demonstrated by $12 - 0 = 12$ and $12 \div 1 = 12$.
Final Answer
Identity Property of Subtraction and Identity Property of Division

• Subtracting zero from a number leaves it unchanged (Identity Property of Subtraction).

• Dividing a number by one leaves it unchanged (Identity Property of Division).

Problem 8a FRACTIONS
A spinner is divided into $10$ equal sectors, and $8$ sectors are shaded blue. What reduced fraction of the spinner is shaded blue?
Final Answer
$\frac{4}{5}$

$$\frac{8}{10} = \frac{8 \div 2}{10 \div 2} = \frac{4}{5}$$

Problem 8b

$$\frac{2}{10} = \frac{1}{5}$$

Problem 10 CONCEPTS
Explain the difference between a proper fraction and an improper fraction.
Final Answer
In a proper fraction, the numerator is less than the denominator. In an improper fraction, the numerator is greater than or equal to the denominator.

• Proper fraction example: $\frac{3}{4}$ ($\text{Numerator} < \text{Denominator}$)

• Improper fraction example: $\frac{5}{4}$ or $\frac{4}{4}$ ($\text{Numerator} \ge \text{Denominator}$)

Problem 17 JUSTIFY
Provide reasons for steps (a) and (b) in solving $15 + (q + 5) = 40$: $$\begin{align*} 15 + (q + 5) &= 40 && \text{Given} \\ 15 + (5 + q) &= 40 && \text{a. }\underline{\quad\quad\quad\quad} \\ (15 + 5) + q &= 40 && \text{b. }\underline{\quad\quad\quad\quad} \end{align*}$$
Final Answer

a. Commutative Property of Addition

b. Associative Property of Addition

• Step a changes the order of addition inside parentheses ($q + 5 \rightarrow 5 + q$).

• Step b changes the grouping of addends ($15 + (5 + q) \rightarrow (15 + 5) + q$).

Problem 27 ORDER OF OPS
Compare: $$600 - (200 - 50) \bigcirc (600 - 200) - 50$$
Final Answer
$>$

Left side: $600 - (200 - 50) = 600 - 150 = 450$

Right side: $(600 - 200) - 50 = 400 - 50 = 350$

Since $450 > 350$, the comparison symbol is $>$.

Problem 28 GEOMETRY
Parallelogram $ABCD$ has two acute angles ($\angle A$ and $\angle C$) and two obtuse angles ($\angle B$ and $\angle D$). Define acute and obtuse angles.
Final Answer
An acute angle measures less than $90^\circ$. An obtuse angle measures between $90^\circ$ and $180^\circ$.

• $\angle A, \angle C < 90^\circ$ (Acute)

• $90^\circ < \angle B, \angle D < 180^\circ$ (Obtuse)

Problem 29 GEOMETRY
Refer to square $XYZW$:
a. Identify a segment parallel to segment $\overline{XY}$.
b. Identify a segment perpendicular to segment $\overline{XY}$.
Final Answer

a. $\overline{WZ}$ (or $\overline{ZW}$)

b. $\overline{XW}$ (or $\overline{YZ}$)

a. Opposite sides of a square are parallel ($\overline{XY} \parallel \overline{WZ}$).

b. Adjacent sides meet at right angles ($90^\circ$), forming perpendicular lines ($\overline{XW} \perp \overline{XY}$).

Problem 30 GEOMETRY
Explain the difference in mathematical meaning between a ray ($\overrightarrow{AB}$) and a line ($\overleftrightarrow{AB}$).
Final Answer
Ray $\overrightarrow{AB}$ has one fixed endpoint ($A$) and extends endlessly in one direction through $B$. Line $\overleftrightarrow{AB}$ extends infinitely in both directions without endpoints.

• $\overrightarrow{AB}$: Single arrow indicates infinite extension in one direction starting from point $A$.

• $\overleftrightarrow{AB}$: Double arrows indicate infinite extension in both opposite directions.
