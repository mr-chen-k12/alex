Lesson Practice

a. When possible, cancel numbers and units before multiplying: $\frac{3 \text{ dollars}}{1 \text{ hour}} \times \frac{8 \text{ hours}}{1}$
Answer: $24 \text{ dollars}$

Explanation: Cancel the "hours" unit from the numerator and denominator. Then multiply the numbers: $3 \times 8 = 24$. The remaining unit is "dollars".
b. $\frac{6 \text{ baskets}}{10 \text{ shots}} \times \frac{100 \text{ shots}}{1}$
Answer: $60 \text{ baskets}$

Explanation: Cancel "shots". Cancel a zero from 100 and 10 (which is dividing both by 10). You are left with $6 \text{ baskets} \times 10 = 60 \text{ baskets}$.
c. $\frac{10 \text{ cents}}{1 \text{ kwh}} \times \frac{26.3 \text{ kwh}}{1}$
Answer: $263 \text{ cents}$

Explanation: Cancel the "kwh" units. Multiply $10 \times 26.3$. Multiplying by 10 moves the decimal point one place to the right, yielding $263 \text{ cents}$.
d. $\frac{160 \text{ km}}{2 \text{ hours}} \times \frac{10 \text{ hours}}{1}$
Answer: $800 \text{ km}$

Explanation: Cancel "hours". Simplify $\frac{10}{2}$ to $5$. Multiply $160 \text{ km} \times 5 = 800 \text{ km}$.
e. Multiply 18 teachers by 29 students per teacher.
Answer: $522 \text{ students}$

Explanation: Setup the ratio: $18 \text{ teachers} \times \frac{29 \text{ students}}{1 \text{ teacher}}$. The "teachers" unit cancels out. $18 \times 29 = 522$.
f. Multiply 2.3 meters by 100 centimeters per meter.
Answer: $230 \text{ centimeters}$

Explanation: Setup the ratio: $2.3 \text{ m} \times \frac{100 \text{ cm}}{1 \text{ m}}$. Meters cancel. $2.3 \times 100$ moves the decimal two places to the right, giving $230 \text{ cm}$.
g. Solve this problem by multiplying two ratios: How far will the train travel in 6 hours at 45 miles per hour?
Answer: $270 \text{ miles}$

Explanation: $\frac{6 \text{ hours}}{1} \times \frac{45 \text{ miles}}{1 \text{ hour}}$. Hours cancel. $6 \times 45 = 270 \text{ miles}$.

Written Practice

1. What is the total price of a $45.79 item when 7% sales tax is added to the price?
Answer: $\$49.00$

Explanation: First, find the tax amount by multiplying the price by the decimal equivalent of 7% (0.07): $45.79 \times 0.07 \approx 3.2053$. Round to the nearest cent: $\$3.21$. Add the tax to the original price: $45.79 + 3.21 = 49.00$.
2. Jeff is 1.67 meters tall. How many centimeters tall is Jeff? (Multiply 1.67 meters by 100 centimeters per meter.)
Answer: $167 \text{ cm}$

Explanation: $1.67 \text{ m} \times \frac{100 \text{ cm}}{1 \text{ m}}$. The meters cancel out, and multiplying $1.67$ by $100$ shifts the decimal point two places to the right.
3. If $\frac{5}{8}$ of the 40 seeds sprouted, how many seeds did not sprout?
Answer: $15 \text{ seeds}$

Explanation: The question in the image simply asks "how many seeds sprouted?" but let's address both. If it asks how many sprouted: $\frac{5}{8} \times 40 = 25 \text{ seeds}$. (If it asks how many did *not* sprout, it would be $40 - 25 = 15$. Based on the exact image text: "how many seeds sprouted?" -> 25).
4. Write this number in standard notation: $(5 \times 100) + (6 \times 10) + (7 \times \frac{1}{10}) + (3 \times \frac{1}{100})$
Answer: $560.73$

Explanation: Calculate each part: $500 + 60 + 0.7 + 0.03$. Add them all together to get $560.73$.
5. Change $\frac{1}{6}$ to its percent equivalent by multiplying $\frac{1}{6}$ by 100%.
Answer: $16 \frac{2}{3}\%$ or $16.6\overline{6}\%$

Explanation: $\frac{1}{6} \times \frac{100\%}{1} = \frac{100}{6}\%$. Divide 100 by 6 to get $16$ with a remainder of $4$. The fraction is $\frac{4}{6}$, which simplifies to $\frac{2}{3}$.
6. What is the percent equivalent of 2.5?
Answer: $250\%$

Explanation: To convert a decimal to a percent, multiply by 100 and add the percent sign. $2.5 \times 100 = 250$.
7. How much money is 30% of $12.00?
Answer: $\$3.60$

Explanation: Convert 30% to a decimal (0.30) and multiply by the total amount. $12.00 \times 0.30 = 3.60$.
8. The minute hand of a clock turns $180^\circ$ in how many minutes?
Answer: $30 \text{ minutes}$

Explanation: A full circle is $360^\circ$ and takes 60 minutes. $180^\circ$ is exactly half of a circle, which takes half the time: $60 / 2 = 30$ minutes.
9. The circumference of the front tire on Elizabeth's bike is 6 feet. How many complete turns does the front wheel make as Elizabeth rides down her 30-foot driveway?
Answer: $5 \text{ turns}$

Explanation: Divide the total distance by the distance covered in one turn (the circumference). $30 \text{ feet} / 6 \text{ feet/turn} = 5 \text{ turns}$.
10. Chad built this stack of one-cubic-foot boxes. What is the volume of the stack?
Answer: $36 \text{ cubic feet}$

Explanation: Look at the image dimensions. The front face is $4$ boxes wide and $3$ boxes high. The depth is $3$ boxes. Total volume = $length \times height \times depth = 4 \times 3 \times 3 = 36$ boxes. Since each box is 1 cubic foot, the volume is $36 \text{ ft}^3$.
11. $\frac{3}{4} + \frac{3}{5}$
Answer: $1 \frac{7}{20}$

Explanation: Find a common denominator, which is 20. $\frac{3 \times 5}{4 \times 5} + \frac{3 \times 4}{5 \times 4} = \frac{15}{20} + \frac{12}{20} = \frac{27}{20}$. Convert the improper fraction to a mixed number: $27 \div 20 = 1$ with a remainder of $7$, so $1 \frac{7}{20}$.
12. $18 \frac{1}{8} - 12 \frac{1}{2}$
Answer: $5 \frac{5}{8}$

Explanation: Get a common denominator of 8. $18 \frac{1}{8} - 12 \frac{4}{8}$. You can't subtract $\frac{4}{8}$ from $\frac{1}{8}$, so borrow 1 from 18. $18 \frac{1}{8}$ becomes $17 \frac{9}{8}$. Now subtract: $17 \frac{9}{8} - 12 \frac{4}{8} = 5 \frac{5}{8}$.
13. $3 \frac{3}{4} \times 2 \frac{2}{3} \times 1 \frac{1}{10}$
Answer: $11$

Explanation: Convert to improper fractions: $\frac{15}{4} \times \frac{8}{3} \times \frac{11}{10}$. Cross-cancel before multiplying to make it easier. $\frac{15 \times 8 \times 11}{4 \times 3 \times 10} = \frac{1320}{120} = 11$.
14. $\frac{2^5}{2^3}$
Answer: $4$

Explanation: When dividing powers with the same base, subtract the exponents: $2^{(5-3)} = 2^2$. And $2^2 = 4$.
15. How many fourths are in $2 \frac{1}{2}$?
Answer: $10$

Explanation: First, convert $2 \frac{1}{2}$ to an improper fraction: $\frac{5}{2}$. To find how many fourths are in it, find an equivalent fraction with 4 as the denominator: multiply the top and bottom by 2 to get $\frac{10}{4}$. There are 10 fourths.

17. $(1.5)^2$

18. $6 \frac{2}{5} \div 0.8$ (decimal answer)
Answer: $8$

Explanation: First, convert the fraction to a decimal. $\frac{2}{5} = 0.4$, so the problem becomes $6.4 \div 0.8$. Move the decimal point one place to the right for both: $64 \div 8 = 8$.
19. Find the sum of $6 \frac{1}{4}$, $4.95$, and $8.21$ by rounding each number to the nearest whole number before adding. Explain how you arrived at your answer.
Answer: $19$

Explanation:
- $6 \frac{1}{4}$ (or $6.25$) rounds down to $6$.
- $4.95$ rounds up to $5$.
- $8.21$ rounds down to $8$.
Sum: $6 + 5 + 8 = 19$.
20. The diameter of a round tabletop is 60 inches.
a. What is the radius of the tabletop?
b. What is the area of the tabletop? (Use 3.14 for $\pi$)
Answer: a. $30 \text{ inches}$; b. $2826 \text{ sq inches}$

Explanation:
a. Radius is half the diameter. $60 \div 2 = 30 \text{ inches}$.
b. Area formula is $A = \pi r^2$. $A = 3.14 \times (30)^2 = 3.14 \times 900 = 2826 \text{ sq inches}$.
21. Arrange these numbers in order from least to greatest: $\frac{1}{4}, 4\%, 0.4$
Answer: $4\%, \frac{1}{4}, 0.4$

Explanation: Convert all to decimals to compare:
$\frac{1}{4} = 0.25$
$4\% = 0.04$
$0.4 = 0.40$
Comparing the decimals: $0.04 < 0.25 < 0.40$, so the original order is $4\%, \frac{1}{4}, 0.4$.
22. Find the unknown number: $y + 3.4 = 5$
Answer: $y = 1.6$

Explanation: Subtract 3.4 from both sides to isolate $y$: $5.0 - 3.4 = 1.6$.
23. Find the unknown number: $\frac{4}{8} = \frac{x}{12}$
Answer: $x = 6$

Explanation: Simplify the fraction $\frac{4}{8}$ to $\frac{1}{2}$. The equation is now $\frac{1}{2} = \frac{x}{12}$. Cross-multiply: $2x = 12$, so $x = 6$.
24. A cube has edges that are 6 cm long.
a. What is the area of each face of the cube?
b. What is the volume of the cube?
c. What is the surface area of the cube?
Answer: a. $36 \text{ cm}^2$; b. $216 \text{ cm}^3$; c. $216 \text{ cm}^2$

Explanation:
a. Area of one face = $side \times side = 6 \times 6 = 36 \text{ cm}^2$.
b. Volume = $side^3 = 6 \times 6 \times 6 = 216 \text{ cm}^3$.
c. Surface Area = $6 \times (\text{Area of one face}) = 6 \times 36 = 216 \text{ cm}^2$.
25. $\overline{AB}$ is 24 mm long. $\overline{AC}$ is 42 mm long. How long is $\overline{BC}$?
Answer: $18 \text{ mm}$

Explanation: Since B is between A and C, the length of AC is equal to the sum of AB and BC. So, $BC = AC - AB = 42 - 24 = 18 \text{ mm}$.
26. $6^2 \div \sqrt{9} + 2 \times 2^3 - \sqrt{400}$
Answer: $8$

Explanation: Follow order of operations (PEMDAS):
1. Exponents and Roots: $36 \div 3 + 2 \times 8 - 20$
2. Multiply/Divide left to right: $12 + 16 - 20$
3. Add/Subtract left to right: $28 - 20 = 8$.
27. What is the ratio of a pint of water to a quart of water?
Answer: $1:2$

Explanation: There are 2 pints in 1 quart. Therefore, the ratio of pints to quarts is 1 to 2, or $\frac{1}{2}$.
28. The formula for the area of a parallelogram is $A = bh$. If the base of a parallelogram is 1.2 m and the height is 0.9 m, what is the area of the parallelogram? How can estimation help you check your answer?
Answer: $1.08 \text{ m}^2$

Explanation: Multiply the base by the height: $1.2 \times 0.9 = 1.08 \text{ m}^2$. You can estimate to check by rounding $1.2$ to $1$ and $0.9$ to $1$. $1 \times 1 = 1 \text{ m}^2$, which is very close to $1.08$, confirming the decimal placement is correct.
29. Multiply 2.5 liters by 1000 milliliters per liter: $\frac{2.5 \text{ liters}}{1} \times \frac{1000 \text{ milliliters}}{1 \text{ liter}}$
Answer: $2500 \text{ milliliters}$

Explanation: Cancel the "liters" unit. Multiply $2.5 \times 1000$. This shifts the decimal three places to the right: $2500 \text{ milliliters}$.
30. If this spinner is spun once, what is the probability that the arrow will end up pointing to an even number? Express the probability ratio as a fraction and as a decimal. (Spinner shows 1, 2, 3, 4)
Answer: Fraction: $\frac{1}{2}$, Decimal: $0.5$

Explanation: The even numbers on the spinner are 2 and 4. That is 2 favorable outcomes out of 4 possible outcomes. Probability = $\frac{2}{4}$, which simplifies to $\frac{1}{2}$. As a decimal, $\frac{1}{2} = 0.5$.

Real-World Connection

The local university football stadium seats 60,000 fans, and average attendance at home games is 48,500. It has been determined that an average fan consumes 2.25 beverages per game.

a. If each beverage is served in a cup, about how many cups are used during an average game? Express your answer in scientific notation.
b. Next week is the homecoming game, which is always sold out. A box of cups contains $1 \times 10^3$ cups. How many boxes of cups will be needed for the game?
Answer: a. $1.09125 \times 10^5 \text{ cups}$; b. $135 \text{ boxes}$

Explanation:
a. Multiply average attendance by beverages per fan: $48,500 \times 2.25 = 109,125 \text{ cups}$. To express in scientific notation, move the decimal 5 places to the left: $1.09125 \times 10^5$.
b. A sold-out game means 60,000 fans. Multiply by beverages per fan: $60,000 \times 2.25 = 135,000 \text{ cups}$. A box holds $1 \times 10^3 = 1,000 \text{ cups}$. Divide total cups by cups per box: $135,000 \div 1,000 = 135 \text{ boxes}$.
