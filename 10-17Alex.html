1.(28)
Five volunteers collected bottles to be recycled. The number they collected were: 242, 236, 248, 268, and 226. What was the average number of bottles collected by the volunteers?
Answer
\(244\text{ bottles}\)
Solution
Add the five numbers: \(242+236+248+268+226=1220\). Divide by the number of volunteers: \(\dfrac{1220}{5}=244\).
2.(28)
Yori ran a mile in 5 minutes 14 seconds. How many seconds did it take Yori to run a mile?
Answer
\(314\text{ seconds}\)
Solution
Convert minutes to seconds: \(5\text{ min}=5\times 60=300\text{ s}\). Then \(300+14=314\text{ s}\).
3.(28)
Luisa bought a pair of pants for \(\$24.95\) and 3 blouses for \(\$15.99\) each. Altogether, how much did she spend?
Answer
\(\$72.92\)
Solution
Cost of blouses: \(3\times \$15.99=\$47.97\). Total: \(\$24.95+\$47.97=\$72.92\).

5.(22)
Salma led for 75% of the 5000-meter race.
a. Salma led the race for how many meters?
b. Salma did not lead the race for how many meters?
Answer
\(\text{a. }3750\text{ m}\quad\text{b. }1250\text{ m}\)
Solution
\(75\%=\dfrac{3}{4}\).
a. \(\dfrac{3}{4}\times 5000=3750\) meters.
b. \(5000-3750=1250\) meters.
6.(19, 20)
This rectangle is twice as long as it is wide.
a. What is the perimeter of the rectangle?
b. What is the area of the rectangle?
Answer
\(\text{a. }24\text{ in.}\quad\text{b. }32\text{ in.}^2\)
Solution
The 8 in. side is the length. Width \(=8\div 2=4\) in.
a. Perimeter \(=2(8+4)=2(12)=24\) in.
b. Area \(=8\times 4=32\) in.\(^2\).

Answer
\(\text{a. }280\quad\text{b. }300\)
Solution

9.(24)
GENERALIZE Write 56 and 240 as products of prime numbers. Then reduce \(\dfrac{58}{240}\).
Answer
\(56=2^3\cdot 7,\; 240=2^4\cdot 3\cdot 5,\; \dfrac{58}{240}=\dfrac{29}{120}\)
Solution
\(56=2\cdot 28=2\cdot 2\cdot 14=2^3\cdot 7\).
\(240=24\cdot 10=2^3\cdot 3\cdot 2\cdot 5=2^4\cdot 3\cdot 5\).
\(\dfrac{58}{240}=\dfrac{2\cdot 29}{2^4\cdot 3\cdot 5}\). Cancel one factor of 2: \(\dfrac{29}{2^3\cdot 3\cdot 5}=\dfrac{29}{120}\).

11.(15)
For a and b, find an equivalent fraction that has a denominator of 24.
a. \(\dfrac{7}{8}\)    b. \(\dfrac{11}{12}\)
c. What property do we use to find equivalent fractions?
Answer
\(\text{a. }\dfrac{21}{24}\quad\text{b. }\dfrac{22}{24}\quad\text{c. Identity Property of Multiplication}\)
Solution
a. \(\dfrac{7}{8}=\dfrac{7\cdot 3}{8\cdot 3}=\dfrac{21}{24}\).
b. \(\dfrac{11}{12}=\dfrac{11\cdot 2}{12\cdot 2}=\dfrac{22}{24}\).
c. Multiplying by a form of 1 (such as \(\tfrac33\) or \(\tfrac22\)) uses the Identity Property of Multiplication.
12.(21)

b. Find \(\sqrt{3600}\).
Answer
\(\text{a. }2^4\cdot 3^2\cdot 5^2\quad\text{b. }60\)
Solution

13.(28)
SUMMARIZE Describe how to find the mean of 45, 36, 42, 29, 16, and 24.
Answer
\(32\)
Solution
To find the mean: add all the numbers, then divide by how many numbers there are.
Sum: \(45+36+42+29+16+24=192\). There are 6 numbers, so mean \(=\dfrac{192}{6}=32\).

29.(18)
In the figure below, the two triangles are congruent.
a. Which angle in \(\triangle ACD\) corresponds to \(\angle CAB\) in \(\triangle ABC\)?
b. Which segment in \(\triangle ABC\) corresponds to \(\overline{AD}\) in \(\triangle ACD\)?
c. If the area of \(\triangle ABC\) is \(7\dfrac{1}{2}\) in.\(^2\), what is the area of figure \(ABCD\)?
Answer
\(\text{a. }\angle ACD\quad\text{b. }\overline{BC}\quad\text{c. }15\text{ in.}^2\)
Solution
a. In parallelogram \\(ABCD\\), \\(AB\\parallel CD\\), so \\(\\angle CAB=\\angle ACD\\) (alternate interior angles). The corresponding angle is \\(\\angle ACD\\).
b. Opposite sides of a parallelogram are equal: \\(AD=BC\\). The segment corresponding to \\(\\overline{AD}\\) is \\(\\overline{BC}\\).
c. The diagonal divides the parallelogram into two congruent triangles, so area of \\(ABCD=2\\times 7\\dfrac12=15\\) in.\\(^2\\).
30.(17)
With a ruler draw \(\overline{PQ}\) \(2\dfrac{3}{4}\) in. long. Then with a protractor draw \(\overrightarrow{QR}\) so that \(\angle PQR\) measures \(30^\circ\). Then, from point \(P\), draw a ray perpendicular to \(\overline{PQ}\) that intersects \(\overrightarrow{QR}\). (You may need to extend \(\overrightarrow{QR}\) to show the intersection.) Label the point where the rays intersect point \(M\). Use a protractor to measure \(\angle PMQ\).
Answer
\(\angle PMQ=60^\circ\)
Solution
Since \(M\) lies on ray \\(QR\\), \\(\\angle PQM=\\angle PQR=30^\\circ\\). The ray from \\(P\\) is perpendicular to \\(\\overline{PQ}\\), so \\(\\angle QPM=90^\\circ\\). The angles of \\(\\triangle PQM\\) sum to \\(180^\\circ\\): \\(\\angle PMQ=180^\\circ-90^\\circ-30^\\circ=60^\\circ\\).
