4.Solve for x: 56x + 3 = 18

6.Find the least common multiple (LCM) of 16, 24, and 40.

Prime factors: 16 = 24, 24 = 23×3, 40 = 23×5.

7.Convert 2150 to a percentage.

0.42 × 100 = 42%

8.A triangle has a base of 22 cm and an area of 154 cm². What is its height?

Use A = 12bh.

154 = 12(22)h = 11h

Answer: 14 cm

9.Evaluate: (−4)3 + 52 + 7

(−4)3 = −64 and 52 = 25.

−64 + 25 + 7 = −32

10.Solve for y: 6(y − 2) = 3(y + 5)

Distribute: 6y − 12 = 3y + 15

Subtract 3y and add 12: 3y = 27

Answer: y = 9

11.A jacket originally costs $320. It is marked down by 15%, and then an additional 20% coupon is applied to the sale price. What is the final price?

After 15% off: 320 × 0.85 = $272

After another 20% off: 272 × 0.80 = $217.60

12.Find the volume of a rectangular prism with length 6.4 m, width 3.75 m, and height 2 m.

V = lwh = 6.4 × 3.75 × 2 = 48 m³

13.Calculate the mean of the numbers: 18, 23, 27, 16, 25, 11.

Sum: 18 + 23 + 27 + 16 + 25 + 11 = 120

120 ÷ 6 = 20

14.Solve the inequality: 15 − 5x < −20

Answer: x > 7

16.A 15-foot pole casts a 10-foot shadow. At the same time, a tree casts a 26-foot shadow. How tall is the tree?

17.Simplify: √324 + √100 − √36

18.Find the perimeter of a semicircle with radius 11 cm. Use π ≈ 3.14 and include the straight diameter edge.

Semicircle arc length: πr = 3.14 × 11 = 34.54 cm

Diameter: 2r = 22 cm

Total: 34.54 + 22 = 56.54 cm

30.Deposit $2,000 into a bank account with 6% compound interest compounded annually. What is the total balance after 2 years?

Year 2: $2,120 × 1.06 = $2,247.20

31.Use PEMDAS to evaluate: 8.8 − [34 × (1.2 + 0.8)2] + 25

Parentheses first: 1.2 + 0.8 = 2

Exponent next: 22 = 4

Multiply by the fraction: 34 × 4 = 3

Convert the remaining fraction: 25 = 0.4

Finish left to right: 8.8 − 3 + 0.4 = 6.2
