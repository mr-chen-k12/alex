9. Fraction Addition
\( \frac{1}{2} + \frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{6} \)

Find the Least Common Denominator (LCD), which is 6.

\( \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{6} \)

\( \frac{2}{3} = \frac{4}{6} \)

\( \frac{1}{6} = \frac{1}{6} \)

Add them: \( \frac{3+4+1}{6} = \frac{8}{6} \).

Simplify: \( \frac{8}{6} = \frac{4}{3} = 1 \frac{1}{3} \).

Answer: \( 1 \frac{1}{3} \)

10. Fraction Subtraction
\( 3 \frac{1}{4} - 1 \frac{7}{8} \)

Convert to common denominator (8).

\( 3 \frac{1}{4} = 3 \frac{2}{8} \)

We cannot subtract \( \frac{7}{8} \) from \( \frac{2}{8} \), so we borrow from the whole number 3.

\( 3 \frac{2}{8} = 2 \frac{8+2}{8} = 2 \frac{10}{8} \).

Now subtract: \( 2 \frac{10}{8} - 1 \frac{7}{8} = 1 \frac{3}{8} \).

Answer: \( 1 \frac{3}{8} \)

11. Fraction Multiplication
\( \frac{5}{8} \cdot \frac{3}{5} \cdot \frac{4}{5} \)

You can cross-cancel before multiplying.

Cancel the 5 in the numerator and a 5 in the denominator.

\( \frac{1}{8} \cdot \frac{3}{1} \cdot \frac{4}{5} \)

Cancel the 4 in the numerator with the 8 in the denominator (8 becomes 2).

\( \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{3}{1} \cdot \frac{1}{5} \)

Multiply straight across: \( \frac{1 \times 3 \times 1}{2 \times 1 \times 5} = \frac{3}{10} \).

Answer: \(\frac{3}{10}\)

12. Mixed Number Multiplication
\( 3 \frac{1}{3} \times 3 \)

Convert mixed number to improper fraction: \( 3 \frac{1}{3} = \frac{10}{3} \).

\( \frac{10}{3} \times \frac{3}{1} \)

The 3s cancel out.

Answer: 10

13. Fraction Division
\( \frac{3}{4} \div 1 \frac{1}{2} \)

Convert mixed number to improper fraction: \( 1 \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{2} \).

Rewrite as multiplication by the reciprocal: \( \frac{3}{4} \times \frac{2}{3} \).

The 3s cancel. \( \frac{2}{4} \) simplifies to \( \frac{1}{2} \).

Answer: \(\frac{1}{2}\)

21. Prime Factorization
Write prime factorization of numerator and denominator of \( \frac{40}{96} \), then reduce.

40: \( 4 \times 10 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 5 = 2^3 \times 5 \).

96: \( 3 \times 32 = 3 \times 2^5 = 2^5 \times 3 \).

Fraction: \( \frac{2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 5}{2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 3} \).

Cancel three 2s from top and bottom.

Remaining Numerator: 5.

Remaining Denominator: \( 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 = 12 \).

Answer: \(\frac{5}{12}\)

24. Classify Shapes
Why are some rectangles not squares?

A rectangle is defined as a quadrilateral with four right angles. A square is a rectangle with four congruent sides.

If a rectangle has sides of different lengths (e.g., length 5, width 2), it fits the definition of a rectangle but not a square.

Answer: Because they do not have four congruent sides.

26. Quadrilateral Properties
In quadrilateral PQRS:
a. Which segment appears parallel to PQ?
b. Which segment appears perpendicular to PQ?

a. The segment opposite to PQ is SR. They both appear horizontal.

a. SR

b. Perpendicular means forming a 90-degree angle.

Visually, the sides PS and QR are slanted. While no side is perfectly vertical in the drawing relative to the horizontal PQ, the question asks what "appears" to be. In standard problems like this, if a side is slanted, the answer is usually "None". However, if one must be chosen, PS is less slanted than QR. Strict answer: None.

b. None (or possibly PS depending on strictness of "appears")
