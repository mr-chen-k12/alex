Simplify the following:
a. $-2 + -3 - -4 + -5$
b. $-3 + (+2) - (+5) - (-6)$
c. $+3 + -4 - +6 + +7 - -1$
d. $2 + (-3) - (-9) - (+7) + (+1)$
e. $3 - -5 + -4 - +2 + +8$
f. $(-10) - (+20) - (-30) + (-40)$

Explanations: Convert subtractions to additions of the opposite, then combine like signs.

  • a. $-2 - 3 + 4 - 5 = -10 + 4 = $ $-6$
  • b. $-3 + 2 - 5 + 6 = -8 + 8 = $ $0$
  • c. $3 - 4 - 6 + 7 + 1 = 11 - 10 = $ $1$
  • d. $2 - 3 + 9 - 7 + 1 = 12 - 10 = $ $2$
  • e. $3 + 5 - 4 - 2 + 8 = 16 - 6 = $ $10$
  • f. $-10 - 20 + 30 - 40 = -70 + 30 = $ $-40$

Written Practice

1. A pyramid with a square base has how many more edges than vertices?

Explanation: A square-based pyramid has 5 vertices (4 at the base, 1 at the apex) and 8 edges (4 around the base, 4 reaching to the apex). The difference is $8 - 5 = 3$.

Answer: 3

2. Becki weighed 7 lb 8 oz when she was born and 12 lb 6 oz at 3 months. How many pounds and ounces did Becki gain in 3 months?

Explanation: Subtract the birth weight from the 3-month weight.
$12\text{ lb } 6\text{ oz} - 7\text{ lb } 8\text{ oz}$. Since 6 oz is less than 8 oz, borrow 1 lb (16 oz) from the 12 lbs.
$11\text{ lb } (6+16)\text{ oz} = 11\text{ lb } 22\text{ oz}$.
Now subtract: $11\text{ lb } 22\text{ oz} - 7\text{ lb } 8\text{ oz} = 4\text{ lb } 14\text{ oz}$.

Answer: 4 lb 14 oz

3. There are 6 fish and 10 snails in the aquarium. What is the ratio of fish to snails?

Explanation: The ratio is $\frac{\text{Fish}}{\text{Snails}} = \frac{6}{10}$. Reduce the fraction by dividing by 2 to get $\frac{3}{5}$.

Answer: 3/5

4. A team's win-loss ratio was 3 to 2. If the team had played 20 games without a tie, how many games had it won?

Explanation: Total ratio parts = $3 \text{ (wins)} + 2 \text{ (losses)} = 5 \text{ parts}$.
Total games = 20. Divide total games by total parts: $\frac{20}{5} = 4 \text{ games per part}$.
Multiply by the win parts: $3 \times 4 = 12 \text{ wins}$.

Answer: 12 games

5. If Molly tosses a coin and rolls a number cube, what is the probability of the coin landing heads up and the number cube stopping with a 6 on top?

Explanation: These are independent events. Multiply their probabilities.
$P(\text{Heads}) = \frac{1}{2}$. $P(6) = \frac{1}{6}$.
$P(\text{Heads and } 6) = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{6} = \frac{1}{12}$.

Answer: 1/12

6. a. What is the perimeter of this parallelogram?
b. What is the area of this parallelogram?

Explanation:
a. Perimeter is the distance around the outside. $P = 2(\text{base}) + 2(\text{side}) = 2(8) + 2(7) = 16 + 14 = 30\text{ cm}$.
b. Area is base $\times$ height. $A = 8\text{ cm} \times 6\text{ cm} = 48\text{ cm}^2$.

Answer: a. 30 cm; b. 48 cm$^2$

7. If each acute angle of a parallelogram measures $59^\circ$, then what is the measure of each obtuse angle?

Explanation: Consecutive angles in a parallelogram are supplementary (they add up to $180^\circ$).
$180^\circ - 59^\circ = 121^\circ$.

Answer: $121^\circ$

8. The center of this circle is the origin. The circle passes through (2, 0).
a. Estimate the area of the circle in square units by counting squares.
b. Calculate the area of the circle by using 3.14 for $\pi$.

Explanation:
a. Counting full and partial squares inside the circle roughly yields 12 to 13 squares.
b. The radius is 2 units. Area $= \pi r^2 \approx 3.14 \times (2)^2 = 3.14 \times 4 = 12.56$.

Answer: a. ~12-13 sq units; b. 12.56

9. Which ratio forms a proportion with $\frac{2}{3}$?
A $\frac{2}{4}$     B $\frac{3}{4}$     C $\frac{4}{6}$     D $\frac{3}{2}$

Explanation: A proportion is an equation stating two ratios are equal. Reduce each option to see which equals $\frac{2}{3}$. Only C ($\frac{4}{6}$) reduces to $\frac{2}{3}$.

Answer: C

10. Complete this proportion: $\frac{6}{8} = \frac{a}{12}$

Explanation: Cross-multiply to solve for $a$:
$8 \times a = 6 \times 12$
$8a = 72$
$a = 9$

Answer: 9

11. What is the perimeter of the hexagon at right? Dimensions are in centimeters.

Explanation: Find the missing side lengths.
The unknown inner vertical side + 6 (right vertical side) = 12 (total left vertical side). So the inner vertical side is $12 - 6 = 6$.
The unknown inner horizontal side + 5 (top horizontal) = 13 (bottom horizontal). So the inner horizontal side is $13 - 5 = 8$.
Perimeter $= 12 \text{ (left)} + 5 \text{ (top)} + 6 \text{ (inner vertical)} + 8 \text{ (inner horizontal)} + 6 \text{ (right)} + 13 \text{ (bottom)} = 50\text{ cm}$.

Answer: 50 cm

Sale Price = $0.60 \times 6.95 = 4.17$.

16. Between which two consecutive whole numbers is $\sqrt{220}$?

Explanation: Find perfect squares near 220.
$14^2 = 196$ and $15^2 = 225$. Since 220 is between 196 and 225, $\sqrt{220}$ is between 14 and 15.

Answer: 14 and 15

17. Compare: $\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^3 \bigcirc$ the probability of 3 consecutive "heads" coin tosses

Explanation:
Left side: $\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^3 = \frac{1}{8}$.
Right side: $P(3\text{ heads}) = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{8}$.
They are equal.

Answer: $=$

18. Divide 0.624 by 0.05 and round the quotient to the nearest whole number.

Explanation: $0.624 \div 0.05 = 62.4 \div 5 = 12.48$.
Rounding to the nearest whole number gives 12.

19. The average of three numbers is 20. What is the sum of the three numbers?

Explanation: Average $= \frac{\text{Sum}}{\text{Count}}$.
Therefore, $\text{Sum} = \text{Average} \times \text{Count} = 20 \times 3 = 60$.

Answer: 60

20. Write the prime factorization of 450 using exponents.

Explanation: Create a factor tree for 450.
$450 = 45 \times 10$
$45 = 9 \times 5 = 3 \times 3 \times 5$
$10 = 2 \times 5$
Combine all prime factors: $2 \times 3 \times 3 \times 5 \times 5 = 2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5^2$.

Answer: $2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5^2$

21. $-3 + -5 - -4 - +2$

Explanation: Change subtractions to adding the opposite.
$-3 - 5 + 4 - 2 = -8 + 4 - 2 = -4 - 2 = -6$.

Answer: $-6$

22. $3^4 + 5^2 \times 4 - \sqrt{100} \times 2^3$

Explanation: Follow order of operations (PEMDAS/GEMDAS).
Exponents and roots first: $81 + 25 \times 4 - 10 \times 8$.
Multiplication: $81 + 100 - 80$.
Addition and Subtraction: $181 - 80 = 101$.

Answer: 101

23. How many blocks 1 inch on each edge would it take to fill a shoe box that is 12 inches long, 6 inches wide, and 5 inches tall?

Explanation: Find the volume of the box in cubic inches.
$V = \text{length} \times \text{width} \times \text{height} = 12 \times 6 \times 5 = 360$. Since each block is 1 cubic inch, it takes 360 blocks.

Answer: 360 blocks

24. Three fourths of the 60 athletes played in the game. How many athletes did not play?

Explanation: If $\frac{3}{4}$ played, then $\frac{1}{4}$ did not play.
$\frac{1}{4} \text{ of } 60 = 60 \div 4 = 15$.

Answer: 15 athletes

25. The distance a car travels can be found by multiplying the speed of the car by the amount of time the car travels at that speed. How far would a car travel in 4 hours at 88 kilometers per hour?
$\frac{88 \text{ km}}{1 \text{ hr}} \times \frac{4 \text{ hr}}{1}$

Explanation: Multiply the given fractions, canceling the "hr" units.
$88 \times 4 = 352\text{ km}$.

Answer: 352 km

27. Colby measured the circumference and diameter of four circles. Then he divided the circumference by the diameter of each circle to find the number of diameters in a circumference. Here are his answers:
3.12, 3.2, 3.15, 3.1
Find the average of Colby's answers. Round the average to the nearest hundredth.

Explanation: Add the numbers and divide by 4.
$3.12 + 3.20 + 3.15 + 3.10 = 12.57$.
$12.57 \div 4 = 3.1425$.
Rounding to the nearest hundredth (two decimal places) gives $3.14$.

Answer: 3.14

28. Hector was thinking of a two-digit counting number, and he asked Simon to guess the number. Describe how you can find the probability that Simon will guess correctly on the first try.

Explanation: The two-digit counting numbers go from 10 to 99. To find the total amount, subtract the highest from lowest and add 1 ($99 - 10 + 1 = 90$), or just realize it's all numbers 1-99 minus the single digits 1-9. There are 90 possible numbers. Since Simon makes exactly 1 guess, his chance is 1 out of the 90 possibilities.

Answer: The probability is 1/90. (Find total numbers from 10 to 99, which is 90, and set the guess as 1 favorable outcome over 90).

29. The coordinates of three vertices of a triangle are (3, 5), (-1, 5), and (-1, -3). What is the area of the triangle?

Explanation: Plotting these points reveals a right triangle.
The base connects (-1, 5) and (3, 5). Length $= 3 - (-1) = 4$ units.
The height connects (-1, 5) and (-1, -3). Length $= 5 - (-3) = 8$ units.
$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = \frac{1}{2} \times 4 \times 8 = 16\text{ sq units}$.

Answer: 16 square units

30. $\frac{2 \text{ gal}}{1} \times \frac{4 \text{ qt}}{1 \text{ gal}} \times \frac{2 \text{ pt}}{1 \text{ qt}}$

Explanation: Cancel out the units that appear in both the numerator and the denominator (gallons and quarts).
You are left with $2 \times 4 \times 2 \text{ pt} = 16 \text{ pints}$.

Answer: 16 pt
