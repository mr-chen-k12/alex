Math Practice: Multiple Choice Variations

Variations of Problem 4 (Fraction Division)

1. Simplify: \(\frac{2}{3} \div \frac{8}{9}\)
Explanation: Multiply by the reciprocal: \(\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{9}{8}\). Cross-simplify: 2 and 8 reduce to 1 and 4. 9 and 3 reduce to 3 and 1. Result: \(\frac{1}{1} \times \frac{3}{4} = \frac{3}{4}\).
2. Simplify: \(\frac{3}{5} \div \frac{9}{10}\)
Explanation: Multiply by the reciprocal: \(\frac{3}{5} \times \frac{10}{9}\). Cross-simplify: 3 and 9 reduce to 1 and 3. 10 and 5 reduce to 2 and 1. Result: \(\frac{1}{1} \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{2}{3}\).
3. Simplify: \(\frac{7}{8} \div \frac{14}{4}\)
Explanation: Multiply by the reciprocal: \(\frac{7}{8} \times \frac{4}{14}\). Cross-simplify: 7 and 14 reduce to 1 and 2. 4 and 8 reduce to 1 and 2. Result: \(\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{4}\).

Variations of Problem 7 (Percentages)

4. What is 20% of 150?
Explanation: Convert 20% to a decimal (0.20). Multiply: \(0.20 \times 150 = 30\). (Alternatively, 10% of 150 is 15, so 20% is \(15 \times 2 = 30\)).
5. What is 35% of 60?
Explanation: Convert 35% to a decimal (0.35). Multiply: \(0.35 \times 60 = 21\).
6. What is 5% of 240?
Explanation: 10% of 240 is 24. Since 5% is half of 10%, divide 24 by 2 to get 12.

Variations of Problem 8 (Discounts)

7. An $80 pair of shoes is on sale for 30% off. What is the final price?
Explanation: Find the discount: 30% of $80 is \(0.30 \times 80 = $24\). Subtract from original price: \($80 - $24 = $56\).
8. A $120 phone is on sale for 15% off. What is the final price?
Explanation: Find the discount: 15% of $120 is \(0.15 \times 120 = $18\). Subtract from original price: \($120 - $18 = $102\).
9. A $45 shirt is on sale for 20% off. What is the final price?
Explanation: Find the discount: 20% of $45 is \(0.20 \times 45 = $9\). Subtract from original price: \($45 - $9 = $36\).

Variations of Problem 13 (Cylinder Volume: \(V = \pi r^2 h\))

10. Find the volume of a cylinder with a radius of 4 cm and a height of 5 cm. (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\))
Explanation: \(V = 3.14 \times (4)^2 \times 5 = 3.14 \times 16 \times 5 = 3.14 \times 80 = 251.2\).
11. Find the volume of a cylinder with a radius of 2 in and a height of 12 in. (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\))
Explanation: \(V = 3.14 \times (2)^2 \times 12 = 3.14 \times 4 \times 12 = 3.14 \times 48 = 150.72\).
12. Find the volume of a cylinder with a radius of 5 m and a height of 8 m. (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\))
Explanation: \(V = 3.14 \times (5)^2 \times 8 = 3.14 \times 25 \times 8 = 3.14 \times 200 = 628\).

Variations of Problem 18 (Pounds to Ounces)

13. Convert 3.5 pounds to ounces.
Explanation: There are 16 ounces in 1 pound. Multiply: \(3.5 \times 16 = 56\).
14. Convert 2.25 pounds to ounces.
Explanation: Multiply by 16: \(2.25 \times 16 = 36\). (2 lbs is 32 oz, and 0.25 lb is 4 oz. \(32 + 4 = 36\)).
15. Convert 5.5 pounds to ounces.
Explanation: Multiply by 16: \(5.5 \times 16 = 88\).

Variations of Problem 20 (Elapsed Time)

16. A train leaves at 1:15 PM and arrives at 4:30 PM. How long is the trip?
Explanation: From 1:15 to 4:15 is exactly 3 hours. From 4:15 to 4:30 is 15 minutes. Total: 3 hours and 15 minutes.
17. A train leaves at 8:40 AM and arrives at 11:10 AM. How long is the trip?
Explanation: 8:40 AM to 9:00 AM is 20 minutes. 9:00 AM to 11:00 AM is 2 hours. 11:00 AM to 11:10 AM is 10 minutes. Total: 2 hours + 30 minutes.
18. A train leaves at 10:55 AM and arrives at 2:20 PM. How long is the trip?
Explanation: 10:55 AM to 11:00 AM is 5 minutes. 11:00 AM to 2:00 PM is 3 hours. 2:00 PM to 2:20 PM is 20 minutes. Total: 3 hours and 25 minutes.

Variations of Problem 24 (Evaluating Expressions)

19. Evaluate \(2x^2 + 4x\) when \(x = 4\).
Explanation: Substitute 4 for x: \(2(4)^2 + 4(4)\). Exponents first: \(4^2 = 16\). Multiply: \(2(16) + 16 = 32 + 16 = 48\).
20. Evaluate \(5x^2 - 3x\) when \(x = 2\).
Explanation: Substitute 2 for x: \(5(2)^2 - 3(2)\). Exponents first: \(2^2 = 4\). Multiply: \(5(4) - 6 = 20 - 6 = 14\).
21. Evaluate \(x^2 - 7x\) when \(x = 5\).
Explanation: Substitute 5 for x: \((5)^2 - 7(5)\). Exponents first: \(25\). Multiply: \(25 - 35 = -10\).

Variations of Problem 26 (Least Common Multiple)

22. What is the Least Common Multiple (LCM) of 8 and 12?
Explanation: Multiples of 12: 12, 24, 36... Since 8 goes evenly into 24 (\(8 \times 3 = 24\)), 24 is the LCM. (Note: 4 is the Greatest Common Factor, not the LCM).
23. What is the Least Common Multiple (LCM) of 14 and 21?
Explanation: Multiples of 21: 21, 42, 63... Since 14 goes evenly into 42 (\(14 \times 3 = 42\)), 42 is the LCM.
24. What is the Least Common Multiple (LCM) of 9 and 15?
Explanation: Multiples of 15: 15, 30, 45, 60... Since 9 goes evenly into 45 (\(9 \times 5 = 45\)), 45 is the LCM.

Variations of Problem 30 (Probability)

25. If you roll a fair 6-sided die, what is the probability of getting an even number?
Explanation: The even numbers on a die are 2, 4, and 6. That is 3 successful outcomes out of 6 possible outcomes. \(\frac{3}{6}\) simplifies to \(\frac{1}{2}\).
26. If you draw one card from a standard 52-card deck, what is the probability of drawing a heart?
Explanation: There are 4 suits in a deck (Hearts, Diamonds, Clubs, Spades), all equal in number. Therefore, 13 out of 52 cards are hearts. \(\frac{13}{52}\) simplifies to \(\frac{1}{4}\).
27. If you flip a fair coin 3 times, what is the probability of getting heads all three times?
Explanation: The probability of heads on each flip is \(\frac{1}{2}\). Multiply the independent probabilities: \(\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{8}\).
