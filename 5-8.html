Saxon Math Course 1 - Analogous Solutions

Lesson Practice

a. Find the sum: $5\text{ ft } 9\text{ in} + 3\text{ ft } 6\text{ in}$
Answer: $9\text{ ft } 3\text{ in}$

Explanation: First, add the feet and inches separately:
$5\text{ ft} + 3\text{ ft} = 8\text{ ft}$
$9\text{ in} + 6\text{ in} = 15\text{ in}$
Since $12\text{ inches} = 1\text{ foot}$, convert $15\text{ inches}$ into $1\text{ foot } 3\text{ inches}$.
Total: $8\text{ ft} + 1\text{ ft } 3\text{ in} = 9\text{ ft } 3\text{ in}$.
b. Find the difference: $4\text{ hr } 20\text{ min} - 2\text{ hr } 50\text{ min}$
Answer: $1\text{ hr } 30\text{ min}$

Explanation: You cannot subtract 50 min from 20 min, so borrow 1 hour (60 minutes) from the 4 hours.
$4\text{ hr } 20\text{ min}$ becomes $3\text{ hr } 80\text{ min}$.
Now subtract: $(3\text{ hr} - 2\text{ hr}) = 1\text{ hr}$, and $(80\text{ min} - 50\text{ min}) = 30\text{ min}$.
c. Write in standard notation: $3.4 \times 10^5$
Answer: 340,000

Explanation: Multiplying by $10^5$ shifts the decimal point 5 places to the right. $3.4 \rightarrow 34 \rightarrow 340 \rightarrow 3400 \rightarrow 34000 \rightarrow 340000$.
d. Write in standard notation: 2.8 million
Answer: 2,800,000

Explanation: "Million" means multiplying by 1,000,000.
$2.8 \times 1,000,000 = 2,800,000$.
e. Write in standard notation: $1 \frac{1}{4}$ billion
Answer: 1,250,000,000

Explanation: Change the fraction to a decimal: 1.25. Multiply by 1 billion (1,000,000,000).
$1.25 \times 1,000,000,000 = 1,250,000,000$.
f. Write in standard notation: $\frac{3}{4}$ million
Answer: 750,000

Explanation: Convert $\frac{3}{4}$ to the decimal 0.75. Multiply by 1,000,000.
$0.75 \times 1,000,000 = 750,000$.

Written Practice

1. For helping with a neighborhood bake sale, five students were given a total of $90.00. If they divide the money equally, how much money will each student receive?
Answer: $18.00

Explanation: Divide the total money by the number of students:
$90.00 ÷ 5 = $18.00.
2. Which of the following is the best estimate of the length of a standard family car?
A. 0.5 m    B. 1.5 m    C. 4.5 m    D. 15 m
Answer: C. 4.5 m

Explanation: 0.5 m and 1.5 m are too short (the size of a box or a bicycle). 15 m is too long (the size of a bus). 4.5 meters (approx 14.7 feet) is a realistic length for a standard car.
3. If the chance of snow is 70%, what is the probability that it will not snow? Express the answer as a decimal.
Answer: 0.3

Explanation: The total probability of all outcomes is 100%.
Chance of no snow = 100% - 70% = 30%.
To express 30% as a decimal, move the decimal point two places left: 0.30 or 0.3.
4. The ratio of boys to girls in the school choir is 4 to 3. If there are 140 students in total, how many boys are in the choir?
Answer: 80

Explanation: The ratio is 4 (boys) : 3 (girls). Total ratio parts = $4 + 3 = 7$.
Find the value of one part: $140 \div 7 = 20$ students per part.
Number of boys = $4\text{ parts} \times 20 = 80$ boys.
5. Write $7.2 \times 10^5$ as a standard numeral.
Answer: 720,000

Explanation: Move the decimal point 5 places to the right: $7.2 \rightarrow 720000$.
6. Calculate mentally:
a. $(-15)(+4)$    b. $(-15)(-4)$    c. $\frac{-15}{+3}$    d. $\frac{-15}{-3}$
Answer:
a. -60
b. 60
c. -5
d. 5

Explanation:
When multiplying or dividing:
- Different signs yield a negative result.
- Like signs yield a positive result.
7. Calculate mentally:
a. $-10 + -5$    b. $-10 - -5$    c. $+5 + -10$    d. $+5 - -10$
Answer:
a. -15
b. -5
c. -5
d. 15

Explanation:
a. Adding two negatives makes a larger negative.
b. Subtracting a negative is adding a positive: $-10 + 5 = -5$.
c. Positive plus negative: subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger, keep sign of the larger.
d. Subtracting a negative is adding a positive: $5 + 10 = 15$.
8. Describe a method for arranging these fractions from least to greatest: $\frac{2}{3}, \frac{5}{6}, \frac{3}{4}$. Then arrange them.
Answer: Least to greatest: $\frac{2}{3}, \frac{3}{4}, \frac{5}{6}$

Explanation: Find a common denominator. For 3, 6, and 4, the least common multiple (LCM) is 12.
$\frac{2}{3} = \frac{8}{12}$
$\frac{5}{6} = \frac{10}{12}$
$\frac{3}{4} = \frac{9}{12}$
Comparing numerators: $8 < 9 < 10$, thus $\frac{2}{3} < \frac{3}{4} < \frac{5}{6}$.

13. $2 \frac{1}{2}\text{ ft} \times 1 \frac{1}{5}\text{ ft}$
Answer: $3\text{ sq ft}$

Explanation: Convert to improper fractions:
$\frac{5}{2} \times \frac{6}{5} = \frac{30}{10}$.
Simplify: $\frac{30}{10} = 3$.
14. $(4\text{ cm})(4\text{ cm})(4\text{ cm})$
Answer: $64\text{ cm}^3$

Explanation: Multiply the numbers: $4 \times 4 \times 4 = 64$.
Multiply the units: $\text{cm} \times \text{cm} \times \text{cm} = \text{cm}^3$.
15. $0.8\text{ m} \times 0.4\text{ m}$
Answer: $0.32\text{ m}^2$

Explanation: $0.8 \times 0.4 = 0.32$.
The units become $\text{m}^2$.
16. $4^3 + 3^2$
Answer: 73

Explanation: Evaluate the exponents first:
$4^3 = 4 \times 4 \times 4 = 64$
$3^2 = 3 \times 3 = 9$
Add them: $64 + 9 = 73$.
17. Find the area of this trapezoid. Show and explain your work to justify your answer.
(Top base = 6 ft, Bottom base = 12 ft, Height = 5 ft)
Answer: 45 sq ft

Explanation: The formula for the area of a trapezoid is $A = \frac{1}{2}(b_1 + b_2)h$.
Substitute the given values: $A = \frac{1}{2}(12 + 6) \times 5$.
$A = \frac{1}{2}(18) \times 5 = 9 \times 5 = 45$.
18. $4\text{ feet } 2\text{ inches} - 2\text{ feet } 8\text{ inches}$
Answer: $1\text{ foot } 6\text{ inches}$

Explanation: You cannot subtract 8 in from 2 in, so borrow 1 foot (12 inches) from the 4 feet.
$4\text{ ft } 2\text{ in}$ becomes $3\text{ ft } 14\text{ in}$.
Subtract: $(3\text{ ft} - 2\text{ ft}) = 1\text{ ft}$. $(14\text{ in} - 8\text{ in}) = 6\text{ inches}$.
19. Consider a capital letter 'E' drawn with a vertical line on the left and three horizontal bars.
a. Does a horizontal line passing straight through the middle bar act as a line of symmetry?
b. Does the letter 'E' have rotational symmetry? Explain your answer.
Answer:
a. Yes, it is a line of symmetry.
b. No, it does not have rotational symmetry.

Explanation:
a. A line of symmetry divides a figure into two identical halves that are mirror images. Folding the top half of the 'E' over the middle horizontal line perfectly covers the bottom half.
b. If you rotate the letter 'E' $180^\circ$ around its center, it faces backward (like a '3'). It requires a full $360^\circ$ rotation to look identical again, so it lacks rotational symmetry.
20. How many cubes one centimeter on each edge would be needed to fill this box?
(Dimensions: 6 cm × 4 cm × 3 cm)
Answer: 72 cubes

Explanation: Find the volume of the rectangular prism: $V = \text{length} \times \text{width} \times \text{height}$.
$V = 6 \times 4 \times 3 = 72\text{ cm}^3$.
Since each cube is $1\text{ cm}^3$, exactly 72 cubes are needed.
21. Marcus worked for five days and earned $350. At that rate, how much would he earn in seven days?
Answer: $490

Explanation: First, find his daily rate by dividing the total earned by the number of days:
$350 \div 5\text{ days} = \$70\text{ per day}$.
Then multiply the daily rate by 7 days: $\$70 \times 7 = \$490$.
22. One hundred five (105) is the product of which three prime numbers?
Answer: 3, 5, and 7

Explanation: Find the prime factorization of 105.
Since it ends in 5, $105 = 5 \times 21$.
$21 = 3 \times 7$.
So, $105 = 3 \times 5 \times 7$. All three are prime.
23. Jupiter is about 480 million miles from the Sun. Write that distance in standard notation.
Answer: 480,000,000 miles

Explanation: "Million" signifies 6 zeros after the number, or multiplying by 1,000,000.
$480 \times 1,000,000 = 480,000,000$.
24. Use a rhombus with Side = 12 in, Height = 10 in, and an acute angle of $45^\circ$ for problems a-c.
a. What is the perimeter of this rhombus?
b. What is the area of this rhombus?
c. What is the measure of each obtuse angle in this rhombus?
Answer:
a. 48 in.
b. 120 sq in.
c. $135^\circ$

Explanation:
a. A rhombus has 4 equal sides. $P = 4 \times 12\text{ in} = 48\text{ in.}$
b. Area of a rhombus is base × height. $A = 12\text{ in} \times 10\text{ in} = 120\text{ sq in.}$
c. Consecutive angles in a rhombus are supplementary (add up to $180^\circ$). Obtuse angle = $180^\circ - 45^\circ = 135^\circ$.
25. The ratio of dimes to nickels in a vending machine is 3 to 7. If there are 90 dimes, how many nickels are there?
Answer: 210 nickels

Explanation: Set up a proportion: $\frac{\text{Dimes}}{\text{Nickels}} = \frac{3}{7} = \frac{90}{x}$.
Since $3 \times 30 = 90$, multiply the denominator by the same factor: $7 \times 30 = 210$.
26. a. The coordinates of the three vertices of a right triangle are (0, 0), (6, 0), and (6, 8). What is the area of the triangle?
b. If the triangle were reflected across the x-axis, what would be the coordinates of the vertices of the reflection?
Answer:
a. 24 square units
b. (0, 0), (6, 0), and (6, -8)

Explanation:
a. Plotting the points reveals a right triangle with a base of 6 units and a height of 8 units. Area = $\frac{1}{2}bh = \frac{1}{2}(6)(8) = 24$.
b. Reflecting across the x-axis negates the y-coordinates: $(x, y) \rightarrow (x, -y)$. The points on the x-axis stay the same.
27. What is the median of this data set?
(Data: 15, 10, 8, 12, 12, 14, 10, 15)
Answer: 12

Explanation: First, list the 8 numbers in order from least to greatest:
8, 10, 10, 12, 12, 14, 15, 15.
Since there is an even number of values, the median is the average of the 4th and 5th middle values: $\frac{12 + 12}{2} = 12$.
28. What is the mean of this data set?
(Data: 15, 10, 8, 12, 12, 14, 10, 15)
Answer: 12

Explanation: The mean is the sum of all values divided by the number of values.
Sum = $15+10+8+12+12+14+10+15 = 96$.
Mean = $96 \div 8 = 12$.
29. The diameter of a circular clock face is 20 cm. What is the circumference of the clock? (Use 3.14 for $\pi$.) How can estimation help you determine if your answer is reasonable?
Answer: 62.8 cm

Explanation: Circumference formula: $C = \pi \times d$.
$C = 3.14 \times 20 = 62.8\text{ cm}$.
Estimation: Since $\pi$ is approximately 3, the circumference should be roughly $3 \times 20 = 60$. Since 62.8 is close to 60, the answer is reasonable.
30. Find the value of $V$ in $V = s^3$ when $s$ is 4 cm.
Answer: $64\text{ cm}^3$

Explanation: Substitute 4 for $s$ in the formula:
$V = (4\text{ cm})^3$
$V = 4\text{ cm} \times 4\text{ cm} \times 4\text{ cm} = 64\text{ cm}^3$.
