Exercise h
The number $7000$ is divisible by which single-digit numbers?
Answer:

1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 8

Step-by-Step Explanation:

Test each single-digit number ($1$ to $9$):

  • 1: All whole numbers are divisible by 1. ($\checkmark$)
  • 2: Ends in $0$ (even number). ($\checkmark$)
  • 3: Sum of digits is $7 + 0 + 0 + 0 = 7$, which is not divisible by 3. ($\times$)
  • 4: Last two digits are $00$, $7000 \div 4 = 1750$. ($\checkmark$)
  • 5: Ends in $0$. ($\checkmark$)
  • 6: Must be divisible by both 2 and 3. Since it is not divisible by 3, it is not divisible by 6. ($\times$)
  • 7: $7000 \div 7 = 1000$. ($\checkmark$)
  • 8: $7000 \div 8 = 875$. ($\checkmark$)
  • 9: Sum of digits is $7$, which is not divisible by 9. ($\times$)

Exercise j
Find the greatest common factor (GCF) of $24$ and $40$.
Answer:

8

Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Factors of $24$: $1, 2, 3, 4, 6, \mathbf{8}, 12, 24$
  • Factors of $40$: $1, 2, 4, 5, \mathbf{8}, 10, 20, 40$

Common factors: $1, 2, 4, 8$. The largest factor common to both is 8.

Answer:

4

Step-by-Step Explanation:

29a. Analyze
Name the property illustrated by the following equation: $$x \cdot 5 = 5x$$
Answer:

Commutative Property of Multiplication

Step-by-Step Explanation:

The equation shows that changing the order of factors ($x \cdot 5$ to $5 \cdot x$) does not alter the product.

29b. Summarize
In your own words explain the meaning of this property.
Sample Explanation:

The Commutative Property of Multiplication means that the order in which two numbers or variables are multiplied does not change the final product. For any numbers $a$ and $b$, $a \cdot b = b \cdot a$.

30. Justify
List the properties used in the first three steps to simplify the expression $(8 \times 7) \times 5$. $$\begin{aligned} &(8 \times 7) \times 5 && \text{Given expression} \\ &8 \times (7 \times 5) && \mathbf{a.\ \text{\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_}} \\ &8 \times (5 \times 7) && \mathbf{b.\ \text{\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_}} \\ &(8 \times 5) \times 7 && \mathbf{c.\ \text{\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_}} \\ &40 \times 7 && 8 \times 5 = 40 \\ &280 && 40 \times 7 = 280 \end{aligned}$$
Answers:
  • a. Associative Property of Multiplication (regrouping factors from $(8 \times 7) \times 5$ to $8 \times (7 \times 5)$)
  • b. Commutative Property of Multiplication (reordering factors inside parentheses from $(7 \times 5)$ to $(5 \times 7)$)
  • c. Associative Property of Multiplication (regrouping factors to $(8 \times 5) \times 7$)
