1.(28)
EVALUATE Ms. Gonzalez's class and Mr. O'Brien's class are going to use the money they raise at the school raffle to buy several new maps for their classrooms. The maps cost \($89.89\). Ms. Gonzalez's class has raised \($26.47\). Mr. O'Brien's class has raised \($32.54\). How much more money do they need to raise to buy the maps?
Answer
\(\$30.88\)
Solution
Together they have raised \(\$26.47 + \$32.54 = \$59.01\). The maps cost \(\$89.89\), so they still need \(\$89.89 - \$59.01 = \$30.88\).
2.(28)
Norton read 4 books during his vacation. The first book had 326 pages, the second had 288 pages, the third had 349 pages, and the fourth had 401 pages. The 4 books he read had an average of how many pages per book?
Answer
\(341\text{ pages}\)
Solution
Total pages: \(326 + 288 + 349 + 401 = 1364\). Average: \(1364 \div 4 = 341\) pages per book.

5.(19)
EXPLAIN A square and a regular hexagon share a common side. The perimeter of the square is 24 in. Describe how to find the perimeter of the hexagon.
Answer
\(36\text{ in.}\)
Solution
First find the shared side: the square's perimeter is 24 in. and a square has 4 equal sides, so each side is \(24 \div 4 = 6\) in. The hexagon shares that side, and a regular hexagon has 6 equal sides, so its perimeter is \(6 \times 6 = 36\) in.
6.(22)
In the following statement, change the percent to a reduced fraction and answer the questions.
Kelly bought a book of 20 stamps. She has used 80% of them.
a. How many stamps has Kelly used?
b. How many stamps does she have left?
Answer
\(\text{a. }16\text{ stamps} \qquad \text{b. }4\text{ stamps}\)
Solution
80% as a reduced fraction is \(\frac{80}{100} = \frac{4}{5}\). a. \(\frac{4}{5}\) of 20 is \(20 \div 5 \times 4 = 16\) stamps used. b. \(20 - 16 = 4\) stamps left.

13.(15)
Complete each equivalent fraction:
a. \(\frac{5}{?} = \frac{15}{24}\)   b. \(\frac{7}{12} = \frac{?}{24}\)   c. \(\frac{?}{6} = \frac{4}{24}\)
Answer
\(\text{a. }8 \qquad \text{b. }14 \qquad \text{c. }1\)
Solution
a. \(\frac{15}{24}\) reduces by 3 to \(\frac{5}{8}\), so the missing denominator is 8. b. Multiplying numerator and denominator of \(\frac{7}{12}\) by 2 gives \(\frac{14}{24}\). c. \(\frac{4}{24} = \frac{1}{6}\), so the missing numerator is 1.

15.(18)
Draw two parallel lines. Then draw two more parallel lines that are perpendicular to the first pair of lines. Label the points of intersection A, B, C, and D consecutively in a counterclockwise direction. Draw segment AC. Refer to the figure to answer a and b.
a. What kind of quadrilateral is figure ABCD?
b. Triangles ABC and CDA are congruent. Which angle in \(\triangle ABC\) corresponds to \(\angle DAC\) in \(\triangle CDA\)?

\(\text{a. rectangle} \qquad \text{b. }\angle BCA\)

a. The figure has four right angles and opposite sides parallel and equal, so it is a rectangle. b. In the correspondence ABC ↔ CDA, vertex A of \(\triangle CDA\) matches vertex C of \(\triangle ABC\), so \(\angle DAC\) corresponds to \(\angle BCA\).
16.(14)
Twelve red marbles and 15 blue marbles are in a bag. If one marble is drawn from the bag, what is the probability the marble is not red?
Answer
\(\frac{5}{9}\)
Solution
There are \(12 + 15 = 27\) marbles in all. The marbles that are not red are the 15 blue ones, so the probability is \(\frac{15}{27} = \frac{5}{9}\).
17.(2, 9)
Name the properties used to solve this equation.
\(\frac{4}{3}\left(\frac{3}{4}x\right) = \frac{4}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{4}\)   Given
\(\left(\frac{4}{3} \cdot \frac{3}{4}\right)x = \frac{4}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{4}\)   a. ______________
\(1x = \frac{4}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{4}\)   b. ______________
\(x = \frac{4}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{4}\)   c. ______________
\(x = \frac{1}{3}\)
Answer
\(\text{a. Associative Property of Multiplication} \quad \text{b. Inverse Property of Multiplication} \quad \text{c. Identity Property of Multiplication}\)
Solution
a. The factors were regrouped — \(\frac{4}{3}\) and \(\frac{3}{4}\) were grouped together — so this is the Associative Property of Multiplication. b. \(\frac{4}{3} \cdot \frac{3}{4} = 1\) because the factors are reciprocals, so this is the Inverse Property of Multiplication. c. \(1x = x\), so this is the Identity Property of Multiplication. (Then \(x = \frac{4}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{3}\).)

29.(4, 20)
The rule of the following sequence is \(k = 10^n\). Use words to name the sixth term.
\(10, 100, 1{,}000, \ldots\)
Answer
\(\text{one million}\)
Solution
The terms are \(10^1 = 10\), \(10^2 = 100\), \(10^3 = 1{,}000\), so the sixth term is \(10^6 = 1{,}000{,}000\), which in words is one million.
30.(17)
a. A half circle or half turn measures how many degrees?
b. A quarter of a circle measures how many degrees?
c. An eighth of a circle measures how many degrees?
Answer
\(\text{a. }180^\circ \qquad \text{b. }90^\circ \qquad \text{c. }45^\circ\)
Solution
A full circle is \(360^\circ\). a. Half: \(360^\circ \div 2 = 180^\circ\). b. Quarter: \(360^\circ \div 4 = 90^\circ\). c. Eighth: \(360^\circ \div 8 = 45^\circ\).
