Math Course Solutions

Lesson Practice

a. Ninety students were assigned to four classrooms as equally as possible. How many students were in each of the four classrooms?

Answer: Two classrooms had 22 students, and two classrooms had 23 students.

Explanation: Divide the total number of students by the number of classrooms: $90 \div 4 = 22$ with a remainder of $2$. To distribute them as equally as possible, you give 22 students to all four classrooms, and the 2 leftover students are distributed to two of the classrooms, making them 23.

b. Movie tickets cost $9.50. Aluna has $30.00. How many movie tickets can she buy?

Answer: 3 tickets.

Explanation: Divide Aluna's total money by the cost per ticket: $30.00 \div 9.50 \approx 3.15$. Since she cannot buy a fraction of a ticket, she can only afford 3 whole tickets.

c. Twenty-eight children need a ride to the fair. Each van can carry six children. How many vans are needed?

Answer: 5 vans.

Explanation: Divide the children by the van capacity: $28 \div 6 = 4$ with a remainder of $4$. Four vans will hold 24 children. Because 4 children still need a ride, you need a 5th van.

d. Corinne folded an $8\frac{1}{2}$ in. by 11 in. piece of paper in half. Then she folded the paper in half again as shown. After the two folds, what are the dimensions of the rectangle that is formed?

Answer: $4\frac{1}{4}$ in. by $5\frac{1}{2}$ in.

Explanation: The first fold cuts the 11 in. side in half, making the paper $8\frac{1}{2}$ in. by $5\frac{1}{2}$ in. The second fold cuts the $8\frac{1}{2}$ in. side in half ($8.5 \div 2 = 4.25$). So the final dimensions are $4\frac{1}{4}$ in. by $5\frac{1}{2}$ in.

e. Kevin ordered four books at the book fair for summer reading. The books cost $6.95, $7.95, $6.45, and $8.85. Find the average (mean) price of the books.

Answer: $7.55

Explanation: First, sum the prices: $\$6.95 + \$7.95 + \$6.45 + \$8.85 = \$30.20$. Then, divide by the number of books (4) to find the mean: $\$30.20 \div 4 = \$7.55$.

Written Practice

1. Eighty students will be assigned to three classrooms. How many students should be in each class so that the numbers are as balanced as possible? (Write the numbers.)

Answer: 27, 27, 26

Explanation: Divide 80 by 3: $80 \div 3 = 26$ with a remainder of $2$. Base number is 26. The two remaining students go into two of the classes. Therefore, the classes will have 27, 27, and 26 students.

2. Four friends went out to lunch. Their bill was $45. If the friends divide the bill equally, how much will each friend pay?

Answer: $11.25

Explanation: Divide the total bill by 4: $\$45.00 \div 4 = \$11.25$.

3. Shauna bought a sheet of 39¢ stamps at the post office for $15.60. How many stamps were in the sheet?

Answer: 40 stamps

Explanation: Divide the total cost by the cost per stamp. First, align the units (15.60 dollars $\div$ 0.39 dollars): $15.60 \div 0.39 = 40$.

4. Eight cubes were used to build this 2-by-2-by-2 cube. How many cubes are needed to build a cube that has three cubes along each edge?

Answer: 27 cubes

Explanation: To find the total number of smaller cubes in a larger 3-by-3-by-3 cube, find the volume by multiplying the lengths of the edges: $3 \times 3 \times 3 = 27$.

5. Write the standard notation for the following: $(5 \times 10^3) + (4 \times 10^1) + (3 \times 10^0)$

Answer: 5,043

Explanation: Expand each term: $5 \times 1000 = 5000$. $4 \times 10 = 40$. $3 \times 1 = 3$. Add them together: $5000 + 40 + 3 = 5043$. (Notice there is no $10^2$ term, so there is a 0 in the hundreds place).

6. Use a centimeter ruler and an inch ruler to answer this question. Twelve inches is closest to how many centimeters? Round the answer to the nearest centimeter.

Answer: 30 cm

Explanation: One inch is exactly 2.54 centimeters. $12 \text{ inches} \times 2.54 \text{ cm/in} = 30.48 \text{ cm}$. Rounded to the nearest whole centimeter, this is 30 cm.

7. Create a scale drawing of a room at home or of your classroom. Choose a scale that allows the drawings to fit on one sheet of paper. Write the legend on the scale drawing.

Answer: Answers will vary.

Explanation: This is a hands-on exercise. Measure the length and width of a room. Choose a scale, for example: $1 \text{ inch} = 2 \text{ feet}$. Divide your actual room measurements by 2 to get the drawing measurements in inches. Draw the rectangle and label your legend "$1 \text{ in.} = 2 \text{ ft.}$".

8. If two angles of a triangle measure $70^\circ$ and $80^\circ$, then what is the measure of the third angle?

Answer: $30^\circ$

Explanation: The sum of the interior angles of any triangle is always $180^\circ$. Add the known angles: $70^\circ + 80^\circ = 150^\circ$. Subtract from the total: $180^\circ - 150^\circ = 30^\circ$.

9. Complete the table: Fraction $11/20$ -> a. Decimal? b. Percent?

Answer: a. $0.55$ b. $55\%$

Explanation: Multiply numerator and denominator by 5 to get a fraction out of 100: $\frac{11 \times 5}{20 \times 5} = \frac{55}{100}$. As a decimal, this is 0.55. As a percent, it's 55%.

10. Complete the table: Decimal $1.5$ -> a. Fraction? b. Percent?

Answer: a. $1 \frac{1}{2}$ (or $\frac{3}{2}$) b. $150\%$

Explanation: 1.5 read aloud is "one and five tenths", which is $1 \frac{5}{10}$, simplified to $1 \frac{1}{2}$. To convert a decimal to a percent, shift the decimal two places to the right: $1.5 \rightarrow 150\%$.

11. Complete the table: Percent $1\%$ -> a. Fraction? b. Decimal?

Answer: a. $\frac{1}{100}$ b. $0.01$

Explanation: Percent means "out of 100", so 1% is $\frac{1}{100}$. As a decimal, one hundredth is written as 0.01.

12. Calculate mentally: a. $-6 + -12$ b. $-6 - -12$ c. $-12 + +6$ d. $-12 - +6$

Answer: a. $-18$ b. $6$ c. $-6$ d. $-18$

Explanation:
a. Adding two negatives: keep the sign and add: $-6 - 12 = -18$.
b. Subtracting a negative is adding a positive: $-6 + 12 = 6$.
c. Adding a positive to a larger negative: subtract absolute values and keep the negative sign: $-12 + 6 = -6$.
d. Subtracting a positive is adding a negative: $-12 - 6 = -18$.

13. $6\frac{1}{4} \div 100$

Answer: $\frac{1}{16}$ or $0.0625$

Explanation: Convert $6\frac{1}{4}$ to an improper fraction: $\frac{25}{4}$. Dividing by 100 is multiplying by $\frac{1}{100}$. So, $\frac{25}{4} \times \frac{1}{100} = \frac{25}{400}$. Divide top and bottom by 25 to simplify: $\frac{1}{16}$.

14. $0.3m = \$4.41$

Answer: $m = \$14.70$

Explanation: Isolate $m$ by dividing both sides by 0.3: $m = 4.41 \div 0.3$. Move the decimal point one place to the right in both numbers: $44.1 \div 3 = 14.7$. In monetary format, this is $\$14.70$.

15. Kim scored 15 points, which was $30\%$ of the team's total. How many points did the team score in all?

Answer: 50 points

Explanation: Let $T$ be the total points. We know $0.30 \times T = 15$. Divide both sides by 0.30: $T = 15 \div 0.30$. Move decimals two places: $1500 \div 30 = 50$.

16. Andrea received the following scores in a gymnastic event: 6.7, 7.6, 6.6, 6.7, 6.5, 6.7, 6.8. The highest score and the lowest score are not counted. What is the average of the remaining scores?

Answer: 6.7

Explanation: The highest score is 7.6 and the lowest is 6.5. Remove them. The remaining 5 scores are 6.7, 6.6, 6.7, 6.7, 6.8. Add them: $6.7 + 6.6 + 6.7 + 6.7 + 6.8 = 33.5$. Divide by 5: $33.5 \div 5 = 6.7$.

17. Refer to problem 16 to write a comparison question about the scores Andrea received from the judges. Then answer the question.

Answer: Example Question: "What is the difference between Andrea's highest score and her lowest score?" Answer: 1.1

Explanation: Highest score (7.6) minus lowest score (6.5) equals $1.1$.

18. What is the area of the quadrilateral below? (A trapezoid with height 5m, top base 5m, bottom base 11m)

Answer: $40 \text{ m}^2$

Explanation: The formula for the area of a trapezoid is $A = \frac{1}{2}(b_1 + b_2)h$. Substitute the values: $A = \frac{1}{2}(5 + 11) \times 5 = \frac{1}{2}(16) \times 5 = 8 \times 5 = 40$.

19. What is the ratio of vertices to edges on a pyramid with a square base?

Answer: $5 : 8$ or $\frac{5}{8}$

Explanation: A square pyramid has 5 vertices (4 around the square base + 1 at the top apex). It has 8 edges (4 forming the square base + 4 ascending to the apex). The ratio is 5 to 8.

20. Line $r$ is called a line of symmetry because it divides the equilateral triangle into two mirror images. Which other line is also a line of symmetry?

Answer: Lines $s$ and $t$

Explanation: In an equilateral triangle, a line of symmetry can be drawn from any vertex to the midpoint of the opposite side. Lines $s$ and $t$ also bisect the triangle into mirror images.

21. Solve and check: $3m + 1 = 100$

Answer: $m = 33$

Explanation: Subtract 1 from both sides: $3m = 99$. Divide by 3: $m = 33$. Check: $3(33) + 1 = 99 + 1 = 100$.

22. Write the prime factorization of 600 using exponents.

Answer: $2^3 \times 3 \times 5^2$

Explanation: Break 600 down into prime factors: $600 = 6 \times 100$. $6 = 2 \times 3$. $100 = 10 \times 10 = (2 \times 5) \times (2 \times 5)$. Combine them all: $2 \times 3 \times 2 \times 5 \times 2 \times 5$. Count the occurrences: three 2s, one 3, and two 5s. That gives $2^3 \times 3^1 \times 5^2$.

23. You need to make a three-dimensional model of a soup can using paper and tape. What 3 two-dimensional shapes do you need to cut to make the model?

Answer: Two circles and one rectangle.

Explanation: A soup can is a cylinder. The top and bottom are circles. When the side of a cylinder is unrolled and flattened, it forms a rectangle.

24. The price of an item is 89¢. The sales-tax rate is 7%. What is the total for the item, including tax?

Answer: 95¢ or $0.95

Explanation: Calculate the tax: $0.89 \times 0.07 = 0.0623$. Round the tax to the nearest cent: $0.06$ (or 6¢). Add tax to the original price: $0.89 + 0.06 = 0.95$.

25. The probability of winning a prize in the drawing is one in a million. What is the probability of not winning a prize in the drawing?

Answer: $\frac{999,999}{1,000,000}$

Explanation: The sum of the probability of an event happening and not happening is 1. If winning is $\frac{1}{1,000,000}$, then not winning is $1 - \frac{1}{1,000,000} = \frac{999,999}{1,000,000}$.

26. Which angle in triangle $ABC$ corresponds to angle $D$ in triangle $CDA$?

Answer: Angle $B$

Explanation: Congruence statements list corresponding vertices in order. If $\Delta ABC \cong \Delta CDA$, then vertex $B$ (the middle letter) corresponds to vertex $D$ (the middle letter). Geometrically, they are the opposite right angles in the rectangle formed by the two triangles.

27. Which transformations would position triangle $CDA$ on triangle $ABC$?

Answer: A 180-degree rotation (or a half-turn) about the midpoint of the diagonal $AC$.

Explanation: To map $D$ to $B$, $C$ to $A$, and $A$ to $C$, you rotate the entire triangle 180 degrees around the center point of the shared side $AC$.

28. Malik used a compass to draw a circle with a radius of 5 centimeters. What was the circumference of the circle? (Use 3.14 for $\pi$)

Answer: 31.4 cm

Explanation: The formula for the circumference of a circle is $C = 2\pi r$. Substitute the given values: $C = 2 \times 3.14 \times 5 = 10 \times 3.14 = 31.4$.

29. Solve this proportion: $\frac{10}{16} = \frac{25}{y}$

Answer: $y = 40$

Explanation: Use cross-multiplication: $10 \times y = 16 \times 25$. This gives $10y = 400$. Divide by 10: $y = 40$.

30. Find the value of $d$ in $d = rt$ when $r$ is $50 \frac{\text{mi}}{\text{hr}}$ and $t$ is $5 \text{ hr}$.

Answer: $250 \text{ mi}$

Explanation: Substitute the given values into the formula: $d = 50 \times 5$. Therefore, $d = 250$. The hours units cancel out, leaving the distance in miles.
