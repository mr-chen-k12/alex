🌈 How & Why We See Colors

How we see color: A prism scatters visible white light into bands of different colors by separating light according to wavelength (longest to shortest).


Why we see color: When light shines on an object, the color you see is the light wavelength that the object reflected instead of absorbed.

🏜️ How a Mirage is Formed

Air masses of different temperatures sit on top of each other just above hot desert sand. Light rays are bent (refracted) in a way that places the image well above the actual position of the object!

🌌 Why the Sky is Blue

Atmospheric particles reflecting energy in all directions scatter the light. Clear sky looks blue because out of all colors in visible light, blue is the shortest wavelength that scatters the most!

🕶️ Items That Can Be Polarized

Polarizing restricts light wave oscillations into a single direction. Items include:

  • 📷 Camera Films
  • 🕶️ Sunglasses
  • 🚗 Car Windows

📊 Science Variables & Methods

  • Independent Variable: The factor deliberately adjusted or changed in an experiment.
  • Dependent Variable: The factor measured to observe the effect.
  • Turbidity: A measure of how clear water is.
