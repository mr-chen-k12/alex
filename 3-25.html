Math Practice Problems: Set 2

1. Connect: Complete the table by finding the decimal and percent for $\frac{2}{5}$.

Answer: Decimal: $0.4$ | Percent: $40\%$

Explanation: $\frac{2}{5} = 2 \div 5 = 0.4$. To convert $0.4$ to a percent, multiply by $100$ to get $40\%$.

2. A 2-foot piece of string is cut into $1 \frac{1}{2}$-inch pieces. How many pieces of string will there be?

Answer: 16 pieces

Explanation: Convert 2 feet into inches: $2 \times 12 = 24 \text{ inches}$. Divide the total length by the cut length: $24 \div 1 \frac{1}{2}$. Convert to an improper fraction: $24 \div \frac{3}{2} = 24 \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{48}{3} = 16$.

3. A standard basketball is the shape of what geometric solid?

Answer: Sphere

Explanation: A basketball is perfectly round in 3D space, which is the definition of a sphere.

4. If $\frac{1}{6}$ of the students voted for candidate A and $\frac{2}{6}$ voted for candidate B, what fraction of the students did not vote for either?

Answer: $\frac{1}{2}$

Explanation: Add the fractions of voters: $\frac{1}{6} + \frac{2}{6} = \frac{3}{6}$. Subtract from the whole ($1$ or $\frac{6}{6}$): $\frac{6}{6} - \frac{3}{6} = \frac{3}{6}$. Simplify the fraction to $\frac{1}{2}$.

5. Eight months is
a. what fraction of a year?
b. what percent of a year?

Answer: a. $\frac{2}{3}$ | b. $66 \frac{2}{3}\%$

Explanation: There are 12 months in a year.
a. $\frac{8}{12}$ simplifies to $\frac{2}{3}$.
b. $\frac{2}{3}$ as a percent is $66.6\overline{6}\%$ or $66 \frac{2}{3}\%$.

6. Boxes measuring 1 cubic foot are stacked 5 boxes wide, 2 boxes deep, and 4 boxes high. What is the total volume of the stack?

Answer: 40 cubic feet

Explanation: Volume = length $\times$ width $\times$ height = $5 \times 2 \times 4 = 40$. Since each box is 1 cubic foot, the volume is $40 \text{ cubic feet}$.

7. Maria was facing south. Then she turned clockwise $270^\circ$. After the turn, what direction was she facing?

Answer: East

Explanation: Starting South, turning clockwise (to the right) $90^\circ$ faces West. Another $90^\circ$ faces North. A final $90^\circ$ (total $270^\circ$) faces East.

8. If $\frac{3}{8}$ of the pizza was eaten, what percent of the pizza was left?

Answer: $62.5\%$

Explanation: If $\frac{3}{8}$ was eaten, $\frac{5}{8}$ is left. Convert $\frac{5}{8}$ to a percentage: $5 \div 8 = 0.625$, which is $62.5\%$.

9. Write the percent form of $\frac{1}{9}$.

Answer: $11 \frac{1}{9}\%$

Explanation: $\frac{1}{9} \times 100 = \frac{100}{9}$. $100 \div 9 = 11$ with a remainder of $1$, which makes $11 \frac{1}{9}\%$.

10. $7 \frac{1}{4} - 4.15$ (decimal answer)

Answer: $3.10$

Explanation: $\frac{1}{4} = 0.25$, so $7 \frac{1}{4} = 7.25$. Subtract: $7.25 - 4.15 = 3.10$.

11. $8 \cdot 7 \cdot 0 \cdot 5$

Answer: $0$

Explanation: By the zero property of multiplication, any sequence of products containing a zero equals zero.

12. $\frac{6.4}{0.16}$

Answer: $40$

Explanation: Multiply top and bottom by 100 to remove the decimal: $640 \div 16$. Since $16 \times 4 = 64$, $640 \div 16 = 40$.

13. $\sqrt{3600}$

Answer: $60$

Explanation: $\sqrt{3600} = \sqrt{36} \times \sqrt{100} = 6 \times 10 = 60$.

14. $\sqrt{100} + 3^2 - \sqrt{16} \times (4 + 1)$

Answer: $-1$

Explanation:
Parentheses: $(4 + 1) = 5$
Exponents/Roots: $\sqrt{100} = 10$, $3^2 = 9$, $\sqrt{16} = 4$
Substitute: $10 + 9 - 4 \times 5$
Multiply: $4 \times 5 = 20$
Add/Subtract: $10 + 9 - 20 = 19 - 20 = -1$.

15. Solve this proportion: $\frac{14}{21} = \frac{18}{n}$

Answer: $n = 27$

Explanation: Simplify the first fraction: $\frac{14}{21} = \frac{2}{3}$. So, $\frac{2}{3} = \frac{18}{n}$. Since $2 \times 9 = 18$, multiply the denominator by 9 as well: $3 \times 9 = 27$.

16. $5 \frac{1}{3} \times 1 \frac{1}{8} \times 3$

Answer: $18$

Explanation: Convert to improper fractions: $\frac{16}{3} \times \frac{9}{8} \times \frac{3}{1}$. Cross simplify: the 3s cancel out. $16 \div 8 = 2$. Multiply the remaining numerators: $2 \times 9 = 18$.

17. $(5.4 \times 0.05) \div 9$

Answer: $0.03$

Explanation: First, multiply: $5.4 \times 0.05 = 0.27$. Then divide: $0.27 \div 9 = 0.03$.

18. If the sales-tax rate is $6\%$, what is the tax on a $\$150.00$ purchase?

Answer: $\$9.00$

Explanation: Convert $6\%$ to a decimal ($0.06$) and multiply: $\$150.00 \times 0.06 = \$9.00$.

19. Look at this set of ages: $12, 14, 12, 18, 20$
a. Find the mode.
b. Find the median.

Answer: a. $12$ | b. $14$

Explanation:
a. The mode is the most frequent number, which is $12$.
b. Arrange in order: $12, 12, 14, 18, 20$. The middle number is $14$.

20. Write the prime factorization of 360 using exponents.

Answer: $2^3 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5$

Explanation: Break down 360: $360 = 36 \times 10 = (4 \times 9) \times (2 \times 5) = (2^2 \times 3^2) \times (2 \times 5) = 2^3 \times 3^2 \times 5$.

21. What is the greatest common factor (GCF) of $17$ and $23$?

Answer: $1$

Explanation: Both $17$ and $23$ are prime numbers. Their only common factor is $1$.

22. The perimeter of an equilateral triangle is $1.5\text{ meters}$. How many centimeters long is each side?

Answer: $50\text{ cm}$

Explanation: Convert perimeter to cm: $1.5\text{ m} = 150\text{ cm}$. An equilateral triangle has $3$ equal sides. $150 \div 3 = 50\text{ cm}$.

23. A triangle has a base of $12\text{ cm}$ and an altitude (height) of $5\text{ cm}$. What is the area of this triangle?

Answer: $30\text{ cm}^2$

Explanation: Area $= \frac{1}{2}bh = \frac{1}{2}(12)(5) = 6 \times 5 = 30\text{ cm}^2$.

24. Three of the interior angles of a quadrilateral measure $90^\circ$, $80^\circ$, and $100^\circ$. What is the measure of the fourth angle?

Answer: $90^\circ$

Explanation: The sum of interior angles in any quadrilateral is $360^\circ$. Add the known angles: $90 + 80 + 100 = 270^\circ$. Subtract from $360^\circ$: $360 - 270 = 90^\circ$.

25. A line segment is $3 \frac{1}{2}\text{ inches}$ long. If a midpoint divides it exactly in half, what is the length of each new segment?

Answer: $1 \frac{3}{4}\text{ inches}$

Explanation: Divide $3 \frac{1}{2}$ by $2$. Convert to improper fraction: $\frac{7}{2}$. Multiply by $\frac{1}{2}$: $\frac{7}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{7}{4}$. Convert back: $1 \frac{3}{4}\text{ inches}$.

26. A jar contains 50 marbles. If 15 of them are red, what is the probability of pulling out a red marble at random?

Answer: $\frac{3}{10}$ or $30\%$

Explanation: Favorable outcomes over total outcomes: $\frac{15}{50}$. Divide numerator and denominator by $5$ to simplify: $\frac{3}{10}$.

27. Compare: $2\text{ gallons} \bigcirc 8\text{ liters}$

Answer: $2\text{ gallons} < 8\text{ liters}$

Explanation: $1\text{ gallon}$ is approximately $3.785\text{ liters}$. Therefore, $2\text{ gallons}$ is about $7.57\text{ liters}$, which is less than $8\text{ liters}$.

28. A function table uses the rule $y = 3x$. If the input values ($x$) are $2, 4,$ and $6$, what are the respective output values ($y$)?

Answer: $6, 12,$ and $18$

Explanation: Multiply each input by 3.
$2 \times 3 = 6$
$4 \times 3 = 12$
$6 \times 3 = 18$
