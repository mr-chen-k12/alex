Part 1: Word Problems & Variable Equations
1. If the product of 10 and 20 is divided by the sum of 20 and 30, what is the quotient?
Product of 10 and 20: \(10 imes 20 = 200\)
Sum of 20 and 30: \(20 + 30 = 50\)
Quotient: \( rac{200}{50} = 4\)
Answer: 4

Part 3: Mathematical Properties
29.
a. ANALYZE: Name the property illustrated by the following equation: \(x \cdot 5 = 5x\)
b. SUMMARIZE: In your own words explain the meaning of this property.
a. Property Named: Commutative Property of Multiplication
b. Explanation: Changing the order of the factors being multiplied does not alter their final product. Whether you multiply \(x\) by \(5\) or \(5\) by \(x\), the value remains identical.
Answer: Commutative Property of Multiplication
30. JUSTIFY: List the properties used in the first three steps to simplify the expression \((8 imes 7) imes 5\).
\((8 imes 7) imes 5\)      Given expression
\(8 imes (7 imes 5)\)      a. ___________________
\(8 imes (5 imes 7)\)      b. ___________________
\((8 imes 5) imes 7\)      c. ___________________
a. Associative Property of Multiplication (regrouped brackets from 8 and 7 to 7 and 5)
b. Commutative Property of Multiplication (swapped the internal order from 7 × 5 to 5 × 7)
c. Associative Property of Multiplication (regrouped brackets from 5 and 7 to 8 and 5)
Answer: a. Associative | b. Commutative | c. Associative
