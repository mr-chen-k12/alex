Problem 1
Jasmine grew ninety-two mums to sell at the school fundraiser. At the end of the fundraiser, twenty-four mums remained. How many mums did Jasmine sell?

Explanation:

This is a subtraction problem. Let $m$ be the number of mums sold:

$$\text{Total Mums} - \text{Mums Remaining} = \text{Mums Sold}$$ $$92 - 24 = m$$

Performing vertical subtraction:

$$\begin{array}{r} 92 \\ - 24 \\ \hline 68 \end{array}$$
Answer: Jasmine sold $68$ mums.
Problem 2
Rochelle collected 42 seashells. Then Zuri collected some seashells. They collected 83 seashells in all. How many seashells did Zuri collect?

Explanation:

Let $s$ be the number of seashells Zuri collected. Write an addition equation and solve for $s$ by subtracting:

$$42 + s = 83 \implies s = 83 - 42$$ $$\begin{array}{r} 83 \\ - 42 \\ \hline 41 \end{array}$$
Answer: Zuri collected $41$ seashells.
Problem 3
Conner estimated that the radius of one of the circles on the playground was 2 yards. If Conner was correct, then:
a. the radius was how many feet?
b. the diameter was how many feet?

Explanation:

We know that $1\text{ yard} = 3\text{ feet}$.

  • a. Radius in feet: Multiply yards by $3$: $$2\text{ yards} \times 3\text{ feet/yard} = 6\text{ feet}$$
  • b. Diameter in feet: The diameter is twice the radius: $$\text{Diameter} = 2 \times \text{Radius} = 2 \times 6\text{ feet} = 12\text{ feet}$$
Answer: a. $6\text{ feet}$, b. $12\text{ feet}$
Problem 4
Find the missing numbers in the counting sequence: $$\dots, 8, \underline{\quad}, \underline{\quad}, 32, 40, \underline{\quad}, \dots$$

Explanation:

Determine the rule of the sequence by looking at adjacent terms: $40 - 32 = 8$. The sequence counts by $8$s (multiples of 8):

$$8 \times 1 = 8$$ $$8 \times 2 = 16$$ $$8 \times 3 = 24$$ $$8 \times 4 = 32$$ $$8 \times 5 = 40$$ $$8 \times 6 = 48$$
Answer: Missing numbers are $16, 24, 48$
Problem 5
Find the missing numbers in the counting sequence: $$\dots, 14, \underline{\quad}, \underline{\quad}, 35, 42, \dots$$

Explanation:

Find the common difference: $42 - 35 = 7$. The sequence counts by $7$s (multiples of 7):

$$14 + 7 = 21$$ $$21 + 7 = 28$$
Answer: Missing numbers are $21, 28$
Problem 6
Use the digits 4, 5, and 6 to write a three-digit odd number less than 640. Each number may be used only once.

Explanation:

  1. To make an odd number, the last digit must be odd. Among $\{4, 5, 6\}$, the only odd digit is $5$. Thus, the ones digit is $5$.
  2. The remaining digits for hundreds and tens are $4$ and $6$.
  3. The number must be less than 640. If $6$ is placed in the hundreds place, $645 > 640$ (too large).
  4. Therefore, $4$ must be in the hundreds place, leaving $6$ for the tens place.
Answer: $465$
Problem 7

Answer: $209 > 190$
Problem 8
Fernando arrived home from school at the time shown on the clock (3:25). He snacked for 5 minutes, and then he spent 35 minutes completing his homework. What time did Fernando complete his homework?

Explanation:

  1. Arrival time: The clock shows $3:25$.
  2. After snack: $3:25 + 5\text{ minutes} = 3:30$.
  3. After homework: $3:30 + 35\text{ minutes} = 4:05$.
Answer: $4:05\text{ p.m.}$ (or $4:05$)
Problem 9
Draw a rectangle $3\text{ cm}$ long and $1\text{ cm}$ wide. Then shade two thirds of it.

Explanation:

Draw a rectangle with length $3\text{ cm}$ and width $1\text{ cm}$. Divide it into $3$ equal sections of $1\text{ cm} \times 1\text{ cm}$ squares. Shade $2$ of the $3$ sections to represent $\frac{2}{3}$.

Answer: Shade $2$ of the $3$ equal $1\text{ cm}^2$ segments.

Problem 11
In this figure (parallelogram ABCD), what type of angle is angle A? Explain how you know.

Explanation:

Angle A opens wider than a right angle ($90^\circ$). An angle that measures greater than $90^\circ$ and less than $180^\circ$ is defined as an obtuse angle.

Answer: Obtuse angle (it measures greater than $90^\circ$).
Problem 12
To what number is the arrow pointing on the number line? (Tick mark is halfway between $-20$ and $-10$).

Explanation:

The distance between $-20$ and $-10$ is $10$ units. The tick mark splits this segment into two equal halves of $5$ units:

$$-20 + 5 = -15$$
Answer: $-15$

Problem 26
Multiple Choice: Which of these does not equal 24?
A $3 \times 8$      B $4 \times 6$      C $2 \times 12$      D $8 \times 4$

Explanation:

  • A: $3 \times 8 = 24$
  • B: $4 \times 6 = 24$
  • C: $2 \times 12 = 24$
  • D: $8 \times 4 = 32 \neq 24$
Answer: D ($8 \times 4$)
Problem 27
Name the property of multiplication shown by each of these examples:
a. $0 \times 50 = 0$
b. $9 \times 6 = 6 \times 9$
c. $1 \times 75 = 75$

Explanation:

  • a. Zero Property of Multiplication: Multiplying any number by $0$ results in $0$.
  • b. Commutative Property of Multiplication: Changing the order of factors does not change the product.
  • c. Identity Property of Multiplication: Multiplying any number by $1$ leaves it unchanged.
Answer: a. Zero Property, b. Commutative Property, c. Identity Property
Problem 28
a. Round $\$3.49$ to the nearest dollar.
b. Round $\$3.49$ to the nearest 25 cents.

Explanation:

  • a. Nearest dollar: $49\text{ cents}$ is less than $50\text{ cents}$, so round down to $\$3.00$.
  • b. Nearest 25 cents: Multiples of $25\text{ cents}$ are $\$3.00, \$3.25, \$3.50, \$3.75$. Since $\$3.49$ is only $1\text{ cent}$ away from $\$3.50$, it rounds to $\$3.50$.
Answer: a. $\$3.00$, b. $\$3.50$

Problem 30
Suppose $x + y = z$. Write one more addition and two subtraction equations using $x, y,$ and $z$.

Explanation:

  • Addition (Commutative Property): $$y + x = z$$
  • Subtraction 1 (Inverse Operation): $$z - x = y$$
  • Subtraction 2 (Inverse Operation): $$z - y = x$$
Answer: Addition: $y + x = z$; Subtractions: $z - x = y$ and $z - y = x$
