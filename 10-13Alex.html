1 (11)

After the first hour of the monsoon, 23 millimeters of precipitation had fallen. After the second hour a total of 61 millimeters of precipitation had fallen. How many millimeters of precipitation fell during the second hour?

Answer: 38 millimeters

Solution: Subtract the first hour's total from the two-hour total: \(61 - 23 = 38\) millimeters fell during the second hour.

2 (13)

Each photograph enlargement cost 85¢ and Willie needed 26 enlargements. What was the total cost of the enlargements Willie needed?

Answer: $22.10

Solution: Multiply: \(26 \times 85¢ = 2210¢ = \$22.10\).

3 (12)

Connect The Byzantine Empire can be said to have begun in 330 when the city of Byzantium was renamed Constantinople and became the capital of the Roman Empire. The Byzantine Empire came to an end in 1453 when the city of Constantinople was renamed Istanbul and became the capital of the Ottoman Empire. About how many years did the Byzantine Empire last?

Answer: About 1123 years

Solution: Subtract the starting year from the ending year: \(1453 - 330 = 1123\) years.

4 (11)

At the movie theater, Dolores gave $20 to the ticket seller and got $10.25 back in change. How much did her movie ticket cost?

Answer: $9.75

Solution: Subtract the change from the amount given: \(\$20.00 - \$10.25 = \$9.75\).

5 (13)

A gross is a dozen dozens. A gross of pencils is how many pencils?

Answer: 144 pencils

Solution: A dozen is 12, so a dozen dozens is \(12 \times 12 = 144\).

6 (22)

Model Diagram this statement and answer the questions that follow. Begin by changing the percent to a reduced fraction.

Forty percent of the 60 marbles in the bag were blue.

a. How many of the marbles in the bag were blue?

b. How many of the marbles in the bag were not blue?

Answer: a. 24 blue marbles   b. 36 marbles not blue

Solution: Change the percent to a reduced fraction: \(40\% = \frac{40}{100} = \frac{2}{5}\). a. \(\frac{2}{5}\) of 60 \(= \frac{2 \times 60}{5} = 24\) blue marbles. b. \(60 - 24 = 36\) marbles were not blue.

7 (16)

a. Roan estimated that the weight of the water in a full bathtub is a quarter ton. How many pounds is a quarter of a ton?

b. Explain Describe how you got your answer.

Answer: a. 500 pounds

Solution: a. One ton equals 2000 pounds, so a quarter ton is \(2000 \div 4 = 500\) pounds. b. I changed tons to pounds using the conversion \(1\text{ ton} = 2000\text{ lb}\), then divided by 4 because a quarter means one of four equal parts.

8 (8, 9)

The figure shows a one-inch square. A smaller square that is \(\frac{7}{10}\) of an inch on each side is shaded.

a. What fraction of the square inch is shaded?

b. What percent of the square is not shaded?

Answer: a. \(\frac{49}{100}\)   b. 51%

Solution: a. The shaded square is \(\frac{7}{10}\) in. by \(\frac{7}{10}\) in., so its area is \(\frac{7}{10} \times \frac{7}{10} = \frac{49}{100}\) of the square inch (49 of the 100 small squares). b. \(1 - \frac{49}{100} = \frac{51}{100} = 51\%\) is not shaded.

9 (24)

a. Write 210 and 252 as products of prime numbers. Then reduce \(\frac{210}{252}\).

b. Find the GCF of 210 and 252.

Answer: a. \(210 = 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 7\); \(252 = 2^2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 7\); \(\frac{210}{252} = \frac{5}{6}\)   b. 42

Solution: a. \(210 = 2 \cdot 105 = 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 35 = 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 7\). \(252 = 2 \cdot 126 = 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 63 = 2^2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 7\). Cancel common factors: \(\frac{210}{252} = \frac{\cancel{2} \cdot \cancel{3} \cdot 5 \cdot \cancel{7}}{\cancel{2} \cdot 2 \cdot \cancel{3} \cdot 3 \cdot \cancel{7}} = \frac{5}{2 \cdot 3} = \frac{5}{6}\). b. GCF \(= 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 7 = 42\).

11 (15)

Find the number that makes the two fractions equivalent.

a. \(\frac{5}{8} = \frac{?}{24}\)    b. \(\frac{5}{12} = \frac{?}{24}\)

c. Add the fractions you found in a and b.

Answer: a. 15   b. 10   c. \(1\frac{1}{24}\)

Solution: a. \(8 \times 3 = 24\), so \(5 \times 3 = 15\). b. \(12 \times 2 = 24\), so \(5 \times 2 = 10\). c. \(\frac{15}{24} + \frac{10}{24} = \frac{25}{24} = 1\frac{1}{24}\).

Represent Draw a 2-by-2 grid. On the grid sketch a \(1\frac{1}{2}\) by \(1\frac{1}{2}\) square. Assume that the sketch illustrates a square with sides \(1\frac{1}{2}\) inches long. What is the area of the square? Explain how the sketch illustrates the area of the square.

Answer: \(2\frac{1}{4}\) square inches

Solution: Area \(= 1\frac{1}{2} \times 1\frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{2} \times \frac{3}{2} = \frac{9}{4} = 2\frac{1}{4}\) square inches. The sketch shows why: the shaded square covers 1 whole unit square, plus two half-unit strips, plus one quarter-unit corner square: \(1 + \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{4} = 2\frac{1}{4}\).

13 (8)

Draw \(\overline{AB}\) 2 in. long. Then draw \(\overline{BC}\) \(1\frac{1}{2}\) in. long perpendicular to \(\overline{AB}\). Complete \(\triangle ABC\) by drawing \(\overline{AC}\). How long is \(\overline{AC}\)?

Answer: \(2\frac{1}{2}\) inches

Solution: The triangle is a right triangle with legs 2 in. and \(1\frac{1}{2}\) in. Measuring \(\overline{AC}\) with a ruler gives about \(2\frac{1}{2}\) inches. Check with the Pythagorean theorem: \(AC = \sqrt{2^2 + (1\frac{1}{2})^2} = \sqrt{4 + 2.25} = \sqrt{6.25} = 2.5\) inches.

26 (2, 9)

Justify Name each property used to simplify this equation.

\(\frac{3}{2}\left(\frac{2}{3}x\right) = \frac{5}{6}\)   Given
\(\left(\frac{3}{2} \cdot \frac{2}{3}\right)x = \frac{5}{6}\)   a. ___
\(1x = \frac{5}{6}\)   b. ___
\(x = \frac{5}{6}\)   c. ___

Answer: a. Associative Property of Multiplication   b. Inverse Property of Multiplication   c. Identity Property of Multiplication

Solution: a. The factors were regrouped: \(\frac{3}{2}\left(\frac{2}{3}x\right) = \left(\frac{3}{2} \cdot \frac{2}{3}\right)x\) — Associative Property of Multiplication. b. \(\frac{3}{2} \cdot \frac{2}{3} = 1\) — a number times its reciprocal is 1: Inverse Property of Multiplication. c. \(1x = x\) — multiplying by 1 leaves a number unchanged: Identity Property of Multiplication.

27 (14, 15)

Max is thinking of a counting number from 1 to 10. Deb guesses 7. What is the probability Deb's guess is correct?

Answer: \(\frac{1}{10}\)

Solution: There are 10 equally likely counting numbers (1 through 10) and only 1 of them matches Deb's guess, so \(P = \frac{1}{10}\).

28 (1, 9)

Analyze Evaluate the following expressions for \(x = 3\) and \(y = 6\):

a. \(x - \frac{y}{x}\)    b. \(\frac{xy}{y}\)    c. \(\frac{x}{y} \cdot \frac{y}{x}\)

d. Which property is illustrated by c?

Answer: a. 1   b. 3   c. 1   d. Inverse Property of Multiplication

Solution: a. \(3 - \frac{6}{3} = 3 - 2 = 1\). b. \(\frac{3 \cdot 6}{6} = \frac{18}{6} = 3\). c. \(\frac{3}{6} \cdot \frac{6}{3} = \frac{18}{18} = 1\). d. \(\frac{x}{y}\) and \(\frac{y}{x}\) are reciprocals, and their product is 1 — the Inverse Property of Multiplication.

Predict The rule of the following sequence is \(k = 3n - 2\). Find the ninth term.

Answer: 25

Solution: Substitute \(n = 9\) into the rule: \(k = 3(9) - 2 = 27 - 2 = 25\). (Check: \(n = 1 \to 1\), \(n = 2 \to 4\), \(n = 3 \to 7\), \(n = 4 \to 10\) ✓)

30 (Inv. 2)

Conclude The central angle of a half circle is \(180^\circ\). The central angle of a quarter circle is \(90^\circ\). How many degrees is the central angle of an eighth of a circle?

Answer: \(45^\circ\)

Solution: A full circle is \(360^\circ\). One eighth of a circle is \(360^\circ \div 8 = 45^\circ\). (Also: half of the \(90^\circ\) quarter-circle angle.)

Real-World Connection You and some friends volunteered to paint the concession stand for your local ball park. The back and side walls (both inside and outside) need painting. The two side walls measure \(9\frac{3}{5}\) by \(8\frac{1}{3}\) feet each, while the back wall measures 39 feet by \(8\frac{1}{3}\) feet. Find the total area that must be painted (in square feet). Show each step of your work.

Note: the photo was cut off at the back-wall dimensions, so this solution assumes the back wall is 39 ft by \(8\frac{1}{3}\) ft (same height as the side walls).

Answer: 970 square feet

Solution: Step 1 — one side wall: \(9\frac{3}{5} \times 8\frac{1}{3} = \frac{48}{5} \times \frac{25}{3} = \frac{1200}{15} = 80\) sq ft. Step 2 — two side walls: \(2 \times 80 = 160\) sq ft. Step 3 — back wall: \(39 \times 8\frac{1}{3} = 39 \times \frac{25}{3} = 325\) sq ft. Step 4 — one side of the stand: \(160 + 325 = 485\) sq ft. Step 5 — paint inside and outside: \(2 \times 485 = 970\) square feet.
