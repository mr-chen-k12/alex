Math Worksheet Solutions (Variant B)

1. Samantha bought bananas at 8 cents per pound at the market. She spent 120 cents. How many pounds of bananas did Samantha buy?

Answer: 15 pounds

Explanation: To find the total pounds, divide the total cost by the cost per pound.
$120 \div 8 = 15$.

2. The library had 315 books on a display shelf. Students checked out 128 of them. How many books stayed on the display shelf?

Answer: 187 books

Explanation: Subtract the number of books checked out from the initial total.
$315 - 128 = 187$.

3. Mark watched a quarter of a 120-minute movie. How many minutes of the movie did he watch?

Answer: 30 minutes

Explanation: Divide the total minutes by 4 to find a quarter.
$120 \div 4 = 30$.

4. One fifth of the students went to the gymnasium. What fraction of the students did not go to the gymnasium? What percent of the students did not go to the gymnasium?

Answer: Fraction: $\frac{4}{5}$, Percent: 80%

Explanation: The whole group is represented by 1. The fraction that did not go is $1 - \frac{1}{5} = \frac{4}{5}$. To convert $\frac{4}{5}$ to a percentage, multiply by 100: $\frac{4}{5} \times 100 = 80\%$.

5. If one side of an equilateral triangle is 5 centimeters long, then what is its perimeter in (a) centimeters? (b) millimeters?

Answer: (a) 15 cm, (b) 150 mm

Explanation: An equilateral triangle has 3 equal sides.
(a) Perimeter in cm: $5 + 5 + 5 = 15$ cm.
(b) Since 1 cm = 10 mm, $15 \text{ cm} \times 10 = 150$ mm.

6. Which of these arrows could be pointing to 4250? (Given a number line from 4000 to 5000 with arrows A, B, C evenly spaced at the quarters)

Answer: Arrow A

Explanation: The number line starts at 4000 and ends at 5000. 4250 is exactly one-quarter of the way between 4000 and 5000. Arrow A represents the 1/4 position.

7. Two cups equal a pint. Two pints equal a quart. Four quarts equal a gallon. A pint is what fraction of a gallon?

Answer: $\frac{1}{8}$

Explanation: There are 4 quarts in a gallon, and 2 pints in each quart. $4 \times 2 = 8$ pints in a gallon. Therefore, 1 pint is $\frac{1}{8}$ of a gallon.

8. A large watermelon weighed 7 kilograms. How many grams is that?

Answer: 7000 grams

Explanation: There are 1000 grams in a kilogram. $7 \times 1000 = 7000$ grams.

9. Estimate the product of 489 and 6.

Answer: 3000

Explanation: To estimate, round 489 to the nearest hundred, which is 500. Multiply $500 \times 6 = 3000$.

10. It is morning. What time was it 12 hours ago? (Clock face shows 8:15)

Answer: 8:15 p.m.

Explanation: Since it is morning, the time on the clock is 8:15 a.m. Exactly 12 hours prior to an AM time is the identical PM time: 8:15 p.m.

11. Compare: $\frac{2}{3} \bigcirc \frac{5}{8}$. Draw and shade two congruent rectangles to show the comparison.

Answer: $\frac{2}{3} > \frac{5}{8}$

Explanation: Getting a common denominator of 24, we see $\frac{2}{3} = \frac{16}{24}$ and $\frac{5}{8} = \frac{15}{24}$. Therefore, $\frac{2}{3}$ is slightly larger.

Explanation:
$8 \div 2 = 4$ remainder $0$.
Bring down 3. $3 \div 2 = 1$ remainder $1$.
Bring down 5 to make 15. $15 \div 2 = 7$ remainder $1$.

19. $487 \times 5$

Answer: $2435$

Explanation:
$5 \times 7 = 35$ (write 5, carry 3).
$5 \times 8 = 40$; $40 + 3 = 43$ (write 3, carry 4).
$5 \times 4 = 20$; $20 + 4 = 24$. Result is 2435.

20. $53 \times 40$

Answer: $2120$

Explanation: Multiply 53 by 4 and then attach a zero at the end.
$4 \times 3 = 12$ (write 2, carry 1).
$4 \times 5 = 20$; $20 + 1 = 21$. Result is 212. Append the 0 to get 2120.

25. $6N = 138$

Answer: $N = 23$

Explanation: Isolate $N$ by dividing both sides by 6.
$138 \div 6 = 23$.

26. $Y \times 2^3 = 104$

Answer: $Y = 13$

Explanation: First solve the exponent: $2^3 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 = 8$. The equation becomes $Y \times 8 = 104$. Divide both sides by 8:
$104 \div 8 = 13$.

27. $6 + 9 + 3 + 5 + N + 4 + 8 + 2 = 45$

Answer: $N = 8$

Explanation: Combine all the known numbers:
$6+9+3+5+4+8+2 = 37$.
The equation becomes $37 + N = 45$. Subtract 37 from 45 to get $N = 8$.

28. Find the perimeter and area of a rectangle with a length of 9 units and a width of 5 units.

Answer: Perimeter = 28 units, Area = 45 sq units

Explanation:
$\text{Perimeter} = 2 \times (\text{Length} + \text{Width}) = 2 \times (9 + 5) = 2 \times 14 = 28$ units.
$\text{Area} = \text{Length} \times \text{Width} = 9 \times 5 = 45$ square units.

29. Name the geometric transformation that moves a square 5 units to the right and 2 units up without flipping or turning it.

Answer: Translation

Explanation: A translation (or slide) moves a shape in a specific direction without changing its orientation or size. Because the shape just moved right and up, it is a translation.

30. The first four multiples of 15 are 15, 30, 45, and 60. What are the first four multiples of 150?

Answer: 150, 300, 450, 600

Explanation: Since 150 is ten times 15, its multiples are simply ten times the multiples of 15. Just append a zero to the original sequence of multiples.
