Topic 1: Complex Probability
Your sock drawer has 5 pairs of blue socks, 4 pairs of red socks, and 3 pairs of white socks. If you pull out a pair of red socks and don't replace them, what's the probability you will pull out another pair of red socks?

Explanation:

Step 1: First pull (already happened): There were 4 red out of 12 total (\( rac{4}{12} = rac{1}{3}\)).

Step 2: Second pull: Since you removed a red pair, there are now 3 red pairs left and 11 total pairs left.

Step 3: The probability of the second pull is \( rac{3}{11}\).

Step 4: To find the total probability of both events happening together, multiply them: \( rac{1}{3} imes rac{3}{11} = rac{3}{33} = rac{1}{11}\).

Topic 2: Decimal to Fraction
Convert \(0.7\) to a fraction in simplest form.

Explanation:

Step 1: Write the decimal numerals as the numerator (7).

Step 2: Write the decimal place value as the denominator. The 7 is in the "tenths" place, so the denominator is 10.

Result: \( rac{7}{10}\). It cannot be reduced further.

Topic 3: Finding a Ratio
What is the boy-girl ratio in a class of 26 students if there are 14 girls?

Explanation:

Step 1: Find the number of boys. Total students (26) - girls (14) = 12 boys.

Step 2: Make a fraction in the order requested (boy-girl). Boys on top, girls on bottom: \( rac{12}{14}\).

Step 3: Reduce to simplest form: Divide by 2 to get \( rac{6}{7}\).

Topic 4: Order of Operations
Solve: \(3^3 - 2^2 - 4 imes 5\)

Explanation:

Step 1: Exponents first. \(3^3 = 27\) and \(2^2 = 4\). The equation is now \(27 - 4 - 4 imes 5\).

Step 2: Multiplication next. \(4 imes 5 = 20\). The equation is now \(27 - 4 - 20\).

Step 3: Subtraction from left to right. \(27 - 4 = 23\).

Step 4: \(23 - 20 = 3\).

Topic 5: Two-Step Equation
Solve for \(m\): \(5m + 7 = 22\)

Explanation:

Step 1: Subtract 7 from both sides to get the variable alone: \(5m = 22 - 7\) → \(5m = 15\).

Step 2: Divide by 5 to find the missing factor: \(m = 15 \div 5\) → \(m = 3\).

Topic 6: Volume of a Rectangular Prism
What is the volume of a rectangular prism with measurements of 8 inches, 3 inches, and 6 inches?

Explanation:

Step 1: Use the formula \(V = Length imes Width imes Height\).

Step 2: Multiply the measurements: \(8 imes 3 imes 6\).

Step 3: \(24 imes 6 = 144\) cubic inches.

Topic 7: Adding & Subtracting Integers
Solve: \(-4 + (-5) - (-3)\)

Explanation:

Step 1: Change the subtraction symbol to addition and flip the following sign. The equation becomes \(-4 + (-5) + (+3)\).

Step 2: Add the first two negatives: \(-4 + (-5) = -9\).

Step 3: Add the final number (different signs, subtract and take the sign of the larger digit): \(-9 + 3 = -6\).

Topic 8: Solving a Proportion
Solve for \(a\): \( rac{6}{15} = rac{a}{20}\)

Explanation:

Step 1: Multiply the known cross products: \(6 imes 20 = 120\).

Step 2: Divide by the remaining number: \(120 \div 15 = 8\).

Therefore, \(a = 8\).

Topic 9: Subtracting Mixed Numbers (with Regrouping)
Solve: \(5 rac{1}{6} - 2 rac{2}{3}\)

Explanation:

Step 1: Find a common denominator. Change \(2 rac{2}{3}\) to \(2 rac{4}{6}\).

Step 2: The problem is \(5 rac{1}{6} - 2 rac{4}{6}\). You cannot subtract 4 from 1, so you must regroup.

Step 3: Borrow 1 whole from 5 (making it 4). Add \( rac{6}{6}\) to \( rac{1}{6}\) to get \( rac{7}{6}\). The problem is now \(4 rac{7}{6} - 2 rac{4}{6}\).

Step 4: Subtract: \(4 - 2 = 2\) and \( rac{7}{6} - rac{4}{6} = rac{3}{6}\).

Step 5: Reduce \(2 rac{3}{6}\) to \(2 rac{1}{2}\).

Topic 10: Divide Mixed Numbers
Solve: \(3 rac{3}{4} \div 2\)

Explanation:

Step 1: Change the mixed number and whole number into improper fractions: \( rac{15}{4} \div rac{2}{1}\).

Step 2: Use "Keep, Change, Flip". Keep \( rac{15}{4}\), change division to multiplication, flip \( rac{2}{1}\) to \( rac{1}{2}\).

Step 3: Multiply: \( rac{15}{4} imes rac{1}{2} = rac{15}{8}\).

Step 4: Convert back to a mixed number: 8 goes into 15 one time with 7 left over → \(1 rac{7}{8}\).

Topic 11: Putting it all together
Order from least to greatest: \( rac{1}{4}, -4, 0.4\)

Explanation:

Step 1: Change all to the same form (decimals are easiest). \( rac{1}{4} = 0.25\).

Step 2: Compare the numbers: \(-4\), \(0.25\), and \(0.4\).

Step 3: -4 is the smallest because it is negative. Between 0.25 and 0.4, 0.4 (or 0.40) is larger.

Result: \(-4\), \( rac{1}{4}\), \(0.4\).

Topic 12: Area of a Circle
The diameter of a circle is 8 inches. What is the area of the circle? (Use \(\pi = 3.14\))

Explanation:

Step 1: First, find the radius. \(Radius = Diameter \div 2\), so \(8 \div 2 = 4\) inches.

Step 2: Use the area formula: \(A = \pi imes r^2\).

Step 3: \(A = 3.14 imes 4^2 = 3.14 imes 16\).

Step 4: Multiply: \(3.14 imes 16 = 50.24\).

Topic 13: Area of a Complex Shape
Find the area of the hexagon below.

Explanation:

Step 1: Break the complex shape into 2 rectangles. For example, draw a vertical line down from the inner corner.

Step 2: This creates a left rectangle and a right rectangle. The left rectangle has a width of 3 cm and a full height of 8 cm. \(Area_1 = 3 imes 8 = 24\) sq cm.

Step 3: The bottom width is 7 cm, and the left rectangle takes up 3 cm of it, leaving \(7 - 3 = 4\) cm for the width of the right rectangle. The height of the right rectangle is given as 2 cm. \(Area_2 = 4 imes 2 = 8\) sq cm.

Step 4: Add the areas together: \(24 + 8 = 32\) sq cm.
