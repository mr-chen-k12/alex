6.Find the least common multiple (LCM) of 12, 18, and 30.

Prime factors: 12 = 2²×3, 18 = 2×3², 30 = 2×3×5.

Use the greatest power of each prime: 2²×3²×5 = 180.

8.A triangle has a base of 18 cm and an area of 81 cm². What is its height?

Use A = 12bh.

81 = 12(18)h = 9h

Answer: 9 cm

9.Evaluate: (−2)5 + 32 × 4

10.Solve for y: 4(y + 2) = 2(y + 11)

Subtract 2y and then 8: 2y = 14

11.A backpack originally costs $150. It is marked down by 30%, and then an additional 20% coupon is applied to the sale price. What is the final price?

After 30% off: 150 × 0.70 = $105

After another 20% off: 105 × 0.80 = $84
