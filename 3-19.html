Lesson Practice (Page 505)

a. Which line in the figure at right is a transversal?
Answer: Line \(c\)
Explanation: A transversal is a line that passes through two or more other lines. In the figure, line \(c\) intersects parallel lines \(f\) and \(g\).
b. Which angle is an alternate interior angle to \(\angle 3\)?
Answer: \(\angle 5\)
Explanation: Alternate interior angles are on opposite sides of the transversal and between the parallel lines. \(\angle 3\) is on the left side of \(c\) and inside the parallel lines. Its alternate interior angle is \(\angle 5\) on the right side of \(c\).
c. Which angle corresponds to \(\angle 8\)?
Answer: \(\angle 4\)
Explanation: Corresponding angles are in the same relative position at each intersection. \(\angle 8\) is in the top-left position at the bottom intersection, so it corresponds to \(\angle 4\), which is in the top-left position at the top intersection.
d. Which angle is an alternate exterior angle to \(\angle 7\)?
Answer: \(\angle 1\)
Explanation: Alternate exterior angles are on opposite sides of the transversal and outside the parallel lines. \(\angle 7\) is on the bottom-left exterior. Its alternate exterior angle is \(\angle 1\) on the top-right exterior.
e. CONCLUDE If the measure of \(\angle 1\) is \(105^{\circ}\), what is the measure of each of the other angles in the figure?
Answer:
\(\angle 3\), \(\angle 5\), and \(\angle 7\) all equal \(105^{\circ}\).
\(\angle 2\), \(\angle 4\), \(\angle 6\), and \(\angle 8\) all equal \(75^{\circ}\).
Explanation:
\(\angle 1\) and \(\angle 3\) are vertical angles, so \(\angle 3 = 105^{\circ}\).
\(\angle 1\) and \(\angle 2\) form a linear pair, so \(\angle 2 = 180^{\circ} - 105^{\circ} = 75^{\circ}\).
\(\angle 4\) is vertical to \(\angle 2\), so \(\angle 4 = 75^{\circ}\).
The remaining angles correspond to the top angles: \(\angle 5 = \angle 1\) (or alt interior to \(\angle 3\)), \(\angle 6 = \angle 2\), \(\angle 7 = \angle 3\), and \(\angle 8 = \angle 4\).

1. How many quarter-pound hamburgers can be made from 100 pounds of ground beef?
Answer: 400 hamburgers
Explanation: Divide the total weight by the weight per hamburger: \(100 \div 0.25 = 100 \div \frac{1}{4} = 100 \times 4 = 400\).
2. CONNECT On the Fahrenheit scale water freezes at \(32^{\circ}\text{F}\) and boils at \(212^{\circ}\text{F}\). What temperature is halfway between the freezing and boiling temperatures of water?
Answer: \(122^{\circ}\text{F}\)
Explanation: Find the average of the two temperatures: \(\frac{32 + 212}{2} = \frac{244}{2} = 122\).

Explanation: Convert \(\frac{5}{8}\) to a decimal. \(5 \div 8 = 0.625\). Since \(0.625 < 0.675\), the answer is \(<\).

9. Use division by primes to find the prime factors of 320. Then write the prime factorization of 320 using exponents.
Answer: \(2^6 \cdot 5\)
Explanation:
\(320 \div 2 = 160\)
\(160 \div 2 = 80\)
\(80 \div 2 = 40\)
\(40 \div 2 = 20\)
\(20 \div 2 = 10\)
\(10 \div 2 = 5\)
The prime factors are six 2s and one 5. Using exponents: \(2^6 \cdot 5\).
10. In one minute the second hand of a clock turns \(360^{\circ}\). How many degrees does the minute hand of a clock turn in one minute?
Answer: \(6^{\circ}\)
Explanation: The minute hand takes 60 minutes to complete a full circle (\(360^{\circ}\)). In one minute, it turns \(\frac{360^{\circ}}{60} = 6^{\circ}\).
11. ANALYZE Jason likes to ride his skateboard around Parallelogram Park. If he made four turns on each trip around the park, what was the average number of degrees in each turn?
Answer: \(90^{\circ}\)
Explanation: The sum of the exterior angles of any convex polygon (one full trip around) is always \(360^{\circ}\). Since there are 4 turns, the average is \(\frac{360^{\circ}}{4} = 90^{\circ}\).
12. \(6\frac{3}{4} + 5\frac{7}{8}\)
Answer: \(12\frac{5}{8}\)
Explanation: Find a common denominator (8): \(6\frac{6}{8} + 5\frac{7}{8} = 11\frac{13}{8}\). Since \(\frac{13}{8} = 1\frac{5}{8}\), add this to 11 to get \(12\frac{5}{8}\).
13. \(6\frac{1}{3} - 2\frac{1}{2}\)
Answer: \(3\frac{5}{6}\)
Explanation: Find a common denominator (6): \(6\frac{2}{6} - 2\frac{3}{6}\). Borrow 1 from 6 to subtract: \(5\frac{8}{6} - 2\frac{3}{6} = 3\frac{5}{6}\).
14. \(2\frac{1}{2} \div 100\)
Answer: \(\frac{1}{40}\) (or \(0.025\))
Explanation: Convert to an improper fraction: \(\frac{5}{2} \div 100\). Multiply by the reciprocal: \(\frac{5}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{100} = \frac{5}{200}\). Simplify to \(\frac{1}{40}\).
15. \(6.93 + 8.429 + 12\)
Answer: \(27.359\)
Explanation: Align the decimals and add: \(6.930 + 8.429 + 12.000 = 27.359\).
16. \((1 - 0.1)(1 \div 0.1)\)
Answer: \(9\)
Explanation: Solve the parentheses first. \((1 - 0.1) = 0.9\). \((1 \div 0.1) = 10\). Then multiply: \(0.9 \times 10 = 9\).
17. \(4.2 + \frac{7}{8}\) (decimal answer)
Answer: \(5.075\)
Explanation: Convert \(\frac{7}{8}\) to a decimal: \(0.875\). Add to 4.2: \(4.200 + 0.875 = 5.075\).
18. Jovita bought \(3\frac{1}{3}\) cubic yards of mulch for the garden. She will need \(2.5\) cubic yards for the flowerbeds. How much mulch is left for Jovita to use for her vegetable garden? Write your answer as a fraction.
Answer: \(\frac{5}{6}\) cubic yards
Explanation: Convert 2.5 to a fraction (\(2\frac{1}{2}\)). Subtract: \(3\frac{1}{3} - 2\frac{1}{2}\). Find a common denominator (6): \(3\frac{2}{6} - 2\frac{3}{6}\). Borrow 1: \(2\frac{8}{6} - 2\frac{3}{6} = \frac{5}{6}\).
19. ANALYZE If \(80\%\) of the 30 students passed the test, how many students did not pass?
Answer: 6 students
Explanation: If \(80\%\) passed, then \(20\%\) did not pass. Calculate \(20\%\) of 30: \(0.20 \times 30 = 6\).
20. Compare: \(\frac{1}{2} \div \frac{1}{3} \bigcirc \frac{1}{3} \div \frac{1}{2}\)
Answer: \(\frac{1}{2} \div \frac{1}{3} > \frac{1}{3} \div \frac{1}{2}\)
Explanation: Left side: \(\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{3}{1} = \frac{3}{2}\). Right side: \(\frac{1}{3} \times \frac{2}{1} = \frac{2}{3}\). Since \(1.5 > 0.66\dots\), the left side is greater.
21. PREDICT What is the next number in this sequence? ..., 1000, 100, 10, 1, ...
Answer: \(0.1\) (or \(\frac{1}{10}\))
Explanation: The pattern is dividing by 10 for each subsequent term. \(1 \div 10 = 0.1\).
22. \(a + 60 + 70 = 180\)
Answer: \(a = 50\)
Explanation: Combine like terms: \(a + 130 = 180\). Subtract 130 from both sides: \(a = 180 - 130 = 50\).
23. \(\frac{7}{4} = \frac{w}{44}\)
Answer: \(w = 77\)
Explanation: Cross-multiply or notice the relationship. Since \(4 \times 11 = 44\), multiply the top by 11 as well: \(7 \times 11 = 77\).
24. The perimeter of this square is 48 in. What is the area of one of the triangles? (Diagram shows a square split by a diagonal).
Answer: 72 sq in.
Explanation: A square has 4 equal sides. Side length = \(48 \div 4 = 12\) in. The total area of the square is \(12 \times 12 = 144\) sq in. The diagonal splits the square into two equal triangles, so the area of one triangle is \(144 \div 2 = 72\) sq in.

30. What is the probability of rolling a composite number with one roll of a number cube?
Answer: \(\frac{1}{3}\)
Explanation: A standard number cube has faces 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6. The composite numbers (numbers with more than two factors) are 4 and 6. There are 2 composite numbers out of 6 possible outcomes, so the probability is \(\frac{2}{6}\), which simplifies to \(\frac{1}{3}\).
