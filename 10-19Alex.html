1.(16, 28)
* In the 1996 Summer Olympics, Charles Austin won the high jump event by jumping 7 feet 10 inches. How many inches did he jump?
Answer
\(94\text{ inches}\)
Solution
Convert feet to inches: \(7\text{ ft} = 7 \times 12 = 84\text{ in}\). Then \(84 + 10 = 94\text{ in}\).
2.(13)
JUSTIFY If 8 pounds of bananas cost \(\$5.52\), what does 1 pound of bananas cost? How did you find the cost per pound?
Answer
\(\$0.69\text{ per pound}\)
Solution
Divide the total cost by the number of pounds: \(\$5.52 \div 8 = \$0.69\). Dividing finds the unit price.

5.(22)
EVALUATE In the following statement, change the percent to a reduced fraction. Then diagram the statement and answer the questions.
Forty percent of the 80 birds were robins.
a. How many of the birds were robins?
b. How many of the birds were not robins?
Answer
\(\text{a. }32\text{ robins}\qquad\text{b. }48\text{ not robins}\)
Solution
\(40\% = \dfrac{40}{100} = \dfrac{2}{5}\). a. \(\dfrac{2}{5} \times 80 = 32\) robins. b. \(80 - 32 = 48\) were not robins.

REPRESENT b. Use prime factorization to find the LCM of 16 and 36.
Answer
\(\text{a. }24\qquad\text{b. }144\)
Solution

14.(19, 20)
INFER The relationships between the lengths of the sides of the rectangles are as follows: \[AB + FE = BC \qquad AF + CD = AC \qquad AB = 2\text{ in.}\]a. Find the perimeter of rectangle \(ABEF\).
b. Find the area of rectangle \(BCDE\).
Answer
\(\text{a. }10\text{ in.}\qquad\text{b. }12\text{ in}^2\)
Solution
In rectangle \(ABEF\), \(AB = FE = 2\) in., so \(BC = 2 + 2 = 4\) in. The vertical sides are all equal (\(AF = BE = CD\)), and \(AC = AB + BC = 6\) in., so \(2 \cdot AF = 6 \Rightarrow AF = 3\) in. a. \(P = 2(2 + 3) = 10\) in. b. \(A = BC \cdot CD = 4 \cdot 3 = 12\) in\(^2\).
15.(18)
INFER Triangle \(ABF\) is congruent to \(\triangle EFB\).
a. Which angle in \(\triangle ABF\) corresponds to \(\angle EBF\) in \(\triangle EFB\)?
b. What is the measure of \(\angle A\)?
Answer
\(\text{a. }\angle AFB\qquad\text{b. }90^\circ\)
Solution
a. The congruence \(\triangle ABF \cong \triangle EFB\) matches vertices \(A \leftrightarrow E\), \(B \leftrightarrow F\), \(F \leftrightarrow B\). \(\angle EBF\) is at vertex \(B\) of \(\triangle EFB\), which corresponds to vertex \(F\) of \(\triangle ABF\) → \(\angle AFB\). b. \(\angle A\) is a corner of rectangle \(ABEF\), so \(m\angle A = 90^\circ\).

25.(2)
The rule of the following sequence is \(k = 2n + 1\). Find the fifth term of the sequence.
\[3,\ 5,\ 9,\ 17,\ \ldots\]
Answer
\(33\)
Solution
Each term after the first is one less than twice the previous term (\(5 = 2 \cdot 3 - 1\), \(9 = 2 \cdot 5 - 1\), \(17 = 2 \cdot 9 - 1\)); equivalently the differences double: \(2, 4, 8, \ldots\). The next difference is 16, so the fifth term is \(17 + 16 = 33\).
