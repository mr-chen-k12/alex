Part 2: Surface Area of a Cube (Questions 11-20)

Question 11
A cube has edges that are 2 inches long. What is the area of one face of the cube?
  • A) 4 sq in
  • B) 8 sq in
  • C) 24 sq in
  • D) 12 sq in
Correct Answer: A) 4 sq in

Explanation: The face of a cube is a square. Area = side \( \times \) side = \( 2 \times 2 = 4 \).

Question 12
A cube has edges that are 4 cm long. What is the total surface area of the cube?
  • A) 64 sq cm
  • B) 16 sq cm
  • C) 96 sq cm
  • D) 24 sq cm
Correct Answer: C) 96 sq cm

Explanation: Area of one face is \( 4 \times 4 = 16 \). Total surface area is \( 16 \times 6 = 96 \).

Question 13
A cube has edges that are 5 feet long. What is the area of one face?
  • A) 150 sq ft
  • B) 25 sq ft
  • C) 125 sq ft
  • D) 30 sq ft

Explanation: Area = \( 5 \times 5 = 25 \).

Question 14

Part 3: Percentages (Questions 21-30)

Question 21
A farmer planted wheat on 40% of his 200 acres. How many acres of wheat did he plant?
  • A) 40 acres
  • B) 80 acres
  • C) 160 acres
  • D) 120 acres
Correct Answer: B) 80 acres

Explanation: 40% is 0.40. \( 200 \times 0.40 = 80 \).

Question 22
Sarah read 25% of a 300-page book. How many pages did she read?
  • A) 100 pages
  • B) 25 pages
  • C) 75 pages
  • D) 225 pages
Correct Answer: C) 75 pages

Explanation: 25% is \( \frac{1}{4} \). \( 300 \div 4 = 75 \).

Question 23
A student answered 80% of the 50 questions correctly. How many questions were correct?
  • A) 10
  • B) 35
  • C) 45
  • D) 40
Correct Answer: D) 40

Explanation: 80% is 0.8. \( 50 \times 0.8 = 40 \).

Question 24
10% of 500 apples are rotten. How many apples are rotten?
  • A) 50
  • B) 5
  • C) 100
  • D) 10
Correct Answer: A) 50

Explanation: To find 10%, move the decimal one place to the left. 500 becomes 50.

Question 25
A discount is 20% off a $60 item. What is the amount of the discount?
  • A) $20
  • B) $6
  • C) $12
  • D) $48
Correct Answer: C) $12

Explanation: 10% of 60 is 6. So 20% is \( 6 \times 2 = 12 \).

Question 26
There are 40 students in a class. 50% of them are girls. How many are girls?
  • A) 25
  • B) 20
  • C) 10
  • D) 30
Correct Answer: B) 20

Explanation: 50% means half. Half of 40 is 20.

Question 27
A jar contains 80 marbles. 75% are blue. How many marbles are blue?
  • A) 40
  • B) 20
  • C) 60
  • D) 70
Correct Answer: C) 60

Explanation: 75% is \( \frac{3}{4} \). \( \frac{1}{4} \) of 80 is 20, so \( \frac{3}{4} \) is \( 20 \times 3 = 60 \).

Question 28
30% of a 200-liter tank is full. How many liters are in the tank?
  • A) 30 liters
  • B) 60 liters
  • C) 90 liters
  • D) 140 liters
Correct Answer: B) 60 liters

Explanation: 10% of 200 is 20. So 30% is \( 20 \times 3 = 60 \).

Question 29
A team won 90% of their 20 games. How many games did they win?
  • A) 19
  • B) 10
  • C) 18
  • D) 2
Correct Answer: C) 18

Explanation: 90% is 0.9. \( 20 \times 0.9 = 18 \).

Question 30
5% of 100 dollars is how much?
  • A) $5
  • B) $10
  • C) $1
  • D) $50
Correct Answer: A) $5

Explanation: Percent literally means 'per 100'.
