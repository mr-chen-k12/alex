Written Practice

1. A foot-long ribbon can be cut into how many $1 \frac{1}{2}$-inch lengths?

Answer: 8 lengths

Explanation: First, convert 1 foot into inches: $1 \text{ foot} = 12 \text{ inches}$. Next, divide the total length by the cut length: $12 \div 1 \frac{1}{2}$. Convert the mixed number to an improper fraction: $1 \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{2}$. Now divide: $12 \div \frac{3}{2} = 12 \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{24}{3} = 8$.

2. A can of beans is the shape of what geometric solid?

Answer: Cylinder

Explanation: A can has a circular base and a curved side that connects it to an identical circular top, which defines a cylinder.

3. If $\frac{3}{8}$ of the group voted yes and $\frac{3}{8}$ voted no, then what fraction of the group did not vote?

Answer: $\frac{1}{4}$

Explanation: Add the fractions of people who voted: $\frac{3}{8} + \frac{3}{8} = \frac{6}{8}$. The whole group is represented by $1$, or $\frac{8}{8}$. Subtract the voters from the total: $\frac{8}{8} - \frac{6}{8} = \frac{2}{8}$. Simplify the fraction: $\frac{2}{8} = \frac{1}{4}$.

4. Nine months is
a. what fraction of a year?
b. what percent of a year?

Answer: a. $\frac{3}{4}$ | b. $75\%$

Explanation: There are 12 months in a year.
a. Fraction: $\frac{9}{12}$, which simplifies to $\frac{3}{4}$ by dividing the numerator and denominator by 3.
b. Percent: $\frac{3}{4}$ is equivalent to $0.75$, which is $75\%$.

5. One-cubic-foot boxes were stacked as shown. What was the volume of the stack of boxes?

Answer: 36 cubic feet

Explanation: Counting the visible dimensions of the rectangular prism stack from the image, it is $4$ boxes wide, $3$ boxes deep, and $3$ boxes high. Volume = length $\times$ width $\times$ height = $4 \times 3 \times 3 = 36$. Since each box is 1 cubic foot, the total volume is $36 \text{ cubic feet}$.

6. Tom was facing east. Then he turned counterclockwise $270^\circ$. After the turn, what direction was Tom facing?

Answer: South

Explanation: Starting East, turning counterclockwise (to the left) $90^\circ$ faces North. Another $90^\circ$ (total $180^\circ$) faces West. Another $90^\circ$ (total $270^\circ$) faces South.

7. If $\frac{1}{5}$ of the pie was eaten, what percent of the pie was left?

Answer: $80\%$

Explanation: If $\frac{1}{5}$ was eaten, then $1 - \frac{1}{5} = \frac{4}{5}$ was left. Convert $\frac{4}{5}$ to a percentage by multiplying by $100$: $\frac{4}{5} \times 100 = 80\%$.

8. Write the percent form of $\frac{1}{7}$.

Answer: $14 \frac{2}{7}\%$

Explanation: To convert a fraction to a percent, multiply by $100$. $\frac{1}{7} \times 100 = \frac{100}{7}$. Perform the division: $100 \div 7 = 14$ with a remainder of $2$. Therefore, it is $14 \frac{2}{7}\%$.

9. $6 \frac{3}{4} - 6.2$ (decimal answer)

Answer: $0.55$

Explanation: First convert the fraction to a decimal. $\frac{3}{4} = 0.75$, so $6 \frac{3}{4} = 6.75$. Now subtract: $6.75 - 6.20 = 0.55$.

10. $5 \cdot 4 \cdot 3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1 \cdot 0$

Answer: $0$

Explanation: By the zero property of multiplication, any number multiplied by zero equals zero.

11. $\frac{4.5}{0.18}$

Answer: $25$

Explanation: To eliminate the decimal in the denominator, multiply top and bottom by 100. The problem becomes $450 \div 18$. $450 \div 18 = 25$.

12. $\sqrt{1600}$

Answer: $40$

Explanation: Notice that $1600 = 16 \times 100$. The square root is $\sqrt{16} \times \sqrt{100} = 4 \times 10 = 40$.

13. $\sqrt{64} + 5^2 - \sqrt{25} \times (2 + 3)$

Answer: $8$

Explanation: Follow order of operations (PEMDAS):
Parentheses: $(2 + 3) = 5$
Exponents/Roots: $\sqrt{64} = 8$, $5^2 = 25$, $\sqrt{25} = 5$
Substitute back: $8 + 25 - 5 \times 5$
Multiply: $5 \times 5 = 25$
Add/Subtract left to right: $8 + 25 - 25 = 33 - 25 = 8$.

14. Solve this proportion: $\frac{15}{20} = \frac{24}{n}$

Answer: $n = 32$

Explanation: Cross-multiply to solve for $n$: $15 \times n = 20 \times 24$. This simplifies to $15n = 480$. Divide by 15: $n = 480 \div 15 = 32$. Alternatively, simplify the fraction first: $\frac{15}{20} = \frac{3}{4}$, so $\frac{3}{4} = \frac{24}{n}$. Multiply top and bottom by 8 to see $n = 32$.

15. $12 \frac{1}{2} \times 1 \frac{3}{5} \times 5$

Answer: $100$

Explanation: Convert to improper fractions: $\frac{25}{2} \times \frac{8}{5} \times \frac{5}{1}$. Cancel out terms to simplify: The $5$ in the numerator and denominator cancel. You are left with $\frac{25 \times 8}{2} = \frac{200}{2} = 100$.

16. $(4.2 \times 0.05) \div 7$

Answer: $0.03$

Explanation: First, multiply the numbers in parentheses: $4.2 \times 0.05 = 0.21$. Next, divide by 7: $0.21 \div 7 = 0.03$.

17. If the sales-tax rate is $7\%$, what is the tax on a $\$111.11$ purchase?

Answer: $\$7.78$

Explanation: Convert $7\%$ to a decimal ($0.07$) and multiply by the purchase amount: $\$111.11 \times 0.07 = 7.7777$. Since currency is rounded to the nearest cent (hundredth), it rounds up to $\$7.78$.

18. The table shows the percent of the population aged 25-64 with some senior high school education.
Data: Peru 44%, Iceland 57%, Poland 46%, Italy 43%, Greece 51%, Chile 46%, Luxembourg 53%
a. Find the mode of the data.
b. If the data were arranged from least to greatest, which country or countries would have the middle score?
c. What is the term used for the answer to problem b? Will this quantity always be the same as the mode in every set of data? Explain.

Answer:
a. $46\%$
b. Poland and Chile
c. Median. No.

Explanation:
a. The mode is the number that appears most frequently. $46\%$ appears twice.
b. Ordered data: 43(Italy), 44(Peru), 46(Poland), 46(Chile), 51(Greece), 53(Luxembourg), 57(Iceland). The middle (4th) score is 46%, representing Poland and Chile.
c. The middle number is called the Median. It will not always be the same as the mode; the median depends on the order and position of values, while the mode only relies on frequency.

19. Write the prime factorization of 900 using exponents.

Answer: $2^2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5^2$

Explanation: Break 900 down into prime factors: $900 = 9 \times 100 = (3 \times 3) \times (10 \times 10) = 3^2 \times (2 \times 5) \times (2 \times 5) = 3^2 \times 2^2 \times 5^2$. Grouping them sequentially gives $2^2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5^2$.

20. Think of two different prime numbers, and write them on your paper. Then write the greatest common factor (GCF) of the two prime numbers.

Answer: $1$

Explanation: Prime numbers only have factors of 1 and themselves. Therefore, any two *different* prime numbers (e.g., 3 and 5) share no factors other than 1. Their GCF is always 1.

21. The perimeter of a square is 2 meters. How many centimeters long is each side? Explain your thinking.

Answer: $50\text{ cm}$

Explanation: First, convert the perimeter to centimeters: $2\text{ meters} = 200\text{ centimeters}$. A square has 4 equal sides, so divide the total perimeter by 4: $200 \div 4 = 50\text{ cm}$.

22. Look at the triangle below.
a. What is the area of this triangle?
b. Is this an acute, right, or obtuse triangle?

Answer: a. $20\text{ cm}^2$ | b. Obtuse triangle

Explanation:
a. The area formula is $\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}bh$. The base $b$ is $8\text{ cm}$ and the vertical height $h$ (the dashed line outside) is $5\text{ cm}$. Area $= \frac{1}{2} \times 8 \times 5 = 20\text{ cm}^2$.
b. Because the altitude falls outside the base of the triangle, the interior angle opposite the longest side is greater than $90^\circ$. Therefore, it is an obtuse triangle.

23. Look at quadrilateral $ABCD$ below.
a. What is the measure of $\angle A$ in quadrilateral $ABCD$?
b. What is the measure of the exterior angle at $D$?

Answer: a. $85^\circ$ | b. $70^\circ$

Explanation:
a. The sum of interior angles of a quadrilateral is $360^\circ$. We know $\angle B = 90^\circ$ (indicated by the square), $\angle C = 75^\circ$, and $\angle D \text{ (interior)} = 110^\circ$.
$\angle A = 360^\circ - (90^\circ + 75^\circ + 110^\circ) = 360^\circ - 275^\circ = 85^\circ$.
b. Interior and exterior angles on a straight line add to $180^\circ$. $180^\circ - 110^\circ = 70^\circ$.

27. Draw $\overline{AC}$ $1 \frac{1}{4}$ inches long. Find and mark the midpoint of $\overline{AC}$, and label the midpoint $B$. What are the lengths of $\overline{AB}$ and $\overline{BC}$?

Answer: $\frac{5}{8}\text{ inch}$ each

Explanation: The midpoint divides the segment exactly in half. $1 \frac{1}{4}$ is equal to $\frac{5}{4}$. To find half of it, divide by 2 (or multiply by $\frac{1}{2}$): $\frac{5}{4} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{5}{8}$. Therefore, $\overline{AB} = \frac{5}{8}\text{ inch}$ and $\overline{BC} = \frac{5}{8}\text{ inch}$.

28. There are 32 cards in a bag. Eight of the cards have letters written on them. What is the chance of drawing a card with a letter written on it?

Answer: $\frac{1}{4}$ or $25\%$

Explanation: Probability = (favorable outcomes) / (total outcomes). Here, $\frac{8}{32}$. Divide top and bottom by 8 to simplify the fraction to $\frac{1}{4}$.

29. Compare: $1\text{ gallon} \bigcirc 4\text{ liters}$

Answer: $1\text{ gallon} < 4\text{ liters}$

Explanation: 1 gallon is approximately $3.785$ liters. Since $3.785 < 4$, 1 gallon is less than 4 liters.

Real-World Connection: Jesse displays trophies on 4 shelves in the family room. Two of the 6 trophies on each shelf are for soccer. How many trophies are NOT for soccer? Write one equation and use it to solve the problem.

Answer: $16\text{ trophies}$

Explanation:
Equation: $T = 4 \times (6 - 2)$
Find how many trophies on *each* shelf are not for soccer: $6 - 2 = 4$ non-soccer trophies per shelf. Since there are 4 shelves, multiply $4 \times 4$ to get a total of 16 trophies that are not for soccer.
