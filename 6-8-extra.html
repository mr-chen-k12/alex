Problem 12 Extensions: Parts of a Group As Fractions
Problem 12 - Variation 1
Two hundred thirty-nine of the one thousand birds in the wildlife sanctuary were flamingos. Flamingos made up what fraction of the birds in the sanctuary?
Correct Answer: C) \(\frac{239}{1000}\)
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Identify the targeted part of the group: there are \(239\) flamingos.
  • Identify the total number of items in the entire group: there are \(1,000\) birds.
  • Construct the group fraction by placing the subset size over the entire population baseline:
    $$\text{Fraction} = \frac{\text{Part}}{\text{Whole}} = \frac{239}{1000}$$
Problem 12 - Variation 2
Out of one hundred students surveyed at a middle school, fifty-seven preferred playing soccer over other sports. What fraction of the surveyed students preferred soccer?
Correct Answer: B) \(\frac{57}{100}\)
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • The size of the specific category group is \(57\) students.
  • The size of the entire survey collection population is \(100\) students.
  • Form the fraction matching this information:
    $$\text{Fraction} = \frac{\text{Students choosing soccer}}{\text{Total students surveyed}} = \frac{57}{100}$$
Problem 12 - Variation 3
Seven hundred three trees out of one thousand in an orchard were apple trees. What fraction of the trees in the orchard were apple trees?
Correct Answer: C) \(\frac{703}{1000}\)
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • The requested fraction must isolate the counts of apple trees against all available trees.
  • Apple trees count = \(703\). Total orchard baseline count = \(1,000\).
  • Arrange this data to create a proper rational part-to-whole fraction:
    $$\text{Fraction} = \frac{703}{1000}$$
Problem 21 Extensions: Double-Digit Multiplication Practice
Problem 21 - Variation 1
Calculate the product:

    43
 × 18
    ---
Correct Answer: A) 774
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Multiply \(43\) by the ones place digit (\(8\)):
    $$43 \times 8 = 344$$
  • Multiply \(43\) by the tens place digit (\(1\), which represents \(10\)):
    $$43 \times 10 = 430$$
  • Add the partial products to find the final result:
    $$\begin{array}{r} 344 \\ + \ 430 \\ \hline 774 \end{array}$$
Problem 21 - Variation 2

    ---

    $$\begin{array}{r} 100 \\ + \ 750 \\ \hline 850 \end{array}$$
