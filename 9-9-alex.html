Formula: Combining ($A + M = T$) & Separating ($S - G = R$)
Practice a Combining
$42$ pupils were in the room. Then $19$ more pupils came in. How many pupils were in the room in all? Write an equation and solve.
Answer: $42 + 19 = p$; $p = 61$ pupils

Step-by-step explanation:

1. Identify the formula for combining: $\text{Some} + \text{More} = \text{Total}$ ($A + M = T$).

2. Substitute given values: $\text{Some} = 42$, $\text{More} = 19$, $\text{Total} = p$.

3. Equation: $42 + 19 = p$.

4. Add the numbers: $42 + 19 = 61$.

Practice b Combining
Tim had some marbles. Then he bought $15$ more marbles. Now he has $52$ marbles. How many marbles did Tim have at first? Write an equation and solve.
Answer: $m + 15 = 52$; $m = 37$ marbles

Step-by-step explanation:

1. Formula: $\text{Some} + \text{More} = \text{Total}$.

2. Let $m$ be the starting number of marbles. Equation: $m + 15 = 52$.

3. Solve for $m$ by subtracting $15$ from $52$: $m = 52 - 15 = 37$.

Practice c Separating
The school choir had $65$ members. Some members graduated, leaving $42$ members. How many members graduated? Write an equation and solve.
Answer: $65 - g = 42$; $g = 23$ members

Step-by-step explanation:

1. Formula for separating: $\text{Starting Amount} - \text{Some Went Away} = \text{What Remains}$ ($S - G = R$).

2. Let $g$ be the number of graduating members: $65 - g = 42$.

3. Solve for $g$: $g = 65 - 42 = 23$.

7 [Lesson 8] Fractions & Degrees
What fraction of a full circle ($360^\circ$) is represented by an angle of $60^\circ$? Simplify the fraction.
Answer: $\frac{1}{6}$

Step-by-step explanation:

1. Write as a fraction: $\frac{60^\circ}{360^\circ}$.

2. Divide numerator and denominator by $60$: $\frac{60 \div 60}{360 \div 60} = \frac{1}{6}$.

9 [Lesson 5] Prime Factorization
Find the prime factorization of $120$ using exponents.
Answer: $2^3 \times 3 \times 5$

Step-by-step explanation:

1. Break down $120$: $120 = 12 \times 10$.

2. Factor $12 = 2 \times 2 \times 3$ and $10 = 2 \times 5$.

3. Combine prime factors: $2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 5 = 2^3 \times 3 \times 5$.

10 [Lesson 6] Long Division
Divide: $4,328 \div 15$. Write the answer with a remainder.
Answer: $288\text{ R }8$

Step-by-step explanation:

1. $43 \div 15 = 2$ with remainder $13$.

2. Bring down $2 \rightarrow 132$. $132 \div 15 = 8$ ($8 \times 15 = 120$), remainder $12$.

3. Bring down $8 \rightarrow 128$. $128 \div 15 = 8$ ($8 \times 15 = 120$), remainder $8$.

4. Result: $288\text{ R }8$.

Step-by-step explanation:

3. Since $81 \gt 64$, $3^4 \gt 4^3$.
