Practice i
Point \( A \) is located at \( 3\frac{1}{4} \text{ inches} \) and Point \( B \) is located at \( 8\frac{7}{8} \text{ inches} \) on a ruler. Find the distance \( AB \).
Final Answer
\( AB = 5\frac{5}{8} \text{ inches} \)

Subtract the smaller coordinate from the larger coordinate:

\[ 8\frac{7}{8} - 3\frac{1}{4} = 8\frac{7}{8} - 3\frac{2}{8} = 5\frac{5}{8} \text{ inches} \]

Practice j
A precision scale is marked in sixteenths of an inch. Assuming the scale is used correctly, what is the greatest possible measurement error that can occur? Express your answer as a fraction of an inch.
Final Answer
\( \frac{1}{32} \text{ inch} \)

The maximum measurement error is half of the smallest unit mark on the ruler scale:

\[ \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{16} = \frac{1}{32} \text{ inch} \]

Practice k
Arrange these fractions in order from least to greatest: \[ \frac{1}{3}, \quad \frac{1}{12}, \quad \frac{1}{4}, \quad \frac{1}{16} \]
Final Answer
\( \frac{1}{16}, \, \frac{1}{12}, \, \frac{1}{4}, \, \frac{1}{3} \)

When unit fractions have equal numerators (\(1\)), larger denominators correspond to smaller numeric values.

Problem 1 REPRESENT
Use digits and a comparison symbol to write "Four and two thirds is less than four and three quarters."
Final Answer
\( 4\frac{2}{3} < 4\frac{3}{4} \)

"Four and two thirds" is \( 4\frac{2}{3} \), "is less than" is \( < \), and "four and three quarters" is \( 4\frac{3}{4} \).

Problem 2
Point \( R \) is at \( 2\frac{1}{2} \text{ in.} \) and point \( S \) is at \( 7\frac{3}{8} \text{ in.} \) on a ruler. Find length \( RS \).
Final Answer
\( 4\frac{7}{8} \text{ inches} \)

Subtract position \( R \) from position \( S \):

\[ 7\frac{3}{8} - 2\frac{1}{2} = 7\frac{3}{8} - 2\frac{4}{8} = 6\frac{11}{8} - 2\frac{4}{8} = 4\frac{7}{8} \text{ inches} \]
