If the perimeter of a square is 20 cm, what is its area?

Answer: \(25\) cm\(^2\).

What is the area of a park that is 100 yards square?

Answer: \(10{,}000\) square yards.

Solution: "100 yards square" means a square 100 yd by 100 yd. Area \(= 100 \times 100 = 10{,}000\) square yards.

Write a squaring fact and a square root fact illustrated by this 4-by-4 square.

Answer: Squaring fact: \(4^2 = 16\). Square root fact: \(\sqrt{16} = 4\).

Written Practice — Problems 1–8

1. (13)

There were 628 students in 4 college dormitories. Each dormitory housed the same number of students. How many students were housed in each dormitory?

Answer: \(157\) students.

Solution: Divide the students equally among the 4 dormitories: \(628 \div 4 = 157\).

2. (11)

A candidate for the U.S. Senate from Arkansas wants to visit every county in Arkansas. She has already traveled to 36 counties. She has 39 left to visit. How many counties are there in Arkansas?

Answer: \(75\) counties.

Solution: Add the counties visited to those remaining: \(36 + 39 = 75\).

*4. (19, 20)

Choose the formula for the area of a square.

A \(P = 2L + 2W\)    B \(P = 4s\)    C \(A = s^2\)

Answer: C.

Solution: The area of a square is side squared: \(A = s^2\). Choices A and B are perimeter formulas.

*5. (4, 20) PREDICT

The rule of the following sequence is \(k = 2^n\). Find the sixth term of the sequence.

\(2, 4, 8, 16, \ldots\)

Answer: \(64\).

Solution: Substitute \(n = 6\) into the rule: \(k = 2^6 = 64\).

6. (4, 8)

a. Arrange these numbers in order from least to greatest: \(\frac{1}{3}, -2, 1, -\frac{1}{2}, 0\)

b. JUSTIFY Draw a number line with the numbers from a to show that your answer to a is correct.

c. Which of these numbers are not integers?

Answer: a. \(-2, -\frac{1}{2}, 0, \frac{1}{3}, 1\)    b. See the number line below.    c. \(\frac{1}{3}\) and \(-\frac{1}{2}\).

Solution: a. On the number line, values increase from left to right: \(-2\) is farthest left, then \(-\frac{1}{2}\), then \(0\), then \(\frac{1}{3}\), then \(1\). c. Integers are whole numbers (…, −2, −1, 0, 1, …), so the fractions \(\frac{1}{3}\) and \(-\frac{1}{2}\) are not integers.

7. (8)

Which is the best estimate of how much of this rectangle is shaded?

A 50%    B \(33\frac{1}{3}\)%    C 25%    D 60%

Answer: B — \(33\frac{1}{3}\)%.

Solution: The rectangle is divided into 3 equal parts and 1 part is shaded, so about \(\frac{1}{3} = 33\frac{1}{3}\)% is shaded.

8. (7)

Each angle of a rectangle is a right angle. Which two sides are perpendicular to side \(\overline{BC}\)?

Answer: Sides \(\overline{AB}\) and \(\overline{CD}\).

Solution: In rectangle \(DABC\), side \(\overline{BC}\) is the bottom side. The sides that meet it at the right angles at \(B\) and \(C\) are \(\overline{AB}\) and \(\overline{CD}\), so those two sides are perpendicular to \(\overline{BC}\).

Written Practice — Problems 9–14

*9. (20) EVALUATE

Simplify:

a. \(\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^3\)    b. \(10^4\)    c. \(\sqrt{12^2}\)

Answer: a. \(\frac{1}{27}\)    b. \(10{,}000\)    c. \(12\).

Solution: a. \(\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^3 = \frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{27}\). b. \(10^4 = 10 \cdot 10 \cdot 10 \cdot 10 = 10{,}000\). c. \(\sqrt{12^2} = \sqrt{144} = 12\).

10. (15)

For each fraction, find an equivalent fraction that has a denominator of 36:

a. \(\frac{2}{9}\)    b. \(\frac{3}{4}\)

c. EXPLAIN Name one reason we might want to find equivalent fractions.

Answer: a. \(\frac{8}{36}\)    b. \(\frac{27}{36}\)    c. To add or subtract fractions with different denominators (or to compare them).

Solution: a. \(9 \times 4 = 36\), so \(\frac{2 \times 4}{9 \times 4} = \frac{8}{36}\). b. \(4 \times 9 = 36\), so \(\frac{3 \times 9}{4 \times 9} = \frac{27}{36}\). c. Fractions must have a common denominator before they can be added or subtracted.

11. (6)

List the factors of each number:

a. 10    b. 7    c. 1

Answer: a. 1, 2, 5, 10    b. 1, 7    c. 1.

Solution: a. \(10 = 1 \times 10 = 2 \times 5\). b. 7 is prime: \(1 \times 7\). c. 1 has only one factor, itself.

*12. (16, 18)

The perimeter of a certain square is 2 feet. How many inches long is each side of the square?

Answer: \(6\) in.

Solution: Convert to inches: \(2\text{ ft} = 24\text{ in.}\) Each side \(= \frac{24\text{ in.}}{4} = 6\text{ in.}\)

*14. (14) GENERALIZE

What is the probability of rolling an even number with one roll of a number cube?

Answer: \(\frac{1}{2}\).

Solution: The even faces are 2, 4, and 6 — 3 favorable outcomes out of 6 possible: \(\frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}\).

19. (10, 15)

\(1\frac{8}{9} + 1\frac{7}{9}\)

Answer: \(3\frac{2}{3}\).

Solution: Add the whole numbers and the fractions: \((1+1) + \left(\frac{8}{9} + \frac{7}{9}\right) = 2 + \frac{15}{9} = 2 + 1\frac{6}{9} = 3\frac{6}{9} = 3\frac{2}{3}\).

23. (9)

\(\frac{3}{4} - \left(\frac{1}{4} + \frac{2}{4}\right)\)

Answer: \(0\).

Solution: First add inside the parentheses: \(\frac{1}{4} + \frac{2}{4} = \frac{3}{4}\). Then subtract: \(\frac{3}{4} - \frac{3}{4} = 0\).

24. (10)

\(\left(\frac{3}{4} - \frac{1}{4}\right) + \frac{2}{4}\)

Answer: \(1\).

Solution: First subtract inside the parentheses: \(\frac{3}{4} - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{2}{4}\). Then add: \(\frac{2}{4} + \frac{2}{4} = \frac{4}{4} = 1\).

Written Practice — Problems 25–27

25. (1, 9)

Evaluate the following expressions for \(m = 3\) and \(n = 10\):

a. \(\frac{m}{n} + \frac{m}{n}\)    b. \(\frac{m}{n} \cdot \frac{m}{n}\)

Answer: a. \(\frac{3}{5}\)    b. \(\frac{9}{100}\).

Solution: a. \(\frac{3}{10} + \frac{3}{10} = \frac{6}{10} = \frac{3}{5}\). b. \(\frac{3}{10} \cdot \frac{3}{10} = \frac{9}{100}\).

*26. (15) EVALUATE

Find a fraction equivalent to \(\frac{1}{2}\) that has a denominator of 10. Add \(\frac{3}{10}\) to that fraction and reduce the sum.

Answer: \(\frac{4}{5}\).

Solution: \(\frac{1}{2} = \frac{5}{10}\). Add: \(\frac{5}{10} + \frac{3}{10} = \frac{8}{10} = \frac{4}{5}\) (reduced).

27. (10, 15)

Write \(1\frac{4}{5}\) as an improper fraction. Then multiply the improper fraction by \(\frac{1}{3}\) and reduce the product.

Answer: \(\frac{3}{5}\).

Solution: \(1\frac{4}{5} = \frac{9}{5}\). Multiply: \(\frac{9}{5} \times \frac{1}{3} = \frac{9}{15} = \frac{3}{5}\) (reduced).

Written Practice — Problems 28–30

*28. (2, 9, 15)

Which properties are illustrated by these equations?

a. \(\frac{3}{4} \times \frac{4}{3} = 1\)    b. \(\frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{2}{2} = \frac{2}{6}\)

Answer: a. Inverse property of multiplication.    b. Identity property of multiplication.

Solution: a. \(\frac{3}{4}\) and \(\frac{4}{3}\) are reciprocals; a number times its reciprocal equals 1 (inverse property). b. \(\frac{2}{2} = 1\), and multiplying by 1 leaves the value unchanged (identity property).

*29. (19, 20) GENERALIZE

A common floor tile is 12 inches square.

a. What is the perimeter of a common floor tile?

b. What is the area of a common floor tile?

Answer: a. \(48\) in.    b. \(144\) in\(^2\).

Solution: a. Perimeter \(= 4s = 4 \times 12 = 48\) in. b. Area \(= s^2 = 12^2 = 144\) square inches.

*30. (19)

What is the perimeter of this hexagon?

Answer: \(36\) in.

Solution: Trace the 6 sides: top \(5\), right side \(8\), bottom \(10\), left side \(4\), inner step \(10-5 = 5\), inner down \(8-4 = 4\). Adding: \(5 + 8 + 10 + 4 + 5 + 4 = 36\) in.

Real-World Connection

Baseboards line the perimeter of a room, covering the joint formed by the wall and the floor. A new house requiring baseboards has rooms with the following dimensions (in feet):

12 by 12      6 by 8
10 by 12      15 by 10
24 by 10      12 by 11

a.

How many total feet of baseboards is this? Show your work.

Answer: \(284\) ft.

Solution: Find each room's perimeter (\(P = 2l + 2w\)): \(12 \times 12 \to 48\); \(6 \times 8 \to 28\); \(10 \times 12 \to 44\); \(15 \times 10 \to 50\); \(24 \times 10 \to 68\); \(12 \times 11 \to 46\). Total: \(48 + 28 + 44 + 50 + 68 + 46 = 284\) ft.

b.

If the baseboard comes in 8-foot sections, how many sections must be bought? Show your work. (Assume that sections of baseboard can be joined together as needed.)

Answer: \(36\) sections.

Solution: Divide the total footage by 8: \(\frac{284}{8} = 35.5\). Since sections can't be bought in halves, round up: 36 sections must be bought.
