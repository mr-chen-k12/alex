1. Difference between sum and product of 0.8 and 0.2.
Sum: \( 0.8 + 0.2 = 1.0 \)
Product: \( 0.8 \times 0.2 = 0.16 \)
Difference: \( 1.0 - 0.16 \)
Answer: 0.84
2. Difference in elevation between Mt. McKinley (20,310 ft above sea level) and Badwater Basin (282 ft below sea level).
\( 20,310 - (-282) = 20,310 + 282 \)
Answer: 20,592 ft
3. Temperature drop from 45° by 18°. What is the final temperature?
\( 45 - 18 \)
