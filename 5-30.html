Problem d
Thirty is \( \frac{5}{6} \) of what number?
Answer: 36
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Set up the equation: \( 30 = \frac{5}{6}x \).
  • To solve for \( x \), multiply both sides by the reciprocal \( \frac{6}{5} \):
    $$x = 30 \times \frac{6}{5} = \frac{180}{5} = 36$$
Problem e
Five eighths of the students in the class were girls. If there were 20 girls in the class, how many students were in the class altogether?
Answer: 32 students
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Let \( x \) be the total number of students. We know \( \frac{5}{8}x = 20 \).

Problem 1
Three fourths of the townspeople voted. If 240 of the townspeople voted, how many people live in the town?
Answer: 320 people
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Let \( T \) be the total townspeople. \( \frac{3}{4}T = 240 \).

Problem 2
If 155 children are separated as equally as possible into four groups, how many will be in each group? (Write four numbers, one for each of the four groups.)
Answer: 39, 39, 39, 38
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Divide 155 by 4: \( 155 \div 4 = 38 \) with a remainder of 3.
  • This means the base group size is 38. Since there is a remainder of 3, three of the groups will have one extra child (38 + 1 = 39).
  • Therefore, the groups will be 39, 39, 39, and 38.

Problem 4
If the area of the square is 64 m², then what is the area of the circle inscribed within it? (Use 3.14 for \( \pi \).)
Answer: 50.24 m²
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • The area of the square is \( s^2 = 64 \), so the side length \( s \) is 8 m.
  • The circle fits perfectly inside the square, so its diameter is 8 m, which means its radius \( r \) is 4 m.
  • Area of the circle:
    $$\pi r^2 = 3.14 \times (4)^2 = 3.14 \times 16 = 50.24 \text{ m}^2$$

Problem 30
a. Which of these figures has the greatest number of lines of symmetry?
A (Rhombus), B (Regular Hexagon), C (Scalene Triangle)
b. Which of these figures has rotational symmetry?
Answer: a. B (Regular Hexagon)  |  b. A and B
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • a. A rhombus generally has 2 lines of symmetry (the diagonals), a regular hexagon has 6, and a scalene triangle has 0.
  • b. A rhombus has rotational symmetry of 180° (order 2), and a regular hexagon has rotational symmetry of 60° (order 6). A scalene triangle only maps to itself at 360°, which doesn't count as rotational symmetry.
Real-World Connection
You and your friends are going to a local arcade. Arcade A charges a $12 admission fee but only $0.50 per game. Arcade B charges a $5 admission fee but $1.00 per game.
a. If you and your friends plan to play 10 games, which arcade has the better deal? Show all your work.
b. Which arcade is the better value if you play 20 games?
Answer: a. Arcade B  |  b. Arcade A
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • a. For 10 games:
    Arcade A cost: \( \$12 + (\$0.50 \times 10) = \$12 + \$5 = \$17 \)
    Arcade B cost: \( \$5 + (\$1.00 \times 10) = \$5 + \$10 = \$15 \)
    Arcade B is cheaper for 10 games.
  • b. For 20 games:
    Arcade A cost: \( \$12 + (\$0.50 \times 20) = \$12 + \$10 = \$22 \)
    Arcade B cost: \( \$5 + (\$1.00 \times 20) = \$5 + \$20 = \$25 \)
    Arcade A is cheaper for 20 games.
