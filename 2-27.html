Lesson Practice (Page 449)

a. The radius of this circle is 4 units. Estimate the area of the circle by counting the squares within the circle.
Answer: Approximately 50 square units.

Explanation: If you count the fully enclosed squares and piece together the partial squares along the edge, you will find roughly 50 full squares. We can verify this with the formula \(A \approx 3.14 \times 4^2 = 3.14 \times 16 = 50.24\).
b. Calculate the area of a circle with a radius of 4 cm. (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\))
Answer: \(50.24 \text{ cm}^2\)

Explanation: Use the formula \(A = \pi r^2\).
\(A \approx 3.14 \times 4^2 = 3.14 \times 16 = 50.24\) square centimeters.
c. Calculate the area of a circle with a radius of 2 feet. (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\))
Answer: \(12.56 \text{ ft}^2\)

Explanation: Use the formula \(A = \pi r^2\).
\(A \approx 3.14 \times 2^2 = 3.14 \times 4 = 12.56\) square feet.
d. Calculate the area of a circle with a diameter of 2 feet. (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\))
Answer: \(3.14 \text{ ft}^2\)

Explanation: First, find the radius. The radius is half the diameter, so \(r = 1\) foot.
\(A = \pi r^2 \approx 3.14 \times 1^2 = 3.14 \times 1 = 3.14\) square feet.
e. Calculate the area of a circle with a diameter of 10 inches. (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\))
Answer: \(78.5 \text{ in}^2\)

Explanation: Find the radius first: \(10 \div 2 = 5\) inches.
\(A = \pi r^2 \approx 3.14 \times 5^2 = 3.14 \times 25 = 78.5\) square inches.

Written Practice

1. What is the quotient when the decimal number ten and six tenths is divided by four hundredths?
Answer: \(265\)

Explanation: Set up the division: \(10.6 \div 0.04\).
To clear the decimal in the divisor, multiply both numbers by 100: \(1060 \div 4\).
\(1060 \div 4 = 265\).
2. The time in Los Angeles is 3 hours earlier than the time in New York. If it is 1:15 p.m. in New York, what time is it in Los Angeles?
Answer: \(10:15 \text{ a.m.}\)

Explanation: Subtract 3 hours from 1:15 p.m.
1 hour earlier is 12:15 p.m.
2 hours earlier is 11:15 a.m.
3 hours earlier is 10:15 a.m.
3. Geraldine paid with a $10 bill for 1 dozen keychains that cost 75¢ each. How much should she get back in change?
Answer: \(\$1.00\)

Explanation: 1 dozen = 12 keychains.
Total cost = \(12 \times \$0.75 = \$9.00\).
Change = \(\$10.00 - \$9.00 = \$1.00\).
4. \(32 + 1.8(50)\)
Answer: \(122\)

Explanation: Use order of operations (PEMDAS), so multiply first:
\(1.8 \times 50 = 90\)
Then add: \(32 + 90 = 122\).
5. If each block has a volume of one cubic inch, what is the volume of this tower?
Answer: \(24 \text{ cubic inches}\)

Explanation: Count the blocks in the diagram. The base is 3 blocks long and 2 blocks wide (\(3 \times 2 = 6\) blocks per layer). The tower is 4 layers high.
Total blocks = \(6 \times 4 = 24\). Since each is 1 cubic inch, the volume is 24 cubic inches.
6. The ratio of hardbacks to paperbacks in the school library was 5 to 2. If there were 600 hardbacks, how many paperbacks were there?
Answer: \(240 \text{ paperbacks}\)

Explanation: Set up a proportion: \(\frac{\text{Hardbacks}}{\text{Paperbacks}} = \frac{5}{2} = \frac{600}{x}\).
Solve for \(x\): \(5x = 1200\), so \(x = 240\).
7. Nate missed three of the 20 questions on the test. What percent of the questions did he miss?
Answer: \(15\%\)

Explanation: Fraction missed = \(\frac{3}{20}\).
Convert to percent by multiplying by 100: \(\frac{3}{20} \times 100 = 3 \times 5 = 15\%\).
8. The credit card company charges 1.5% (0.015) interest on the unpaid balance each month. If Mr. Jones has an unpaid balance of $2000, how much interest does he need to pay this month?
Answer: \(\$30\)

Explanation: Multiply the balance by the interest rate decimal.
\(2000 \times 0.015 = 30\).
9. a. Write \(\frac{4}{5}\) as a decimal number.
b. Write \(\frac{4}{5}\) as a percent.
Answer: a. \(0.8\)    b. \(80\%\)

Explanation:
a. Divide 4 by 5 to get the decimal: \(4 \div 5 = 0.8\).
b. Multiply the decimal by 100 to get the percent: \(0.8 \times 100 = 80\%\).
10. Serena is stuck on a multiple-choice question that has four choices. She has no idea what the correct answer is, so she just guesses. What is the probability that her guess is correct?
Answer: \(\frac{1}{4}\) or \(25\%\)

Explanation: There is 1 correct choice out of 4 total possible choices. The probability is \(\frac{1}{4}\), which is equal to 25%.
11. \(5 \frac{1}{2} + 3 \frac{7}{8}\)
Answer: \(9 \frac{3}{8}\)

Explanation: Find a common denominator (8).
\(5 \frac{4}{8} + 3 \frac{7}{8} = 8 \frac{11}{8}\).
Since \(\frac{11}{8} = 1 \frac{3}{8}\), add that to 8: \(8 + 1 \frac{3}{8} = 9 \frac{3}{8}\).
12. \(3 \frac{1}{4} - \frac{5}{8}\)
Answer: \(2 \frac{5}{8}\)

Explanation: Find a common denominator (8).
\(3 \frac{2}{8} - \frac{5}{8}\). Because 2 is smaller than 5, borrow 1 from the whole number 3 (which is \(\frac{8}{8}\)).
\(2 \frac{10}{8} - \frac{5}{8} = 2 \frac{5}{8}\).
13. \((4 \frac{1}{2})(\frac{2}{3})\)
Answer: \(3\)

Explanation: Convert the mixed number to an improper fraction: \(4 \frac{1}{2} = \frac{9}{2}\).
Multiply: \(\frac{9}{2} \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{18}{6} = 3\).
14. \(12 \frac{1}{2} \div 100\)
Answer: \(0.125\) or \(\frac{1}{8}\)

Explanation: Convert to decimals: \(12.5 \div 100\). Dividing by 100 moves the decimal two places to the left, yielding \(0.125\). As a fraction, this is \(\frac{1}{8}\).
15. \(5 \div 1 \frac{1}{2}\)
Answer: \(3 \frac{1}{3}\)

Explanation: Convert to improper fractions: \(5 \div \frac{3}{2}\).
Multiply by the reciprocal: \(5 \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{10}{3}\).
Convert back to mixed number: \(3 \frac{1}{3}\).
16. \(\frac{5}{6} \text{ of } \$30\)
Answer: \(\$25\)

Explanation: "Of" means multiply. \(\frac{5}{6} \times 30 = \frac{150}{6} = 25\).
17. \(4.72 + 12 + n = 50.4\)
Answer: \(n = 33.68\)

Explanation: Combine the known numbers on the left: \(16.72 + n = 50.4\).
Subtract 16.72 from both sides: \(n = 50.4 - 16.72 = 33.68\).
18. \(\$10 - m = \$9.87\)
Answer: \(m = \$0.13\)

Explanation: Rearrange the equation: \(m = \$10.00 - \$9.87\).
\(m = \$0.13\).
19. \(3n = 0.48\)
Answer: \(n = 0.16\)

Explanation: Divide both sides by 3: \(n = \frac{0.48}{3} = 0.16\).
20. \(\frac{w}{8} = \frac{25}{20}\)
Answer: \(w = 10\)

Explanation: Cross multiply: \(20w = 8 \times 25\).
\(20w = 200\). Divide by 20: \(w = 10\).
(Alternatively, simplify \(\frac{25}{20}\) to \(\frac{5}{4}\), then multiply by 8: \(w = \frac{5}{4} \times 8 = 10\)).
21. What are the next three terms in this sequence of perfect squares?
\(1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49, 64, 81, 100, \_\_\_, \_\_\_, \_\_\_\)
Answer: \(121, 144, 169\)

Explanation: The sequence is \(1^2, 2^2, 3^2, ... , 10^2\). The next three are \(11^2 = 121\), \(12^2 = 144\), and \(13^2 = 169\).
22. This parallelogram is divided into two congruent triangles. The base is 15 cm, the height is 10 cm.
a. What is the area of the parallelogram?
b. What is the area of each triangle?
Answer: a. \(150 \text{ cm}^2\)    b. \(75 \text{ cm}^2\)

Explanation:
a. Area of parallelogram = \(base \times height = 15 \times 10 = 150\).
b. The two triangles are congruent, so they equally split the area: \(150 \div 2 = 75\).
23. Sydney drew a circle with a radius of 10 cm. What was the approximate area of the circle? (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\).)
Answer: \(314 \text{ cm}^2\)

Explanation: Formula: \(A = \pi r^2\).
\(A \approx 3.14 \times 10^2 = 3.14 \times 100 = 314\) square centimeters.
24. Choose the appropriate unit for the area of a garage.
A. square inches    B. square feet    C. square miles
Answer: B. square feet

Explanation: Square inches are too small (used for paper), and square miles are too large (used for cities). Square feet is the standard unit for measuring building space.
25. Which two ratios form a proportion? How do you know?
\(\frac{9}{12}\), \(\frac{8}{14}\), \(\frac{12}{21}\), \(\frac{20}{36}\)
Answer: \(\frac{8}{14}\) and \(\frac{12}{21}\)

Explanation: Reduce each fraction to its simplest form to compare:
\(\frac{9}{12} = \frac{3}{4}\)
\(\frac{8}{14} = \frac{4}{7}\)
\(\frac{12}{21} = \frac{4}{7}\)
\(\frac{20}{36} = \frac{5}{9}\)
Since \(\frac{8}{14}\) and \(\frac{12}{21}\) both simplify to \(\frac{4}{7}\), they form a proportion.
26. The wheel of a covered wagon turned around once in about 12 feet. The diameter of the wheel was about
A. 6 feet    B. 4 feet    C. 3 feet    D. 24 feet
Answer: B. 4 feet

Explanation: The distance of one full turn is the circumference (\(C = 12\) feet).
The formula for circumference is \(C = \pi \times d\).
\(12 \approx 3.14 \times d\). To estimate, use \(\pi \approx 3\).
\(12 \approx 3 \times d\), which means \(d \approx 4\) feet.
27. Anabel drove her car 348 miles in 6 hours. Divide the distance by the time to find the average speed of the car.
Answer: \(58 \text{ miles per hour}\)

Explanation: Speed = Distance / Time.
\(348 \div 6 = 58\).
28. The opposite angles of a parallelogram are congruent. The adjacent angles are supplementary. If \(\angle X\) measures \(110^\circ\), then what are the measures of \(\angle Y\) and \(\angle Z\)?
Answer: \(\angle Y = 70^\circ\), \(\angle Z = 110^\circ\)

Explanation: \(\angle Y\) is adjacent to \(\angle X\), so it must be supplementary (adding to \(180^\circ\)): \(180^\circ - 110^\circ = 70^\circ\).
\(\angle Z\) is opposite to \(\angle X\), so it must be congruent (equal): \(110^\circ\).
29. The diameter of each wheel on a lawn mower is 10 inches. How far must the lawn mower be pushed in order for each wheel to complete one full turn? Round the answer to the nearest inch. (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\).)
Answer: \(31 \text{ inches}\)

Explanation: One full turn equals the circumference of the wheel.
\(C = \pi \times d \approx 3.14 \times 10 = 31.4\) inches.
Rounding 31.4 to the nearest inch gives 31.
30. The coordinates of three vertices of a parallelogram are \((-3, 3)\), \((2, 3)\), and \((4, -1)\). What are the coordinates of the fourth vertex?
Answer: \((-1, -1)\)

Explanation: The top side of the parallelogram connects \((-3, 3)\) to \((2, 3)\), which is a horizontal line of length 5 (from \(x=-3\) to \(x=2\)).
The bottom side must also be a horizontal line of length 5. One bottom vertex is \((4, -1)\). The other vertex must be 5 units to the left of it to complete the shape.
\(x\text{-coordinate} = 4 - 5 = -1\). The \(y\text{-coordinate}\) remains \(-1\). Thus, the point is \((-1, -1)\).
Real-World Connection: The Great Frigates are large birds with long, slender wings. Frigates are great flyers and have one of the greatest wingspan-to-weight ratios of all birds. If a 3-pound Great Frigate bird has a wingspan of 6 feet, what would be the approximate wingspan of a 4-pound Great Frigate bird? Assume that the ratio of wingspan to weight is fairly constant.
Answer: \(8 \text{ feet}\)

Explanation: Set up a proportion using the constant ratio of wingspan to weight:
\(\frac{6 \text{ feet}}{3 \text{ lbs}} = \frac{x \text{ feet}}{4 \text{ lbs}}\)
\(2 = \frac{x}{4}\)
Multiply both sides by 4: \(x = 8\).
