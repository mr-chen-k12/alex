Problem a Ratio
The ratio of boys to girls in the cafeteria was 6 to 5. If there were 60 girls, how many boys were there?

Answer: 72 boys

Explanation:

We are given the ratio of Boys to Girls: \(\frac{\text{Boys}}{\text{Girls}} = \frac{6}{5}\).

We know the actual count of girls is 60. We can set up a proportion or find the constant factor.

$$ \frac{6}{5} = \frac{x}{60} $$

To get from 5 to 60, we multiply by 12 (since \(60 \div 5 = 12\)).

Multiply the numerator (boys part of the ratio) by the same factor:

$$ 6 \times 12 = 72 $$

Problem b Ratio

Answer: 64 ants

Ratio of Ants to Flies = \(8 : 3\).

Find the constant factor: \(24 \div 3 = 8\).

Problem 1 Statistics
What is the mean of 96, 49, 68, and 75? What is the range?

Answer: Mean = 72, Range = 47

Explanation:

Mean (Average): Add the numbers and divide by the count.

Sum: \(96 + 49 + 68 + 75 = 288\)

Count: 4 numbers.

Mean: \(288 \div 4 = 72\).

Range: Subtract the smallest number from the largest number.

Largest: 96, Smallest: 49.

Range: \(96 - 49 = 47\).

Problem 2 Measurement
The average depth of the ocean beyond the edges of the continents is \(2\frac{1}{2}\) miles. How many feet is that? (1 mile = 5280 ft)

Answer: 13,200 feet

Explanation:

Convert \(2\frac{1}{2}\) miles to a decimal: 2.5 miles.

Multiply by the number of feet in a mile:

$$ 2.5 \times 5280 = 13,200 $$

Problem 3 Formulate
The 168 girls who signed up for soccer were divided equally into 12 teams. How many players were on each team? Write an equation and solve the problem.

Answer: 14 players

Equation: \(12p = 168\) (where p is players per team)

Explanation:

To find the number of players (p), divide the total girls by the number of teams:

$$ p = \frac{168}{12} = 14 $$

Problem 4 Geometry
a. What is the perimeter of the parallelogram?
b. What is the area of the parallelogram?

Answer a (Perimeter): 16 inches

Perimeter is the sum of outer sides: \(3 + 5 + 3 + 5 = 16\).

Answer b (Area): 12 square inches

Since the sides are 3, 4, and 5, the triangles inside are Right Triangles ($3^2 + 4^2 = 5^2$).

This means the side of length 3 is perpendicular to the diagonal of length 4. Wait, usually in a parallelogram, the height is perpendicular to the base.

Looking at the right-angle symbols in the diagram: The angle at B (inside triangle ABD) and D (inside triangle CDB) are right angles. This means the base is 3 and height is 4 (or vice versa) for the triangle.

Area of one triangle = \(\frac{1}{2} \times 3 \times 4 = 6\).

Total Area = Area of 2 triangles = \(6 + 6 = 12\).

Problem 5 Geometry
a. What is the perimeter of each triangle?
b. What is the area of each triangle?

Answer a: 12 inches

Perimeter = sum of the three sides of the triangle (3, 4, and 5).

\(3 + 4 + 5 = 12\).

Answer b: 6 square inches

Problem 6 Conclude
This quadrilateral has one pair of parallel sides. What is the name of this kind of quadrilateral?

Answer: Trapezoid

Problem 7 Verify
"All squares are rectangles." True or false?

Answer: True

A rectangle is a quadrilateral with four right angles. A square has four right angles (and equal sides), so it fits the definition of a rectangle.

Problem 29 Algebra

Answer: 24

Explanation: Substitute the values into the formula:

$$ A = \frac{1}{2} \times 6 \times 8 $$

$$ A = 3 \times 8 = 24 $$

Problem 30 Model
Draw a pair of parallel segments that are the same length. Form a quadrilateral by drawing two segments between the endpoints of the parallel segments. Is the quadrilateral a parallelogram?

Answer: Yes

Explanation: If a quadrilateral has one pair of sides that are both equal in length and parallel, the quadrilateral is necessarily a parallelogram.
