Lesson Practice

Note: Problems a-c refer to Quadrilateral ABCD divided into two triangles by segment AC.

a. What is the sum of \(m\angle 1, m\angle 2\), and \(m\angle 3\)?
Answer: 180°

Explanation: Angles 1, 2, and 3 represent the three interior angles of \(\Delta ABC\). The sum of the interior angles of any triangle is always \(180^\circ\).

b. What is the sum of \(m\angle 4, m\angle 5\), and \(m\angle 6\)?
Answer: 180°

Explanation: Angles 4, 5, and 6 represent the three interior angles of \(\Delta ADC\). Like any triangle, their sum is \(180^\circ\).

c. GENERALIZE: What is the sum of the measures of the four interior angles of the quadrilateral?
Answer: 360°

Explanation: The quadrilateral is composed of the two triangles from parts (a) and (b). By adding the sum of the angles of both triangles, we get \(180^\circ + 180^\circ = 360^\circ\).

d. What is \(m\angle P\) in \(\Delta PQR\)? (Given \(m\angle Q = 75^\circ\) and \(m\angle R = 30^\circ\))
Answer: 75°

Explanation: The sum of angles in a triangle is \(180^\circ\).
\(m\angle P = 180^\circ - (m\angle Q + m\angle R)\)
\(m\angle P = 180^\circ - (75^\circ + 30^\circ) = 180^\circ - 105^\circ = 75^\circ\).

e. What is the measure of each interior angle of a regular quadrilateral?
Answer: 90°

Explanation: A regular quadrilateral is a square. All four angles are equal, and their sum is \(360^\circ\).
\(360^\circ \div 4 = 90^\circ\).

f. MODEL: Elizabeth made five left turns as she ran around the park. Draw a sketch that shows the turns in her run around the park. Then find the average number of degrees in each turn.
Answer: 72°

Explanation: Running completely around a park and returning to the original direction means completing one full rotation, which is \(360^\circ\). These turns represent the exterior angles of the path.
Average degrees per turn = \(360^\circ \div 5 = 72^\circ\).

Written Practice

1. When the sum of \(\frac{1}{2}\) and \(\frac{1}{4}\) is divided by the product of \(\frac{1}{2}\) and \(\frac{1}{4}\), what is the quotient?
Answer: 6

Explanation:
Sum: \(\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{2}{4} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{3}{4}\)
Product: \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{8}\)
Quotient: \(\frac{3}{4} \div \frac{1}{8} = \frac{3}{4} \cdot \frac{8}{1} = \frac{24}{4} = 6\).

2. ANALYZE: Jenny is \(5 \frac{1}{2}\) feet tall. She is how many inches tall?
Answer: 66 inches

Explanation: There are 12 inches in a foot.
\(5 \frac{1}{2} \text{ feet} = 5.5 \times 12 \text{ inches} = 66 \text{ inches}\).

3. If \(\frac{4}{5}\) of the 200 runners finished the race, how many runners did not finish the race?
Answer: 40 runners

Explanation: If \(\frac{4}{5}\) finished, then \(\frac{1}{5}\) did not finish.
\(\frac{1}{5} \text{ of } 200 = 200 \div 5 = 40\).

4. Lines \(p\) and \(q\) are parallel.
a. Which angle is an alternate interior angle to \(\angle 2\)?
b. If \(\angle 2\) measures \(85^\circ\), what are the measures of \(\angle 6\) and \(\angle 7\)?
Answer: a. ∠8, b. m∠6 = 85° and m∠7 = 95°

Explanation:
Based on the diagram layout where vertical lines \(p\) and \(q\) are cut by horizontal transversal \(m\):
a. Alternate interior angles are on opposite sides of the transversal and between the parallel lines. The alternate interior angle to \(\angle 2\) is \(\angle 8\).
b. \(\angle 2\) and \(\angle 6\) are corresponding angles, so they are equal: \(m\angle 6 = 85^\circ\). \(\angle 6\) and \(\angle 7\) form a linear pair (supplementary), so \(m\angle 7 = 180^\circ - 85^\circ = 95^\circ\).

5. ANALYZE: The circumference of the earth is about 25,000 miles. Write that distance in expanded notation using exponents.
Answer: (2 × 10⁴) + (5 × 10³)

Explanation: The digit 2 is in the ten-thousands place (\(10^4\)) and the digit 5 is in the thousands place (\(10^3\)). Expanded: \( (2 \times 10^4) + (5 \times 10^3) \).

6. ESTIMATE: Use a ruler to measure the diameter of a quarter to the nearest sixteenth of an inch. How can you use that information to find the radius and the circumference of the quarter?
Answer: Divide by 2 for radius; multiply by π for circumference.

Explanation: (A standard US quarter has a diameter of roughly \(\frac{15}{16}\) inch). To find the radius, divide the measured diameter by 2 (\(r = \frac{d}{2}\)). To find the circumference, multiply the measured diameter by pi (\(C = \pi d\)).

7. CONNECT: Which of these bicycle wheel parts is the best model of the circumference of the wheel?
A. spoke    B. axle    C. tire
Answer: C. tire

Explanation: The circumference is the distance around the outside of a circle. The tire forms the outside boundary of the bicycle wheel.

8. PREDICT: As this sequence continues, each term equals the sum of the two previous terms. What is the next term in this sequence?
1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, ...
Answer: 21

Explanation: This is the Fibonacci sequence. The next term is found by adding the two previous numbers: \(8 + 13 = 21\).

9. If there is a 20% chance of rain, what is the probability that it will not rain?
Answer: 80%

Explanation: The sum of all probabilities must equal 100%.
\(100\% - 20\% \text{ (chance of rain)} = 80\% \text{ (chance of no rain)}\).

10. Write \(1 \frac{1}{3}\) as a percent.
Answer: 133 1/3 %

Explanation: To convert a fraction to a percent, multiply by 100%.
\(1 \frac{1}{3} = \frac{4}{3}\).
\(\frac{4}{3} \times 100\% = \frac{400}{3}\% = 133 \frac{1}{3}\%\).

11. ANALYZE: \(0.08w = \$0.60\)
Answer: w = 7.5

Explanation: Divide both sides by 0.08 to isolate \(w\).
\(w = \frac{0.60}{0.08} = \frac{60}{8} = 7.5\).

12. \(\frac{1 - 0.001}{0.03}\)
Answer: 33.3

Explanation: First, subtract the numerator: \(1 - 0.001 = 0.999\).
Then, divide by the denominator: \(\frac{0.999}{0.03} = \frac{999}{30} = 33.3\).

13. \(3 \frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{3}{100}\)
Answer: 1/10

Explanation: Convert the mixed number to an improper fraction:
\(3 \frac{1}{3} = \frac{10}{3}\).
Multiply the fractions: \(\frac{10}{3} \cdot \frac{3}{100} = \frac{30}{300} = \frac{1}{10}\).

14. If the volume of each small block is one cubic inch, what is the volume of this rectangular prism?
Answer: 24 cubic inches

Explanation: The prism in the image is 4 blocks wide, 2 blocks deep, and 3 blocks high.
Volume = \(length \times width \times height = 4 \times 2 \times 3 = 24\) blocks.
Since each block is 1 cubic inch, the total volume is 24 cubic inches.

15. \(6 \frac{1}{2} + 4.95\) (decimal)
Answer: 11.45

Explanation: Convert \(6 \frac{1}{2}\) to a decimal: \(6.5\).
Add the decimals: \(6.50 + 4.95 = 11.45\).

16. \(2 \frac{1}{6} - 1.5\) (fraction)
Answer: 2/3

Explanation: Convert \(1.5\) to a fraction: \(1 \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{2}\). Convert \(2 \frac{1}{6}\) to an improper fraction: \(\frac{13}{6}\).
Find a common denominator (6): \(\frac{3}{2} = \frac{9}{6}\).
Subtract: \(\frac{13}{6} - \frac{9}{6} = \frac{4}{6}\).
Simplify: \(\frac{4}{6} = \frac{2}{3}\).

17. If a shirt costs $19.79 and the sales-tax rate is 6%, what is the total price including tax? Explain how you can check your answer using estimation.
Answer: $20.98

Explanation:
Tax = \( \$19.79 \times 0.06 = 1.1874 \approx \$1.19 \).
Total Price = \( \$19.79 + \$1.19 = \$20.98 \).
Estimation Check: Round $19.79 to $20.00. 6% of $20 is $1.20. $20.00 + $1.20 = $21.20. Since the actual price is slightly less than $20, our answer of $20.98 is reasonable.

18. What fraction of a foot is 3 inches?
Answer: 1/4

Explanation: There are 12 inches in a foot. The fraction is \(\frac{3}{12}\), which simplifies to \(\frac{1}{4}\).

19. What percent of a meter is 3 centimeters?
Answer: 3%

Explanation: There are 100 centimeters in a meter. 3 out of 100 is represented as \(\frac{3}{100}\), which is exactly \(3\%\).

20. The ratio of children to adults in the theater was 5 to 3. If there were 45 children, how many adults were there?
Answer: 27 adults

Explanation: Set up a proportion: \(\frac{\text{Children}}{\text{Adults}} = \frac{5}{3} = \frac{45}{x}\).
Multiply by a scale factor: \(5 \times 9 = 45\), so multiply the bottom by 9 as well.
\(3 \times 9 = 27\).

21. Arrange these numbers in order from least to greatest:
\(1, -1, 0, \frac{1}{2}, -\frac{1}{2}\)
Answer: -1, -1/2, 0, 1/2, 1

Explanation: Negative numbers are smaller than positive numbers. Larger negative numbers have smaller values. Order on a number line: \(-1, -\frac{1}{2}, 0, \frac{1}{2}, 1\).

22. CLASSIFY: These two triangles together form a quadrilateral with only one pair of parallel sides. What type of quadrilateral is formed?
Answer: Trapezoid

Explanation:
By definition, a quadrilateral with exactly one pair of parallel sides is called a trapezoid.

23. CONCLUDE: Do the triangles in this quadrilateral appear to be congruent or not congruent?
Answer: Not congruent

Explanation: Based on the visual diagram, the two triangles have different side lengths and angles, meaning they are not the exact same size and shape.

24. a. ANALYZE: What is the measure of \(\angle A\) in \(\Delta ABC\)?
b. ANALYZE: What is the measure of the exterior angle marked \(x\)?
Answer: a. m∠A = 30°, b. x = 70°

Explanation:
a. The interior angles of \(\Delta ABC\) shown are \(\angle C = 40^\circ\) and \(\angle B = 110^\circ\). The sum of angles in a triangle is \(180^\circ\).
\(m\angle A = 180^\circ - (110^\circ + 40^\circ) = 180^\circ - 150^\circ = 30^\circ\).
b. Angle \(x\) is the exterior angle adjacent to the interior angle at \(B\). Exterior and adjacent interior angles are supplementary (add up to \(180^\circ\) on a straight line).
\(x = 180^\circ - 110^\circ = 70^\circ\).

25. Write 40% as a:
a. simplified fraction.
b. simplified decimal number.
Answer: a. 2/5, b. 0.4

Explanation:
a. \(40\% = \frac{40}{100}\). Divide top and bottom by 20 to simplify: \(\frac{2}{5}\).
b. \(40\% = \frac{40}{100} = 0.40 = 0.4\).

26. The diameter of this circle is 20 mm. What is the area of the circle? (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\).)
Answer: 314 mm²

Explanation:
First, find the radius: \(r = \frac{diameter}{2} = \frac{20}{2} = 10\text{ mm}\).
Area formula: \(A = \pi r^2\).
\(A \approx 3.14 \times (10)^2 = 3.14 \times 100 = 314\text{ mm}^2\).

27. \(2^3 + \sqrt{81} \div 3^2 + \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2\)
Answer: 9.25 (or 9 1/4)

Explanation: Follow the order of operations (PEMDAS/GEMDAS).
Exponents and roots: \(2^3 = 8\), \(\sqrt{81} = 9\), \(3^2 = 9\), \(\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{4}\) (or \(0.25\)).
Substitute back into equation: \(8 + 9 \div 9 + 0.25\).
Division: \(9 \div 9 = 1\).
Addition: \(8 + 1 + 0.25 = 9.25\).

28. Multiply 120 inches by 1 foot per 12 inches.
\(\frac{120\text{ in.}}{1} \times \frac{1\text{ ft}}{12\text{ in.}}\)
Answer: 10 ft

Explanation: Multiply the fractions and cancel the units of inches.
\(\frac{120}{12}\text{ ft} = 10\text{ ft}\).

29. A bag contains 20 red marbles and 15 blue marbles.
a. What is the ratio of red marbles to blue marbles?
b. If one marble is drawn from the bag, what is the probability that the marble will be blue?
Answer: a. 4:3 (or 4/3), b. 3/7

Explanation:
a. Ratio = Red / Blue = \( \frac{20}{15} \). Divide top and bottom by 5 to get \(\frac{4}{3}\).
b. Total marbles = \(20 + 15 = 35\). Probability of blue = \( \frac{\text{Blue}}{\text{Total}} = \frac{15}{35} \). Simplify by dividing by 5 to get \(\frac{3}{7}\).

30. CONCLUDE: An architect drew a set of plans for a house. In the plans, the roof is supported by a triangular framework. When the house is built, two sides of the framework will be 19 feet long and the base will be 33 feet long. Classified by side length, what type of triangle will be formed?
Answer: Isosceles triangle

Explanation:
A triangle with at least two sides of equal length (in this case, the two 19-foot sides) is classified as an isosceles triangle.
