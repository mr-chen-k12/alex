There were 744 students divided equally among 6 auditoriums. How many students were in each auditorium?

Answer: 124 students.

Solution: Divide the students equally: 744÷6=124.

9. (18)

The test scores of five students were 82, 90, 88, 94, and 86. What was the average (mean) test score?

Answer: 88.

Solution: Sum of scores =82+90+88+94+86=440. Average =440÷5=88.

10. (14)

In a school club, the ratio of boys to girls is 3 to 5. If there are 40 members in total, how many girls are in the club?

Answer: 25 girls.

Solution: Total parts =3+5=8. Each part =40÷8=5. Number of girls =5×5=25.

11. (7)

On a coordinate plane, three vertices of a rectangle are (−2,3), (4,3), and (4,−1). What are the coordinates of the fourth vertex, and what is the area of the rectangle?

Answer: Fourth vertex: (−2,−1); Area =24 square units.

Solution: Length =4−(−2)=6; Width =3−(−1)=4. Fourth vertex aligns horizontally with (4,−1) and vertically with (−2,3) to give (−2,−1). Area =6×4=24.

Solution: Prime numbers between 1 and 8 are 2, 3, 5, and 7 (4 outcomes). Probability =48=12.

31. (13) — Terminal Decimal Division

Divide: 14.28÷0.35

Answer: 40.8

Solution: Move decimal point 2 places to the right for both numbers: 1428÷35=40.8. The quotient terminates cleanly at the tenths place.

32. (13) — Infinite Repeating Decimal Division

Divide 7.4÷0.3 and write the quotient with a bar over the repeating digit.

Answer: 24.6¯ (or 24.666…)

Solution: Move decimal point 1 place right: 74÷3=24.666…. The digit 6 repeats infinitely, written as 24.6¯.

33. (16) — Percentage Problem

In a class of 35 students, 80% of the students passed a math exam. How many students passed the exam, and how many did not pass?

Answer: 28 students passed; 7 students did not pass.

Solution: Number who passed =0.80×35=28. Number who did not pass =35−28=7.

34. (5, 20) — Order of Operations (PEMDAS)

Simplify: −3.5+34×(−2.4+6.8)÷(−0.5)2

Answer: 9.7

Solution:
1. Parentheses: −2.4+6.8=4.4
2. Exponents: (−0.5)2=0.25
3. Convert fraction: 34=0.75
4. Multiply/Divide left to right: 0.75×4.4=3.3; then 3.3÷0.25=13.2
5. Add signed numbers: −3.5+13.2=9.7
