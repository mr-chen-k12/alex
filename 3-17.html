Unit Multipliers & Ratios

1. Cancel numbers and units before multiplying: $\frac{15 \text{ dollars}}{1 \text{ hour}} \times \frac{40 \text{ hours}}{1}$
Answer: 600 dollars

Explanation: Cancel the "hours" unit. Multiply the numbers: $15 \times 40 = 600$. The remaining unit is "dollars".
2. $\frac{12 \text{ dollars}}{1 \text{ kg}} \times \frac{3.5 \text{ kg}}{1}$
Answer: 42 dollars

Explanation: Cancel the "kg" unit. Multiply $12 \times 3.5 = 42$.
3. $\frac{60 \text{ words}}{1 \text{ minute}} \times \frac{15 \text{ minutes}}{1}$
Answer: 900 words

Explanation: Cancel the "minutes" unit. Multiply $60 \times 15 = 900$. The remaining unit is "words".
4. $\frac{500 \text{ miles}}{10 \text{ gallons}} \times \frac{25 \text{ gallons}}{1}$
Answer: 1250 miles

Explanation: Cancel "gallons". Simplify $\frac{500}{10}$ to $50$. Multiply $50 \times 25 = 1250 \text{ miles}$.
5. Multiply 14 days by 24 hours per day.
Answer: 336 hours

Explanation: Setup the ratio: $14 \text{ days} \times \frac{24 \text{ hours}}{1 \text{ day}}$. Days cancel. $14 \times 24 = 336 \text{ hours}$.
6. Multiply 5.4 kilometers by 1000 meters per kilometer.
Answer: 5400 meters

Explanation: Multiply $5.4 \times 1000$. Move the decimal point three places to the right to get 5400.
7. How far will a car travel in 4.5 hours at 60 miles per hour?
Answer: 270 miles

Explanation: $\frac{4.5 \text{ hours}}{1} \times \frac{60 \text{ miles}}{1 \text{ hour}}$. Hours cancel out. $4.5 \times 60 = 270 \text{ miles}$.

General Math Practice

8. What is the total price of a $55.00 item when an 8% sales tax is added?
Answer: $59.40

Explanation: Find the tax: $55.00 \times 0.08 = 4.40$. Add the tax to the original price: $55.00 + 4.40 = 59.40$.
9. Sarah is 1.85 meters tall. How many centimeters tall is she?
Answer: 185 cm

Explanation: Multiply $1.85 \text{ m} \times 100 \text{ cm/m}$. The decimal shifts two places to the right.
10. If $\frac{3}{7}$ of the 42 apples were bruised, how many apples were bruised?
Answer: 18 apples

Explanation: Multiply the fraction by the total: $\frac{3}{7} \times 42$. Divide 42 by 7 to get 6, then multiply by 3: $6 \times 3 = 18$.
11. Write this number in standard notation: $(4 \times 1000) + (2 \times 10) + (9 \times \frac{1}{100})$
Answer: 4020.09

Explanation: Calculate the parts: $4000 + 20 + 0.09$. Add them together to get 4020.09.
12. Change $\frac{3}{8}$ to its percent equivalent.
Answer: 37.5% or $37 \frac{1}{2}\%$

Explanation: $\frac{3}{8} \times 100\% = \frac{300}{8}\%$. Divide 300 by 8 to get 37.5.
13. What is the percent equivalent of 1.75?
Answer: 175%

Explanation: Multiply by 100 and add the percent sign: $1.75 \times 100 = 175$.
14. How much money is 40% of $25.00?
Answer: $10.00

Explanation: Convert 40% to 0.40. Multiply: $25.00 \times 0.40 = 10.00$.
15. The minute hand of a clock turns $90^\circ$ in how many minutes?
Answer: 15 minutes

Explanation: A full circle is $360^\circ$ (60 minutes). $90^\circ$ is one-quarter of a circle, which is $\frac{60}{4} = 15$ minutes.
16. The circumference of a tire is 8 feet. How many complete turns does it make to travel 64 feet?
Answer: 8 turns

Explanation: Divide total distance by the circumference: $64 \div 8 = 8$ turns.
17. A rectangular stack of 1-cubic-foot boxes is 2 boxes wide, 4 boxes long, and 5 boxes high. What is the volume?
Answer: 40 cubic feet

Explanation: Volume = $length \times width \times height = 4 \times 2 \times 5 = 40$.
18. $\frac{2}{3} + \frac{4}{7}$
Answer: $1 \frac{5}{21}$

Explanation: Common denominator is 21. $\frac{14}{21} + \frac{12}{21} = \frac{26}{21}$. Convert to mixed number: $1 \frac{5}{21}$.
19. $15 \frac{1}{4} - 9 \frac{3}{4}$
Answer: $5 \frac{1}{2}$

Explanation: Borrow 1 from 15 to make it $14 \frac{5}{4}$. Subtract: $14 \frac{5}{4} - 9 \frac{3}{4} = 5 \frac{2}{4}$, which simplifies to $5 \frac{1}{2}$.
20. $2 \frac{1}{2} \times 1 \frac{3}{5}$
Answer: 4

Explanation: Convert to improper fractions: $\frac{5}{2} \times \frac{8}{5}$. The 5s cancel out, leaving $\frac{8}{2} = 4$.
21. $\frac{3^4}{3^2}$
Answer: 9

Explanation: Subtract exponents: $3^{(4-2)} = 3^2$. And $3^2 = 9$.
22. How many fifths are in $3 \frac{2}{5}$?
Answer: 17

Explanation: Convert to an improper fraction: $\frac{3 \times 5 + 2}{5} = \frac{17}{5}$. There are 17 fifths.
23. $14.2 + 3.05 + 0.87$
Answer: 18.12

Explanation: Line up the decimals: $14.20 + 3.05 + 0.87 = 18.12$.
24. $(2.5)^2$
Answer: 6.25

Explanation: Multiply $2.5 \times 2.5$. $25 \times 25 = 625$. With two decimal places total, it becomes 6.25.
25. $8.1 \div 0.09$
Answer: 90

Explanation: Move the decimal point two places to the right for both numbers: $810 \div 9 = 90$.
26. A circle has a radius of 10 cm. What is its Area? (Use 3.14 for $\pi$)
Answer: 314 $\text{cm}^2$

Explanation: Area = $\pi r^2 = 3.14 \times 10^2 = 3.14 \times 100 = 314$.
27. Arrange in order from least to greatest: $\frac{1}{3}, 30\%, 0.35$
Answer: $30\%, \frac{1}{3}, 0.35$

Explanation: Convert to decimals: $30\% = 0.30$, $\frac{1}{3} \approx 0.333$, $0.35 = 0.35$. Order is $0.30 < 0.333 < 0.35$.
28. Find the unknown number: $x - 4.2 = 7.5$
Answer: $x = 11.7$

Explanation: Add 4.2 to both sides: $7.5 + 4.2 = 11.7$.
29. Find the unknown number: $\frac{3}{5} = \frac{y}{20}$
Answer: $y = 12$

Explanation: Cross multiply: $5y = 60$. Divide by 5: $y = 12$. Alternatively, note that $5 \times 4 = 20$, so multiply the top by 4: $3 \times 4 = 12$.
30. If a standard 6-sided die is rolled, what is the probability of rolling a number greater than 4? Express as a fraction.
Answer: $\frac{1}{3}$

Explanation: The numbers greater than 4 are 5 and 6. That's 2 favorable outcomes out of 6 possible outcomes. $\frac{2}{6}$ simplifies to $\frac{1}{3}$.
