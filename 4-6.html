Lesson Practice

Simplify the following:
a. $-5 + -3 - -4 + -2$
b. $-6 + (+3) - (+5) - (-4)$
c. $+4 + -2 - +5 + +7 - -1$
d. $5 + (-2) - (-7) - (+4) + (+1)$

Explanations: Convert subtractions to additions of the opposite, then combine from left to right.

  • a. $-5 - 3 + 4 - 2 = -8 + 4 - 2 = -4 - 2 = $ $-6$
  • b. $-6 + 3 - 5 + 4 = -3 - 5 + 4 = -8 + 4 = $ $-4$
  • c. $4 - 2 - 5 + 7 + 1 = 2 - 5 + 7 + 1 = -3 + 7 + 1 = 4 + 1 = $ $5$
  • d. $5 - 2 + 7 - 4 + 1 = 3 + 7 - 4 + 1 = 10 - 4 + 1 = 6 + 1 = $ $7$

Written Practice

1. A square pyramid has how many more edges than faces?

Explanation: A square pyramid has 5 faces (1 square base, 4 triangular sides) and 8 edges (4 on the base, 4 extending to the peak). The difference is $8 - 5 = 3$.

Answer: 3

2. A baby weighed 6 lb 12 oz at birth. At her one-month checkup, she weighed 9 lb 2 oz. How many pounds and ounces did the baby gain?

Explanation: Subtract the birth weight from the one-month weight.
$9\text{ lb } 2\text{ oz} - 6\text{ lb } 12\text{ oz}$. Since 2 oz is less than 12 oz, borrow 1 lb (16 oz) from the 9 lbs.
$8\text{ lb } (2+16)\text{ oz} = 8\text{ lb } 18\text{ oz}$.
Now subtract: $8\text{ lb } 18\text{ oz} - 6\text{ lb } 12\text{ oz} = 2\text{ lb } 6\text{ oz}$.

Answer: 2 lb 6 oz

3. There are 15 apples and 20 oranges in a fruit basket. What is the ratio of apples to oranges?

Explanation: The ratio is $\frac{\text{Apples}}{\text{Oranges}} = \frac{15}{20}$. Reduce the fraction by dividing the numerator and denominator by 5 to get $\frac{3}{4}$.

Answer: 3/4

4. A volleyball team's win-loss ratio was 3 to 2. If the team played 30 games total (with no ties), how many games did they win?

Explanation: Total ratio parts = $3 \text{ (wins)} + 2 \text{ (losses)} = 5 \text{ parts}$.
Total games = 30. Divide total games by total parts: $\frac{30}{5} = 6 \text{ games per part}$.
Multiply by the win parts: $3 \times 6 = 18 \text{ wins}$.

Answer: 18 games

5. If Sarah tosses a standard coin and rolls a standard number cube, what is the probability of the coin landing heads up and the number cube stopping on an even number?

Explanation: These are independent events. Multiply their probabilities.
$P(\text{Heads}) = \frac{1}{2}$. $P(\text{Even}) = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}$.
$P(\text{Heads and Even}) = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{4}$.

Answer: 1/4

6. a. What is the perimeter of this parallelogram?
b. What is the area of this parallelogram?

Explanation:
a. Perimeter is the distance around the outside. $P = 2(\text{base}) + 2(\text{side}) = 2(12) + 2(6) = 24 + 12 = 36\text{ m}$.
b. Area is base $\times$ height. $A = 12\text{ m} \times 5\text{ m} = 60\text{ m}^2$.

Answer: a. 36 m; b. 60 m$^2$

7. If an acute angle of a parallelogram measures $55^\circ$, what is the measure of each obtuse angle?

Explanation: Consecutive angles in a parallelogram are supplementary (they add up to $180^\circ$).
$180^\circ - 55^\circ = 125^\circ$.

Answer: $125^\circ$

8. The center of this circle is the origin. The circle passes through (4, 0).
a. What is the length of the radius?
b. Calculate the area of the circle using 3.14 for $\pi$.

Explanation:
a. The circle goes from the origin (0,0) to (4,0), which is 4 units.
b. The radius is 4. Area $= \pi r^2 \approx 3.14 \times (4)^2 = 3.14 \times 16 = 50.24$.

Answer: a. 4 units; b. 50.24 sq units

9. Which ratio forms a proportion with $\frac{4}{7}$?
A $\frac{8}{15}$     B $\frac{12}{21}$     C $\frac{7}{4}$     D $\frac{16}{24}$

Explanation: Reduce each option to see which equals $\frac{4}{7}$. Option B ($\frac{12}{21}$) reduces to $\frac{4}{7}$ if you divide both the top and bottom by 3.

Answer: B

10. Complete this proportion: $\frac{5}{12} = \frac{x}{36}$

Explanation: Notice that $12 \times 3 = 36$. Multiply the numerator by the same factor: $5 \times 3 = 15$. Alternatively, cross-multiply: $12x = 5 \times 36 \rightarrow 12x = 180 \rightarrow x = 15$.

Answer: 15

11. What is the perimeter of the polygon at right? Dimensions are in inches.

Explanation: Add all the outer sides together. We know the top is 10, right is 5, inner horizontal is 5. Wait, looking at the full dimensions: Top = 10, Total Left = 10. The rightmost edge is 5. So the inner vertical edge must be $10 - 5 = 5$. The bottom edge is 5, so the inner horizontal edge is $10 - 5 = 5$.
Perimeter $= 10 \text{ (left)} + 10 \text{ (top)} + 5 \text{ (right)} + 5 \text{ (inner horizontal)} + 5 \text{ (inner vertical)} + 5 \text{ (bottom)} = 40\text{ in}$.

Answer: 40 in

15. A board game is on sale for 20% off the regular price of $15.00. What is the sale price?

Explanation: If it is 20% off, you pay 80% of the price.
Sale Price = $0.80 \times 15.00 = 12.00$.

Answer: $12.00

16. Between which two consecutive whole numbers is $\sqrt{200}$?

Explanation: Find perfect squares near 200.
$14^2 = 196$ and $15^2 = 225$. Since 200 is between 196 and 225, $\sqrt{200}$ is between 14 and 15.

Answer: 14 and 15

17. Compare: $\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^3 \bigcirc$ the probability of flipping 3 heads in a row with a fair coin

Explanation:
Left side: $\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^3 = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{8}$.
Right side: $P(\text{3 heads}) = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{8}$.
They are equal.

Answer: $=$

18. Divide 0.945 by 0.05 and round the quotient to the nearest whole number.

Explanation: $0.945 \div 0.05 = 94.5 \div 5 = 18.9$.
Rounding 18.9 to the nearest whole number gives 19.

Answer: 19

19. The average of five numbers is 20. What is the sum of the five numbers?

Explanation: Average $= \frac{\text{Sum}}{\text{Count}}$.
Therefore, $\text{Sum} = \text{Average} \times \text{Count} = 20 \times 5 = 100$.

Answer: 100

20. Write the prime factorization of 420 using exponents.

Explanation: Create a factor tree for 420.
$420 = 42 \times 10$
$42 = 6 \times 7 = 2 \times 3 \times 7$
$10 = 2 \times 5$
Combine all prime factors: $2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 5 \times 7 = 2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 7$.

Answer: $2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 7$

21. $-8 + -3 - -6 - +4$

Explanation: Change subtractions to adding the opposite.
$-8 - 3 + 6 - 4 = -11 + 6 - 4 = -5 - 4 = -9$.

Answer: $-9$

22. $3^4 + 5^2 \times 2 - \sqrt{81} \times 2^3$

Explanation: Follow order of operations (PEMDAS/GEMDAS).
Exponents and roots first: $81 + 25 \times 2 - 9 \times 8$.
Multiplication: $81 + 50 - 72$.
Addition and Subtraction: $131 - 72 = 59$.

Answer: 59

23. How many blocks 1 inch on each edge would it take to fill a box that is 12 inches long, 6 inches wide, and 5 inches tall?

Explanation: Find the volume of the box in cubic inches.
$V = \text{length} \times \text{width} \times \text{height} = 12 \times 6 \times 5 = 360$. Since each block is 1 cubic inch, it takes 360 blocks.

Answer: 360 blocks

24. Three eighths of the 40 students ride the bus to school. How many students do NOT ride the bus?

Explanation: If $\frac{3}{8}$ ride the bus, then $\frac{5}{8}$ do not ride the bus.
$\frac{5}{8} \text{ of } 40 = (40 \div 8) \times 5 = 5 \times 5 = 25$.

Answer: 25 students

25. Use a unit multiplier to find how far a car would travel in 4 hours at a steady speed of 65 miles per hour.
$\frac{65 \text{ miles}}{1 \text{ hr}} \times \frac{4 \text{ hr}}{1}$

Explanation: Multiply the given fractions, canceling the "hr" units.
$65 \times 4 = 260\text{ miles}$.

Answer: 260 miles
