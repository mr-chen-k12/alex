Lesson Practice

Simplify the following:
a. $-7 + -2 - -5 + -3$
b. $-4 + (+6) - (+8) - (-2)$
c. $+3 + -5 - +2 + +8 - -4$
d. $8 + (-3) - (-5) - (+6) + (+2)$

Explanations: Convert subtractions to additions of the opposite, then combine from left to right.

  • a. $-7 - 2 + 5 - 3 = -9 + 5 - 3 = -4 - 3 = $ $-7$
  • b. $-4 + 6 - 8 + 2 = 2 - 8 + 2 = -6 + 2 = $ $-4$
  • c. $3 - 5 - 2 + 8 + 4 = -2 - 2 + 8 + 4 = -4 + 8 + 4 = 4 + 4 = $ $8$
  • d. $8 - 3 + 5 - 6 + 2 = 5 + 5 - 6 + 2 = 10 - 6 + 2 = 4 + 2 = $ $6$

Written Practice

1. A hexagonal prism has how many more edges than faces?

Explanation: A hexagonal prism has 8 faces (2 hexagonal bases, 6 rectangular sides) and 18 edges (6 on each hexagon base, 6 connecting the bases). The difference is $18 - 8 = 10$.

Answer: 10

2. A package weighed 7 lb 14 oz before extra padding was added. After padding, it weighed 11 lb 6 oz. How much weight was added in pounds and ounces?

Explanation: Subtract the original weight from the new weight.
$11\text{ lb } 6\text{ oz} - 7\text{ lb } 14\text{ oz}$. Since 6 oz is less than 14 oz, borrow 1 lb (16 oz) from the 11 lbs.
$10\text{ lb } (6+16)\text{ oz} = 10\text{ lb } 22\text{ oz}$.
Now subtract: $10\text{ lb } 22\text{ oz} - 7\text{ lb } 14\text{ oz} = 3\text{ lb } 8\text{ oz}$.

Answer: 3 lb 8 oz

3. There are 12 cars and 16 trucks in a parking lot. What is the ratio of cars to trucks?

Explanation: The ratio is $\frac{\text{Cars}}{\text{Trucks}} = \frac{12}{16}$. Reduce the fraction by dividing the numerator and denominator by 4 to get $\frac{3}{4}$.

Answer: 3/4

4. A basketball team's win-loss ratio was 5 to 2. If the team played 35 games total, how many games did they win?

Explanation: Total ratio parts = $5 \text{ (wins)} + 2 \text{ (losses)} = 7 \text{ parts}$.
Total games = 35. Divide total games by total parts: $\frac{35}{7} = 5 \text{ games per part}$.
Multiply by the win parts: $5 \times 5 = 25 \text{ wins}$.

Answer: 25 games

5. If Daniel tosses a coin and rolls a standard number cube, what is the probability of the coin landing tails up and the number cube landing on a number greater than 4?

Explanation: These are independent events. The numbers greater than 4 on a standard cube are 5 and 6 (2 outcomes).
$P(\text{Tails}) = \frac{1}{2}$. $P(>4) = \frac{2}{6} = \frac{1}{3}$.
$P(\text{Tails and } >4) = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{6}$.

Answer: 1/6

6. a. What is the perimeter of this parallelogram?
b. What is the area of this parallelogram?

Explanation:
a. Perimeter is the distance around the outside. $P = 2(\text{base}) + 2(\text{side}) = 2(15) + 2(8) = 30 + 16 = 46\text{ in}$.
b. Area is base $\times$ height. $A = 15\text{ in} \times 6\text{ in} = 90\text{ in}^2$.

Answer: a. 46 in; b. 90 in$^2$

7. If an acute angle of a parallelogram measures $48^\circ$, what is the measure of each obtuse angle?

Explanation: Consecutive angles in a parallelogram are supplementary (they add up to $180^\circ$).
$180^\circ - 48^\circ = 132^\circ$.

Answer: $132^\circ$

8. The center of this circle is the origin. The circle passes through (5, 0).
a. What is the length of the radius?
b. Calculate the area of the circle using 3.14 for $\pi$.

Explanation:
a. The circle goes from the origin (0,0) to (5,0), which is 5 units.
b. The radius is 5. Area $= \pi r^2 \approx 3.14 \times (5)^2 = 3.14 \times 25 = 78.5$.

Answer: a. 5 units; b. 78.5 sq units

9. Which ratio forms a proportion with $\frac{5}{8}$?
A $\frac{10}{24}$     B $\frac{15}{24}$     C $\frac{8}{5}$     D $\frac{20}{30}$

Explanation: Reduce each option to see which equals $\frac{5}{8}$. Option B ($\frac{15}{24}$) reduces to $\frac{5}{8}$ if you divide both the top and bottom by 3.

Answer: B

10. Complete this proportion: $\frac{6}{14} = \frac{x}{35}$

Explanation: Cross-multiply to solve for x:
$14 \times x = 6 \times 35$
$14x = 210$
$x = 210 \div 14 = 15$.

Answer: 15

11. What is the perimeter of the polygon at right? Dimensions are in centimeters.

Explanation: Find the missing inner sides.
The total left side is 12, and the right-most side is 4. The inner vertical side must be $12 - 4 = 8$.
The total bottom side is 15, and the top-most side is 8. The inner horizontal side must be $15 - 8 = 7$.
Perimeter $= 12 \text{ (left)} + 8 \text{ (top)} + 8 \text{ (inner vertical)} + 7 \text{ (inner horizontal)} + 4 \text{ (right)} + 15 \text{ (bottom)} = 54\text{ cm}$.

Answer: 54 cm

15. A sweater is on sale for 25% off the regular price of $24.00. What is the sale price of the sweater?

Explanation: If it is 25% off, you pay 75% of the price.
Sale Price = $0.75 \times 24.00 = 18.00$.

Answer: $18.00

16. Between which two consecutive whole numbers is $\sqrt{300}$?

Explanation: Find perfect squares near 300.
$17^2 = 289$ and $18^2 = 324$. Since 300 is between 289 and 324, $\sqrt{300}$ is between 17 and 18.

Answer: 17 and 18

17. Compare: $\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^2 \bigcirc$ the probability of rolling a 3 on two standard number cubes simultaneously.

Explanation:
Left side: $\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{9}$.
Right side: $P(\text{two 3s}) = \frac{1}{6} \times \frac{1}{6} = \frac{1}{36}$.
Since $\frac{1}{9}$ is greater than $\frac{1}{36}$, the left side is larger.

Answer: $>$

18. Divide 1.26 by 0.04 and round the quotient to the nearest whole number.

Explanation: $1.26 \div 0.04 = 126 \div 4 = 31.5$.
Rounding 31.5 to the nearest whole number gives 32.

Answer: 32

19. The average of six numbers is 12. What is the sum of the six numbers?

Explanation: Average $= \frac{\text{Sum}}{\text{Count}}$.
Therefore, $\text{Sum} = \text{Average} \times \text{Count} = 12 \times 6 = 72$.

Answer: 72

20. Write the prime factorization of 540 using exponents.

Explanation: Create a factor tree for 540.
$540 = 54 \times 10$
$54 = 6 \times 9 = 2 \times 3 \times 3 \times 3$
$10 = 2 \times 5$
Combine all prime factors: $2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 \times 3 \times 5 = 2^2 \cdot 3^3 \cdot 5$.

Answer: $2^2 \cdot 3^3 \cdot 5$

21. $-6 + -4 - -8 - +5$

Explanation: Change subtractions to adding the opposite.
$-6 - 4 + 8 - 5 = -10 + 8 - 5 = -2 - 5 = -7$.

Answer: $-7$

22. $4^3 + 6^2 \times 2 - \sqrt{100} \times 2^2$

Explanation: Follow order of operations (PEMDAS/GEMDAS).
Exponents and roots first: $64 + 36 \times 2 - 10 \times 4$.
Multiplication: $64 + 72 - 40$.
Addition and Subtraction: $136 - 40 = 96$.

Answer: 96

23. How many blocks 1 inch on each edge would it take to fill a box that is 15 inches long, 4 inches wide, and 6 inches tall?

Explanation: Find the volume of the box in cubic inches.
$V = \text{length} \times \text{width} \times \text{height} = 15 \times 4 \times 6 = 360$. Since each block is 1 cubic inch, it takes 360 blocks.

Answer: 360 blocks

24. Four ninths of the 45 students in the class play a sport. How many students do NOT play a sport?

Explanation: If $\frac{4}{9}$ play a sport, then $\frac{5}{9}$ do not.
$\frac{5}{9} \text{ of } 45 = (45 \div 9) \times 5 = 5 \times 5 = 25$.

Answer: 25 students

25. Use a unit multiplier to find how far a truck would travel in 5 hours at a steady speed of 55 miles per hour.
$\frac{55 \text{ miles}}{1 \text{ hr}} \times \frac{5 \text{ hr}}{1}$

Explanation: Multiply the given fractions, canceling the "hr" units.
$55 \times 5 = 275\text{ miles}$.

Answer: 275 miles
