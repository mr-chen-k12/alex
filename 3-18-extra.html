Math Practice Worksheet - Set 3

1. A farmer plants 8 rows of apple trees in an orchard. If there are 15 trees in each row, how many apple trees are there in total?

Answer: 120 trees

Explanation: Multiply the number of rows by the number of trees per row: 8 × 15 = 120.

2. Find the perimeter and area of this rectangle:

(7 units wide by 4 units high)

Answer: Perimeter = 22 units, Area = 28 sq units

Explanation:
Perimeter is the distance around the outside: 7 + 4 + 7 + 4 = 22 units.
Area is the space inside (length × width): 7 × 4 = 28 square units.

3. Write the following number using digits: "Sixty-two and nineteen hundredths."

Answer: 62.19

Explanation: "Sixty-two" is the whole number. The word "and" represents the decimal point. "Nineteen hundredths" translates to 0.19.

4. A chocolate bar is broken into 12 equal pieces. Mark eats 5 of the pieces. What fraction of the chocolate bar is left?

Answer: \(\frac{7}{12}\)

Explanation: The whole bar is \(\frac{12}{12}\). Subtract the pieces eaten: \(\frac{12}{12} - \frac{5}{12} = \frac{7}{12}\).

5. In problem 4, is the fraction of the chocolate bar left more than 50% or less than 50%?

Answer: More than 50%

Explanation: Exactly half (50%) of 12 pieces is 6 pieces, or \(\frac{6}{12}\). Since 7 pieces are left (\(\frac{7}{12}\)), it is more than 50%.

6. An octagon and a pentagon are drawn on a whiteboard. What is the total number of sides of both polygons combined?

Answer: 13 sides

Explanation: An octagon has 8 sides, and a pentagon has 5 sides. 8 + 5 = 13.

7. To what mixed number is the arrow pointing?

Answer: \( 8 \frac{3}{5} \)

Explanation: The space between 8 and 9 is divided into 5 equal sections (fifths). The arrow points to the third mark after the 8, making it \( 8 \frac{3}{5} \).

8. The Mariana Trench reaches thirty-six thousand, thirty-seven feet below sea level. Use digits to write that number.

Answer: 36,037

Explanation: Translating the word format into numbers: "Thirty-six thousand" is 36,000, and "thirty-seven" is 37. Combined, it is 36,037.

9. A train travels 150 miles in 3 hours at a steady speed. How many miles does it travel in 1 hour?

Answer: 50 miles

Explanation: Divide the total distance by the total time: 150 ÷ 3 = 50.

10. Estimate the sum of 5,812 and 2,190 by rounding each number to the nearest thousand before adding.

Answer: 8,000

Explanation:
5,812 rounded to the nearest thousand is 6,000.
2,190 rounded to the nearest thousand is 2,000.
6,000 + 2,000 = 8,000.

11. $30.00 - ($15.25 + $2.50)

Answer: $12.25

Explanation: Solve the parentheses first: $15.25 + $2.50 = $17.75. Then subtract that sum from thirty dollars: $30.00 - $17.75 = $12.25.

12. \( 7^2 - 2^2 \)

Answer: 45

Explanation: Find the squares first: \( 7^2 = 49 \) and \( 2^2 = 4 \). Then subtract: 49 - 4 = 45.

13. 56.48 - 23.15

Answer: 33.33

Explanation: Align the decimals and subtract straight down: 8-5=3, 4-1=3, 6-3=3, 5-2=3. The answer is 33.33.

14. 65¢ × 5

Answer: 325¢ or $3.25

Explanation: Multiply 65 by 5 to get 325. 325 cents is equal to 3 dollars and 25 cents ($3.25).

15. $5.20 × 3

Answer: $15.60

Explanation: Multiply ignoring the decimal initially: 520 × 3 = 1560. Place the decimal back in two spots from the right: $15.60.

16. $8.45 × 6

Answer: $50.70

Explanation: Multiply 845 × 6 = 5070. Moving the decimal two places to the left gives $50.70.

17. Rewrite this addition problem as a multiplication problem and find the answer:

    28 + 28 + 28

Answer: 3 × 28 = 84

Explanation: The number 28 is added 3 times. This translates to multiplication: 3 × 28 = 84.

18. 72 × 30

Answer: 2,160

Explanation: Multiply 72 by 3 to get 216, then attach the zero to the end to get 2,160.

19. \( 7 \overline{) 165 } \)

Answer: 23 R 4

Explanation: 7 goes into 16 two times (14), leaving a remainder of 2. Bring down the 5 to make 25. 7 goes into 25 three times (21), leaving a remainder of 4. The quotient is 23 with a remainder of 4.

20. Draw a pentagon. A pentagon has how many vertices?

Answer: 5 vertices

Explanation: The prefix "penta-" means five. Therefore, a pentagon is a polygon with 5 straight sides. The points where the sides meet form 5 vertices.
