6.Find the least common multiple (LCM) of 8, 12, and 18.

Prime factors: 8 = 2³, 12 = 2²×3, 18 = 2×3².

Use the greatest power of each prime: 2³×3² = 8×9 = 72.

7.Convert 1740 to a percentage.

17 ÷ 40 = 0.425

0.425 × 100 = 42.5%

8.A triangle has a base of 18 cm and an area of 117 cm². What is its height?

Use A = 12bh.

117 = 12(18)h = 9h

Answer: 13 cm

9.Evaluate: (−2)3 + 52 − 9

−8 + 25 − 9 = 8

10.Solve for y: 6(y + 2) = 4(y + 9)

Distribute: 6y + 12 = 4y + 36

Subtract 4y and 12: 2y = 24

Answer: y = 12

11.A coat originally costs $240. It is marked down by 20%, and then an additional 15% coupon is applied to the sale price. What is the final price?

After 20% off: 240 × 0.80 = $192

After another 15% off: 192 × 0.85 = $163.20

12.Find the volume of a rectangular prism with length 7.5 m, width 4.2 m, and height 5 m.

V = lwh = 7.5 × 4.2 × 5 = 157.5 m³

13.Calculate the mean of the numbers: 15, 23, 31, 18, 12, 21.

Sum: 15+23+31+18+12+21 = 120

Answer: x ≥ 7

16.A 5-foot post casts a 3-foot shadow. At the same time, a flagpole casts a 21-foot shadow. How tall is the flagpole?

17.Simplify: √225 + √64 − √36

18.Find the perimeter of a semicircle with radius 10 cm. Use π ≈ 3.14 and include the straight diameter edge.

Semicircle arc length: πr = 3.14×10 = 31.4 cm

Diameter: 2r = 20 cm

Total: 31.4 + 20 = 51.4 cm

31.Use PEMDAS to evaluate: 8.5 − [35 × (4.2 + 0.8)] + 34

Parentheses first: 4.2 + 0.8 = 5.0

Multiply: 35 × 5 = 3

Convert the remaining fraction: 34 = 0.75

Finish left to right: 8.5 − 3 + 0.75 = 6.25
