Lesson Practice

Write each fraction as a percent:

a. \(\frac{31}{100}\)
Answer: 31%

A percent is a ratio whose denominator is 100. Since the denominator is already 100, the numerator is the percent.

b. \(\frac{1}{100}\)
Answer: 1%

The denominator is 100, so the numerator (1) represents the percentage directly.

c. \(\frac{1}{10}\)
Answer: 10%

To make the denominator 100, multiply the top and bottom by 10:
\(\frac{1 \times 10}{10 \times 10} = \frac{10}{100} = 10\%\)

d. \(\frac{3}{50}\)
Answer: 6%

Multiply numerator and denominator by 2 to get a denominator of 100:
\(\frac{3 \times 2}{50 \times 2} = \frac{6}{100} = 6\%\)

e. \(\frac{7}{25}\)
Answer: 28%

Multiply numerator and denominator by 4:
\(\frac{7 \times 4}{25 \times 4} = \frac{28}{100} = 28\%\)

f. \(\frac{2}{5}\)
Answer: 40%

Multiply numerator and denominator by 20:
\(\frac{2 \times 20}{5 \times 20} = \frac{40}{100} = 40\%\)

g. Twelve of the 30 students earned a B on the test. What percent of the students earned a B?
Answer: 40%

1. Write as a fraction: \(\frac{12}{30}\)
2. Reduce the fraction (divide by 6): \(\frac{2}{5}\)
3. Convert to percent (multiply by 20/20): \(\frac{40}{100} = 40\%\)

h. Jorge correctly answered 18 of the 20 questions on the test. What percent of the questions did he answer correctly?
Answer: 90%

1. Write as a fraction: \(\frac{18}{20}\)
2. Convert denominator to 100 (multiply by 5): \(\frac{18 \times 5}{20 \times 5} = \frac{90}{100} = 90\%\)

Written Practice

1. What is the reciprocal of two and three fifths?
Answer: \(\frac{5}{13}\)

1. Convert the mixed number to an improper fraction: \(2 \frac{3}{5} = \frac{10+3}{5} = \frac{13}{5}\)
2. The reciprocal flips the numerator and denominator: \(\frac{5}{13}\)

2. What time is one hour thirty-five minutes after 2:30 p.m.?
Answer: 4:05 p.m.

Start: 2:30
Add 1 hour: 3:30
Add 30 mins: 4:00
Add remaining 5 mins: 4:05 p.m.

3. A 1-pound box of candy cost $4.00. What was the cost per ounce (1 pound = 16 ounces)?
Answer: $0.25 (or 25¢) per ounce

\(\text{Cost per ounce} = \frac{\text{Total Cost}}{\text{Total Ounces}} = \frac{\$4.00}{16} = \$0.25\)

4. Freda bought a sandwich for $4.00 and a drink for 94¢. Her grandson ordered a meal for $6.35. What was the total price of all three items when 8% sales tax was added? Explain how to use estimation to check whether your answer is reasonable.
Answer: $12.19

Step 1: Calculate Exact Total
Items: \(4.00 + 0.94 + 6.35 = 11.29\)
Tax: \(11.29 \times 0.08 = 0.9032\) (Round to $0.90)
Total: \(11.29 + 0.90 = \$12.19\)

Step 2: Estimation
Round items to easy numbers: $4.00 + $1.00 + $6.35 ≈ $11.35.
Estimate Tax (10% is easier than 8%): 10% of 11.35 is about $1.10.
Estimated Total: \(11.35 + 1.10 = 12.45\).
Since $12.45 is close to $12.19, the answer is reasonable.

5. If the chance of rain is 50%, then what is the chance it will not rain?
Answer: 50%

The total probability is 100%.
\(100\% - 50\% = 50\%\)

6. Draw a cube. How many edges does a cube have?
Answer: 12 edges

A cube has 12 edges (4 on the top face, 4 on the bottom face, and 4 vertical ones connecting them).

9. Write 12% as a reduced fraction. Then write the fraction as a decimal number.
Answer: \(\frac{3}{25}\) and 0.12

Fraction: \(\frac{12}{100}\). Divide by 4: \(\frac{3}{25}\)
Decimal: 12% means 12 hundredths, which is 0.12.

10. \(\frac{7}{10} = \frac{n}{100}\)
Answer: n = 70

Multiply the numerator and denominator of the first fraction by 10:
\(\frac{7 \times 10}{10 \times 10} = \frac{70}{100}\). Therefore, \(n = 70\).

11. \(5 - m = 3 \frac{1}{8}\)
Answer: \(m = 1 \frac{7}{8}\)

\(m = 5 - 3 \frac{1}{8}\)
Borrow 1 from 5: \(5\) becomes \(4 \frac{8}{8}\)
\(4 \frac{8}{8} - 3 \frac{1}{8} = 1 \frac{7}{8}\)

12. \(1 - w = 0.95\)
Answer: w = 0.05

\(w = 1.00 - 0.95 = 0.05\)

13. \(m + 1 \frac{2}{3} = 3 \frac{1}{6}\)
Answer: \(m = 1 \frac{1}{2}\)

\(m = 3 \frac{1}{6} - 1 \frac{2}{3}\)
Find common denominator (6): \(1 \frac{2}{3} = 1 \frac{4}{6}\)
Equation: \(3 \frac{1}{6} - 1 \frac{4}{6}\)
Borrow from 3: \(2 \frac{7}{6} - 1 \frac{4}{6} = 1 \frac{3}{6}\)
Reduce: \(1 \frac{1}{2}\)

14. \((\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3}) - \frac{1}{6}\)
Answer: \(\frac{2}{3}\)

Common denominator is 6.
\(\frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{6}\), \(\frac{1}{3} = \frac{2}{6}\)
\((\frac{3}{6} + \frac{2}{6}) = \frac{5}{6}\)
\(\frac{5}{6} - \frac{1}{6} = \frac{4}{6}\)
Reduce: \(\frac{2}{3}\)

15. \(3 \frac{1}{2} \times 1 \frac{1}{3} \times 1 \frac{1}{2}\)
Answer: 7

Convert to improper fractions:
\(\frac{7}{2} \times \frac{4}{3} \times \frac{3}{2}\)
Multiply numerators: \(7 \times 4 \times 3 = 84\)
Multiply denominators: \(2 \times 3 \times 2 = 12\)
Divide: \(84 \div 12 = 7\)

19. ESTIMATE The diameter of the big tractor tire was about 5 feet. As the tire rolled one full turn, the tire rolled about how many feet? Round the answer to the nearest foot. (Use 3.14 for π.)
Answer: 16 feet

One full turn is the Circumference (\(C = \pi \times d\)).
\(C \approx 3.14 \times 5\)
\(C \approx 15.7\)
Round to nearest foot: 16 feet.

20. Which digit in 4.87 has the same place value as the 9 in 0.195?
Answer: 7

In 0.195, the 9 is in the hundredths place.
In 4.87, the 7 is in the hundredths place.

21. Write the prime factorization of both the numerator and denominator of \(\frac{18}{30}\). Then reduce the fraction.
Answer: \(\frac{3}{5}\)

Numerator 18: \(2 \times 3 \times 3\)
Denominator 30: \(2 \times 3 \times 5\)
Cancel common factors (2 and 3):
\(\frac{2 \times 3 \times 3}{2 \times 3 \times 5} = \frac{3}{5}\)

22. What is the greatest common factor of 18 and 30?
Answer: 6

Factors of 18: 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18
Factors of 30: 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 10, 15, 30
Largest common factor is 6.

23. If the product of two numbers is 1, then the two numbers are which of the following?
Answer: B (reciprocals)

By definition, a number multiplied by its reciprocal equals 1 (e.g., \(2 \times \frac{1}{2} = 1\)).

24. Why is every rectangle a quadrilateral?
Answer:

A quadrilateral is defined as a polygon with four sides. Since every rectangle has four sides, every rectangle is a quadrilateral.

25. If \(b\) equals 8 and \(h\) equals 6, what does \(\frac{bh}{2}\) equal?
Answer: 24

Substitute the values:
\(\frac{8 \times 6}{2} = \frac{48}{2} = 24\)

26. Find the prime factorization of 400 using a factor tree. Then write the prime factorization of 400 using exponents.
Answer: \(2^4 \times 5^2\)

\(400 = 4 \times 100\)
\(4 = 2 \times 2\)
\(100 = 10 \times 10 = (2 \times 5) \times (2 \times 5)\)
Combine all primes: \(2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 5 \times 5\)
Exponent form: \(2^4 \times 5^2\)

28. Refer to the rectangle drawn in problem 27 to answer parts a and b below.
a. What is the perimeter of the rectangle?
b. What is the area of the rectangle?
Answer: a. 12 units, b. 8 square units

Length (from x=-1 to 3) = 4 units.
Width (from y=-1 to 1) = 2 units.

29. a. What is the perimeter of this parallelogram? (Base 20cm, Side 15cm)
b. What is the area of this parallelogram? (Base 20cm, Height 12cm)
Answer: a. 70 cm, b. 240 sq cm

a. Perimeter = \(2(20 + 15) = 2(35) = 70\) cm.
b. Area = base \(\times\) height = \(20 \times 12 = 240\) cm².

30. Draw two parallel segments of different lengths. Then form a quadrilateral by drawing two segments that connect the endpoints of the parallel segments. Is the quadrilateral a rectangle?
Answer: No.

The resulting shape is a trapezoid, not a rectangle. Because the parallel sides have different lengths, the connecting sides must be slanted (or at least one must be), so the angles are not 90 degrees.

Real-World Connection

The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite. The average distance from Earth to the Moon is approximately \(620^2\) kilometers. This distance is about 30 times the diameter of Earth.
a. Simplify \(620^2\) kilometers.
b. Find the diameter of Earth. Round your answer to the nearest kilometer.
Answer: a. 384,400 km, b. 12,813 km

a. \(620^2 = 620 \times 620 = 384,400\) km.
b. Diameter = Distance \(\div\) 30
\(384,400 \div 30 = 12,813.333...\)
Rounded to the nearest kilometer: 12,813 km.
