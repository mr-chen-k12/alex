Math Problem Solutions (Questions 1 - 30)

1. Two hundred students are traveling by bus on a field trip. The maximum number of students allowed on each bus is 84. How many buses are needed for the trip?
Explanation: To find the number of buses needed, divide the total number of students by the capacity of each bus: \( 200 \div 84 pprox 2.38 \). Since you cannot have a fraction of a bus, you must round up to the next whole number to accommodate all students.

Answer: 3 buses
2. The wingspan of a jumbo jet is about 210 feet. The wingspan of a model of a jumbo jet measures 25.2 inches.
a. What is the approximate scale of the model?
b. The model is 28 inches long. To the nearest foot, how long is the jumbo jet?
Explanation:
a. First, convert the jet's wingspan to inches so the units match. \( 210 ext{ feet} imes 12 ext{ inches/foot} = 2520 ext{ inches} \). The scale is the ratio of the model to the actual jet: \( rac{25.2}{2520} = rac{1}{100} \).
b. Using the scale \( 1:100 \), multiply the model's length by 100 to find the actual length in inches: \( 28 ext{ inches} imes 100 = 2800 ext{ inches} \). Convert this back to feet: \( 2800 \div 12 = 233.33 ext{ feet} \).

Answer: a. 1:100 or 1/100, b. 233 feet
3. Calculate mentally:
a. \( (-2)(-6) \)
b. \( rac{+6}{-2} \)
c. \( rac{-6}{-6} \)
d. \( (-2)(+6) \)
Explanation: When multiplying or dividing real numbers: two negatives make a positive, and a positive and negative make a negative.
a. \( (-2) imes (-6) = +12 \)
b. \( (+6) \div (-2) = -3 \)
c. \( (-6) \div (-6) = +1 \)
d. \( (-2) imes (+6) = -12 \)

Answer: a. 12, b. -3, c. 1, d. -12
4. Calculate mentally:
a. \( (-2) - (-6) \)
b. \( -2 - -6 \)
c. \( +2 + -6 \)
d. \( (-2) + (+6) \)
Explanation:
a & b. Subtracting a negative is the same as adding a positive: \( -2 + 6 = 4 \).
c. Adding a negative number means moving left on the number line: \( 2 - 6 = -4 \).
d. Adding a positive number: \( -2 + 6 = 4 \).

Answer: a. 4, b. 4, c. -4, d. 4
5. The chef chopped 27 carrots, which was 90% of the carrots in the bag. How many carrots remained?
Explanation: Let \( x \) be the total number of carrots. Since 90% is 27, we have the equation \( 0.90x = 27 \). Solving for \( x \): \( x = rac{27}{0.90} = 30 \). There were 30 carrots in total. The remaining carrots are \( 30 - 27 = 3 \).

Answer: 3 carrots
6. Write twenty million, five hundred ten thousand in expanded notation using exponents.
Explanation: First, write the number in standard form: 20,510,000. Now break it into place values:
\( 2 imes 10,000,000 + 5 imes 100,000 + 1 imes 10,000 \).
Using exponents for powers of 10:
\( (2 imes 10^7) + (5 imes 10^5) + (1 imes 10^4) \).

Answer: \( (2 imes 10^7) + (5 imes 10^5) + (1 imes 10^4) \)
7. Find 8% of $3.65 and round the product to the nearest cent.
Explanation: Convert 8% to a decimal: \( 0.08 \). Multiply by $3.65: \( 0.08 imes 3.65 = 0.292 \). Round to the nearest cent (two decimal places), which gives $0.29.

Answer: $0.29
8. Calculate: \( ( rac{1}{2})^2 + rac{1}{8} \div rac{1}{2} \)
Explanation: Follow the order of operations (PEMDAS):
1. Exponents: \( ( rac{1}{2})^2 = rac{1}{4} \)
2. Division: \( rac{1}{8} \div rac{1}{2} = rac{1}{8} imes rac{2}{1} = rac{2}{8} = rac{1}{4} \)
3. Addition: \( rac{1}{4} + rac{1}{4} = rac{2}{4} = rac{1}{2} \)

Answer: 1/2

Explanation: Rearrange to solve for \( m \): \( m = 5 rac{1}{2} - 2 rac{5}{6} \).

Since \( rac{3}{6} \) is less than \( rac{5}{6} \), borrow 1 from the 5: \( 5 rac{3}{6} = 4 rac{9}{6} \).

Simplify the fraction: \( 2 rac{2}{3} \).

16. All eight books in the stack are the same weight. Three books weigh a total of six pounds.
a. How much does each book weigh?
b. How much do all eight books weigh?
Explanation:
a. Divide the total weight of 3 books by 3 to find the weight of one book: \( 6 ext{ lbs} \div 3 = 2 ext{ lbs/book} \).
b. Multiply the weight of one book by 8: \( 8 imes 2 = 16 ext{ lbs} \).

Answer: a. 2 lbs, b. 16 lbs
17. Find the volume of a rectangular prism using the formula \( V = lwh \) when the length is 8 cm, the width is 5 cm, and the height is 2 cm.
Explanation: Plug the given values into the formula: \( V = 8 ext{ cm} imes 5 ext{ cm} imes 2 ext{ cm} = 80 ext{ cm}^3 \).

Answer: 80 cm³
18. How many millimeters is 1.2 meters (1 m = 1000 mm)?
Explanation: Multiply the value in meters by the conversion factor (1000): \( 1.2 imes 1000 = 1200 ext{ mm} \).

Answer: 1200 mm
19. What is the perimeter of the polygon at right? (Dimensions are in millimeters: Top=15, Right=12, Bottom=5, Left=5. The inner lengths can be deduced).
Explanation: Looking at the L-shaped polygon, we have outer vertical sides summing to the total height, and outer horizontal sides summing to the total width.
The top horizontal edge is 15. The right vertical edge is 12.
Left vertical edge is 5. Therefore, the inner vertical edge is \( 12 - 5 = 7 \).
Bottom horizontal edge is 5. Therefore, the inner horizontal edge is \( 15 - 5 = 10 \).
Perimeter = sum of all sides: \( 15 + 12 + 5 + 7 + 10 + 5 = 54 ext{ mm} \).
Shortcut: The perimeter of such an L-shape is exactly equal to the perimeter of the bounding rectangle: \( 2 imes (15 + 12) = 54 \).

Answer: 54 mm
20. What is the area of the polygon in problem 19?
Explanation: Split the polygon into two rectangles.
Option 1: A top horizontal rectangle (15 by 5) and a bottom right vertical rectangle (5 by 7). Area = \( (15 imes 5) + (5 imes 7) = 75 + 35 = 110 \).
Option 2: A right vertical rectangle (12 by 5) and a left inner horizontal rectangle (10 by 5). Area = \( (12 imes 5) + (10 imes 5) = 60 + 50 = 110 \).

Answer: 110 mm²

22. Which one of these numbers is not a composite number?
A. 34    B. 35    C. 36    D. 37
Explanation: A composite number has factors other than 1 and itself.
34 is divisible by 2.
35 is divisible by 5.
36 is divisible by 2 (and 3, 4, 6, etc.).
37 is only divisible by 1 and 37, making it a prime number, not composite.

Answer: D. 37
23. Debbie wants to decorate a cylindrical wastebasket by wrapping it with wallpaper. The diameter of the wastebasket is 12 inches. The length of the wallpaper should be at least how many inches? Round up to the next inch.
Explanation: The length of the wallpaper needed to wrap around the cylinder is equal to its circumference.
\( C = \pi imes d \)
\( C = 3.14159... imes 12 pprox 37.699 ext{ inches} \).
Rounding up to the next inch, she needs 38 inches.

Answer: 38 inches
24. a. Which one of these letters has two lines of symmetry?

b. Which letter has rotational symmetry?
Explanation:
a. The letter H can be folded vertically down the middle and horizontally across the middle, resulting in perfect overlaps (2 lines of symmetry). 'A' and 'V' have 1 vertical line. 'E' has 1 horizontal line.
b. Rotational symmetry means the letter looks the same when rotated less than 360 degrees. The letter H looks identical when rotated 180 degrees.

Answer: a. H, b. H

26. The ratio of nonfiction to fiction books on Shawna's bookshelf is 2 to 3. If the total number of books on her shelf is 30, how many nonfiction books are there?
Explanation: The ratio 2 to 3 means for every 5 books (2+3), 2 are nonfiction.
Divide the total number of books by the total ratio parts: \( 30 \div 5 = 6 \).
Each "part" represents 6 books. Since nonfiction is 2 parts: \( 2 imes 6 = 12 \).

Answer: 12 nonfiction books
27. What are the coordinates of the point that is halfway between (-2, -3) and (6, -3)?
Explanation: Since the y-coordinates are the same (-3), this is a horizontal segment.
Find the average of the x-coordinates: \( rac{-2 + 6}{2} = rac{4}{2} = 2 \).
The y-coordinate remains -3.

Answer: (2, -3)
28. A set of 40 number cards contains one each of the counting numbers from 1 through 40. A multiple of 10 is drawn from the set and is not replaced. A second card is drawn from the remaining 39 cards. What is the probability that the second card will be a multiple of 10?
Explanation: The multiples of 10 between 1 and 40 are 10, 20, 30, and 40 (4 cards total).
If one is drawn and not replaced, there are exactly 3 multiples of 10 left in the deck.
The total number of cards remaining is 39.
The probability is \( rac{3}{39} \), which simplifies to \( rac{1}{13} \).

Answer: 1/13
29. Combine the areas of the two triangles to find the area of this trapezoid. (Top base = 10 in, Bottom base = 8 in, Height = 6 in)
Explanation: The area of a triangle is \( rac{1}{2}bh \).
Bottom-left triangle: base = 8, height = 6. Area = \( rac{1}{2} imes 8 imes 6 = 24 ext{ sq in} \).
Top-right triangle: base = 10, height = 6. Area = \( rac{1}{2} imes 10 imes 6 = 30 ext{ sq in} \).
Total Area = \( 24 + 30 = 54 ext{ sq in} \).
(Check using trapezoid formula: \( rac{8+10}{2} imes 6 = 9 imes 6 = 54 \))

Answer: 54 sq in
30. Calculate: \( 3^2 + 2 imes 5^2 - 50 \div \sqrt{25} \)
Explanation: Order of operations (PEMDAS):
1. Exponents and Roots: \( 3^2 = 9 \), \( 5^2 = 25 \), \( \sqrt{25} = 5 \).
Substitute back: \( 9 + 2 imes 25 - 50 \div 5 \)
2. Multiplication and Division (left to right): \( 2 imes 25 = 50 \) and \( 50 \div 5 = 10 \).
Substitute back: \( 9 + 50 - 10 \)
3. Addition and Subtraction: \( 59 - 10 = 49 \).

Answer: 49
