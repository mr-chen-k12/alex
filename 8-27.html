Practice Problems

1. Perform long division (terminating decimal result): $14.175 \div 0.35$
Move decimal 2 places to the right: $1417.5 \div 35$
$141 \div 35 = 4$ (remainder $1.7 \to 17$)
$175 \div 35 = 5$
Calculation: $1417.5 \div 35 = 40.5$
Answer: 40.5
2. Perform long division (repeating decimal result): $7 \div 0.6$. Express as a fraction and as a repeating decimal.
Multiply numerator and denominator by 10: $\frac{70}{6} = \frac{35}{3}$
Divide: $35 \div 3 = 11.6666...$
Answer: $11.\bar{6}$ or $11\frac{2}{3}$
3. Evaluate: $3.4 \times (5.1 - 1.9) + 4.28$
Inside parentheses: $5.1 - 1.9 = 3.2$
Multiply: $3.4 \times 3.2 = 10.88$
Add: $10.88 + 4.28 = 15.16$
Answer: 15.16
4. Solve for $x$: $\frac{2}{5}x - 3 = 7$
$\frac{2}{5}x = 10$
Multiply by $\frac{5}{2}$: $x = 10 \times \frac{5}{2} = 25$
Answer: 25
5. Evaluate: $5\frac{1}{3} - 2\frac{3}{4}$
Common denominator is 12:
$5\frac{4}{12} - 2\frac{9}{12} = 4\frac{16}{12} - 2\frac{9}{12} = 2\frac{7}{12}$
Answer: $2\frac{7}{12}$
6. Find the least common multiple (LCM) of 14, 21, and 28.
Prime factorizations:
$14 = 2 \times 7$
$21 = 3 \times 7$
$28 = 2^2 \times 7$
$\text{LCM} = 2^2 \times 3 \times 7 = 4 \times 21 = 84$
Answer: 84
7. Convert $\frac{13}{25}$ to a percentage.
$\frac{13}{25} = \frac{52}{100} = 52\%$
Answer: 52%
8. A right triangle has a base of $14\text{ cm}$ and an area of $56\text{ cm}^2$. What is its height?
$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times b \times h$
$56 = \frac{1}{2} \times 14 \times h$
$56 = 7h \implies h = 8\text{ cm}$
Answer: 8 cm
9. Evaluate: $(-3)^4 - 4^2 \times (-2)$
$(-3)^4 = 81$
$4^2 = 16$
$16 \times (-2) = -32$
$81 - (-32) = 81 + 32 = 113$
Answer: 113
10. Solve for $y$: $5(y - 3) = 2(y + 6)$
$5y - 15 = 2y + 12$
$3y = 27$
$y = 9$
Answer: 9
11. A jacket original price is $120. It is marked down by 20%, and then an additional 10% coupon is applied to the sale price. What is the final price?
First discount: $120 \times (1 - 0.20) = $96$
Second discount: $96 \times (1 - 0.10) = $86.40$
Answer: $86.40
12. Find the volume of a rectangular prism with length $8.5\text{ m}$, width $4\text{ m}$, and height $3\text{ m}$.
$\text{Volume} = l \times w \times h$
$\text{Volume} = 8.5 \times 4 \times 3 = 34 \times 3 = 102\text{ m}^3$
Answer: $102\text{ m}^3$
13. Calculate the mean of the numbers: 14, 22, 19, 31, 24, 10.
$\text{Sum} = 14 + 22 + 19 + 31 + 24 + 10 = 120$
$\text{Count} = 6$
$\text{Mean} = 120 \div 6 = 20$
Answer: 20
14. Solve the inequality: $4 - 2x > -10$
$-2x > -14$
Divide by -2 and flip inequality sign:
$x < 7$
Answer: $x < 7$
15. Evaluate: $3\frac{3}{5} \times 2\frac{1}{3}$
Convert to improper fractions: $\frac{18}{5} \times \frac{7}{3}$
Simplify: $\frac{6 \times 7}{5} = \frac{42}{5} = 8\frac{2}{5}$
Answer: $8\frac{2}{5}$ (or $8.4$)
16. If a 15-foot flagpole casts a 9-foot shadow, how tall is a tree that casts a 21-foot shadow at the same time?
Set up proportion: $\frac{\text{height}}{\text{shadow}} = \frac{15}{9} = \frac{h}{21}$
$\frac{5}{3} = \frac{h}{21} \implies 3h = 105 \implies h = 35\text{ feet}$
Answer: 35 feet
17. Simplify: $\sqrt{144} + \sqrt{81} - \sqrt{49}$
$12 + 9 - 7 = 21 - 7 = 14$
Answer: 14
18. Find the perimeter of a semi-circle with radius $7\text{ cm}$ (use $\pi \approx \frac{22}{7}$). Include the straight diameter edge.
Curved arc $= \pi r = \frac{22}{7} \times 7 = 22\text{ cm}$
Diameter $= 2r = 14\text{ cm}$
Total Perimeter $= 22 + 14 = 36\text{ cm}$
Answer: 36 cm
19. Evaluate: $|-25| \div 5 - | -3 \times 4 |$
$25 \div 5 - | -12 | = 5 - 12 = -7$
Answer: -7
20. What number is 160% of 45?
$1.60 \times 45 = 72$
Answer: 72
21. Solve for $a$: $\frac{a + 4}{3} = \frac{a - 2}{2}$
Cross-multiply: $2(a + 4) = 3(a - 2)$
$2a + 8 = 3a - 6$
$8 + 6 = 3a - 2a$
$a = 14$
Answer: 14
22. A map scale is $1\text{ inch} = 25\text{ miles}$. If two cities are $6.4\text{ inches}$ apart on the map, what is the actual distance?
$\text{Distance} = 6.4 \times 25 = 160\text{ miles}$
Answer: 160 miles
23. Evaluate: $4^3 - 2^5 + 3 \times (-7)$
$4^3 = 64$, $2^5 = 32$
$3 \times (-7) = -21$
$64 - 32 - 21 = 32 - 21 = 11$
Answer: 11
24. Find the range and mode of the following data set: 15, 22, 17, 22, 30, 19, 22, 28.
Range $= 30 - 15 = 15$
Mode (most frequent value) $= 22$
Answer: Range: 15, Mode: 22
25. A sphere has a radius of $3\text{ cm}$. Find its surface area in terms of $\pi$ (Formula: $A = 4\pi r^2$).
$\text{Area} = 4 \times \pi \times 3^2 = 4 \times \pi \times 9 = 36\pi\text{ cm}^2$
Answer: $36\pi\text{ cm}^2$
26. Convert $0.\bar{45}$ (repeating decimal) into a fraction in simplest form.
Let $x = 0.4545...$
$100x = 45.4545...$
$99x = 45 \implies x = \frac{45}{99} = \frac{5}{11}$
Answer: $\frac{5}{11}$
27. Simplify the algebraic expression: $4(2x - 3y) - 3(x - 4y)$
$8x - 12y - 3x + 12y$
Combine like terms: $(8x - 3x) + (-12y + 12y) = 5x$
Answer: $5x$
28. Determine whether a triangle with side lengths $7\text{ cm}$, $24\text{ cm}$, and $25\text{ cm}$ is a right triangle.
Check Pythagorean theorem: $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$
$7^2 + 24^2 = 49 + 576 = 625$
$25^2 = 625$
Since $625 = 625$, it is a right triangle.
Answer: Yes, it is a right triangle.
29. Evaluate: $\frac{5}{8} \div \frac{15}{16}$
$\frac{5}{8} \times \frac{16}{15} = \frac{1 \times 2}{1 \times 3} = \frac{2}{3}$
Answer: $\frac{2}{3}$
30. Deposit $1,500 into a bank account with 4% compound interest compounded annually. What is the total balance after 2 years?
Year 1 interest: $1500 \times 0.04 = $60 \implies \text{Balance} = $1560$
Year 2 interest: $1560 \times 0.04 = $62.40 \implies \text{Balance} = $1622.40$
Answer: $1,622.40
