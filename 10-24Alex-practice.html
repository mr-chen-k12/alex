1.(28)
EVALUATE Two classes are raising money at the school raffle to buy new maps costing \($94.75\). One class has raised \($31.68\) and the other has raised \($28.35\). How much more money do they need to raise to buy the maps?
Answer
\(\$34.72\)
Solution
Together they have raised \(\$31.68 + \$28.35 = \$60.03\). The maps cost \(\$94.75\), so they still need \(\$94.75 - \$60.03 = \$34.72\).
2.(28)
Maya read 4 books during her vacation. The first book had 412 pages, the second had 367 pages, the third had 298 pages, and the fourth had 435 pages. The 4 books she read had an average of how many pages per book?
Answer
\(378\text{ pages}\)
Solution
Total pages: \(412 + 367 + 298 + 435 = 1512\). Average: \(1512 \div 4 = 378\) pages per book.

A monthly magazine has 12 issues per year, so \(\$19.80 \div 12 = \$1.65\) per issue.

5.(19)
EXPLAIN A square and a regular hexagon share a common side. The perimeter of the square is 36 in. Describe how to find the perimeter of the hexagon.
Answer
\(54\text{ in.}\)
Solution
First find the shared side: the square's perimeter is 36 in. and a square has 4 equal sides, so each side is \(36 \div 4 = 9\) in. The hexagon shares that side, and a regular hexagon has 6 equal sides, so its perimeter is \(6 \times 9 = 54\) in.
6.(22)
In the following statement, change the percent to a reduced fraction and answer the questions.
Liam bought a book of 25 stamps. He has used 60% of them.
a. How many stamps has Liam used?
b. How many stamps does he have left?
Answer
\(\text{a. }15\text{ stamps} \qquad \text{b. }10\text{ stamps}\)
Solution
60% as a reduced fraction is \(\frac{60}{100} = \frac{3}{5}\). a. \(\frac{3}{5}\) of 25 is \(25 \div 5 \times 3 = 15\) stamps used. b. \(25 - 15 = 10\) stamps left.

Name the shaded part of this square

b. as a decimal.

Answer

12.(Inv. 3)
REPRESENT The vertices of a square are located at (4, 4), (4, −4), (−4, −4), and (−4, 4).

b. What is the perimeter of the square?
c. What is the area of the square?
Answer

13.(15)
Complete each equivalent fraction:
a. \(\frac{3}{?} = \frac{18}{30}\)   b. \(\frac{5}{8} = \frac{?}{40}\)   c. \(\frac{?}{9} = \frac{6}{27}\)
Answer
\(\text{a. }5 \qquad \text{b. }25 \qquad \text{c. }2\)
Solution
a. \(\frac{18}{30}\) reduces by 6 to \(\frac{3}{5}\), so the missing denominator is 5. b. Multiplying numerator and denominator of \(\frac{5}{8}\) by 5 gives \(\frac{25}{40}\). c. \(\frac{6}{27} = \frac{2}{9}\), so the missing numerator is 2.
14.(21)

\(\text{a. }5^2 \cdot 11^2 \qquad \text{b. }55\)

15.(18)
Draw two parallel lines. Then draw two more parallel lines that are perpendicular to the first pair of lines. Label the points of intersection A, B, C, and D consecutively in a counterclockwise direction. Draw segment AC. Refer to the figure to answer a and b.
a. What kind of quadrilateral is figure ABCD?
b. Triangles ABC and CDA are congruent. Which angle in \(\triangle ABC\) corresponds to \(\angle DCA\) in \(\triangle CDA\)?
Answer
\(\text{a. rectangle} \qquad \text{b. }\angle BAC\)
Solution
a. The figure has four right angles and opposite sides parallel and equal, so it is a rectangle. b. In the correspondence ABC ↔ CDA, vertex C of \(\triangle CDA\) matches vertex A of \(\triangle ABC\), so \(\angle DCA\) corresponds to \(\angle BAC\).
16.(14)
Eighteen red marbles and 12 blue marbles are in a bag. If one marble is drawn from the bag, what is the probability the marble is not red?
Answer
\(\frac{2}{5}\)
Solution
There are \(18 + 12 = 30\) marbles in all. The marbles that are not red are the 12 blue ones, so the probability is \(\frac{12}{30} = \frac{2}{5}\).
17.(2, 9)
Name the properties used to solve this equation.
\(\frac{2}{5}\left(\frac{5}{2}y\right) = \frac{2}{5} \cdot \frac{3}{7}\)   Given
\(\left(\frac{2}{5} \cdot \frac{5}{2}\right)y = \frac{2}{5} \cdot \frac{3}{7}\)   a. ______________
\(1y = \frac{2}{5} \cdot \frac{3}{7}\)   b. ______________
\(y = \frac{2}{5} \cdot \frac{3}{7}\)   c. ______________
\(y = \frac{6}{35}\)
Answer
\(\text{a. Associative Property of Multiplication} \quad \text{b. Inverse Property of Multiplication} \quad \text{c. Identity Property of Multiplication}\)
Solution
a. The factors were regrouped — \(\frac{2}{5}\) and \(\frac{5}{2}\) were grouped together — so this is the Associative Property of Multiplication. b. \(\frac{2}{5} \cdot \frac{5}{2} = 1\) because the factors are reciprocals, so this is the Inverse Property of Multiplication. c. \(1y = y\), so this is the Identity Property of Multiplication. (Then \(y = \frac{2}{5} \cdot \frac{3}{7} = \frac{6}{35}\).)

24.(26)
Simplify: \(3\frac{1}{2} \div 2\frac{1}{3}\)

\(1\frac{1}{2}\)
Solution
Convert to improper fractions: \(3\frac{1}{2} = \frac{7}{2}\) and \(2\frac{1}{3} = \frac{7}{3}\). Multiply by the reciprocal: \(\frac{7}{2} \cdot \frac{3}{7} = \frac{21}{14} = \frac{3}{2} = 1\frac{1}{2}\).

a. A three-quarter turn measures how many degrees?
b. A sixth of a circle measures how many degrees?
c. A twelfth of a circle measures how many degrees?
Answer
\(\text{a. }270^\circ \qquad \text{b. }60^\circ \qquad \text{c. }30^\circ\)

35.(tail)
Simplify: \(2^3 - (-4) \times 0.25 + \frac{1}{2} - 0.5\)
Answer
\(9\)
Solution
Exponents first: \(2^3 = 8\). Then multiplication: \((-4) \times 0.25 = -1\), so \(8 - (-1) = 9\). Finally \(9 + \frac{1}{2} - 0.5 = 9 + 0.5 - 0.5 = 9\).
