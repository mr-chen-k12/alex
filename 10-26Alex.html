1.(28)
There were 3 towns on the mountain. The population of Hazelhurst was 9418. The population of Jesup was 4248. The population of Baxley was 3584. What was the average population of the 3 towns on the mountain?
Answer
\(5750\)
Solution
Add the three populations: \(9418 + 4248 + 3584 = 17{,}250\). Divide by 3 towns: \(17{,}250 \div 3 = 5750\).
2.(28)
The film was a long one, lasting 206 minutes. How many hours and minutes long was the film?
Answer
\(3\text{ hours }26\text{ minutes}\)
Solution
\(206 \div 60 = 3\) remainder 26, so 206 minutes is 3 hours and 26 minutes.

4.(18, 19)
a. A square and a regular pentagon share a common side. The perimeter of the square is 20 cm. What is the perimeter of the pentagon?
b. JUSTIFY Explain how you got your answer.
Answer
\(\text{a. }25\text{ cm}\)
Solution
a. A square has 4 equal sides, so the shared side is \(20 \div 4 = 5\) cm. A regular pentagon has 5 equal sides, so its perimeter is \(5 \times 5 = 25\) cm.
b. Because the two figures share a side, the pentagon's side must equal the square's side (5 cm). Multiplying by the pentagon's 5 sides gives 25 cm.

Diagram this statement. Then answer the questions that follow.
Five eighths of the troubadour's 200 songs were about love and chivalry.
a. How many of the songs were about love and chivalry?
b. How many of the songs were not about love and chivalry?
Answer
\(\text{a. }125\text{ songs} \qquad \text{b. }75\text{ songs}\)
Solution
a. \(\frac{5}{8}\) of 200 is \(200 \div 8 \times 5 = 25 \times 5 = 125\) songs. b. \(200 - 125 = 75\) songs were not about love and chivalry.

CONCLUDE The coordinates of three vertices of a rectangle are \((-5, 3)\), \((-5, -2)\), and \((2, -2)\).
a. What are the coordinates of the fourth vertex?
b. What is the area of the rectangle?
Answer
\(\text{a. }(2, 3) \qquad \text{b. }35\text{ square units}\)
Solution
a. The missing vertex shares its x-coordinate with \((2, -2)\) and its y-coordinate with \((-5, 3)\), so it is \((2, 3)\). b. The rectangle is 7 units wide (from −5 to 2) and 5 units tall (from −2 to 3), so its area is \(7 \times 5 = 35\) square units.

Refer to the figure below to answer a–c.
a. Which chord is not a diameter?
b. Name a central angle that is a right angle.
c. Name an inscribed angle.
Answer
\(\text{a. }\overline{CB} \qquad \text{b. }\angle COB \qquad \text{c. }\angle OBC\)

a. \(\overline{AB}\) passes through the center O, so it is a diameter. \(\overline{CB}\) has both endpoints on the circle but does not pass through the center, so it is a chord but not a diameter.

Real-World Connection.(RWC)
On Saturday morning, David rode his bike to Jordan's house, which is \(1\frac{3}{4}\) miles away. Then they rode to the park \(2\frac{3}{5}\) miles away and played basketball. Afterwards they rode back to Jordan's house. Finally, David rode back to his house. Calculate the number of miles David rode his bike on Saturday.
Answer
\(8\frac{7}{10}\text{ miles}\)
Solution
David's route: home → Jordan's \(1\frac{3}{4}\) miles, Jordan's → park \(2\frac{3}{5}\) miles, park → Jordan's \(2\frac{3}{5}\) miles, Jordan's → home \(1\frac{3}{4}\) miles. Total: \(2 \times \left(1\frac{3}{4} + 2\frac{3}{5}\right) = 2 \times \left(\frac{7}{4} + \frac{13}{5}\right) = 2 \times \left(\frac{35}{20} + \frac{52}{20}\right) = 2 \times \frac{87}{20} = \frac{174}{20} = 8\frac{7}{10}\) miles.
