1.(Lesson 37)RATIO
In a survey, \(\frac{3}{5}\) of the students in a class chose soccer as their favorite sport, and the rest chose basketball. What is the ratio of students who chose soccer to students who chose basketball?
Answer
\(3:2\)
Solution
The rest of the class is \(1 - \frac{3}{5} = \frac{2}{5}\). Compare the two parts: \(\frac{3}{5} : \frac{2}{5} = 3:2\).
2.(Lesson 37)EQUIVALENT FRACTIONS
Complete each equivalent fraction: a. \(\frac{4}{7} = \frac{?}{28}\)   b. \(\frac{?}{9} = \frac{15}{27}\)   c. \(\frac{5}{6} = \frac{25}{?}\)
Answer
\(\text{a. }16 \qquad \text{b. }5 \qquad \text{c. }30\)
Solution
a. The denominator \(7\) was multiplied by \(4\) to get \(28\), so multiply the numerator by \(4\): \(4 \times 4 = 16\). b. Reduce \(\frac{15}{27}\) by dividing both terms by \(3\): \(\frac{15}{27} = \frac{5}{9}\), so the missing numerator is \(5\). c. The numerator \(5\) was multiplied by \(5\) to get \(25\), so multiply the denominator by \(5\): \(6 \times 5 = 30\).
3.(Lesson 37)ROUNDING
Round 5273.8462: a. to two decimal places. b. to the nearest hundred.
Answer
\(\text{a. }5273.85 \qquad \text{b. }5300\)
Solution
a. The digit after the hundredths place is \(6\), so the hundredths digit rounds up: \(5273.8462 \approx 5273.85\). b. The tens digit of \(5273\) is \(7\), so the hundreds digit rounds up: \(5273 \approx 5300\).
4.(Lesson 37)SAMPLE SPACE & PROBABILITY
The face of this spinner is divided into fourths labeled A, B, C, and D. The spinner is spun twice. One possible outcome is A then A (AA). a. List the sample space of all possible outcomes. b. What is the probability that A appears at least once in the two spins?
Answer
\(\text{a. 16 outcomes (see solution)} \qquad \text{b. }\frac{7}{16}\)
Solution
a. Each spin can land on A, B, C, or D, so there are \(4 \times 4 = 16\) outcomes: AA, AB, AC, AD, BA, BB, BC, BD, CA, CB, CC, CD, DA, DB, DC, DD. b. The outcomes containing an A are AA, AB, AC, AD, BA, CA, and DA — \(7\) of the \(16\) outcomes: \(P(\text{A at least once}) = \frac{7}{16}\).
5.(Lesson 37)PERCENT & REDUCING
a. Write \(4\%\) as a reduced fraction. b. Reduce \(\frac{540}{810}\).
Answer
\(\text{a. }\frac{1}{25} \qquad \text{b. }\frac{2}{3}\)
Solution
a. Percent means “out of 100.” Divide both terms by \(4\): \(4\% = \frac{4}{100} = \frac{1}{25}\). b. Both terms divide by \(270\): \(\frac{540}{810} = \frac{540 \div 270}{810 \div 270} = \frac{2}{3}\).
6.(Lesson 37)EXPONENTS
Find the missing exponent: \(3^1 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 3^3 = 3^n\)
Answer
\(n = 6\)
Solution
When multiplying powers with the same base, add the exponents: \(3^{1+2+3} = 3^6\), so \(n = 6\).
7.(Lesson 38)PROBABILITY
The face of this spinner is divided into 8 equal sectors. a. What is the probability of spinning a 2? b. What is the probability of not spinning a 4?
Answer
\(\text{a. }\frac{3}{8} \qquad \text{b. }\frac{7}{8}\)
Solution
There are \(8\) equal sectors. The number \(2\) appears on \(3\) sectors, so \(P(2) = \frac{3}{8}\). The number \(4\) appears on only \(1\) sector, so \(7\) of the \(8\) sectors are not \(4\): \(P(\text{not }4) = \frac{7}{8}\).

Find the length of this line segment: a. in centimeters. b. in millimeters.
Answer
\(\text{a. }3.4\text{ cm} \qquad \text{b. }34\text{ mm}\)
Solution
a. The segment begins at \(0\) and ends at the \(3.4\) cm mark. b. Convert to millimeters: \(3.4 \times 10 = 34\) mm.
9.(Lesson 38)MEASUREMENT
Refer to this beaker to answer a and b. a. How many milliliters of liquid are in the beaker? b. How many milliliters less than 1 liter is that?
Answer
\(\text{a. }250\text{ mL} \qquad \text{b. }750\text{ mL}\)
Solution
a. The liquid reaches the \(250\) mL mark. b. One liter is \(1000\) mL, so \(1000 - 250 = 750\) mL less than a liter.
10.(Lesson 38)DECIMAL COMPARISON
Which number is greater, \(45.207\) or \(45.27\)? How do you know?
Answer
\(45.27\)
Solution
Write \(45.27\) with a thousandths digit: \(45.27 = 45.270\). Compare hundredths: \(45.207\) has \(0\) hundredths, while \(45.270\) has \(7\) hundredths. Since \(7 > 0\), \(45.27\) is greater.
11.(Lesson 38)ANGLES
The figure shows a right angle split into two angles by a ray. One angle measures \(38^\circ\). What is the measure of the other angle, marked \(x\)?
Answer
\(x = 52^\circ\)
Solution
The two angles together form a right angle, so they add to \(90^\circ\): \(x = 90^\circ - 38^\circ = 52^\circ\).
12.(Lesson 38)NUMBER LINE
Refer to the number line to answer a–c. a. What number does point A name? b. Which point represents \(3.8\)? c. How far apart are points B and D?
Answer
\(\text{a. }2.4 \qquad \text{b. Point C} \qquad \text{c. }1.2\)
Solution
Each small tick is one tenth. a. Point A sits at \(2.4\). b. Counting tenths from \(2\): \(3.8\) is point C. c. Point B is \(3.3\) and point D is \(4.5\), so the distance is \(4.5 - 3.3 = 1.2\).
13.(Lesson 39)LINE GRAPHS
The graph shows the weight of a puppy at ages 2 through 7 months. a. How many pounds did the puppy gain from month 5 to month 6? b. Between which two consecutive months did the puppy gain the most weight?
Answer
\(\text{a. }3\text{ lb} \qquad \text{b. Between months 4 and 5}\)
Solution
Read the weights from the graph: month 2: \(8\) lb, month 3: \(12\) lb, month 4: \(15\) lb, month 5: \(21\) lb, month 6: \(24\) lb, month 7: \(29\) lb. a. From month 5 to month 6: \(24 - 21 = 3\) lb. b. The gains are \(4, 3, 6, 3,\) and \(5\) lb. The greatest gain, \(6\) lb, was between months 4 and 5.
14.(Lesson 39)PROPORTIONS
Solve the proportion: \(\frac{5}{8} = \frac{n}{40}\)
Answer
\(n = 25\)
Solution
Cross-multiply: \(8n = 5 \times 40 = 200\). Divide by \(8\): \(n = 200 \div 8 = 25\).
15.(Lesson 39)PROPORTIONS
A recipe uses 2 cups of flour to make 12 muffins. How many cups of flour are needed to make 30 muffins at the same rate?
Answer
\(5\text{ cups}\)
Solution
Set up a proportion: \(\frac{2}{12} = \frac{x}{30}\). Cross-multiply: \(12x = 60\), so \(x = 60 \div 12 = 5\) cups.
16.(Lesson 39)PERCENT
What percent of 60 is 45? Use the bar diagram to help you.
Answer
\(75\%\)
Solution
The shaded part of the bar is \(45\) out of \(60\): \(\frac{45}{60} = \frac{3}{4} = 0.75 = 75\%\).
17.(Lesson 39)ORDERING FRACTIONS
Order these fractions from least to greatest: \(\frac{7}{12}\), \(\frac{3}{4}\), \(\frac{5}{6}\)
Answer
\(\frac{7}{12} < \frac{3}{4} < \frac{5}{6}\)
Solution
Use the common denominator \(24\): \(\frac{7}{12} = \frac{14}{24}\), \(\frac{3}{4} = \frac{18}{24}\), \(\frac{5}{6} = \frac{20}{24}\). Since \(14 < 18 < 20\), the order is \(\frac{7}{12} < \frac{3}{4} < \frac{5}{6}\).
18.(Lesson 39)MIXED-NUMBER DIVISION
Simplify: \(8 \div 2\frac{2}{3}\)
Answer
\(3\)
Solution
Change the mixed number to an improper fraction and multiply by its reciprocal: \(8 \div \frac{8}{3} = 8 \times \frac{3}{8} = \frac{24}{8} = 3\).
19.(Lesson 40)ANGLES
Two straight lines intersect as shown. One angle measures \(42^\circ\). a. What is the measure of the angle directly across from it (the vertical angle)? b. What is the measure of an angle next to it?
Answer
\(\text{a. }42^\circ \qquad \text{b. }138^\circ\)
Solution
a. Vertical angles are equal, so the angle across from the \(42^\circ\) angle also measures \(42^\circ\). b. Adjacent angles on a straight line are supplementary: \(180^\circ - 42^\circ = 138^\circ\).
20.(Lesson 40)UNIT CONVERSION
Simplify: \(2.5\text{ m} - 35\text{ cm}\). Give the answer in centimeters.
Answer
\(215\text{ cm}\)
Solution
Convert meters to centimeters first: \(2.5\text{ m} = 250\text{ cm}\). Then subtract: \(250 - 35 = 215\) cm.
21.(Lesson 40)COORDINATE AREA
A triangle has vertices at \((1, 1)\), \((6, 1)\), and \((1, 5)\) on a coordinate grid. What is the area of the triangle?
Answer
\(10\text{ square units}\)
Solution
The side from \((1, 1)\) to \((6, 1)\) is the base: \(6 - 1 = 5\) units. The side from \((1, 1)\) to \((1, 5)\) is the height: \(5 - 1 = 4\) units. Area: \(\frac{1}{2} \times 5 \times 4 = 10\) square units.
22.(Lesson 40)NUMBER LINE
What decimal number names the point marked with an arrow on this number line?
Answer
\(0.57\)
Solution
The distance between \(0.5\) and \(0.6\) is divided into \(10\) hundredths (\(0.01\) each). The arrow points to \(7\) ticks past \(0.5\): \(0.5 + 0.07 = 0.57\).
23.(Lesson 40)PROBABILITY
A bag contains 4 red marbles, 6 blue marbles, and 2 green marbles. One marble is drawn at random. a. What is the probability of drawing a red marble? b. What is the probability of not drawing a green marble?
Answer
\(\text{a. }\frac{1}{3} \qquad \text{b. }\frac{5}{6}\)
Solution
There are \(4 + 6 + 2 = 12\) marbles in all. a. \(P(\text{red}) = \frac{4}{12} = \frac{1}{3}\). b. Marbles that are not green: \(4 + 6 = 10\), so \(P(\text{not green}) = \frac{10}{12} = \frac{5}{6}\).
24.(Lesson 40)UNIT RATE
A car traveled 240 miles on 8 gallons of gasoline. a. How many miles per gallon did the car average? b. At this rate, how far could the car travel on 14 gallons?
Answer
\(\text{a. }30\text{ mpg} \qquad \text{b. }420\text{ miles}\)
Solution
a. Divide miles by gallons: \(240 \div 8 = 30\) miles per gallon. b. Multiply by the new amount: \(30 \times 14 = 420\) miles.
