Percentage Conversion Quiz

Test your skills! Convert the given percentage into a fraction (in simplest form) and a decimal. Click on an option to check your answer.
Question 1
Convert 45% to a fraction and a decimal.
Explanation:
45% as a decimal is $0.45$.
As a fraction, it is $\frac{45}{100}$. Divide the numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor, which is 5:
$45 \div 5 = 9$
$100 \div 5 = 20$
So, the simplified fraction is $\frac{9}{20}$.
Question 2
Convert 80% to a fraction and a decimal.
Explanation:
80% as a decimal is $0.80$ (or $0.8$).
As a fraction, it is $\frac{80}{100}$. Divide the numerator and denominator by 20:
$80 \div 20 = 4$
$100 \div 20 = 5$
So, the simplified fraction is $\frac{4}{5}$.

Question 5
Convert 120% to a fraction and a decimal.
Explanation:
120% as a decimal is $1.20$.
As a fraction, it is $\frac{120}{100}$. First, cancel a zero to get $\frac{12}{10}$. Then divide by 2 to get the improper fraction $\frac{6}{5}$.
Converting $\frac{6}{5}$ to a mixed number gives $1 \frac{1}{5}$.
