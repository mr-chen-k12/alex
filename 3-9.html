Saxon Math Course 1 - Lesson 63: Polygons

Written Practice

1. Three feet equals 1 yard. A car that is 15 feet long is how many yards long?
Answer: 5 yards

Explanation: Divide the length in feet by 3, since 3 feet = 1 yard: $15 \div 3 = 5$.
6. Erin opened a 1-gallon bottle of milk and began filling glasses. Each glass held 1 cup of milk. Two cups equals a pint. Two pints equals a quart. Four quarts equals a gallon. How many glasses could Erin fill?
Answer: 16 glasses

Explanation: $1 \text{ gallon} = 4 \text{ quarts} = 8 \text{ pints} = 16 \text{ cups}$. Since each glass is 1 cup, she can fill 16 glasses.
7. To what mixed number is the arrow pointing?
Answer: $6 \frac{1}{2}$

Explanation: The arrow points to the midpoint between 6 and 7 on the number line, which is exactly $6 \frac{1}{2}$.
11. (a) What is the name of this polygon?
(b) Each side is the same length. What is the perimeter of this polygon?
Answer: (a) Hexagon, (b) $6\text{ cm}$

Explanation: The polygon has 6 sides, making it a hexagon. Checking the ruler graphic, one side lines up perfectly between the 0 and 1 cm marks, meaning each side is $1\text{ cm}$ long. $1\text{ cm} \times 6\text{ sides} = 6\text{ cm}$.
23. Divide: $6\overline{)34}$
Answer: $5 \text{ R } 4$ (or $5\frac{2}{3}$)

Explanation: The problem asks to divide 34 by 6. 6 goes into 34 five times ($6 \times 5 = 30$). Subtracting 30 from 34 leaves a remainder of 4.
24. Divide: $8\overline{)62}$
Answer: $7 \text{ R } 6$ (or $7\frac{3}{4}$)

Explanation: The problem asks to divide 62 by 8. 8 goes into 62 seven times ($8 \times 7 = 56$). Subtracting 56 from 62 leaves a remainder of 6.
28. Which of these numbers is a factor of 10?
A. 3     B. 5     C. 15     D. 40
Answer: B. 5

Explanation: A factor is a number that divides evenly into another number without a remainder. $10 \div 5 = 2$. None of the other options divide evenly into 10.
29. (a) What fraction of the large square is shaded?
(b) What percent of the large square is not shaded?
Answer: (a) $0$, (b) $100\%$

Explanation: There are 100 small squares making up the grid. Because exactly 0 of them are shaded, $0$ is the fraction shaded, and $100\%$ of the square remains unshaded.
