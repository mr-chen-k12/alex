Math Practice - Comprehensive Review

Practice Problems 1-30

1. There are 12 inches in 1 foot. A board that is 4 feet long is how many inches long?
Answer: 48 inches

Explanation: Multiply the length in feet by 12. 4 × 12 = 48.
2. How many quarts are in 3 gallons? (Hint: 4 quarts = 1 gallon)
Answer: 12 quarts

Explanation: Multiply the number of gallons by 4. 3 × 4 = 12.
3. To what mixed number is the arrow pointing?
Answer: $2\frac{1}{4}$

Explanation: The space between 2 and 3 is divided into 4 equal segments. The arrow points to the first mark, which represents $\frac{1}{4}$.
4. A regular pentagon has a side length of 5 cm. What is its perimeter?
Answer: 25 cm

Explanation: A regular pentagon has 5 equal sides. 5 cm × 5 = 25 cm.
5. Divide: $7\overline{)45}$
Answer: 6 R 3

Explanation: 7 goes into 45 six times (7 × 6 = 42). 45 - 42 leaves a remainder of 3.
6. Divide: $9\overline{)85}$
Answer: 9 R 4

Explanation: 9 goes into 85 nine times (9 × 9 = 81). 85 - 81 leaves a remainder of 4.
7. Which of these numbers is a factor of 24?
A. 5     B. 7     C. 8     D. 10
Answer: C. 8

Explanation: 24 divided by 8 is exactly 3. The other numbers do not divide evenly into 24.
8. What fraction of the large square is shaded? What percent is shaded?
Answer: Fraction: $\frac{1}{4}$, Percent: 25%

Explanation: The square is divided into 4 equal quadrants. 1 out of 4 is shaded, which is $\frac{1}{4}$ or 25%.
9. What is the area of a rectangle with a length of 8 meters and a width of 5 meters?
Answer: 40 square meters

Explanation: Area = length × width. 8 × 5 = 40.
10. What is the volume of a cube that has a side length of 4 inches?
Answer: 64 cubic inches

Explanation: Volume = side × side × side. 4 × 4 × 4 = 64.
11. Find the mean (average) of the following numbers: 10, 15, and 20.
Answer: 15

Explanation: Add the numbers together: 10 + 15 + 20 = 45. Divide by the count (3): 45 ÷ 3 = 15.
12. A train leaves the station at 8:30 a.m. and arrives at its destination at 1:15 p.m. How long did the trip take?
Answer: 4 hours and 45 minutes

Explanation: From 8:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. is 4 hours. From 12:30 p.m. to 1:15 p.m. is 45 minutes.
13. In a class of 20 students, 8 are boys. What percent of the class are boys?
Answer: 40%

Explanation: The fraction is $\frac{8}{20}$. Multiply numerator and denominator by 5 to get a base of 100: $\frac{40}{100}$, which is 40%.
14. How many edges does a rectangular prism have?
Answer: 12 edges

Explanation: A rectangular prism (like a standard box) has 4 edges on the top, 4 on the bottom, and 4 vertical edges connecting them.
15. The diameter of a circle is 14 cm. What is its radius?
Answer: 7 cm

Explanation: The radius is exactly half of the diameter. 14 ÷ 2 = 7.
16. What is the area of a circle with a radius of 10 inches? (Use 3.14 for $\pi$)
Answer: 314 square inches

Explanation: Area = $\pi r^2$. 3.14 × $10^2$ = 3.14 × 100 = 314.
17. Arrange these numbers from least to greatest: -5, 2, -1, 0
Answer: -5, -1, 0, 2

Explanation: Negative numbers are smaller than zero. -5 is further to the left on the number line than -1.
18. Find the value of $n$: $$8n = 72$$
Answer: $n = 9$

Explanation: Divide both sides by 8. 72 ÷ 8 = 9.
19. What is the area of this triangle?
Answer: 25 square cm

Explanation: Area = $\frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}$. $\frac{1}{2} \times 10 \times 5 = 25$.
20. A triangle has sides of 6 cm, 8 cm, and 10 cm. What is its perimeter?
Answer: 24 cm

Explanation: Add all the side lengths together: 6 + 8 + 10 = 24.
21. The ratio of apples to oranges in a basket is 3 to 4. If there are 12 apples, how many oranges are there?
Answer: 16 oranges

Explanation: The ratio is $\frac{3}{4}$. If apples (the 3) scales up to 12 (multiplied by 4), then scale the oranges (the 4) by 4 as well. 4 × 4 = 16.
22. Add: $1\frac{1}{2} + 2\frac{1}{4}$
Answer: $3\frac{3}{4}$

Explanation: Find a common denominator. $1\frac{1}{2}$ becomes $1\frac{2}{4}$. Add $1\frac{2}{4} + 2\frac{1}{4} = 3\frac{3}{4}$.
23. Subtract: $5 - 2\frac{1}{3}$
Answer: $2\frac{2}{3}$

Explanation: Borrow from the whole number: 5 becomes $4\frac{3}{3}$. Now subtract: $4\frac{3}{3} - 2\frac{1}{3} = 2\frac{2}{3}$.
24. Multiply: $\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{3}{4}$
Answer: $\frac{1}{2}$

Explanation: Multiply straight across: $\frac{6}{12}$. Simplify by dividing numerator and denominator by 6 to get $\frac{1}{2}$.
25. Divide: $1.25 \div 10$
Answer: 0.125

Explanation: Dividing a decimal by 10 moves the decimal point one place to the left.
26. Round 4.672 to the nearest hundredth.
Answer: 4.67

Explanation: Look at the thousandths place (2). Since it is less than 5, keep the hundredths place (7) exactly the same.
27. Evaluate: $\sqrt{81} + \sqrt{64}$
Answer: 17

Explanation: The square root of 81 is 9. The square root of 64 is 8. Add them: 9 + 8 = 17.
28. Simplify using the order of operations: $5 + 3 \times 4 - 2$
Answer: 15

Explanation: Multiply first: $3 \times 4 = 12$. Then add and subtract from left to right: $5 + 12 - 2 = 17 - 2 = 15$.
29. Two dozen cookies are shared equally among 6 people. How many cookies does each person get?
Answer: 4 cookies

Explanation: Two dozen equals 24 cookies. 24 divided by 6 people equals 4 cookies per person.
30. Simplify: $50\text{ cm} + 200\text{ mm}$ (Write the answer in centimeters.)
Answer: 70 cm

Explanation: First, convert mm to cm. There are 10 mm in 1 cm, so 200 mm = 20 cm. Then add: 50 cm + 20 cm = 70 cm.
