Practice Math Problem Solutions

1. Twelve inches equals 1 foot. A wooden board that is 60 inches long is how many feet long?
Answer: 5 feet
To convert inches to feet, divide the total number of inches by 12.
$60 \div 12 = 5$.
2. Write four multiplication/division facts using the numbers 4, 8, and 32.
Answer: $4 \times 8 = 32$, $8 \times 4 = 32$, $32 \div 8 = 4$, $32 \div 4 = 8$
These make up the "fact family" for these three numbers, showing the inverse relationship between multiplication and division.
3. Marcus had five quarters, four dimes, and twelve pennies. How much money did he have in all?
Answer: $1.77
Calculate the value of each coin type, then sum them up:
Quarters: $5 \times \$0.25 = \$1.25$
Dimes: $4 \times \$0.10 = \$0.40$
Pennies: $12 \times \$0.01 = \$0.12$
Total: $\$1.25 + \$0.40 + \$0.12 = \$1.77$
4. What is the sum of the even numbers that are greater than 20 but less than 30?
Answer: 100
First, identify the even numbers between 20 and 30: 22, 24, 26, and 28.
Then, find their sum: $22 + 24 + 26 + 28 = 100$.
5. Estimate the sum of 823 and 481 by rounding the numbers to the nearest hundred and then adding.
Answer: 1300
Round 823 to the nearest hundred: 800.
Round 481 to the nearest hundred: 500.
Add the rounded numbers: $800 + 500 = 1300$.
6. David opened a 1-gallon jug of apple cider and began filling pint glasses. Two cups equals a pint. Two pints equals a quart. Four quarts equals a gallon. How many pint glasses could David fill?
Answer: 8 pint glasses
Work backwards to find out how many pints are in a gallon:
1 gallon = 4 quarts.
4 quarts = $4 \times 2 = 8$ pints.
Since 1 glass holds 1 pint, he can fill 8 glasses.
7. To what mixed number is the arrow pointing? (Imagine a number line showing an arrow at the 3rd tick mark out of 4 between 8 and 9).
Answer: $8 \frac{3}{4}$
The space between 8 and 9 is divided into 4 equal segments, meaning each mark represents one-fourth ($\frac{1}{4}$). The arrow points to the third mark past 8, which is $8 \frac{3}{4}$.
8. The pizza was cut into 8 equal slices. Three of the slices were eaten. What fraction of the pizza was left?
Answer: $\frac{5}{8}$
Subtract the eaten pieces from the total pieces: $8 - 3 = 5$. There are 5 pieces left out of the original 8, making the fraction $\frac{5}{8}$.
9. The product of 5 and 4 is how much greater than the sum of 5 and 4?
Answer: 11
Calculate the product: $5 \times 4 = 20$.
Calculate the sum: $5 + 4 = 9$.
Find the difference: $20 - 9 = 11$.
10. What is the sum of $8^2$ and $\sqrt{16}$?
Answer: 68
Calculate eight squared: $8^2 = 64$.
Calculate the square root of sixteen: $\sqrt{16} = 4$.
Add them together: $64 + 4 = 68$.
11. (a) What is the name of a regular polygon with 5 sides? (b) If each side is 2 cm long, what is the perimeter of this polygon?
Answer: (a) Pentagon (b) 10 cm
(a) A polygon with 5 sides is called a pentagon.
(b) Since all 5 sides are the same length, the perimeter is $5 \times 2\text{ cm} = 10\text{ cm}$.
12. Maria could sort 45 apples in 9 minutes. At that rate, how many apples could she sort in 1 minute?
Answer: 5 apples
Divide the total number of apples by the total number of minutes to find the unit rate: $45 \div 9 = 5$.
13. Leo could build 12 widgets in 1 minute. At that rate, how many widgets could he build in 4 minutes?
Answer: 48 widgets
Multiply the rate per minute by the number of minutes: $12 \times 4 = 48$.
14. $\$50.00 - D = \$3.75$
Answer: $D = \$46.25$
To isolate $D$, subtract the result from the total: $\$50.00 - \$3.75 = \$46.25$.
15. $4 \times N = 10 + \sqrt{36}$
Answer: $N = 4$
First, evaluate the square root: $\sqrt{36} = 6$.
Substitute back into the equation: $4 \times N = 10 + 6$.
Simplify: $4 \times N = 16$.
Divide both sides by 4: $N = 4$.
16. $7 \times 3 \times 100$
Answer: 2100
Multiply left to right: $7 \times 3 = 21$. Then multiply by 100: $21 \times 100 = 2100$.
17. $4^3$
Answer: 64
An exponent of 3 means multiplying the base number by itself three times: $4 \times 4 \times 4 = 64$.
18. $4.2 + 1.35$
Answer: 5.55
Align the decimal points by adding a zero to 4.2 to make it 4.20. Then add: $4.20 + 1.35 = 5.55$.
19. $2.85 - 0.3$
Answer: 2.55
Align the decimal points by adding a zero to 0.3 to make it 0.30. Then subtract: $2.85 - 0.30 = 2.55$.
20. $\$5.00 - (\$1.25 + \$2.50)$
Answer: $1.25
Following order of operations, solve parentheses first: $\$1.25 + \$2.50 = \$3.75$.
Subtract that sum from five dollars: $\$5.00 - \$3.75 = \$1.25$.
21. $428 \times 6$
Answer: 2568
Multiply: $428 \times 6 = 2568$.
22. $\$8.40 \times 5$
Answer: $42.00
Multiply the values ignoring the decimal first ($840 \times 5 = 4200$), then place the decimal two spots from the right: $\$42.00$.
23. $45 \div 7$ (From long division symbol $7\overline{)45}$)
Answer: 6 R 3 (or $6 \frac{3}{7}$)
7 goes into 45 six times ($7 \times 6 = 42$). Subtracting 42 from 45 leaves a remainder of 3.
24. $75 \div 9$ (From long division symbol $9\overline{)75}$)
Answer: 8 R 3 (or $8 \frac{1}{3}$)
9 goes into 75 eight times ($9 \times 8 = 72$). Subtracting 72 from 75 leaves a remainder of 3. (As a fraction, $\frac{3}{9}$ simplifies to $\frac{1}{3}$).
25. $38 \div 6$ (From long division symbol $6\overline{)38}$)
Answer: 6 R 2 (or $6 \frac{1}{3}$)
6 goes into 38 six times ($6 \times 6 = 36$). Subtracting 36 from 38 leaves a remainder of 2. (As a fraction, $\frac{2}{6}$ simplifies to $\frac{1}{3}$).
26. $\frac{72}{8}$
Answer: 9
A fraction bar represents division. $72 \div 8 = 9$.
27. If 75% of the marbles in a jar are blue, what percent of the marbles are not blue?
Answer: 25%
The total amount is always 100%. Subtract the percentage of blue marbles to find the remaining percent: $100\% - 75\% = 25\%$.
28. Which of these numbers is a factor of 12?
A. 5    B. 7    C. 24    D. 4
Answer: D. 4
A factor is a number that divides evenly into another number without a remainder. 4 divides evenly into 12 ($12 \div 4 = 3$). *Note: 24 is a multiple, not a factor.*
29. Imagine a 10x10 square grid. 4 full columns of the grid are shaded. (a) What fraction of the large square is shaded? (b) What percent of the large square is not shaded?
Answer: (a) $\frac{2}{5}$ (b) 60%
(a) The grid has 100 small squares. 4 full columns are shaded, meaning 40 squares are shaded. The fraction is $\frac{40}{100}$, which simplifies to $\frac{4}{10}$, and further simplifies to $\frac{2}{5}$.
(b) If 40 out of 100 squares (40%) are shaded, then the unshaded portion is $100\% - 40\% = 60\%$.
