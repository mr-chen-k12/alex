Written Practice - Lesson 81

1. The Jones family had 2 gallons of milk. They used 2 quarts. How many quarts are left?

1 gallon = 4 quarts. So, 2 gallons = 8 quarts.

Calculation: \(8 \text{ quarts} - 2 \text{ quarts} = 6 \text{ quarts}\).

Answer: 6 quarts

4. A plane flew 1200 miles in 3 hours. Find the average speed.

Speed = Distance ÷ Time

$$\frac{1200 \text{ miles}}{3 \text{ hours}} = 400 \text{ mph}$$

Answer: 400 miles per hour

7. What is the area of the parallelogram (Base 24m, Height 25m, Slant 26m)?

Area of a parallelogram = \(Base \times Height\). We use the perpendicular height (25m), not the slant (26m).

$$24\text{m} \times 25\text{m} = 600\text{m}^2$$

Answer: 600 m²

18. Simplify: \(\frac{5}{6} \cdot \frac{4}{5} \cdot \frac{3}{8}\)

Multiply numerators and denominators (or cancel out first):

$$\frac{5 \cdot 4 \cdot 3}{6 \cdot 5 \cdot 8} = \frac{60}{240} = \frac{1}{4}$$

Answer: 1/4

21-22. Perimeter of square is 2 meters. Find side in cm, and circle diameter/circumference.

21: Perimeter = 2m = 200cm. Side = \(200 / 4 = 50\text{cm}\).

22a: The diameter of the inscribed circle equals the side of the square. Diameter = 50cm.

22b: Circumference = \(\pi d = 3.14 \times 50 = 157\text{cm}\).

Answers: 21) 50cm | 22a) 50cm | 22b) 157cm

27. What is the name of the geometric solid?

The solid has a circular base and tapers to a single point (vertex).

Answer: Cone
