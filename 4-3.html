Lesson Practice

Simplify the following:
a. $-4 + -2 - -6 + -1$
b. $-5 + (+4) - (+2) - (-3)$
c. $+2 + -5 - +3 + +6 - -2$
d. $4 + (-1) - (-6) - (+5) + (+2)$
e. $7 - -2 + -6 - +3 + +1$
f. $(-20) - (+30) - (-40) + (-50)$

Explanations: Convert subtractions to additions of the opposite, then combine like signs.

  • a. $-4 - 2 + 6 - 1 = -7 + 6 = $ $-1$
  • b. $-5 + 4 - 2 + 3 = -7 + 7 = $ $0$
  • c. $2 - 5 - 3 + 6 + 2 = 10 - 8 = $ $2$
  • d. $4 - 1 + 6 - 5 + 2 = 12 - 6 = $ $6$
  • e. $7 + 2 - 6 - 3 + 1 = 10 - 9 = $ $1$
  • f. $-20 - 30 + 40 - 50 = -100 + 40 = $ $-60$

Written Practice

1. A triangular prism has how many more edges than faces?

Explanation: A triangular prism has 5 faces (2 triangular bases, 3 rectangular sides) and 9 edges (3 on each triangle base, 3 connecting them). The difference is $9 - 5 = 4$.

Answer: 4

2. A puppy weighed 5 lb 10 oz when it was born and 9 lb 4 oz at 2 months. How many pounds and ounces did the puppy gain in 2 months?

Explanation: Subtract the birth weight from the 2-month weight.
$9\text{ lb } 4\text{ oz} - 5\text{ lb } 10\text{ oz}$. Since 4 oz is less than 10 oz, borrow 1 lb (16 oz) from the 9 lbs.
$8\text{ lb } (4+16)\text{ oz} = 8\text{ lb } 20\text{ oz}$.
Now subtract: $8\text{ lb } 20\text{ oz} - 5\text{ lb } 10\text{ oz} = 3\text{ lb } 10\text{ oz}$.

Answer: 3 lb 10 oz

3. There are 8 cats and 12 dogs at the animal shelter. What is the ratio of cats to dogs?

Explanation: The ratio is $\frac{\text{Cats}}{\text{Dogs}} = \frac{8}{12}$. Reduce the fraction by dividing by 4 to get $\frac{2}{3}$.

Answer: 2/3

4. A team's win-loss ratio was 4 to 1. If the team had played 25 games without a tie, how many games had it won?

Explanation: Total ratio parts = $4 \text{ (wins)} + 1 \text{ (losses)} = 5 \text{ parts}$.
Total games = 25. Divide total games by total parts: $\frac{25}{5} = 5 \text{ games per part}$.
Multiply by the win parts: $4 \times 5 = 20 \text{ wins}$.

Answer: 20 games

5. If Marco tosses a coin and rolls a number cube, what is the probability of the coin landing tails up and the number cube stopping with a 3 on top?

Explanation: These are independent events. Multiply their probabilities.
$P(\text{Tails}) = \frac{1}{2}$. $P(3) = \frac{1}{6}$.
$P(\text{Tails and } 3) = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{6} = \frac{1}{12}$.

Answer: 1/12

6. a. What is the perimeter of this parallelogram?
b. What is the area of this parallelogram?

Explanation:
a. Perimeter is the distance around the outside. $P = 2(\text{base}) + 2(\text{side}) = 2(10) + 2(5) = 20 + 10 = 30\text{ cm}$.
b. Area is base $\times$ height. $A = 10\text{ cm} \times 4\text{ cm} = 40\text{ cm}^2$.

Answer: a. 30 cm; b. 40 cm$^2$

7. If each acute angle of a parallelogram measures $42^\circ$, then what is the measure of each obtuse angle?

Explanation: Consecutive angles in a parallelogram are supplementary (they add up to $180^\circ$).
$180^\circ - 42^\circ = 138^\circ$.

Answer: $138^\circ$

8. The center of this circle is the origin. The circle passes through (3, 0).
a. Estimate the area of the circle in square units by counting squares.
b. Calculate the area of the circle by using 3.14 for $\pi$.

Explanation:
a. Counting full and partial squares inside the circle roughly yields about 28 squares.
b. The radius is 3 units. Area $= \pi r^2 \approx 3.14 \times (3)^2 = 3.14 \times 9 = 28.26$.

Answer: a. ~28 sq units; b. 28.26

9. Which ratio forms a proportion with $\frac{3}{5}$?
A $\frac{6}{15}$     B $\frac{9}{15}$     C $\frac{5}{3}$     D $\frac{4}{5}$

Explanation: A proportion is an equation stating two ratios are equal. Reduce each option to see which equals $\frac{3}{5}$. Only B ($\frac{9}{15}$) reduces to $\frac{3}{5}$ (divide top and bottom by 3).

Answer: B

10. Complete this proportion: $\frac{4}{10} = \frac{x}{15}$

Explanation: Cross-multiply to solve for $x$:
$10 \times x = 4 \times 15$
$10x = 60$
$x = 6$

Answer: 6

11. What is the perimeter of the polygon at right? Dimensions are in centimeters.

Explanation: Find the missing side lengths.
The unknown inner vertical side + 4 (right vertical side) = 10 (total left vertical side). So the inner vertical side is $10 - 4 = 6$.
The unknown inner horizontal side + 6 (top horizontal) = 14 (bottom horizontal). So the inner horizontal side is $14 - 6 = 8$.
Perimeter $= 10 \text{ (left)} + 6 \text{ (top)} + 6 \text{ (inner vertical)} + 8 \text{ (inner horizontal)} + 4 \text{ (right)} + 14 \text{ (bottom)} = 48\text{ cm}$.

Answer: 48 cm

15. Alex bought a book for 30% off the regular price of $8.50. What was the sale price of the book?

Explanation: If it is 30% off, he pays 70% of the price.
Sale Price = $0.70 \times 8.50 = 5.95$.

16. Between which two consecutive whole numbers is $\sqrt{150}$?

Explanation: Find perfect squares near 150.
$12^2 = 144$ and $13^2 = 169$. Since 150 is between 144 and 169, $\sqrt{150}$ is between 12 and 13.

Answer: 12 and 13

17. Compare: $\left(\frac{1}{6}\right)^2 \bigcirc$ the probability of rolling two 6s on two standard number cubes

Explanation:
Left side: $\left(\frac{1}{6}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{36}$.
Right side: $P(\text{two 6s}) = \frac{1}{6} \times \frac{1}{6} = \frac{1}{36}$.
They are equal.

Answer: $=$

18. Divide 0.815 by 0.04 and round the quotient to the nearest whole number.

Explanation: $0.815 \div 0.04 = 81.5 \div 4 = 20.375$.
Rounding to the nearest whole number gives 20.

Answer: 20

19. The average of four numbers is 15. What is the sum of the four numbers?

Explanation: Average $= \frac{\text{Sum}}{\text{Count}}$.
Therefore, $\text{Sum} = \text{Average} \times \text{Count} = 15 \times 4 = 60$.

Answer: 60

20. Write the prime factorization of 360 using exponents.

Explanation: Create a factor tree for 360.
$360 = 36 \times 10$
$36 = 6 \times 6 = 2 \times 3 \times 2 \times 3$
$10 = 2 \times 5$
Combine all prime factors: $2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 \times 5 = 2^3 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5$.

Answer: $2^3 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5$

21. $-5 + -2 - -7 - +3$

Explanation: Change subtractions to adding the opposite.
$-5 - 2 + 7 - 3 = -7 + 7 - 3 = 0 - 3 = -3$.

Answer: $-3$

22. $2^5 + 4^2 \times 3 - \sqrt{144} \times 3^2$

Explanation: Follow order of operations (PEMDAS/GEMDAS).
Exponents and roots first: $32 + 16 \times 3 - 12 \times 9$.
Multiplication: $32 + 48 - 108$.
Addition and Subtraction: $80 - 108 = -28$.

Answer: $-28$

23. How many blocks 1 inch on each edge would it take to fill a box that is 10 inches long, 8 inches wide, and 4 inches tall?

Explanation: Find the volume of the box in cubic inches.
$V = \text{length} \times \text{width} \times \text{height} = 10 \times 8 \times 4 = 320$. Since each block is 1 cubic inch, it takes 320 blocks.

Answer: 320 blocks

24. Two fifths of the 50 students brought their lunch to school. How many students did not bring their lunch?

Explanation: If $\frac{2}{5}$ brought lunch, then $\frac{3}{5}$ did not bring lunch.
$\frac{3}{5} \text{ of } 50 = (50 \div 5) \times 3 = 10 \times 3 = 30$.

Answer: 30 students

25. The distance a train travels can be found by multiplying the speed of the train by the amount of time it travels at that speed. How far would a train travel in 3 hours at 75 kilometers per hour?
$\frac{75 \text{ km}}{1 \text{ hr}} \times \frac{3 \text{ hr}}{1}$

Explanation: Multiply the given fractions, canceling the "hr" units.
$75 \times 3 = 225\text{ km}$.

Answer: 225 km

26. Use the figure on the right to answer a - c.
a. What is the area of the shaded rectangle?
b. What is the area of the unshaded rectangle?
c. What is the combined area of the two rectangles?

Explanation:
a. The shaded rectangle is 10 cm high and 4 cm wide. Area $= 10 \times 4 = 40\text{ cm}^2$.
b. The unshaded rectangle is 5 cm high and 7 cm wide. Area $= 5 \times 7 = 35\text{ cm}^2$.

27. A student measured the circumference and diameter of four circles. Then she divided the circumference by the diameter of each circle to find the number of diameters in a circumference. Here are her answers:
3.13, 3.18, 3.14, 3.11
Find the average of her answers. Round the average to the nearest hundredth.

Explanation: Add the numbers and divide by 4.
$3.13 + 3.18 + 3.14 + 3.11 = 12.56$.
$12.56 \div 4 = 3.14$.
The average is already at the nearest hundredth.

Answer: 3.14

28. Elena was thinking of a specific day of the year (in a non-leap year), and she asked Juan to guess the date. Describe how you can find the probability that Juan will guess correctly on the first try.

Explanation: A standard non-leap year has 365 days. Since Juan makes exactly 1 guess, his chance is 1 out of the 365 possibilities.

Answer: The probability is 1/365. (Set the correct guess as 1 favorable outcome over 365 total possible days).

29. The coordinates of three vertices of a triangle are (4, 2), (-2, 2), and (-2, -4). What is the area of the triangle?

Explanation: Plotting these points reveals a right triangle.
The base connects (-2, 2) and (4, 2). Length $= 4 - (-2) = 6$ units.
The height connects (-2, 2) and (-2, -4). Length $= 2 - (-4) = 6$ units.
$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = \frac{1}{2} \times 6 \times 6 = 18\text{ sq units}$.

Answer: 18 square units

30. Convert 3 yards to inches using unit multipliers:
$\frac{3 \text{ yd}}{1} \times \frac{3 \text{ ft}}{1 \text{ yd}} \times \frac{12 \text{ in}}{1 \text{ ft}}$

Explanation: Cancel out the units that appear in both the numerator and the denominator (yards and feet).
You are left with $3 \times 3 \times 12 \text{ in} = 108 \text{ inches}$.
