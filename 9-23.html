Problem 1
Forty-four of the one hundred ninety-three flags of countries around the world do not feature the color red. How many flags do feature red?

Explanation: Subtract the number of flags that do not feature red from the total number of flags.

$$\text{Total flags} = 193$$

$$\text{Flags without red} = 44$$

$$\text{Flags with red} = 193 - 44 = 149$$

Answer: 149 flags
Problem 2
At Henry's egg ranch 18 eggs are packaged in each carton. How many cartons would be needed to package 4500 eggs?

Explanation: Divide the total number of eggs by the capacity of one carton.

$$\text{Cartons needed} = \frac{4500}{18} = 250$$

Answer: 250 cartons

Problem 4
EXPLAIN: If a coin is flipped once, which outcome is more likely, heads or tails? Explain your answer.

Explanation: A fair coin has two equally likely outcomes (heads or tails). The probability for either outcome is $\frac{1}{2}$ or $50\%$.

Answer: Neither; both outcomes are equally likely because both have a probability of $\frac{1}{2}$.
Problem 5
Replace each circle with the proper comparison symbol:

a. $\frac{8}{10} \bigcirc \frac{4}{5}$

b. $\frac{8}{5} \bigcirc 1\frac{2}{5}$

Explanation:

a. Reduce $\frac{8}{10}$ by dividing numerator and denominator by 2: $\frac{8 \div 2}{10 \div 2} = \frac{4}{5}$. Thus, $\frac{8}{10} = \frac{4}{5}$.

b. Convert $1\frac{2}{5}$ to an improper fraction: $1\frac{2}{5} = \frac{7}{5}$. Since $\frac{8}{5} > \frac{7}{5}$, we have $\frac{8}{5} > 1\frac{2}{5}$.

Answer: a. $=$   |   b. $>$

Problem 7
Write each number as a reduced fraction or mixed number:

a. $\frac{8}{12}$      b. $40\%$      c. $6\frac{10}{12}$

Explanation:

a. $\frac{8}{12} = \frac{8 \div 4}{12 \div 4} = \frac{2}{3}$

b. $40\% = \frac{40}{100} = \frac{40 \div 20}{100 \div 20} = \frac{2}{5}$

c. $6\frac{10}{12} = 6 + \frac{10 \div 2}{12 \div 2} = 6\frac{5}{6}$

Answer: a. $\frac{2}{3}$   |   b. $\frac{2}{5}$   |   c. $6\frac{5}{6}$
Problem 8
ANALYZE: For each fraction, find an equivalent fraction that has a denominator of 24:

a. $\frac{5}{6}$      b. $\frac{3}{8}$      c. $\frac{1}{4}$

Explanation: Multiply the numerator and denominator by the factor that makes the denominator 24:

a. $\frac{5 \times 4}{6 \times 4} = \frac{20}{24}$

b. $\frac{3 \times 3}{8 \times 3} = \frac{9}{24}$

c. $\frac{1 \times 6}{4 \times 6} = \frac{6}{24}$

Answer: a. $\frac{20}{24}$   |   b. $\frac{9}{24}$   |   c. $\frac{6}{24}$
Problem 9
ANALYZE: Arrange these fractions in order from least to greatest. Explain the steps you used to order the fractions. $$\frac{5}{6}, \quad \frac{5}{8}, \quad \frac{3}{4}$$

Explanation:

  1. Find a common denominator for 6, 8, and 4, which is 24.
  2. Convert each fraction: $$\frac{5}{6} = \frac{20}{24}, \quad \frac{5}{8} = \frac{15}{24}, \quad \frac{3}{4} = \frac{18}{24}$$
  3. Compare numerators: $15 < 18 < 20$, which corresponds to $\frac{5}{8} < \frac{3}{4} < \frac{5}{6}$.
Answer: $\frac{5}{8}, \frac{3}{4}, \frac{5}{6}$
Problem 10

a. What percent of a yard is a foot?

b. What fraction of a gallon is a quart?

Explanation:

a. $1\text{ yard} = 3\text{ feet}$. So $1\text{ foot}$ is $\frac{1}{3}$ of a yard. $\frac{1}{3} = 33\frac{1}{3}\%$.

b. $1\text{ gallon} = 4\text{ quarts}$. So $1\text{ quart}$ is $\frac{1}{4}$ of a gallon.

Answer: a. $33\frac{1}{3}\%$   |   b. $\frac{1}{4}$
Problem 11
The number 630 is divisible by which single-digit numbers?

Explanation: Test single digits from 1 through 9:

  • 1: Every integer is divisible by 1 (Yes).
  • 2: Ends in 0 (even) $\rightarrow$ Yes.
  • 3: Sum of digits $6+3+0=9$ (divisible by 3) $\rightarrow$ Yes.
  • 4: $630 \div 4 = 157.5$ $\rightarrow$ No.
  • 5: Ends in 0 $\rightarrow$ Yes.
  • 6: Divisible by 2 and 3 $\rightarrow$ Yes.
  • 7: $630 \div 7 = 90$ $\rightarrow$ Yes.
  • 8: $630 \div 8 = 78.75$ $\rightarrow$ No.
  • 9: Sum of digits $6+3+0=9$ (divisible by 9) $\rightarrow$ Yes.
Answer: 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 9
Problem 12
Convert each improper fraction to either a whole number or a mixed number:

a. $\frac{16}{7}$      b. $3\frac{16}{8}$      c. $2\frac{16}{9}$

Explanation:

a. $16 \div 7 = 2\text{ remainder } 2 \implies 2\frac{2}{7}$

b. $\frac{16}{8} = 2 \implies 3 + 2 = 5$

c. $\frac{16}{9} = 1\frac{7}{9} \implies 2 + 1\frac{7}{9} = 3\frac{7}{9}$

Answer: a. $2\frac{2}{7}$   |   b. $5$   |   c. $3\frac{7}{9}$
Problem 13
Which properties are illustrated by these equations?

a. $\frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{2}{2} = \frac{2}{4}$

b. $\frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{3}{1} = 1$

Explanation:

a. Multiplying a number by 1 (expressed as $\frac{2}{2}$) does not change its value. This is the Identity Property of Multiplication.

b. Multiplying a number by its reciprocal yields 1. This is the Inverse Property of Multiplication (Multiplicative Inverse Property).

Answer: a. Identity Property of Multiplication   |   b. Inverse Property of Multiplication

Problem 26

Problem 27
If two intersecting lines are not perpendicular, then they form which two types of angles?

Explanation: When two lines intersect at an angle other than $90^\circ$, two angles are less than $90^\circ$ (acute) and two angles are greater than $90^\circ$ (obtuse).

Answer: Acute angles and obtuse angles

Problem 29
Find a fraction equivalent to $\frac{2}{3}$ that has a denominator of 6. Then add that fraction to $\frac{1}{6}$. What is the sum?

Explanation:

  1. Find equivalent fraction: $\frac{2 \times 2}{3 \times 2} = \frac{4}{6}$.
  2. Add to $\frac{1}{6}$: $$\frac{4}{6} + \frac{1}{6} = \frac{5}{6}$$
Answer: Equivalent fraction: $\frac{4}{6}$   |   Sum: $\frac{5}{6}$
Problem 30
How many $\frac{3}{8}$s are in 1?

Explanation: Divide 1 by $\frac{3}{8}$, which is equivalent to multiplying by its reciprocal.

$$1 \div \frac{3}{8} = 1 \times \frac{8}{3} = \frac{8}{3} = 2\frac{2}{3}$$

Answer: $2\frac{2}{3}$ (or $\frac{8}{3}$)
