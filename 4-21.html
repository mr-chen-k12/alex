Lesson Practice

Problem a
Draw a square and a rhombus that is not a square. Draw all the lines of symmetry for each shape, if any exist.
Answer & Visual:
Explanation:

A square is a regular quadrilateral and has exactly 4 lines of symmetry (horizontal, vertical, and two diagonals). A non-square rhombus has exactly 2 lines of symmetry, running straight through its opposite vertices (diagonally relative to the shape).

Problem b
All but one of these letters can be drawn to have a line of symmetry. Which of these letters does not have a line of symmetry?

A   C   F   I   T   Y
Answer:

F

Explanation:

A, I, T, and Y have a vertical line of symmetry. C and I have a horizontal line of symmetry. The letter F cannot be folded over any line to match perfectly.

Problem c
Which three of these letters have rotational symmetry? (Hint: Rotating your paper upside down might help.)

N   P   S   U   W   Z
Answer:

N, S, and Z

Explanation:

If you rotate the letters N, S, and Z by 180 degrees (upside down), they look identical to their original orientation. The others (P, U, W) will look upside down or change into other letters (W becomes M).

Written Practice

Problem 1
When the greatest four-digit number is divided by the greatest two-digit number, what is the quotient?
Answer:

101

Explanation:

The greatest four-digit number is 9999. The greatest two-digit number is 99.
$$9999 \div 99 = 101$$

Problem 2
The ratio of boys to girls in a school chorus is 5 to 4. If there are 20 girls in the chorus, how many boys are there?
Answer:

25 boys

Explanation:

Set up a proportion: $$\frac{\text{Boys}}{\text{Girls}} = \frac{5}{4} = \frac{B}{20}$$ Cross-multiply or find the scale factor: $4 \times 5 = 20$, so $5 \times 5 = 25$.

Problem 3
A wooden cube has side lengths of 5 inches. How many cubic inches is the volume of the cube?
Answer:

125 cubic inches

Explanation:

The volume of a cube is calculated by cubing the side length ($s^3$).
$$V = 5 \times 5 \times 5 = 25 \times 5 = 125 \text{ in}^3$$

Problem 4
A full turn is 360 degrees. How many degrees is $\frac{1}{6}$ of a turn?
Answer:

60 degrees

Explanation:

Multiply the full turn by the fraction: $$360^\circ \times \frac{1}{6} = 60^\circ$$

Problem 5
Does a regular pentagon have rotational and line symmetry? Use words to explain how you know.
Answer:

Yes, it has both.

Explanation:

Line Symmetry: A regular pentagon has 5 lines of symmetry (each connecting a vertex to the midpoint of the opposite side).
Rotational Symmetry: Because all 5 sides and angles are equal, rotating the pentagon by 72 degrees ($360 \div 5$) around its center perfectly maps it onto its original position.

Problem 6
Sketch a rectangle similar to a 5 cm by 8 cm rectangle. Make the scale factor from the original rectangle to your sketch 2. What is the perimeter of the rectangle you sketched?
Answer:

52 cm

Explanation:

The original rectangle has dimensions 5 cm and 8 cm. With a scale factor of 2, the new side lengths are $5 \times 2 = 10 \text{ cm}$ and $8 \times 2 = 16 \text{ cm}$.
The perimeter of the new rectangle is $10 + 16 + 10 + 16 = 52 \text{ cm}$.

Problem 7
What is the area of a right triangle with legs of 5 cm and 12 cm, and a hypotenuse of 13 cm?
Answer:

30 cm²

Explanation:

The area of a right triangle is calculated using the two legs as the base and height (ignore the hypotenuse).
$$Area = \frac{1}{2} \times 5 \times 12 = \frac{1}{2} \times 60 = 30 \text{ cm}^2$$

Problem 8
Draw a ratio box for this problem. Then solve the problem using a proportion.

A library shelf contains 60 books. If 30% of the books are fiction and the rest are non-fiction, how many non-fiction books are on the shelf?
Answer:

42 non-fiction books

Explanation:

If 30% are fiction, then 70% are non-fiction (100% - 30%). Set up a proportion comparing the non-fiction percentage to the total: $$\frac{\text{Percent}}{\text{Total}} \Rightarrow \frac{70}{100} = \frac{x}{60}$$ Cross-multiplying gives: $100x = 4200$, so $x = 42$.
There are 42 non-fiction books.

Problem 9
Convert the fraction $4 \frac{1}{4}$ to a decimal (a) and a percent (b).
Answer:

a. 4.25    b. 425%

Explanation:

$\frac{1}{4}$ is equivalent to 0.25, so $4 \frac{1}{4} = 4.25$. To find the percent, multiply the decimal by 100 to get 425%.

Problem 10
Convert the decimal 3.6 to a mixed fraction (a) and a percent (b).
Answer:

a. $3 \frac{3}{5}$    b. 360%

Explanation:

3.6 is "three and six-tenths" ($3 \frac{6}{10}$), which simplifies to $3 \frac{3}{5}$ by dividing numerator and denominator by 2. Multiply 3.6 by 100 to find the percent: 360%.

Problem 11
Convert 65% to a fraction (a) and a decimal (b).
Answer:

a. $\frac{13}{20}$    b. 0.65

Explanation:

65% as a decimal is 0.65. As a fraction, it is $\frac{65}{100}$, which reduces by dividing numerator and denominator by 5 to get $\frac{13}{20}$.

Problem 12
$$8 \frac{1}{2} + 4 \frac{3}{4} + 2 \frac{1}{8}$$
Answer:

$15 \frac{3}{8}$

Explanation:

Find a common denominator for the fractions, which is 8: $$8 \frac{4}{8} + 4 \frac{6}{8} + 2 \frac{1}{8}$$ Add the whole numbers: $8 + 4 + 2 = 14$.
Add the fractions: $\frac{4}{8} + \frac{6}{8} + \frac{1}{8} = \frac{11}{8}$.
Convert $\frac{11}{8}$ to a mixed number ($1 \frac{3}{8}$) and add to 14:
Total: $15 \frac{3}{8}$.

Problem 13
$$\left(5 \frac{1}{3} \div 2\right) \div 1 \frac{1}{3}$$
Answer:

2

Explanation:

Convert mixed numbers to improper fractions: $$\left(\frac{16}{3} \div \frac{2}{1}\right) \div \frac{4}{3}$$ Follow PEMDAS, evaluate parentheses by multiplying by the reciprocal: $$\left(\frac{16}{3} \times \frac{1}{2}\right) \div \frac{4}{3} = \frac{16}{6} \div \frac{4}{3} = \frac{8}{3} \div \frac{4}{3}$$ Multiply by the reciprocal of the second fraction: $$\frac{8}{3} \times \frac{3}{4} = \frac{24}{12} = 2$$

Problem 14
$$30 - (5.5 + 4.75)$$
Answer:

19.75

Explanation:

First, add the numbers in parentheses (aligning the decimals): $5.50 + 4.75 = 10.25$.
Subtract from 30: $30.00 - 10.25 = 19.75$.

Problem 15
$$0.20x = \$40.00$$
Answer:

$x = 200$

Explanation:

Divide both sides by 0.20: $$x = \frac{40.00}{0.20} = \frac{4000}{20} = 200$$

Problem 16
Find 6.5% of $54.00 by multiplying 0.065 by $54.00. Round the product to the nearest cent.
Answer:

$3.51

Explanation:

$$0.065 \times 54.00 = 3.51$$
The product is exactly $3.51, so no additional rounding is required. It is already to the nearest cent.

Problem 17
Round the quotient to the nearest hundredth: $$0.094 \div 4$$
Answer:

0.02

Explanation:

Divide: $$0.094 \div 4 = 0.0235$$ The hundredths place is 2. The next digit is 3, which is less than 5, so keep the 2 as is. The rounded answer is 0.02.

Problem 18
Write the prime factorization of 900 using exponents.
Answer:

$2^2 \times 3^2 \times 5^2$

Explanation:

Break down 900: $900 = 9 \times 100$.
$9 = 3 \times 3 = 3^2$.
$100 = 10 \times 10 = (2 \times 5) \times (2 \times 5) = 2^2 \times 5^2$.
Combine them in numerical order: $2^2 \times 3^2 \times 5^2$.

Problem 19
"All squares are similar." True or false?
Answer:

True

Explanation:

All squares have four equal angles of 90 degrees and four equal sides. Because their corresponding angles are always identical and corresponding sides proportional, they are universally similar shapes.

Problem 20
$$5^3 - 5^2 \div 5 - 5 \times 2$$
Answer:

110

Explanation:

Follow the order of operations (PEMDAS/BODMAS):
Exponents first: $125 - 25 \div 5 - 5 \times 2$
Multiplication and Division (left to right): $125 - 5 - 10$
Addition and Subtraction (left to right): $120 - 10 = 110$.

Problem 21
What is the perimeter of this polygon? (An L-shaped polygon with top horizontal edge = 20 cm, left vertical edge = 15 cm, right vertical edge = 6 cm, and bottom-inner horizontal edge = 12 cm).
Answer:

70 cm

Explanation:

To find the perimeter, add all the outer boundaries.
Top edge = 20 cm
Left edge = 15 cm
Right edge = 6 cm
Bottom-inner horizontal edge = 12 cm
The missing bottom horizontal segment is $20 \text{ cm} - 12 \text{ cm} = 8 \text{ cm}$.
The missing inner vertical "step-down" segment is $15 \text{ cm} - 6 \text{ cm} = 9 \text{ cm}$.
$$Perimeter = 20 + 15 + 8 + 9 + 12 + 6 = 70 \text{ cm}$$

Problem 22
What is the area of the polygon from problem 21?
Answer:

192 cm²

Explanation:

Split the polygon into two rectangles.
Rectangle 1 (left side column): Width = 8 cm, Height = 15 cm. Area = $8 \times 15 = 120 \text{ cm}^2$.
Rectangle 2 (right side step): Width = 12 cm, Height = 6 cm. Area = $12 \times 6 = 72 \text{ cm}^2$.
Total Area = $120 + 72 = 192 \text{ cm}^2$.

Problem 23
Figures A and B are congruent trapezoids. Figure A is pointing upwards, while Figure B is pointing downwards and has been shifted to the right. Name the transformations that would position Figure A on Figure B.
Answer:

Reflection and Translation (or Rotation and Translation)

Explanation:

Because the figure has had its directional orientation flipped upside down, a reflection over a horizontal axis (or a 180-degree rotation) is required. To then slide it perfectly into the location of Figure B to the right, a translation is needed.

Problem 24
The perimeter of a rectangle is 60 cm. If the width of the rectangle is 12 cm, what is the length?
Answer:

18 cm

Explanation:

The perimeter formula for a rectangle is $P = 2l + 2w$.
$$60 = 2l + 2(12)$$ $$60 = 2l + 24$$ $$36 = 2l$$ $$l = 18 \text{ cm}$$

Problem 25
A circular park has a diameter of 400 m. Find the circumference of the park to the nearest ten meters.
Answer:

1260 m

Explanation:

Circumference formula is $C = \pi \times d$. Using 3.14 for $\pi$:
$$C \approx 3.14 \times 400 = 1256 \text{ m}$$
The question asks to round to the nearest ten meters. 1256 rounded to the nearest ten is 1260 m.

Problem 26
Use a ruler to draw line segment $\overline{AB}$ $7 \frac{1}{2}$ inches long. Then draw a dot at the midpoint of $AB$, and label the point $M$. How long is $\overline{AM}$?
Answer:

$3 \frac{3}{4}$ inches

Explanation:

The midpoint divides the segment exactly in half. Divide $7 \frac{1}{2}$ by 2: $$7 \frac{1}{2} = \frac{15}{2}$$ $$\frac{15}{2} \div 2 = \frac{15}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{15}{4} = 3 \frac{3}{4}$$

Problem 27
Use a compass to draw a circle on a coordinate plane. Make the center of the circle the origin, and make the radius 9 units. At which two points does the circle cross the x-axis?
Answer:

(9, 0) and (-9, 0)

Explanation:

Since the circle is centered at the origin (0,0) and has a radius of 9, it will extend 9 units straight to the right and 9 units straight to the left along the x-axis.

Problem 28
What is the area of the circle in problem 27? (Use 3.14 for $\pi$.)
Answer:

254.34 square units

Explanation:

The area formula for a circle is $A = \pi \times r^2$.
Radius $r = 9$, so $r^2 = 81$.
$$A = 3.14 \times 81 = 254.34$$

Problem 30
If John rolls a standard 6-sided die three times, what is the probability that he rolls an odd number all three times?
Answer:

$\frac{1}{8}$

Explanation:

The probability of rolling an odd number (1, 3, or 5) on a single roll is $\frac{3}{6}$, which simplifies to $\frac{1}{2}$. Since the three rolls are independent events, multiply the probabilities together:
$$\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{8}$$
