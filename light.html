What you should know

Big idea: Light is energy. We see objects when light reaches our eyes. Objects can absorb, reflect, or transmit light.

Important ideas from your study sheet

COLORHow do we see color?White light contains many colors. A prism can separate white light into a rainbow. An object looks a certain color because that color of light is reflected to your eyes while many other colors are absorbed.
MIRAGEHow does a desert mirage form?Layers of air can have different temperatures. Light bends as it passes through those layers, making an image seem to appear higher or in a different place.
SKYWhy is the sky blue?Sunlight is scattered by tiny particles and molecules in the atmosphere. Blue light is scattered strongly in many directions, so the sky often looks blue.
POLARIZATIONWhat can be polarized?Examples include sunglasses, camera filters/films, and some car windows. Polarizing filters reduce light vibrating in certain directions.
