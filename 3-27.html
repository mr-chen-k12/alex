Lesson Practice

a. Sparrows and crows perched on the wire in the ratio of 5 to 3. If the total number of sparrows and crows on the wire was 72, how many were crows?

Answer: 27 crows

Explanation: The ratio of sparrows to crows is \(5:3\). This means for every 8 birds (\(5 + 3 = 8\)), 3 are crows. To find the multiplier, divide the total number of birds by the ratio sum: \(72 \div 8 = 9\).
Multiply the crow ratio by this multiplier: \(3 \times 9 = 27\).

b. Raisins and nuts were mixed by weight in a ratio of 2 to 3. If 60 ounces of mix were prepared, how many ounces of raisins were used?

Answer: 24 ounces

Explanation: The ratio of raisins to nuts is \(2:3\), making a total of 5 ratio parts (\(2 + 3 = 5\)). To find the value of one part, divide the total weight by 5: \(60 \div 5 = 12\) ounces per part.
Since raisins make up 2 parts, multiply by 12: \(2 \times 12 = 24\) ounces.

c. Using 20 red and 20 yellow color tiles, create a ratio of 3 to 2. How many of each color do you have?

Answer: 18 red and 12 yellow

Explanation: We need to maintain a \(3:2\) ratio using the maximum possible tiles without exceeding 20 of either color. Let the multiplier be \(x\). We need \(3x \le 20\) and \(2x \le 20\). The largest integer \(x\) that satisfies \(3x \le 20\) is 6.
Using \(x = 6\): Red = \(3 \times 6 = 18\), Yellow = \(2 \times 6 = 12\).

Written Practice

1. The ratio of boys to girls in the class was 3 to 2. If there were 30 students in the class, how many girls were there?

Answer: 12 girls

Explanation: The ratio is \(3:2\) (boys to girls). Total parts = \(3 + 2 = 5\). The multiplier is \(30 \div 5 = 6\). The number of girls is \(2 \times 6 = 12\).

2. A shoe box is the shape of what geometric solid?

Answer: Rectangular prism

Explanation: A shoe box is a 3-dimensional solid with 6 rectangular faces, which is officially called a rectangular prism.

3. If the average of six numbers is 12, what is the sum of the six numbers?

Answer: 72

Explanation: The formula for average is \(\text{Average} = \frac{\text{Sum}}{\text{Count}}\). Therefore, \(\text{Sum} = \text{Average} \times \text{Count}\).
\(\text{Sum} = 12 \times 6 = 72\).

4. If the diameter of a circle is \(1\frac{1}{2}\) inches, what is the radius of the circle?

Answer: \(\frac{3}{4}\) inch (or 0.75 inches)

Explanation: Radius is half of the diameter. \(1\frac{1}{2} = 1.5\). Half of \(1.5\) is \(0.75\). As a fraction: \(\frac{3}{2} \div 2 = \frac{3}{4}\).

5. What is the cost of 2.6 pounds of meat priced at $1.65 per pound?

Answer: $4.29

Explanation: Multiply the weight by the cost per pound: \(2.6 \times \$1.65 = \$4.29\).

6. Suppose \(\overline{AC}\) is 12 cm long. If \(\overline{AB}\) is \(\frac{1}{4}\) the length of \(\overline{AC}\), then how long is \(\overline{BC}\)?

Answer: 9 cm

Explanation: First, find the length of \(\overline{AB}\): \(\frac{1}{4} \times 12 = 3\) cm.
Since point B is between A and C, \(\overline{AB} + \overline{BC} = \overline{AC}\).
\(3 + \overline{BC} = 12 \implies \overline{BC} = 12 - 3 = 9\) cm.

9. Two coins are tossed.
a. What is the probability that both coins will land heads up?
b. What is the probability that one of the coins will be heads and the other tails?

Answer:
a. \(\frac{1}{4}\) (or 25%)
b. \(\frac{1}{2}\) (or 50%)

Explanation: The possible outcomes for two coins are HH, HT, TH, TT (4 total equally likely outcomes).
a. Only HH satisfies this. Probability = \(\frac{1}{4}\).
b. HT and TH satisfy this. Probability = \(\frac{2}{4} = \frac{1}{2}\).

15. \((0.4)^2 \div 2^3\)

Answer: 0.02

Explanation: \((0.4)^2 = 0.4 \times 0.4 = 0.16\).
\(2^3 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 = 8\).
\(0.16 \div 8 = 0.02\).

16. \(x + 2\frac{1}{2} = 5\)

Answer: \(x = 2\frac{1}{2}\)

Explanation: Subtract \(2\frac{1}{2}\) from both sides: \(x = 5 - 2.5 = 2.5\).

17. \(\frac{8}{5} = \frac{40}{x}\)

Answer: \(x = 25\)

Explanation: Using cross-multiplication: \(8x = 5 \times 40\).
\(8x = 200 \implies x = 200 \div 8 = 25\).
Alternatively, note that the numerator was multiplied by 5 (\(8 \times 5 = 40\)), so multiply the denominator by 5 (\(5 \times 5 = 25\)).

18. \(0.06n = \$0.15\)

Answer: \(n = 2.5\)

Explanation: Divide both sides by 0.06: \(n = \frac{0.15}{0.06} = \frac{15}{6} = 2.5\).

19. \(6n = 21 \cdot 4\)

Answer: \(n = 14\)

Explanation: Multiply the right side: \(21 \times 4 = 84\).
Solve for n: \(6n = 84 \implies n = 84 \div 6 = 14\).

20. Nia's garage is 20 feet long, 20 feet wide, and 8 feet high.
a. How many 1-by-1-by-1-foot boxes can she fit on the floor (bottom layer) of her garage?
b. Altogether, how many boxes can Nia fit in her garage if she stacks the boxes 8 feet high?

Answer:
a. 400 boxes
b. 3,200 boxes

Explanation:
a. The floor area is \(20 \times 20 = 400\) square feet. Each box takes up 1 square foot of floor space, so 400 boxes fit on the bottom layer.
b. If she stacks them 8 feet high, there are 8 layers of 400 boxes. \(400 \times 8 = 3200\) boxes (which is equivalent to the volume: \(20 \times 20 \times 8\)).

21. ESTIMATE. If a roll of tape has a diameter of \(2\frac{1}{4}\) inches, then removing one full turn of tape yields about how many inches? Choose the closest answer.
A. \(2\frac{1}{2}\) in.    B. 5 in.    C. \(7\frac{1}{4}\) in. / 7 in.    D. \(9\frac{1}{4}\) in.

Answer: C

Explanation: One full turn corresponds to the circumference of the circle.
Circumference \(C = \pi \times d\). Using \(\pi \approx 3.14\) and \(d = 2.25\) inches:
\(C \approx 3.14 \times 2.25 \approx 7.065\) inches.
Option C is the closest estimate to 7 inches.

Explanation: Find the difference between the final temperature and the initial temperature:

27. What is the probability of rolling a perfect square with one roll of a number cube?

Answer: \(\frac{1}{3}\)

Explanation: A standard number cube has 6 faces: {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}. The perfect squares in this set are 1 (since \(1^2 = 1\)) and 4 (since \(2^2 = 4\)).
Since 2 out of the 6 outcomes are perfect squares, the probability is \(\frac{2}{6}\), which simplifies to \(\frac{1}{3}\).

Additional Problems (from First Image)

28. What is the area of a triangle with vertices located at (4, 0), (0, -3), and (0, 0)?

Answer: 6 square units

Explanation: Plotting these points creates a right triangle where the right angle is at the origin (0,0).
The base lies on the x-axis from 0 to 4 (length = 4).
The height lies on the y-axis from 0 to -3 (length = 3).
Area = \(\frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = \frac{1}{2} \times 4 \times 3 = 6\).

29. How can you convert 18 feet to yards?

Answer: Divide by 3. (6 yards)

Explanation: There are 3 feet in 1 yard. To convert from a smaller unit (feet) to a larger unit (yards), divide by the conversion factor.
\(18 \text{ feet} \div 3 = 6 \text{ yards}\).

30. If a gallon of milk costs $3.80, what is the cost per quart?

Answer: $0.95 per quart

Explanation: There are 4 quarts in a gallon. Divide the total cost by 4:
\(\$3.80 \div 4 = \$0.95\).

Real-World Connection: The surface of the Dead Sea is approximately 408 meters below sea level. Its greatest depth is 330 meters. In contrast, Mt. Everest reaches a height of 8,850 meters. What is the difference in elevation between the summit of Mt. Everest and the bottom of the Dead Sea? Show your work.

Answer: 9,588 meters

Explanation:
1. First, find the elevation of the bottom of the Dead Sea. It starts at -408m, and goes down another 330m.
\(-408 - 330 = -738\) meters.
2. To find the difference between Mt. Everest (8850m) and the bottom of the Dead Sea (-738m), subtract the lower elevation from the higher elevation:
\(8850 - (-738) = 8850 + 738 = 9588\) meters.
