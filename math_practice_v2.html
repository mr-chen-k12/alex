Written Practice - Set 2

1. Divide 650 by 15 and write the quotient
a. with a remainder.
b. as a mixed number.

Explanation:
First, perform long division: $650 \div 15$.
$15 \times 40 = 600$
$650 - 600 = 50$
$15 \times 3 = 45$
$50 - 45 = 5$ (This is the remainder).
So, the quotient is 43 with a remainder of 5.

For the mixed number, write the remainder over the divisor: $43 \frac{5}{15}$, which simplifies to $43 \frac{1}{3}$.

Answer:
a. 43 R 5
b. $43 \frac{1}{3}$

2. Six gymnasts scored 9.65, 9.7, 9.8, 9.2, 9.9, and 9.85. The lowest score was not counted. What was the sum of the five highest scores?

Explanation:
Identify the lowest score: 9.2. Exclude it from the list.
The five highest scores are: 9.65, 9.7, 9.8, 9.85, 9.9.
Add them together: $9.65 + 9.70 + 9.80 + 9.85 + 9.90 = 48.90$.

Answer: 48.9

3. Martin noticed that the five drummers made up 5% of the band. The band had how many members?

Explanation:
Let $x$ be the total number of band members.
$5\%$ of $x = 5$
$0.05 \times x = 5$
$x = 5 \div 0.05$
$x = 100$

Answer: 100 members

4. Twelve is $\frac{3}{4}$ of what number?

Explanation:
Set up an equation where $x$ is the unknown number:
$12 = \frac{3}{4}x$
To solve for $x$, multiply both sides by the reciprocal ($\frac{4}{3}$):
$x = 12 \times \frac{4}{3} = \frac{48}{3} = 16$

Answer: 16

5. Write the standard number for the following: $(2 \times 10^5) + (4 \times 10^4) + (7 \times 10^2)$

Explanation:
Expand the scientific notation:
$2 \times 10^5 = 200,000$
$4 \times 10^4 = 40,000$
$7 \times 10^2 = 700$
Notice there is no $10^3$ term, so its place value is zero.
Add them together: $200,000 + 40,000 + 700 = 240,700$.

Answer: 240,700

6. On Sarah's scale drawing, each inch represents 10 feet. One of the rooms in her drawing is $3 \frac{1}{4}$ inches long. How long is the actual room?

Explanation:
Multiply the drawing length by the scale factor:
$3 \frac{1}{4} \text{ inches} \times 10 \text{ feet/inch}$
Convert $3 \frac{1}{4}$ to an improper fraction: $\frac{13}{4}$.
$\frac{13}{4} \times 10 = \frac{130}{4} = 32.5$.

Answer: 32.5 feet

7. Two angles of a triangle measure 30° and 60°.
a. What is the measure of the third angle?
b. Make a rough sketch of the triangle.

Explanation:
a. The sum of angles in a triangle is always 180°. Subtract the two known angles:
$180^\circ - 30^\circ - 60^\circ = 90^\circ$.
b. A triangle with angles 90°, 60°, and 30° is a right scalene triangle.

Answer:
a. 90°
b. (See sketch above: a right scalene triangle)

8. Convert $12 \frac{1}{2}\%$ to a fraction.

Explanation:
First, convert the mixed number to an improper fraction: $12 \frac{1}{2} = \frac{25}{2}$.
Since it is a percent, multiply by $\frac{1}{100}$:
$\frac{25}{2} \times \frac{1}{100} = \frac{25}{200}$.
Simplify by dividing numerator and denominator by 25:
$\frac{25 \div 25}{200 \div 25} = \frac{1}{8}$.

Answer: $\frac{1}{8}$

9. Fifteen dollars is what percent of $20?

Explanation:
Create a fraction showing the part over the whole: $\frac{15}{20}$.
Simplify the fraction: $\frac{15}{20} = \frac{3}{4}$.
Convert $\frac{3}{4}$ to a percentage by multiplying by 100: $\frac{3}{4} \times 100 = 75\%$.

Answer: 75%

10. Forty percent of what number is 24?

Explanation:
Let $x$ be the unknown number. Convert 40% to a decimal (0.40) or fraction ($\frac{2}{5}$).
Using fraction: $\frac{2}{5}x = 24$
Multiply both sides by $\frac{5}{2}$:
$x = 24 \times \frac{5}{2} = \frac{120}{2} = 60$.

Answer: 60

15. $3 \frac{1}{4} - 1 \frac{5}{6}$

Convert to improper fractions:
$\frac{13}{4} - \frac{11}{6}$
Find a common denominator (12):
$\frac{13 \times 3}{4 \times 3} - \frac{11 \times 2}{6 \times 2} = \frac{39}{12} - \frac{22}{12}$

16. Alice competes in a two-event race made up of running and biking. The ratio of the length of the distance run to the length of the bike ride is 3 to 8. If the distance run was 15 kilometers, then what was the total length of the two-event race?

Explanation:
Ratio of Run to Bike = $3:8$.
Let the run distance be $3x$ and bike distance be $8x$.
We are given the run distance is 15 km, so: $3x = 15 \rightarrow x = 5$.
The bike distance is $8x = 8(5) = 40$ km.
Total length = Run + Bike = $15 \text{ km} + 40 \text{ km} = 55$ km.

Answer: 55 kilometers

17. The area of the shaded triangle is 4.5 cm². What is the area of the parallelogram?

Explanation:
A diagonal divides a parallelogram into two congruent triangles. Therefore, the total area of the parallelogram is exactly twice the area of the shaded triangle.
Area = $4.5 \text{ cm}^2 \times 2 = 9.0 \text{ cm}^2$.

Answer: 9.0 cm²

18. A storage container is 12 in. long, 4 in. wide, and 5 in. deep. What is the volume of the container?

Explanation:
The volume of a rectangular prism is calculated as $\text{Length} \times \text{Width} \times \text{Height (Depth)}$.
$V = 12 \text{ in.} \times 4 \text{ in.} \times 5 \text{ in.} = 240 \text{ cubic inches}$.

Answer: 240 in.³

19. A regular pentagon has a total of how many lines of symmetry?

Explanation:
A regular polygon with $n$ sides has $n$ lines of symmetry. A regular pentagon has 5 sides of equal length and 5 equal angles, so it has 5 lines of symmetry (each passing through a vertex and the midpoint of the opposite side).

Answer: 5

24. Which of these polygons is NOT a quadrilateral?
A. rhombus     B. hexagon     C. rectangle

Explanation:

26. The coordinates of three points that are on the same line are (-2, -3), (0, 0), and ($x$, 6). What number should replace $x$ in the third set of coordinates?

Explanation:
Find the slope ($m$) of the line using the first two points: (-2, -3) and (0, 0).
$m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} = \frac{0 - (-3)}{0 - (-2)} = \frac{3}{2}$.
The equation of the line passing through origin is $y = \frac{3}{2}x$.
Plug in the third point ($x$, 6) where $y = 6$:
$6 = \frac{3}{2}x \rightarrow x = 6 \times \frac{2}{3} = 4$.

Answer: 4

27. Lucy flipped a coin. It landed tails up. She flipped the coin a second time. It landed tails up again. If she flips the coin a third time, what is the probability that it will land tails up?

Explanation:
Coin flips are independent events. The outcome of previous flips does not change the probability of future flips. A standard coin has 1 Tails side out of 2 total sides.

Answer: $\frac{1}{2}$

28. Mark is going to flip a coin four times. What is the probability that the coin will land tails up all four times?

Explanation:
Since each flip is an independent event with a probability of $\frac{1}{2}$ for getting tails, multiply the probabilities together for the four events:
$P(\text{T, T, T, T}) = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{16}$.

Answer: $\frac{1}{16}$

29. The diameter of the circle is 12 cm.
a. What is the area of the square?
b. What is the area of the circle?

Explanation:
The diameter is 12 cm, so the radius ($r$) is half of that, which is 6 cm. The side length of the shaded square is exactly equal to the radius of the circle (6 cm).
a. Area of square = $\text{side} \times \text{side} = 6 \times 6 = 36 \text{ cm}^2$.
b. Area of circle = $\pi r^2 = \pi \times 6^2 = 36\pi \text{ cm}^2$.

Answer:
a. 36 cm²
b. $36\pi \text{ cm}^2$

30. What is the mode and the range of this set of numbers?
5, 8, 2, 5, 7, 3, 5, 9, 10, 1

Explanation:
First, put the numbers in order: 1, 2, 3, 5, 5, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10.
Mode: The number that appears most frequently. 5 appears three times, which is more than any other number.
Range: The difference between the highest and lowest numbers. Maximum is 10, Minimum is 1. $10 - 1 = 9$.

Answer:
Mode = 5
Range = 9
