Lesson Practice

a. Maria was heading North on her scooter. When she reached Elm Street, she turned $90^\circ$ to the left. Then at Maple Avenue, she turned $90^\circ$ to the left again. Assuming each street was straight, in which direction was Maria heading on Maple Avenue?
Answer: South

Explanation: Maria was initially heading North. A $90^\circ$ turn to the left means she faces West. Another $90^\circ$ turn to the left means she faces South.
b. Leo made one full turn clockwise. Sam made three full turns counterclockwise. How many degrees did Sam turn?
Answer: $1080^\circ$

Explanation: One full turn is $360^\circ$. Because Sam made three full turns, you multiply $360^\circ$ by 3. $360 \times 3 = 1080^\circ$.
c. Sarah ran four laps around the track. On each lap, she made six turns to the right and no turns to the left. What was the average number of degrees in each of Sarah's turns? Draw a picture of the problem.
Answer: $60^\circ$

Explanation: To complete one full lap, Sarah must have turned a total of $360^\circ$. If she made 6 turns per lap, the average degree per turn is $360^\circ \div 6 = 60^\circ$.

Written Practice

1. What is the mean of 3.4, 5.6, and 6.0?
Answer: 5.0

Explanation: Add the numbers together: $3.4 + 5.6 + 6.0 = 15.0$. Then divide by the count of numbers (3): $15.0 \div 3 = 5.0$.
2. The play is 150 minutes long. If it begins at 6:30 p.m., when will it be over?
Answer: 9:00 p.m.

Explanation: 150 minutes is 2 hours and 30 minutes. Adding 2 hours to 6:30 p.m. gives 8:30 p.m. Adding another 30 minutes results in 9:00 p.m.
3. Twelve of the 30 students in the choir are sopranos. What percent of the students in the choir are sopranos?
Answer: 40%

Explanation: The fraction of sopranos is $\frac{12}{30}$. Divide the top and bottom by 6 to simplify it to $\frac{2}{5}$. To convert to a percent, multiply by 100%. $\frac{2}{5} \times 100\% = 40\%$.
4. This rectangular prism has how many more edges than faces? [Image of a rectangular prism]
Answer: 6

Explanation: A rectangular prism has 12 edges and 6 faces. $12 - 6 = 6$.
5. The carpenter cut a 10-inch diameter circle from a piece of wood.
a. What was the radius of the circle?
b. What was the area of the circle? (Use $3.14$ for $\pi$.)
Answer: a. 5 inches, b. 78.5 sq inches

Explanation:
a. The radius is half the diameter. $10 \div 2 = 5$ inches.
b. Area $= \pi r^2$. $A = 3.14 \times 5^2 = 3.14 \times 25 = 78.5$ square inches.
6. Write a description of a parallelogram.
Answer & Explanation: A parallelogram is a quadrilateral with two pairs of opposite sides that are parallel and equal in length.
7. Arrange these numbers in order from least to greatest:
$$-5, 2, 0, -1, \frac{3}{4}$$
Answer: $-5, -1, 0, \frac{3}{4}, 2$

Explanation: The lowest values are the negatives furthest from zero. $-5$ is the smallest, followed by $-1$, then $0$. Finally, the positive values in increasing order are $\frac{3}{4}$, then $2$.
8. Express the unknown factor as a mixed number: $$15n = 50$$
Answer: $3\frac{1}{3}$

Explanation: Divide both sides by 15: $n = \frac{50}{15}$. Simplify by dividing the numerator and denominator by 5 to get $\frac{10}{3}$. As a mixed number, 3 goes into 10 three times with a remainder of 1, so $3\frac{1}{3}$.
Refer to the triangle to answer questions 9-11. 9. What is the area of this triangle?
Answer: $54\text{ cm}^2$

Explanation: Area $= \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}$. The base is 12 cm and the height is 9 cm. $A = \frac{1}{2} \times 12 \times 9 = 6 \times 9 = 54\text{ cm}^2$.
10. What is the perimeter of this triangle?
Answer: $36\text{ cm}$

Explanation: The perimeter is the sum of all sides. $9 + 12 + 15 = 36\text{ cm}$.
11. What is the ratio of the length of the shortest side to the length of the longest side? Express the ratio as a fraction and as a decimal.
Answer: Fraction: $\frac{3}{5}$, Decimal: $0.6$

Explanation: The shortest side is 9 and the longest side is 15. The ratio is $\frac{9}{15}$. Dividing numerator and denominator by 3 simplifies it to $\frac{3}{5}$. As a decimal, $3 \div 5 = 0.6$.
12. Write $8.75$ as a mixed number. Then subtract $\frac{3}{8}$ from the mixed number. What is the difference?
Answer: $8\frac{3}{8}$

Explanation: $8.75$ is equal to $8\frac{3}{4}$. To subtract $\frac{3}{8}$, find a common denominator: $8\frac{3}{4} = 8\frac{6}{8}$. Now subtract: $8\frac{6}{8} - \frac{3}{8} = 8\frac{3}{8}$.
13. Jake was facing West. Then he turned to his right $180^\circ$. What direction was he facing after he turned?
Answer: East

Explanation: A $180^\circ$ turn means turning to face the exact opposite direction. The opposite of West is East.
14. Write $45\%$ as a reduced fraction.
Answer: $\frac{9}{20}$

Explanation: $45\% = \frac{45}{100}$. Divide the numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor, 5: $\frac{45 \div 5}{100 \div 5} = \frac{9}{20}$.
15. Analyze: $$\frac{n}{15} = \frac{16}{24}$$
Answer: $n = 10$

Explanation: Simplify $\frac{16}{24}$ to $\frac{2}{3}$. So, $\frac{n}{15} = \frac{2}{3}$. Cross-multiply: $3n = 30$. Divide by 3: $n = 10$.
16. $0.85 \div 10$
Answer: $0.085$

Explanation: Dividing a decimal by 10 moves the decimal point one place to the left.
17. $\frac{36}{0.4}$
Answer: $90$

Explanation: Multiply the numerator and denominator by 10 to remove the decimal: $\frac{360}{4}$. Divide 360 by 4: $360 \div 4 = 90$.
18. $4\frac{1}{6} + 2\frac{2}{3}$
Answer: $6\frac{5}{6}$

Explanation: Find a common denominator: $2\frac{2}{3} = 2\frac{4}{6}$. Add the fractions: $\frac{1}{6} + \frac{4}{6} = \frac{5}{6}$. Add the whole numbers: $4 + 2 = 6$. Combine them: $6\frac{5}{6}$.
19. $7\frac{1}{5} - 2\frac{3}{5}$
Answer: $4\frac{3}{5}$

Explanation: You cannot subtract 3 from 1, so borrow from the whole number 7: $7\frac{1}{5} = 6\frac{6}{5}$. Now subtract: $6\frac{6}{5} - 2\frac{3}{5} = 4\frac{3}{5}$.
20. $5\frac{1}{4} \times \frac{2}{7} \times 3$
Answer: $4\frac{1}{2}$

Explanation: Convert the mixed number to an improper fraction: $\frac{21}{4}$. The expression is $\frac{21}{4} \times \frac{2}{7} \times \frac{3}{1}$. Cancel the 7 from 21 (leaving 3) and the 2 from 4 (leaving 2). The equation becomes $\frac{3}{2} \times \frac{1}{1} \times \frac{3}{1} = \frac{9}{2}$. Convert to a mixed number: $4\frac{1}{2}$.
21. One fourth of the three dozen apples in the basket were green. How many apples were not green?
Answer: 27 apples

Explanation: Three dozen equals 36. One fourth of 36 is $36 \div 4 = 9$. If 9 apples were green, then $36 - 9 = 27$ apples were not green.
22. Weights totaling 45 grams were placed on the left side of this scale, while weights totaling 25 grams were placed on the right side. How many grams of weights should be moved from the left side to the right side to balance the scale? (Hint: Find the average of the weights on the two sides.)
Answer: 10 grams

Explanation: The total weight is $45 + 25 = 70$ grams. To balance the scale, each side must have the average weight: $70 \div 2 = 35$ grams. Currently, the left side has 45 grams. To get down to 35 grams, $45 - 35 = 10$ grams must be moved.
23. The cube at right is made up of smaller cubes that each have a volume of 1 cubic inch. What is the total volume of the larger cube?
Answer: $64\text{ in}^3$

Explanation: The larger cube is made of a $4 \times 4 \times 4$ grid of smaller cubes. Since each small cube is $1\text{ in}^3$, the total volume is $4 \times 4 \times 4 = 64$ cubic inches.
24. Round sixty-five hundredths to the nearest tenth.
Answer: $0.7$

Explanation: Sixty-five hundredths is written as $0.65$. Look at the hundredths digit (5), which is 5 or greater, so round up the tenths digit from 6 to 7. The answer is $0.7$.
25. $\sqrt{81} - \sqrt{49}$
Answer: $2$

Explanation: The square root of 81 is 9, and the square root of 49 is 7. Substitute the values: $9 - 7 = 2$.
26. The ratio of cars to trucks in the parking lot is 8 to 3. What is the ratio of trucks to cars?
Answer: 3 to 8

Explanation: Simply reverse the terms of the ratio. If cars to trucks is 8 to 3, trucks to cars is 3 to 8.
27. $5 + 5 \times 5 - 5 \div 5$
Answer: $29$

Explanation: Following the order of operations, perform multiplication and division first.
$5 \times 5 = 25$
$5 \div 5 = 1$
The equation becomes: $5 + 25 - 1 = 30 - 1 = 29$.
28. The Hernandez family drinks a gallon of milk every four days. There are two people in the family. Each person drinks an average of how many pints of milk per day? Explain your thinking.
Answer: 1 pint per day per person

Explanation:
1. Convert gallons to pints: 1 gallon = 4 quarts = 8 pints.
2. Calculate the family's daily consumption: 8 pints every 4 days means $8 \div 4 = 2$ pints per day for the whole family.
3. Calculate individual consumption: 2 pints divided by 2 people equals 1 pint per person per day.
29. Simplify:
a. $15\text{ cm} + 40\text{ mm}$ (Write the answer in millimeters.)
b. $400\text{ pencils} \div 20\text{ students}$
Answer: a. $190\text{ mm}$, b. $20\text{ pencils per student}$

Explanation:
a. Convert cm to mm: $15\text{ cm} \times 10 = 150\text{ mm}$. Add the values: $150\text{ mm} + 40\text{ mm} = 190\text{ mm}$.
b. Divide the numbers and keep the units: $400 \div 20 = 20$. The unit becomes pencils/student.
30. On a coordinate plane draw a segment from point $A(-2, 3)$ to point $B(6, 3)$. What are the coordinates of the point that is halfway between points $A$ and $B$?
Answer: $(2, 3)$

Explanation: Both points share the same y-coordinate ($3$), meaning the segment is perfectly horizontal. To find the midpoint's x-coordinate, find the average of the x-coordinates: $\frac{-2 + 6}{2} = \frac{4}{2} = 2$. So the halfway point is $(2, 3)$.

Real-World Connection

The students in Mr. Davis's culinary class will make pancakes using the recipe below. If 40 students each work with a partner to make one batch of pancakes, how much of each ingredient will Mr. Davis need to buy? Hint: Keep each item in the unit measure given.

  • $2\frac{1}{2}$ cups of all-purpose flour
  • $2$ large eggs
  • $\frac{1}{2}$ cup of sugar
  • $1\frac{3}{4}$ cups of milk
Answer:
  • Flour: $50$ cups
  • Eggs: $40$ large eggs
  • Sugar: $10$ cups
  • Milk: $35$ cups

Explanation: If 40 students work in pairs, there are $40 \div 2 = 20$ pairs. This means 20 batches of pancakes are being made. Multiply each recipe ingredient by 20:

- Flour: $2\frac{1}{2} \times 20 = \frac{5}{2} \times 20 = 5 \times 10 = 50$ cups.
- Eggs: $2 \times 20 = 40$ large eggs.
- Sugar: $\frac{1}{2} \times 20 = 10$ cups.
- Milk: $1\frac{3}{4} \times 20 = \frac{7}{4} \times 20 = 7 \times 5 = 35$ cups.
