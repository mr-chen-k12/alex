Saxon Math Course 2 - Lesson 17 Solutions

Lesson 17: Protractor Practice

g. REPRESENT: Use your protractor to draw an angle of $45^\circ$.
Explanation: Align the midpoint of the protractor baseline with the vertex of the angle, line up one side along $0^\circ$, and mark a point at $45^\circ$. Draw a ray from the vertex through the mark to form an acute angle measuring $45^\circ$.
Answer: An acute angle measuring $45^\circ$.
h. REPRESENT: Use your protractor to draw an angle of $120^\circ$.
Explanation: Align the center of the protractor with the vertex, align one ray along $0^\circ$, and mark $120^\circ$ on the scale. Draw a ray from the vertex through the mark to form an obtuse angle.
Answer: An obtuse angle measuring $120^\circ$.
i. REPRESENT: Use your protractor to draw an angle of $100^\circ$.
Explanation: Align the baseline of the protractor, locate $100^\circ$ on the outer/inner scale starting from $0^\circ$, mark the point, and draw the second ray.
Answer: An obtuse angle measuring $100^\circ$.
j. REPRESENT: Use your protractor to draw an angle of $80^\circ$.
Explanation: Place the protractor center on the vertex, measure $80^\circ$ from the $0^\circ$ line, mark the point, and connect it to the vertex.
Answer: An acute angle measuring $80^\circ$.
k. ANALYZE: Perry's protractor is marked at each degree. Assuming the protractor is used correctly, what is the greatest possible error that can be made with Perry's protractor? Express your answer as a fraction of a degree.
Explanation: When a measuring instrument is calibrated in increments of $1^\circ$, any reading is rounded to the nearest marked unit. The maximum measurement error is half of the smallest unit mark: $\frac{1}{2} \cdot 1^\circ = \frac{1}{2}^\circ$.
Answer: $\frac{1}{2}^\circ$

Written Practice

1. Two thousand, four hundred twenty people gathered before noon for the opening of a new park. An additional five thousand, ninety people arrived after noon. How many people were at the opening of the new park?
Explanation: Add the two quantities together:
$$2,420 + 5,090 = 7,510$$
Answer: 7,510 people
2. A number cube is rolled once.
a. What is the probability of rolling a 5?
b. What is the probability of not rolling a 5?
a. Explanation: A standard number cube has 6 equally likely outcomes $\{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}$. There is 1 favorable outcome (rolling a 5).
$$P(\text{rolling a } 5) = \frac{1}{6}$$
b. Explanation: The outcomes that are not 5 are $\{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}$, which is 5 outcomes out of 6.
$$P(\text{not rolling a } 5) = 1 - \frac{1}{6} = \frac{5}{6}$$
Answer: a. $\frac{1}{6}$  |  b. $\frac{5}{6}$
3. There are 210 students in the first-year physical education class. If they are equally divided into 15 squads, how many students will be in each squad?
Explanation: Divide the total number of students by the number of squads:
$$210 \div 15 = 14$$
Answer: 14 students
4. Columbus set sail for the New World in 1492. The Pilgrims set sail for the New World in 1620. How many years are there between the two dates?
Explanation: Subtract the earlier year from the later year:
$$1620 - 1492 = 128$$
Answer: 128 years
5. Which of the following does not equal $1\frac{1}{3}$?
A. $\frac{4}{3}$    B. $1\frac{2}{6}$    C. $\frac{5}{3}$    D. $1\frac{4}{12}$
Explanation: Convert $1\frac{1}{3}$ and compare each option:
• $1\frac{1}{3} = \frac{4}{3}$ (matches A)
• $1\frac{2}{6} = 1\frac{1}{3}$ (matches B)
• $\frac{5}{3} = 1\frac{2}{3} \neq 1\frac{1}{3}$ (does NOT match C)
• $1\frac{4}{12} = 1\frac{1}{3}$ (matches D)
Answer: C. $\frac{5}{3}$
6. ANALYZE: Refer to the figure to answer a–c:
a. Which line is parallel to $\overleftrightarrow{ST}$?
b. Which line is perpendicular to $\overleftrightarrow{ST}$?
c. Angle $QRT$ measures how many degrees?
a. Explanation: $\overleftrightarrow{QR}$ and $\overleftrightarrow{ST}$ are both perpendicular to line segment $RT$, so $\overleftrightarrow{QR} \parallel \overleftrightarrow{ST}$.
b. Explanation: Line $\overleftrightarrow{RT}$ intersects $\overleftrightarrow{ST}$ at a right angle ($90^\circ$), so $\overleftrightarrow{RT} \perp \overleftrightarrow{ST}$.
c. Explanation: Since $\overleftrightarrow{QR}$ is perpendicular to $\overleftrightarrow{RT}$, $\angle QRT$ is a right angle.
Answer: a. Line $QR$ ($\overleftrightarrow{QR}$)  |  b. Line $RT$ ($\overleftrightarrow{RT}$)  |  c. $90^\circ$
7. Write each number as a reduced fraction or mixed number:
a. $\frac{12}{16}$    b. $3\frac{12}{18}$    c. $25\%$
a. Divide numerator and denominator by $\text{GCF}(12, 16) = 4$: $\frac{12 \div 4}{16 \div 4} = \frac{3}{4}$
b. Simplify the fractional part $\frac{12}{18}$ by dividing by $\text{GCF}(12, 18) = 6$: $3\frac{12 \div 6}{18 \div 6} = 3\frac{2}{3}$
c. Convert percentage to fraction and simplify: $25\% = \frac{25}{100} = \frac{1}{4}$
Answer: a. $\frac{3}{4}$  |  b. $3\frac{2}{3}$  |  c. $\frac{1}{4}$
8. GENERALIZE: At the grocery store Len put five apples in a bag and weighed them. The scale showed the weight to be $2\text{ lb } 8\text{ oz}$. What is this weight in ounces?
Explanation: Since $1\text{ pound} = 16\text{ ounces}$:
$$2\text{ lb} = 2 \times 16\text{ oz} = 32\text{ oz}$$
$$32\text{ oz} + 8\text{ oz} = 40\text{ oz}$$
Answer: $40\text{ oz}$
9. Complete each equivalent fraction:
a. $\frac{2}{9} = \frac{?}{18}$    b. $\frac{1}{3} = \frac{?}{18}$    c. $\frac{5}{6} = \frac{?}{18}$
a. Multiply numerator and denominator by $2$: $\frac{2 \times 2}{9 \times 2} = \frac{4}{18}$
b. Multiply numerator and denominator by $6$: $\frac{1 \times 6}{3 \times 6} = \frac{6}{18}$
c. Multiply numerator and denominator by $3$: $\frac{5 \times 3}{6 \times 3} = \frac{15}{18}$
Answer: a. 4  |  b. 6  |  c. 15
10. REPRESENT: Use a protractor to draw a $30^\circ$ angle.
Explanation: Draw a straight reference ray. Place the protractor center at the ray's origin, align the zero line, locate $30^\circ$, make a mark, and connect the vertex to the mark.
Answer: An acute angle measuring $30^\circ$.

Factors of 50: $\{1, 2, 5, 10, 25, 50\}$

12. REPRESENT: Draw $\overline{RS}$ $1\frac{3}{4}\text{ in.}$ long. Then draw $\overline{ST}$ perpendicular to $\overline{RS}$.
Explanation: Use a ruler to draw a segment $\overline{RS}$ measuring $1.75\text{ inches}$. At endpoint $S$, use a protractor or square angle to draw segment $\overline{ST}$ extending perpendicular ($90^\circ$) to $\overline{RS}$.

13. If $x = 4$ and $y = 8$, find:
a. $\frac{y}{x} - \frac{x}{y}$
b. $x - \frac{x}{y}$
a. Substitute $x=4$ and $y=8$:
$$\frac{8}{4} - \frac{4}{8} = 2 - \frac{1}{2} = 1\frac{1}{2}$$
b. Substitute $x=4$ and $y=8$:
$$4 - \frac{4}{8} = 4 - \frac{1}{2} = 3\frac{1}{2}$$
Answer: a. $1\frac{1}{2}$  |  b. $3\frac{1}{2}$

Solving for $x$:

Check: $372 - 231 = 141$ $\checkmark$

15. Find the value of $y$ and check your work: $\$6.30 + y = \$25$

Check: $\$6.30 + \$18.70 = \$25.00$ $\checkmark$

Check: $8 \times \$3.75 = \$30.00$ $\checkmark$

17. Find the value of $m$ and check your work: $\frac{100\%}{m} = 20\%$
Solving for $m$:
$$m = \frac{100\%}{20\%} = 5$$
Check: $\frac{100\%}{5} = 20\%$ $\checkmark$
Answer: $m = 5$
18. Simplify: $3\frac{5}{6} - 1\frac{1}{6}$
Explanation: Subtract the whole numbers and the fractions separately:
$$(3 - 1) + \left(\frac{5}{6} - \frac{1}{6}\right) = 2 + \frac{4}{6} = 2\frac{2}{3}$$
Answer: $2\frac{2}{3}$
19. Simplify: $\frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{2}{3}$
Explanation: Multiply numerators and denominators, or cancel out the common factor of $2$:
$$\frac{1 \times 2}{2 \times 3} = \frac{2}{6} = \frac{1}{3}$$
Answer: $\frac{1}{3}$
20. Simplify: $\frac{\$100.00}{40}$
Explanation: Divide $100$ by $40$:
$$100 \div 40 = 2.5 \implies \$2.50$$
Answer: $\$2.50$
21. Simplify: $55 \cdot 55$
Explanation: Perform the multiplication:
$$55 \times 55 = 3025$$
Answer: 3025
22. Simplify: $2(8\text{ in.} + 6\text{ in.})$
Explanation: Add inside parentheses first:
$$8\text{ in.} + 6\text{ in.} = 14\text{ in.}$$
$$2 \times 14\text{ in.} = 28\text{ in.}$$
Answer: $28\text{ in.}$
23. Simplify: $\frac{3}{4}\text{ in.} + \frac{3}{4}\text{ in.}$
Explanation: Add numerators:
$$\frac{3+3}{4} = \frac{6}{4}\text{ in.} = \frac{3}{2}\text{ in.} = 1\frac{1}{2}\text{ in.}$$
Answer: $1\frac{1}{2}\text{ in.}$
24. Simplify: $\frac{15}{16}\text{ in.} - \frac{3}{16}\text{ in.}$
Explanation: Subtract numerators and simplify:
$$\frac{15 - 3}{16} = \frac{12}{16}\text{ in.} = \frac{3}{4}\text{ in.}$$
Answer: $\frac{3}{4}\text{ in.}$
25. Simplify: $\frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{4}{3} \cdot \frac{9}{2}$
Explanation: Multiply numerators together and denominators together:
$$\text{Numerator: } 1 \times 4 \times 9 = 36$$
$$\text{Denominator: } 2 \times 3 \times 2 = 12$$
$$\frac{36}{12} = 3$$
Answer: 3
26. ANALYZE: The cost of the meal was $\$15.17$. Loretta gave the cashier a $\$20$ bill and a quarter. Name the fewest possible number of bills and coins she could have received in change.
Explanation:
• Total given: $\$20.00 + \$0.25 = \$20.25$
• Total change due: $\$20.25 - \$15.17 = \$5.08$
• Fewest bills and coins for $\$5.08$:
  - Bills: One $\$5$ bill ($1$ bill)
  - Coins: One nickel ($5\text{¢}$) and three pennies ($3\text{¢}$) ($4$ coins)
Answer: 5 total (One $\$5$ bill, 1 nickel, and 3 pennies)
27.
a. Compare: $\left(\frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{3}{4}\right) \cdot \frac{2}{3} \bigcirc \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{3}{4} \cdot \frac{2}{3}\right)$
b. What property is illustrated by the comparison?
a. Calculation:
$$\text{Left side: } \left(\frac{3}{8}\right) \cdot \frac{2}{3} = \frac{6}{24} = \frac{1}{4}$$
$$\text{Right side: } \frac{1}{2} \cdot \left(\frac{6}{12}\right) = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{4}$$
Since both sides equal $\frac{1}{4}$, the comparison sign is $=$.
b. Property: The grouping of factors changes, but the product remains the same.
Answer: a. $=$  |  b. Associative Property of Multiplication

29. Write $3\frac{3}{4}$ as an improper fraction. Then write its reciprocal.
Improper Fraction:
$$3\frac{3}{4} = \frac{(3 \times 4) + 3}{4} = \frac{15}{4}$$
Reciprocal: Invert the fraction $\frac{15}{4} \rightarrow \frac{4}{15}$
Answer: Improper fraction: $\frac{15}{4}$  |  Reciprocal: $\frac{4}{15}$
30. Find a fraction equal to $\frac{3}{4}$ with a denominator of 8. Add the fraction to $\frac{5}{8}$. Write the sum as a mixed number.
Step 1: Convert $\frac{3}{4}$ to an equivalent fraction with denominator 8:
$$\frac{3 \times 2}{4 \times 2} = \frac{6}{8}$$
Step 2: Add $\frac{6}{8}$ to $\frac{5}{8}$:
$$\frac{6}{8} + \frac{5}{8} = \frac{11}{8}$$
Step 3: Convert to mixed number:
$$\frac{11}{8} = 1\frac{3}{8}$$
Answer: Equivalent fraction: $\frac{6}{8}$  |  Sum: $1\frac{3}{8}$
