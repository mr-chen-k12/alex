1.(28)
EXPLAIN During the first six months of the year, the Miller family’s monthly gas bills were $145.50, $132.25, $118.75, $95.50, $78.00, and $60.00. How can the Millers find their average monthly gas bill for the first six months of the year?
Answer
\(\text{Add the six bills and divide by }6\text{ — the average is }\$105.00\)
Solution
To find the average (mean), add all six monthly bills, then divide by 6: \(145.50 + 132.25 + 118.75 + 95.50 + 78.00 + 60.00 = \$630.00\). Then divide by 6: \(630.00 \div 6 = \$105.00\).
2.(23, 30)
There were \(4\frac{1}{3}\) gallons of lemonade in a dispenser before a school event. Afterward, there were \(1\frac{3}{4}\) gallons remaining. How many gallons of lemonade were consumed during the event?
Answer
\(2\frac{7}{12}\text{ gallons}\)
Solution
Subtract the remaining amount from the initial amount: \(4\frac{1}{3} - 1\frac{3}{4}\). Use the common denominator 12: \(4\frac{4}{12} - 1\frac{9}{12}\). Rename \(4\frac{4}{12}\) by borrowing 1: \(3\frac{16}{12} - 1\frac{9}{12} = 2\frac{7}{12}\) gallons.
3.(28)
A one-year subscription to a monthly magazine costs $22.80. The regular newsstand price is $2.50 per issue. How much is saved per issue by paying the subscription price?
Answer
\(\$0.60\text{ per issue}\)
Solution
A monthly magazine has 12 issues per year, so the subscription cost per issue is \(22.80 \div 12 = \$1.90\). The savings per issue is \(2.50 - 1.90 = \$0.60\).

5.(19)
The perimeter of a square equals the perimeter of a regular octagon. Each side of the octagon is 6 cm long. How long is each side of the square?
Answer
\(12\text{ cm}\)
Solution
The regular octagon has 8 equal sides, so its perimeter is \(8 \times 6 = 48\) cm. Since the square has 4 equal sides and the same perimeter: \(48 \div 4 = 12\) cm per side.

JUSTIFY Only \(\frac{3}{16}\) of the 144 students in a middle school band play brass instruments.
a. How many students play brass instruments?
b. How many students do not play brass instruments?
Answer
\(\text{a. }27 \qquad \text{b. }117\)
Solution
a. \(\frac{1}{16}\) of 144 is \(144 \div 16 = 9\). So \(\frac{3}{16}\) is \(3 \times 9 = 27\) students. b. The remaining students are \(144 - 27 = 117\) students.

30.(17)
Use a protractor to draw a triangle that has two 50° angles. What type of triangle is formed?
Answer
\(\text{An isosceles triangle (}50^\circ, 50^\circ, 80^\circ\text{)}\)
Solution
The sum of interior angles in a triangle is 180°. If two angles are 50°, the third angle must be \(180^\circ - 50^\circ - 50^\circ = 80^\circ\). Since two angles (and two sides) are equal, it forms an isosceles triangle.

34.(Extension)
PERCENTAGES & TIPS Carlos and his family had dinner at a restaurant, and the bill came to $85.00.
a. If they want to leave a 20% tip for their server, how much should the tip be?
b. What is the total cost of the dinner including the tip?
Answer
\(\text{a. }\$17.00 \qquad \text{b. }\$102.00\)
Solution
a. Convert 20% to a decimal (\(0.20\)) and multiply by the total bill: \(0.20 \times 85.00 = \$17.00\). b. Add the tip to the initial bill: \(85.00 + 17.00 = \$102.00\).
35.(Extension)
ORDER OF OPERATIONS Simplify using order of operations (PEMDAS):
$$\left( -\frac{1}{2} + 1.5 \right)^3 \cdot (-3)^2 - 5.4 \div (-0.6)$$
Answer
\(18\)
Solution
Step-by-step simplification:
1. Parentheses: Convert fraction to decimal: \(-\frac{1}{2} = -0.5\). Then \(-0.5 + 1.5 = 1.0\).
2. Exponents: \((1.0)^3 = 1\) and \((-3)^2 = 9\).
3. Multiplication: \(1 \cdot 9 = 9\).
4. Division: \(5.4 \div (-0.6) = -9\).
5. Subtraction: \(9 - (-9) = 9 + 9 = 18\).
