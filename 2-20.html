Math Practice: 30 Multiple Choice Questions

Topic 1: Adding Mixed Numbers

1. Simplify: \(3\frac{1}{4} + 1\frac{1}{2}\)
Explanation: Find a common denominator for the fractions (which is 4). \(1\frac{1}{2} = 1\frac{2}{4}\). Add whole numbers: \(3 + 1 = 4\). Add fractions: \(\frac{1}{4} + \frac{2}{4} = \frac{3}{4}\). Result: \(4\frac{3}{4}\).
2. Simplify: \(2\frac{1}{3} + 4\frac{1}{6}\)
Explanation: Common denominator is 6. \(2\frac{1}{3} = 2\frac{2}{6}\). Add: \(2\frac{2}{6} + 4\frac{1}{6} = 6\frac{3}{6}\). Simplify the fraction: \(\frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}\). Result: \(6\frac{1}{2}\).
3. Simplify: \(5\frac{2}{5} + 1\frac{3}{10}\)
Explanation: Common denominator is 10. \(5\frac{2}{5} = 5\frac{4}{10}\). Add: \(5\frac{4}{10} + 1\frac{3}{10} = 6\frac{7}{10}\).

Topic 2: Unit Price

4. If 6 pens cost $4.80, how much do 4 pens cost?
Explanation: Find the price for one pen: \($4.80 \div 6 = $0.80\). Multiply by 4 pens: \($0.80 \times 4 = $3.20\).
5. If 5 notebooks cost $7.50, how much do 3 notebooks cost?
Explanation: Find the price for one notebook: \($7.50 \div 5 = $1.50\). Multiply by 3 notebooks: \($1.50 \times 3 = $4.50\).
6. If 4 folders cost $2.40, how much do 7 folders cost?
Explanation: Find the price for one folder: \($2.40 \div 4 = $0.60\). Multiply by 7 folders: \($0.60 \times 7 = $4.20\).

Topic 3: Solving Basic Equations

7. Solve for x: \(\frac{x}{15} = \frac{2}{5}\)
Explanation: Since \(5 \times 3 = 15\), multiply the numerator by 3 as well: \(2 \times 3 = 6\). Therefore, \(x = 6\).
8. Solve for x: \(\frac{x}{20} = \frac{3}{4}\)
Explanation: Since \(4 \times 5 = 20\), multiply the numerator by 5 as well: \(3 \times 5 = 15\). Therefore, \(x = 15\).
9. Solve for x: \(\frac{x}{18} = \frac{5}{6}\)
Explanation: Since \(6 \times 3 = 18\), multiply the numerator by 3 as well: \(5 \times 3 = 15\). Therefore, \(x = 15\).

Topic 4: Perimeter of Regular Polygons

10. What is the perimeter of a regular octagon with a side length of 5 cm?
Explanation: A regular octagon has 8 sides of equal length. Multiply the side length by 8: \(8 \times 5 = 40\text{ cm}\).
11. What is the perimeter of a regular pentagon with a side length of 9 m?
Explanation: A regular pentagon has 5 sides of equal length. Multiply the side length by 5: \(5 \times 9 = 45\text{ m}\).
12. What is the perimeter of a regular hexagon with a side length of 12 in?
Explanation: A regular hexagon has 6 sides of equal length. Multiply the side length by 6: \(6 \times 12 = 72\text{ in}\).

Topic 5: Circumference of a Circle (\(C = 2\pi r\))

13. What is the circumference of a circle with a radius of 10 m? (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\))
Explanation: \(C = 2 \times 3.14 \times 10\). Multiply 2 and 10 to get 20, then \(20 \times 3.14 = 62.8\text{ m}\).
14. What is the circumference of a circle with a radius of 5 cm? (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\))
Explanation: \(C = 2 \times 3.14 \times 5\). Multiply 2 and 5 to get 10, then \(10 \times 3.14 = 31.4\text{ cm}\).
15. What is the circumference of a circle with a radius of 20 in? (Use 3.14 for \(\pi\))
Explanation: \(C = 2 \times 3.14 \times 20\). Multiply 2 and 20 to get 40, then \(40 \times 3.14 = 125.6\text{ in}\).

Topic 6: Supplementary Angles

16. What is the supplementary angle to 65°?
Explanation: Supplementary angles add up to 180°. Subtract: \(180^\circ - 65^\circ = 115^\circ\).
17. What is the supplementary angle to 105°?
Explanation: Supplementary angles add up to 180°. Subtract: \(180^\circ - 105^\circ = 75^\circ\).
18. What is the supplementary angle to 38°?
Explanation: Supplementary angles add up to 180°. Subtract: \(180^\circ - 38^\circ = 142^\circ\).

Topic 7: Metric Conversions

19. Convert 4.7 kilometers to meters.
Explanation: There are 1000 meters in a kilometer. Multiply by 1000 (move the decimal point 3 places to the right): \(4.7 \times 1000 = 4700\).
20. Convert 0.85 kilometers to meters.
Explanation: Multiply by 1000: \(0.85 \times 1000 = 850\).
21. Convert 12.3 kilometers to meters.
Explanation: Multiply by 1000: \(12.3 \times 1000 = 12300\).

Topic 8: Operations with Negative Numbers

22. Simplify: \(-8 + (-5) - (-10)\)
Explanation: Simplify the signs first: \(-8 - 5 + 10\). Work left to right: \(-8 - 5 = -13\). Then, \(-13 + 10 = -3\).
23. Simplify: \(-15 - (-6) + (-4)\)
Explanation: Simplify the signs first: \(-15 + 6 - 4\). Work left to right: \(-15 + 6 = -9\). Then, \(-9 - 4 = -13\).
24. Simplify: \(12 - (-7) + (-20)\)
Explanation: Simplify the signs first: \(12 + 7 - 20\). Work left to right: \(12 + 7 = 19\). Then, \(19 - 20 = -1\).

Topic 9: Greatest Common Factor (GCF)

25. What is the Greatest Common Factor (GCF) of 24 and 36?
Explanation: Factors of 24: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24. Factors of 36: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 36. The largest number they share is 12.
26. What is the Greatest Common Factor (GCF) of 18 and 45?
Explanation: Factors of 18: 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18. Factors of 45: 1, 3, 5, 9, 15, 45. The largest number they share is 9.
27. What is the Greatest Common Factor (GCF) of 32 and 48?
Explanation: Factors of 32: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32. Factors of 48: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24, 48. The largest number they share is 16.

Topic 10: Mean (Average)

28. What is the mean of the following set of numbers: 4, 7, 9, 12?
Explanation: Add the numbers: \(4 + 7 + 9 + 12 = 32\). Divide by the total count (4): \(32 \div 4 = 8\).
29. What is the mean of the following set of numbers: 10, 15, 15, 20, 25?
Explanation: Add the numbers: \(10 + 15 + 15 + 20 + 25 = 85\). Divide by the total count (5): \(85 \div 5 = 17\).
30. What is the mean of the following set of numbers: 3, 6, 8, 10, 13?
Explanation: Add the numbers: \(3 + 6 + 8 + 10 + 13 = 40\). Divide by the total count (5): \(40 \div 5 = 8\).
