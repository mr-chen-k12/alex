Practice Problems — 8-24

Practice Problems

1. Find the difference between the sum and the product of 0.8 and 0.4.
Sum: $0.8 + 0.4 = 1.2$
Product: $0.8 \times 0.4 = 0.32$
Difference: $1.2 - 0.32$
Answer: 0.88
2. Find the difference in elevation between Mount Denali (20,310 ft above sea level) and Death Valley (282 ft below sea level).
$20,310 - (-282) = 20,310 + 282$
Answer: 20,592 ft
3. The temperature dropped from 42°F by 29°F. What is the final temperature?
$42 - 29 = 13$
Answer: 13°F
4. Write $7\frac{4}{5}$ as an improper fraction.
$\frac{7 \times 5 + 4}{5} = \frac{39}{5}$
Answer: $\frac{39}{5}$
5. Round $846.75 to the nearest ten dollars.
The tens digit is 4, followed by 6. Round up.
Answer: $850
6. What time is $3\frac{1}{2}$ hours after 2:35 p.m.?
2:35 p.m. + 3 hours = 5:35 p.m.
5:35 p.m. + 30 mins = 6:05 p.m.
Answer: 6:05 p.m.
7. Evaluate: $(54 \times 18) \div (54 - 18)$
$54 \times 18 = 972$
$54 - 18 = 36$
$972 \div 36 = 27$
Answer: 27
8. Compare: $\frac{7}{12} \ \bigcirc \ \frac{5}{8}$
Common denominator is 24:
$\frac{7}{12} = \frac{14}{24}$ and $\frac{5}{8} = \frac{15}{24}$
$\frac{14}{24} < \frac{15}{24}$
Answer: <
9. Solve for $k$: $k - 3\frac{2}{5} = 4\frac{1}{10}$
$k = 4\frac{1}{10} + 3\frac{2}{5} = 4\frac{1}{10} + 3\frac{4}{10}$
$k = 7\frac{5}{10} = 7\frac{1}{2}$
Answer: $7\frac{1}{2}$
10. Compare: $\frac{5}{9} \ \bigcirc \ \frac{4}{7}$
Cross-multiply or find common denominator (63):
$\frac{5}{9} = \frac{35}{63}$ and $\frac{4}{7} = \frac{36}{63}$
Answer: <
11. Evaluate: $8\frac{1}{4} - 3\frac{5}{6}$
Common denominator (12):
$8\frac{3}{12} - 3\frac{10}{12} = 7\frac{15}{12} - 3\frac{10}{12} = 4\frac{5}{12}$
Answer: $4\frac{5}{12}$
12. Evaluate: $\frac{5}{12} \times \frac{4}{15}$
$\frac{5 \times 4}{12 \times 15} = \frac{20}{180} = \frac{1}{9}$
Answer: $\frac{1}{9}$
13. Find the area of a triangle with base $14\text{ cm}$ and height $9\text{ cm}$.
$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}$
$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times 14 \times 9 = 7 \times 9 = 63\text{ cm}^2$
Answer: $63\text{ cm}^2$
14. Solve for $y$: $5y + 14 = 39$
$5y = 39 - 14$
$5y = 25$
$y = 5$
Answer: 5
15. A train travels at an average speed of $65\text{ mph}$ for $3.5\text{ hours}$. What is the total distance traveled?
$\text{Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time}$
$\text{Distance} = 65 \times 3.5 = 227.5\text{ miles}$
Answer: 227.5 miles
16. Convert $0.85$ to a fraction in simplest form.
$0.85 = \frac{85}{100} = \frac{17}{20}$
Answer: $\frac{17}{20}$
17. Find the perimeter of a rectangle with length $16.5\text{ m}$ and width $8.2\text{ m}$.
$\text{Perimeter} = 2 \times (\text{length} + \text{width})$
$\text{Perimeter} = 2 \times (16.5 + 8.2) = 2 \times 24.7 = 49.4\text{ m}$
Answer: 49.4 m
18. Evaluate: $3^4 - 2^5 + \sqrt{144}$
$3^4 = 81$, $2^5 = 32$, $\sqrt{144} = 12$
$81 - 32 + 12 = 49 + 12 = 61$
Answer: 61
19. If 4 bags of apples cost $18.00, how much do 7 bags cost at the same unit rate?
Unit price = $18.00 \div 4 = $4.50 per bag
Total for 7 bags = $4.50 \times 7 = $31.50
Answer: $31.50
20. Find the volume of a rectangular prism with length $8\text{ cm}$, width $5\text{ cm}$, and height $12\text{ cm}$.
$\text{Volume} = l \times w \times h = 8 \times 5 \times 12 = 480\text{ cm}^3$
Answer: $480\text{ cm}^3$
21. What is 35% of 240?
$0.35 \times 240 = 84$
Answer: 84
22. Solve for $x$: $\frac{x}{6} + 7 = 15$
$\frac{x}{6} = 15 - 7$
$\frac{x}{6} = 8$
$x = 48$
Answer: 48
23. Find the mean of the numbers: 14, 22, 18, 29, 32.
$\text{Sum} = 14 + 22 + 18 + 29 + 32 = 115$
$\text{Mean} = 115 \div 5 = 23$
Answer: 23
24. Evaluate: $4.5 \div 0.09$
$\frac{4.5}{0.09} = \frac{450}{9} = 50$
Answer: 50
25. A store offers a 20% discount on an item priced at $145. What is the sale price?
Discount = $0.20 \times 145 = $29
Sale Price = $145 - 29 = $116
Answer: $116
26. Calculate the circumference of a circle with a radius of $7\text{ cm}$ (use $\pi \approx 3.14$).
$C = 2 \pi r = 2 \times 3.14 \times 7 = 43.96\text{ cm}$
Answer: 43.96 cm
27. Evaluate: $(-15) - (-28) + (-12)$
$-15 + 28 - 12 = 13 - 12 = 1$
Answer: 1
28. Write $\frac{23}{4}$ as a mixed number.
$23 \div 4 = 5$ R $3$
Answer: $5\frac{3}{4}$
29. Solve for $p$: $3(p - 4) = 21$
$p - 4 = 7$
$p = 11$
Answer: 11
30. What is the least common multiple (LCM) of 12 and 18?
Multiples of 12: 12, 24, 36, 48...
Multiples of 18: 18, 36, 54...
LCM = 36
Answer: 36
