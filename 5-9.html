Saxon Math Course 1 - Analogous Solutions (Set 2)

Lesson Practice

a. Find the sum: $6\text{ ft } 8\text{ in} + 4\text{ ft } 7\text{ in}$
Answer: $11\text{ ft } 3\text{ in}$

Explanation: First, add the feet and inches separately:
$6\text{ ft} + 4\text{ ft} = 10\text{ ft}$
$8\text{ in} + 7\text{ in} = 15\text{ in}$
Since $12\text{ inches} = 1\text{ foot}$, convert $15\text{ inches}$ into $1\text{ foot } 3\text{ inches}$.
Total: $10\text{ ft} + 1\text{ ft } 3\text{ in} = 11\text{ ft } 3\text{ in}$.
b. Find the difference: $5\text{ hr } 15\text{ min} - 2\text{ hr } 45\text{ min}$
Answer: $2\text{ hr } 30\text{ min}$

Explanation: You cannot subtract 45 min from 15 min, so borrow 1 hour (60 minutes) from the 5 hours.
$5\text{ hr } 15\text{ min}$ becomes $4\text{ hr } 75\text{ min}$.
Now subtract: $(4\text{ hr} - 2\text{ hr}) = 2\text{ hr}$, and $(75\text{ min} - 45\text{ min}) = 30\text{ min}$.
c. Write in standard notation: $4.1 \times 10^6$
Answer: 4,100,000

Explanation: Multiplying by $10^6$ shifts the decimal point 6 places to the right. $4.1 \rightarrow 41 \rightarrow 410 \rightarrow 4100 \rightarrow 41000 \rightarrow 410000 \rightarrow 4100000$.
d. Write in standard notation: 5.2 million
Answer: 5,200,000

Explanation: "Million" means multiplying by 1,000,000.
$5.2 \times 1,000,000 = 5,200,000$.
e. Write in standard notation: $2 \frac{1}{2}$ billion
Answer: 2,500,000,000

Explanation: Change the fraction to a decimal: 2.5. Multiply by 1 billion (1,000,000,000).
$2.5 \times 1,000,000,000 = 2,500,000,000$.
f. Write in standard notation: $\frac{1}{4}$ million
Answer: 250,000

Explanation: Convert $\frac{1}{4}$ to the decimal 0.25. Multiply by 1,000,000.
$0.25 \times 1,000,000 = 250,000$.

Written Practice

1. For helping organize a school car wash, six students were given a total of $120.00. If they divide the money equally, how much money will each student receive?
Answer: $20.00

Explanation: Divide the total money by the number of students:
$120.00 ÷ 6 = $20.00.
2. Which of the following is the best estimate of the height of a standard interior room door?
A. 0.5 m    B. 2.0 m    C. 5.0 m    D. 10.0 m
Answer: B. 2.0 m

Explanation: 0.5 m is too short (knee height). 5.0 m and 10.0 m are far too tall (two to three stories high). 2.0 meters (approx 6.5 feet) is a realistic height for a standard door.
3. If the chance of rain is 60%, what is the probability that it will not rain? Express the answer as a decimal.
Answer: 0.4

Explanation: The total probability of all outcomes is 100%.
Chance of no rain = 100% - 60% = 40%.
To express 40% as a decimal, move the decimal point two places left: 0.40 or 0.4.
4. The ratio of cats to dogs at an animal shelter is 5 to 2. If there are 140 animals in total, how many cats are at the shelter?
Answer: 100

Explanation: The ratio is 5 (cats) : 2 (dogs). Total ratio parts = $5 + 2 = 7$.
Find the value of one part: $140 \div 7 = 20$ animals per part.
Number of cats = $5\text{ parts} \times 20 = 100$ cats.
5. Write $8.5 \times 10^4$ as a standard numeral.
Answer: 85,000

Explanation: Move the decimal point 4 places to the right: $8.5 \rightarrow 85000$.
6. Calculate mentally:
a. $(-12)(+3)$    b. $(-12)(-3)$    c. $\frac{-12}{+4}$    d. $\frac{-12}{-4}$
Answer:
a. -36
b. 36
c. -3
d. 3

Explanation:
When multiplying or dividing:
- Different signs yield a negative result.
- Like signs yield a positive result.
7. Calculate mentally:
a. $-8 + -4$    b. $-8 - -4$    c. $+4 + -8$    d. $+4 - -8$
Answer:
a. -12
b. -4
c. -4
d. 12

Explanation:
a. Adding two negatives makes a larger negative.
b. Subtracting a negative is adding a positive: $-8 + 4 = -4$.
c. Positive plus negative: subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger, keep sign of the larger.
d. Subtracting a negative is adding a positive: $4 + 8 = 12$.
8. Describe a method for arranging these fractions from least to greatest: $\frac{3}{5}, \frac{7}{10}, \frac{1}{2}$. Then arrange them.
Answer: Least to greatest: $\frac{1}{2}, \frac{3}{5}, \frac{7}{10}$

Explanation: Find a common denominator. For 5, 10, and 2, the least common multiple (LCM) is 10.
$\frac{3}{5} = \frac{6}{10}$
$\frac{7}{10} = \frac{7}{10}$
$\frac{1}{2} = \frac{5}{10}$
Comparing numerators: $5 < 6 < 7$, thus $\frac{1}{2} < \frac{3}{5} < \frac{7}{10}$.

13. $1 \frac{1}{3}\text{ ft} \times 2 \frac{1}{4}\text{ ft}$

Explanation: Convert to improper fractions:
$\frac{4}{3} \times \frac{9}{4} = \frac{36}{12}$.
Simplify: $\frac{36}{12} = 3$.
14. $(5\text{ cm})(5\text{ cm})(5\text{ cm})$
Answer: $125\text{ cm}^3$

Explanation: Multiply the numbers: $5 \times 5 \times 5 = 125$.
Multiply the units: $\text{cm} \times \text{cm} \times \text{cm} = \text{cm}^3$.
15. $0.7\text{ m} \times 0.6\text{ m}$
Answer: $0.42\text{ m}^2$

Explanation: $0.7 \times 0.6 = 0.42$.
The units become $\text{m}^2$.
16. $5^3 + 2^4$
Answer: 141

Explanation: Evaluate the exponents first:
$5^3 = 5 \times 5 \times 5 = 125$
$2^4 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 2 = 16$
Add them: $125 + 16 = 141$.
17. Find the area of this trapezoid. Show and explain your work to justify your answer.
(Top base = 8 ft, Bottom base = 14 ft, Height = 6 ft)
Answer: 66 sq ft

Explanation: The formula for the area of a trapezoid is $A = \frac{1}{2}(b_1 + b_2)h$.
Substitute the given values: $A = \frac{1}{2}(14 + 8) \times 6$.
$A = \frac{1}{2}(22) \times 6 = 11 \times 6 = 66$.
18. $5\text{ feet } 3\text{ inches} - 1\text{ foot } 9\text{ inches}$
Answer: $3\text{ feet } 6\text{ inches}$

Explanation: You cannot subtract 9 in from 3 in, so borrow 1 foot (12 inches) from the 5 feet.
$5\text{ ft } 3\text{ in}$ becomes $4\text{ ft } 15\text{ in}$.
Subtract: $(4\text{ ft} - 1\text{ ft}) = 3\text{ ft}$. $(15\text{ in} - 9\text{ in}) = 6\text{ inches}$.
19. Consider a capital letter 'H' drawn with two vertical lines and one central horizontal bar.
a. Does a horizontal line passing straight through the middle bar act as a line of symmetry?
b. Does the letter 'H' have rotational symmetry? Explain your answer.
Answer:
a. Yes, it is a line of symmetry.
b. Yes, it has rotational symmetry.

Explanation:
a. A line of symmetry divides a figure into two identical halves. Folding the top half of the 'H' over the horizontal middle perfectly aligns with the bottom half.
b. If you rotate the letter 'H' $180^\circ$ around its exact center, it maps perfectly onto itself before completing a full $360^\circ$ rotation, proving it has rotational symmetry.
20. How many cubes one centimeter on each edge would be needed to fill this box?
(Dimensions: 5 cm × 4 cm × 4 cm)
Answer: 80 cubes

Explanation: Find the volume of the rectangular prism: $V = \text{length} \times \text{width} \times \text{height}$.
$V = 5 \times 4 \times 4 = 80\text{ cm}^3$.
Since each cube is $1\text{ cm}^3$, exactly 80 cubes are needed.
21. Marcus worked for four days and earned $240. At that rate, how much would he earn in six days?
Answer: $360

Explanation: First, find his daily rate by dividing the total earned by the number of days:
$240 \div 4\text{ days} = \$60\text{ per day}$.
Then multiply the daily rate by 6 days: $\$60 \times 6 = \$360$.
22. Seventy (70) is the product of which three prime numbers?
Answer: 2, 5, and 7

Explanation: Find the prime factorization of 70.
Since it is even, $70 = 2 \times 35$.
$35 = 5 \times 7$.
So, $70 = 2 \times 5 \times 7$. All three are prime.
23. Mars is about 140 million miles from the Sun. Write that distance in standard notation.
Answer: 140,000,000 miles

Explanation: "Million" signifies 6 zeros after the number, or multiplying by 1,000,000.
$140 \times 1,000,000 = 140,000,000$.
24. Use a rhombus with Side = 15 in, Height = 12 in, and an acute angle of $60^\circ$ for problems a-c.
a. What is the perimeter of this rhombus?
b. What is the area of this rhombus?
c. What is the measure of each obtuse angle in this rhombus?
Answer:
a. 60 in.
b. 180 sq in.
c. $120^\circ$

Explanation:
a. A rhombus has 4 equal sides. $P = 4 \times 15\text{ in} = 60\text{ in.}$
b. Area of a rhombus is base × height. $A = 15\text{ in} \times 12\text{ in} = 180\text{ sq in.}$
c. Consecutive angles in a rhombus are supplementary (add up to $180^\circ$). Obtuse angle = $180^\circ - 60^\circ = 120^\circ$.
25. The ratio of quarters to dimes in a coin bank is 5 to 8. If there are 150 quarters, how many dimes are there?
Answer: 240 dimes

Explanation: Set up a proportion: $\frac{\text{Quarters}}{\text{Dimes}} = \frac{5}{8} = \frac{150}{x}$.
Since $5 \times 30 = 150$, multiply the denominator by the same factor: $8 \times 30 = 240$.
26. a. The coordinates of the three vertices of a right triangle are (0, 0), (4, 0), and (4, 6). What is the area of the triangle?
b. If the triangle were reflected across the y-axis, what would be the coordinates of the vertices of the reflection?
Answer:
a. 12 square units
b. (0, 0), (-4, 0), and (-4, 6)

Explanation:
a. Plotting the points reveals a right triangle with a base of 4 units and a height of 6 units. Area = $\frac{1}{2}bh = \frac{1}{2}(4)(6) = 12$.
b. Reflecting across the y-axis negates the x-coordinates: $(x, y) \rightarrow (-x, y)$. The point at the origin (0,0) stays the same.
27. What is the median of this data set?
(Data: 20, 15, 12, 18, 18, 22, 15, 20)
Answer: 18

Explanation: First, list the 8 numbers in order from least to greatest:
12, 15, 15, 18, 18, 20, 20, 22.
Since there is an even number of values, the median is the average of the 4th and 5th middle values: $\frac{18 + 18}{2} = 18$.
28. What is the mean of this data set?
(Data: 20, 15, 12, 18, 18, 22, 15, 20)
Answer: 17.5

Explanation: The mean is the sum of all values divided by the number of values.
Sum = $20+15+12+18+18+22+15+20 = 140$.
Mean = $140 \div 8 = 17.5$.
29. The diameter of a circular rug is 30 cm. What is the circumference of the rug? (Use 3.14 for $\pi$.) How can estimation help you determine if your answer is reasonable?
Answer: 94.2 cm

Explanation: Circumference formula: $C = \pi \times d$.
$C = 3.14 \times 30 = 94.2\text{ cm}$.
Estimation: Since $\pi$ is approximately 3, the circumference should be roughly $3 \times 30 = 90$. Since 94.2 is close to 90, the answer is reasonable.
30. Find the value of $V$ in $V = s^3$ when $s$ is 6 cm.
Answer: $216\text{ cm}^3$

Explanation: Substitute 6 for $s$ in the formula:
$V = (6\text{ cm})^3$
$V = 6\text{ cm} \times 6\text{ cm} \times 6\text{ cm} = 216\text{ cm}^3$.
