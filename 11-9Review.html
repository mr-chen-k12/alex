1.(Lesson 28)AVERAGE / MEAN
During the first six months of the year, the Henderson family’s monthly water bills were $68.50, $72.10, $65.40, $78.20, $69.80, and $72.00. How can the Hendersons find their average monthly water bill for the first six months of the year, and what is that average?
Answer
\(\text{Add the six bills and divide by }6\text{ — the average is }\$71.00\)
Solution
To find the average (mean), sum the six bills and divide by 6:
\(68.50 + 72.10 + 65.40 + 78.20 + 69.80 + 72.00 = \$426.00\)
\(426.00 \div 6 = \$71.00\).
2.(Lessons 23, 30)FRACTIONS
There were \(3\frac{1}{4}\) gallons of juice in the refrigerator before a party. Afterward, there were \(1\frac{5}{6}\) gallons remaining. How many gallons of juice were consumed during the party?
Answer
\(1\frac{5}{12}\text{ gallons}\)
Solution
Subtract the remaining juice from the starting amount: \(3\frac{1}{4} - 1\frac{5}{6}\).
Find a common denominator (12): \(3\frac{3}{12} - 1\frac{10}{12}\).
Regroup / borrow: \(2\frac{15}{12} - 1\frac{10}{12} = 1\frac{5}{12}\) gallons.
3.(Lesson 28)UNIT RATE
A one-year subscription to a monthly magazine costs $21.60. The regular newsstand price is $2.25 per issue. How much is saved per issue by paying the subscription price?
Answer
\(\$0.45\text{ per issue}\)
Solution
A monthly subscription includes 12 issues. Subscription cost per issue: \(\$21.60 \div 12 = \$1.80\).
Savings per issue: \(\$2.25 - \$1.80 = \$0.45\).
4.(Lesson 28)ELAPSED TIME
In a track meet, Runner A finished the 400 m race in 1 minute 15 seconds. Runner B finished the same race 18 seconds faster than Runner A. How many seconds did it take Runner B to complete the race?
Answer
\(57\text{ seconds}\)
Solution
Convert Runner A's time to seconds: 1 min 15 sec = 75 seconds.
Subtract 18 seconds: \(75 - 18 = 57\) seconds.
5.(Lesson 19)GEOMETRY
The perimeter of a square equals the perimeter of a regular hexagon. Each side of the hexagon is 10 cm long. How long is each side of the square?
Answer
\(15\text{ cm}\)
Solution
The hexagon has 6 equal sides, so its perimeter is \(6 \times 10 = 60\text{ cm}\).
Since the square has 4 equal sides and the same perimeter: \(60 \div 4 = 15\text{ cm}\).
6.(Lesson 22)WORD PROBLEM
Only \(\frac{2}{15}\) of the 120 students in a middle school grade participate in the chess club.
a. How many students participate in the chess club?
b. How many students do not participate in the chess club?
Answer
\(\text{a. }16\text{ students} \qquad \text{b. }104\text{ students}\)
Solution
a. \(\frac{1}{15}\) of 120 is \(120 \div 15 = 8\). So \(\frac{2}{15}\) is \(2 \times 8 = 16\) students.
b. Students not participating: \(120 - 16 = 104\) students.
7.(Lesson 20)AREA OF COMPOSITE SHAPES
A 4-by-4-cm square is cut from an 8-by-8-cm square sheet of paper as shown. Refer to this figure to answer a–c:
a. What was the area of the original square?
b. What was the area of the square that was cut out?
c. What is the area of the remaining figure?
Answer
\(\text{a. }64\text{ cm}^2 \qquad \text{b. }16\text{ cm}^2 \qquad \text{c. }48\text{ cm}^2\)
Solution
a. Original area: \(8 \times 8 = 64\text{ cm}^2\).
b. Cutout area: \(4 \times 4 = 16\text{ cm}^2\).
c. Remaining area: \(64 - 16 = 48\text{ cm}^2\).

9.(Investigation 3)COORDINATE PLANE
The coordinates of three vertices of a rectangle are (−4, 3), (4, −3), and (−4, −3).
a. What are the coordinates of the fourth vertex?
b. What is the area of the rectangle?
Answer
\(\text{a. }(4, 3) \qquad \text{b. }48\text{ square units}\)
Solution
a. Pair x- and y-values to complete rectangle: (4, 3).

12.(Lesson 33)ESTIMATION

a. About how much did she pay for the gasoline? Show how you rounded.
b. Why do gas stations state prices with tenths of a cent?
Answer
\(\text{a. About }\$38.40 \qquad \text{b. See solution}\)

30.(Extension)PEMDAS
Simplify using the order of operations (PEMDAS):
$$\left( -\frac{3}{4} + 1.25 \right)^2 \cdot (-2)^3 - 3.6 \div (-0.9)$$
Answer
\(2\)
Solution
1. Parentheses: \(-\frac{3}{4} + 1.25 = -0.75 + 1.25 = 0.5\).
2. Exponents: \((0.5)^2 = 0.25\) and \((-2)^3 = -8\).
3. Multiplication: \(0.25 \cdot (-8) = -2\).
4. Division: \(3.6 \div (-0.9) = -4\).
5. Subtraction: \(-2 - (-4) = -2 + 4 = 2\).
